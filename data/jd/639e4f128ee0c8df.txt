At Reality Labs Research, our goal is to explore, innovate and design novel interfaces and hardware for the next generation of virtual, augmented, and mixed
reality experiences. We are driving research towards a vision of natural, seamless experiences in XR environments that are effective, enjoyable, and
functionally indistinguishable from those in the real world.As a Research Scientist Intern, you will work at the intersection of AI agents, laboratory
automation, and wearable computing, alongside a world-leading materials innovation and discovery team. Your primary focus will be on building the software layer
that lets AI agents communicate with and operate laboratory equipment, and on turning first-person capture from smart glasses, together with instrument data,
into a time-aligned digital record of experiments. This work is a step toward self-driving labs, where knowledge captured from expert researchers becomes
structured, executable protocols for agents and, eventually, robots.Our internships are twelve (12) to twenty-four (24) weeks long and we have various start
dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, AI Agents for Autonomous Laboratories (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Design and implement interfaces (for example, MCP servers, APIs, and instrument drivers) that let AI agents query status, issue commands, and retrieve data
from laboratory equipment used for synthesis, characterization, and crystal growth.
- Develop agentic workflows that plan and run experimental steps, with safety constraints, human-in-the-loop approval, and full action logging.
- Build pipelines that convert first-person video, audio, and instrument telemetry from smart glasses into a synchronized experiment timeline and structured,
machine-readable protocols.
- Apply and evaluate multimodal foundation models (vision-language, speech) to recognize lab actions, materials, and events, including failures and anomalies.
- Link captured procedures with characterization and process data in a searchable experiment record.
- Collaborate with materials scientists, chemists, and AI researchers to define requirements, test with real lab users, and measure impact.
- Publish research results and contribute to research that can be applied to Meta product development.
