The Computer Vision Software Engineer Intern will join the Software Engineering team supporting TESSA™ Spatial Surgery, Smith+Nephew’s computer-assisted
solution for orthopedic surgical navigation. Working with experienced engineers, the intern will contribute to real-time computer vision and video software that
helps surgeons visualize guidance and anatomical context during procedure planning and execution.
This role offers hands-on experience across image capture and processing, tracking, calibration, geometric computation, and overlay rendering on GPU-accelerated
Linux platforms. The intern will deliver a defined, meaningful project, learn how software is developed and verified in a regulated medical-device environment,
and gain exposure to the impact of navigation technology in arthroscopy. A hybrid schedule is anticipated to enable regular work with development and test
hardware.
What will you be doing?
- Work with experienced mentors to define and complete a focused internship project with a measurable outcome for Smith+Nephew’s navigation software.
- Develop, test, and maintain computer vision features for real-time surgical navigation, including image processing, augmented-reality overlays, and geometric
computations.
- Use MATLAB, Python, Julia, or similar tools where appropriate to prototype algorithms, analyze data, or create validation scripts.
- Participate in code reviews, technical documentation, and agile team practices using Git, GitLab, CI, and standardized or containerized development
environments.
- Collaborate effectively with colleagues across different locations and time zones.
- Develop C++ skills while learning and applying medical-software practices related to quality, traceability, risk awareness, and verification under the
mentorship of experienced engineers.
- Document the project, its results, and key learnings, and present the outcome to engineering stakeholders and senior leadership.
Planned Start Date: May 2027

What Will You Need To Be Successful
Education:
- Currently enrolled in an accredited Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering,
Software Engineering, or a closely related field.
- Candidates at approximately the third year of undergraduate study or beyond are encouraged to apply; equivalent project-based experience will also be
considered.
- Must be in good academic standing, with a 3.0 minimum GPA preferred.
Experience:
- Demonstrated programming experience through coursework, academic or personal projects, a prior internship, co-op, part-time role, apprenticeship, or research.
- Programming experience in MATLAB, Python, Julia, C++, or another language through coursework or projects. Previous exposure to C++ is an advantage, but it is
not required.
- Coursework or project exposure to computer vision or image processing, such as camera models, image filtering, feature detection, multi-view geometry,
tracking, or camera calibration
- Working knowledge of linear algebra and 3D geometry, including rotations, rigid transforms, and projection.
- Exposure to computer vision libraries or software build tools, such as OpenCV or CMake, is a plus.
- Exposure to NVIDIA Jetson, CUDA, Holoscan, or similar GPU or streaming platforms is a plus.
- Familiarity with Eigen, Ceres, optimization methods, point clouds, registration, video pipelines, tracking, or augmented-reality overlays is a plus.
Competencies:
- Strong interest in computer vision, medical technology, robotics, augmented reality, or real-time embedded systems.
- Curiosity about surgical navigation and enthusiasm for understanding how software can support arthroscopy workflows and patient care.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a practical and methodical approach to experimentation and debugging.
- Ability to design, implement, test, and clearly document software solutions with guidance from experienced engineers.
- Clear written and verbal communication and the ability to collaborate with geographically distributed teams.
- Ability to work independently on defined tasks while seeking feedback and contributing effectively as part of a team.
- High learning agility and motivation to turn academic knowledge into an impactful engineering contribution.
