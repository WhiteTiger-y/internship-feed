Job Description
The Future Talent Program within our Research & Development Division Data, AI, and Genome Sciences (DAGS) Department is offering internships that last 10-12
weeks. We have 1 open position in the AI/ML Methods & Modeling Group. Candidates can apply for the opportunity to be involved in one or more of the projects
listed below. The projects are designed to enable students to further develop their skills and to evaluate if we are the right Company for their long-term
goals. We are seeking highly motivated students with computational skillsets.
Location: Cambridge, MA
Term: Full-time, 12 weeks
AI/ML Methods & Modeling
- Develop statistical and computational methods/pipelines to analyze genetic and genomic datasets.
- Analyze of large-scale human genetic and genomic datasets to facilitate discovery of novel targets and biomarkers of disease risk and progression.
- Work within an integrated, interdisciplinary team supporting validation efforts, including bioinformatic analysis of bulk and single-cell sequencing.
Required Education And Skills
- Must be currently enrolled in a PhD degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, or related technical field.
- Must be available to work full-time for 10-12 weeks beginning in May or June of 2027.
- Excellent software design and development skills and strong proficiency in Python.
- Strong proficiency in modern ML frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX) and experience implementing scalable solutions for complex tasks.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work collaboratively in a multi-disciplinary team.
- Interest in life sciences problems and disease biology, and willing to learn from and teach others.
Preferred Skills And Experience
- Experience fine-tuning foundation models on multiple GPUs is a strong plus.
- Experience with biological foundation models (e.g., single-cell foundation models) is a strong plus.
- Experience with biological data (e.g., perturb-seq) is a strong plus.
- Publications in top-tier ML conferences (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR) is a strong plus.
Please note that this position may be closed before the posted end date or may remain open longer, at the discretion of the company.
