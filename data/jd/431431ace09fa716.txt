Looking for an internship program where you'll make a real difference – rather than just completing busy work? Then you've come to the right place. The Cigna
Group’s Summer Internship Program is designed to assign you projects that have a lasting impact on our business – which touches millions of people across the
globe. As an intern, you'll get an inside look at your desired field, while working next to some of the best in the industry. Program activities, including an
executive speaker series, volunteer events, and career development workshops, give you the opportunity to network with employees across the organization and
prepare you for a full-time career. Where You’ll Work This Internship will be working with the Prospect Analytics Department with a focus on providing reporting
and analytical support for the finance, clinical, sales, and provider focused solutions. This team utilizes pharmacy data to gather insights on prospective
clients. The intern will support departmental tasks and will be exposed to data analytics to enhance insights. During the summer internship, you will have the
opportunity to collaborate within your department and across the business while learning from some of our brightest and most innovative employees in your area
of focus. What You Will Do Real work: Over the course of your internship, you’ll be assigned real business problems that challenge your analytical and technical
skills while helping you build industry knowledge. Your work will also allow you to make direct contributions to our mission and company goals. Example
projects/experiences: Analyze consultant and coalition RFP trends and automate standard bid responses for faster turnaround time Develop prospect benchmarking
metrics by aggregating historical pharmacy and clinical data, defining benchmark metrics across client segments, and calculating measures of variation to
support prospect evaluations and finalist discussions. Gain valuable insights into the pharmacy industry and data analytics, enhancing your understanding of
both. Conduct research on key topics related to pharmacy data and analytics and present your findings to the team. Collaborate cross-functionally with different
teams to support the analytics department in delivering high-quality insights and solutions. Participate in hands-on projects that will directly impact the
business and help drive strategic decision-making. Qualifications Rising junior or senior working towards a bachelor’s degree. Preferred majors include Business
Analytics, Data Science, Economics, Informatics, Statistics, Management Information Systems, or any related quantitative field. Prior experience with SQL is
highly desirable. Exposure to AWS, Python, or R is a plus. Strong attention to detail and an ability to think analytically. Excellent verbal and written
communication skills; with comfortability using Webex and other virtual tools to connect with colleagues. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word,
PowerPoint). A self-starter who is eager to learn, acquire new skills, and take on challenges in a fast-paced environment. Additional Information Location: This
is an in-office internship based at the Morris Plains office (115 Tabor Road, Morris Plains, NJ 07950). Work Authorization: One goal of this internship program
