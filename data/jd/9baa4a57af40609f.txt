About The Program
The Nu AI Core Summer Internship Program is a research-focused internship for PhD students working at the frontier of artificial intelligence and machine
learning.
Over about 12 weeks, interns collaborate with engineers, applied scientists, and researchers on complex AI challenges. Each intern leads an end-to-end research
or applied AI project that combines research methods with large-scale production environments.
The program builds long-term relationships between industry and academia and with emerging AI research talent.
Program Structure
- Duration: ~12 weeks
- Start dates: May–June, aligned with US university calendars
- Location model: Remote, with one in-person week at Nu's Palo Alto office in Silicon Valley, fully covered by us.
What You Get
- Dedicated research mentor
- Ownership of a scoped AI research project
- Access to large-scale datasets and compute infrastructure
- Research talks and technical seminars
- Final research presentation to leadership and AI teams
Research Areas
Interns May Work On Projects Related To
- Machine learning and deep learning
- Large language models and generative AI
- Recommender systems and ranking models
- AI infrastructure and model optimization
- Responsible AI and model evaluation
Projects typically combine research experimentation with applied system development, including model design, experimentation, and large-scale evaluation.
Candidate Profile
This program targets PhD students conducting research in AI and machine learning who are interested in applying their work to real-world systems.
Applicants are typically pursuing a PhD in fields such as:
- Computer Science
- Machine Learning
- Artificial Intelligence
- Statistics
- Mathematics
- Computational Linguistics
- Electrical Engineering
- or related quantitative disciplines.
Basic Qualifications (Application/ Screening Filters)
- Currently enrolled in a PhD program
- Research focus in machine learning, AI, statistics or related areas
- Strong programming skills in Python or similar languages
- Experience working with machine learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow)
- Experience designing experiments and evaluating models
- Experience working with large datasets or training ML models
Preferred Qualifications
Strong candidates may also demonstrate:
- Publications at top AI/ML conferences (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, KDD, RecSys, CVPR, CIKM or equivalent-conferences for related fields)
- Experience working with large-scale ML systems or distributed training
- Experience with LLMs, generative AI, or foundation models
- Open-source contributions in AI or ML
- Experience collaborating in interdisciplinary research environments
What We Value
We are particularly interested in candidates who demonstrate:
- Strong research rigor and experimental methodology
- Ability to translate research ideas into practical systems
- Track record of identifying novel research directions or reframing existing problems
- Intellectual curiosity and independent problem solving
- Collaboration with engineers and cross-functional teams
- Interest in deploying AI systems with real-world impact
