Overview
Headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, Wolf & Company, P.C. is one of the top 100 largest accounting firms in the United States and is the fourth largest in
New England. We have been providing unparalleled guidance to our clients for over 100 years.
Wolf is seeking an AI & Automation Intern in our Office of Strategic Initiatives (OSI) team to join our growing and well-established national firm. The Office
of Strategic Initiatives leads Wolf’s internal transformation around operations and technology, including AI, and operates our mergers & acquisitions and
corporate development functions.
Responsibilities
As an intern, you will support our OSI AI & Automation group by building and testing AI assistants, agents and workflows in Copilot Studio, Agent Builder, Power
Automate, and Claude. You’ll be building out requirements documentation, infrastructure and architecture, and proof of concepts for the AI roadmap. The OSI
Intern will also turn manual processes into working automations by mapping the current steps, building the tool, testing it with the people who use it, and
fixing what breaks.
*Candidate is expected to work at least 1 day each week in our Boston office.
Qualifications
- Currently be enrolled at a college or university
- Strong interest in AI, automation, and emerging technology
- Hands-on experience with generative AI tools such as Copilot, Claude, or ChatGPT
- Independent, self-starting, and self-motivated
- Excellent time management skills
- Demonstrates strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including documenting technical requirements
- Proficient in MS Office, especially Excel
- Experience with low-code tools (Power Automate, Copilot Studio) or basic scripting is a plus
