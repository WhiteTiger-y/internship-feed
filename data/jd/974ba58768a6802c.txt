Molex’s Optical Connectivity Group (OCG) is seeking a curious, analytical, and self-motivated Data & Analytics Intern to support business-focused data and
analytics initiatives during our Summer 2027 internship program.
This internship is designed for a student who enjoys working with data, solving ambiguous problems, and using technology to turn information into actionable
insights. You will support real business challenges and build hands-on skills in data analysis, automation, and data engineering.
We are looking for a self-starter with strong analytical fundamentals who takes ownership of their own learning, understands the purpose behind their work, and
can quickly pick up new technical skills.
Our Team
This role will sit within the Optical Connectivity Group (OCG), the fasting growing group within Molex. Join a global connectivity leader with more than 80
years of innovation. Molex’s interconnect solutions power the industries shaping tomorrow, including data centers, AI infrastructure, automotive, medical, and
industrial technology. As demand for connectivity grows, so does our need for data-driven talent.
What You Will Do
- Support data analysis and data engineering projects that improve how teams access, organize, and use business data.
- Help develop and improve data pipelines, data structures, and backend data processes.
- Use tools such as Snowflake, SQL, Python, Excel, Streamlit, or Power BI to analyze and work with datasets.
- Explore ways to apply AI, automation, and scripting to make existing processes more efficient.
- Translate data into clear insights that support business decisions.
- Develop or enhance dashboards and reporting solutions as needed.
- Partner with cross-functional teams to understand business problems, define analytical questions, and identify solutions.
- Evaluate data, test assumptions, and adjust your approach as new information emerges.
- Present findings, recommendations, and lessons learned to team members and business stakeholders.
Who You Are (Basic Qualifications)
- Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Data Analytics, Data Science, Business Analytics, Computer Science, Economics, Information Systems,
Statistics, Engineering, or a related field.
- Foundational experience with at least one data or analytical tool, such as SQL, Python, R, Excel, Power BI, or Tableau (coursework and personal projects
count).
- Understanding of basic data analysis concepts and a desire to build deeper technical skills.
- Ability to clearly explain the problem, approach, analysis, and outcome of an academic, personal, or professional project.
- Strong problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.
- Curiosity and willingness to independently learn unfamiliar tools and technologies.
- Ability to work through ambiguity, take initiative, and change course when data points in a different direction.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to explain technical findings to non-technical audiences.
What Will Put You Ahead
- Exposure to SQL and relational databases.
- Experience using Python or R for data manipulation, analysis, or automation.
- Exposure to data engineering concepts, including data pipelines, ETL/ELT processes, APIs, or database structures.
- Experience with Power BI, Tableau, or another data visualization platform.
- Coursework or project experience involving AI, machine learning, automation, or advanced analytics.
- Experience completing a project in which data was used to identify a problem, test a hypothesis, or recommend an improvement.
