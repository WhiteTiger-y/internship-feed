Job Requisition ID # 26WD101373 Position Overview As a PhD Research intern in Autodesk Research’s AI Lab, you will explore and apply advanced machine learning
techniques to empower our customers in designing and building a better, safer, and more sustainable world. We are a team of researchers, engineers, and industry
domain experts working on projects that range from learning-based design systems, computer vision, graphics, robotics, human-computer interaction,
sustainability, simulation, manufacturing, architectural design and construction. We are seeking a research intern to join our research team focused on machine
learning and generative methods for content creation in the media and entertainment space. In this role, you will conduct research on novel methods for
character animation and related problems at the intersection of machine learning and computer graphics. Potential research directions include generative motion
models, controllable character animation, data-efficient learning and adaptation, representation learning, and learning approaches that incorporate physics or
simulation. The goal of this research is to enable more intuitive use of our animation tools by automating repetitive tasks. Autodesk's AI Lab is actively
engaged in the broader research community, targeting publications at top-tier conferences such as CVPR, NeurIPS, ICML, SIGGRAPH, and ICLR. We collaborate with
top academic & industry labs, combining the best of an academic environment with product-driven research. Minimum Qualifications Full-time student pursuing a
Ph.D. in Computer Science, Computer Graphics, Computational Physics, Machine Learning or a discipline in an accredited program Broad understanding of machine
learning and deep learning Excellent Programming and software development skills, with experience in Python Proficient with ML & DL platforms & libraries
(PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, ONNX/Runtime, scikit-learn, etc.) Experience conducting research and publishing results An excellent communicator and team player
Preferred Qualifications Deep expertise in one or more areas such as computer graphics, character animation, motion generation, 2D/3D geometry representation
learning, generative models, and unsupervised/self-supervised learning Experience with differentiable physics, data-driven computational physics, solver-driven
learning (physics-in-the-loop), physics-informed models Experience with transfer learning, fine-tuning, few-shot learning, or other data-efficient learning
methods is a plus Bonus: experience with physics real-time engines (such as Bullet, Unreal, or PhysX) Bonus: experience with modeling tools (such as Maya,
Houdini, or Blender) Responsibilities Conduct research on machine learning methods for character animation and related problems in media and entertainment
