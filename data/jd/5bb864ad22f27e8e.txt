Your Future Team
Does the challenge of scaling a fast-growing company by powering it with data, reporting, and analytics excite you? Using your business instincts and
creativity, you will support a market-leading product with nuanced analysis, relevant insights, and recommendations.
What You'll Do
- Develop data-driven hypothesis to identify new opportunities and apply rigorous statistical methodology to evaluate product experiments, feature rollouts and
user behavior
- Partner cross-functionally with data science, data engineering and business teams to translate complex findings into clear, actionable recommendations
- Analyze rich user interaction and telemetry datasets to uncover user journey patterns, identify friction points and surface growth opportunities
- Build robust data artifacts such as interactive dashboards, reproducible notebooks, or AI agents/skills that scale data backed decision-making across teams
Responsibilities
Your Background
- Proficiency with SQL or other query languages and experience with Python or R for statistical computing, data analysis and predictive modeling
- Solid understanding of statistical methods, hypothesis testing, experimental design (A/B testing), or machine learning fundamentals
- Experience with data visualization tools (e.g: Tableau, Databricks, Mode or other BI platforms)
- Familiarity with modern AI tools and GenAI workflows (e.g., LLM APIs, AI coding assistants, prompt engineering, or building AI agents/skills)
- Strong communication skills with the ability to convey quantitative insights to both technical and non-technical teammates
- Availability & Program Commitment: Able to commit to a 12-week full-time (40hrs/week) program during Summer 2027
- Enrollment & Eligibility: Currently enrolled full-time in a Masters degree program and returning to the program after the completion of the internship,
graduating by August 2028
