The ICE Internship Program offers a dynamic opportunity to combine hands-on, impactful work on projects that shape our business, alongside practical career and
professional development. You'll build meaningful connections, strengthen your interpersonal skills, and contribute in ways that truly make a difference.
Beyond your day-to-day responsibilities, you'll participate in weekly sessions led by industry leaders, connect with fellow interns, and deepen your
understanding of the financial services, technology, and mortgage industries.
The Title Data Analyst Intern will research published underwriter manuals and support accurate rate loading into the Settlement Agent Gateway (SAG), directly
supporting the accuracy of premium data surfaced to lenders and consumers at closing.
Responsibilities
- Partner with sister teams on product enhancement priorities and organization
- Learn the title premium process as it pertains to the product workflow and data upkeep
- Provide assistance with organizing product delivery schedules as well as help the team maintain up to date artifacts
- Support Title Team Management in articulating team’s title strategy that accompanies product direction and market expansion
- Assist Title Manager with maintaining volume metrics and title volume reporting that represent data analysis coverage for maintaining the product’s title
database
Knowledge and Experience
- Must be currently enrolled in a degree program at an accredited university and be at least advancing to their junior year of study
- Detail-oriented, with strong accuracy and follow-through in a focused, process-driven role
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel
Internship Program Schedule
To accommodate varying academic calendars, the ICE Internship Program offers two schedule options:
- Option 1: May 24, 2027 – August 6, 2027
- Option 2: June 7, 2027 – August 20, 2027
Please Note: Interns must be available to work full-time, five days a week, at our Jacksonville office for the entire duration of one of the designated program
schedules. Partial participation or alternate dates cannot be accommodated.
