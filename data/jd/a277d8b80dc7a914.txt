Overview: This program is designed to foster a beneficial relationship and provide growth opportunities for Vizient, our employees, and collegiate students.
Since 2000, our summer intern program has provided college undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students with practical experience that exposes them to
multiple aspects of a career field and the healthcare industry. This full-time, paid intern program offers an opportunity to accelerate academic learning, apply
classroom knowledge, and introduce students to a professional setting. Interns are assigned a supervisor to mentor/develop their business skills, as well as
monitor their work experiences. Interns are integral members of the company’s team, setting goals, working toward deadlines, and actively participating in
community service projects. The program requires the intern to commit to 10 weeks of participation in all formal training, mentoring, on-the-job learning, and
opportunities to interact with senior leaders. Program starts June 1, 2027, and ends August 6, 2027 Pay rate: $25 per hour. About the team and role The Item
Master Managed Services (IMMS) team helps healthcare organizations improve the accuracy, consistency, and integrity of their supply chain data. The team works
directly within clients’ ERP systems to manage and maintain item master and contract data, including item additions and changes, product data cleansing and
enrichment, duplicate identification, data standardization, and contract pricing accuracy. By combining healthcare supply chain expertise, standardized
processes, and Vizient data and technology, the team helps clients reduce manual effort, improve operational efficiency, strengthen data governance, and support
more accurate purchasing decisions. The intern will support the team’s ongoing focus on data quality, process improvement, and operational scalability. The role
will contribute to projects that analyze item master data, identify data quality and standardization opportunities, and help improve the processes and tools
used to manage client data. This work will provide exposure to healthcare supply chain operations, ERP data management, analytics, and the use of Vizient
resources such as the Data Management Platform (DMP) and product master data to drive measurable improvements for clients. What You’ll Do Support item master
data quality and enrichment projects, including reviewing and validating product attributes Analyze item master data using available data sources and tools to
identify discrepancies, missing information, duplicates, and opportunities to improve data quality Contribute to process-improvement opportunities that make
item master management more efficient and scalable Collaborate cross-functionally across the department for trainings and project work Summarize findings and
communicate project progress, results, and recommendations to team members and stakeholders What You’ll Learn Develop a practical understanding of healthcare
