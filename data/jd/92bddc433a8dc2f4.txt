Company Description The Light Company is an emerging technology organization focused on advancing robotics and intelligent systems to improve everyday life. The
team combines research-driven experimentation with practical engineering to develop robust, real-world robotic solutions. Interns and employees collaborate
closely with experienced scientists and engineers, gaining exposure to cutting-edge tools, methodologies, and prototyping environments. The company values
curiosity, integrity, and inclusion, and encourages team members at all levels to contribute ideas and take ownership of impactful projects.
Role Description The Robotic Evaluation Research Intern will support the design, execution, and analysis of experiments that assess robotic system performance
and reliability. Day-to-day responsibilities include building and running test scenarios, collecting and organizing data, documenting findings, and helping
refine evaluation protocols in collaboration with senior researchers and engineers. The intern will assist in preparing reports and presentations that summarize
results and recommend improvements to hardware, software, or control strategies. This is a full-time, on-site role located in the San Francisco Bay Area, and
requires regular collaboration with cross-functional team members in lab and workshop environments.
Qualifications
- Foundational knowledge of robotics, control systems, or mechanical/electrical engineering, with the ability to understand robotic architectures and evaluation
needs.
- Experience or coursework in experimental design, data collection, and basic statistical analysis to support rigorous research and performance assessment.
- Ability to document experiments clearly, prepare concise summaries, and communicate technical findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Familiarity with laboratory or workshop safety practices, plus hands-on skills in assembling, troubleshooting, or operating robotic or electromechanical
systems.
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in robotics, engineering, computer science, or a related field.
- Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and the ability to work both independently and collaboratively in an on-site team environment.
- Interest in emerging robotics technologies and a willingness to learn new tools, frameworks, and testing methodologies.
