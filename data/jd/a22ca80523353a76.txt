POSITION SUMMARY:
The National Life Group Internship Program was developed to provide real workplace learning and growth opportunities for undergraduate students. Over the years
the program has provided students with the opportunity to engage in meaningful, career-relevant work at National Life Group in preparation for full-time
employment.
This position currently offers an onsite work schedule, with the expectation that you are in the office four (4) days per week during onsite core days. Our
current onsite core days are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. The work schedule type and core days are subject to change with advance notification and
manager discretion.
RESPONSIBILITIES:
The Market Research Intern will support projects focused on consumer insights, market sizing, and sales trend analysis. This role provides an opportunity for an
undergraduate student to gain hands-on experience working with real business data to inform strategic decisions.
The intern will collaborate with cross-functional teams including marketing, distribution, strategy, and product to help uncover insights about customer
behavior, market opportunities, and business performance. This position is ideal for a student interested in applying analytical and research skills in a
business setting.
- Assist in analyzing consumer data (customer demographic data, survey data, qualitative interview data) to identify trends and insights
- Support market sizing and segmentation analyses to evaluate growth opportunities across products, markets, and customer segments
- Analyze sales performance data to identify trends, drivers of performance, and areas for improvement
- Help develop presentations and reports that communicate insights clearly to stakeholders
- Conduct competitive and industry research to inform strategic initiatives
- Partner with team members to translate data into actionable business recommendations
- Support ad hoc research and analytics projects across marketing and strategy teams
