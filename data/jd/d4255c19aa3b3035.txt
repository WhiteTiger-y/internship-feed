Dr. Hoggatt Krumwiede will serve as the primary faculty mentor for trainees through her research lab in applied visualization. Participants may also engage with
secondary mentors and collaborators from MD Anderson, the School of Health Professions, and partner institutions as appropriate to support interdisciplinary
research activities.
Each participant will develop an Individual Development Plan (IDP) with the faculty mentor to establish research, professional development, and career goals.
The trainee will participate in a structured program that combines mentored research, scholarly development, and professional enrichment.
Required Activities
- Participation in ongoing research projects involving biomedical visualization and virtual reality.
- Literature reviews and synthesis of relevant scientific and educational research.
- Development of biomedical visualizations, educational materials, and virtual reality applications.
- Research study design, protocol development, and project planning.
- Data collection, management, and analysis activities.
- Regular meetings with faculty mentor and research collaborators.
- Participation in seminars, workshops, journal clubs, and professional development activities.
- Preparation and dissemination of scholarly products, including posters, presentations, abstracts, manuscripts, or project reports.
Elective Activities
- Collaboration with clinicians, educators, and researchers across MD Anderson and the Texas Medical Center.
- Conference attendance and professional networking opportunities.
- Leadership and mentoring activities involving students and trainees when appropriate.
All duties and responsibilities are carried out in compliance with institutional policies, ethical research standards, and applicable federal and state
regulations.
LEARNING OBJECTIVES
By the completion of the program, participants will be able to:
Research Methodology
- Understand the design and implementation of research studies related to biomedical visualization, virtual reality, health professions education, or cancer
survivorship.
- Conduct literature reviews and critically evaluate scientific evidence.
- Technical and Laboratory Skills
- Develop biomedical visualizations using industry-standard software.
- Create and implement virtual reality applications using Unity and related technologies.
- Apply principles of instructional design, user-centered design, and visual communication to educational research projects.
Data Analysis
- Collect, manage, analyze, and interpret qualitative and quantitative research data.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of visualization and virtual reality interventions.
Scientific Communication
- Prepare scientific abstracts, posters, presentations, manuscripts, and project reports.
- Communicate research findings effectively to academic, clinical, and community audiences.
Professional Skills and Leadership
- Develop collaborative research and professional relationships.
- Demonstrate ethical conduct of research and professional responsibility.
- Strengthen project management, teamwork, and leadership abilities.
Career Development
- Refine career goals and prepare competitive applications for doctoral education, research fellowships, and research-related careers.
- Develop a professional portfolio demonstrating research and scholarly achievements.
Expected Outcomes
By the end of the program, each participant is expected to achieve measurable outcomes that include:
- Participate in at least one substantial research or scholarly project.
- Development of biomedical visualization or virtual reality products supporting educational or survivorship-focused research initiatives.
- Presentation of research findings through a poster, oral presentation, or similar scholarly venue.
- Submission of at least one abstract, manuscript, project report, or other scholarly product.
- Demonstrated proficiency in biomedical visualization software and virtual reality development tools.
- Completion of an Individual Development Plan (IDP) and end-of-program assessment documenting achievement of training goals.
- Development of collaborative professional relationships within MD Anderson and the broader Texas Medical Center research community.
- Enhanced readiness for doctoral education, research fellowships, or research-focused
Eligibility Requirements
Applicants must meet the following eligibility requirements:
Required Qualifications
- Hold a bachelor's or master's degree from an accredited college or university in biomedical visualization, biomedical communications, biomedical sciences,
health sciences, educational technology, digital media, animation, or a related discipline.
- Have graduated within the previous two years of the proposed appointment start date, or be completing the final year of study prior to program entry.
- Demonstrate a strong academic record with a recommended minimum GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
Possess training or experience in one or more of the following areas:
- Biomedical visualization or biomedical communications
- 3D modeling and animation
- Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro)
- Unity software development
- Virtual reality technologies
- Research methods and scholarly communication
- Preferred Qualifications
- Experience conducting research or scholarly projects.
- Experience developing educational media, simulations, or virtual reality applications.
- Demonstrated interest in biomedical education, cancer survivorship, or health professions education research.
- Experience collaborating on interdisciplinary teams.
Other Eligibility Requirements
- Must not hold a doctoral degree or professional doctorate.
- Must not be concurrently enrolled in another doctoral, residency, fellowship, or clinical training program.
- Must meet all institutional onboarding and compliance requirements before appointment.
