About Trener
Trener is a Physical AI company building software-defined robotics for industrial automation. Our platform is built on the ROS 2 ecosystem, and our product,
Acteris, is running in production with real customers on real factory floors — not in a demo lab.
Backed by a $32M Series A, we operate from dual headquarters in San Jose, California and Trondheim, Norway, with teammates across six countries. We work by four
values: Move Fast Together, Earn Customer Champions, Deliver with Excellence, and Challenge the Possible.
The role is a full time, month to month consultancy or internship focused on data collection.
Description of what we are looking for:
- Collect, organize, and preprocess large-scale robotics datasets.
- Clean, filter, and validate multimodal data (images, videos, robot trajectories).

- Analyze dataset quality, identify gaps, and improve data diversity and coverage.
