Overview
NRDC (the Natural Resources Defense Council) works to safeguard the Earth, its people, its plants and animals, and the natural systems on which all life
depends. NRDC combines the power of more than 3 million members and online activists with the expertise of some 800 policy advocates, lawyers, scientists, and
communication experts to confront the climate crisis, protect nature, ensure the rights of all people to clean air, clean water, and healthy communities. As an
international nonprofit environmental organization, NRDC is working to address the world’s most urgent environmental challenges, and advance innovative,
science-based, and enforceable solutions.
Since our founding in 1970, NRDC has helped craft and deliver many of America’s foundational environmental laws, including the Clean Air Act, Clean Water Act
and Safe Drinking Water Act. Today, we continue to lead in advancing and defending strong environmental protections at the local, state, federal and
international levels, as well as through the marketplace. Our team works across the United States and around the world, from offices in Chicago, Los Angeles,
New York City, San Francisco, and Washington, D.C., as well as in Beijing, and New Delhi (a subsidiary of NRDC India Pvt. Ltd.).
Supported by our members and online activists, NRDC is driving impactful solutions that confront the climate crisis, defend nature and safeguard public health
across Asia, Africa, and the Americas. At NRDC, we believe lasting change happens when people bring their talent, passion, and expertise together for a common
purpose: to build a cleaner, healthier, and more resilient world. Learn more at nrdc.org.
Our interns play an active role on the front lines of the environmental movement. Over the course of an internship, NRDC interns work with lawyers, scientists,
or policy analysts in different program areas and amass experience that they could not have obtained anywhere else. Interns also have access to various events
to enhance their NRDC experience.
NRDC interns may support or work on:
- Research, data collection, and analysis
- Writing and developing materials for state and federal campaigns
- Supporting with designing equitable policy solutions
- Copywriting and editing social media posts and supporting on marketing campaigns
- Brainstorming opportunities for process improvements, new vendors, partnerships, and engagement
- Project management of department and team-wide initiatives

Responsibilities
POSITION SUMMARY
NRDC's Science Office is seeking a graduate intern to support the creation of an information/knowledge management system. This intern will report into the
Senior Director, Scientific Collaborations. This oppportunity is based out of our Washington, DC office and interns are expected to be onsite 3 days per week.
The Knowledge Management Intern will develop knowledge management systems around scientific understandings of environmental challenges and solutions. The intern
is expected to create a user-friendly system to maintain and update scientist-vetted talking points on various topics and develop guidance for interrogating
scientific literature using approved AI tools (e.g., Copilot). The intern may produce scientific literature syntheses, compilations of internal scientific
thinking and systems for vetting that thinking, agents for connecting AI search tools with existing tools, AI SOPS/prompts, and recommendations for
institutional incorporation of AI into workflow. This student should have strong credentials in 1) science and 2) information systems or knowledge management.
