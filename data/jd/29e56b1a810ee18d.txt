About the role
Join Roku as an Engineering Intern and help build the agentic AI powering America’s #1 TV streaming platform. Roku reaches more than 100 million households
worldwide, and we’re building production-grade AI agents across the products and platforms that serve them. We offer a paid 12-week internship, running from
June to early September 2027.
As an Engineering Intern, you’ll be assigned a scoped project with one of our engineering teams and matched to the team that best fits your skills and
interests. You’ll work on real problems, such as agents that investigate failures, automate workflows, review or generate code, or help people make complex
decisions. You’ll learn how to take an AI-powered idea from prototype to something people actually use, with a mentor alongside you and real ownership from day
one.
Our teams work across a variety of languages and technologies, from user interfaces and embedded software to platform services, automation and developer
tooling, and embrace AI-assisted development to boost productivity and quality.
What you’ll be doing
- Own a well-defined project that contributes to the success of your team, from defining the problem through to delivery.
- Build and test AI agents, copilots and automated workflows that perform meaningful, multi-step work.
- Connect agents to trusted data, services and internal tools, using approaches such as retrieval, tool calling and orchestration, and help define what an agent
may do on its own and when it should hand over to a person.
- Evaluate your work: measure whether it does the job well, check its reliability and failure modes, and keep improving it based on real feedback.
- Use AI coding tools every day to move faster, and validate everything they produce through testing, review and your own judgment.
- Write clean, well-tested, documented code, take part in code reviews, and collaborate with engineers, mentors and cross-functional partners.

How will I use AI at Roku?
At Roku, we don’t just use AI, we work with it. AI agents and smart tools help power drafts, analysis, and repetitive workflows, while our people bring
direction, judgment, and accountability. We’re looking for curious, adaptable builders who can show how they’ve used AI or automation to move faster, raise the
bar, and scale their impact.
We value your AI skills if you have built fluency across the agentic engineering toolchain — coding harnesses like Claude Code or Cursor, MCP servers, custom
skills, or agent frameworks. You know how to drive an agent, verify its output, and ramp on an unfamiliar codebase with an agent helping you.
What's Roku's approach to hybrid working?
Roku fosters an inclusive and collaborative environment where teams generally work in the office Monday through Thursday. Fridays are generally flexible for
remote work, except for employees whose specific roles or assigned office location require five days' a week attendance.
