What You’ll Learn
- Project Overview: Modern Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) LLMs spend most of their serving cost in the decode phase, where attention is bound by KV-cache memory
bandwidth while expert FFNs need dense compute. You will investigate Attention–FFN Disaggregation (AFD) — running attention and MoE experts on separately scaled
resources and pipelining micro-batches between them — on NUMA systems, where memory locality and interconnect topology decide whether disaggregation pays off.
You will characterize real workloads, build a performance model, prototype NUMA-aware placement and scheduling in an open-source serving framework, and turn the
results into hardware–software co-design recommendations for memory and interconnect.
- Skills You’ll Learn:
- Performance characterization and modeling of LLM inference (roofline, trace-driven simulation) on multi-socket / multi-GPU NUMA systems
- Disaggregated serving system design: prefill–decode and attention–FFN disaggregation, MoE expert parallelism, and communication–computation overlap
- Hardware–software co-design: translating workload behavior into memory-hierarchy (HBM, CXL) and interconnect architecture requirements
What You’ll Do
The AGI team at Samsung Semiconductor explores system architectures for next-generation AI infrastructure. We co-design memory, interconnect, and software to
make large-scale LLM inference faster and more cost-efficient, and our work informs Samsung’s future memory and system products. As an intern, you will own a
research-driven project end to end — from profiling and modeling to prototyping — working closely with architects and systems engineers, and you will present
your findings to the broader organization at the end of the summer.
Location: Daily onsite presence at our San Jose headquarters in alignment with our Flexible Work policy
Reports to: Director
- Characterize decode-phase behavior of state-of-the-art MoE LLMs (e.g., DeepSeek-V3/R1, Qwen3-MoE class models) on the team’s NUMA testbed, using profilers
such as Nsight Systems, PyTorch Profiler, and Linux perf.
- Build and validate an analytical or trace-driven performance model of Attention–FFN Disaggregation, capturing attention:expert ratios, micro-batch pipelining,
all-to-all communication, and memory placement.
- Design and prototype NUMA-aware placement and scheduling of attention (KV-cache) and expert workers in an open-source serving framework such as vLLM or
SGLang.
- Implement and evaluate communication–computation overlap techniques (e.g., ping-pong micro-batching, expert-parallel dispatch/combine) and measure their
impact on throughput and latency SLOs.
- Explore the hardware design space — memory bandwidth and capacity, HBM vs. CXL-attached memory tiers, and interconnect bandwidth/latency — and quantify which
knobs matter most for disaggregated MoE inference.
- Collaborate weekly with your mentor and with architecture and systems engineers, sharing results in team meetings and incorporating feedback into the next
iteration.
- Communicate your findings through a final presentation and technical report, contributing to Samsung’s AI system architecture roadmap and potential
publications or patents.
- Complete other responsibilities as assigned.
