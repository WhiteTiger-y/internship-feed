functional teams to conceptualize and develop real-time high-performance software to manipulate real-world objects with high accuracy. Apply best practices to
the design of data structures and algorithms. Strive for both simplicity and performance. Produce high-quality software contributing to projects that have a
significant impact on the business. Simulate robot motion using internal simulation tools during feature development. Develop motion planning algorithms and
state machines for robotic manipulators. Design, develop, and implement control software for specialized end-of-arm tools and grippers. Define metrics for pick
and place quality in the field with different sensors and cameras. Work broadly across teams to facilitate integration of specialized manipulation systems from
hardware to software. Optimize processes using data-driven and machine learning, with ownership from data curation and training to inference. Support system
testing of systems and subsystems. Provide technical support for hardware design as needed. Troubleshoot system problems in production or design by
investigating issues related to product performance or production difficulties. Evaluate and process engineering change requests What you’ll need Pursuing a
Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Robotics, Engineering or related field. Experience with general-purpose programming environments, languages
and scripting systems, data analysis packages: Linux, C++, Python, Matlab. Knowledge of Git, Jira, and Confluence or similar tools. Familiarity with DevOps or
CI/CD – GitHub automation and docker or Jenkins. Experience with simulation tools such as MuJoCo, NVIDIA IsaacSim, PyBullet. Familiarity with Forward Kinematics
and Inverse Kinematics for custom kinematic chains. Strong Linear Algebra and 3D Geometry fundamentals. Familiarity with Machine Learning Fundamentals and
Libraries Experience with libraries such as OMPL, OpenRAVE, NVIDIA cuRobo, OpenCV, PCL, ROS, Boost, Eigen. Hands on experience with embedded systems and
platforms like Arduino, STM32, ESP32 or Raspberry Pi. Integrating sensors, motors and actuators using CAN, SPI, I2C. Preferred experience with either of the
