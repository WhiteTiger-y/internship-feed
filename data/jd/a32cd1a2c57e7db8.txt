Role overview
CarGurus Finance is seeking a curious, analytical student to join us as a Finance AI Solutions Intern. You will help Finance teams identify repetitive, data-
intensive work and pilot responsible AI and automation solutions that improve speed, accuracy, and insight.
You will own a primary, high-impact project with a Finance partner while gaining exposure to Accounting, FP&A, Tax, Finance Operations, and Procurement. Along
the way, you will learn how a public-company Finance organization evaluates new technology: not only whether it works, but whether it is accurate, reviewable,
secure, and scalable.
This is an opportunity to build a portfolio of practical work at the intersection of finance, data, process improvement, and AI, while receiving mentorship from
Finance leaders.
As a Finance AI Solutions Intern, you will join a 11-week Summer Internship Program designed to give you hands-on experience, expand your professional network,
and build core industry skills. This full-time opportunity (40 hours per week) runs from June through August 2027. CarGurus operates on a hybrid working model,
and candidates should expect to work out of our Boston, MA office 60% of the time (approximately three days per week).
What you'll do
- Partner with a Finance team to map a manual or data-intensive workflow and identify opportunities to improve it through AI, automation, or better analytics.
- Build and test a scoped prototype using various LLMs and approved tools such as Excel or Google Sheets, SQL, or workflow-automation platforms.
- Conduct research and analysis to support use cases such as reconciliations, exception review, variance analysis, vendor and spend analysis, contract or
invoice data extraction, and finance-operations inquiries.
- Validate outputs with process owners; document assumptions, review steps, limitations, and measurable impact.
- Support Finance and Internal Controls stakeholders in documenting appropriate guardrails for selected AI-assisted workflows, including human review, evidence
retention, and audit trails.
- Work with partners across Finance and, as needed, teams such as Sales Operations, Collections, and Finance Systems to understand data flows and improve
processes responsibly.
- Present your project outcomes, recommendations, and measured business impact to Finance leadership at the end of the internship.
What you'll bring
- You are currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Analytics, Economics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related
field, and are eligible to complete a full-time summer internship.
- You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills and enjoy understanding how processes work before improving them.
- You are proficient in Excel or Google Sheets and comfortable working with structured data.
- You are curious about AI and automation and interested in applying them responsibly in a business setting.
- You communicate clearly in writing and in conversation, and can explain an analysis or recommendation to both technical and business audiences.
- You are organized, detail-oriented, self-directed, and comfortable working through ambiguity with guidance from experienced teammates.
- You have an interest in finance, accounting, operations, or how businesses use data to make decisions.
Helpful, but not required
- Coursework, academic projects, campus employment, or prior internship experience involving data analysis, prompt design, or workflow automation.
- Experience using AI tools to research, analyze data, draft content, or streamline a recurring task.
- Familiarity with CRM, ERP, billing, BI, or financial systems such as Salesforce, NetSuite, Zuora, Looker, or similar platforms.
- Exposure to internal controls, audit, risk, or compliance concepts.
