Position Overview:
Siemens Digital Industries Software is seeking an AI & Engineering Data Intern to support emerging Artificial Intelligence and Digital Thread initiatives within
the Capital PreSales organization. This role provides hands-on experience applying AI technologies to engineering challenges across aerospace, defense, and
automotive industries.
The intern will help create and manage engineering datasets, investigate AI-assisted workflows, and support projects that demonstrate how Digital Threads enable
intelligent decision-making throughout the product lifecycle. The role combines engineering, software, data management, and AI technologies to develop
innovative customer-facing demonstrations and internal capabilities.
Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and curate engineering datasets for AI, analytics, and demonstration use cases.
- Support AI and generative AI proof-of-concepts involving engineering and Digital Thread data.
- Assist in connecting systems, software, and electrical engineering information into reusable data models.
- Evaluate and document AI-assisted engineering workflows and use cases.
- Build dashboards, reports, and visualizations that communicate engineering insights.
- Support technical enablement activities and customer demonstration development.
Requirements:
- Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Comfortable working in a fully remote environment within the United States of America.
- Intelligence, Software Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Experience with Python, data analytics, machine learning, or AI technologies.
- Familiarity with digital engineering, PLM, or engineering data management concepts is a plus.
- Strong communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills.
- Passion for applying emerging technologies to real-world engineering challenges.
