The Opportunity
If you are pursuing an advanced degree to bridge the gap between complex data and real-world financial markets, this internship will put you at the center of
that intersection. Operating within the Data Research & Development Pod, you won't be running basic reports—you will be prototyping next-generation data
products and leveraging agentic AI to drive measurable revenue. This role is designed to give you hands-on experience with modern cloud stacks and derivatives
markets, acting as an ideal launchpad for a career as a Quantitative Analyst, Data Scientist, or AI Product Lead.
Role & Project Scope
Responsibilities
As a Quantitative Data & AI Analytics Intern, you will take ownership of critical in-flight projects, working directly with high-margin data offerings. Your key
responsibilities will include:
- AI for Process & Revenue (Agentic AI): Utilize Gemini Enterprise for rapid visual prototyping of new data products. You will play a key role in continuing our
agentic AI building efforts—deploying AI not just to automate workflows, but to actively uncover and drive new revenue opportunities in the derivatives space.
- Data Product Commercialization: Build out streaming intraday (ITD) and end-of-day (EOD) data suites for volatility analytics, Greeks, and multi-legged option
valuations by assisting in the commercialization of our Theoretical Pricing System (TPS).
- Financial Data Migration & Mapping: Oversee the schema mapping, technical specification, and ingestion of critical historical financial datasets (like Spot
Dairy and Oil auctions dating back to 1997) into CME DataMine. Your work will directly support new commercial offerings and replace legacy pipelines.
- Predictive Modeling & Strategic Partnerships: Support data ingestion and mapping for our external predictive machine learning partnerships via DataMine
Machine Learning, calibrating proprietary datasets to build localized forward curves.
- Research & Publication: Gain visible industry experience by assisting in data extraction, narrative drafting, and quantitative analysis to support the ongoing
publication of our highly successful Volatility Monthly newsletter (e.g., "The Early Move and the Relentless Climb").
Qualifications & Requirements
Core Requirements:
- Education: Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Engineering, Computer Science, Quantitative Finance, Econometrics, or a
related field.
- Technical Stack: Hands-on familiarity with modern data stacks and AI tools, including GCP, Gemini Enterprise, BigQuery, SQL, Looker, and complex data mapping.
- Execution & Time Management: Demonstrable ability to balance multiple ongoing technical deliverables and push projects to completion in a fast-paced
environment.
Preferred / Helpful Qualifications
- Financial Knowledge: Familiarity with derivatives and interest rates (e.g., Treasury futures, yield curve dynamics, volatility smoothing).
- Project Management: Experience with Agile processes and Jira project management to track technical milestones.
