Reality Labs (RL) teams is looking for exceptional interns to help us create next generation interaction technologies for virtual and augmented reality. Join a
team working on computer vision and machine learning to improve robust prediction from visual data. The primary objective is to reduce hard-case and worst-user
tail errors without sacrificing accuracy for well-served cases.The project investigates how pretrained vision models preserve geometric information across
camera, illumination and appearance shifts. Example applications include eye and hand pose estimation for wearable, augmented-reality and virtual-reality
systems, using real and synthetic data. We welcome students with a passion for working in a strongly collaborative team. Our internships are twelve (12) to
twenty-four (24) weeks long and we have various start dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, Machine Learning for Robust 3D Perception (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Design and execute novel computer vision and machine learning algorithms for robust 3D perception, including geometry-aware VFM adaptations for continuous 3D
regression with spatial feature readouts, task-specific adapters and geometry-informed prediction.
- Conduct in-depth literature reviews and build knowledge of the latest advancements in vision foundation models and robust 3D perception.
- Integrate existing synthetic and real data, using model-failure feedback to prioritize or request hard cases through established pipelines, and validate
geometry, preprocessing and ground-truth / pseudo-label quality
- investigate how synthetic geometry supervision and validated camera information can improve representation quality and transfer across imaging conditions.
- Design controlled experiments comparing strong appearance-based and pretrained-model baselines
- evaluate accuracy, robustness and generalization on pose-estimation or other continuous-prediction tasks across held-out users, devices and difficult physical
configurations.
- Analyze model performance, including hard-case and worst-user errors, label noise and uncertainty, and iterate on approaches
- use existing teacher–student workflows to test whether tail gains survive adaptation and distillation while preserving well-served-case accuracy.
- Experiment with cutting-edge machine learning models and frameworks, including image/video foundation-model pretraining or adaptation using predictive and
self-supervised learning, with task-relevant supervision or auxiliary regression objectives, where aligned data and compute permit.
- Collaborate with researchers and engineers, document and share reproducible research findings, contribute to technical reports, and potentially publish in
academic or industry conferences/journals.
- We are accepting applications from those interested in (but not limited to) the following areas:.
- Vision foundation models (VFMs): geometry-aware adaptation of pretrained vision models for continuous 3D regression.
- Synthetic–real learning: real and synthetic data provide the testbed, and model-failure feedback guides training-data selection and adaptation.
- Noisy-supervision-aware training: learning with noisy ground truth or pseudo-labels, including uncertainty-aware prediction.
- Label-efficient learning: learning from limited labels, such as through self-supervised learning or pseudo-labels.
- Teacher–student distillation: carrying hard-case and worst-user accuracy gains over to lightweight models.
