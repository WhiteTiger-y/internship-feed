Artificial Intelligence Engineer Intern (Intern-to-Hire)
Location / Workplace Type: Hybrid — Houston, TX / San Francisco Bay Area / Atlanta, GA (Remote flexibility for standout candidates)
Aligned Recruitment is recruiting on behalf of our client, Searce (https://www.searce.com), a process-first, AI-native technology consultancy that builds cloud,
data, and applied AI solutions for enterprise clients.
ABOUT THE ROLE
Searce is hiring a cohort of paid interns for a 6-month Intern-to-Hire program (40 hours/week) with a clear pathway to a full-time Associate Engineer role for
strong performers. You will work on real enterprise client problems—primarily in AI engineering, with exposure to data and cloud engineering teams. This is a
hands-on role for builders who learn fast and take ownership of outcomes.
Recent Bachelor's graduates and graduating Master's students from Georgia Tech are strongly encouraged to apply.
WHAT THE 6 MONTHS LOOK LIKE
- Month 1: Rapid learning. Get up to speed on Searce’s engineering methods, tools, and cloud/AI platforms.
- Months 2–3: Proof-of-concept builds and certifications (Google Cloud, AWS, Anthropic Claude, and OpenAI paths).
- Months 4–6: Implementation work on live enterprise client projects, with earlier client exposure for high performers.
- You will be paired with a dedicated mentor/manager and engineering buddy.
WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Build, test, and deploy AI, cloud, and data solutions alongside your project team
- Complete assigned cloud and AI certifications and learning paths
- Develop rapid proof-of-concepts and turn them into production-ready solutions
- Communicate technical decisions and business value clearly to stakeholders and clients
- Take ownership of deliverables and share proactive progress updates
WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR
Education & Background: Pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Engineering, Data Science, or a related
technical field (Georgia Tech and other top CS/engineering programs strongly encouraged):
- Master’s Students: Graduating on or before May 2027 (able to work 40 hours/week during your final semester)
- Recent Graduates (Master’s or Bachelor’s): Graduated within the past 1–1.5 years (e.g., Dec 2025 – Dec 2026)
- Bachelor’s Graduates: Graduating in December 2026 (or recent grads) with prior internship, co-op, or applied research experience
- PhD Candidates: Open to candidates with 1–2+ years of prior industry experience
- Strong programming and computer science fundamentals with a short learning curve
- Hands-on exposure to Python, AI/LLMs (RAG, agents, prompt/eval pipelines), application development, and cloud platforms (Google Cloud preferred, AWS welcome)
- Clear, energetic communicator with the maturity and confidence to work directly with clients
- Self-starter who thrives with autonomy, ownership, and adaptability
