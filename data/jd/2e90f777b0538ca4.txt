Our 10-week internship is your chance to experience life as a Deep Learning Researcher at IMC. You'll have access to rich, large-scale datasets and modern
training infrastructure, and see firsthand how rigorous research translates into real-world results. No prior financial industry experience is required; we are
looking for scientific thinkers who are passionate about advancing deep learning research. We provide a highly competitive compensation package with
accommodations included. The bar for talent at IMC is high, and interns who meet our performance expectations will have the opportunity to secure a full-time
Graduate Deep Learning Researcher position at the end of the program. Where you go from here is up to you!
YOUR CORE RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Team up with Deep Learning Researchers on real projects to build or improve models that have potential to impact our business
- Explore research areas such as representation learning, transformers, foundation models, generative AI, and reinforcement learning and other emerging deep
learning techniques
- Develop your research skills with support and feedback from a dedicated mentor and intern lead
- Engage in professional development sessions aimed at helping you envision your future at IMC
- Attend events and social activities to get to know your cohort and current employees
YOUR SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:
- Current university student pursuing a PhD, Master’s or Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics,
Statistics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field, and graduating between September 2027 and July 2028
- Experience in applying deep learning techniques
- Publication records at leading ML venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR) or contributions to open source research a plus
- Strong programming skills in Python and hands-on experience with at least one modern deep learning framework, such as PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow
- A creative problem-solver who is driven, resilient, and able to explain complex ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences
- Must be able to start the internship in person on June 7, 2027
