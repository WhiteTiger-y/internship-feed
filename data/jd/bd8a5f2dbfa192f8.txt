The Opportunity: Data Science & Applied AI 2027 Intern
ICONIQ Capital is seeking a talented undergraduate to intern this summer within the Data & Analytics team, including its Data & Business Intelligence group. The
team sets and executes ICONIQ’s data strategy and maintains firmwide reporting that helps leaders make key decisions.
The Data Science & Applied AI Intern will scope, build, and demonstrate a working prototype of an AI application or machine learning project that solves a real
problem for internal users. The intern will work across ICONIQ’s modern data and analytics stack, including Snowflake, Tableau, Python, and related AI tooling,
from discovery through deployment. The intern will take responsibility for defined projects and collaborate with experienced Data Science and Applied AI
professionals.
Principal Responsibilities:
- Problem Definition: Partner with business stakeholders to define the problem, success metrics, and evaluation criteria for your prototype.
- AI Application Prototyping: Design and build an end-to-end AI application prototype, such as a RAG or chat workflow, summarization, or task automation, using
Python and modern AI tooling.
- Machine Learning & LLMs: Where appropriate, develop, train, and validate ML models (for example regression, classification, gradient-boosted trees, or time-
series) and/or fine-tune LLMs, engineering features, running experiments, and documenting results.
- Data Pipelines: Support the design, build, and optimization of data pipelines that move data reliably across systems.
- Prototype Demonstration: Demonstrate the working prototype to stakeholders and internal users.
- Product Lifecycle: Contribute across the product lifecycle, from use-case discovery to deployment.
Qualifications:
- Undergraduate student pursuing a bachelor’s degree with working proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Experience building analytical workflows, prototypes, or applications through class projects, research, hackathons, or internships.
- Familiarity with machine learning or LLM concepts, such as prompting, embeddings, vector search, and evaluation.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a product mindset and intellectual curiosity.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to collaborate with non-technical partners.
- Organized and detail oriented, with a self-starter’s bias to ship.
- Nice to have: experience with Tableau or similar BI tools, and exposure to frameworks such as LangChain, OpenAI or Anthropic SDKs, or vector databases.
Location: San Francisco
