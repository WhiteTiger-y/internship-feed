As an Artificial Intelligence Intern, you will help build, fine-tune, and evaluate generative AI models, machine learning pipelines, and intelligent system
applications across our enterprise. Working alongside AI researchers and engineers, you will process data streams, refine prompt architectures, and deploy
intelligent agents. You will solve real AI challenges, including model accuracy, responsible AI guardrails, and automated reasoning, seeing your models
transform operational workflows. Successful candidates will experiment with novel AI features and continuous process improvements.
What Success Looks Like (Objectives)
Our 10-week summer internship offers hands-on, on-site experience in the Greater Denver Area (unless otherwise noted per posting). As part of the cohort, you
won't just shadow, you will take direct ownership of a real-world business project, solving a critical operational challenge or implementing an innovative
solution for EchoStar. Through this program, 1:1 mentorship, practical skill building on live enterprise projects, and consideration for future full-time early
career pathways.
- Get oriented, meet your team, and scope your summer project with your manager and mentor
- Partner with AI engineers to develop, fine-tune, and benchmark large language models (LLMs) and custom AI prototypes
- Assist in processing, structuring, and prompt-engineering data streams for machine learning and AI agent applications
- Evaluate AI model outputs for performance, factual accuracy, and responsible AI guardrails
- Collaborate on integrating intelligent AI features into software, customer care, and business operational workflows
- Leverage modern generative AI frameworks, Python ML libraries, and automated testing tools
- Present your project and results to peers and leadership
Core Skills, Competencies, and Key Experiences (What you’ll bring)
- AI & Machine Learning: Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, LLMs, Prompt Engineering, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision
- Data & Cloud AI: Vector Databases (Pinecone/Chroma), LangChain/LlamaIndex, AWS Bedrock/SageMaker, API Integration
- Display a strong continuous-learning mindset, experimenting with novel AI features and emerging tech solutions for continuous process improvement
- Demonstrate strong critical thinking skills
- Exhibit clear written/verbal communication
- Deliver results with full accountability
- Collaborate effectively with cross-functional partners
Optional Exposure a Plus But Not Required
- RAG, vector databases
- REST APIs/FastAPI, cloud platforms like AWS, Git, or SQL
Minimum Requirements
- Minimum Education: Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Machine Learning,
Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical field
- Minimum 3.3 GPA
- Expected graduation between December 2027 and May 2028
- Minimum Experience: Foundational experience in software development, machine learning, data engineering, or AI projects through coursework, research, co-ops,
or internships
