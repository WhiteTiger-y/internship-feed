What You Will Be Doing
We are seeking a Thermal Engineering Intern to support the development and evaluation of thermal management solutions for next-generation AI and high-
performance computing (HPC) systems. As part of the AI Infrastructure Engineering team, you will work alongside experienced thermal, mechanical, platform, and
data center engineers to analyze cooling technologies, perform thermal modeling, support laboratory testing, and contribute to the design of high-density
compute environments.
- We will involve you in evaluating cooling solutions for AI and HPC systems, including air-cooled and liquid-cooled platforms.
- You will assist with thermal and airflow simulations using engineering analysis tools to study temperature, airflow, coolant flow paths, pressure drops, and
cooling performance.
- Your responsibilities will include supporting laboratory testing activities, such as instrumentation setup, sensor deployment, thermal data collection, and
data analysis.
- You will help compare test and simulation results against design expectations, identify trends, and support troubleshooting for thermal performance issues.
- We will involve you in design reviews and technical discussions while you assist in developing engineering reports, presentations, and technical
documentation.
- You will collaborate with cross-functional teams, including mechanical, electrical, system, validation, and data center engineers, and conduct technology
assessments on emerging cooling approaches for AI infrastructure.
Who We Are Looking For
- You are currently pursuing a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering or a closely related discipline, with an expected graduation date within 12 to 24
months of the internship start date.
- You have a strong academic background in heat transfer, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, numerical methods, or engineering analysis.
- You understand fundamental thermal engineering principles and are interested in applying them to electronics cooling, AI infrastructure, and large-scale
computing environments.
- You have experience using MATLAB, Python, or similar tools for technical analysis, data processing, scripting, or data visualization.
- You have exposure to thermal or CFD simulation tools such as ANSYS Icepak, Fluent, FloTHERM, COMSOL, OpenFOAM, or similar modeling software.
- You are familiar with electronics cooling concepts such as heat sinks, thermal interface materials, fans and blowers, cold plates, and heat exchangers.
- Preferred experience includes laboratory testing, instrumentation, data acquisition, academic research, course projects, or prior internships involving
electronics cooling, thermal-fluid systems, HPC, data centers, or energy systems.
- Nice to have: exposure to AI, HPC, server, or data center technologies, as well as sustainability topics such as energy efficiency, PUE, water usage
effectiveness, or advanced cooling technologies.
