As the Intern, Baseball Systems & Analytics, you will report to the Coordinator of Systems and Analytics. This internship at USA Baseball is an essential role
within the Technology and Sports Performance Department, supporting both the Prospect Development Pipeline (PDP) and USA Baseball’s development and national
team programs. This position will assist with organizing and analyzing athlete data collected through advanced technologies to support player identification,
development, and performance initiatives.
Additionally, The Baseball Systems & Analytics intern will support a range of USA Baseball player identification and development programs and initiatives. USA
Baseball’s National Training Complex (NTC) in Cary, N.C. is equipped with the latest TrackMan V3 radar, AWRE Cameras and integrated data collection systems. The
TrackMan radar system allows USA Baseball to enhance its evaluation and selection processes as well as assist in player development and training by adding in
individual pitch and hit tracking data. The Baseball Systems and Analytics Intern will work with both large and small datasets to verify dataset accuracy, build
player reports, identify performance trends, assist with player evaluation and selection, and develop tools that enhance USA Baseball’s understanding and
application of athlete performance data. The Baseball Systems and Analytics Intern will also help to oversee and manage the function of the NTC’s TrackMan and
AWRE systems during events, and aid in the supervision of the TrackMan and AWRE Operators assigned to each field. Additionally, this position may be asked to
support the operations of the PDP, such as assisting with data collection or aiding in the Player Reporting process.
Through this single application, USA Baseball will hire two (2) Interns, Baseball Systems & Analytics.

In This Role
As an Intern, Baseball Systems and Analytics, you will gain hands-on experience in sports technology, data management, and performance analysis while supporting
USA Baseball’s Technology and Sports Performance Department. You may:
- Aggregate and analyze athlete performance data to support player evaluation and development efforts.
- Query databases, clean datasets, and generate insights to assist in athlete identification and program improvement.
- Develop dashboards and tools to monitor data quality and identify areas for improvement.
- Operate and manage advanced baseball technology systems including TrackMan and AWRE to support data collection, analysis, and player evaluation.
- Coordinate and manage scheduling of TrackMan and AWRE operators for events at the NTC.
- Communicate with athletes, families, and stakeholders to coordinate logistics and share reports.
- Attend PDP and USA Baseball events to oversee technology setup, manage data collection, and contribute to player development initiatives.
- Suggest and implement improvements for analytics workflows, reporting systems, and data visualization tools.
