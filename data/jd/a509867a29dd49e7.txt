Key Responsibilities
Data Science and AI interns at CCC may explore how machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, data analysis, and AI technologies can be used to address
real business challenges. Interns may work with structured or unstructured datasets, conduct exploratory analysis, develop and compare models, visualize
results, and communicate findings to technical and nontechnical audiences.
Potential project areas may include machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, data exploration, data visualization, data storytelling, ETL, model
development, and model deployment. CCC’s supporting internship description also references the use of vehicle claim images and other data to produce insights
for the auto insurance industry.
What a Data Science or AI Internship May Include
Depending On The Team, An Intern May
- Explore, clean, organize, and analyze data
- Conduct feasibility studies or experiments
- Build and evaluate machine learning or deep learning models
- Compare modeling approaches
- Generate predictions and evaluate results against project requirements
- Apply computer vision techniques to image-based data
- Create visualizations and communicate findings
- Support data pipelines or ETL processes
- Collaborate with data scientists, machine learning engineers, software engineers, and business partners
- Help translate analytical findings into understandable business insights
- Learn how models move from experimentation into scalable products
