worldwide across 190 countries. HP is about innovation, it’s who we are and what we have been doing since 1939, we keep reinventing ourselves. Together we
challenge convention, pushing each other to surprise the world. Strategy & Planning Business Analyst: Join us as a business analyst in our Strategy & Planning
team to jumpstart your career in a world leading industry and open doors to a successful and expansive future. HP attracts some of the greatest talent from
around the globe and we are currently looking for ambitious and diverse students to join our Internship Program to bring a new perspective to our remarkable
team. Requirements: This is a placement year opportunity which will take place for 13 months, from July 1st 2027, until July 31st 2028 Enrolled in a degree
programme that includes a placement year Able to live and work in the UK as a hybrid worker, with regular travel to the Reading Headquarters. What you will
achieve: As a business analyst intern, you will be responsible for bridging the gap between raw data and readable data, using your analytical skills to assess
sales information and present it back to sales managers, senior leadership teams, and the managing director. You will work closely with numerous teams, from
sales to marketing, based both locally and across the continent with many opportunities to expand your network globally. This role offers incredible opportunity
to learn and collaborate with a wide range of amazing people dedicated to delivering the best user experience. What you put in is what you will get out. Many
interns often return to HP after they have finished their studies, either in the same team or elsewhere across the company. The current CEO, Enrique Lores, was
once an intern. Start your journey with HP; the possibilities are endless. Responsibilities: Analyse market data and present results. Use data analysis tools to
build out product unit plans. Aid category managers and product management. Support finance management duties. Attend local trade shows and partner shows.
Assist End User Sales teams with analysed data. Support Partner Sales team as office-based support for online marketing, sales tools communications to partner
channel. What we are looking for: Someone with an eye for detail, strong organisation and time management skills. Problem solver who can think outside the box.
Analytical mindset who is eager to learn and take advantage of the opportunities HP has to offer. Preferred degrees, but not essential, include Business
Management, Economics, Finance, and Marketing related courses. Further Opportunities: Assist with charity fundraising events and Business Impact Networks.
Develop recruitment plan for your successor. HP Mentor Scheme Intern committee and Intern social events Additional Information: Throughout the program, your
manager will take vested interest in your development and dedicate time to set clear goals, providing you with feedback during your internship. You will also
