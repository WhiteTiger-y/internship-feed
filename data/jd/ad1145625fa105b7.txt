Machine Learning Co-Op
Title of Direct Supervisor: Quality Process Improvement Engineer
Co-op Duration: 2027; estimated to run from January 2027 - December 2027
Position Summary
The Machine Learning Co-Op supports the Quality Engineering team by contributing to machine learning initiatives that improve automated visual inspection,
defect detection, and manufacturing efficiency.
During the Co-Op's duration, the Co-op will assist with inspection data preparation, model training and validation, performance analysis, and optimization in
collaboration with Quality Engineering, Manufacturing, and technical teams. Project deliverables will include documented results, recommendations, and a final
presentation of measurable improvements.
Local Primary Duties
- Support machine learning (ML) initiatives focused on improving automated visual inspection, defect detection, and manufacturing efficiency.
- Assist in the development, testing, validation, and optimization of machine learning models used in manufacturing quality inspection systems.
- Collect, organize, label, and analyze manufacturing and inspection data to support model training, retraining, and performance evaluation.
- Collaborate with Quality Engineering, Manufacturing, and technical teams to troubleshoot model performance and implement improvements.
- Analyze model performance to identify opportunities to reduce false rejects, improve defect detection accuracy, and optimize inspection processes.
- Document project progress, analyze results, and present findings, recommendations, and measurable improvements.
- Perform other duties as assigned.
