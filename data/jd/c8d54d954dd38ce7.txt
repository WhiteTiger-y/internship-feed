Job Description
What you’ll do
This is a paid Summer Engineering Internship Program (mid May to early August). As an intern with our Austin Software group, you’ll work to assist in
development of our autonomous underwater vehicle development program doing software development, algorithm development, simulation and vehicle
operation/testing. Additional responsibilities may include user documentation, testing and requirements gathering .
Your job responsibilities and projects will be based upon your experience and educational background to provide real life application of your classroom
knowledge. You’ll work closely with a mentor to ensure your developmental goals are met and that you meet your performance expectations.
There is an opportunity for team building, meet and greet lunches with our executive team, facility tours, and off-site ventures. This is an exciting
opportunity to learn ‘real world’ experience and accelerate your career by working with a leader in the oil and gas, aerospace, and entertainment industries.
No relocation or housing assistance is being offered.
Location
- Role provides the opportunity to work in hybrid environment, working both virtually and in office when required.
Functions
RESPONSIBILITIES
Essential
- Carries out tasks assigned by Project Manager and the Software Development Manager. Tasks would generally be software development, algorithm development,
simulation, and documentation.
- Assist in preparing presentations, articles, and technical papers.
- Interface inside and outside a meeting setting with project team members and clients.
- Testing software remotely, in simulation and on the designated vehicle.
Required
QUALIFICATIONS
- PhD/Masters candidate at an accredited University, pursuing a degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Robotics, Mechanical Engineering or related
field
- Experience with Microsoft Office
- Experience with C++
- Foundational knowledge of robotics/control systems including coordinates frames, kinematics, state estimation, guidance/navigation/control, 3D path-planning,
obstacle-avoidance and SLAM
- Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without company sponsorship now or in the future.
DESIRED
- Ability to work in a high-performance team on projects with constantly shifting schedules
- Experience with image processing, computer vision, sensor fusion, point-cloud processing, or camera/sonar data
- Experience with simulation, integration testing, or field testing of robotic, marine, or autonomous vehicles
- Experience implementing or evaluating 3D path-planning, motion-planning, obstacle-avoidance, SLAM, or autonomous-vehicle algorithms

About Us
Oceaneering is a global provider of engineered services and products, primarily to the offshore energy industry. We develop products and services for use
throughout the lifecycle of an offshore oilfield, from drilling to decommissioning. We operate the world's premier fleet of work class ROVs. Additionally, we
are a leader in offshore oilfield maintenance services, umbilicals, subsea hardware, and tooling. We also use applied technology expertise to serve the defense,
material handling, aerospace, science, and renewable energy industries.
