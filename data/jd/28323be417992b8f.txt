Position Summary
Join Cell Therapy Research at our Seattle site for a 10-week, hands-on laboratory internship. Interns are matched to a project team based on skills and
interests, and work with a technical advisor to complete a defined project that supports the discovery and development of next-generation cell therapies for
patients.
Depending on placement, projects may focus on cell engineering and gene editing, in vitro functional characterization of engineered T cells, or early process
research. You will learn core laboratory techniques, work with experienced scientists, take part in team and departmental meetings, and gain insight into how
drug discovery and development works.
Key Responsibilities
- Learn and perform laboratory techniques required for your project, including mammalian cell culture and aseptic technique.
- Perform assays to assess cell phenotype, potency, and function (for example flow cytometry, cell sorting, cytotoxicity, and cytokine analysis), depending on
project.
- Design and execute cell culture experiments with supervision, working with scientists to understand experimental design, controls, and goals.
- Collect, organize, and analyze experimental data using statistical and visualization tools.
- Maintain accurate, timely documentation in an electronic lab notebook in line with good laboratory practice.
- Meet regularly with scientific advisors to review progress and communicate project status to the team.
- Present project findings to the team at the end of the program.
- Maintain a safe work environment in accordance with policies, procedures, and regulations.
Qualifications & Experience
- Currently enrolled in a bachelor's degree program in Biology (e.g., Immunology, Cell Biology), Chemistry, Biochemistry, Neuroscience, Bioengineering, or a
related discipline.
- Rising junior or senior, with at least two years of undergraduate study.
- Prior laboratory experience through coursework, an academic lab, research projects, or a previous internship.
- Must be able to work on-site in Seattle for the full 10-week program.
- All candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States at the time of hire and for the duration of their employment. Please note that visa
sponsorship, including for student or temporary work authorization programs, is not available for this role. Candidates must have work authorization that does
not require sponsorship now or in the future.
Core Skills
- Technical basic laboratory skills (e.g., pipetting, biosafety cabinet use); familiarity with sterile tissue culture technique (hands-on experience not
required).
- Analytical data analysis and interpretation; working knowledge of Excel and tools such as GraphPad Prism or FlowJo is helpful.
- Scientific ability to understand experimental design and controls.
- Communication clear written and verbal communication, including summarizing data and presenting to scientific audiences.
- Professional meticulous record keeping, time management, and collaboration in a team environment.
Preferred
- Hands-on experience with mammalian or primary cell culture, preferably immune cells.
- Exposure to immunology, gene editing or gene engineering, flow cytometry, or basic molecular biology methods (e.g., PCR, cloning, ELISA).
