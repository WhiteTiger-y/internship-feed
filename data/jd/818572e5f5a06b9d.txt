Overview
i3 is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Data Management Intern to support critical initiatives in defense data handling and analysis. This role offers
hands-on experience in managing Department of Defense (DoD) test data, facilitating the ingestion of mission-critical information for weapon system analysts,
digitizing a national archive of historical defense records, and contributing to the sustainment of the United States’ most elite missile defense systems.
As an intern, you will:
- Collaborate with experienced professionals in a secure environment, gaining valuable insights into data management practices within high-stakes national
security contexts.
- Receive mentorship from industry experts in data management and defense systems.
- Gain exposure to technologies supporting national missile defense initiatives.
- Receive Oopportunities for professional development, including training in secure data handling and potential contributions to real-world projects.
- Experience a collaborative, mission-driven work environment focused on innovation and excellence.
This internship is for the Summer 2027 timeframe and will be an onsite role in Huntsville, AL.
Responsibilities
- Assist in the ingestion, organization, and processing of DoD test data from various mission sources, ensuring accurate and timely delivery to weapon system
analysts for evaluation and decision-making.
- Support the digitization of a national archive, including scanning, cataloging, and metadata tagging of historical documents to enhance accessibility and
preservation.
- Contribute to data management tasks related to elite missile defense systems, such as data validation, quality assurance, and integration into analytical
workflows.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop and implement data protocols that comply with DoD security standards and regulations.
- Perform routine data audits, generate reports on data integrity, and identify opportunities for process improvements.
- Participate in team meetings and contribute to documentation of data management procedures.
- Other duties as assigned.
Qualifications
Required Qualifications and Skills
- Current enrollment in an undergraduate or graduate program in Data Science, Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field at the
time of the internship (Summer 2027) or the semester immediately following (Fall 2027).
- Basic understanding of data management principles, including data ingestion, storage, and retrieval processes.
- Familiarity with tools and technologies such as databases (e.g., SQL), data processing software (e.g., Python or R for scripting), and digitization platforms;
prior experience with DoD-related data systems is advantageous but not required.
Strong analytical skills with attention to detail and the ability to handle sensitive information responsibly.
- Excellent organizational and communication abilities, with proficiency in producing clear, professional documentation.
- Ability to obtain and maintain a Secret Security clearance. An Active Secret clearance is required prior to start
- U.S. Citizenship is required
- Willingness to undergo background checks as needed.
- Demonstrated interest in defense technologies, national security, or archival digitization projects.
Preferred Qualifications
- Knowledge of DoD data standards, compliance frameworks (e.g., NIST), or mission test data protocols.
- Prior internship or coursework in defense, aerospace, or government sectors.
