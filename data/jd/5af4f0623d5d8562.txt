Meta was built to help people connect and share, and our technologies play a critical role in how people around the world communicate, create, and experience
media. A research internship at Meta offers the opportunity to advance the state of the art while working on systems used across global products.Meta is seeking
Research Scientist Interns to join the Media Foundation Core Audio team. We develop audio technologies used across WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Facebook, and
Meta AI experiences, spanning real-time calling, conversational AI, voice and media messaging, Live, and video-on-demand.Interns will work on one focused
research problem at the intersection of machine learning, audio and speech signal processing, human perception, and efficient systems. Potential areas include
streaming speech and audio enhancement for calling and conversational AI; restoration and super-resolution for creator media and video; neural audio codecs and
compression for speech, music, and general audio; perceptual and context-aware quality metrics; multimodal evaluation; and efficient on-device or server
inference through model architecture, distillation, quantization, and runtime optimization. Projects will be selected based on the intern’s expertise and
current team priorities.The work combines research depth with real-world constraints such as latency, bitrate, compute, memory, power, robustness, and safe
fallback. Interns will collaborate with research scientists, software engineers, and product partners to define the problem, build and evaluate solutions, and
identify a credible path from research result to product impact.Our internships are twelve (12) to sixteen (16), or twenty-four (24) weeks long, with various
start dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, Audio AI (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Perform research to advance audio, speech, multimodal, and machine learning technologies.
- Design, train, and evaluate models for one or more audio applications, such as enhancement, restoration, compression, generation, or quality understanding.
- Build reproducible evaluation pipelines using objective metrics, human listening tests, robustness analysis, and system-level measurements.
- Analyze and improve model quality, efficiency, latency, scalability, and stability under practical device, network, and serving constraints.
- Collaborate with researchers, software engineers, and cross-functional partners
- communicate research plans, progress, tradeoffs, and results.
- Publish research results and contribute to research that can be applied to Meta product development.
