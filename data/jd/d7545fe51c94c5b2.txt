Company Information
PACCAR is a Fortune 500 company established in 1905. PACCAR Inc is recognized as a global leader in the commercial vehicle, financial, and customer service
fields with internationally recognized brands such as Kenworth, Peterbilt, and DAF trucks. PACCAR is a global technology leader in the design, manufacture and
customer support of high-quality light-, medium-, and heavy-duty trucks under the Kenworth, Peterbilt and DAF nameplates. PACCAR also designs and manufactures
advanced diesel engines, provides financial services and information technology, and distributes truck parts related to its principal business.
Kenworth Truck Company
Kenworth Truck Company, founded in 1923, is the manufacturer of The World’s Best® heavy and medium duty trucks. With an excellent heritage of quality,
innovation and technology, Kenworth has played an essential role in the development of trucks that are more fuel efficient, productive, and economical to
operate. Today, Kenworth is leading transportation innovation with the introduction of battery-electric vehicles, exploration of fuel cell electric powertrains,
and autonomous driving systems. Kenworth is committed to fostering an environment of diversity, equality and inclusivity in the workplace.
Requisition Summary
Kenworth Division of PACCAR is looking for highly motivated individuals interested in gaining professional experience at an industry-leading manufacturing
company to join our 2027 Internship Program in Kirkland, WA. This internship will support the Dealer Development team by assisting with dealer network
analytics, customer satisfaction initiatives, business reporting, and strategic projects. Duties will vary based on business needs and ongoing department
initiatives.
Internships are scheduled to begin May or June 2027
Job Functions / Responsibilities
- Support Dealer Development team members with data collection, reporting, and analysis activities
- Analyze dealer operational and financial performance data and identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement
- Assist with maintaining dealer databases and preparing monthly performance reports and scorecards
- Assist with administration of the PremierCare Gold (PCG) certification and Dealer Excellence (DEX) programs
- Create dashboards, maps, presentations, and other business intelligence tools to support strategic initiatives
- Utilize tools such as Excel, Tableau, Snowflake, Python, and other analytics platforms to support data-driven decision making
- Provide periodic updates on project assignments to department management
- Create a PowerPoint summary of internship accomplishments for presentation at the end of the summer
- Flexibility to address special projects as they arise
Skills / Competencies
- Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and PowerPoint
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Resourceful and a self-starter
- Strong professionalism
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
- Experience with Tableau, SQL, Python, Snowflake, or other analytics tools preferred
Education
Currently enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate program in Business, Data Analytics, Marketing, Economics, Information Systems, Supply Chain Management,
Finance, or a related field.
- Junior or senior standing preferred
Intern Benefits
As a U.S. PACCAR intern, you have a full range of benefit options, including:
- Competitive salary and 401k with up to a 5% company match
- Medical, dental, and vision plans for you and your family
- Flexible spending accounts (FSA) and health savings accounts (HSA)
- EAP services: including wellness plans, estate planning, financial counseling, and more
- Global Fortune 500 company with a wide array of growth, training, and development opportunities
- Work alongside experienced goal-oriented colleagues recognized as experts in their fields
Additional Job Board Information
At PACCAR, we value talent and promote growth and development. We carefully consider numerous compensation factors, including your education, training, or
experience. The salary range for internship positions is $25 an hour for undergraduate students and $30 an hour for graduate students. Additionally, this role
is eligible for a range of benefit options listed above.
