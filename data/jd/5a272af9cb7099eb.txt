Internship Overview
The Data Analyst Intern will support analysis of consumer purchasing behavior within our custom-product business sold through home improvement retail stores.
Internship Projects and Responsibilities:
- Use sales and order data to identify trends and patterns in product selection, price points, features, options, and consumer trade-up behavior
- Explore purchasing behavior variability across products, stores, markets, and time periods
- Evaluate impact of product, assortment, and pricing changes
- Work with business partners to translate findings into actionable insights and recommendations that support product and business decisions
- Hands on experience with data analysis, problem solving, data visualization, and communicating findings to business stakeholders
