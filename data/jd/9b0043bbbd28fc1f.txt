Description
We are seeking a highly motivated AI Engineering Intern to support the development of advanced analytics and machine learning solutions for semiconductor
manufacturing equipment. The intern will work closely with Manufacturing Engineers, Equipment Engineers, Industrial Engineers, and Operations teams to leverage
equipment data, fault logs, sensor information, and maintenance records to identify predictive and preventative maintenance opportunities within a high-volume
semiconductor fabrication environment.
This role offers a unique opportunity to apply Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Data Analytics techniques to real-world manufacturing
challenges, helping improve equipment uptime, yield, reliability, and operational efficiency.

Success Metrics
By the end of the internship, the candidate is expected to:
Develop a proof-of-concept predictive maintenance model.

Demonstrate measurable prediction accuracy for selected failure modes.
Deliver dashboards and actionable insights to engineering teams.
Present project outcomes and future recommendations to fab leadership.
Duration of Internship: May -August 2027
Responsibilities
Analyze large volumes of equipment and manufacturing data from semiconductor production tools.
Develop machine learning models to identify patterns that may indicate impending equipment failures or process degradation.
Create predictive maintenance algorithms using historical maintenance records, fault logs, alarms, sensor data, and process metrics.
Evaluate equipment health indicators and recommend preventative maintenance actions.
Develop dashboards and visualizations that communicate equipment performance and risk trends.
Collaborate with equipment engineers to validate model predictions against actual tool performance.
Investigate root causes of recurring equipment failures and identify opportunities for proactive intervention.
Assist in building automated monitoring systems for critical manufacturing assets.
Conduct statistical analysis of maintenance effectiveness and equipment reliability metrics.
Present findings and recommendations to engineering and operations leadership.
Desired Project Areas The intern may contribute to one or more of the following initiatives:
Predictive Maintenance
Forecast equipment failures before they occur.
Identify early warning indicators from sensor and equipment telemetry data.
Develop Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation models.
Fault Detection & Classification
Build anomaly detection models for equipment health monitoring.
Classify equipment alarms and fault signatures.
Detect abnormal operating conditions in real time.
Preventative Maintenance Optimization
Recommend maintenance intervals based on actual equipment condition.
Reduce unnecessary preventative maintenance activities.
Improve maintenance scheduling to minimize production impact.
Equipment Reliability Analytics
Analyze Mean Time Between Failure (MTBF).
Analyze Mean Time To Repair (MTTR).
Identify chronic tool reliability issues and improvement opportunities.
