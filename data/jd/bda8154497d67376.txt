POSITION SUMMARY:
At National Life Group, we are unlocking the power of data and AI to transform our business and enhance customer experience. Our AI Center of Excellence (AICoE)
is leading this effort through innovations in machine learning, advanced analytics, and generative AI.
As a Data Science Intern, you will contribute to projects within our AI CoE. You’ll gain hands-on experience applying computer science and data science
techniques to real business challenges. Projects may range from supporting the development of predictive models to testing and enhancing generative AI
solutions.
This is a unique opportunity to explore both traditional data science methods and emerging AI technologies while working in a collaborative, mission-driven
environment.
RESPONSIBILITIES:
In this role, you will collaborate with AI and data science practitioners, technical experts, and business partners. You will work primarily in National Life’s
Databricks environment to develop and evaluate data-driven solutions. Depending on project priorities, responsibilities may include:
- Prepare, explore, and validate datasets and features for life insurance and annuity use cases such as surrender propensity, premium persistency, and
policyholder behavioral analysis.
- Support the development and evaluation of statistical and machine-learning models, comparing results with established baselines and investigating errors and
limitations.
- Help design experiments and analyze business outcomes using appropriate comparisons, sound statistical methods, and clear measures of success.
- Develop reusable Python and SQL workflows, including validation checks and documentation that allow others to review and reproduce the work.
- Prototype and evaluate generative AI applications, assessing output quality, source support, failure modes, and practical business usefulness.
- Apply responsible AI and data-handling practices, including appropriate treatment of sensitive information and validation of AI-generated outputs.
- Communicate findings and recommendations to technical and business audiences.
QUALIFICATIONS:
- Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, data science, mathematics, statistics, or a related field.
- Strong programming skills in Python, with experience using data analysis or machine-learning libraries such as pandas and scikit-learn. Exposure to Apache
Spark, PyTorch, or TensorFlow is a plus.
- Working knowledge of SQL, including querying, joining, and aggregating data to prepare and validate datasets for analysis and modeling.
- Familiarity with foundational statistical and machine-learning concepts, including regression, classification, model evaluation, and hypothesis testing.
- Coursework or projects integrating generative AI APIs into software is a plus.
- Curiosity and eagerness to learn about the real-world application of both modern AI technologies and foundational statistical methods.
- Strong communication skills and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
