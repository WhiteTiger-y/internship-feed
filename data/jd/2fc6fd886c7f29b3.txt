What You'll Do
- Build and extend components of Addepar's core AI platform and tooling
- Work on AI features and reusable components that are leveraged across multiple product teams
- Prototype and experiment with emerging AI/ML techniques for fintech applications
- Collaborate with the Core AI team on projects with clear, measurable impact
- Learn and demonstrate AI engineering standard processes and principles
- Participate in code reviews, model evaluations, and team ceremonies
- Document your work including model architecture, evaluation results, and design decisions

Who You Are
- Currently enrolled in a university or other post-secondary program
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, or a related field (penultimate or final year ideal, though all
years are welcome to apply)
- Able to commit to the full 12-week program, June 7th, 2027 - August 27th, 2027
- Strong foundations in machine learning, natural language processing, or AI systems
- Experience with Python and ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or similar
- Familiarity with LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG architectures, or AI application development
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to learn in a fast-paced environment and explore novel problem spaces
- Solid understanding of software engineering principles and experience working in a team
- Passion for applying AI to real-world problems in financial technology
