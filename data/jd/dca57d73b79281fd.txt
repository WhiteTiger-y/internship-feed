Summer 2027 Intern – Product Data Engineering An internship with American Family Insurance is a great opportunity to build your skillset and explore your career
interests. Our summer student internship is a 10 to 14 week program that starts in mid to late May and ends in August. During your internship, you'll work on
impactful projects that make a difference at our company, with our customers, and in our communities. You'll also gain exposure to different areas of our
business and have numerous opportunities for networking and coaching. Interns may be eligible for a one-time hiring bonus to assist with housing costs depending
upon proximity to an AmFam office location. Compensation Minimum: $25.00 Compensation Maximum: $39.00 As a Product Data Engineering intern, you will work
alongside experienced engineers on our service-oriented data and AI platform, which supports teams across AmFam, including security and AI. You'll build a real
AI agent, put it into a real workflow, and then help improve the shared tools that make the next agent easier to build. You'll be paired with a mentor engineer
for the full internship and finish with working software and a final presentation of what you built. Responsibilities Develop an understanding of our business,
corporate culture and structure to support your work. Contribute creative ideas and strategic approaches in response to changing situations. Work with your
mentor and a partner team to understand a real business problem, and help turn it into a working AI solution by breaking it into small, clear tasks. Build and
deploy an AI agent on our platform: give it tools, manage its context, and handle the unglamorous parts like retries, timeouts, and unexpected model output.
Contribute to a shared platform capability, such as evaluation, observability, or internal developer tooling, alongside the engineers who own it. Follow team
standards for code quality, testing, code review, and security, and write tests for the code you ship. Help measure how well an existing AI solution works, for
example by turning real failures into test cases, and share what you find with the team. Take part in stand-ups, planning, and code reviews, ask questions
early, and keep your mentor up to date on progress and blockers. Add logging, metrics, and tracing so others can see what your agent did, what it cost, and
whether it is still healthy. Document what you built and hand it off so another engineer can pick it up, and present your work to the team at the midpoint and
end of the internship. Qualifications Must be in student status at an accredited 4-year college or university during the internship, graduating August 2027 or
later. Must be pursuing a Bachelors, Masters, or PhD. Major/program must align with internship role. Demonstratable hands-on project or internship experience
