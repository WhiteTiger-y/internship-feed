this role. The Summer 2027 Data Science Research & Development (DSRD) Graduate Internship offers an exciting opportunity for graduate students to apply advanced
data science, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and software engineering techniques to solve complex clinical and healthcare challenges. This
internship is designed for highly motivated individuals who are passionate about leveraging cutting-edge technology to improve healthcare outcomes, advance
medical research, and drive innovation across the healthcare industry. As a member of the Data Science Research & Development team, the intern will collaborate
with a multidisciplinary group of researchers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, and software developers to develop innovative solutions utilizing
clinical, medical, and healthcare insurance data. Interns will gain hands-on experience working on high-impact research initiatives that directly support
healthcare transformation through advanced analytics, predictive modeling, artificial intelligence, graph technologies, computer vision, and generative AI. The
internship is structured around four specialized research tracks, allowing participants to align their work with their technical background and research
interests: Knowledge Graphs: Design and optimize graph-based healthcare data models, develop GraphQL interfaces, and leverage Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and
GraphRAG architectures to enhance predictive analytics and knowledge discovery. Medical Imaging: Build image processing pipelines and develop computer vision
models for clinical image analysis, anatomical segmentation, and pathology classification. Predictive Modeling: Develop machine learning models focused on
healthcare risk prediction, disease progression, and patient outcome forecasting while ensuring model explainability and fairness. AI Applications: Design and
evaluate advanced AI agent frameworks, natural language processing solutions, prompt engineering strategies, and clinical information extraction workflows using
emerging generative AI technologies. Participants will contribute to innovative research projects, assist with the design and development of analytical
solutions, conduct model evaluation and experimentation, and communicate findings to technical and business audiences. Through mentorship, collaboration, and
applied research, interns will strengthen their technical expertise while gaining valuable exposure to healthcare data science, artificial intelligence, and
