About Our Summer Internship Program
Our Summer Internship Program is designed for students entering their final year of university who are eager to gain meaningful, real-world experience in a fast
paced, collaborative, and professional environment.
As a Summer Intern, you'll participate in a comprehensive onboarding experience led by our University Relations team to set you up for success from day one.
Throughout the program, you'll also have the opportunity to participate in weekly professional development sessions, networking events, social activities, and
other engaging experienced designed to support your personal and professional growth.
The program takes place from June – August 2027 (10-week program).
Your Role
The Center of Expertise (CoE) is a global team built to solve our customers' most complex technical challenges and turn what we learn into repeatable value for
the rest of the business. As our AI & Automation Engineering Intern, you'll spend the summer building. You'll prototype AI-assisted tools, agents, and
automations that help a small, fast-moving team scale its impact without scaling its headcount. This is a hands-on, build-focused role: what you create is meant
to still be running after your internship ends.
- Design, prototype, and test AI-driven tools, agents, and workflow automations that reduce manual effort across the team’s core processes
- Partner with team leads to identify repetitive, high-volume work and convert it into automated or AI-assisted solutions
- Build automation around internal feedback loops so information moves faster and more consistently between teams
- Evaluate AI platforms, frameworks, and tooling, and make practical recommendations on where they add real value
- Document and hand off what you build so the team can maintain, extend, and scale it after the program ends
- Present your work, results, and recommendations to CoE leadership at the close of the internship
Requirements
- Rising Junior or Senior pursuing a Bachelor’s degree or a graduate student in an accredited college or university, preferably in Computer Science, Software
Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical field
- Excellent written, verbal and communication skills
- Hands-on experience with at least one programming language preferred (Python preferred) through coursework, personal projects, or a prior internship
- Familiarity with AI and large language model concepts, and demonstrated curiosity for applying them to real problems — coursework, personal projects, and
hackathons all count
- Comfort working with APIs, scripting, and connecting systems together
- Proficiency in Windows and Microsoft Office
- Ability to work independently with limited direction in a fast-paced environment; must be a high-energy, motivated self-starter
