Position Overview
We are seeking technically capable students for a hands-on applied AI and business automation internship focused on improving real-world business operations
through artificial intelligence, software integration, data analysis, and workflow automation.
This is a practical, project-oriented opportunity for students who want to move beyond classroom learning and AI experimentation to build solutions that address
actual business challenges. Interns will work directly with employees and department leaders to identify repetitive tasks, streamline workflows, integrate AI
into existing business systems, and develop automation solutions that deliver measurable improvements in productivity, accuracy, turnaround time, and operating
costs. The internship will provide exposure to multiple areas of business operations, including customer service, dispatch and logistics, purchasing, sales,
laboratory and manufacturing operations, and accounting.
Interns will be expected to understand business problems, develop and test solutions, work with existing software and data, and demonstrate the results of their
work. The objective is to implement useful, reliable automations that employees can incorporate into their daily operations. The initial program will run for 12
weeks, with the possibility of extending the internship or transitioning successful candidates into permanent positions based on individual performance,
demonstrated business impact, and organizational needs.
Work Location and Arrangement
This internship is based in Houston, TX 77047 and requires on-site attendance. Remote and hybrid work arrangements are not available at this time.
