In this Role, You Will…
- Formulate and build statistical and machine learning models to evaluate system performance and analyze rare, high-consequence safety events.
- Process and analyze large-scale datasets using SQL, Python, or distributed data frameworks (Spark/Databricks) to extract actionable insights on vehicle
behavior and operational risk.
- Apply advanced statistical methods (e.g., Poisson processes, GLMs, Bayesian inference, point process models, or spatial-temporal modeling) to evaluate safety-
critical metrics.
- Collaborate with systems engineers and safety leads to translate complex quantitative models into rigorous mission assurance decisions.
