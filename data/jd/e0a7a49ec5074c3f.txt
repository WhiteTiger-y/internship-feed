The Summer 2027 Data Analyst enGen Undergraduate Internship provides students with an opportunity to gain practical, hands-on experience supporting enterprise
data platforms, data operations, analytics, and AI-driven initiatives within enGen. This internship is designed to develop the next generation of data and
technology professionals through direct exposure to modern DataOps practices, data engineering concepts, analytics platforms, and enterprise data management
solutions. As a member of the DataOps Platform team, the intern will work alongside data engineers, data analysts, platform administrators, architects, and
business stakeholders to support strategic data initiatives and enterprise business projects. Participants will gain valuable experience working with industry-
leading data technologies, including Atlan, Monte Carlo, Starburst, and dbt, while contributing to efforts that improve data accessibility, quality, governance,
observability, and business intelligence capabilities. The program provides hands-on training and exposure to a variety of career paths within Data Analytics,
Data Engineering, Data Operations, Artificial Intelligence, Information Management, and Business Intelligence. Interns will collaborate across multiple data
teams to support data-driven decision-making, improve operational processes, and help deliver innovative solutions that drive business value. Typical
responsibilities include analyzing data, developing SQL-based queries and reporting solutions, supporting data engineering and DataOps initiatives, assisting
with data quality and observability efforts, participating in AI-related projects, researching technical solutions, and collaborating with cross-functional
teams to support enterprise data platforms. Through mentorship, project-based learning, and real-world experience, participants will strengthen their
