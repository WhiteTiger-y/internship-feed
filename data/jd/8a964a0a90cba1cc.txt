Title
Defense & Intel (D&I) - Radar Research Internship
At KBR, we deliver science, technology and engineering solutions to governments and companies around the world. KBR employs approximately 37,000 people
worldwide with customers in more than 85 countries and operations in over 28 countries. KBR is proud to work with its customers across the globe to provide
technology, value-added services, and long-term operations and maintenance services to ensure consistent delivery with predictable results.
Trinzic is the future independent company to be created through the planned spin-off of KBR's Mission Technology Solutions business, expected in January 2027.
Our people work at the frontier of what is possible, delivering solutions in challenging environments and advancing some of the world's highest-priority
national security and space missions.
Backed by decades of mission success, deep expertise, and trusted partnerships, Trinzic will launch as a global organization with more than 18,000 employees
supporting customers around the world. Join us at a defining moment and help shape the future of a company built to tackle complex challenges and make a
meaningful impact.
Candidates who join KBR's Mission Technology Solutions business before the spin-off will transition to Trinzic when the separation is complete and will not need
to reapply for employment.
Project Overview
Contribute to the development of KBR's Research and Development capabilities supporting geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) including topics in Synthetic Aperture
Radar (SAR) and Moving Target Indication (MTI). Apply engineering and analytical skills to develop tools and algorithms for modeling, simulation, visualization,
and assessment of real-world scenarios. Apply modern AI techniques to SAR and RF.
Key Responsibilities
- Apply radar engineering and signal processing concepts to solve real-world problems.
- Develop Radar tools for modeling, simulation, visualization, and analysis.
- Explore the use of AI, machine learning, and generative AI techniques to enhance radar analytics and decision support.
- Collaborate with engineers and subject matter experts on research and development initiatives.
- Present technical findings and recommendations to project stakeholders, which may include KBR staff as well as Government customer staff.
Learning Outcomes & Impact
- Hands-on experience developing advanced geospatial intelligence and radar analytic capabilities.
- Exposure to real-world government mission challenges and engineering workflows.
- Mentorship from experienced Subject Matter Experts (SMEs).
- Opportunity to contribute directly to customer programs and KBR internal R&D initiatives and emerging technologies.
Minimum Qualifications
- Pursuing degree in engineering (electrical, computer or aerospace), computer science signal processing, or physics.
- Academic knowledge and experience with radar systems, digital signal processing, RF.
- Familiarity with one or more programming languages including MATLAB, Python, C++, or Java.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Self-starter capable of working independently and within a team environment.
Preferred Qualifications
- Strong coursework or research involving SAR, radar systems, or RF engineering.
- Pursuing Master’s degree or higher.
- Experience applying machine learning or AI techniques to technical problems.
- Familiarity with data analysis, modeling, and visualization tools.
