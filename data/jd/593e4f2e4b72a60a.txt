About the Company
DwyerOmega is a globally trusted leader in manufacturing innovative instrumentation solutions for the Process Measurement, Automation, Control and HVAC markets.
With very strong brand recognition and high levels of customer loyalty, DwyerOmega has an unmatched reputation for providing customized solutions that meet and
exceed customer needs. Our team of engineering experts help our customers select technical, and often configured, product solutions for their unique
applications.
What You'll Do
The Applied AI Intern will play a key role in the DwyerOmega AI team. The Applied AI Intern will engage in their teams to support the strategy, development and
deployment of AI-powered solutions Inside of DwyerOmega. This internship focuses on applied AI. Building practical tools, automating workflows, analyzing data
and prototyping AI-powered applications that drive business value. This is not a research or model-training role; the ideal candidate is a builder who can
translate business problems into working AI solutions using modern platforms and APIs.
Essential Job Functions:
- Build, Extend and maintain internal AI tools and automations.
- Support data analysis and consolidation efforts across multiple portfolio companies, helping to identify patterns, clean datasets and prepare data for AI-
driven insights.
- Prototype AI-powered proof-of-concept applications that address specific operational challenges within DwyerOmega’s business units.
- Design and refine prompts, system instrucitons and AI agent configurations to improve the quality and reliability of AI-generated outputs.
- Evaluate and recommend emerging AI tools and platforms
- Document technical approaches, architectural decisions and usage guides to support knowledge transfer and long-term maintainability.
Requirements
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering or a related technical field
- Proficiency in Javascript/Typescript and/or python; experience with web development frameworks (React, NodeJS) is a plus
- Hands-on experience with AI/LLM platforms and APIs (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Claude, Hugging Face or similar)
- Familiarity with prompt engineering techniques and/or building applications that integrate large language models.
- Strong problem-solving skills
