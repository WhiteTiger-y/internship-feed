Job Description:
Join Takeda’s Quantitative Clinical Pharmacology (QCP) team, collaborating with Drug Metabolism and Pharmacokinetics & Modeling (DMPK&M). This internship will
explore how data-driven methods and pharmacokinetic modeling can support scientific understanding and drug-development decisions.
We are seeking a motivated summer intern with an interest in applying artificial intelligence, machine learning, and quantitative methods to pharmacology
research. The intern will contribute to an exploratory project investigating how drug characteristics and patient-related factors influence drug exposure, with
a focus on impaired organ function.
Working with scientific mentors, the intern will help organize research data, explore predictive modeling approaches, and evaluate how data-driven methods can
complement established pharmacokinetic modeling. The project offers an opportunity to translate scientific questions into reproducible analyses and communicate
findings to a cross-functional audience.
How You Will Contribute:
- Review scientific literature and relevant public sources to identify data supporting the research objectives.
- Compile, clean, and document datasets containing drug properties and pharmacokinetic outcomes.
- Conduct exploratory analyses and create visualizations to identify patterns and assess data quality.
- Develop and evaluate statistical or machine-learning computational models with guidance from scientific mentors.
- Explore how predictive approaches can complement established pharmacokinetic modeling methods.
- Document methods, assumptions, and limitations, and present findings and recommendations to the team.
Internship Development Opportunities:
- Gain experience at the intersection of AI/ML, clinical pharmacology, and drug metabolism and pharmacokinetics.
- Strengthen skills in scientific data curation, reproducible analysis, model evaluation, and interpretation.
- Learn how quantitative approaches inform pharmaceutical research and development.
- Develop scientific communication and collaboration skills through mentorship and a final project presentation.
Job Requirements:
- Work arrangement :onsite/hybrid
- Office location: 500 East Kindall square, Cambridge, USA
- Currently enrolled in doctoral degree program:in pharmaceutical sciences, pharmacometrics, clinical pharmacology, computational biology, data science,
statistics, biomedical engineering, or a related discipline.
- Working knowledge of Python or R for data analysis and an understanding of statistical or machine-learning concepts.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills, with attention to data quality and documentation.
- Interest in pharmacokinetics, drug development, and the responsible application of AI/ML to scientific research.
- Familiarity with ADME concepts, pharmacokinetic modeling, or physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) modeling.
