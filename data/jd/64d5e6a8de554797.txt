The Opportunity
As a Risk & Reg - Technology & Analytics Senior Associate Intern you will support work across regulatory, risk, technology, and analytics topics within our
Advisory practice. As an Intern you will support teams, participate in projects, and contribute through research, basic analysis, and day-to-day tasks while
building exposure to PwC methods, tools, and client service practices. This role offers the chance to learn how data, analytics, and AI can be applied to help
organizations respond to risk and regulatory needs.
In this role at PwC, you will assist with gathering information, reviewing data, preparing materials, and contributing to project deliverables under guidance.
You will have opportunities to develop your communication, teamwork, and digital tool skills while taking ownership of your learning and adapting to different
assignments. The work may include supporting problem solving, documenting findings, and observing how professionals approach client issues in a fast-moving
environment.
Responsibilities
- Supporting client projects focused on risk, regulatory, and analytics workstreams
- Conducting research on regulatory requirements, control frameworks, and emerging technology topics
- Assisting with data preparation, analysis, and visualization using digital tools and AI-enabled workflows
- Contributing to development of reports, presentations, and other deliverables for client teams
- Documenting findings, tracking project tasks, and organizing supporting materials for review
- Applying analytical skills to identify patterns, summarize information, and flag issues for follow-up
- Participating in team discussions, asking questions, and learning from feedback on assigned work
- Collaborating in a professional setting while building knowledge of advisory and data-driven problem solving
What You Must Have
- Currently pursuing or have completed a Master's of Business Administration degree
- At least 3 years of experience
- Client service Senior Associate intern positions are for job seekers who are one year away from graduation of their MBA program at the time of application
What Sets You Apart
- Leveraging AI to create efficiencies, innovate ways of working and deliver distinctive outcomes
- Utilizing AI fluency to support data-driven client service tasks
- Applying digital tooling to streamline research and analysis activities
- Demonstrating curiosity in risk, regulatory, and analytics topics
- Supporting team projects through active listening and clear communication
- Adapting quickly to feedback, learning, and varied assignments
