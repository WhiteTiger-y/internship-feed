Responsibilities
Mortenson’s i4 department seeks an AI intern to join our team to help further the goals of the organization. This role will support the development of AI agents
and tools that improve how we work – from smarter communication, automation within construction solutions, and predictive insights. The AI Intern will
collaborate with the AI team and Data Solutions to prototype solutions, explore emerging technologies, and contribute to real-world applications of Artificial

Highlights of your time with this Mortenson internship will include:

- Document learning and contribute to internal knowledge sharing
- Effectively work and build relationships with those of diverse backgrounds and organizational levels
- All other duties as assigned
