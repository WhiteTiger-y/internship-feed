About the Role
We are seeking an AI Engineering intern who will work on real AI agentic automation problems, designing, prototyping, and developing AI-powered agents to help
the business and engineers achieve faster, smarter work. You will collaborate directly with senior engineers and architects, receiving mentorship and design
reviews, and gaining exposure to modern GenAI patterns, including agent orchestration, tool-calling, retrieval, and evaluation, within an enterprise and fast-
paced fintech environment.
AI tools change every quarter. Your project will use the best models, agent frameworks, and protocols available in summer 2027. You will work on current
practice, not today's stack.
Launch your career developing real, impactful AI solutions in the lightning-fast world of fintech!
Program Details
Our summer program will be 10-12 weeks from May - August 2027 with the expectation of being in the office 3-4 days per week, as determined by the sponsor.
Compensation will be set at an hourly rate of $35-40 per hour. Top performing associates may have the opportunity to be considered for full-time roles at
DriveWealth at the conclusion of the program, based on performance and team needs. Must have a graduation date in 2027 or 2028 .
What You’ll Do
Design, prototype, and develop agentic AI workflows that automate or augment business or engineering tasks (for example, analysis, triage, documentation, and
knowledge synthesis)
Build internal tools or proof-of-concept systems that combine LLMs, APIs, and automation frameworks into end-to-end agentic solutions
Experiment with different agent architectures (single-agent vs. multi-agent, planner/executor patterns, human-in-the-loop workflows) and document tradeoffs
Apply best practices when building AI-driven automation
Evaluate agent behavior for reliability, correctness, and failure modes, and propose improvements
Clearly document designs, experiments, and lessons learned so work can be reused or extended by the team
Demo your work directly to customers and the leadership team
