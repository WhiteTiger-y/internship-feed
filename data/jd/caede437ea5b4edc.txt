The Role
We are excited to offer an internship opportunity for a talented student to join the data science team at Schonfeld. The data science team is responsible for
creating Schonfeld's proprietary curated and feature datasets, conducting data analytics and research, and ultimately add value to various investment
strategies.
What You’ll Do
As a data scientist, you will work closely with the engineering teams to optimize data ingestion pipelines, ensuring high-quality data input. You will also
collaborate with portfolio manager teams to extract insights from data and systematically generate features or signals that feed into the alpha generation
process. Additionally, you will develop domain expertise across various financial asset classes and datasets, produce curated datasets to standardize research,
conduct quantitative analysis to generate ideas and validate hypothesis through backtesting. The ideal candidate will possess both a passion for data science
disciplines and a strong interest in financial markets.
What You’ll Bring
What you need:
- Knowledge of Python, SQL, or other programming languages
- Proficiency with statistical and machine learning packages for quantitative analysis
- Experience researching and analyzing financial datasets
- Attention to detail and strong communication skills
- Degree in quantitative field such as computer science, data science or financial engineering
We’d love if you had:
- Strong passion for financial markets and related datasets
- Knowledge of deep learning models
- Familiarity with AI tools to accelerate work, with a focus on adding analysis and insight
- Experience with any of the following technologies: Spark, Kafka, Docker, Airflow
- Knowledge of financial asset classes such as equity, futures, and fixed Income
Our Internship
Kick-start your career in finance with the Schonfeld Summer Internship! Over the course of 10 action-packed weeks, you will have the opportunity to build
solutions that tackle real-world challenges and leave a lasting impact on the firm. As an intern, you’ll join a close-knit team that is ready to support your
growth and accelerate your career. We will pair you with a dedicated manager and a mentor who’ll guide your development over the course of the internship and
beyond. Throughout the program, you will have the chance to connect with senior leaders through a series of learning sessions, attend hands-on skills workshops,
network at social events and get a behind-the-scenes look at how various functions power the firm. We’ll empower you to bring your ideas to life and make an
impact.
Who We Are
Schonfeld is a global multi-manager hedge fund that strives to deliver industry-leading risk-adjusted returns for our investors. We leverage both internal and
external portfolio manager teams around the world, seeking to capitalize on inefficiencies and opportunities within the markets. We draw from decades of
experience and a significant investment in proprietary technology, infrastructure and risk analytics to invest across four main strategies: Quant, Tactical,
Fundamental Equity and Discretionary Macro & Fixed Income.
