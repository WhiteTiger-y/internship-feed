What you’ll do
Looking for *that* internship? The game-changing one that’ll help you learn, grow, and chart your path forward? You’ll find it at SAS. Our interns aren’t coffee
runners – they do real, meaningful work. Our award-winning internship program is focused on development, culture, and community. We’ll help you grow

The IoT Analytics group at SAS is a part of SAS Global Engineering responsible for developing tools to analyze high frequency and high dimensional IoT data for
applications in the areas of dimension reduction, anomaly detection, recommendation systems, time frequency analysis, digital signal processing and digital
twins.
As an intern, you might:
- research streaming data analysis methodologies and investigate various algorithms suitable for analysis of sensor generated data
- perform hands on work with SAS’s tools to do statistical analysis of sensor generated data
- check the numerical correctness of the algorithms implemented and their comparison with the current state of the art.
