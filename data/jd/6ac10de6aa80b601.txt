What You Will Do
This position is for a Graduate Research Assistant (GRA) in the Space Remote Sensing and Data Science Group (ISR-6) at Los Alamos National Laboratory. You will
work closely with interdisciplinary teams on projects related to remote sensing, data science, image and signal processing, machine learning, and sensor data
analysis. Responsibilities will be tailored to your background and interests, with opportunities to contribute to innovative research and development.
As a Graduate Research Assistant, you will be involved in advancing methods and technologies for collecting, processing, analyzing, and interpreting data from
space, airborne, ground-based, and laboratory remote-sensing systems. Candidates should have broad technical skills and an interest in applying computational
and analytical techniques to scientific challenges.
In this role, you may contribute to one or more of the following areas:
- Developing and applying algorithms to process and analyze sensor data (including images, spectra, time series, or other types)
- Using statistical, mathematical, or computational methods to address detection and characterization problems
- Modeling sources, sensors, and measurement uncertainties
- Analyzing and interpreting experimental or field-collected data
- Contributing to the design or calibration of sensing systems and instruments
- Communicating research findings through reports, presentations, publications, and teamwork
Duration of this position is at least one year, with the intent to support continued multi-year work.
