Job Description Launch your career in advanced analytics with Vanguard’s College to Corporate (C2C) Internship Program. As a Data Scientist intern, you’ll help
apply advanced analytics, machine learning, and AI to solve business problems and support data-driven decision-making. This immersive internship combines real-
world project work, mentorship, and structured development to help you strengthen your technical depth, business acumen, and leadership capability in a
collaborative, purpose-driven environment. In this internship you will: Support advanced analytics work that helps teams identify patterns, predict outcomes,
and improve decision-making at scale. Prepare, transform, and analyze data to support machine learning, statistical modeling, and AI-driven solutions.
Contribute to model development, testing, validation, or experimentation efforts in partnership with data scientists and business stakeholders. Help communicate
analytical findings, implications, and recommendations in a way that supports business action. Gain exposure to applied AI and advanced analytics work, such as
automation, model operationalization, and insight generation. Build foundational leadership skills by taking ownership of work, collaborating across teams, and
contributing ideas in a fast-paced, learning-focused environment. Build relationships with program managers, alumni, senior leaders, and peers across the
program Receive coaching and mentorship from your department manager and program leaders to support your development. What it takes: Enrolled in a master’s
degree or PhD program — or advanced graduate study — in Data Science, Analytics, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Computer Science, or a related
quantitative field. Strong interest in advanced analytics, machine learning, AI, and using data to solve complex business problems. Experience through
coursework, research, internships, or projects using tools such as SQL, Python, statistical methods, machine learning libraries, or cloud-based analytics tools.
Demonstrated leadership skills and/or leadership aspirations. Strong analytical, communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills. Ability to work
independently and as part of a collaborative, team-oriented environment. Cumulative GPA of 3.2 or higher preferred. Additional Information: This program is a
full-time, paid 10-week summer internship Vanguard is not offering sponsorship for this position The start date for this program will be May/June 2027 Special
