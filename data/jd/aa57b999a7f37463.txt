About the team Zillow's Data Science and Analytics organization turns complex data into insights that inform decisions across the company. As an intern, you'll
join the team and explore how data can help us understand customer behavior, measure outcomes, and find ways to improve the customer experience. You'll work
with data scientists and cross-functional partners, building your analytical skills while bringing a fresh perspective to real business questions. Interns may
focus on areas such as: Business: build reporting, models, and insights that help specific Zillow organizations assess current initiatives and find
opportunities to innovate. Marketing: develop experimentation-first strategies for consumer and business marketing, and measure how each channel performs.
Product: analyze how people use our web and app products so we can deliver a better experience for everything home. Pricing: help develop pricing strategies
that deliver strong value for customers such as Premier Agents, lenders, and landlords. Tools: turn the large volume of data Zillow collects every day into data
products that help partners make informed decisions. About the role At Zillow, interns do meaningful work that contributes to the business from day one. As a
Data Science Intern, you’ll apply the quantitative and programming skills you’re building in school to a scoped, real-world project. With guidance from your
manager and team, you’ll work through the analytical process—from defining a question and preparing data to interpreting results and sharing recommendations.
Our nine-week summer internship program, running June 14 through August 13, 2027, gives students the opportunity to bring their ideas and perspectives to real
projects alongside Zillow teams. Interns build hands-on experience, connect with mentors and leaders, and take part in learning and community experiences with
their intern cohort. Our 2027 internships are based in Seattle and follow a hybrid schedule, with interns generally working together in person Tuesday through
Thursday. Eligible interns will receive a stipend to help offset summer housing and relocation costs. During your internship, you will have opportunities to:

Python/R to explore, prepare, and validate data, documenting assumptions and flagging data-quality issues. Analyze patterns in customer behavior or business
performance to uncover insights and potential opportunities. Apply statistical methods, such as regression, hypothesis testing, or experiment analysis
