Overview
Epsilon is seeking a PhD Data Science Intern to join our Decision Sciences Team. As a Data Science Intern, you will apply your academic research and machine
learning skills to propose effective solutions for our technology and business challenges. Your research background will determine your placement in one of our
six Decision Sciences teams, each enhancing various aspects of our personalization platform. Epsilon's personalization relies on analyzing vast amounts of
anonymized data online to assess a trillion advertising opportunities monthly in real-time .
This is a hybrid role out of the listed locations and may also be offered remotely.
Epsilon’s PhD Internship Program
As an intern, you’ll participate in a formal 12-week summer program developed to provide you with a comprehensive grasp of digital marketing and Epsilon’s
personalization platform. During your internship, you’ll undertake a research project addressing a specific technology or business challenge, allowing you to
apply your machine learning expertise practically. Lastly, you’ll re ceive mentorship and feedback to help you successfully p resent your findings and approach
to the entire Decision Sciences department .
Responsibilities
- Impact: Develop innovative solutions, with a high degree of autonomy, contributing to work that has the possibility of future integration into production
systems.
- Career Growth: Apply your field of study to solve real problems with an opportunity to directly impact our business, gaining hands-on experience with
internet-scale data and industry-level applications.
Qualifications
What you’ll bring with you:
- Currently enrolled in a PhD program with relevant coursework, research, and background in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or related
quantitative field
- Hands-on involvement with research projects involving formulating hypotheses, exploring data, and developing models for insights
- Established track record in developing and validating machine learning models, with a strong understanding of model performance evaluation and optimization
- Proficiency in one or more programming languages such as Python, Scala, SQL, or R
Why you might stand out from other talent :
- Experience working with big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, Cassandra, or MPP databases
- Proven capacity to communicate complex data science concepts, methodologies, and findings to diverse audiences
- Ability to conduct independent research to identify and recommend the best approaches for solving complex technology and business problems
