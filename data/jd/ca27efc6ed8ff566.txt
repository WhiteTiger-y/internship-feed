Overview
Milliman’s Indianapolis Health practice is seeking a curious and motivated early-career Data Scientist to join our growing practice through a 12-week intern-to-
hire program. This role is focused on applied data science and analytics in healthcare, supporting the enhancement of existing analytical products and the
development of new AI and ML solutions under the mentorship of experienced data scientists and consultants. The program is designed for candidates who are newer
in their careers and show strong potential, giving them the opportunity to build hands-on experience and technical depth while demonstrating their capabilities
on real client and product work. Interns who successfully complete the 12-week internship will receive immediate consideration for a full-time Data Scientist
position.
Internship Structure
The internship runs for 12 weeks. Each intern is assigned to the Indianapolis Data Engineering, Architecture, and Science (IDEAS) team, reports to an
experienced data scientist, and is paired with a peer guide for day-to-day support. Interns contribute to active client and product work rather than a
standalone project. Performance is reviewed through regular check-ins with the supervisor and a formal end-of-internship evaluation against the entry-level (1i)
expectations of Milliman’s Data Science job family, covering technical work, quality and documentation, communication, and teamwork. Interns who meet those
expectations are considered for conversion to a full-time Data Scientist position immediately following the internship.
Responsibilities
In this role, you will support:
- Analytics & Product Support: Support enhancements to existing AI/analytics products by implementing techniques outlined by project leads, building prototypes,
and testing new ideas with guidance and review.
- Data Preparation & Exploration: Use SQL, Python, or R to pull, clean, manipulate, and visualize healthcare data (e.g., claims and enrollment), and conduct
exploratory analyses that help frame business questions.
- Model Development: Help build and evaluate statistical and machine learning models, documenting assumptions, inputs, and results so that methods are
transparent and reproducible.
- Quality & Reasonability Checks: Incorporate reasonability checks into work files, recognize possible issues with data and results, and raise them promptly
with the project team.
- Documentation & QRM: Follow Milliman’s quality and risk management (QRM) standards, accurately document work product, and complete QRM documentation as
directed.
- Communication: Prepare clear tables, charts, and short written summaries of analyses for internal review, proactively communicate status updates, and clarify
task expectations when needed.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work alongside actuarial, clinical, pharmacy, policy, and data engineering colleagues to understand requirements and see how
analyses support real client decisions.
- Engineering Practices: Learn and apply team coding standards, including version control (Git/GitHub), code reviews, and modular, reusable code in shared
codebases.
- Learning & Development: Actively participate in training, mentoring, and development opportunities to build knowledge of the US healthcare landscape,
Medicaid, and data science methods.
Qualifications
- Analytical Foundation: Coursework or project experience in statistics, machine learning, predictive modeling, or quantitative problem solving, with the
ability to explain what you did and why.
- Programming Skills: Beginner to intermediate experience in Python, R, or SQL to manipulate, analyze, and visualize data.
- Data Tools: Familiarity with commonly used tools such as Excel, notebooks (e.g., Jupyter), and Git; exposure to Databricks, Spark, or cloud data platforms is
a plus.
- Interest in Healthcare: Interest in US healthcare and public programs such as Medicaid, with a basic understanding of how health coverage and financing work
or a strong desire to learn.
- Attention to Detail: Sensitivity to accuracy and data quality, including checking results for reasonableness before sharing them.
- Communication: Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to summarize findings for both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Work Management: Ability to stay organized, complete assigned tasks within given timelines, and balance multiple priorities.
- Collaboration & Coachability: Eagerness to learn and work as part of a team, openness to constructive feedback, and willingness to ask questions.
- Basic knowledge of Gen AI tools, how to utilize AI to enhance productivity, and potential ethical considerations or concerns.

experience required.
