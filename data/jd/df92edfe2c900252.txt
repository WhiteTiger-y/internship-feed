Job Overview Are you interested in using environmental data and technology to help one of the world’s busiest airports prepare for future challenges? The
Environmental Technical Projects Internship provides an opportunity to gain hands-on experience improving data quality, developing meaningful metrics, and
supporting environmental resilience at DFW Airport. Working alongside environmental professionals, the intern will help organize and standardize data across
Environmental Affairs Department programs. This work will support the development of a Resilience Stress Testing Tool designed to help assess potential risks
and operational performance under extreme conditions. This internship is designed for students interested in environmental science, data analytics, engineering,
or a related field. What you will be doing: Inventories environmental and operational datasets, documenting data sources, formats, ownership, and current uses
across department programs. Reviews datasets for accuracy, completeness, and consistency; identifies gaps and assists with cleaning and standardizing
information for analysis. Supports the preparation and validation of data for use in the Resilience Stress Testing Tool, working with team members to understand
data requirements. Assists with reviewing and refining program metrics and developing reports or visualizations that help department leaders assess performance
and potential risks. Collaborates with program teams to document data definitions, quality checks, and maintenance procedures and recommend improvements that
support future analytics projects. What you need: Must be an active student in a Bachelor’s degree program in environmental science, environmental engineering,
data analytics, data science, information systems, civil engineering, or a related field. 18 years of age or older. What we desire: Current, cumulative GPA of
3.0 or higher, including but not limited to students attending the following universities: University of North Texas, University of Texas at Arlington,
University of Texas at Dallas, Paul Quinn College, or University of Oklahoma. We are interested in every qualified candidate who is eligible to work in the
