About our Emerging Talent Program:
Intuit invests in Emerging Talent because fresh perspectives strengthen our products and teams. The program is backed by a culture that values curiosity and
collaboration, and you'll be supported by dedicated managers and mentors who are invested in your growth. As an intern or co-op, you'll work on meaningful
projects with business impact, many centered on AI and emerging technologies, and contribute to driving decisions. You'll build skills through function-specific
learning opportunities, connect with a global community of interns and co-ops through dedicated programming and social events, and get exposure to senior
leadership and cross-functional work across Intuit's product ecosystem. Learn more about our Intern and Co-op Program here .
About the Team:
Intuit’s Finance team drives business growth and profitability through strategic, financial and operational transformation. At the Financial Integrity,
Transformation & Governance Data team, we are redesigning how Finance operates — with clear ownership, integrated roadmaps, embedded AI, and disciplined value
realization at scale. Our interns have opportunities to apply their knowledge into real-world problems such as process optimization, performance, ROI, and
multiple other areas.
The primary objective of our summer internship program is to bring highly talented people into the organization and provide them an opportunity to work on
interesting challenges. Summer internships are a direct pipeline to full time positions in strategic positions with frequent exposure to company leaders, core
businesses, and technologies – where you can quickly make meaningful contributions.
