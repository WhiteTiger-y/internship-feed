The Cboe Quantitative Risk Analyst & Data Analytics intern.
The Quantitative Risk Analyst & Data Analyst Intern’s at Cboe will assist members of the various groups across Cboe with their innovations and risk management
programs.
Your responsibilities and learning objectives will be:
The Quantitative Risk Analyst Intern at Cboe will
- Assist the team with quantitative analysis of historical market data as well as portfolios and margin requirements to determine scope for improvements to the
margin model. This will give interns an opportunity to utilize programing and quantitative skills on real world data and impact on Cboe products.
- Create (or improve) reports using tools such as Python, Power BI or other tools available within the organization. The intern will have opportunities to
utilize tools that we widely used for creating reports and will get opportunities to understand how to design impactful analytics and reports.
- Assist in documentation of risk management process and reports which would prepare interns with necessary skills for technical roles in corporate setting.
- Support daily monitoring efforts and document observations when exceptions are noted.
- Have the opportunity to design additional analysis and/or monitoring tools to assist in the clearinghouse’s risk management efforts.
The ideal candidate has:
- Should be currently a rising senior in their Bachelors for the Data Analyst roles and second year in a Master’s program for the Quant roles in Financing
Mathematics, Financial Engineering, Computer Science, or related field and should not be scheduled to graduate before December of the year in which the
internship takes place.
- Proficiency in SQL.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python.
- Strong communication skills and analytical skills.
- Able to work on multiple assignments and prioritize initiatives.
You’ll really stand out with:
- Experience with github for checking in and checking out code, creating pull requests
