Overview: This program is designed to foster a beneficial relationship and provide growth opportunities for Vizient, our employees, and collegiate students.
Since 2000, our summer intern program has provided college undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students with practical experience that exposes them to
multiple aspects of a career field and the healthcare industry. This full-time, paid intern program offers an opportunity to accelerate academic learning, apply
classroom knowledge, and introduce students to a professional setting. Interns are assigned a supervisor to mentor/develop their business skills, as well as
monitor their work experiences. Interns are integral members of the company’s team, setting goals, working toward deadlines, and actively participating in
community service projects. The program requires the intern to commit to 10 weeks of participation in all formal training, mentoring, on-the-job learning, and
opportunities to interact with senior leaders. Program starts June 1, 2027, and ends August 6, 2027 Pay rate: $25 per hour. About the team and role Vizient’s
Contract Operations team runs the national contracting lifecycle, from bid launch to contract execution to ongoing contract performance. We partner with
Category Management, Analytics, Legal, Spend Management Delivery, and Technology to help healthcare providers get the most value from their contracts. In this
internship, you will help build the foundation for how Contract Operations and Category Management measure performance. You will create internal dashboards and
establish baseline metrics that give teams a clear, shared view of where they stand today and a benchmark to measure improvement against. You will also help
drive adoption of these tools and build accountability routines, so leaders and teams use the data to track progress, close gaps, and prioritize improvement.
What You’ll Do Partner with Contract Operations and Category Management leaders to identify the metrics that matter most, such as turnaround times, cycle times,
contracting effectiveness (short-term vs long-term extensions), notification performance, internal scorecards, and define them clearly and consistently. Gather,
clean, and analyze operational data to establish baseline metrics and benchmarks that teams can measure improvement against. Design and build internal
dashboards in UCP, Power BI, and Excel that give leaders and teams an easy-to-use view of performance and trends. Drive adoption by training users, gathering
feedback, and refining dashboards so they become part of how teams plan, review, and manage their work. Help set up accountability routines, such as recurring
performance reviews and scorecards, that connect metrics to owners, targets, and improvement actions. Present baseline findings, dashboard insights, and
recommendations to Contract Operations leadership and other senior stakeholders at the end of the program. Create reusable assets, such as metric definitions, a
data dictionary, user guides, and a simple refresh process, so teams can keep dashboards current after the program. What You’ll Learn How a national group
purchasing organization sources and contracts for healthcare providers, and how contracting drives value for clients Dashboard design, data modeling, and
visualization using UCP, Excel, Power BI, and PowerPoint How to define KPIs, set baselines and benchmarks, and use data to drive continuous improvement How
