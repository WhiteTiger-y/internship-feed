optimizing the subscription journey through testing. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will gain hands-on experience designing, tracking, and

Product, etc.). You Will: Research industry trends and evaluate competitor tactics to pitch your own test ideas, seeing your hypotheses evolve from an initial
concept into a live digital experiment. Manage the end-to-end experiment pipeline by setting up cross-platform automations that keep tests moving smoothly from
backlog grooming to real-time status updates. Build out a smart, data-rich experiment repository that maps test results to specific site features and conversion
levers to easily calculate win rates and overall CVR impact. Partner with analysts to monitor live A/B and multivariate tests, diving into behavioral data and
user funnels to uncover what drives readers to subscribe. Translate complex experiment results and performance dashboards into clear, exciting takeaways for
cross-functional partners in Product, Engineering, and the Newsroom. You Have: Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree, preferably in a quantitative field
(Business Analytics, Data Science, Statistics, Economics, or related fields). Coursework or foundational knowledge of experimentation principles (hypothesis
testing, A/B testing concepts, or statistical significance). Basic foundational knowledge of SQL and data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau). Exposure to or
strong interest in learning experimentation and paywall platforms Proficiency with Excel or Google Sheets for data manipulation and analysis. Ability to
synthesize experimental data into clear, easy-to-understand recommendations for non-technical stakeholders. High attention to detail and a strong commitment to
data integrity. Curiosity about digital audience behavior, user experience, and subscription growth strategies. Program Eligibility:  Enrollment in a 4-year
