Responsibilities
This is a role well suited for a highly motivated student looking to build their skill set and gain hands-on experience. As a Research Intern, you will be
responsible for:
- Developing and fine-tuning multimodal vision/language/action models for downstream robotic tasks
- Designing pipelines that fuse multi-sensor imaging and kinematic signals into unified spatial representations
- Connecting perception to planning and control through VLA-based policies and agentic AI decision-making frameworks
- Building policy-learning workflows for embodied robotic behavior, including teleoperation-driven data collection
Skills
This position may suit you best if you are familiar with the areas and concepts below, and would like to develop your skills within Healthineers:
- M.S., Ph.D., or postdoctoral researcher in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, or a closely related field
- Strong programming skills in Python and experience with modern deep learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch)
- Demonstrated research experience in computer vision, deep learning, or multimodal machine learning (e.g., publications, projects, or advanced coursework)
Required skills and education to have for the success of this role:
- Currently enrolled in and pursuing studies at a foreign degree- or certificate-granting post-secondary academic institution outside the United States OR Has
