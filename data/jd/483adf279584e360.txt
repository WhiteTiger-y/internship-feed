USA - Tax - Tax Technology and Transformation (TTT) - Artificial Intelligence - Intern - Summer 2027
Will you shape the future or will the future shape you?
The Opportunity
Join our dynamic EY Tax team, where our global expertise and deep technical knowledge across all tax disciplines are enhanced by the latest in innovation and
cutting-edge technology. We pride ourselves on exceptional client service, strong accountability, and governance, making us the top choice for industry
executives and the largest revenue-generating US and global tax practice.
Our Tax Technology and Transformation (TTT) team offers services to enhance the operational efficiency of tax functions and the delivery of technical services,
supported by a team of over 1,200 professionals. Our core focus areas include transforming tax functions, integrating with finance and business processes, and
optimizing enterprise systems, all bolstered by strategic alliances. We aim to revolutionize tax and finance operations through strategy development,
digitalization, and leveraging technology to automate compliance, reporting, and provide valuable insights to our clients.
Your Key Responsibilities
As an Intern within the Tax Technology and Transformation (TTT) Alwin team, you will focus on AI technologies and develop best-in-class AI solutions across Tax
technology products for our clients.
You will learn about the latest trends in tax technology and how to become a trusted client advisor. You will learn about organization’s cultures, challenges
and goals. You will work on client deliverables and may participate directly with clients in strategy meetings and workshops. Our clients are located
nationwide, so travel may be a part of the role.
Your day-to-day responsibilities may include:
- Assuming full responsibility for designated tasks and ensuring their follow-through.
- Designing and building AI Agents to automate and enhance complex business tasks.
- Integration of AI components into full-scale solutions to support business processes.
- Performing various data science tasks including data mining, data profiling, feature selection and engineering, and model building and optimizing using
machine learning techniques.
- Exploring opportunities to streamline repetitive processes through AI automation with current and new tech solutions.
- Providing project support to team members to guarantee the punctual delivery of project outcomes.
- Investigating cutting-edge technologies like AI and machine learning to develop relevant business cases for their implementation.
Skills and attributes for success
To qualify for the role, you must have:
- A predicted undergraduate or graduate degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Economics, Management Information Systems, or other related
majors; supported by a strong academic record.
- 3.0 cumulative college/university GPA.
- Record of building and fine-tuning machine learning models and prompting generative AI models Hands-on experience with building or configuring AI Agents
- Excellent Python skills.
- Excellent communication and business writing skills.
- A natural passion for problem solving, and an entrepreneurial approach to work.
- Strong organizational and time management skills, with the confidence to prioritize your workload within tight deadlines.
- An understanding of common data management tools and principles.
- Knowledge of how to leverage firm-approved AI tools in a business setting, including Microsoft Copilot.
- An interest working in our team-led and leader-enabled hybrid model. Our expectation is for most people in external, client serving roles to work together in
person 40-60% of the time over the course of an engagement, project or year.
- Access to reliable transportation to/from the EY office and client sites. A driver’s license is strongly recommended as ride share and public transportation
options may not be available in all locations.
- The ability and willingness to travel and work in excess of standard hours when necessary. In certain circumstances, travel may be required beyond your work
location based on client and project needs.
- You must reside within a commutable distance of the office location associated with this position.
- Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. without the need for employer sponsorship, now and in the future.
Ideally, you will have:
- Cumulative college/university GPA of 3.2 or above.
- Experience working with Microsoft Azure AI stack or any other Cloud environment including AWS, GPT, or others.
- Experience with Microsoft Fabrics, Databricks, Snowflakes or other data tools.
Please note this position is not able to consider candidates with an Accounting major. CPA-track candidates with an interest in Tax should explore opportunities
in 360 Careers Tax; you can learn more about the 360 Careers Experience here.
