Position Summary
Here at Parker Lord, our purpose is simple yet powerful: we work alongside customers as a trusted partner to enable technology breakthroughs that change the
world for the better. To do this, we need the strongest possible talent and our Internship Programs have been developed in a way that you get an opportunity to
show us your potential. These programs have been in place for years at Parker Lord and some of our most successful leaders started their career with us in an
Internship role. Do you want to become one of our future success stories? By joining our Internship Program, you will become a valued member of our team -
tackling challenging issues, delivering truly impactful results, and providing you the opportunity to grow both technically and personally through practical
working experience.
You will be responsible for providing support to various product development, technical service initiatives and application development growth projects
involving Parker Lord coatings and adhesives. Typical tasks may include chemical synthesis, product formulation, product property and performance testing,
sample preparation, product application, and preparation of technical documentation/reports, data mining, and statistical analysis (design of experiments). Your
objective is to enable technology breakthroughs that serve the most important, yet least satisfied, outcomes of interest for our customers, providing solutions
that deliver compelling value and unparalleled performance.
