Engineering & Analytics team builds the data, reporting and AI that help Panera run its cafés and serve its guests. As an intern, you will work on real projects
with real business impact, not side assignments. You will work alongside our data engineers, BI developers and analytics partners, and you will present what you

prescriptive analytics that go beyond reporting what happened to recommending what to do next. Data quality and governance: Profile key datasets, flag gaps or
anomalies, and help document KPI definitions so everyone uses the same numbers. Insights and storytelling: Turn analysis into clear visuals and recommendations

Working knowledge of SQL and Python Coursework or projects in statistics or machine learning Curiosity, ownership, and the ability to explain technical work

restaurants, retail or consumer businesses The actual pay offered will be determined by multiple factors, including but not limited to the candidate’s relevant
