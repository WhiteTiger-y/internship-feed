Company Overview
Docusign brings agreements to life. Over 1.5 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions to accelerate the
process of doing business and simplify people’s lives. With intelligent agreement management, Docusign unleashes business-critical data that is trapped inside
of documents. Until now, these were disconnected from business systems of record, costing businesses time, money, and opportunity. Using Docusign’s Intelligent
Agreement Management platform, companies can create, commit, and manage agreements with solutions created by the #1 company in e-signature and contract
lifecycle management (CLM).
What you'll do
Are you passionate about bridging the gap between scientific research and scalable, real-world applications? As an Applied Science Intern, you will work
alongside world-class scientists and engineers to tackle complex, ambiguous problems. This role is designed for post-graduate students (Master's or PhD) who
want to apply advanced mathematical, statistical, and machine learning techniques to build innovative solutions that directly impact our products and customers.
You will work on advanced models for complex document understanding-such as automated clause extraction, semantic risk detection, and structural analysis-or
design intelligent AI agents that orchestrate end-to-end agreement workflows. Your research will directly power smart, autonomous features that help millions of
users seamlessly draft, negotiate, and analyze contracts at scale. During this 10 to 12-week summer internship, you will own a high-impact project from
conception to prototyping, gaining hands-on experience with massive datasets, cutting-edge technologies, and collaborative agile workflows.
This is an internship role for 3 months of duration.
This position is an individual contributor role reporting to the Senior Manager, Applied Science.
Responsibility
- Design, develop, and evaluate novel algorithms and statistical models to solve complex business and technical challenges
- Analyze and manipulate large-scale, structured and unstructured datasets to extract meaningful insights and drive model features
- Partner closely with Product Managers and Software Engineering teams to translate theoretical concepts into functional, scalable prototypes
- Design and execute comprehensive evaluation frameworks, including offline simulations and controlled A/B experiments, to rigorously assess model performance,
identify edge cases, and ensure statistically significant outcomes
- Clearly document your methodology, findings, and code, and present your project outcomes to both technical and non-technical stakeholders at the conclusion of
your internship
