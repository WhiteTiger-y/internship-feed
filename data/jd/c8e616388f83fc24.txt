Wet Lab Sensor Research Co-Op
The Deep-Tech Architecture (DTA) group is seeking a motivated Electrochemical Sensor Research Intern to support the Analog Garage, ADI’s Research and Advanced
Development Center in downtown Boston that empowers ADI by pioneering breakthrough technologies. The DTA group is applying world-class circuit and device
design, and nano-scale fabrication to develop long-term solutions to big societal problems. You will join the team which is leveraging proven and mature
semiconductor technologies combined with electrochemical sensing to develop innovative solutions in health of human, health of planet, and other strategic focus
areas for ADI.
Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
- Design, fabricate, and characterize electrochemical sensors for applications in complex sample matrices.
- Develop and execute experimental protocols for sensor calibration and validation.
- Present findings of your research and proposed solutions to the project team.
Minimum qualifications:
- Master's (preferred) or undergraduate candidate in Chemistry, Biotechnology, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Electrochemistry, or a closely related
field.
- Hands-on experience in a wet lab environment such as pipetting, handling chemicals and safety procedure awareness.
- Strong hands-on technical skills.
Preferred qualifications:
- Experience with electrochemical measurement techniques and laboratory instrumentation.
- Familiarity with sensor design, membrane chemistry, or thin-film deposition techniques would be considered an asset. et.
