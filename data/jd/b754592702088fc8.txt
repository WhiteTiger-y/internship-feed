Who We Are
MEMX is a technology-driven exchange operator founded by members to benefit all investors. We operate MEMX Equities, MEMX Options, and MX2 Options, and provide
market technology to clients. Our mission is to create healthy competition and advocate for market structure reform that results in more efficient and sensible
trading experiences for all investors.
Description
MEMX is seeking a Data Engineering intern who has a passion for technology, an interest in financial markets, and the enthusiasm to contribute to our U.S.
equities and options exchanges. This internship position will be offered as hybrid with 2 days in-person at our co-working office in Midtown-NYC.
*MEMX has currently approved ONLY the following states for remote work: California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Kansas, Maine, Maryland,
Massachusetts, Michigan, Nevada, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, Texas, & Utah.
What You’ll Be Doing
- Work alongside MEMX’s technology team on data engineering projects that support our exchanges
- Help collect, organize, and validate data used for reporting and analysis
- Assist with building and improving data processes and tolls, with projects tailored to your skills and the team’s needs
- Collaborate with colleagues to solve problems, share ideas, and communicate your findings
- Gain hands-on experience in a growing exchange and see how your work contributes to the broader business
Requirements
- Current undergraduate student entering their junior or senior year, pursuing a degree Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Data Engineering, Electrical
Engineering, or a related field
- Experience with technical projects through coursework, internships, or personal work
- Ability to communicate technical ideas and work collaboratively with a team
- Ability to work in person at our Midtown Manhattan office during the summer
- Permanent residence in one of MEMX’s approved states for remote work during the internship
What You’ll Gain
- Hands-on experience working with data that supports MEMX’s exchanges
- Exposure to how data is collected, organized, and used for reporting an analysis
- Mentorship and guidance from experienced data engineers
- Opportunities to collaborate with technology teams across MEMX and connect with fellow interns
Why MEMX
At MEMX, you’ll contribute to meaningful work alongside experienced professionals while developing your skills and learning how our exchanges operate. As part
of our summer internship program, you’ll also have access to:
- Learning opportunities: Attend Lunch & Learn sessions, professional development workshops, and relevant courses
- Mentorship and support: Learn from a mentor and a designated buddy who can help guide you throughout the summer
- An intern cohort: Connect with interns working across other MEMX teams
- Social events: Get to know colleagues, with opportunities to connect with members of MEMX’s C-suite
- Regular feedback: Check in with your manager and HR to discuss your progress, ask questions, and get support
- Future opportunities: All interns are considered for potential internship extensions or a full-time opportunity after graduation, based on performance and
business needs
Pay Rate: $30/hourly for undergraduate students
