Job Description
CDM Smith is looking for an Environmental Scientist Intern to join our Environmental Services Team in Newport News, Virginia!
During this internship, you will support our environmental services team by researching, compiling, and analyzing various scientific data. You will perform
routine tasks such as proofreading and copying technical and non-technical reports, specifications, and other administrative duties. Interns may also
participate in fieldwork, including site investigations, GIS support, and map production.
Qualifications
Currently pursuing a Bachelors degree in Environmental Science, Hydrology, Geology, or a related science degree is required.
Amount Of Travel Required
20% - 40%
Driving Requirements
An appropriate and valid driver's license is required.
