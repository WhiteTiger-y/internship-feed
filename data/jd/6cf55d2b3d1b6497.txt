site maintenance and operational strategies. In addition, the intern will contribute to the design and enhancement of visual and interactive dashboards used by
the ROC to identify performance trends, operational risks, and optimization opportunities across the fleet. The role also includes supporting the Senior ROC
Analyst in the development and maintenance of databases to enable scalable analytics and collaboration between operations and engineering teams. This internship

assist with positively impacting the performance, reliability, and growth of Xcel’s renewable energy portfolio. Typical intern responsibilities may include but
are not limited to: • Support analytical and operational initiatives under the guidance of the ROC Sr. Ops Manager and Sr. Analyst. • Collaborate with ROC
Operators, ROC Analysts, onsite operations, engineering, IT support teams, transmission, and renewable operations leadership to support performance improvement
initiatives by assisting with the implementation of technical efficiencies. • Assist the ROC Senior Analyst with data software service initiatives, helping
ensure Renewable Operations can deliver accurate and timely reports to internal and external stakeholders. • Help collect, validate, and organize operational
data used for reporting, trend analysis, and post-event reviews. • Assist the Senior ROC Analyst with trend analysis and reporting to identify and escalate
potential indicators of equipment degradation to ROC Operators and onsite leadership. • Support the ROC’s preparation for future renewable growth by assisting
