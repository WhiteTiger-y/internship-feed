Meta is seeking AI Research interns to join our Video Recommendations team within Facebook. Videos (Reels, long videos, and Live streams) are increasingly the
formats of choice across the social media industry, and making all these formats successful is a top priority for Facebook, and the core mission owned by this
org. Time spent on Facebook watching videos accounts for 1.2+ Billion hours/day, which is over 56% of time spent globally on the App, and this number is
expected to continue growing.This team is directly in charge of recommending all sorts of videos to users, powered by our large-scale unified video
recommendation models. In this role, you will work on Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): building AI agents that autonomously propose, run, evaluate, and learn
from improvements to these models, closing the loop between ML experimentation and model quality. Our interns will have the opportunity to frame practical,
high-impact challenges in recommendation into rigorous machine learning problems, and bring their own expertise to build innovative solutions under the guidance
of our Research Scientists and Software Engineers.More concretely, you will develop an agent-driven RSI prototype that continuously proposes, evaluates, and
refines improvements for a selected video recommendation task. The scope spans training data improvement, environment and experiment setup, automated offline
model evaluation, and feedback collection from model quality signals such as normalized entropy (NE) and recall. You will use experiment outcomes to improve
both the agent's intervention strategy and its evaluation criteria, and deliver a working end-to-end prototype with evidence against a fixed baseline. Work will
be mentored by the team, with production changes and online experiments subject to owner review and approval.Our internships are twelve (12) to twenty-four (24)
weeks long and we have various start dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, Video RecSys (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Design and build an agent-driven Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) system that continuously proposes, evaluates, and refines improvements to a large-scale
video recommendation model.
- Profile training data quality and signal coverage, and implement bounded training-data interventions such as filtering, sampling, and reweighting.
- Automate environment and experiment setup, including reproducible training configurations, offline model evaluation pipelines, and evaluation benchmarks.
- Build feedback loops that collect and attribute model quality signals (e.g., NE and recall gains or losses) and use them to improve the agent's intervention
strategy and evaluation criteria.
- Measure downstream model quality alongside data-processing and training costs, and maintain versioned experiment records and automated checks.
- Collaborate with researchers and cross-functional partners including communicating research plans, progress, and results.
- Publish research results and contribute to research that can be applied to Meta product development.
