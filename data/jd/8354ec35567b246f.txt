About Precision Neuroscience
Precision Neuroscience is building a next-generation brain–computer interface (BCI) to heal and empower millions of people living with neurological conditions.
Our first product, Layer 7, is designed to help people with severe paralysis operate digital devices using only their thoughts—opening up new possibilities for
daily life.
Our team brings together experts in neurosurgery, AI and machine learning, microfabrication, electrical engineering, clinical science, and more. We combine deep
technical rigor with a people-first mindset to turn breakthrough research into real-world medical solutions.
As a Precision employee, you’ll join one of the fastest-moving and best-capitalized companies in the emerging field of brain–computer interfaces. Since our
founding in 2021, we have raised more than $430 million, advanced our technology through validation, and initiated human trials with leading hospitals across
the country.
Our Values
We build for human impact, measuring progress by the lives our work can change.
We do no harm, holding ourselves to the highest standards of safety, integrity, and responsibility.
We innovate with urgency because the stakes are high and our users can’t wait.
We bring sharp minds, open ears, pairing expertise with curiosity, humility, and respect.
And we lead the way, taking ownership of our work and helping to shape the future of our field.
We are seeking a Machine Learning Research Intern to develop machine learning models that improve the reliability and generalization of our neural decoders
across time and across subjects.
In this role you will work with machine learning researchers, neuroscientists and clinicians to build models that decode motor intent and behavioral state from
high-density neural recordings of our Layer 7 brain–computer interface. You will help design and implement models that will remain reliable over long periods of
time, with minimal need for recalibration.
This position will be based in our New York office. We are unable to consider remote workers or individuals who are not currently based in the US and not
legally authorized to work in the US.
Key Responsibilities
- Work with cross-functional teams of neuroscientists, clinicians and software engineers to improve the reliability of our brain–computer interfaces.
- Analyze large, complex neural datasets.
- Develop novel machine learning models for reliable and robust neural decoding and behavioral state classification.
- Design and run experiments to evaluate models against established baselines.
- Communicate results clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
Skills, Knowledge And Expertise
- Currently pursuing a PhD in machine learning, computational neuroscience, biomedical engineering, electrical engineering, computer science or a related field.
- Prior experience implementing and training machine learning models, shown through publications or projects.
- Deep understanding of machine learning fundamentals.
- Experience analyzing large, complex datasets.
- Experience with signal processing of time-series data.
- Fluency in Python and experience with a deep learning framework such as PyTorch.
- Strong plus: experience with analysis and modeling of neural data.
- Strong plus: experience with transfer learning and domain adaptation.
