About The Program:
The CrowdStrike Launch Internship Program is an exclusive early-career opportunity designed for exceptional freshmen and sophomore level students (graduating
between 2029 and 2030). This program offers students the chance to gain hands-on experience at one of the world's leading cybersecurity companies while still
early in their academic journey.
About The Role:
The scale of CrowdStrike means we have problems of all different sizes, shapes, and types of data — where all fields of AI and ML are applied as needed, and
their gaps form the problems our research team tackles. Identifying what is missing, what capabilities don't exist off-the-shelf in Open Source software, and
inventing new techniques, methods, and algorithms to fill that gap.
We are looking for a Launch Intern who has the drive, motivation, and capacity to tackle some of our research questions under the mentorship of CrowdStrike
experts. You will be pushed to deeply understand and interrogate the math behind the problem, grapple with effective communication and reduction of invention to
technical document, and leverage all of our tools — without being reliant upon them — to produce the kind of high-quality research our team regularly publishes
at premier venues like AAAI, NeurIPS, and beyond.
What You’ll Do:
- Identify gaps in existing AI/ML capabilities and open source tooling as they apply to real CrowdStrike research problems
- Work under the mentorship of CrowdStrike researchers to investigate and scope research questions end-to-end
- Develop a deep mathematical understanding of the problem space — interrogating assumptions, derivations, and limitations
- Invent, implement, and evaluate novel techniques, methods, or algorithms to address identified gaps
- Reduce your work to clear, rigorous technical writing suitable for submission to premier research venues such as AAAI and NeurIPS
- Communicate findings and progress effectively to technical mentors and peers, incorporating feedback iteratively
What You’ll Need:
- Must be currently enrolled as a freshman or sophomore at an accredited 4-year undergraduate university, working towards a degree. Graduating during or between
May 2029 and December 2030.
- Python proficiency and hands-on experience with ML/DL frameworks such as scikit-learn, PyTorch, JAX, or similar
- Ability to produce coherent written technical communication, unaided by GenAI tooling
- Broad at-level math skills with deeper knowledge in at least one domain (e.g., linear algebra, stochastic methods, control theory)
- Experience implementing an algorithm from scratch without relying on existing implementations or references
- Evidence of having worked on a complex project with the ability to receive and act on technical feedback and guidance
- A strong sense of humor
Bonus Points:
- Experience in lower-level programming languages such as C++ or Rust
- Previous high-quality written samples, technical blog posts, or published papers
- Familiarity with advanced mathematics broadly, and statistical learning theory or similar AI/ML proof methods
- Deeper exposure to cybersecurity work such as reverse engineering, or systems skills like compiler design
- An even stronger sense of humor — one capable of detecting humor from at least 100 feet away
