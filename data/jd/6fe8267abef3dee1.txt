AI INTERN, TECHNOLOGY
JOB DESCRIPTION
Company Background
Founded in 1993, DivcoWest is a multi-disciplinary real estate investment firm headquartered in San Francisco, with offices in Los Angeles, Menlo Park, Boston,
Washington DC, Austin, and New York City. Known for our long-standing relationships and track record of success in innovation markets, DivcoWest combines
entrepreneurial spirit with an institutional approach. DivcoWest aims to create environments that inspire ingenuity, promote growth, and enhance the health,
happiness, and well-being of all people. A disciplined code of ethics is at the core of all that we do. We believe that the collective energy of a diverse team
is what drives our creative ideas and solutions.
Summary
The AI Intern will join the Technology team to design and implement intelligent solutions that make work smoother, smarter, and more efficient across the
organization. Working closely with teams throughout the firm, this role will explore how generative AI and automation can streamline operations, surface
insights, and improve efficiency across multiple departments.
This position is based in our San Francisco headquarters and requires 4+ days in office. The internship is full-time for approximately 12 weeks, with the option
for extension. Exact schedule can be adjusted to accommodate the right candidate.
Responsibilities
Process Improvement
- Conduct workflow audits across departments to identify and document processes most ready for automation.
- Prototype automated processes and workflows to reduce manual effort and increase consistency.
- Partner with the Technology team and department stakeholders to integrate systems that support smoother cross-functional workflows.
AI Solutions & Tool Development
- Prototype an AI-assisted tool for a target department as a pilot project.
- Create visual dashboards integrating operational data and company metrics to generate actionable leadership insights.
- Contribute to the design of custom AI assistants for internal teams, supporting content structure, data organization, and prompt library development.
- Research, identify, and propose outside AI tools to assist with internal efficiency; assist with training and implementation.
Knowledge & Data Management
- Build and maintain AI learning resources and trainings for teams across the firm to support adoption of key AI tools.
- Own data management and documentation for assigned projects, including tracking milestones, decisions, and outcomes.
- Document project discussions and action items to promote accountability across AI-related workstreams.
Qualifications
- Current undergraduate or graduate student pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Business, or a related field.
- Demonstrated curiosity about AI, automation, and digital transformation in the workplace.
- Familiarity with tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft CoPilot, Zapier, Notion AI, or similar platforms.
- Strong analytical and communication skills with the ability to translate technical ideas into practical business solutions.
- Experience or coursework in Python, workflow automation, or dashboard design a plus.
- Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive in managing multiple projects.
- Thrives in a collaborative and fast-paced environment.
We provide reasonable accommodation to support individuals of all abilities in performing essential functions.
Compensation
- $20 - $30/ hour depending on experience
- Weekly lunch stipend
