this role. The Summer 2027 Clinical and Population Health Graduate Internship provides graduate students with an opportunity to gain practical, hands-on
experience using advanced analytics to address complex healthcare challenges and improve health outcomes. This internship is designed to develop the next
generation of healthcare analytics professionals through direct exposure to population health, clinical quality, healthcare economics, and data-driven strategic
decision-making. As a member of the Clinical and Population Health team, the intern will leverage clinical, member, financial, and administrative data to
identify meaningful patterns, develop actionable insights, and evaluate opportunities aligned with the Quadruple Aim of Healthcare: improving health outcomes
and quality of care, reducing per capita healthcare costs, and enhancing member/patient and provider experiences with care delivery. The intern will work
alongside experienced analytics, clinical, operational, financial, and technical professionals to support the design, development, execution, and interpretation
of clinical and population health analyses, metrics, and reports. Participants will gain valuable experience translating complex data into meaningful insights
and recommendations that can help improve healthcare quality, affordability, utilization, and outcomes. Typical responsibilities include applying advanced
analytical techniques to complex healthcare datasets, developing and monitoring population health and clinical quality metrics, evaluating trends and
opportunities, communicating analytical findings to business and clinical stakeholders, and supporting projects from initial analysis through successful
completion. Through mentorship, cross-functional collaboration, and meaningful project work, participants will strengthen their analytical, healthcare,
communication, and strategic-thinking skills while building a strong foundation for a career in healthcare analytics and population health. SUMMARY OF JOB
