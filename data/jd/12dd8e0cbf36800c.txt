The Role
Our Spring/Summer Co-op Program runs from January 18, 2027 through June 25, 2027 . All applicants must be available for the full duration of the program.
In this role, you will be a key contributor to the Research team, focusing on the integration and application of novel technologies that enhance our development
and commercial processes. This opportunity is designed to provide hands-on experience for students currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program,
offering the opportunity to work on innovative projects that align with Moderna's mission to deliver transformative medicines.
Here’s What You’ll Do
- Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team of engineers and scientists to design, develop, and implement technology solutions across various stages of drug
development and manufacturing.
- Assist in the design and testing of automated systems and technologies, ensuring they meet the operational and quality standards required for clinical and
commercial production.
- Support the development of process automation strategies and contribute to the creation of documentation and training materials.
- Participate in the analysis and troubleshooting of existing systems, providing input on potential improvements and optimizations.
- Engage in continuous learning through team meetings, training sessions, and project collaboration to stay updated on the latest advancements in applied
technologies.
- Contribute to the advancement of technology integration processes that drive efficiency and innovation in Moderna’s production pipeline.
- Additional duties as may be assigned, with a focus on expanding your knowledge and skills in applied technologies.
Here’s What You’ll Need (Basic Qualifications)
- Current student enrolled in a Bachelors or Masters program in Engineering (e.g., Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical), Computer Science, or a related field.
- Strong interest in applied technologies, automation, and systems integration.
- Basic knowledge of engineering principles and an eagerness to apply them in real-world scenarios.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Effective communication skills and the ability to work well in a team-oriented environment.
- A proactive approach to learning and applying new skills.
- At Moderna, we are focused on delivering on our mission by enabling talent to thrive. For this role, we are currently unable to offer immigration sponsorship.
Candidates must already hold work authorization in US and be able to maintain that status without the need for future sponsorship.
- This position is site-based, requiring you to be at Moderna’s site full-time. This position is not eligible for remote work.
Here’s What You’ll Bring to the Table (Preferred Qualifications)
- GPA of 3.5 on a 4 scale or equivalent.
- Previous internship/Co-op or project experience related to automation, robotics, or technology development.
- Familiarity with programming languages or software used in automation and systems engineering.
- Experience with CAD software or other design tools.
- An enthusiasm for working on innovative projects that push the boundaries of current technology.
- A collaborative mindset and a desire to be part of a high-growth, transformational company that is Bold, Relentless, Curious, and Collaborative.
Our Working Model
As we build our company, we have always believed an in-person culture is critical to our success. Moderna champions the significant benefits of in-office
collaboration by embracing a 70/30 work model. This 70% in-office structure helps to foster a culture rich in innovation, teamwork, and direct mentorship. Join
us in shaping a world where every interaction is an opportunity to learn, contribute, and make a meaningful impact.
Moderna is a smoke-free, alcohol-free, and drug-free work environment.
