Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2
billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect

The Community You Will Join:
Airbnb is seeking MBA students to join the Finance organization as Strategic Finance & Analytics Interns for our 2027 Summer Intern Program from May 24, 2027 -
August 13, 2027 .
At Airbnb, we believe finance is about more than numbers. Our Strategic Finance & Analytics (SFA) team sits at the intersection of finance, strategy, and the
business, partnering directly with leaders to decide where Airbnb invests, how we grow, and what we do next. We expect every member of the team to bring a CFO
mindset to budget management, producing actionable insights, owning our performance story, and supporting day-to-day execution.
We are looking for interns who are eager to learn, grow, and sharpen their strategic and analytical skills alongside leaders across Finance. While a return
offer is never guaranteed, we have a track record of bringing exceptional summer interns back as full-time employees after graduation.

- Identify and interpret trends and patterns based on business data and market knowledge
