Requisition Summary
Peterbilt Motors Company, a PACCAR Company, has an internship for students currently enrolled in college. This opening is for a Data Scientist Intern to add to
our Advanced Analytics team in Denton, Texas.
Job Responsibilities
This position will be working on various projects and reports to include but not limited to:
- Write complex SQL queries, including joins, aggregations, common table expressions, and window functions, to prepare and analyze data.
- Develop scalable data science solutions, from exploratory analysis, hypothesis testing, and feature engineering through model development, validation,
deployment, and monitoring.
- Apply machine learning, statistical methods, decision models, and Generative AI methodologies to transform data into actionable business insights.
- Collaborate with senior team members to understand business needs, translate those needs into analytical problems, and communicate updates and results
clearly.
- Develop Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) workflows using embeddings, vector databases, and build harness engineered LLM workflows under the guidance of
senior team members.
- Build, test, deploy, and monitor AI solutions using cloud platforms and managed services such as AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Agent Platform (previously Vertex
AI), or Azure AI Foundry.
- Use GitHub for version control and team collaboration and apply Markdown-Driven Development (MDD) best practices to agent-assisted solution development.
- Document methodologies, assumptions, designs, test results, and operational procedures while following responsible AI, privacy, security, and model governance
standards.
Required Qualifications
The candidate we seek will currently be enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s degree program in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Business Analytics,
Information Systems, or a related quantitative program.
The Candidate Should Also Have
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, and written and verbal communication skills.
- Python Programming experience and its data science ecosystem (e.g., pandas, scikit-learn, plotly, numpy, scipy), including modular development, object-
oriented programming, testing, exception handling, and logging.
- Working knowledge of SQL, including the ability to develop queries using joins, aggregations, and window functions.
- Good Knowledge of Machine Learning, statistical analysis, LLMs and Data Science concepts.
- Experience developing solutions using one or more cloud platforms, such as AWS, Google Cloud Platform, or Microsoft Azure.
- Experience analyzing data, evaluating results, and communicating findings to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Ability to work collaboratively with team members and stakeholders in a project-based environment.
- Understanding of responsible AI principles, including privacy, security, bias, explainability, and model governance.
Preferred Qualifications
The qualifications that are important to be successful in this position are as follows:
- Experience working with Snowflake and writing SQL for data preparation and analysis.
- Experience using Git-based development workflows, including branching, pull requests, and release management.
- Familiarity with Large Language Models, Decision Models, prompt engineering, embedding, tool-calling workflows, and multi-step agents both using open source
(Langgraph) and managed cloud AI services.
- Experience using managed Generative AI services, such as AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Agent Platform (previously Vertex AI), or Azure AI Foundry.
- Design solution architectures for machine learning, RAG, and agent-based applications, including model selection, retrieval strategy, orchestration,
evaluation, observability, version control, testing, and production monitoring.
- Experience developing Retrieval-Augmented Generation solutions using a vector database, such as pgvector, Pinecone, or an equivalent platform.
- Familiarity with MLOps practices, including version control (Git), and experiment tracking (MLflow, OpenTelemetry, Arize Phoenix, or similar).
- Design and execute experiments, including A/B tests and hypothesis tests, and translate findings into clear business recommendations.
