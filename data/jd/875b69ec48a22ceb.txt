Haskell’s Design & Consulting Team unites architecture, engineering, and consulting professionals from across industries and geographies. While some clients are
looking for turnkey design-build or engineer-procure-construct projects, The Design & Consulting Team serves clients who need specialized, fee-for-service
design or consulting projects. These types of projects allow our team to form a strong rapport with client teams, marshaling them as they grow and helping them
address industry and market challenges. The Design & Consulting Team serves four primary markets: Government, primarily the Department of Defense, U.S. Army
Corps of Engineers, Air and Army National Guard, and the U.S. Naval Facilities Command; Industrial, focusing on food and beverage, consumer products, and
utilities; Civil Infrastructure; and Healthcare.
We are seeking a motivated AI Engineering Design Intern to join our team and contribute to innovative projects at the intersection of artificial intelligence
and engineering design . This role offers hands-on experience in applying AI-driven tools, algorithms, and workflows to solve real-world engineering challenges,
from concept generation to optimization.
Job Responsibilities :
- Assist in developing and testing AI models for engineering design applications (e.g., generative design, simulation optimization, CAD automation).
- Support data collection, preprocessing, and annotation for AI training datasets.
- Collaborate with engineers and designers to integrate AI solutions into existing design workflows.
- Conduct research on emerging AI techniques relevant to engineering design.
- Document processes, results, and recommendations for future improvements.
Education/Experience:
- Pursuit of a degree in Architecture, Mechanical, Electrical, Civil , and/or Structural Engineering .
Specific Knowledge, Skills, License, Certifications :
- Basic understanding of AI/ML concepts (e.g., supervised learning, neural networks).
- Familiarity with engineering design tools (e.g., SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Fusion 360) is a plus.
- Proficiency in Python or MATLAB; experience with AI libraries (TensorFlow, PyTorch , scikit-learn) preferred.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Must have good personal habits
- Work well with others
- Be able to stay on task and complete work on schedule
- Be able to work independently as well as on a team
Preferred Skills :
- Knowledge of generative design principles or topology optimization.
- Understanding of cloud-based AI platforms (AWS, Azure, ChatGPT, Co-Pilot and/or Gemini ).
What You’ll Gain :
- Practical experience applying AI to engineering design challenges.
- Mentorship from experienced engineers and AI specialists.
- Exposure to cutting-edge tools and workflows shaping the future of design.
- Opportunity to contribute to impactful, real-world projects.
