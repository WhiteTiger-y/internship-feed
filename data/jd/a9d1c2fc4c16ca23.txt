EmbodyX is a Silicon Valley startup developing next-generation Physical AI and robotics software to address the global labor shortage through intelligent
automation. Our team includes experts from leading technology companies such as Google and Snap, as well as renowned robotics research labs including
Northeastern University, Carnegie Mellon University, and MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab.
We are looking for a Robotics Engineer Intern to join our engineering team part-time/ full-time. In this role, you will work on the development, evaluation, and
deployment of state-of-the-art robot foundation models and embodied AI systems. You will collaborate closely with researchers and engineers to build scalable
learning pipelines and deploy advanced AI models on real robotic platforms.
This is a deployment-oriented role. Engineers may work on-site at customer facilities for extended periods, and the primary work location may change as projects
and customer deployments evolve. Candidates should be comfortable with this level of mobility and on-site work.

- Develop, optimize, and deploy robotic manipulation pipelines using commercial robotic platforms (e.g., Interbotix, WidowX, Kinova, Unitree, ABB).

π0, Lingbo-VA, and emerging foundation models.
