Research Scientist Intern, FAIR - Video Generation Self-Improvement (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Conduct fundamental and applied research in video generation, including generative models, video synthesis, and multimodal learning.
- Develop and optimize post-training paradigms for large-scale video and multimodal models, including reinforcement learning from human and AI feedback and
reward modeling.
- Research self-improving and agentic generation systems, including multi-agent LLM/VLM pipelines and LLM/VLM-as-a-judge.
- Collaborate with teams across Meta to build perceptual foundations for real-time embodied agents and conversational AI.
- Contribute to the development of frontier models (e.g., Llama, LMMs) and push the boundaries of video and media generation.
- Brainstorm with research mentors, review literature and existing solutions of a challenging real-world research problem.
- Develop novel solutions, implement prototypes, and perform extensive experiments to test the proposed solutions in meaningful benchmarks and metrics, analyze
the results and verify the conclusions.
- Draft and polish research reports and/or publications.
- Present research outcomes to internal and/or external audiences.
- Contribute research that can be applied to Meta product development.
