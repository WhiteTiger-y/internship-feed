Company Description Grow Pro Genetics LLC is a seed research organization dedicated to creating high-performing seed genetics that deliver measurable value to
agricultural customers. The company focuses on sustainable products that support producers, retailers, and farmers in improving crop performance and long-term
farm profitability. By combining applied research with field-based testing, Grow Pro Genetics works to advance innovative seed solutions tailored to real-world
growing conditions. Interns have the opportunity to contribute to meaningful research that directly impacts modern agriculture and sustainable food production.
Role Description This Summer Research Intern role is an on-site position based in Hamel, IL. The intern will assist with field and plot preparation, planting,
crop maintenance, and data collection throughout the growing season. Daily tasks may include recording observations, measuring plant growth and development,
helping with trial evaluations, and entering data into research databases. The intern will support the research team with sample handling, basic lab or
greenhouse activities as needed, and upkeep of equipment and work areas. This internship role offers hands-on experience in agricultural research and exposure
to seed genetics and sustainable farming practices.
Qualifications
- Interest in agricultural science, plant science, agronomy, or a related field, with a desire to learn about seed genetics and crop research.
- Strong observational, data collection, and basic analytical skills to accurately record and report field trial results.
- Ability to perform hands-on outdoor work, including lifting, walking in fields, and working in varying weather conditions.
- Effective communication and teamwork skills, with the ability to follow research protocols and work collaboratively with staff.
- Organizational skills and attention to detail for maintaining records, managing tasks, and supporting multiple projects.
- Basic familiarity with Microsoft Office or similar tools for data entry and documentation; previous field or farm experience is a plus.
- Current enrollment in a high school, college, or university program; relevant coursework in agriculture or biology is beneficial.
- Commitment to safety, reliability in attendance, and a positive, curious attitude toward learning in a research environment.
