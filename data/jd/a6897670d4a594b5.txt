About Us
Scripps Research is a nonprofit biomedical institute ranked as one of the most influential in the world for its impact on innovation. We celebrated our 100-year
legacy in 2024. This significant milestone marks a century of seminal discoveries in immunology, infectious diseases (such as COVID-19, flu, HIV), neuroscience,
heart disease, cancer, and more. Located in La Jolla, California, the institute houses six research departments, multiple Nobel laureates, a top-ranked graduate
school and a leading postdoctoral training program. Scripps Research encompasses two elite and highly innovative institutes, the Calibr-Skaggs Institute for
Innovative Medicines and Scripps Research Translational Institute, which merge foundational studies in biology, chemistry and computer science with
translational science to produce pioneering drugs and advances in digital and precision medicine. Together, we cultivate new scientific leaders and expand the
frontiers of knowledge to deliver medical breakthroughs impacting human health around the globe. If you have a passion for making a difference, this could be
your opportunity to join our transformative team.
POSITION TITLE: Intern-Research, Student (Non-Paid)
Position Summary
This internship provides hands-on experience in computational neuroscience and bioinformatics within a collaborative academic research laboratory at Scripps
Research. The intern will support the analysis of single-nucleus RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq) and related genomic datasets to investigate the molecular mechanisms
underlying alcohol and drug abuse; neuroHIV; and neurological and psychiatric disorders.
The intern will receive training in data quality control, cell-type identification, differential gene expression, pathway analysis, and gene regulatory network
analysis. Depending on the project, the intern may use algorithms such as ARACNe3 and NaRnEA to investigate regulatory networks and identify potential
therapeutic targets. Through this experience, the intern will develop practical skills in programming, statistical analysis, scientific visualization, and the
broader research processes.
Previous undergraduate interns and research assistants in our laboratory have received rigorous training in scientific research, contributed to peer-reviewed
publications, and subsequently been admitted to competitive PhD and MD programs at leading institutions, including UCLA, UC Davis, Stanford, and others. This
internship provides an excellent opportunity for undergraduate students interested in neuroscience, bioinformatics, computer science, statistics, biomedical
sciences, or related fields to gain meaningful research experience and prepare for future graduate or professional training. Some experience with R or Python is
helpful; prior experience with these specialized algorithms is not required.
