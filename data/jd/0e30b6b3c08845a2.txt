Summary of Responsibilities:
The GTB Advisory Data & AI Analyst is a member of the Global Transaction Banking (“GTB”) Advisory team within BBVA’s Corporate & Investment Banking division in
New York. GTB Advisory is a multidisciplinary team that supports the broader GTB organization across three core pillars: Working Capital Advisory,
Sustainability, and Data & Artificial Intelligence.
This Summer Intern position will primarily support the Data & AI pillar. The Summer will work with large and diverse datasets, develop dashboards and analytical
tools, develop forecasting and predictive models, automate processes, and help design and implement AI-enabled solutions for GTB. The role requires strong
technical and analytical capabilities, intellectual curiosity, proactivity, and the ability to translate data and technology into practical business insights
and solutions.
While primarily focused on Data & AI, the Summer Intern will contribute to broader, interdisciplinary GTB Advisory initiatives as needed, including Working
Capital Advisory and Sustainability.
Main responsibilities:
- Support with analysis, organization, and interpretation of large and complex datasets from multiple internal and external sources
- Assist with the development, maintenance, and enhancement of dashboards and reporting tools that provide actionable insights into GTB business activity,
client behavior, product performance, revenues, opportunities, and other relevant performance indicators.
- Develop forecasting and predictive models using historical and current data to identify trends, anticipate business outcomes, and support business planning
and commercial decision-making.
- Build and maintain analytical workflows, data pipelines, scripts, queries, and applications using SQL, Google Apps Script, Excel, business intelligence
platforms (Data Studio, Tableau, etc.), and other relevant technologies. Python is a plus.
- Develop AI-enabled solutions, leveraging generative AI, machine learning, natural language processing, and other emerging technologies to address business
needs and improve existing processes.
- Partner with GTB product teams and other stakeholders to understand business challenges and translate them into data, analytics, automation, and AI solutions.
- Develop prototypes and proofs of concept, evaluate their effectiveness, and help translate successful concepts into practical tools and scalable solutions for
GTB.
Skills, knowledge, abilities/competencies:
- Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Finance, Business Analytics, or
another related field.
- Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with the ability to work with complex datasets and identify meaningful insights.
- Python and SQL knowledge is preferred.
- Advanced proficiency in Excel.
- Experience with Google Apps Script or comparable scripting and automation technologies is preferred; demonstrated ability to learn new development
environments quickly is important.
- Familiarity with generative AI and AI-enabled development or analytical tools, with an interest in applying them to real business problems.
- Exposure to machine learning, natural language processing, APIs, cloud-based data environments, or software development is a plus.
- Experience with financial datasets or knowledge of banking, corporate finance, treasury, or transaction banking is a plus
- Time management skills and flexibility to operate across multiple functions
- Proactive/self-starter: ability to perform functions in a timely and accurate manner
- Ability to translate complex analytical or technical findings into clear, concise business communication.
- Strong attention to detail, particularly when working with data, code, financial information, and client or management materials.
