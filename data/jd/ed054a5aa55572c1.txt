Job Description
Position Overview:
Are you a tech enthusiast who thrives on challenge and loves pushing the boundaries of what’s possible with AI? Do you want to be part of a team where
innovation, learning, and fun go hand in hand? We’re searching for an energetic AI Developer Intern ready to unleash creativity on real, impactful projects at
the frontier of artificial intelligence. Here, you’ll get hands-on with the latest technologies, collaborate with brilliant minds, and work on projects that are
as rewarding as they are cutting edge. If you’re eager to code, build, and solve problems while having a blast, this is the place for you!
What You'll Do
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to identify needs and uncover opportunities for AI-driven solutions.
- Develop, deploy, and optimize AI applications by writing effective scripts and code.
- Create backend scripts utilizing GraphQL to support data-driven functionalities.
- Write and maintain code to accelerate development and streamline testing processes.
- Design AI workflows incorporating both single-agent and multi-agent architectures.
- Document your development work thoroughly to ensure clarity, transparency, and continuity for the team.
- Develop automated testing frameworks based on agent vision modules to improve reliability and efficiency.
- Design evaluation processes to maintain consistency and ensure production-quality standards are met.
- Work closely with our Solution and UX Architect to deliver intelligent, user-friendly experiences.
What We're Looking For
- Detail-oriented, deadline-driven, and self-directed individuals.
- Good understanding of key AI frameworks and ability to adapt them to business problems.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- A team player who excels in a collaborative environment.
Basic Qualifications
- Junior or Senior standing by December 2025.
- GPA of 3.0 or higher.
- Pursuing a BS or MS degree in Computer Engineering, Data Science or related degrees.
- Experience with development frameworks like Vue.js, React and Node.js.
- Familiarity with the workings of LLMs, prompt engineering, context engineering and fine tuning.
Preferred Qualifications
- Has completed at least one front-end project using Vue.js or React and is able to share a demonstration.
- Has completed at least one smart application project and is able to share a demonstration.
- Familiarity with cloud DevOps or/and MLOps or/and AIOps.
Where you will be: This role is hybrid, requiring three days a week in-office at our HQ in Shakopee, MN or Fully remote in Germany as specified in the job
description. At Entrust, we have a distributed workforce.
