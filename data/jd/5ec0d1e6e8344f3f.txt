Job Summary
The Aftersales Data Analyst Intern will be responsible for cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing data sets to support decision-making across the organization.
This is an excellent opportunity for a student or soon to be graduate to gain hands-on experience working with data sets and building dashboards to inform
business decisions.
Here's what you will do in this role:
- Learn business systems and how data is created in Aftersales
- Perform ad-hoc analysis using SQL, Python, and ETL skills along with report building as the business dictates
- Generate ad-hoc queries to support Aftersales analysis requests from internal/external stakeholders
- Gather information, analyze data, and collaborate to assist with projects to support warranty reduction
- Create visualizations and reports to communicate findings to various stakeholders
- Work with Aftersales leadership to gather requirements, develop data solutions and foster data-driven decision making across the organization
Required Skills:
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively to make sound decisions
- Ability to prioritize workload in a fast-paced, changing environment
- Excellent analytical, organizational and communication skills
- Experience with data analytics, data modeling, data management, and data reporting and visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI or similar)
- Relevant experience with cloud-based data platforms (AWS, Teradata, Snowflake, Salesforce)
- Proficiency with SQL and Python programming languages
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite
Education
The candidate we seek will be graduating in 2027 or is currently enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate program in Computer Science, Data Analytics, Business
Administration, Economics, Statistics, Finance, Marketing, or related program.
