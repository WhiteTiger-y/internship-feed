As a Research & Development Engineering Co-op within the Interventional Cardiology division, you’ll support projects spanning exploratory research to new
product development, including work on next-generation single-use and capital equipment devices. This role offers hands-on experience with real-world problem
solving, design, and system-level engineering, with responsibilities tailored to your strengths. We’re looking for a motivated engineer ready to contribute
meaningful solutions in a collaborative, fast-paced med-tech environment.
Your Responsibilities Will Include
- Design, fabricate, and prototype new device concepts to address clinical feedback
- Develop new and innovative test methods for in vitro device testing
- Assist new product development teams with device design and testing
- Perform troubleshooting on new products/process problems as related to design, material, or process
- Research and analyze clinical and market data to identify new market opportunities
- At times you will be asked to work independently to plan and schedule own activities necessary to meet business timelines and provide updates to other team
members
