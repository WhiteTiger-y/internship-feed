The Costco Digital Services Division is inviting students currently enrolled in an IT or related undergraduate or graduate program at a college or university to
participate in a 12-week paid internship program during 2027. Interns will work within specific areas in Costco’s Information Systems Division. Interns will
attend presentations given by various members of Executive Management from IT and other areas of the Company. At the end of the internship period, the intern
will create and present a summary of their experience to their management and peers. Interns will be responsible for their own travel and accommodation
expenses. Housing is not provided. The program runs mid-June to early September and is located in Issaquah, WA. No out of state work allowed. All interns are
required to be in the office 5 days a week and must adhere to the CDS.
Are you excited by modern AI engineering, building with large language models, agentic systems, and AI-assisted development workflows, while grounding it in
strong software fundamentals? The InfoSec AI/ML team at Costco is looking for innovative minds to help protect the Company and its members. Our team applies big
data, automation, and AI/ML to strengthen Costco's information security posture. You'll work on modern, cloud-native applications that combine enterprise data
with applied AI, including LLM- and agent-based systems that help our analysts work faster and make better decisions. It's a chance to see how AI is designed,
built, and operated responsibly in a real production environment with meaningful data and real stakes. Our team operates on a fast-paced schedule and expects
everyone to effectively utilize AI tools while also having a comprehensive understanding and ownership of the code they write. Possessing and sharpening the
fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, system design, and writing clean, testable code) is key for succeeding in this role. As part of this team, you'll get
hands-on experience with real data science, applied AI engineering, AI-augmented software development, and cloud engineering, guided by experienced
professionals.
NOTE: While participating in the IT Internship program, an individual will be classified as an hourly temporary employee for the duration of the program. During
the program, the individual may be asked to undergo the normal interview process for a level 1 position. If the individual is offered a position, they will be
converted to the employment status matching the open job they were selected for. If they are not selected for a regular position, the internship position will
be complete in a maximum of twelve weeks.
