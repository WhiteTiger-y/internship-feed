Overview
BISSELL is looking for a motivated student interested in artificial intelligence and automation to join our Data & Analytics team. This is a hands-on learning
opportunity to explore AI technologies, build intelligent agents, and develop practical automation solutions that solve real business problems.
You'll work alongside experienced data professionals to learn modern AI development practices, experiment with large language models (LLMs), and create working
solutions that demonstrate the potential of AI in an enterprise environment. This role is ideal for students curious about AI who want to gain practical
experience building and deploying intelligent systems.
The Program will run from May 17th - August 5th 2027. We are only considering candidates with an anticipated graduation date of December 2027 or May 2028.
Responsibilities
- Learn and apply AI technologies including large language models (LLMs), prompt engineering, and API integration through hands-on project
- Build AI agents and automation workflows that assist with business processes such as data analysis, content generation, or information retrieval
- Develop working prototypes and proof-of-concept solutions that demonstrate practical AI applications for internal stakeholders
- Experiment with different AI tools, frameworks, and approaches to solve business challenges and document findings
- Collaborate with team members to understand business needs and translate them into AI-powered solutions
- Create documentation and presentations to share learnings and demonstrate project outcomes
- Contribute to the team's AI enablement efforts by exploring emerging technologies and identifying potential use cases
Qualifications
Required:
- Currently enrolled at an accredited four-year university majoring in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, or related technical field
- Must have an anticipated graduation date of December 2027 or May 2028.
- 3.0 GPA or greater preferred.
- Basic programming experience in Python or similar language
- Curiosity about AI and interest in learning new technologies
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to learn independently
- Good communication skills and comfort explaining technical concepts
- Willingness to experiment, iterate, and learn from failures
Preferred:
- Exposure to AI/ML concepts through coursework or personal projects
- Experience with APIs or web development
- Familiarity with ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI tools
- Interest in automation, data analytics, or business intelligence
- Self-starter attitude with ability to take initiative on projects
