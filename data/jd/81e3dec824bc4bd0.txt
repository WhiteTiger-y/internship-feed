UMB’s Artificial Intelligence (AI) team is responsible for driving automation, data-driven decision making and software modernization using AI centric tools.
Currently, we leverage machine learning, natural language processing, predictive analytics, and generative AI to enhance customer experience, and improve
operational efficiency. This includes utilizing fraud protection software to ensure regulatory and system availability.
We are looking for a highly motivated and curious AI Intern to join our AI team. In this role, you will work alongside senior AI Engineers and Data Scientists
to design, test, and deploy AI-driven features that solve real-world problems. This is a hands-on opportunity to move beyond theory and see how AI is integrated
into production-level products. You will gain experience with cutting-edge technologies like Large Language Models (LLMs), agentic frameworks, and data
pipelines while receiving mentorship from industry experts.
This role is hybrid (Mon through Thu on-site / Fri remote) at the UMB Technology & Operations Center located in downtown Kansas City, MO.
How you will spend your time
- Model Development & Testing Assist in building, training, and evaluating machine learning and deep learning models to improve product functionality.
- Generative AI Exploration Experiment with LLMs, prompt engineering, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines to enhance automation and user
experience.
- Data Engineering Support data collection, cleaning, and preprocessing efforts to ensure high-quality datasets for model training.
- Prototyping Collaborate with cross-functional teams to build and iterate on AI-powered prototypes and internal tools.
- Research & Documentation Stay updated on the latest AI trends and research papers, documenting experimental results and contributing to technical reports.
- Deployment Support Help integrate AI models into existing software architectures and assist in monitoring their performance in real-world scenarios.
We are excited to talk if you have
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, or a related technical field.
- Programming Strong proficiency in Python (familiarity with libraries like NumPy, Pandas, and Scikit-learn).
- AI Frameworks Basic understanding of machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or Keras.
- Core Fundamentals Solid grasp of mathematics (linear algebra, statistics) and data structures.
- Soft Skills Excellent problem-solving abilities, a proactive learning mindset, and the ability to communicate technical concepts to non-technical
stakeholders.
Bonus Points if you have
- Experience with GenAI tools like LangChain, LangGraph, or vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate).
- Prior experience with APIs (FastAPI/REST) and version control systems like Git.
- A portfolio of personal AI/ML projects or contributions to open-source communities.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
Applicants must have legal authority to work in the United States. Work Visa sponsorship is not available for this position.
