About The Role
This role provides students with the opportunity to apply AI/ML/RL concepts to real-world engineering challenges in Robotics. The ideal candidate will help
design and implement state-of-the-art learning-based algorithms and robotic systems that go beyond the limitations of traditional approaches.
Responsibilities include:
- Contribute to the development of advanced reinforcement learning algorithms (e.g., policy gradient, actor-critic, and sim-to-real transfer) for general and
dexterous robots using state-of-the-art techniques.
- Create sandbox simulations, reward designs, training environments, and evaluation frameworks for algorithm performance and robustness.
- Contribute to data requirements, data collection setup and procedure, and data curation.
- Stay abreast of the latest developments in machine learning and robotics from reputable groups.
Qualifications:
- Currently enrolled in a Ph.D. program in Computer Science or Engineering with a focus on robotics
- Strong background in machine learning and robotics, and experience in:
- Deep RL algorithms (e.g., PPO, SAC, TD3) and reward design for continuous-control robotic manipulation.
- Sim-to-real transfer techniques (e.g., domain randomization, system identification) for deploying RL policies on physical robots.
- Behavior cloning and Imitation Learning for manipulation.
- Planning and control algorithms (e.g., A*, MPC).
- Optimization techniques (e.g., linear/nonlinear optimization).
- Training deep learning models with PyTorch.
- Developing simulation environments and synthetic data generation pipelines using NVIDIA Isaac Sim or MuJoCo
Preferred:
- Familiarity with:
- Foundation models and VLA models.
- Robotics toolkits (e.g., ROS/ROS2, Gazebo)
- Robotic systems and mechanical design
