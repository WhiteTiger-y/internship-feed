Group/Division
The Semiconductor Products and Customers (Semi PC) business unit designs, builds and sells KLA’s product portfolio to help chip manufacturers meet high-quality
standards for technologies like AI, data centers, automotive and electronic devices. Our inspection, metrology, and analytics systems detect and monitor defects
across logic and memory chips, specialty semiconductors, wafers, reticles, advanced packaging, process equipment and materials. We also develop technologies for
specialty processes, IC substrates, and PCB manufacturing, including etch and deposition, component inspection, and imaging and analytics. The RAPID division
leads the industry in reticle inspection solutions, helping ensure high lithography yields and cost-effective semiconductor manufacturing. Its systems support
both mask shops and fabs by detecting defects early in the process, enabling faster, more reliable production.
What You'll Do
In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise.
Responsibilities
- Develop Python-based software and AI/ML prototypes that combine analytical models with image-based diagnostics.
- Prepare representative image and signal datasets and assess selected metrics: resolution, noise, spectral content, sampling and aliasing, uniformity and
contrast, and optical or system aberrations.
- Evaluate prototypes quantitatively against analytical baselines and expert assessments; document accuracy, uncertainty, and failure cases.
- Identify performance-limiting parameters and likely contributors to degradation, and recommend calibration actions for human review.
- Build reproducible code, tests, and technical documentation; collaborate with engineers using approved data and AI tools while protecting proprietary
information.
- Explore AI-assisted troubleshooting, engineering analysis, or workflow automation with your mentor to support the core diagnostics work.
Preferred Qualifications
- Exposure to image or signal processing, optics, imaging systems, or calibration methods.
- Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn, and familiarity with software testing, version control, or reproducible experimentation.
- Familiarity with LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, AI coding assistants, or workflow automation is helpful but not required.
Minimum Qualifications
Required qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a BS, MS, or PhD program in computer science, electrical engineering, applied mathematics, physics, or a related discipline.
- Proficiency in Python and scientific computing, with foundational knowledge of machine learning.
- Experience analyzing data through coursework, research, or projects; strong problem-solving skills and clear written and verbal communication.
