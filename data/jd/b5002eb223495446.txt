About the Role
DriveWealth is exploring how AI can change the way we onboard, support, and serve our people as we scale. As an AI Consulting Associate on the People team, you
will act as an internal consultant, analyst, and builder for focused People projects, using AI tools to scale support and create a consistent experience for our
employees and internal client groups.
You will map how work gets done today, measure a baseline, build and pilot two working tools, and hand off a repeatable process the People team keeps using. You
will partner with HR Business Partners, People Operations specialists, and managers, and present your results to People and business leadership in August.
Program Details
Our summer program will be 10-12 weeks from mid-May - August 2027 with the expectation of being in the office 3-4 days per week, as determined by the sponsor.
Compensation will be set at an hourly rate of $35-40 per hour. Top performing associates may have the opportunity to be considered for full-time roles at
DriveWealth at the conclusion of the program, based on performance and team needs. Must have an expected graduation date from December 2027 to December 2028.
What You’ll Do
Process Mapping and Baseline
Document how common HR workflows and employee lifecycle tasks are handled today, and identify the biggest pain points and opportunities for automation and AI
assistance.
For each opportunity, establish a baseline and agree on a target with your sponsor, so we can tell whether what you build worked.
Identify high-impact opportunities for HR optimization (we expect about two, but the number and type are driven by what you find).
Build AI Solutions
Build working solutions for the People team that combine LLMs, APIs, and automation frameworks. Solutions might be AI assistants, automated workflows,
dashboards, etc.
Focus areas may include: Onboarding/Offboarding, HR Helpdesk & Knowledge Base, Employee Lifecycle documentation (offer letters, onboarding communications, role
change letters)
Run a small pilot and measure results against your baseline.
Throughout the Program
Document what you build, including prompts, workflows, and a short playbook, so the team can maintain and extend it without you.
Use only approved AI tools, and keep personal or confidential information out of tools that are not approved.
Check for accuracy, tone, and correctness, and keep a list of topics the tools should not engage with (policy, pay, benefits, etc.)
Present results and recommended next steps to People and business leadership.
What You’ll Need
Current undergraduate or graduate student in Human Resources, Psychology, Machine Learning, Data Science, Data Analytics, or a Related Field.
Hands-on experience using AI tools (such as LLMs Claude, ChatGPT or automation tools) on a real project, which you should be ready to describe in your
application.
Strong process thinking: the ability to map a workflow, find where it breaks down, and turn an ambiguous problem into a working solution.
Clear writing, including the ability to explain policies and procedures in plain language.
Working comfort with data. Excel or Google Sheets is required; SQL or Python is a plus.
Sound judgment and discretion with confidential employee information.
Comfort working with HR partners, managers, and business leaders, and curiosity about how people, culture, and technology come together at a fast-growing
fintech.
