The Data & Digital Strategy Intern will support data modernization, analytics, automation, reporting, and AI initiatives across The Geysers geothermal
operations. This role offers hands-on experience working with operational data, business intelligence tools, databases, and emerging technologies to improve
business processes and decision-making. These paid internships will last approximately 10 weeks starting June 7th, 2027 through August 13th, 2027. This job will
be based at the Geysers power plant located at 10350 Socrates Mine Road, Middletown, CA 95461.
Key Responsibilities
• Analyze operational, engineering, maintenance, and business data.
• Develop reports, dashboards, and data visualizations.
• Support data modernization and reporting initiatives.
• Assist with data documentation, governance, and quality improvement efforts.
• Help identify opportunities to automate business processes.
• Support development and testing of reporting and analytics applications.
• Research and evaluate AI and digital technology opportunities.
• Create documentation and communicate findings to technical and business stakeholders.
Qualifications
Master's student (preferred) or Senior Undergraduate pursuing a degree in: Computer Information Systems, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Computer Science,
Software Engineering, Industrial Engineering or a related technical field.
Required Skills
• SQL
• Python
• Advanced Microsoft Excel
• Data analysis and problem solving
• Strong written and verbal communication
• Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks
Preferred Skills
• Power BI
• Microsoft Fabric
• VBA/Macros
• SharePoint
• Data modeling and database design
• AI / Generative AI tools
