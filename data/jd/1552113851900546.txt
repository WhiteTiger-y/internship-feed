Job Description:
Takeda is seeking a PhD summer research intern to evaluate and advance statistical machine learning methods for identifying clinically meaningful patient
subgroups with heterogeneous treatment responses. The primary project will systematically evaluate existing algorithms and benchmark in-house machine learning
methods against established approaches for patient subgroup identification, heterogeneous treatment effect (HTE) estimation, and uplift modeling.
The intern will design rigorous, reproducible experiments across diverse benchmark datasets and will also have the opportunity to apply selected methodologies
to Takeda clinical trial datasets, exploring their potential to identify clinically meaningful patient subgroups and inform precision medicine strategies. This
work will bridge methodological research and practical applications in drug development.
How You Will Contribute:
By the end of the internship, the intern is expected to deliver
- Algorithm review A structured review of relevant methods for patient subgroup identification, HTE estimation, and uplift modeling.
- Benchmarking framework Reproducible code and experimental protocols for comparing algorithms across datasets.
- Comparative evaluation A systematic assessment of predictive performance, treatment-effect modeling, subgroup stability, interpretability, and computational
efficiency.
- Scientific recommendations Evidence-based conclusions about the strengths, limitations, and most promising use cases of the evaluated methods.
- Applying of selected methods to Takeda clinical trial datasets.
- (Optional) Methodological extension A proposal or prototype for improving an in-house method or developing a new approach, where feasible.
- Final presentation and report A clear summary of results, conclusions, and recommendations, with potential for scientific publication.
Internship Development Opportunities:
The intern will collaborate with researchers applying advanced statistical and machine learning methods to pharmaceutical research questions. The project offers
opportunities to
- Apply methodological research to precision medicine and personalized treatment.
- Work on interpretable machine learning approaches for identifying clinically meaningful patient subgroups.
- Gain experience in reproducible algorithm benchmarking and research software development.
- Contribute to research with potential applications in clinical development and biomarker-driven treatment strategies.
- Explore opportunities for scientific publication and further methodological development.
Required Qualifications:
- Currently pursuing a PhD in Statistics, Biostatistics, Computer Science, Machine Learning, Data Science, Biomedical Informatics, or a related quantitative
discipline.
- Strong foundation in statistical modeling, machine learning, experimental design, and quantitative research.
- Proficiency in Python or R, including experience implementing, evaluating, or comparing analytical methods.
- Experience with cross-validation, model selection, hyperparameter tuning, and out-of-sample evaluation.
- Familiarity with predictive modeling methods, including classification, regression, and decision trees.
- Ability to design rigorous experiments, interpret quantitative results, and communicate methodological strengths and limitations.
- Strong analytical thinking, scientific writing, and presentation skills.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with causal inference, heterogeneous treatment effects, uplift modeling, or treatment-effect estimation.
- Familiarity with causal trees, causal forests, meta-learners, or other subgroup discovery approaches.
- Experience designing large-scale benchmarking studies across multiple datasets and algorithms.
- Prior research publications or evidence of independent methodological research.
