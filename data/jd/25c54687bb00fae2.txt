The Opportunity The Adobe Data Science team is looking for a summer intern! The team applies AI and machine learning techniques to big customer data to address
and challenge business problems, such as driving go-to-market strategies, sales prioritization, increasing product engagement and customer service improvement.
All 2026 Adobe interns will be co-located hybrid. This means that interns will work between their assigned office and home. Interns will be based in the office
where their manager and/or team are located, where they will get the most support to ensure collaboration and the best employee experience. Managers and their
organization will determine the frequency they need to go into the office to meet priorities. What You’ll Do Turn business needs into architecture options. Work
with architects and business partners to understand requirements. Then help write up solution options that weigh architecture standards, readiness,
implementation effort and total cost of ownership. Build AI tools for architecture work. Prototype AI assistants or agents that help with architecture reviews,
risk analysis and documentation, such as summarizing designs, checking them against standards and flagging gaps early. Make architecture guidance easier to use.
Apply AI to our standards, reference patterns and architecture repositories (for example, LeanIX) so teams can find and use the guidance on their own. Work
across business domains. Partner with Adobe architects across Marketing, Sales, Post-Sales and Finance to design solutions that help customers get started,
renew and get more value from Adobe. Evaluate new technology. Research and test new AI and platform technologies, such as agent frameworks, MCP integration and
AI observability tools. Share recommendations that feed into the Enterprise Architecture roadmap. Share what you learn. Present your prototypes, findings and
recommendations to architects and leaders at the end of your internship. What You Need to Succeed Currently enrolled full time and pursuing a Bachelor's or
