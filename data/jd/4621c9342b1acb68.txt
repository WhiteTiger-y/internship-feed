Your Opportunity as a Data Science Intern As a Data Science Intern on our Data Science team, you will play a key role in supporting data-driven decision-making
across sales, marketing and other business-critical processes. This is an opportunity to apply your analytical, statistical, and technical skills in a real-
world environment, directly impacting sales strategies. Location: Remote Work Arrangements: Remote In this role, you will: Collect, clean, and analyze large
datasets from multiple sources using statistical and programming techniques. Interpret complex data to generate actionable insights and effectively communicate
findings through dashboards, visualizations, and presentations. Apply statistical modeling and machine learning techniques (both supervised and unsupervised) to
solve real-world business problems in the sales domain. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support issue resolution, identify proactive improvement
opportunities, and contribute to business outcomes. Enhance your technical, analytical, and professional skills while building meaningful relationships across
the organization. What we are looking for: Enrolled in a graduate program (MS, PhD) in Statistics, Applied Mathematics, Data Science, Computer Science,
Economics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field. Strong foundational knowledge in statistical modeling, machine learning (e.g., regression,
classification, clustering), forecasting, and data analysis. Proficiency in at least one programming language used in data science (e.g., Python, R, SQL).
Familiarity with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, matplotlib, ggplot) and an ability to tell compelling stories with data. Relevant coursework
completed, including but not limited to: Statistical Methods (e.g., Applied Statistics, Regression Analysis, Bayesian Statistics) Machine Learning (e.g.,
Supervised and Unsupervised Learning, Neural Networks, Deep Learning) Programming and Data Wrangling (e.g., Python for Data Science, Data Manipulation with R,
SQL for Data Analysis) Data Visualization and Communication (e.g., Data Storytelling, Advanced Visualization Techniques) Domain-Specific or Advanced Topics
(e.g., Time Series Analysis, Forecasting, Optimization Techniques, Econometrics) Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to “connect-the-dots” across
