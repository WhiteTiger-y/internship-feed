Overview
Note: By applying to this position, your application is automatically submitted to our Summer Internship Program openings in over 150 locations throughout the
U.S
TRC is seeking highly motivated and diverse Data Science Interns for our Summer 2027 intern program. This is a paid full-time position. Anticipated duration
will be from June through August 2027 (flexible). This position has the potential to transition into a Full-Time role upon graduation. The successful candidate
will work under the supervision of Project Managers and Team Leaders and partner with engineers, scientists, and technologists.
Responsibilities
- Will assist with improving process efficiency, automating data extraction, reporting and analysis of environmental data.
- Will entail conducting research, working on client software planning and implementation projects.
- Provide support with an interest in learning and education.
- Support the preparation of reports, plans and specifications for client.
- Will be supported by leaders, mentors and teammates.
- Perform other related duties and responsibilities as necessary.
Qualifications
- Must be at least 18 years old.
- Junior, senior, or master's student pursuing PhD, Masters, Bachelor or Associate Degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science or similar or any related Science
field.
- Minimum GPA of 3.0.
- Has excellent critical thinking, verbal and written communication skills.
- Analytical background.
- Experience with Microsoft products (e.g. Excel, SharePoint, Teams, Power Automate, PowerBI, etc.).
- Python or similar, OpenAI/Claude or similar, Excel, SQL, SQL Server Management Studio, R or similar.
- Possesses solid technical and problem-solving skills.
- Self-motivated, detail orientated person.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, and PowerPoint)
- Honest, fun, hard-working and ready to learn and grow.
- Any experience with workflow mapping and/or use of Mural or similar software a plus.
