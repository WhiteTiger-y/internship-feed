Position Overview
This internship will start in January of 2027.
The Mercedes-Benz AI Experiences department is seeking an AI/ML Intern for Spring and Summer 2027 (both terms) to join the MB.Intelligence Context team. You
will build on-device long-term memory for automotive agents using small language models (SLMs), Liquid AI's multimodal LFM3-Omni model (text, image, video, and
audio inputs), and trigger-based activation, helping deliver personalized, privacy-preserving experiences in Mercedes-Benz vehicles.
Job Responsibilities
- Design and implement multimodal long-term memory (RAG) for automotive agents in resource-constrained in-vehicle environments.
- Fine-tune LFM3 models, especially LFM3-Omni-2B, and other small language models for memory, preference extraction, and function calling (SFT, LoRA/QLoRA).
- Apply reinforcement learning (e.g., DPO, GRPO) to improve memory, activation, and tool-use decisions.
- Build trigger-based activation from multimodal cabin and vehicle signals, using decision models (e.g., LFM3 d1-omni-2B) and lightweight classifiers.
- Build classifiers for turn detection, PII detection, and intent routing.
- Optimize and profile models for on-device deployment (quantization, distillation) on in-vehicle hardware.
- Build evaluation frameworks for memory, retrieval, and trigger accuracy, and present findings to Mercedes-Benz teams.
Qualifikationen
Minimum Qualifications
- Master's or higher degree, in CS/CE/EE, Math, Statistics, or related fields
- Python and PyTorch; Git-based workflows
- Fine-tuning small language models (Hugging Face Transformers, TRL, PEFT, LoRA/QLoRA)
- Familiarity with reinforcement learning for language models (DPO, GRPO, or PPO)
- Familiarity with multimodal models (text, image, video, and audio)
- Familiarity with decision models and classifiers (e.g., calibrated decision models such as LFM3 d1) RAG, embedding models, and vector search
- Model optimization (quantization, distillation) and ML evaluation methodology
- Strong collaboration and written communication skills
Preferred Qualifications
- Hands-on experience with Liquid AI models (LFM2.5, LFM3)
- Edge LLM inference (llama.cpp, LiteRT, ExecuTorch, ONNX Runtime)
- Privacy-preserving ML, agentic AI/MCP, or Android embedded systems
- Always-on perception (wake-word, voice activity, or audio/visual event detection)
