Discipline/Focus
Transportation/TPTO
Overview
Kimley-Horn is looking for Data Science students to join our Transportation Planning/Traffic Operations team in the Mesa, Arizona (AZ) office in 2027! This is
not a remote position.
Responsibilities
- Utilize technical software programs on a regular basis to analyze, produce, and QC deliverables for our clients.
- Analyzing transportation datasets such as traffic volumes, speeds, travel times, crashes, probe/connected-vehicle data, signal data, and other roadway or
mobility datasets.
- Supporting development of analytical tools, performance measures, and methods for evaluating transportation system performance using Microsoft Power BI and
other visualization tools such as web apps.
- Use Python, SQL, and other data analysis tools to clean, process, integrate, and analyze large or complex datasets and support automated workflows.
- Collaborating with engineers, planners, and other technical staff to identify opportunities where data science and automation can improve project workflows
and decision-making.
- Solve complex problems, assist project managers, and collaborate across disciplines to translate data into meaningful insights, tools, and recommendations for
clients.
- Develop familiarity with the federal, state, and local regulations, as well as Kimley-Horn’s and our client’s practices, procedures, and standards.
- Work with a variety of technical tools and platforms, which may include Power BI, Python, SQL, ArcGIS, VISSIM, Synchro, SimTraffic, and Microsoft Office.
Qualifications
- In the process of receiving a degree (Bachelors or Masters) in one of the following majors:
- Civil and/or Environmental Engineering
- Mechanical, Electrical, or Biosystems Engineering
- Engineering Technology
- Computer Science or Data Science
