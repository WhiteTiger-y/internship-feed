The Opportunity: Data & Analytics 2027 Intern
ICONIQ Capital is seeking a talented undergraduate to intern this summer within the Data & Analytics team. The team sets and executes ICONIQ’s data strategy and
maintains the firmwide reporting and data insights that leaders use to make decisions.
The Data & Analytics Intern will contribute to technical initiatives across the full development cycle, from scoping and design through testing and deployment.
The intern will solve real business problems on a modern technology stack that includes Snowflake, Tableau, and Python. The intern will take responsibility for
defined projects and collaborate with experienced Data & Analytics professionals.
Principal Responsibilities:
- Data Extraction & Transformation: Use SQL to extract, clean, and transform data from multiple sources, ensuring accuracy and relevance for business needs.
- Dashboard Development: Design and build dashboards that communicate insights and key performance indicators to stakeholders.
- Requirement Gathering & Translation: Collaborate with business teams to gather requirements and translate them into BI solutions.
- Data Analysis & Reporting: Conduct ad hoc analyses and develop reports that identify trends, patterns, and actionable insights to support strategic decisions.
- Data Pipelines: Support the design, build, and optimization of data pipelines that move data reliably across systems.
- Solution Delivery: Contribute to project scoping, design, development, testing, and deployment of solutions to real business problems.
Qualifications:
- Undergraduate student pursuing a bachelor’s degree.
- SQL experience and skills, developed through coursework, projects, or prior experience.
- Experience building business reports and dashboards. Familiarity with Tableau is a plus.
- Familiarity with data pipeline concepts and tools, including ETL/ELT, scheduling, and data validation, along with strong data management skills.
- Experience with a scripting language such as Python or R. Knowledge of Python analytical libraries such as Pandas, scikit-learn, and NumPy is a plus.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with excellent written and verbal communication.
- Self-starter who is organized, detail oriented, and quick to learn.
Location: New York
