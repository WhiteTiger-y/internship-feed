Overview: This program is designed to foster a beneficial relationship and provide growth opportunities for Vizient, our employees, and collegiate students.
Since 2000, our summer intern program has provided college undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students with practical experience that exposes them to
multiple aspects of a career field and the healthcare industry. This full-time, paid intern program offers an opportunity to accelerate academic learning, apply
classroom knowledge, and introduce students to a professional setting. Interns are assigned a supervisor to mentor/develop their business skills, as well as
monitor their work experiences. Interns are integral members of the company’s team, setting goals, working toward deadlines, and actively participating in
community service projects. The program requires the intern to commit to 10 weeks of participation in all formal training, mentoring, on-the-job learning, and
opportunities to interact with senior leaders. · Program starts June 1, 2027, and ends August 6, 2027 · Pay rate: $25/hr What we will do: We provide the tools
and training necessary to succeed, as well as provide intentional development opportunities such as: · Exposure to senior leadership to discuss the project,
strategy, and milestones. · Network opportunities with cross-functional teams to learn about various areas within the organization. · Personalized strengths
coaching. · Participate in company meetings and key culture events. · Value-added project management skills. · Intern-led community impact project and various
team-building activities outside of work. What you will do: The intern will support the Digital Connections & Collaboration team in exploring practical
applications of artificial intelligence, workflow automation, business intelligence, and digital process optimization. Working alongside leaders responsible for
community engagement, digital collaboration, reporting, and operational excellence, the intern will help identify opportunities to streamline work, improve
access to information, enhance reporting, and increase staff capacity through technology. This role will focus on practical use cases such as Microsoft Copilot,
Power BI, Power Automate, AI agents, workflow design, reporting frameworks, knowledge management, and digital engagement analytics while contributing to real
business challenges and strategic initiatives. By the end of the internship, the intern will leave behind usable artifacts such as working flows, prototypes,
dashboards, reporting frameworks, documented processes, and implementation recommendations that the team can maintain, refine, and scale after the summer. AI
Exploration & Enablement · Research and prototype practical AI use cases that improve productivity, reporting, knowledge discovery, and decision-making. ·
Explore Microsoft Copilot, GPT-based tools, AI agents, workflow orchestration concepts, and related emerging technologies. · Design and test prompts,
instructions, and workflows that help users retrieve information and complete tasks more efficiently. · Document findings, recommendations, and responsible-use
considerations for future implementation. Reporting, Analytics & Insights · Partner with leaders and key stakeholders to identify the questions group leaders
and senior leadership need answered quickly and consistently. · Support development of dashboards, reports, and KPI frameworks using Power BI, enterprise data

appropriate for business use. Process Improvement, Automation & Innovation · Prototype reporting workflows that connect adoption signals, member engagement,
