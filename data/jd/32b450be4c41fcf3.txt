We invented that. ATC’s story is written in the success of missions here on Earth and far, far into space. Our innovation extends beyond incremental change. We

inspiring change in our endless pursuit of what is next.
We are searching for a Research Intern to join our extraordinary team. In this role you will perform the research, development, and analysis of algorithms to
detect, acquire, and track objects of interest. You will apply this research to understanding system-level implications, analyzing new missions and enabling
cutting-edge technologies to make a real-world impact.
You will participate on teams implementing this understanding to develop new and novel system architectures, and evaluate performance of existing architectures.
Basic Qualifications
Currently enrolled in an accredited degree program such as physics, applied physics, applied mathematics, computer science, computer engineering, chemistry,
mechanical engineering, or electrical engineering
- Exposure to or awareness of suitable scientific/engineering disciplines related to algorithms, sensors, image processing, tracking and filtering, and modeling
& simulation of physical systems.
- Strong technical writing, presentation and overall communication skills for reports, proposals, technical presentations, and interaction with team members and
academic/industry collaborators.
Desired Skills
- Scientific, data, and information visualization; and data exploitation
- Experience with software programming: Python, C/C++, MATLAB, and/or Java
- Image and signal processing
- Artificial Intelligence / Machine learning
- Modeling and simulation
