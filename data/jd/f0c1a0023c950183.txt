Job Description
Summer intern, Digital Signal/ Imaging AI
Position Components
In this role, you will work with colleagues in the J&J Innovative Medicine R&D Data, Data Science, and AI (DDSAI) organization and partner with key businesses
across J&J Innovative Medicine to apply advanced data science and analytics to answer key research questions. As an intern, you will get the opportunity to work
on cutting-edge problems at the intersection of AI and healthcare. You will be a member of a dynamic team comprising data scientists and subject matter experts
to create and iterate on data science solutions. Projects include, but are not limited to, processing and analysis of wearable sensor and neuroimaging data,
development of machine learning models for digital biomarker discovery, validation of digital health endpoints, generation of insights from real-world
physiological and behavioral data, modeling and prediction of disease severity and progression, and discovering of disease subtypes. Interns could be located
out of our US locations in San Diego CA, San Francisco CA, Titusville NJ, Raritan NJ, Spring House PA, Cambridge MA, or out of Europe locations in Beerse
(Belgium), Leiden (The Netherlands), Zug (Switzerland) as well as remote.
Minimum Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a PhD degree in Biomedical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Neuroscience, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science, or a related
quantitative discipline.
- Strong working knowledge of AI and machine learning.
- Advanced in Python or R
- Strong skills in predictive modeling, statistics, and analysis of large datasets.
- Strong technical communication and presentation skills.
- Basic knowledge of data cleaning and data quality
Preferred Qualifications
Experience in one or more of the following:
- Experience with processing and analyzing sensor, wearable device, clinical records, and/or medical imaging data.
- Proficiency with pytorch.
- Experience with semi-supervised and/or self-supervised learning.
- Experience with Foundation Models
