DESCRIPTION AND POSITION REQUIREMENTS R&D Engineer Intern Job Description and Position Requirements: We are searching for a motivated undergraduate student to
join the Information Sciences Division, MLOps Department at the Applied Research Laboratory (ARL) at Penn State. This position will be located at a government
facility in Key West, Florida, during the Summer of 2027. The selected students will support research and development activities focused on applying data
analysis, information science, software, and security technologies to real-world government applications. The intern will work alongside engineers, researchers,
and government personnel to support the development, integration, testing, and evaluation of emerging technologies in an operational environment. Typical focus
areas include: Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) Data analytics, data mining, and visualization Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and
geospatial data analysis Relational and network analysis tools Software development, integration, and testing Information and cybersecurity, both in theory and
practice Students majoring in the following fields are encouraged to apply: B.S. in an engineering discipline B.S. in Computer Science, Data Science,
Information Science, or a related technical discipline Interest Skills/Experience areas include: Experience with or interest in programming and scripting
languages such as Python, Java, C++, or similar languages Familiarity with data analysis, data visualization, or data-processing techniques and tools Interest
in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or data-driven applications Familiarity with GIS, geospatial technologies, relational databases, or network
analysis tools Understanding of basic software development, systems integration, or cybersecurity concepts Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with an
ability to troubleshoot technical challenges Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a multidisciplinary technical team Strong written and
verbal communication skills, including the ability to document technical activities and results Current eligibility for access to classified information at the
Secret level or higher is preferred. You may also be subject to a government background investigation to upgrade clearance eligibility, if required. You will
work up to 40 hours/week over the summer. This is a paid internship opportunity, and location will be: Fully on-site located in Key West, FL. BACKGROUND
