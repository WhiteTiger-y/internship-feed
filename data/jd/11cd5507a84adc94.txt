you'll engage in a hands-on project focused on research, data analytics and trend analysis related to our airport consumer insights group. What you will be
doing: You will play a pivotal role in our annual Parking Benchmark study for the Transportation Business Unit, taking on a position that involves several

project. Your responsibilities will encompass survey programming, ensuring meticulous sample management, and executing comprehensive data analysis and
visualization. You will also actively contribute to various ad hoc studies while engaging in end-to-end research functions to provide valuable insights to our
internal clients, support other research initiatives, and participate in secondary research on pertinent topics. You will lend your experience to the analysis

be an active student in a bachelor's degree program in Marketing Research or related fields. Experience in data analysis using tools such as Excel, SPSS, and Q

interpret data and lead it to key learnings that inform strategic recommendations. Proficiency in creating clear and visually engaging PowerPoint presentations
