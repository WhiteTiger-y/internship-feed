Job Description:
The Role and Impact
As a Software Solutions Engineering PhD Intern, you will contribute to the development of integrated software solutions tailored to customer and platform needs.
Your work will involve collaborating with cross-functional teams to combine system-level understanding with application development, enabling scalable and
optimized solutions. You will also build essential skills and explore future career opportunities through hands-on experience and impactful projects that align
with Intel's business goals.
Business group
The Intel Foundry team is dedicated to driving innovation and delivering cutting-edge solutions to meet customer and platform requirements. This group focuses
on enabling Intel's mission of creating world-changing technology, contributing to advancements in hardware, firmware, and software engineering. Joining this
team means becoming part of a collaborative and forward-thinking environment that supports Intel's vision and fosters your professional growth.
Key Responsibilities
- Develop integrated software solutions by combining system-level understanding with application development.
- Assist in solution architecture design, feature prototyping, and performance analysis for diverse workloads and environments.
- Collaborate with hardware, firmware, and customer engineering teams to deliver scalable and optimized solutions.
- Refactor, consolidate, and standardize yield reports.
- Develop frameworks to optimize interconnectivity and report consolidation, enabling developer collaboration.
- Create design UI style guidelines and assets for reporting tools.
- Explore new analytic capabilities to provide additional impact on software and system performance.
Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications
- Must be pursuing a PhD in Computer Science or a related field.
- Strong computational capabilities and problem-solving skills.
- Proficiency in Python and SQL for programming, analytics, and reporting.
- Basic understanding of semiconductor defect analysis workflows.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with PDF Extension is a plus but not required.
- Knowledge of JSL and Power BI for programming, analytics, and reporting.
- Familiarity with industry trends or prior exposure to analytic tools is advantageous.
- Strong communication and teamwork skills, with a focus on collaboration across disciplines.
- Enthusiasm for learning and applying new concepts in a fast-paced and innovative environment.
This is your opportunity to shape the future of technology while gaining invaluable experience in a dynamic, collaborative environment. We are excited to see
how your expertise and passion will make an impact on our team.
