About SafeLab
SafeLab AI Engineering provides custom AI and ML solutions to enterprises worldwide.
What you’ll work on
- Scope incoming problems: define the task (detection, segmentation, keypoints, tracking, reconstruction), the metric (mAP, IoU, HOTA, Chamfer distance), and a
realistic target before building
- Train and fine-tune task-specific models, such as YOLO or DETR-family detectors, Mask R-CNN or U-Net segmentation, and keypoint models, on client datasets
that often contain only a few hundred images
- Combine learned models with classical methods where they work better: line and contour extraction, Hough transforms, homography estimation, camera
calibration, multi-view geometry
- Plan data strategy for low-data settings: annotation protocols, augmentation, synthetic data, active learning
- Deliver working prototypes with error analysis a client engineer can audit, and export models for deployment (ONNX, TensorRT)
Required
- PhD candidate in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field
- First-author paper at a peer-reviewed CV or ML venue (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, WACV, BMVC, or similar); please list it on your CV
- Experience training and evaluating vision models in PyTorch on datasets you collected or labeled
- Working knowledge of OpenCV and projective geometry
Preferred
- Experience in document image analysis or vectorization, multi-object tracking, pose estimation, 3D reconstruction, or point clouds
- Inference optimization experience (quantization, pruning, ONNX Runtime, TensorRT)
- Prior work with industry data or collaborators outside your lab
Details
- Part-time with no minimum hours; work scales with client projects and fits around your research
- Remote, or hybrid from our Irvine, CA office
