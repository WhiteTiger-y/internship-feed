Position Summary
The 2027 NBA Summer Internship is a 10-week program that runs from June 7 – August 13, 2027. The program gives participants the opportunity to learn about the
business of basketball, develop critical career skills, and contribute to meaningful projects across a range of departments in the NBA and its affiliate leagues
(WNBA & G League). In addition to on-the-job experience, interns are invited to participate in a broad array of professional development programming as well as
community and social experiences.
Interns should reflect excellent academic standing and have strong written and verbal communication skills. Additionally, we look for candidates who possess
leadership skills as demonstrated through relevant extracurricular or community activities.
More information on the Summer Internship Program experience can be found here.
Role Overview
The Artificial Intelligence Project Intern will join a specialized cohort focused on building AI products and processes that improve the NBA’s league and
commercial operations. As part of a core project team managed by NBA AI and technology leaders, this intern will support the NBA in using AI, including agentic
solutions, across fan experience, basketball operations, player health and enterprise applications. The goal is to transform workflows, improve efficiency and
raise the quality of work. Interns contribute directly to that effort.
This role is designed for students who demonstrate strong interest and experience in technology, engineering, artificial intelligence, business consulting, and
data-driven problem solving.
This role requires interns to demonstrate a foundational mastery of AI technologies in driving project execution and achieving business objectives.
Major priority areas will include the application of Artificial Intelligence to fan experience, basketball operations, player health, and other business areas
as needed.
Major Responsibilities
As an AI Project Intern, you will:
- Identify areas where AI can improve business processes and enterprise applications across the organization
- Develop and test AI-enabled prototypes that address operational needs across multiple business and basketball functions
- Support end-to-end development cycles, including problem definition, data acquisition, model creation, evaluation, and stakeholder demo
- Translate business needs into AI/ML solution frameworks under guidance from AI and data governance leaders
- Work within a project team to maintain quality, alignment, and responsible AI practices
- Evaluate and determine new AI use case implementation for current workflows or emerging initiatives
- Document methodologies, workflows, and findings to ensure transferability and scale
Projects are confirmed each spring based on league priorities. 2027 projects are expected to fall within five areas:
- Enterprise Applications: for example, building agentic and AI solutions that redesign workflows across league office functions such as Finance,
Communications, Events, HR, Legal and IT.
- Basketball Operations & Research: for example, building visual outputs for basketball, media and fans and supporting analytics for officiating, scheduling and
game operations.
- Player Health: for example, building tools that help medical and performance staff support league-wide biomechanics and player-health data programs.
- Fan Experience: for example, AI-powered personalization, recommendations and assistants across NBA platforms.
- Data Intelligence & Platform Analytics: for example, building data pipelines, retrieval and search over NBA data, and AI-assisted reporting and dashboards.
