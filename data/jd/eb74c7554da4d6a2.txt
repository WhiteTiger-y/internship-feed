About Nirmata:
Nirmata is the Creator of Kyverno — the Kubernetes-Native Policy Engine — and the innovator behind the AIControls Platform. Nirmata allows enterprises to
securely scale Kubernetes and govern autonomous software by enabling multi-cluster policy management and continuous compliance. With AIControls, we govern every
AI agent, LLM, and tool call in Kubernetes clusters, enforced by deterministic policy and logged for audit. Nirmata empowers enterprise DevOps and security
teams by fully automating the complex operations, governance, and management of cloud-native applications and AI workloads.
Job Description:
As an AI/ML Software Engineer Intern, you will define and implement the infrastructure powering Nirmata's AIControls platform. You will contribute to the
development of robust AI-governance features, ensuring secure, compliant, and cost-effective execution of AI agents across enterprise environments. A critical
part of this role involves integrating secure runtime environments, specifically leveraging OpenShell (sandboxing) to enforce deterministic runtime controls and
contain agent behavior safely. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to a rapidly growing domain at the intersection of generative AI and Kubernetes
security within a fast-moving startup.
