Internship Overview
Springs Window Fashions is looking for a motivated and hardworking individual to join our Information Technology Team! This Intern would help build a repeatable
system for measuring whether Springs' AI powered features are correct, and for catching quality regressions when the underlying model or prompt changes. This
internship provides an exciting opportunity to assist the team with project support while providing valuable work experience related to your chosen field of
study. Our paid internship program is known for having in-depth, challenging project work with cross-functional teams, as well as professional development, job
shadowing and other growth opportunities.
Internship Responsibilities
- AI Feature Validation Harness
- Golden test sets (100–300 expert-reviewed cases per feature)
- Scoring logic (deterministic checks + calibrated model-as-judge + human spot-check)
- On-demand test runner, triggered manually or on model/prompt change
- Trend dashboard (pass rate by category, over time, diff between runs)
- Playbook for adding features and setting release gates
- Named permanent owner and one live workflow gated by the suite

Requirements
What We’re Looking For
- Pursuing a 4-year degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, or a related technical field; Junior or Senior standing preferred
- Previous internship or coursework involving data handling, scripting, or software development preferred
- Working proficiency in Python (or a comparable scripting language) for building test cases and automation — this project is data/scripting-heavy, not a
specialized ML role
- Familiarity with, or willingness to quickly learn, SQL and basic data analysis concepts
- Proficient computer skills, including Microsoft Office
- Strong problem-solving and structured, methodical thinking — the core of this role is designing test cases that actually catch failures, not just writing code
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including comfort conducting structured interviews with non-technical Associates to extract precise
workflow requirements — this is roughly 60% of the actual work and the top screening priority
- Comfortable with ambiguity and an iterative, build-as-you-learn scope, rather than a fully pre-specified task list
- Interest in AI/ML applications is a plus; prior AI/ML coursework is not required
- Positive attitude, high attention to detail, and eagerness to learn Springs' business processes
