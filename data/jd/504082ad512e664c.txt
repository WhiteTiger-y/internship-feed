Role Overview and Core Responsibilities Conduct a research project independently or as part of a team, under the supervision of a senior member of the Data
Science & Analytics team. Review the proposed research topic and generate a problem-solving methodology in collaboration with the supervisor. Design an
appropriate testing environment to measure applicability of the proposed methodology to the needs of the business unit and compare with alternative approaches,
as appropriate. Create software code necessary to produce findings and support project execution. Carefully document all sources and research findings,
including preparing a summary of findings in an internal white paper for senior management. Present summary of findings to research sponsors and conduct
training for other associates as appropriate. Provide support for Data Science & Analytics projects beyond the scope of the research project, as needs arise.
