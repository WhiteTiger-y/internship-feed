The Role
This is a full-time winter-spring Co-Op based in Cambridge, MA from January 18th- June 25th, 2027 . Candidates must be available for the entire duration of the
program.
Moderna’s Research Analytics department is seeking a talented and motivated Co-Op to join our team supporting biological assay development for Research and
Early Development. The Co-Op will lead a mentored research project focused on the development, optimization, and miniaturization of a multiplex Luminex assay
for preclinical antibody-binding assessment.
The project will provide hands-on experience across the assay development lifecycle, progressing from antigen-bead conjugation and single plex assay development
through multiplex optimization, assay characterization, and miniaturization from 96-well to 384-well format. The Co-Op will ultimately compare the newly
developed 384-well Luminex assay with an established 384-well indirect ELISA to evaluate analytical performance, biological agreement, and sample conservation.
This role provides an opportunity to develop practical experience in immunoassay development, multiplex technology, experimental design, data analysis, assay
miniaturization, and scientific decision-making. The goal is for the Co-Op to progressively develop the skills needed to interpret experimental results,
troubleshoot assay performance, propose next steps, and make data-supported recommendations.
Applicants should be curious, collaborative, detail-oriented, and motivated to learn in a dynamic research environment.
Here’s What You’ll Do
- Support the development and optimization of a multiplex Luminex immunoassay for preclinical antibody-binding assessment.
- Gain hands-on experience with antigen-bead conjugation, single plex and multiplex assay development, and assay characterization.
- Help miniaturize a 96-well Luminex assay into a 384-well format and evaluate assay performance following scale-down.
- Design and execute laboratory experiments, analyze results, troubleshoot assay performance, and use findings to guide subsequent experiments.
- Compare the newly developed Luminex assay with an established 384-well indirect ELISA to evaluate analytical performance and biological agreement.
- Assess the potential sample-sparing and operational benefits of multiplexing for sample-limited preclinical studies.
- Maintain clear experimental documentation and communicate results through team discussions, data reviews, and a final project presentation.
- Work closely with a scientific mentor while developing increasing independence in experimental design, data interpretation, and scientific decision-making.
Here’s What You’ll Need (Minimum Qualifications)
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program in a STEM field (e.g., Biology, Biochemistry, Immunology, Molecular Biology, Bioengineering,
Biotechnology, or related discipline).
- Foundational laboratory skills applicable to bioprocessing or analytical testing.
