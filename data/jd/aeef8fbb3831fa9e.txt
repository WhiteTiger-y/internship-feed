Summary
The Data Science & AI/ML Intern joins our Data Science team to tackle real business challenges using machine learning, optimization, and Generative AI, with
some exposure to MLOps.
This hands-on role lets you work alongside Data Scientists and cross-functional business and technology teams. You will own project deliverables and present
your work and recommendations to leadership.
General Responsibilities
- Analyze large datasets to identify insights and business opportunities.
- Develop analytical and machine learning solutions using Python and SQL.
- Support machine learning, forecasting, optimization, and Generative AI/LLM use cases.
- Build, test, and enhance ML/MLOps pipelines and prototypes.
- Gain exposure to cloud technologies including GCP, BigQuery, and Vertex AI.
- Work under the mentorship of Data Scientist and partner with Data Engineering, Product, Supply Chain, Merchandising, Operations, and other business teams.
- Present project outcomes and recommendations to D&A and business leaders.
Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a bachelor's or master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Operations Research, Applied Mathematics, Industrial
Engineering, or a related quantitative field.
- Rising or Current Senior or master’s student at the time of application (Expected Graduation between December 2027 – May 2028).
- Strong foundation in Python, SQL, statistics, and machine learning.
- Familiarity with Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow, or similar packages.
- Exposure to Generative AI/LLMs, MLOps, optimization, GCP, or other cloud platforms is a plus.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and presentation skills.
- Ability to navigate ambiguity, learn quickly, and collaborate across technical and business teams.
What You'll Gain
- Hands-on experience solving real business problems with Data Science and AI.
- Exposure to modern AI/ML, Generative AI, MLOps, and cloud technologies.
- Mentorship from experienced Data Scientists and Data Science leadership.
- Cross-functional experience working with technology and business teams.
- Opportunity to present your work to leadership.
- Potential consideration for future full-time opportunities based on performance and business needs.
