Role Description
This is a remote internship role for a Data Analyst Internship And Training Program. The role will involve day-to-day tasks related to data analytics, including
data management, data analysis, data modeling, and developing analytical skills. The intern will have the opportunity to work on real-world data projects and
gain practical experience in the field of data analytics.
Resposibilities
- Work closely with our esteemed data science team to gather, refine, and analyze extensive datasets, refining skills in data preprocessing and visualization.
- Apply machine learning and data mining methods to derive insights from large datasets, including identifying Excel interface structures.
- Utilize Python programming for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) in practical business contexts, with a focus on risk analytics in banking and finance.
- Develop scientific tests and optimize model performance using cutting-edge modeling technologies such as no-code databases and data mining.
- Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team to construct machine learning pipelines for various applications in commerce, finance, and procurement.
- Engage with internal stakeholders to explore and comprehend business data, develop hypotheses, and evaluate strategies using techniques like T-tests.
- Address specific challenges and devise innovative solutions by leveraging modeling technologies such as bike share systems.
- Acquire and apply advanced analytical techniques using tools like Tableau.
- Create reports and presentations to effectively convey data analysis methodologies and findings.
Qualifications
- Strong problem-solving and critical thinking abilities
- Currently pursuing any degree showcasing a strong commitment to continuous learning and professional growth.
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills
- Ability to work independently and remotely
