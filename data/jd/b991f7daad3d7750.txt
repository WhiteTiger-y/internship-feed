A Taste of What You’ll Do
Are you passionate about Data Analytics? Elevate your career by joining our Winegrowing Data Analytics Internship Program, where you’ll collaborate with leading
scientists and technical experts at our state-of-the-art production sites. This internship offers an unmatched experience in technical training and professional
development, allowing you to contribute to a range of corporate initiatives and research projects. Embrace this opportunity to refine your skills and grow with
us—apply now!
In this role, you'll enhance your data analytics abilities by engaging with a diverse project portfolio and collaborating with cross-functional teams such as
Viticulture, Chemistry, Enology, Micro and Systems Biology, and Winemaking. You will play a crucial part in understanding and documenting the inputs and outputs
of data to support ongoing development. Your expertise will be instrumental in creating dashboards that empower our partners to make informed, data-driven
decisions.
Additionally, you will mine data to identify patterns for predictive insights, modeling both structured and unstructured data as you contribute to the
development of machine learning and other advanced analytical models. This internship is designed to challenge and inspire you, providing a platform for
innovation and creativity.
We are committed to fostering an inclusive environment that values diverse perspectives and experiences. We welcome individuals from all backgrounds to join our
team, where your unique contributions will be celebrated and nurtured. Become part of a community that thrives on collaboration and excellence, and take the
next step in your career with us. Apply today to start your journey in revolutionizing the future of winegrowing through data analytics.
