Join the Enterprise Pricing Strategy team for a hands-on, 10-week internship focused on strengthening the data foundation behind pricing and commercial
decisions. In a collaborative, curious, and results-oriented environment, you will work with pricing and financial data, partner with team members and
organizational stakeholders, and apply analytics to a defined business question. Your work will progress from assessing and improving data quality to
recommending sustainable governance practices and presenting actionable insights to the Pricing team and corporate leadership.
Interns at Jack Henry earn $25.00 per hour and work a full-time schedule of 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday.
The Summer 2027 Internship Program will run from June 7 through August 6, 2027.
What you’ll be responsible for:
- Assess and improve pricing and deal data by identifying inconsistencies, duplicates, gaps, and standardization opportunities, then executing a structured
cleanup effort.
- Analyze current data definitions, classifications, and sources; document key data elements; and recommend standards that support ongoing governance and future
reporting.
- Use the improved dataset to analyze pricing, discounting, deal, or customer trends and present findings and recommendations to the Pricing and Corporate
leadership.
What you’ll need to have:
- Pursuing a degree in Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Finance, Economics, Statistics, Information Systems, Data Science, Business Administration, or a
related field.
- Demonstrated foundational knowledge or early experience with data analysis, data quality, data visualization, statistics, or business problem-solving,
including proficiency with Microsoft Excel and familiarity with tools such as Power BI, SQL, Python, or similar analytics technologies.
- Must be a U.S. citizen and not require sponsorship for employment authorization now or in the future.
What would be nice for you to have:
- Strong analytical curiosity and attention to detail, with the ability to investigate root causes, recognize patterns, and translate data into clear business
insights.
- Effective written and verbal communication, collaboration, organization, and time-management skills, with an interest in financial technology, pricing
strategy, or data governance.
