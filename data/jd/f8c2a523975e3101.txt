Undergraduate Intern-R&D (Oncology Research) (Summer 2027)
What You Will Do
Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. During this program, you will engage in Oncology discovery research to develop and evaluate innovative approaches for
characterizing therapeutic candidates while generating decision-enabling data to support early-stage screening and candidate prioritization.
Key Responsibilities:
- Design and execute experiments to characterize target biology and evaluate therapeutic candidate activity
- Develop and optimize in vitro assays to evaluate assay sensitivity, reproducibility, and performance
- Analyze and interpret results and keep detailed records using an electronic notebook
- Summarize and present findings during lab meetings and at an internal symposium
Program Eligibility Requirements
- Expected completion of one year of study from an accredited college or university prior to the internship commencing
- Expected continued enrollment in an accredited college or university following the internship program
- Student must be located in the United States for the duration of the internship program
- Must be available to work a full-time schedule.
What We Expect of You
We are all different, yet we all use our unique contributions to serve patients. We’re seeking a highly motivated, creative and curious summer intern with these
qualifications:
Basic Qualifications:
Amgen requires that all individuals applying for an undergrad internship or a co-op assignment at Amgen must meet the following criteria:
- 18 years or older
- Currently enrolled in a full-time Bachelor’s Degree program from an accredited college or university with a 3.0 minimum GPA or equivalent
Preferred Qualifications
- Degree concentration in Molecular/Cell Biology, Biochemistry, Immunology, or similar
- Familiarity with wet lab workflows, including mammalian cell culture and aseptic technique
- Strong communication and presentation skills
