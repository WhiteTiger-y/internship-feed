As An Intern, You’ll Participate In a Summer Internship Program To Develop Competencies In a Variety Of Data Science, Machine Learning, And Predictive Analytics
Specializations That May Include
- Collaborating closely with software engineers, analysts, and data scientists to understand data requirements and deliver robust, integrated solutions.
- Architecting and developing custom, maintainable data science solutions from the ground up.
- Building high-performance statistical and machine learning tools to derive insights from complex data sets.
- Performing deep data modeling and abstracting reusable patterns for long-term scalability.
- Writing modular, maintainable code and contributing to tests and documentation for data architectures, workflows, and processes.
- Applying and championing software engineering best practices in the data domain, including unit/integration testing, CI/CD automation (e.g., Git, Docker), and
strong documentation.
- Tuning and optimizing complex algorithms within an on-premise infrastructure for performance and reliability.
