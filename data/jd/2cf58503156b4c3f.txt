Job Description: Summary: The Summer College Internship Program is a 10-week paid internship experience that provides undergraduate and graduate students with
the opportunity to attend development workshops, network, and be part of our company. Our college interns work on meaningful projects, are mentored under their
respective managers, and continue to develop throughout the entire summer. Through the internship program, our interns can attend development workshops that are
focused on key business concepts. Interns may work within the skills-based tracks of Business Administration, Information Technology, Actuarial, Human
Resources, Analytics & Data, Communications, Finance, Marketing & Sales, Project Management, Clinical Studies, Customer Experience, Digital Operations, and
other non-technical and/or technical based degree programs. This is a paid 10-week summer internship from the first Monday in June to the second Friday in
August (up to 40 hours per week). Interns may reside in either a technical or non-technical area. Development workshops are provided to help with the transition
from school to the corporate environment. Housing is not provided as part of our program. Essential Accountabilities: Specific duties will vary by assignment;
specific divisional callouts are listed below. The intern will gain hands-on experience supporting enterprise Records and Information Management initiatives.
Key responsibilities may include assisting with the operationalization of retention policies across SharePoint sites, supporting Information Enterprise
Repository (IER) content ingestion activities, and participating in RIM program governance and oversight processes. Additional opportunities include supporting
operational reviews, developing training and awareness materials, assisting with file migration efforts, creating team communications, documenting procedures,
and helping track program metrics and compliance activities. Through these projects, the intern will gain exposure to records management, information
governance, Microsoft 365, data lifecycle management, and organizational change management practices. Works with a mentor and supports key strategic projects.
Completes work assignments based on business needs, departmental priorities, and individual qualifications. Performs a mix of technical and/or non-technical
tasks that will cover a broad range of business-related projects. Creates and delivers presentations at completion of internship. Consistently demonstrates high
standards of integrity by supporting the Lifetime Healthcare Companies' mission and values, adhering to the Corporate Code of Conduct, and leading to the
Lifetime Way values and beliefs. Maintain confidentiality and protection of member privacy in accordance with the company policies. Regular and reliable
attendance is expected and required. Performs other functions as assigned by management. Minimum Qualifications: Desired Majors/Degree Programs Include:
Information Systems, Information Science, or Business, Cybersecurity, Legal or Pre-law, Data Management or Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS)

(i.e. Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT Networking, Cybersecurity, etc.) ;Mathematics & Data Science (i.e. Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science, etc.)
or similar program(s) Preferred (but not required) experience with: Microsoft 365, SharePoint, project work, documentation, training development, process
improvement, and student leadership activities is desirable. Candidates should have an interest in information governance, records management, data lifecycle
management, compliance, and emerging areas such as AI governance and digital transformation. High School diploma along with proof of continuous college
enrollment (enrolled in a full-time undergraduate or graduate degree program). Must be in good academic standing. Excellent written and verbal communication
skills, with ability to interact with technical and non-technical audiences and interface with staff at all levels of the company. Ability to multitask in a
fast-paced environment while maintaining strong attention to detail changing environment and deliver high-quality work product. Ability to work well in a team
establishing good working relationships with peers. Demonstrates strong organizational, analytical, problem-solving and prioritization skills. Proactive
