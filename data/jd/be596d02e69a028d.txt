DESCRIPTION AND POSITION REQUIREMENTS Your working location will be fully on-site located in University Park, PA. The All-Domain Analytics and Signatures Office
(A2SO) is seeking multiple highly motivated student interns to join our team as a Research & Development Engineer Intern. This internship provides a unique
opportunity to explore and experiment with emerging AI capabilities within A2SO’s research domains. The selected interns will work closely with A2SO teams to
understand available tools, evaluate AI opportunities and limitations, and design an innovative project that advances A2SO’s use of artificial intelligence in
research and operational workflows. ARL is an authorized DoD SkillBridge partner and welcomes all transitioning military members to apply. You will: Learn and
evaluate existing AI-related tools and resources available within A2SO. Collaborate with project teams to help them understand how to access and effectively use
these tools. Assess and clearly communicate the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and risks of using AI in A2SO research areas. Develop and execute an
independent research project that experiments with AI integration into current or future A2SO efforts. Present findings, recommendations, and prototype results
to A2SO leadership and project teams at the conclusion of the internship. Preferred Skills/Experience areas include: Candidates will ideally have experience or
exposure in one or more of the following areas: Operational AI implementation Data analytics using AI systems or approaches Applied data science in research or
technical environments AI-enabled technology development or optimization Agentic AI integration or related experimentation Who Should Apply This role is ideal
for students who are curious, innovative, and excited about pushing the boundaries of AI in applied research. Students from engineering, data science, computer
science, information sciences, or related fields are strongly encouraged to apply. Duration: 10 weeks, full-time (40 hours/week) with possibly follow-on in the
Fall Semester Start Date: May 2027 ARL’s purpose is to research and develop innovative solutions to challenging scientific, engineering, and technology problems
in support of the Navy, the Intel Community (IC), and other federal government customers. FOR FURTHER INFORMATION on ARL, visit our website at www.arl.psu.edu.
