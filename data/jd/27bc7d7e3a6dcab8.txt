Job Description
Visa's Internship Program offers a 12-week immersive experience where you'll work on impactful projects that advance Visa's mission. As a Visa intern, you'll
build meaningful connections, strengthen your communication and business skills, and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic, global environment.
You'll have access to interactive workshops and learning sessions designed to build expertise, expand industry knowledge, and elevate your professional skills.
You'll not only learn, you'll contribute, collaborate, and innovate. Beyond professional development, enjoy social events that foster community and connection.
The program culminates in a final presentation, where you'll share your project achievements and recommendations with Visa leaders-a chance to demonstrate
impact and growth.

About The Quantum Computing And Information Team
Our mission is to harness quantum computing and information technologies to enable breakthroughs in computation, security, and data analytics. Our research
focuses on:
- Quantum algorithms and applications
- Quantum machine learning
- Quantum optimization
- Quantum cryptography
- Tackle complex challenges in fraud detection, risk modeling, secure transactions, and large-scale financial data analysis
- These efforts empower Visa and its partners to maintain leadership in efficiency, security, and innovation across global payments
