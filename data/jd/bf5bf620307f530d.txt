What We Are Looking For
The BOLT (Building Opportunities & Learning Together) Intern Program is Kinder Morgan’s elite summer internship program. Do you want to spend your summer
gaining hands-on experience in the energy industry while enhancing your professional and interpersonal skills?  At Kinder Morgan, you will have the opportunity
to do just that by participating in meaningful projects that support our vision of delivering energy to improve lives and create a better world.
Kinder Morgan is one of the largest energy infrastructure companies in North America. We have an interest in or operate approximately 78,000 miles of pipelines
and 136 terminals. Our pipelines transport natural gas, gasoline, crude oil, carbon dioxide (CO 2 ) and more. Our terminals store and handle renewable fuels,
petroleum products, chemicals, vegetable oils and other products. We are a market leader in each of our businesses – Natural Gas Pipelines, Products Pipelines,
CO2, and Terminals. We have an unparalleled, large footprint of diversified and strategically located assets that are core to North American energy
infrastructure and help deliver needed energy products to high-demand markets.
What You Will Do
An ESG intern will contribute to the ESG department and will learn about midstream energy sustainability topics; data management, data analytics, and testing;
and working across multiple departments within a large company. At the end of the BOLT internship, the intern will be required to present a summary of their
summer to the management of the department. This will be an opportunity for the intern to demonstrate what they have learned throughout their internship and
provide insight on their experience to senior business leaders and key stakeholders.
The internship opportunities will be focused in one or more of the following areas:
- Collecting ESG-related data from multiple departments;
- Evaluating the collected data using Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QAQC) methods, determining if data is accurate and complete;
- Using IT systems, disclose and describe the collected data in the annual sustainability report and assist with the preparation of the annual sustainability
report for publications;
- Developing and implementing efficiencies using technology to improve data collection and data analysis;
- Benchmarking company sustainability efforts against peers;
- Conducting gap analyses for new or changing regulatory requirements or changing voluntary sustainability standards; and
- Assisting with the ESG ratings company engagements.
What You Need
POSITION REQUIREMENTS:
- Working towards Bachelor’s or Master's degree in data science, sustainability, engineering, environmental sciences.
- Minimum GPA: 3.0
- Demonstrated experience leading teams of people, either during school, work, or personal life - candidate must describe in detail during the interview
- Self-motivated and driven, with demonstrated interpersonal skills
- Ability to function effectively in both a team and individual environment
- Attention to detail and strong computer skills are essential
- Driven to discover and determined to deliver
- Open to travel in the United States
- Houston Headquarters Work Location
The above statements describe the general nature and level of work being performed. This position may perform other duties as assigned.
The successful candidate will be required to clear a drug screen and a complete background check, after an offer has been extended and prior to being employed.
DESIRED MAJORS
- Data Science, Sustainability, Engineering, Environmental Sciences
