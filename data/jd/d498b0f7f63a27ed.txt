About the role:
As a Fuel Engineering Data Analyst intern, you will design and develop solutions and tools to enhance our operations, support project management processes, and
interface with internal functional groups to perform work assignments.
You will report to the GNFE Transformation & Growth Manager and be located at our Cranberry, PA facility. This is a paid full-time hybrid position for the
summer, May - August 2027.
Key Responsibilities:
- Operational Excellence: Ensure the smooth operation of business-critical workflows and functions
- Solution Development: Design and develop innovative solutions and tools to enhance operational efficiency
- Project Support: Support project management processes for several internal projects
- Collaboration: Work with internal functional groups to complete work assignments and support cross-functional projects
Qualifications:
- Pursuing a bachelor's degree in an engineering discipline
- Completed Sophomore year of college
- Proficiency in data analysis tools and software
We are committed to transparency and equity in all of our people practices. The hourly rate for this position, which is dependent upon experience,
qualifications and skills, is estimated to be $25.40 to $29.00 per hour.
