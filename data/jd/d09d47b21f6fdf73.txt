Overview
Research Internships at Microsoft provide a dynamic environment for research careers with a network of world-class research labs led by globally-recognized
scientists and engineers, who pursue innovation in a range of scientific and technical disciplines to help solve complex challenges in diverse fields, including
computing, healthcare, economics, and the environment.
The AI & Human Systems group at Microsoft Research New York City (MSR NYC) and Microsoft Research New England (MSR NE) is seeking multiple Research Interns.
AI & Human Systems brings together researchers and applied scientists who have been part of the Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics in AI (FATE),
Sociotechnical Alignment Center (STAC), Economics and Computation , and Computational Social Science (CSS) groups. This posting is for candidates interested in
working with researchers and applied scientists across any of these areas. Group members collaborate extensively with one another and with other groups across
Microsoft.
The group shares a common goal: a world where AI boosts productivity, accelerates innovation, and helps people flourish by focusing their attention, enhancing
their capabilities, and safeguarding their agency. Research Interns will contribute to this goal by working with mentors on research that advances it.
Our research draws on diverse disciplinary perspectives to study, measure, and design AI systems and understand their effects on people, teams, organizations,
markets, economies, and societies. Topics include, but are not limited to: AI measurement and evaluation; measuring the impacts of AI on individuals,
organizations, and society; human–AI interaction and meaningful human engagement with AI; and the design of agentic systems, markets, and institutions that
shape how AI participates in the economy and society.
Applicants should demonstrate an interest in studying AI and human systems, a desire to work in a highly interdisciplinary environment, and experience using AI
to thoughtfully enhance their own research. We welcome applicants pursuing a doctoral degree in technical fields (e.g., machine learning, artificial
intelligence, natural language processing, statistics, computer science), economics, computational social science, or sociotechnical fields (e.g.,
human–computer interaction, information science, law, linguistics, science and technology studies) as well as JD candidates. Applicants should be proficient
with AI tools.
Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis. We recommend submitting by December 1, 2026.
Responsibilities
Alongside fellow doctoral candidates and some of the world’s best researchers, Research Interns learn, collaborate, and network for life. Research Interns not
only advance their own careers, but they also contribute to exciting research and development strides. During the 12-week internship, Research Interns are
paired with mentors and expected to collaborate with other Research Interns and researchers, present findings, and contribute to the vibrant life of the
community. Research internships are available in all areas of research, and are offered year-round, though they typically begin in the summer.
