Job Description:
The Role and Impact
As an AI Software Engineering PhD Intern, you will contribute to the design, development, and optimization of cutting-edge AI software solutions across domains
such as computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning. You will implement and tune AI models for performance and accuracy, explore applied research
opportunities, and engage in hardware-software integration to enable scalable and efficient AI solutions. Your work will directly support Intel's technological
advancements, giving you hands-on experience to develop skills while contributing to impactful projects in a collaborative environment.
Business group
Intel Foundry is dedicated to delivering world-class manufacturing and technology capabilities that support Intel's mission to create smart, connected systems.
The group fosters innovation across hardware and software domains, driving advancements that enable efficient and scalable computing solutions. As part of this
group, you will work on initiatives that enhance Intel's ability to deliver transformative products and solutions to customers worldwide.
Key Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize machine vision models for processing and analyzing image data.
- Build statistical models to assess and predict risk and impact experienced by semiconductor wafers.
- Apply machine learning and data science techniques to solve real-world manufacturing and engineering problems.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to analyze data, derive insights, and improve process efficiency.
Qualifications
You must possess the below minimum qualifications to be initially considered for this position. Preferred qualifications are in addition to the minimum
qualifications and are considered a plus factor in identifying top candidates. Experience listed below would be obtained through a combination of your
schoolwork, classes, research, relevant previous job, and/or internship experiences.
Minimum Qualifications
- Must be currently pursuing a PhD degree in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Statistics, or a related field
- Proficiency in Python programming and machine learning techniques.
Preferred Qualifications
- GPA 3.0
- Experience with data analysis and visualization.
- Knowledge of statistical analysis and modeling techniques.
- Experience with machine vision and image processing.
- Familiarity with machine learning and statistical modeling of time-series data.
