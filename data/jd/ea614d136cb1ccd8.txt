About GlobalFoundries GlobalFoundries (GF) is a leading manufacturer of essential semiconductors, enabling AI at scale from the cloud to the physical world.
Through deep partnerships with customers, GF delivers differentiated, power‑efficient and high‑performance solutions for high‑growth markets. GF is a trusted
and holistic technology partner for customers around the world. GF's talented, global team remains focused every day on security, longevity and sustainability.
For more information, visit www.gf.com. Internship Overview: Our summer internships give students hands-on experience on real projects in a fast-paced
semiconductor manufacturing environment. Interns work alongside experienced engineers and data scientists, receive day-to-day mentorship, and build networking
connections and practical skills they can carry back to school and into their careers. Summary of Role: GlobalFoundries is seeking a summer intern with a data
science background to join the Digital Manufacturing team and help develop data-driven solutions for semiconductor manufacturing. Working under the guidance of
a Digital Manufacturing mentor and alongside Fab teams, the intern will help prepare manufacturing datasets, apply statistical and machine learning methods,
prototype AI and agentic tools for a well-scoped project, and share findings with the team at the end of the summer. Essential Responsibilities include: Work
with a Digital Manufacturing mentor to scope a summer project with clear deliverables, milestones, and success measures; document code, methods, and results so
the work can be reused after the internship. Learn how manufacturing and engineering teams use data in their daily work and, with mentor support, help translate
their questions into focused analyses. Collect, clean, and prepare datasets from multiple sources; apply statistical analysis, data visualization, and machine
learning techniques to identify patterns, trends, and improvement opportunities. Help build and test prototypes of analytics or AI/agentic tools for a real
manufacturing use case, and present findings, limitations, and recommendations to the team in a final internship presentation. Other Responsibilities: Perform
all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and programs. Required Qualifications:
Education: Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Industrial Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer
Engineering, or a related field from an accredited degree program, and entering junior or senior year in Fall 2027. Completed coursework in programming (Python
or R or similar) and introductory statistics or probability. Must have at least an overall 3.0 GPA and proven good academic standing. Language Fluency: English
(Written & Verbal) Preferred Qualifications: Coursework, class projects, research, or hackathon experience involving data analysis, statistics, machine
learning, or data visualization. Familiarity with Python data tools (such as pandas, NumPy, or scikit-learn), R, or SQL; exposure to Jupyter notebooks or Git is
a plus. Strong interest in leveraging AI tools and emerging technologies to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and support effective decision-making.
Curiosity and eagerness to learn, with the ability to work through open-ended problems with guidance. Good written and verbal communication skills. Good
planning and organizational skills. Prior internship, research, or leadership experience (school organizations, team projects, competitions, etc.) is a plus but
