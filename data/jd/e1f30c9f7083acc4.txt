Company Description
See how Epsilon powers connected marketing by unlocking the full potential of data and identity.
Overview
Epsilon is seeking an Identity Analytics Intern to support our Identity Analytics Team. As an Identity Analytics Intern, you’ll join a team that owns the
analytics of CORE ID. Your role will be exposed to analyses that measure and explain CORE ID performance, prepare Epsilon for third-party identifier
deprecation, and support the globalization of CORE ID. You will also partner with the team that creates CORE ID, Product and Engineering, and other Analytics
teams to perform exploratory analyses and proof of concepts for methods under exploration.
Epsilon's Internship Program
As an intern you’ll participate in a formal Intern Program, which is an 11-week summer program that aims to create a meaningful early career experience for
university students. The program offers professional development & hands-on training, networking opportunities with people throughout the business including
multiple projects & social events, and the consideration for full-time employment.
Responsibilities
This candidate will develop scenario analyses to meet business objectives based on various identity strategies. Additionally, you’ll better understand how CORE
ID impacts from ecosystem perspective, to ultimately contribute insights and planning for anticipated advertising ecosystem changes.
Qualifications
What you’ll bring with you:
- Diligent, able to multi-task and prioritize in a fast-paced environment driven by measurable results
- Ability to present analytics findings using easy-to-understand visualizations
- Strong analytic thought process and ability to interpret findings
Why you might stand out from other talent :
- Pursuing Bachelor’s degree in Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in a quantitative field
- Exposure to programming and analytic tools preferred (SAS, R, Python, Tableau, SQL, etc.)
- Intermediate to advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills
- Experience with databases and digital marketing a plus
