Compensation: $23.63/hour + Relocation Assistance to Charlottesville, Virginia
CornerStone’s AI & Automation Internship offers a unique opportunity at the intersection of artificial intelligence and investing. Interns will work closely
with the investment team to explore and implement AI-powered tools and workflows. Our program welcomes all majors and is well suited for students who are:
- Interested in building technical, AI-driven solutions, as well as investing
- Problem solvers with strong discernment and prioritization skills
- Self-starters and team players with strong technical communication
This role will join other interns in CornerStone’s Internship Program, which is a two-month commitment from June through July 2027. In this structured program,
interns gain exposure to a broad spectrum of asset classes and investment strategies through project-based assignments conducted in collaboration with the
firm’s investment professionals. The program concludes with a formal presentation to the entire firm, in which interns present the results of their work.
Interns are immersed as members of the investment team and work on projects that are considered important to the ongoing success of the investment portfolio.
For the AI & Automation Intern, technical projects will be selected based on a combination of firm needs and the skill set of the individual Intern. All Interns
complete an Excel and financial modeling training program and have significant networking opportunities throughout the summer. We also offer Interns the
opportunity to obtain the Bloomberg Market Concepts certification through completion of a self-paced course that provides an interactive introduction to the
financial markets and the Bloomberg Terminal.
We work collaboratively with our Interns, over the course of their summer with us and beyond, to help identify their desired next steps and help them achieve
their goals. This is a paid internship and offers the option to work remotely. Following the end of the program, Interns may be considered for our full-time
Investment Analyst program.
CornerStone Partners is an investment management firm based in Charlottesville, Virginia. Our clients rely on returns generated by their investment portfolios
to fund their missions in health, education, conservation, and building and improving communities. They hire CornerStone to manage their investment program,
including investment selection, asset allocation, and investment operations. We seek to invest client portfolios globally with the top investment managers in
different asset classes, including public equity, private equity, fixed income, hedge funds, venture capital, and real assets. The firm manages approximately
$32.7 billion (as of 6/30/2026) and has 40 employees.
