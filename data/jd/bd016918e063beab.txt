About The Role
Join our Research and Development team and gain hands-on experience supporting meaningful projects. In this role, you’ll collaborate with colleagues and
external partners while contributing to research and development initiatives.
Schedule Information
This position is a full time, 40-hour week position, running from January - May 2027.
What You’ll Do
- Contribute to Process Engineering and Development projects focused on new allografts and improvements to recovery and processing methods.
- Collect and analyze data using statistical tools to help inform project decisions.
- Help bring new and improved processes into production.
- Support the preparation of reports and manuscripts for internal or external review and publication.
- Assist with surgical procedures that support preclinical research projects, as needed.
- Follow standard practices and procedures to help ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
- Share recommendations and help create or update work instructions, job aids, and policies.
- Pitch in with other responsibilities as needed.
