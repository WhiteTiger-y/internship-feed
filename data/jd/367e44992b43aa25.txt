Role Overview
Qualcomm develops leading-edge ASIC and SoC solutions spanning compute, connectivity, and AI acceleration, including Snapdragon processors. Our team focuses on
connectivity and data-center silicon, developing advanced ASIC/SoC solutions for high-bandwidth networking, infrastructure compute, and AI workloads.
We are looking for a motivated Software Systems & AI Co-op student to build ASIC design flows and methodologies into full-stack applications, AI-powered tools,
and automation for engineering teams. We’re looking for someone who has hands-on experience with EDA/CAD tools, ASIC Design, Firmware Development and also good
understanding of Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, JavaScript/TypeScript/python, modern web technologies and project management, plus an interest in applying AI to
real engineering problems.
Responsibilities may include:
* Building responsive web applications using React
* Developing backend services and APIs using Node.js (JavaScript/TypeScript/python)
* Designing and working with PostgreSQL databases
* Building AI-powered tools and agentic workflows, including orchestrating AI agents and integrating LLMs with tools, APIs, and engineering systems
* Exploring AI-assisted CAD/design and engineering automation
* Improving developer tools, testing, automation, and engineering workflows
* Collaborating across software, hardware, firmware, AI, and DevOps teams
* AI assisted project management, including tracking tasks and deliverables, coordinating across teams, following up on action items, and helping keep projects
on track
Minimum Qualifications:
* Pursuing Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer/Electrical Engineering, or a related degree
* Demonstrated experience building full-stack applications using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL
* Strong JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, REST API, Git, and Linux fundamentals
* Understanding of LLMs, generative AI, AI agents, tool calling, and agent orchestration
* Interest or experience in AI-assisted CAD/design or engineering workflows
* Strong problem-solving skills and ability to learn and build quickly
Preferred Qualifications:
* Python and experience with LLM APIs, RAG, or agent frameworks
* CAD/EDA, design automation, or hardware/software experience
* C/C++, networking, Docker, CI/CD, or automated testing
* Personal, academic, or internship projects demonstrating full-stack or AI development
Education Requirements:
* In study towards a bachelor’s degree in one of the following: Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Engineering Science, Computer Science, or related
field
* For 4-month internships: must be available January 2027 - April/May 2027
* Expected graduation date of November 2027 or later.
* 1+ years of academic experience with programming languages such as C, C++, Python, etc
