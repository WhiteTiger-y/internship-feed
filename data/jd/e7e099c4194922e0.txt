Why intern at HSB?
At Hartford Steam Boiler (HSB), a Munich Re company, you will learn what it is like to pursue a career at one of the world’s largest reinsurance companies.
- As a valued Data Science Intern within our team in the Hartford, CT Office, you will work with HSB’s Data Science team on large scale machine learning
projects supporting one of our major business areas (insurance, engineering or technology solutions).
Desired Major(s) and Level of Education:
- MS or PhD Student in Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, Electrical Engineering or a related applied science with a heavy quantitative
emphasis.
- The ideal candidate is adept at using large data sets to find opportunities for product and process optimization.
- Exceptional undergraduate students will also be considered.
Skills:
- Strong programming experience with Python/R/SQL, with the ability to write well-documented production-level code.
- Practical knowledge & experience with statistical methods
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; ability to work creatively in problem solving environment.
- Basic software engineering skills (Git, API, Docker Container, DevOps best practices)
- Experience with Spark and distributed computing is a plus.
- Experience with Azure cloud development is a plus.
- Experience with learning frameworks such as PyTorch or Tensorflow is a plus.
- Experience developing or experimenting with generative AI models (ex. LLMs) and associated utilties/workflows is a plus.
- Available to start the internship in May 2027 and work a hybrid schedule in the Hartford, CT Office
HSB’s Summer Internship Program provides a well-rounded, engaging and enjoyable experience.
Below are some of the key experiences and benefits you can expect.
Program Highlights:
- Gain hands on experience.
- Meet and gain insights from the Senior Leadership Team as well as other leaders and professionals in the organization to develop lasting professional
relationships.
- Networking opportunities.
- Hybrid work schedule to include working on-site in the Hartford, CT office and working from home.
- Participate in community outreach.
- Opportunity to gain career advice and help you prepare for navigating your career search.
- Learn about great careers within the insurance and the impact they make.
- Potential full-time career opportunities after graduation.
