Who We Are
Persona AI is building humanoid robots for the most demanding environments in heavy industry — shipyards, steel mills, fabrication facilities, and offshore
platforms — performing welding, grinding, maintenance, inspection, and material-handling work that is dangerous, physically demanding, and increasingly
difficult to staff.
We are backed by leading strategic and financial investors and engaged with global industrial leaders across Korea, Japan, the United States, and Singapore.
Korea is the center of gravity for our early commercial strategy, anchored by relationships with the world’s leading shipbuilders and steelmakers. Our work
spans both the robot platform itself and the systems, partners, and playbooks required to deploy it at scale.
We're seeking a Humanoid Robotics Software Intern to join our team in Houston, TX or Pensacola, FL and contribute to the software systems that bring our
humanoid robots to life. You'll work on robotics software including perception, locomotion, manipulation, and machine learning systems.
Based on team needs, you may work on things such as:
Humanoid Locomotion : Develop algorithms for walking, balance, and bipedal movement control systems
Perception Systems : Work on computer vision, sensor fusion, and environmental understanding capabilities
Manipulation Software : Contribute to robotic arm and hand control systems for grasping and dexterous manipulation
Machine Learning : Implement learning algorithms, training pipelines, and model optimization for robotic applications
Autonomy: Develop navigation, planning, and decision-making algorithms for real-world operation
Platform Software: Build the core infrastructure, tooling, and runtime systems that power the robot stack

Our Internship Program
At Persona AI, our internship program gives you direct, hands-on experience building cutting-edge humanoid robotics alongside industry experts. We offer full-

Spring 2027: January – May
Summer 2027: May – August
