The Aerospace Corporation is hiring a Researcher in Formal Methods, Graduate Intern, for the Cyber and Mission Technology Department within the Information
Systems and Cyber Division.
Information Systems and Cyber Division (ISCD) staff couple the latest in information system technologies, such as elastic compute clouds, containerization,
microservices, real-time operating systems, and visualization frameworks, with expertise in cyber security, software architecture, software engineering, data
science, artificial Intelligence, process improvement, and software development to deliver responsive, resilient, high-performance software intensive systems to
our IC, DoD, and civilian customers.
The Cyber and Mission Technology Department (CMTD) focuses on use-inspired basic research encompassing full-stack cyber and reasoning. As an intern within CMTD,
you will be involved in fundamental research in formal methods applied to self-healing systems. Self-healing systems are fault-tolerant architectures that
explicitly account for malicious actors, baking in assumptions such as intentional correlated failures. This internship will focus on creating novel research in
formal methods to develop computationally viable deterministic mathematical/logical guarantees for real-time generated patches. You will take an active part in
shifting the cybersecurity landscape in favor of the defender.
The selected candidate will be required to work full-time, on-site at our facility in El Segundo CA, Chantilly VA, Colorado Springs CO, and other Aerospace
locations.
