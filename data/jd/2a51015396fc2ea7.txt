What is a day in the life of an AI Engineer Intern?
- Assist with data preprocessing, cleaning, and analysis to prepare high-quality datasets for model training.
- Support the development and optimization of machine learning models, including tasks like fine-tuning hyperparameters and evaluating performance.
- Participate in the deployment of AI solutions, collaborating with senior engineers to integrate models into production systems.
- Document AI experiments, code, and project workflows to ensure clear and reproducible processes.
- Research cutting-edge AI technologies and methodologies to contribute to the team's ongoing innovation efforts.
