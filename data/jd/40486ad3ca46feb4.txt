Responsibilities
You will work as an AI/ML Computational Scientist on the AI Safety team. Your responsibilities as part of the team include:
- Apply advanced analytical, computational, and research skills to evaluate, secure, and govern real-world AI systems.
- Establish and apply methods and tools to evaluate AI deployments, assess readiness and impact, and remediate bias and behavior failures.
- Build AI safety into Accenture offerings through runtime protection, AI security, agent safeguards, monitoring, and incident readiness.
- Work with the team to build data flywheels that turn real-world interactions, failures, and adversarial signals into more secure and reliable AI agents, and
help move AI systems from prototype to production while connecting enterprise usage back to model improvement.
- Enrolled in (or expected to enroll in) a Masters/PhD in relevant field and three years of experience in AI agent security, safely evaluation, adversarial
testing and attack simulation.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience, including prior internship or research.
