About the Team
Join our B2B Demand Generation team as a Marketing Intern building real, shipped AI tooling for the Upgraders motion. Our team has already started building its
own tools out of necessity, reusable dashboard skills, campaign automation, and internal systems of record. This role isn't a research exercise: you'll take
something currently manual or one-off, tied to marketing workflows like campaign reporting, audience segmentation, or GTM tracking, and turn it into a reusable
tool the team actually uses after you leave. The specific tooling gap will be finalized closer to your start date based on what's live at the time, but the
pattern is consistent: build, ship, and hand off something real.
Responsibilities
Identify a manual or one-off marketing workflow, campaign reporting, audience segmentation, GTM tracking, and build a reusable AI-powered tool to solve it
Work hands-on with AI-assisted development tools (Claude/Cursor), QuickBase APIs, and internal marketing data systems
Partner directly with the marketing team's existing tooling efforts (audience insight dashboards, campaign automation) to extend proven patterns
Ship a working tool the marketing team adopts and continues using after the internship ends
Document the tool clearly enough for the team to maintain and extend it independently
