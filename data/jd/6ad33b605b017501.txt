About the team
The USDS Payment team at TikTok builds the core payment capabilities supporting a smooth, secure, and reliable payment experience across the platform — spanning
payment acquisition, disbursement, transaction monitoring, payment method management, FX conversion, accounting, and reconciliation.
Within this org, the Payment Data Intelligence team applies data science and machine learning to improve payment services at scale. The team leverages large-
scale datasets and advanced algorithms to power business-critical use cases including financial risk control, payment compliance, user growth, and operational
optimization — at the intersection of ML, data infrastructure, and real-world fintech challenges.
This internship focuses on building agent-first AI applications for payment scenarios. The role is designed for candidates who want to apply the latest AI
paradigms, including LLM-based agents, workflow orchestration, prompt engineering, MCP-based tool integration, and AI application evaluation, to solve practical
payment business problems.
We are looking for talented individuals to join us for an internship. Our internship program offers students hands-on experience, industry exposure, and
opportunities to apply their knowledge to real-world challenges while building a strong foundation for personal and professional growth.
Interns will gain practical experience, explore potential career paths, and participate in social events, learning programs, and development workshops alongside
industry professionals.
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so we encourage you to apply early. Please clearly state your availability in your resume, including your start
and end dates.
Responsibilities:
- Work with product, strategy, engineering teams to design, develop, and deploy ML solutions for payment-related business problems
- Translate business needs into data-driven and algorithmic approaches
- Contribute to use cases such as fraud detection, risk modeling, user experience optimization, and campaign effectiveness
- Participate in data processing and feature engineering workflows at scale
- Experiment with ML models and improve components of the model lifecycle (data labeling, feature systems, model robustness, automation)
- Explore advanced AI techniques (deep neural networks, LLMs) in payment intelligence scenarios
