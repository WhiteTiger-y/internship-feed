POSITION OVERVIEW
2027 Summer Internship
About the Role The Value-Based Care team supports Trilogy's work in Medicare programs and care models that reward quality outcomes and better resident
experiences. During this internship, you'll take a real VBC operational challenge from analysis to solution, using data to identify opportunities, develop and
test an improvement, and evaluate the results. Your work will help shape a process and tool that supports VBC operations across our campuses.
Key Responsibilities
Data Review and Organization
- Gather and organize campus-level data related to current and potential VBC opportunities.
- Bring together information such as ACO and physician relationships, external referral sources, current I-SNP and ACO performance, and campus census details.
- Review data for completeness and consistency and identify gaps that may impact the analysis.
Data Analysis + Opportunity Identification
- Analyze campus-level data to identify trends and differences in VBC opportunity across Trilogy.
- Evaluate how individual factors may contribute to a campus’s overall VBC opportunity.
- Identify campuses or markets with the strongest potential for increased VBC participation, alignment, or impact.
Solution Development + Action Planning
- Develop a scoring methodology that combines key VBC indicators into a campus-level opportunity score.
- Apply the methodology across campuses to identify and prioritize areas of opportunity.
- Create clear recommendations for how the VBC team could use the findings to focus resources, outreach, or future initiatives.
Pilot + Feedback
- Pilot the opportunity score with a selected group of campuses and VBC stakeholders.
- Gather feedback on whether the model accurately reflects known campus opportunities and challenges.
- Refine the scoring methodology based on feedback and lessons learned.
- Develop a final recommendation for how the tool could be used and maintained going forward.
Qualifications
Education
- Currently pursuing a degree in Healthcare Administration, Business, Public Health, Data Analytics, Economics, or a related field.
Skills & Competencies
- Strong Excel skills. Experience with Power BI or similar tools is a plus.
- Ability to turn complex information into clear, concise takeaways.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Curious, detail-oriented, and quick to learn new tools and topics.
- Able to manage multiple tasks and work both on your own and with a team.
Physical Requirements
- Ability to sit or work at a computer for extended periods.
- Ability to communicate in person, by phone, and electronically.
What This Internship Offers
- Hands-on experience in value-based care, one of the fastest-growing areas of healthcare.
- An opportunity to interact and learn from various stakeholders including senior leadership, campus leaders, and health plan partners.
- Real projects that directly support team decisions and campus operations.
- Mentorship from the Director of Value-Based Care Operations.
- Exposure to Medicare programs, health plan partnerships, and how healthcare strategy works.
