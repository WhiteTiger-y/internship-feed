Description
Come Smile with Us!
As a Marketing Analyst Intern for Delta Dental of Iowa, you will work alongside our talented Marketing team to help turn data into actionable insights. This is
an opportunity to analyze marketing campaigns, improve tools, and automate processes while gaining skills in report building, model development, and researching
new analytics techniques to support and enhance the Marketing teams’ efforts.
Here is your chance to apply what you’ve learned in the classroom with meaningful work, network with fellow interns, and grow your data analytics/marketing
career all while making a difference in our community.
This temporary, full-time internship will start on May 19, 2027, and work from our Johnston, Iowa location. Hours will be Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. –
5:00 p.m.

Why Delta Dental of Iowa?
For your smile. For your health. For your community.
At Delta Dental of Iowa, we’re more than just a dental and vision insurance company. Improving health and wellness is at the center of everything we do. As a
not-for-profit, we aim to build stronger communities by investing in actions that promote and improve health. Delta Dental of Iowa invests 40% of our dollars
directly back into the communities we serve.
Come see why we are passionate about "Bringing Smiles" to our customers. Our team enjoys competitive pay and benefits, an awesome "One Team" approach, and a
company culture that fosters "Exceptional Quality Service" and "Leadership at All Levels." Together, we can make a difference – not only in Iowa but across the
country.
Learning Objectives:
- Learn how to analyze marketing campaigns and review results using Power BI to look for successes and opportunities.
- Understand programming and model development.
- Develop new reporting tools using programming languages such as Python, SQL and VBA.
- Research new analytic developments.
- Build macros to automate processes.
- Improve current marketing tools for analytics and building campaign lists.
- Assist Marketing team with reviewing projects, sharing ideas, and enhancing tools.
- Establish and maintain effective working relationships with internal and external teams.
- Complete special projects on time with high quality.
- Engage in programs and networking opportunities to strengthen business acumen in the insurance industry.
- Support internship program by motivating and driving participation among intern peers to fully engage with opportunities available throughout the summer.
- Represent Delta Dental of Iowa as well as the other Delta Dental companies in a professional manner both internally and externally.
- Maintain strict confidentiality.
- Perform other duties as assigned.
Requirements
Required:
- Must be an undergraduate student working toward a bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Analytics or related fields.
- Must be able to demonstrate a current 3.0 GPA or higher through transcripts.
- Must be registered as a student to a college or university.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel.
- Coursework in business, statistics, research, marketing, mathematics, or related field is required.
- Excellent written and verbal communication as well as presentation skills required.
- Participants will be required to submit a written analysis of project work and present a summary of analysis to leadership at the end of the program.
- Freshman and Sophomore candidates will participate in the Insure Your Future program with specific leadership development/networking opportunities.
Preferred:
1. Experience with Python and SQL.
2. Knowledge of Power BI.
