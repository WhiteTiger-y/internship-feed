Job Description
Innovation, quality and reliability are at the heart of Bosch Power Tools, a leading global supplier of power tools, garden tools, accessories and measuring
technology , with brands including Bosch and Dremel .
These placements combine creative marketing with analytical and commercial experience . You’ll support a wide range of activities, including campaigns, content
creation, product launches, events, customer and digital marketing , while using data and insights to inform decisions.
You’ll gain broad exposure to the Bosch Power Tools business across both professional and DIY markets , supported by training in marketing, communications,
digital tools, sales and commercial activities , as well as dedicated soft-skills development. You’ll also join a wider intern community across Bosch in the UK
& Ireland.
This is an ideal opportunity for someone who is creative, curious and commercially minded , with an interest in data and Excel and in understanding the impact
of marketing.
Main Role Responsibilities
- Digital & Content : Support sales channels and update product listings with engaging content.
- Product & Portfolio : Support product launches, portfolio reviews, and tracking product performance.
- Collaboration : Partner with sales and marketing teams to understand business requirements and provide analytical support.
- Communications : Translate commercial insights into clear presentations and actively present them to business stakeholders.
- Market & Competitor : Research competitor activity, pricing, and market trends to identify key business opportunities.
- Commercial & Pricing : Support pricing analysis and evaluate the commercial performance of key promotions.
- Sales Analysis : Use Excel to analyse sales-in and sales-out data to support forecasting and demand planning.
- Reporting & Data : Organise, validate, and prepare reports and commercial dashboards using Excel, Power BI, and PowerPoint.
- Events & Projects : Contribute to wider marketing projects and participate in customer, trade, and internal events.
