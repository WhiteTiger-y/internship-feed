Overview
This is a hybrid role, with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office.
First Citizens Bank offers an exciting summer internship program that provides development opportunities and hands-on work experience while creating a talent
pipeline for the Bank. Our interns will apply classroom knowledge in a corporate environment while making a positive impact across the organization and the
communities we serve. Come help First Citizens build for the future!
The Applied AI team sits within Enterprise AI, which brings together data science, applied AI, Responsible AI, and AI governance for the bank. We build the
machine learning and AI solutions used across the General Bank, Commercial Bank, and Enterprise Functions — and we are responsible for testing and evidencing
that those solutions work before they reach production.
This is an early and fast-moving area at First Citizens. Evaluation practices, delivery patterns, and the bank’s AI platform are all being built now, and the
team works across U.S. and India locations. We use machine learning, advanced analytics, and generative AI where each is the right fit — we do not assume every
problem needs a generative AI solution. An intern joining this team will see what it takes to build AI that holds up in a regulated environment, which is a more
exacting standard than most coursework prepares you for.
Responsibilities & Qualifications
Duties & Responsibilities
In This Role, You Will
- Build and test components of a real AI or analytics solution — work the team would otherwise do itself, not a side exercise
- Prepare, explore, and analyze data using Python and SQL in approved bank environments
- Develop model prototypes, features, prompt and retrieval configurations, scripts, and notebooks under the direction of an assigned mentor
- Help create evaluation datasets and run evaluations — depending on the project, testing accuracy, relevance, groundedness, retrieval quality, or content
safety
- Document assumptions, limitations, and test results clearly enough that someone else can pick the work up after the summer ends
- Learn how an AI use case moves through the bank’s governance and Responsible AI review, and what evidence it has to produce along the way
- Take part in team code reviews, technical discussions, and knowledge-sharing sessions
- Be part of a dynamic team with interesting projects.
- Accountabilities can and may include areas such as research, analytics, projects, strategy, process review and improvement, as well as technology enhancements
and support.
- Attend professional development sessions, informational sessions, networking and community service events.
- Have a dedicated manager and program manager to help you navigate the bank and the internship program.
- Be assigned a project, designed to improve a specific process or solve a problem that you will present at the close of the internship.
- Prepare a wrap-up presentation to be shared with the division leader and other key stakeholders in the company to outline your accomplishments and experience
during the internship
