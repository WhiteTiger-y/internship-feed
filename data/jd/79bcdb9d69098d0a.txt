organization is seeking a talented Data Scientist Intern to join our Business Automation team. Our team applies data science, AI, and intelligent automation to
improve business processes, increase efficiency, and enable better data-driven decision-making across CCS. We partner with business and technical teams to
analyze data, develop predictive capabilities, and create innovative solutions to real-world business challenges. What You Can Expect As an intern, you will
contribute directly to a data science project focused on transforming historical business data into predictive insights. You can also expect to: Clean and
prepare historical business data for analysis and machine learning, identifying and resolving data quality issues, inconsistencies, and missing values. Analyze
historical datasets to identify trends, relationships, outliers, and features that may contribute to predictive performance. Develop and train machine learning
models using Python and established data science libraries to predict business outcomes based on historical data. Evaluate and compare modeling approaches using
appropriate statistical and machine learning techniques to assess accuracy and reliability. Document and present the methodology, model performance, findings,
and recommendations to the Business Automation team and business stakeholders. What We're Looking For Minimum Requirements: Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s
Degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Applied Mathematics, or a related field, with an expected graduation date between Fall 2027 and Summer
2028. Proficiency in Python for data processing, analysis, and machine learning, including experience with libraries such as pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn.
Strong foundation in statistics, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, and machine learning fundamentals. Experience working with structured datasets
and using SQL to query, manipulate, and analyze data. Ability to analyze technical results and clearly communicate findings to both technical and non-technical
audiences. Preferred Requirements: Experience developing and evaluating predictive models using techniques such as regression, classification, decision trees,
random forests, or gradient boosting. Experience developing and evaluating predictive models using modern and classical techniques such as LLM inference,
embedding models, SVM, or XGBoost. Familiarity with Git or similar source-control and collaborative development tools. Prior academic, research, or project
experience using real-world datasets to develop machine learning or predictive analytics solutions. Expected Base Pay Range (USD) 29 - 57, $ per hour. The
