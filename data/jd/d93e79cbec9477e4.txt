Consulting Internship: Data Analysis/Data Engineering (Summer 2027)
- Intern
- Compensation: up to USD 30 - hourly
Company Description
CapTech is an award-winning consulting firm that collaborates with clients to achieve what’s possible through the power of technology. At CapTech, we’re
passionate about the work we do and the results we achieve for our clients. From the outset, our founders shared a collective passion to create a consultancy
centered on strong relationships that would stand the test of time. Today we work alongside clients that include Fortune 100 companies, mid-sized enterprises,
and government agencies, a list that spans across the country. Learn more at captechconsulting.com.
Job Description
Do you want to work on real world problems with smart, collaborative peers and leaders who are invested in your success? CapTech is looking for undergraduate
Juniors that are passionate about technology with a desire to solve complex business and technical problems to join our internship program, Elevate.
Elevate provides meaningful immersion into the full life cycle of a typical IT consulting project. This unique 10-week program takes place in person in our
Richmond, Virginia Headquarters and simulates a client engagement where one can achieve a full understanding of the consulting experience while solving relevant
client problems. Interns work in self-led teams and are paired with full-time consultants for guidance and mentorship. Additionally, interns enjoy weekly lunch-
and-learns with our leadership team. CapTech interns are given the opportunity to build something that matters and create impactful value.
Elevate has been ranked the Best Consulting Internship nationwide by Vault.com.
Some responsibilities you could have are:
- Clean and sort data using advanced analytics
- Design and implement a relational database to power data visualizations and analytics
- Analyze data to identify and implement key business metrics and KPIs
- Build a dashboard for executive-level clients using a data visualization tool
- Work with stakeholders and subject matter experts to identify how to structure/analyze/display the data for business needs/requirements
Qualifications
We are seeking data analysts and data engineers for Summer 2027 who will work on integrated teams to determine and implement a solution to a business problem
over the course of the summer. Characteristics of a qualified candidate include:
- Current undergraduates with expected graduation dates in December 2027 - May 2028 who will be seeking full-time work after graduation
- Demonstrated an ability to be self-motivated contributors in a team environment
- Experience with databases (especially SQL), datasets, ETL, Python, and/or data visualization tools helpful
- All majors welcome to apply; Analytics, Computer Science, BIT, Systems Engineering majors preferred but not required
- Availability to work in-person 40 hours/week for 10 weeks in Richmond, VA from June 1 through August 6, 2027 (housing stipend provided for those living more
than 50-miles away)
