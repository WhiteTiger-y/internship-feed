About The Program
Keysight's AI team builds adaptive engineering intelligence systems where measurement, simulation, and machine learning converge - models that learn from
complex physical data and adapt to new design and testing conditions.
This internship focuses on machine learning surrogate models for engineering simulation and measurement. High-fidelity simulations and physical measurements can
dominate the design and test loop for RF, high-speed, and other engineering problems, and their cost limits how much of a design space an engineer can explore.
You will develop models that predict engineering responses at a fraction of the cost, investigate how such models can be adapted efficiently as the design
problem changes, and integrate the result into an engineering ML runtime environment.
Role Overview
You will design, train, and evaluate neural surrogate models that predict engineering responses from simulation or measurement data far faster than repeated
high-fidelity simulation or physical characterization. This spans the full arc of the problem: generating or assembling the data, choosing and evaluating model
architectures and output representations, establishing where accuracy holds and where it degrades, and quantifying the result against a realistic simulation or
measurement budget.
In the second phase you will study how a trained model can be specialized efficiently when the design problem shifts, using LoRA and related parameter-efficient
adaptation methods. You will work alongside AI researchers, simulation and measurement experts, RF designers, and runtime engineers.
Learning Outcomes
- Apply modern machine learning to realistic engineering simulation and measurement data.
- Learn how surrogate model accuracy depends on data quality, sampling strategy, representation choices, and simulation budget.
- Develop practical experience with parameter-efficient adaptation methods such as LoRA under data-scarce engineering conditions.
- Build fluency with uncertainty estimation, physical consistency checks, and engineering-relevant error analysis.
- Take a research model from Python experimentation toward robust C++ and LibTorch integration.
- Collaborate across machine learning, RF design, simulation, measurement, and runtime engineering disciplines.
Responsibilities
- Generate parametric engineering datasets using simulation and/or measurement sources, with attention to sampling strategy and to numerical noise in the
underlying data.
- Design, train, and evaluate neural surrogate models for engineering response prediction.
- Investigate how the choice of output representation affects accuracy, particularly across operating regions or frequency ranges where the response varies
sharply.
- Establish rigorous baselines and quantify accuracy as a function of simulation or measurement budget.
- Evaluate LoRA and related parameter-efficient methods for specializing trained models to new problem settings, against full and partial fine-tuning.
- Develop calibrated uncertainty estimates so that predictions can be trusted or deferred appropriately.
- Verify physical consistency and engineering constraints - including passivity, causality, and reciprocity where applicable - and report engineering-relevant
metrics including worst-case behavior.
- Analyze the simulation corpus and model behavior: parameter sensitivity, response dimensionality, data quality and solver noise, and where prediction error
concentrates across frequency and design space.
- Prototype model training and inference integration in C++ and LibTorch within an engineering ML runtime environment.
