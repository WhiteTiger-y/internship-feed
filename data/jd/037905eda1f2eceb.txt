As a Finance AI Engineering Intern, you will join a team at the intersection of financial strategy and cutting-edge technology. NVIDIA's Finance organization is
more than just a support function; it is a critical engine that leverages data-driven insights to fuel our global innovation. In this role, you will help bridge
the gap between complex technical solutions and business strategy, utilizing the power of AI and advanced analytics to design and build the applications and
models that transform how we understand and predict the future of our business. If you are a curious problem-solver with a passion for data science, software
engineering, finance, and AI, this is your opportunity to drive real-world impact in a dynamic, high-growth environment.
What You'll Be Doing
- You will partner with Finance, technical team members, and business leaders to improve the decision insight capabilities of Finance's agentic models and to
help design, build, and maintain the AI-powered applications, agents, and automation that Finance depends on. Your responsibilities may include Advanced
Analytics, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, prototyping, feature development, testing, evaluation, DevOps, and/or agentic AI development.
- Collaborate with finance analysts and managers to understand the business questions behind the data, and work with data engineers, software engineers, and
data scientists to build, evaluate, and deploy the skills, agents, models, and applications that drive meaningful business results.
- Translate technical solutions and results into business language, communicating with technical and nontechnical stakeholders so they understand how the
solutions work and validate what we build meets business needs.
What We Need To See
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, software engineering, data science, engineering, or a related technical field.
Additional finance and accounting coursework are a plus.
- Strong engineering fundamentals, including strong proficiency in Python and SQL, familiarity with version control (Git), and the ability to write clean,
testable code.
- Understanding of machine learning and deep learning concepts (e.g., model training, evaluation, and neural network basics), or experience building
applications or tools through coursework, internships, or personal projects.
- Familiarity with LLMs, harnesses, skill development, and agentic tooling.
- Interest in supporting the Finance organization and curiosity about how AI can help with business decisions and help finance teams work faster.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a desire to build successful partnerships across all levels of the company.
