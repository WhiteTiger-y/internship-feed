Job Description:
The Parenteral Drug Product (PDP) group within Takeda’s Pharmaceutical Sciences organization develops formulations and manufacturing processes for synthetic and
protein therapeutics, supporting clinical development through commercial launch and scale-up to global manufacturing sites.
Join Takeda’s Parenteral Drug Product (PDP) team to apply AI and data science to real-world pharmaceutical development challenges. In this internship, you will
help develop an AI-enabled tool that transforms historical drug product development records into structured, validated data for our In-Silico Formulation
Platform. Working alongside pharmaceutical scientists and data science experts, you will develop AI workflows and agents to extract organized development data
from unstructured text and tables in Word and PDF reports. You will develop evaluation datasets, design database schema, implement data quality checks, and
benchmark extraction accuracy, completeness, and efficiency against curated reference data. You will also document the solution and present your findings to the
team.
This role offers hands-on experience at the intersection of applied AI, scientific data engineering, and pharmaceutical sciences. You will gain exposure to drug
product development workflows and the regulatory context in which pharmaceutical research operates.
How You Will Contribute:
- Develop Python workflows using AI agents and large language models (LLMs) to extract information from development reports.
- Map extracted data to the existing formulation database schema.
- Implement checks for data accuracy, completeness, unit consistency, and schema conformance.
- Benchmark extraction performance against manually curated reference data and assess time savings.
- Document the workflow, communicate findings, and contribute validated datasets to expand database coverage.
Internship Development Opportunities:
- Collaborate with pharmaceutical scientists on parenteral drug product development.
- Learn drug product development processes and regulatory requirements.
- Apply AI agents and advanced LLMs to real-world development data.
- Build skills in data engineering, validation, benchmarking, and technical communication.
Job Requirements:
- Location: Onsite in Lexington, MA.
- Education: Pursuing a degree in computer science, data science, informatics, pharmaceutical sciences, or a related field. Master’s or PhD candidates
preferred; qualified undergraduates considered.
- Required: Proficiency in Python; experience with AI coding agents or assistants such as Claude Code, Codex, or GitHub Copilot; experience with SQL and NoSQL
databases; understanding of relational database systems; and knowledge of RAG system development.
- Preferred: NLP experience and background knowledge of drug product development.
- Strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills, with attention to data quality.
