Overview
Systems Planning and Analysis, Inc. (SPA) delivers high-impact, technical solutions to complex national security issues. With over 50 years of business
expertise and consistent growth, we are known for continuous innovation for our government customers, in both the US and abroad. Our exceptionally talented team
is highly collaborative in spirit and practice, producing Results that Matter . Come work with the best! We offer opportunity, unique challenges, and clear-
sighted commitment to the mission. SPA: Objective. Responsive. Trusted.
The Strategic Division (SD) supports high-reliability organizations including Navy Strategic Systems Programs (SSP), Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, and the
Department of Energy (DOE), among others. Our support across our client base is expanding rapidly and encompasses important services, such as data analytics and
visualization, threat analysis, risk management, modeling and simulation, cost estimating, earned value management, program management, system engineering,
independent evaluation, and High Consequence Event prevention.
Strategic Systems Analysis Group (SSAG) provides multiple organizations within the Department of Defense with timely and objective assessments and
recommendations that integrate technical, operational, programmatic, policy and business analysis. We focus on key clients in the undersea community including
Navy’s Strategic Systems Programs (SSP). We work to provide integrated solutions based on information and communications throughout the chain of command to
ensure clear and consistent analysis and recommendations that are aligned to strategic and leadership goals while still being executable by the working level
technical communities.

Responsibilities
The successful candidate will be expected to provide relevant, objective, timely, and clear analytical support to SSAG customers to help them make better
decisions. The candidate will be able to work collaboratively to formulate problems, propose solution methods, and conduct analysis for the customer.
The candidate will be familiar with ideas and techniques in Operations Research, Data Science, and/or Physics-Based Modeling. They will possess an inquisitive
mind, a desire to broaden their skills, and be open to new, challenging problems that may stretch them to achieve success. They will be able to work with
autonomy and tenacity to complete tasks and take initiative to support others when needed.
Tasks include, but are not limited to:
- Developing and applying operations research techniques such as mathematical optimization, simulation, decision analysis, and statistical analysis.
- Decomposing complex issues into simplified problem statements and clearly and explicitly connecting analysis to specific aspects of the problem.
- Developing and maintaining tools and models to support analytic projects and processes.
- Creating data visualizations in support of decision analysis, model verification, and client communication.
- Assisting in the development of briefings, memos, and other contract deliverables.
Qualifications
Required Qualifications:
- US Citizenship required and the ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Top Secret Clearance.
- Working toward a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mathematics, Operations Research, Data Science, Computer Science, Physics, or a related technical
discipline.
- Coursework or other experience with linear optimization, discrete optimization, machine learning, Bayesian statistics, probability theory, or simulation.
- Experience with Python, R, or a similar programming language.
- Ability to report to designated work location in Alexandria, VA, up to full time, based on the needs of the customer.
Desired Qualifications:
- Experience using Python, R, or a similar programming language to build models, perform statistical analysis, or do other quantitative analysis work.
- Experience with tools such as Tableau or Power BI that can integrate disparate data sources into visualizations that aid analysis and decision making.
- Proactive interest in learning new skills and engaging new challenges.
- Strong customer service skills, with interest in proactively anticipating and addressing client needs.
