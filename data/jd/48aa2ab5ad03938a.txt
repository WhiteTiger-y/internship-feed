Overview
The Machine Learning & AI (ML&AI) team is a group of applied data scientists within Marketing Data Science who build machine-learning models, run experiments,
and develop segmentation strategy to power smarter, more personalized marketing. We translate member data into predictive intelligence so our marketing partners
can target the right members with the right message at the right time.
Potential Summer Associate Project
- Build, rebuild, and refine machine-learning models for marketing, Design and setup experiments, including A/B tests, statistical testing, randomized control
tests (RCTs), Segmentation tests/Creative Testing
The Summer Associate Program is a 12-week internship program beginning in May 2027 and ending in August 2027. Students will work on impactful projects and
meaningful work during their internship. To qualify for this position, applicants must be currently pursuing a degree from an accredited college or university
and have an anticipated graduation date of December 2027 or later.
Responsibilities
- Build, rebuild, and refine machine-learning models for marketing use cases (e.g., propensity and likelihood models)
- Design and setup experiments, including A/B tests, statistical testing, randomized control tests (RCTs), Segmentation tests/Creative Testing
- Analyze member data to better understand and predict customer needs and behaviors
- Contribute to marketing segmentation strategy and support ad hoc analyses for current and upcoming campaigns
- Develop dashboards that enable stakeholders to explore data and self-serve insights
- Develop and present analytic findings to stakeholders, including senior executives
- Collaborate with a range of partners to drive progress on cross-functional projects
- Become acquainted with the functioning of the team
Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field (AI-focused coursework a
strong plus)
- A passion for analytics, machine learning, and data-driven problem solving
- Working knowledge of machine-learning algorithms, deep learning, and the statistical/mathematical logic behind analytical methods
- Experience with data extraction/wrangling
- Experience using analytic tools such as: Databricks, SQL, R, Python, PySpark, Power BI or Tableau
- Good communication and presentation skills
- Comfort working with large datasets and telling stories with data
- Ability to work independently and think outside of the box
- Strong strategic, critical, and analytical thinking skills
- Basic knowledge of marketing a plus
