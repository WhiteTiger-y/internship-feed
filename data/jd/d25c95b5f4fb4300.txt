Summer Internship Program Overview
Join us as a summer intern and discover the world of finance and technology. Dive into our culture, engaging in real projects that offer a comprehensive
business overview. Interns take on immediate responsibility, guided by a mentor and line manager. Forge connections, explore new horizons, and unlock growth
opportunities in this 10 week journey. Join us during the summer of your penultimate year of university to build skills, expand knowledge, and embark on an
impactful path towards a promising career.
Why the Credit Research Summer Intern Program?
Barclays' award-winning Credit Research team empowers internal and external clients to make well-informed investment choices by providing expert insights.
Covering Investment Grade, High Yield, Emerging Markets, Municipals, and Credit Strategy, this team offers actionable insights and innovative products daily.
You can test your potential as a summer analyst here.
Credit Research analysts focus on specific sectors, conducting company and industry analysis. They collaborate with both internal departments (trading, sales,
investment banking) and external clients (money managers, pension funds, hedge funds) regularly to enhance credit market evaluation. This dynamic role involves
close collaboration with Sales & Trading, offering a fast-paced, engaging experience.
As a summer analyst, you'll rotate through two Credit Research coverage areas, gaining hands-on experience and making meaningful contributions. Guided by a
senior analyst, you'll employ critical thinking to provide insights for investment theses and reports. Responsibilities include analyzing macro industry trends,
maintaining financial models, gathering and interpreting data, crafting client-centric marketing materials, and possibly participating in client interactions.
Moreover, you'll have the chance to collaborate with other teams, like our Quantitative Portfolio Strategies and Data Science and Applied AI teams, gaining
insights into advanced financial analysis and staying abreast of the field's latest developments.
Research at Barclays
Research is an ideas factory. We dig deep into the underlying factors that affect the world’s financial markets and provide insights that empower our clients to
take key strategic and investment decisions. Research is a centre of subject-matter expertise on the companies, markets and securities that we cover, delivering
real time intelligence and long-term vision. It’s vital to the performance of our global investment bank.

Purpose of the role
To actively contribute to the Research business by conducting financial analysis and delivering insights that support senior colleagues in producing
differentiated content for clients.
Accountabilities
- Conduct financial industry analysis on covered companies and the broader sector to support the team’s investment thesis.
- Research sector trends that may impact company/sector recommendations.
- Update financial models of covered companies from information obtained through company conference calls and press releases as well as other sources.
- Assist with sector notes and reports on companies and industries followed.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support business initiatives.
- Participate in training and development programs to enhance skills and knowledge, including in support of Research Department initiatives.
- Cultivate relationships across the Research Department to foster knowledge-sharing and best practices.
