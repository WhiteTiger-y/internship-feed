As a Data Science & Analytics Intern, you will help transform massive enterprise data streams into predictive models, business intelligence, and actionable
insights that drive executive decision-making. Working with large-scale structured and unstructured data, you will build data pipelines, statistical models, and
automated dashboards. You will tackle real data challenges, such as churn prediction, network optimization, and subscriber segmentation, seeing your models
directly influence business strategy. Successful candidates will integrate modern AI frameworks and automated analytics workflows to solve complex quantitative
problems.
What Success Looks Like (Objectives)
Our 10-week summer internship offers hands-on, on-site experience in the Greater Denver Area (unless otherwise noted per posting). As part of the cohort, you
won't just shadow, you will take direct ownership of a real-world business project, solving a critical operational challenge or implementing an innovative
solution for EchoStar. Through this program, 1:1 mentorship, practical skill building on live enterprise projects, and consideration for future full-time early
career pathways.
- Get oriented, meet your team, and scope your summer project with your manager and mentor
- Query, extract, and clean complex datasets across distributed cloud databases and enterprise storage
- Assist in building, training, and validating predictive machine learning models and statistical algorithms
- Develop automated reporting dashboards and data visualizations (Tableau, Power BI) to communicate key metrics to business leads
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover operational trends, anomaly patterns, and growth opportunities
- Partner cross-functionally with business leads, product teams, and data engineering to translate questions into quantitative solutions
- Leverage modern generative AI and NLP frameworks to extract insights from unstructured text data
- Present your project and results to peers and leadership
Core Skills, Competencies, and Key Experiences (What you’ll bring)
- Programming & Querying: Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn), SQL, R, PySpark
- Machine Learning & Modeling: Predictive Analytics, Classification/Regression, Clustering, Time-Series Forecasting, NLP
- Data Visualization & Cloud: Tableau, Power BI, AWS Redshift/Snowflake, Jupyter Notebooks
- Display a strong continuous-learning mindset, experimenting with novel AI features and emerging tech solutions for continuous process improvement
- Demonstrate strong critical thinking skills
- Exhibit clear written/verbal communication
- Deliver results with full accountability
- Collaborate effectively with cross-functional partners
- (Optional exposure, a plus but not required: ETL pipelines, BigQuery/Snowflake/Databricks, NLP or sentiment analysis, A/B testing, or Git)
Minimum Requirements
- Minimum Education: Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Data Science, Data Analytics, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related
technical field
- Minimum 3.3 GPA
- Expected graduation between December 2027 and May 2028
- Minimum Experience: Foundational experience in data analysis, statistical modeling, business intelligence, or data processing through coursework, projects,
