About the Role
As an AI Platform Engineer Intern, you’ll work alongside engineers across Product, ML, and Infrastructure to build the backend services and platform
capabilities that power Brain Co.’s AI applications. You’ll contribute to APIs, shared services, developer tooling, and production systems used in enterprise
and government environments.
This internship is designed for current undergraduate students, specifically juniors and seniors graduating no later than June 2028, who want hands-on
experience building real production software. You’ll take on scoped projects across backend systems, distributed services, AI/ML platform integrations,
reliability, and developer experience, with mentorship through code reviews, design discussions, and hands-on collaboration with the team.
What You’ll Work On
Build and improve backend services and APIs—including REST, gRPC, and event-driven systems—that power AI applications and shared platform capabilities.
Take scoped features from technical design through implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
Develop modular, reusable platform components and developer-facing tooling that help teams build and ship reliably.
Work with databases, queues, caches, and distributed systems, learning how to handle consistency, retries, idempotency, and failure modes.
Improve latency, throughput, reliability, and cost through profiling, observability, and thoughtful system design.
Contribute to systems that support AI inference, data pipelines, and customer-facing workflows.
Partner with Product, ML, Infrastructure, and customer-facing teams to translate open-ended needs into clear technical solutions.
Participate in code reviews, architecture discussions, and technical problem-solving, and see your work deployed to real users.
You Might Be a Great Fit If You…
Are a current undergraduate junior or senior graduating no later than June 2028 and pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical
field.
Have strong foundations in data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming, systems, and databases.
Have experience building software in Go, TypeScript, Rust, Python, Java, C++, or a similar language.
Have built a backend service, API, data-intensive application, or developer tool through coursework, an internship, research, or a personal project.
Understand—or are eager to learn—API design, databases, distributed systems, and reliability concepts.
Are comfortable with Git, testing, and debugging, and are interested in cloud or containerized deployment.
Enjoy breaking down ambiguous problems from first principles and communicating your approach clearly.
Are curious, proactive, and excited to own technical problems while seeking feedback when needed.
