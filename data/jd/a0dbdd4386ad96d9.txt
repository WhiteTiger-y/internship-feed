Acuity Inc. (NYSE: AYI) is a market-leading industrial technology company. We use technology to solve problems in spaces, light and more things to come. Through
our two business segments, Acuity Brands Lighting (ABL) and Acuity Intelligent Spaces (AIS), we design, manufacture, and bring to market products and services
that make a valuable difference in people’s lives.
We achieve growth through the development of innovative new products and services, including lighting, lighting controls, building management solutions, and an
audio, video and control platform. We focus on customer outcomes and drive growth and productivity to increase market share and deliver superior returns. We
look to aggressively deploy capital to grow the business and to enter attractive new verticals.
Acuity Inc. is based in Atlanta, Georgia, with operations across North America, Europe and Asia. The Company is powered by approximately 13,000 dedicated and
talented associates. Visit us at www.acuityinc.com .
Job Summary
Acuity’s Internship Program is a 10–12 week summer experience open to college students. During this paid program, students gain hands-on experience, learning
through meaningful work while exploring future career opportunities.
Interns work on real projects, build relationships across the business and gain exposure to leaders, teams and functions throughout Acuity. Internships serve as
an important early-career pipeline, helping students explore future opportunities at Acuity, including the BUILD program and full-time roles.
BUILD is Acuity's two-year rotational development program for recent graduates, designed to provide hands-on experience, structured development, and exposure to
career opportunities across the business.
Why Intern at Acuity
- Contribute to work that solves real problems and delivers meaningful outcomes for our customers
- Build professional and technical skills
- Learn from experienced leaders and teammates
- Develop relationships across the business
- Explore potential career paths and future opportunities at Acuity

- Communicate effectively and collaborate well with others

- Embrace technology and are eager to learn new tools and ways of working

While specific projects and activities vary during your internship, you can expect to:
