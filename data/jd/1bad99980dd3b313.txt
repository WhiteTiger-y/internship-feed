Role Overview
Before there were smartphones or smart cities, before autonomous cars or 360° virtual reality videos, there was our technology. Headquartered in San Diego, for
over 30 years Qualcomm inventions have inspired others to make the impossible, possible. From 5G to artificial intelligence, from IoT to automotive and extended
reality applications, Qualcomm is inventing the technologies of an intelligently connected future, spearheading research efforts for the next global wireless
standard, and collaborating with industry leaders in the wireless value chain to make this future a commercial reality.
The field of computer graphics is being rapidly transformed by advances in generative AI. This internship will explore AI-driven techniques for neural
rendering, interactive neural rendering, or world modeling, with a focus on improving visual quality, temporal consistency, controllability, and real-time
rendering performance. The work will be inspired by recent developments in generative rendering, interactive graphics, and world simulation systems.
Responsibilities may include:
* Research and develop novel generative AI techniques for neural rendering and computer graphics applications.
* Investigate image and video generation, neural rendering, or interactive rendering.
* Explore efficient approaches to improve rendering quality, temporal consistency, controllability, and real-time performance.
* Implement and evaluate state-of-the-art methods from recent literature.
* Design and conduct benchmarking, experimental evaluations, and ablation studies.
* Develop scalable data generation, training, and evaluation pipelines for generative graphics systems.
* Collaborate with researchers and engineers to advance next-generation AI-powered rendering technologies.

Research Impact:
The research conducted during this internship will advance the state of the art in neural rendering, interactive rendering, and world models, with the potential
to contribute to publications at leading computer vision, graphics, and machine learning conferences.
Minimum Qualifications:
* Experience developing deep learning models for image and video applications.
* Strong foundation in deep learning fundamentals, including modern architectures, optimization, and training methodologies.
* Experience with Python and PyTorch for deep learning research and development.
* Prior experience with neural rendering, generative AI, computer graphics, or 3D reconstruction is a plus.
