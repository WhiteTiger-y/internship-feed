The Community You Will Join:
Airbnb is seeking an Advanced Analytics Intern to join the Finance Data Science & Strategy team within the Finance organization for the 2027 Summer Intern
Program.
The Finance Data Science & Strategy team builds the statistical models behind Airbnb’s top line forecast and explains how the business performs against it. The
work runs as a loop. We figure out where actuals differ from forecast and why, share the answer with partners across Finance and the business, and feed what we
learned into the next version of the forecast, so each cycle starts from a better baseline than the last.
We are looking for someone curious about business and data, who can understand both the ‘what’ and the ‘why’ behind a number and help translate them into
decisions. An ideal candidate would have some familiarity with analytical methodologies, BI tooling, and business communication.
The Difference You Will Make:
As an Advanced Analytics Intern on our team, you’ll help explain how the business performed against the forecast and use what you find to improve it. You’ll
have the opportunity to:
- Build comprehensive and high quality analyses of how the business is performing against the forecast, and explain what drove the difference
- Work with the team’s data scientists to turn those findings into improvements in the next version of the forecast
- Communicate findings clearly to partners across Finance and the business, in written readouts and recurring reviews
A Typical Day:
During your internship you’ll be integrated into a team where you’ll contribute to challenging projects. Your manager, team, and our incredible employee
community will strive to be superhosts to you during the summer. We provide the support, mentorship, fun, and real world experience needed to accelerate your
learning and make a real impact on our business, as Airbnb revolutionizes connection, belonging, and travel. You’ll be expected to:
- Own and complete a capstone project from beginning to end
- Collaborate with multiple team members to achieve project milestones
- Communicate with stakeholders across different teams to provide project updates
- Seek and provide feedback throughout the internship
- Actively participate in and contribute to the Finance Data Science & Strategy team and broader Airbnb community
