What We Are Looking For
We're searching for Summer 2027 Perception & Mapping Interns. You'll work on the models that let the Aurora Driver see and understand the world — object
detection and tracking, sensor fusion, semantic segmentation, VLMs, environmental/sensor conditions, and the ML-based map models and data that ground the
system.
In this role you will
- Develop onboard and/or offboard perception or mapping models to improve autonomous vehicle performance
- Process sensor data (camera, radar, lidar) to detect and track objects, actors, signs, traffic lights, and hazards
- Analyze, root-cause, and help resolve system deficiencies such as false positives/negatives and misclassifications
- Work with large real-world datasets across training, evaluation, and visualization pipelines
- Partner with full-time engineers to contribute to the next generation of the perception or mapping system
Required Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical Engineering, or a related field
- Strong ML and computer vision fundamentals
- Hands-on deep learning experience in PyTorch (or comparable), including model training and debugging
- Proficiency in Python and C++
- Comfort working with large datasets across training, evaluation, and debugging pipelines
- Has built and trained a perception or mapping model end-to-end, not only fine-tuned an existing backbone
Desirable Qualifications
- Experience with VLMs or other pretrained foundation models
- Prior publication record at a top-tier venue
- Knowledge of object tracking, Kalman filtering, or Hidden Markov Models
Compensation
The hourly pay rate for this internship in Mountain View or San Francisco is:
- $58.00 per hour for interns pursuing a master’s degree; and
- $65.00 per hour for interns pursuing a doctoral degree.
The hourly pay rate for this internship in Pittsburgh is:
- $52.00 per hour for interns pursuing a master’s degree; and
- $58.00 per hour for interns pursuing a doctoral degree.
