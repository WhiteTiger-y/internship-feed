Job Description: About the Internship At Martin Marietta, we are always looking for the best and the brightest, for people who have the potential to be the
Company's future leaders. We are building on our foundation of success by selecting the finest people and helping them realize their potential. When you decide
to build your career at Martin Marietta, you'll know what it's like to be respected, challenged, and rewarded. As a Telematics Intern, you will work alongside
experienced professionals to support the administration, monitoring, and optimization of the Company's telematics platform and related reporting solutions. This
internship provides an opportunity to apply academic knowledge in information systems, data analytics, and technology support while gaining hands-on experience
with fleet telematics, dashboard development, process documentation, and operational reporting. This position is not remote, and candidates will be expected to
work onsite at a Martin Marietta office location on scheduled workdays. Key Responsibilities Follow and promote Martin Marietta’s safety, environmental, and
ethical standards in all work activities. Actively participate in company initiatives and support a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous
improvement. Monitor and respond to requests submitted through the telematics support inbox. Manage user access, permissions, and system settings within the
Samsara telematics platform. Collaborate with division teams to monitor device performance and maintain the health of Samsara telematics devices across the
organization. Track inventory, coordinate shipments, and maintain organization of telematics devices and related equipment. Troubleshoot telematics hardware and
software issues and assist with replacement device processing, including return merchandise authorizations (RMA). Build, maintain, and enhance dashboards and
reports using Tableau and other reporting tools to provide operational insights. Analyze telematics and operational data to identify trends, opportunities, and
areas for process improvement. Create and maintain clear, easy-to-follow work instructions, job aids, and support documentation. Assist in developing and
standardizing procedures related to telematics administration and reporting activities. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support operational and
technology initiatives. Demonstrate initiative, professionalism, adaptability, attention to detail, and a commitment to continuous learning while working
independently and as part of a team. Perform all responsibilities in accordance with the Company’s Mission, Vision, Values, policies, procedures, and Code of
Ethical Business Conduct. Qualifications Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Engineering, or a
related field. Rising Junior or Senior preferred. Familiarity with Tableau or other data visualization and reporting tools. Strong analytical, organizational,
and problem-solving skills. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and effectively manage
multiple priorities. Ability to collaborate effectively in a team-based environment. Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy. Interest in fleet
technology, telematics systems, operational analytics, and business process improvement. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, is
preferred. Must be available to work onsite at a Martin Marietta office location; this position is not remote. Why Martin Marietta? At Martin Marietta, we
invest in developing future leaders. Our Telematics Internship provides meaningful project work, experienced mentors, and exposure to technology solutions that
support one of North America's leading building materials companies. Interns gain valuable hands-on experience in telematics administration, operational
