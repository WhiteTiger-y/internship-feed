What You Will Do
Your specific responsibilities will be defined based on your background and the current needs of the business. Examples might include some of the following:
- Depositing and characterizing thin film layer materials to understand how their deposition conditions and properties affect their response to heat treatment
or how they impact durability.
- Testing new process technologies such as ion beam assist or high power impulse magnetron sputtering to determine the impact on coating behavior.
- Developing new test methods to improve our knowledge of coating materials so that we might more effectively transfer processes from development into
production and from one production site to another.
- Develop methods to deploy AI in our research to more efficiently build knowledge and wisdom from data and information.
- Identify the factors that control coating response to heat treatment by comparing production and development conditions and executing systematic experiments.
- Learn and apply a wide range of thin film and materials characterization techniques including stress measurement as a function of temperature, electrochemical
impedance spectroscopy (EIS), ellipsometry, spectral reflection and transmission, x-ray diffraction (XRD), scanning electron microscopy (SEM).
- Create new software that allows more efficient product change-overs and enables effective deployment of optical models into production.
- Characterize the process and behavior of polymer protective coatings to improve manufacturing efficiency.
- Identify and develop durability enhancing processes to improve product manufacturability.
- Model interactions between birds and glass to improve our understanding of avian vision and improve effectiveness of bird deterrent products.
