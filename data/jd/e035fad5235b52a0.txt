About Trace
Trace is building the data, infrastructure, and research that enable robots to learn from the real world. General-purpose robotics requires a deeper
understanding of how robots learn from real-world data. Our research explores how to transform human experiences, including egocentric video, demonstrations,
hand-object interactions, and multimodal sensory signals, into transferable representations, actionable knowledge, and generalizable robot behaviors. At our
Brooklyn research lab, we combine fundamental research in robot learning, computer vision, and embodied AI with large-scale datasets, machine learning
infrastructure, and real-world robotic experimentation. Our goal is to advance the science of robot learning while developing the datasets and learning
foundations for increasingly capable, adaptable, and reliable robots.
About The Role
We are looking for highly motivated and technically curious Robotics Engineering Interns to join our team in a hands-on hardware-and-software role, contributing
to the development of robotic systems, data infrastructure, and experimental platforms that support cutting-edge research in robot learning and embodied AI.
This internship is designed primarily for Master's and undergraduate students, although PhD students are also welcome to apply. We value practical robotics
experience, strong engineering fundamentals, problem-solving ability, and a genuine interest in robotics research. Prior research publications are not required.
As a Robotics Engineering Intern, you will work closely with our research scientists, research interns, and engineers to build and operate robotic platforms,
develop reliable data collection and processing pipelines, implement and evaluate learning-based robotic systems, and support experiments that connect human and
robot data to downstream policy performance. You will have opportunities to take ownership of well-defined engineering and experimental projects, from hardware
and software integration through implementation, testing, evaluation, and documentation. Depending on your background and interests, you may also contribute to
research experiments, algorithm development, and technical publications. Our goal is to provide meaningful hands-on experience at the intersection of robotics
engineering and robot learning research, while giving students opportunities to develop practical skills, deepen their research understanding, and contribute to
advances in physical AI.

What You’ll Learn And Build
- Build and integrate robotic systems, working with robot arms, dexterous hands, sensors, cameras, and associated software and hardware interfaces.
- Develop and maintain robotics software and data pipelines for data acquisition, synchronization, processing, storage, visualization, and experimental
evaluation.

experiments in simulation and/or on real robotic platforms.

- Debug and troubleshoot complex robotic systems, identifying issues across hardware, perception, control, data processing, and learning components.
- Analyze experimental results and failure cases, helping the research team understand how system design, data collection, and implementation choices affect
robotic learning and performance.

robotic systems.
- Document implementations, experimental procedures, and results to support reproducibility, knowledge sharing, and continued development.
