The Summer 2027 Enterprise Data Governance (EDGO) Undergraduate Internship provides students with an opportunity to gain practical, hands-on experience
supporting Highmark Health's enterprise Data Governance program. This internship is designed to develop the next generation of business and data professionals
through direct exposure to enterprise data management, data governance, data quality, metadata management, analytics, artificial intelligence (AI), and
healthcare data operations. As a member of the Enterprise Data Governance Office (EDGO), the intern will work closely with Data Governance leaders, business
Data Owners and Data Stewards, technology teams, architects, analytics professionals, and business partners to improve how critical enterprise data is defined,
documented, measured, governed, and utilized across the organization. The program provides hands-on training and exposure to a variety of career opportunities
within Data Governance, Data Management, Data Quality, Business Analytics, Information Management, Data Architecture, AI Governance, and Healthcare Technology.
Interns will gain practical experience working with enterprise data domains such as Member, Provider, and Clinical data while helping establish frameworks that
improve data quality, consistency, transparency, and business value. Typical responsibilities include supporting Data Governance initiatives, documenting
Critical Data Elements (CDEs), researching business definitions and data requirements, analyzing governance and data quality metrics, maintaining governance
documentation, supporting governance working sessions, developing dashboards and reporting tools, documenting data lineage, and contributing to process
improvement and automation initiatives. Through mentorship, project-based learning, and cross-functional collaboration, participants will build strong
analytical, technical, communication, and business skills while developing a foundation in enterprise data governance and healthcare data management. SUMMARY OF
JOB RESPONSIBILITIES This job has the goal of providing the opportunity to acquire practical experience through direct exposure to the related business line(s)
and to develop the next generation of diverse business professionals. To that end, the program will provide hands-on training and experience in the business

recommendations or proposals for action. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS Full or part-time enrollment in an accredited college or university baccalaureate program.
