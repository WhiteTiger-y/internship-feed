Your Team:
ServiceNow has a large and highly skilled security team located at multiple sites globally and as part of a rapidly growing organization ServiceNow is looking
to recruit students in their final year of education (college or university) to a Security Intern position within Field Security. This role is an opportunity to
gain real-world experience and work across multiple Security teams, which are growing rapidly, while finishing up your degrees. This is also an opportunity to
learn ServiceNow’s platform, one of the fastest growing SaaS services amongst large enterprises.
What you get to do in this role:
- As an engineer on the ServiceNow Product Security Team, you will be responsible in identifying security vulnerabilities within our platform.
- In this role, you will interface with external researchers and customers that perform security assessments against ServiceNow.
- You will have the opportunity to run dynamic security testing tools, plan projects, and be a security advocate.
- A key part of this position is to effectively report issues to application owners, provide meaningful remediation recommendations, and validate that issues
have been resolved.
To be successful in this role you have:
- Currently enrolled in an accredited university, working on a Masters degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science or related field and graduating
December 2027 or later.
- Experience in leveraging or critically thinking about how to integrate AI into work processes, decision-making, or problem-solving. This may include using AI-
powered tools, automating workflows, analyzing AI-driven insights, or exploring AI's potential impact on the function or industry.
- Working knowledge of software development, including writing, reading, and debugging code in a common language.
- Foundational understanding of AI or supply chain security concepts, such as dependency risk, software bills of materials, or model risk.
- Prior experience in web security/strong familiarity with OWASP.
- Strong understanding of web and mobile application security assessment techniques.
- Ability to articulate complex issues to executives and customers.
- Experience working with the ServiceNow Platform a plus.
- Security certifications a plus.
- Hands-on experience building or integrating tools into pipelines is a plus.
- Familiarity with MCP or other agent-to-tool integration patterns is a plus.
- Experience taking a prototype through testing toward a production-ready service is a plus.
