About Dr. Nwabufo’s Research Program
Dr. Nwabufo’s innovative research program focuses on developing novel technologies and methods grounded in pharmacology, immunology, biochemistry, and
engineering principles to transform how we diagnose diseases, develop drugs, and prescribe treatments, improving clinical outcomes for patients with life-
threatening diseases.
The Position
We have documented an extensive body of work using in vitro, in vivo, ex vivo, and in silico models to demonstrate how SARS-CoV-2 infection affects drug
processing and therapeutic outcomes in the respiratory system, to inform the detection and management of patients with disease-drug interactions. As such, we
have an exciting opportunity for highly motivated research interns to work on several ongoing review papers that summarize what we have learned about the
immunology and pharmacology of SARS-CoV-2 infection and the corresponding drug development programs. This is a research volunteer position beginning in November
and continuing through the upcoming quarters. The position is open to students.
Suitable candidates will typically have extensive demonstrable skills and experience in:
1. infectious diseases and immunology
2. pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics
3. prior scientific writing and/or scientific publication
4. scientific research and a penchant for graduate education
