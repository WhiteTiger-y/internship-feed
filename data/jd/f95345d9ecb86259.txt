The Role
Joining Moderna means advancing mRNA science to transform medicine. Work with exceptional global teams on a broad pipeline and build a career that makes a real
difference for patients.
Moderna is strengthening its presence in Cambridge, Massachusetts, home to our global headquarters and a center of mRNA innovation. Working closely with leading
universities and research institutions, our Cambridge team advances groundbreaking science that shapes the future of medicine. We welcome talent driven by
innovation and committed to improving global health.
This individual-contributor co-op opportunity within Moderna’s Global Clinical Operations Strategic Business Analytics team offers hands-on exposure to the
financial, contractual, operational, and analytical foundations of clinical trial execution. You will work alongside experienced colleagues to support
investigator site budgets, clinical trial agreements, study start-up activities, financial data management, reporting, and collaboration with Contract Research
Organizations.
You will build foundational experience across investigator grant budgeting, budget tracking, contract management, Fair Market Value benchmarking, data
ingestion, financial analytics, and business operations while contributing to projects designed to improve the efficient execution of clinical trials. The role
also provides opportunities to work with evolving digital, automation, and Generative AI-enabled approaches that can improve data quality, reporting efficiency,
analysis, and operational decision-making.
This opportunity is designed for a curious, detail-oriented, and organized college student who is eager to learn in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.
Previous pharmaceutical industry or professional work experience is not required.
Here's What You'll Do
- Support the Investigator Site Budgets & Contracts team with the preparation, review, organization, and maintenance of investigator site budget information for
clinical trials.
- Perform data entry, validation, and maintenance of investigator grant budget information using Excel and other internal budgeting tools.
- Review budget data to help identify discrepancies, missing information, and inconsistencies for follow-up by senior team members.
- Gain practical exposure to Fair Market Value benchmarking concepts and understand how FMV data supports investigator site budget development and negotiation.
- Collect, organize, and analyze historical investigator grant budget information to support future clinical trial budget planning and financial analysis.
- Analyze historical investigator grant costs, site attributes, and financial benchmarks to identify trends and support more data-driven budget negotiations.
- Maintain trackers and reports covering clinical trial agreements, investigator site budgets, and contract negotiation progress.
- Monitor key milestones and timelines associated with investigator site budget negotiations, contract progression, and clinical trial site activation.
- Organize and maintain budget- and contract-related documentation so information remains accurate, complete, accessible, and ready for team use.
- Identify outstanding items and track follow-up activities under the guidance of experienced team members.
- Support the development and ongoing maintenance of Excel-based reports, dashboards, and trackers used to monitor investigator site budget and contract
activities.
- Compile and summarize key performance indicators, including contract negotiation cycle times, budget status, and site activation milestones.
- Perform foundational data analysis to identify trends, inconsistencies, and opportunities to strengthen reporting.
- Prepare summaries, charts, presentations, and other materials that communicate budget, contract, and financial information clearly to the team.
- Support the ingestion, validation, standardization, and integration of investigator grant and clinical study financial data from multiple sources for
departmental analytics.
- Develop ad hoc and recurring departmental reports, dashboards, and customized data views that address business reporting needs, support investigator grant
negotiations, enable study financial oversight, and contribute to departmental objectives.
- Gain exposure to the systems and tools supporting financial data ingestion and analytics, including opportunities to improve data quality, reporting
efficiency, standardization, and automation.
- Explore how emerging digital and Generative AI tools can support data validation, analytical workflows, reporting, and operational efficiency while working
within Moderna’s established processes and governance.
- Assist with documenting departmental processes, workflows, and standard operating procedures related to investigator site budgets and contracts.
- Contribute to projects focused on improving data quality, reporting efficiency, and the standardization of budget and contract tracking activities.
- Participate in team meetings to build an understanding of clinical trial operations, study start-up, financial oversight, and cross-functional collaboration.
- Collaborate with colleagues on special projects and other operational activities as assigned.
- Develop an understanding of how Investigator Site Budgets & Contracts partners with Clinical Operations, Legal, Clinical Contracts, Outsourcing, and Contract
Research Organization partners to support clinical trial execution.
- Collaborate with the Strategic Business Analytics team on financial data integration, reporting, analytics, and broader departmental initiatives.
- Build practical experience across financial analysis, data management, business operations, clinical research, investigator grant budgeting, contract
management, and clinical study start-up while contributing to the effective execution of Moderna’s global clinical trials.
