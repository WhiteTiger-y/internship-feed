Flexible working
Flexible working
Getting face-to-face is something we value and is essential for our roles and to support our growth. We operate a hybrid working policy, with staff in the
office at least three days a week, with the option to work two days from home.
Many of our staff work flexibly in other ways, including part-time, We also offer relocation assistance. Please talk to us at interview about the flexibility
and support you need.
Application process
This is very much a two-way process - a chance for you to learn more about the role and Riverlane and for us to find out about you and your work experience.
Our recruitment process is transparent and relaxed. You can expect the following:
01
HR interview
We want to learn more about you, your job motivations and how well you align with our
values. The interview also gives us a chance to answer your questions about the role and
what it's like to work at Riverlane.
30 min
02
Technical interview
Our hiring managers want to understand more about your technical skills and experience
and what you can contribute to the role. They will also provide more details about the
role and answer any questions you may have.
45 min
03
Skills challenge
To help us assess your technical capabilities, we will give you one week to solve a
role-specific technical challenge. Feel free to ask questions along the way!
1 week
04
Final interview
You've made it to the end of the process! Your final interview takes place at our
Cambridge office or via Teams, introducing you to some of our technical team and a senior
staff member. We'll ask you to discuss your submitted solutions to the technical
challenge, and the team will dig deeper into your skills and experience.
90 min
