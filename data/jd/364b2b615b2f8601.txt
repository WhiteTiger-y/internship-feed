Raytheon has an opportunity for an energetic and motivated intern in Test Engineering. An internship with Engineering will allow for broad exposure to both
senior leadership and Enterprise-wide initiatives as well as learning and using Raytheon’s tools and resources. Raytheon Vision Systems is seeking a summer
intern to support the Engineering Department.
The ideal candidate should have strong academic credentials and experience in working in a teamwork environment, having not only academic, but also
interpersonal skills to help optimize our teams. Additionally, the candidate should possess an inquisitive nature and a strong desire to learn real world
applications to complement their academic experience.
This position is an onsite role in Goleta, CA.
What You Will Do:
- You will support a multi-disciplinary engineering team completed tasks such as planning, development, trade studies, and performance analysis.
- You will perform integration, verification and validation testing to support delivery of new EO/IR Sensor Systems.
- You will support execution and procurement activities in support of an engineering build.
- You will collaborate with operations and manufacturing teams.
Qualifications You Must Have:
- Must be pursuing Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Science, Technology, Engineering or Mathematics (STEM) and actively enrolled through the completion of the
internship/co-op session.
- Must have a minimum of 60 hours of credit by the start of the internship / co-op session.
- Experience with programming in LABVIEW and/or MATLAB for test automation and/or data analysis
Experience may be obtained through on-the-job experience, research, coursework, or projects.
Qualifications We Prefer:
- Experience with instrumentation and collection of large data sets, including the application of scripting or automation to data collection and analysis
- Prior internship experience
