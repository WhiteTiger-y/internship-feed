BRING YOUR ENERGY TO AN OUTSTANDING INTERNSHIP.
Our internships allow you to provide your energy, your collaboration, and your contribution as you experience the same types of challenges as our full-time
hires. We believe internships are a great way for us to get to know you and for you to get to know our company. The majority of our University New Hires come
through our intern program. Get more information at careers.phillips66.com.
Summer internship activities may include the following:
- Orientation and training events, field trips, social activities, weekly seminars and opportunities to meet and interact with functional leaders.
- Participating in continuous coaching conversations with your manager, who will provide direction throughout your internship.
- Provided with an assigned mentor for further guidance and to help you with the technical challenges of your role and give assistance in navigating through the
day-to-day questions regarding working in the corporate world.
- Giving a presentation at the end of the summer to senior business leaders and key stakeholders. This is an opportunity for you to demonstrate what you learned
in your internship and provide insight on your experience.
At the Phillips 66 Bartlesville Energy Research and Innovation (ERI) organization, employees from multiple business disciplines deliver critical support and
solutions that enable strong operating excellence and growth opportunities for the company worldwide.
A summer internship with the Phillips 66 Energy Research and Innovation (ERI) Digital Products team provides an opportunity to work at the intersection of
engineering, data, software, and business value. Projects vary widely in scope, ranging from involving modeling, optimization tools, dashboards, data pipelines,
and decision-support applications. A successful intern has a customer-first mindset and is a self-starter who can learn quickly, collaborate effectively, and
work independently.
- Learn about the Energy, Research and Innovation organization by working with experts throughout the company.
- Collaborate with business units across the company, including refining, midstream, commercial, and renewables, to bring data and digital solutions to the
field.
Key candidate characteristics include, but not limited to:
- Problem-solving capability
- Attention to detail
- Solution-oriented
- Customer and user focus
- Values collaboration
- Communication skills
- Innovative thinking
Requirements for a Phillips 66 Internship:
Basic/Required:
- Legally authorized to work in the job posting country
- Currently enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate program in engineering, computer science, data science, data analytics, information systems, or a related
field.
- Demonstrated technical project experience through coursework, research, internships, co-ops, or personal projects involving data analysis, programming,
software development, engineering modeling, process improvement, or visualization.
- Available for a minimum of ten continuous weeks
- Available to work at the Bartlesville, OK research facility
Preferred:
- Strong academic performance
- Programming experience or using analytical tools; including AI tools
- Exposure to data visualization, dashboards, APIs, databases, or data pipelines
- Experience using Git/GitHub, software testing, or collaborative development practices
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint
Compensation Range
This position has a pay range of $2,404.00 – $3,325.00 bi-weekly, dependent on a candidate's degree type and expected graduation date.
