Overview
Research Internships at Microsoft provide a dynamic environment for research careers with a network of world-class research labs led by globally-recognized
scientists and engineers, who pursue innovation in a range of scientific and technical disciplines to help solve complex challenges in diverse fields, including
computing, healthcare, economics, and the environment.
We are hiring a Research Intern to make coding agents better, cheaper and faster through post-training: reinforcement learning, fine-tuning, distillation,
compression and inference optimization.
Coding agents now plan, edit repositories, run tests and fix failures over long, multi-step trajectories. Making them reliable and affordable is a post-training
problem as much as a modeling one. Our team trains, evaluates and ships agentic coding models that power developer tools at scale.
As a Research Intern, you will own a research question end to end; form a hypothesis, build the experiment, measure it on realistic agent benchmarks, and push
results toward a paper or a product integration. You will work with researchers and engineers across model training, agent infrastructure and hardware
acceleration.
Responsibilities
Research Interns put inquiry and theory into practice. Alongside fellow doctoral candidates and some of the world’s best researchers, Research Interns learn,
collaborate, and network for life. Research Interns not only advance their own careers, but they also contribute to exciting research and development strides.
During the 12-week internship, Research Interns are paired with mentors and expected to collaborate with other Research Interns and researchers, present
findings, and contribute to the vibrant life of the community. Research internships are available in all areas of research, and are offered year-round, though
they typically begin in the summer.

Preferred Qualifications
- At least one additional quarter/semester of school remaining following the completion of the internship.
- Research experience in at least one of: LLM post-training (RL, SFT, preference optimization), model compression, or efficient inference.
- Proficient Python and PyTorch skills, with hands-on experience training or fine-tuning transformer models
- First-author publications at top venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, MLSys, ICSE, FSE) or equivalent research impact.
- Experience with RL for LLMs at scale using frameworks such as TRL, verl, OpenRLHF or DeepSpeed-Chat.
- Experience building or training code-generation models or coding agents, and with benchmarks such as SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, Terminal-Bench or HumanEval.
- Hands-on work with quantization, distillation, pruning or speculative decoding; familiarity with serving stacks such as vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM or ONNX
Runtime.
- Familiarity with distributed training (FSDP, DeepSpeed, Megatron) and GPU profiling or kernel work (CUDA, Triton).
- Clear communication, self-direction, curiosity, and a bias toward hands-on experimentation.
- Experience collaborating across teams, disciplines and cultures.
