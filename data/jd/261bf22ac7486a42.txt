benefits for eligible candidates. A Day in the Life Day in the Life of a Sustainability Intern As a Sustainability Intern, you will support Burlington’s
Sustainability, Environmental Compliance and Analytics team through hands-on exposure to environmental data analytics, renewable energy management,
environmental compliance, and cross-functional collaboration. You will gain real-world experience through: Sustainability Data Analytics Analyzing data trends
and anomalies in energy and waste performance and preparing summaries to support internal reporting and decision-making. Supporting the development and
maintenance of forecasting models for energy and waste data, including updating inputs and assisting with output analysis. Coordinating with Energy Engineering
and internal teams to develop and implement project planning based on environmental analytics findings, including opportunity identification, resolution
tracking progress, and resulting savings models and validation. Renewable Energy Analytics Compiling and tracking renewable energy credits (RECs) from multiple
sources. Monitoring and managing active solar arrays to ensure optimal performance. This includes energy production trending and initiating repairs and
maintenance for owned solar. Supporting improvements to renewable energy data tracking and reporting processes. Environmental Compliance Coordinating data and
documentation required for sustainability disclosures. Assisting the Sustainability team in the preparation of data sets and reporting for various federal,
state, and local environmental compliance programs. Assisting in identifying and addressing gaps in data controls and compliance processes. You'll Come With Our
Sustainability Interns Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's degree program in Sustainability, Environmental Science, Environmental Engineering, Data Analytics,

Microsoft Word. Effective written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and functions. Demonstrated interest in
learning, problem solving, and applying new concepts. Ability to manage multiple tasks, maintain attention to detail, and stay organized in a dynamic
