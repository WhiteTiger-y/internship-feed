Overview
Susquehanna’s energy and power trading is informed by a strong fundamental understanding of weather prediction as well as supply and demand characteristics. As
an Electricity Strategist Intern on Susquehanna's Energy Research team, you will be partnered with our senior strategists and traders to identify information
and datasets to improve Susquehanna’s modeling and understanding the United States power grid.
What we’re looking for
- Intention to graduate with a bachelor’s or master’s degree within one year of the internship program in Electrical Engineering or a similar major
- Creative problem solving and intellectual curiosity about electricity markets and fundamentals
- Strong attention to detail; ability to complete data entry and mapping
- Knowledge of technologies related to specific technical domain (including but not limited to: Python, Pandas, MySQL)
- Must be a self-starter, a quick learner, an effective communicator, and resourceful in solving problems

About Susquehanna
Susquehanna is a global quantitative trading firm powered by scientific rigor, curiosity, and innovation. Our culture is intellectually driven and highly
collaborative, bringing together researchers, engineers, and traders to design and deploy impactful strategies in our systematic trading environment. To meet
the unique challenges of global markets, Susquehanna applies machine learning and advanced quantitative research to vast datasets in order to uncover actionable
insights and build effective strategies. By uniting deep market expertise with cutting-edge technology, we excel in solving complex problems and pushing
boundaries together.
