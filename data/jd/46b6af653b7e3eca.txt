The University of Miami/UHealth Department of SCCC has an exciting opportunity for a Full-Time Data Science & AI Intern to work at UHealth in Miami, Florida.
Core Job Summary
The Sylvester Data Science and AI Innovations Internship Program provides supervised training opportunities for students, trainees, research fellows, and others
interested in developing tools to analyze and visualize clinical and research data in oncology. Daily activities will include processing and analyzing
molecular, sociodemographic, psychosocial, patient‑reported, lifestyle, wearable, and other clinical and research data, as well as publicly available
population‑ and neighborhood‑level datasets. These activities occur across Sylvester’s research laboratories and the Sylvester Survivorship and Supportive Care
Institute.
As a Casual Worker, this individual supplies services on an irregular or flexible basis to meet the University’s fluctuating demand for work. Additionally, the
Casual Worker assists the assigned department(s) in meeting strict deadlines to advance the institution’s related goals and objectives.
Core Job Functions
- Performs assigned tasks and projects.
- Provides clerical, research, professional or technical support to assigned division.
- Adheres to all company policies and procedures.
- Meets or exceeds applicable goals and deadlines.
- Develops relationships with department staff and other parties to carry out job functions.
- Keeps supervising staff members informed of work progress and any issues encountered.
- Adheres to University and unit-level policies and procedures and safeguards University assets. This list of duties and responsibilities is not intended to be
all-inclusive and may be expanded to include other duties or responsibilities as necessary.
DEPARTMENT SPECIFIC:
- Develop foundational knowledge of oncology research with emphasis on sociodemographic, psychosocial, biobehavioral, lifestyle, wearable, patient-reported
outcomes, psychosocial and behavioral intervention mechanisms, molecular (e.g., genomic), and digital health data.
- Support supervised data cleaning, documentation, harmonization, and integration of diverse clinical and research datasets.
- Assist with analytic and technical activities that link individual-level clinical, genomics, digital health, and patient-reported outcomes data with
sociodemographic, neighborhood, and population-level data.
- Contribute to research examining how clinical, behavioral, social, and population-level factors guide cancer care delivery and influence outcomes across
cancer sites and treatment modalities.
- Support the development and evaluation of AI-driven risk algorithms, analytic tools, dashboards, and AI-enabled applications that enhance visualization,
interpretation, and use of clinical and research data.
- Support the development and evaluation of digital health, precision medicine, and other supportive care tools and software.
- Assist in developing and implementing risk-tailored, AI-powered adjunctive supportive care interventions delivered through eHealth platforms.
- Participate in regular one-on-one and small-group meetings with mentors and program faculty to review data pipelines, troubleshoot methods, refine research
questions, and clarify role expectations.
