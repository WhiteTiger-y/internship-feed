Data Analyst Summer Internship
Systems & Technology Research (STR) is a growing technology company. We specialize in advanced research and development for defense, intelligence, and national
security.
We are seeking a Data Analyst Intern to support data analysis, business systems, process improvement, and operational documentation across STR. This is an in-
person role out of our Arlington, VA or Woburn, MA site. This position will provide hands-on experience in data interpretation, reporting, workflow analysis,
software applications, and the development of standardized procedures and training materials. The intern will collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve
data visibility, streamline business processes, maintain accurate documentation, and support operational efficiency.
Responsibilities
In this role, you will:
- Assist with collecting, organizing, validating, and analyzing data to support business operations and internal decision-making.
- Develop and maintain spreadsheets, reports, dashboards, and data trackers using Microsoft Excel and other analytical tools.
- Analyze data trends, identify discrepancies, and summarize findings to support operational reporting and process improvement initiatives.
- Support the use and administration of business systems, software applications, databases, and other operational tools.
- Evaluate existing workflows and business processes to identify inefficiencies, opportunities for improvement, and potential areas for automation or
standardization.
- Assist with documenting business processes, developing standard operating procedures (SOPs), and maintaining clear, consistent process documentation.
- Create training documentation, instructional materials, and presentations to support employee education and the adoption of business systems and processes.
- Develop and update user guides, training materials, and presentations using Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, and applicable content development tools such as
Articulate.
- Assist with organizing, maintaining, and improving SharePoint sites, document libraries, and other document management systems.
- Support data and document quality initiatives by maintaining accurate records, validating information, and ensuring documentation is current and accessible.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements, troubleshoot system or process issues, and communicate findings and recommendations.
- Assist with implementing standardized reporting methods, improving data accessibility, and enhancing operational workflows.
- Perform other duties as assigned.
Requirements
- Currently pursuing a degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Analytics, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Must be a U.S. citizen due to the nature of STR's work and applicable regulations.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret data, recognize patterns, and identify opportunities for improvement.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.
- Experience with or an interest in data visualization and reporting tools such as Power BI, as well as querying languages such as SQL, is preferred.
- Ability to learn new software applications, business systems, and technical tools quickly.
- Strong written communication skills, with the ability to develop clear, organized, and user-friendly documentation, SOPs, and training materials.
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, particularly when working with data, documentation, and business processes.
- Familiarity with or an interest in SharePoint, document management, workflow analysis, and process standardization.
- Self-motivated, adaptable, and collaborative, with the ability to work independently and communicate effectively with cross-functional teams.
- Previous experience with data analysis, reporting, technical documentation, process improvement, or business systems is beneficial but not required.
