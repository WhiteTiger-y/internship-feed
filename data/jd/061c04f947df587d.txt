About the team
The Enterprise SG&A team within Corporate Finance builds the systems that power Anduril's SG&A finance teams. That means the data pipelines, automations,
dashboards, and AI tools that finance partners use to plan, forecast, report, and close. As Anduril scales, our job is to make sure the finance organization's
tooling scales with it.
We're looking for an intern who wants to work where finance, data, and AI meet. You'll work with real data from day one, ship tools that finance teams and
department owners use every month, and take on a wide range of projects.
What you'll do
- Build and maintain automations that replace manual steps in month-end close, reporting, and planning workflows
- Write SQL and Python against Anduril's financial data warehouse (Databricks) to pull, clean, and analyze GL, vendor, and headcount data
- Design self-serve dashboards and tools so finance partners and department owners can get answers without ad hoc data requests
- Apply AI to finance workflows, including building prompts, agents, and reusable skills, and testing their output for accuracy before it reaches users
- Set up scheduled tasks and data refreshes so recurring deliverables run on their own
- Partner with FP&A, Accounting, and business owners to understand how they work today, find bottlenecks, and design and document better processes
- Build checks that catch data issues early, such as miscoded charges, duplicate spend, or breaks between source systems
- Take projects from a rough idea through build, testing, and handoff, and keep improving them after they launch
We're looking for someone who treats the initial ask as a starting point. If you're asked for a one-time analysis and see that it should be a repeatable tool,
or you're fixing a broken number and find the upstream cause, we want you to chase it.

Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams
Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated
phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers
often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.
To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:
No Financial Requests: Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social
security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
- Please always verify communications:
- Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will only come from an @anduril.com address.
- Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious
activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to contact@anduril.com .
