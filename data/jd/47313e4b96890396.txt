Grad Intern – AI-Enabled Knowledge Graphs for Patient Journey Analytics (Summer 2027)
What You Will Do
Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. During this program, you will explore how artificial intelligence, knowledge graphs, and graph analytics can be used to
better understand patient journeys and address complex epidemiology and real-world evidence questions—ultimately supporting our mission to serve patients.
Responsibilities may include:
- Design and develop a prototype knowledge graph representing longitudinal patient journeys, including relevant relationships among patient characteristics,
treatments, clinical events, and outcomes
- Identify and apply established graph algorithms to investigate epidemiology questions that can be challenging to address using traditional tabular data
structures and predictive modeling approaches
- Explore approaches for stratifying patients based on treatment patterns and outcomes and for identifying patient subgroups with similar clinical journeys
- Use AI-assisted tools and methods to support project development—for example, accelerating knowledge graph design, data mapping, code development,
documentation, and quality review—while applying appropriate human oversight, validation, and responsible AI practices
- Evaluate additional use cases, such as identifying common treatment pathways, characterizing transitions in care, and assessing relationships among
treatments, clinical events, and outcomes
- Compare knowledge graph–based methods with selected traditional analytical approaches, documenting their relative strengths, limitations, and appropriate
applications
- Deliver reusable analytical code, technical documentation, a working prototype or demonstration, and a final presentation summarizing findings, organizational
value, and recommendations for future development
