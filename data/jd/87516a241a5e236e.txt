At Toyota Research Institute (TRI), we’re on a mission to improve the quality of human life. We’re developing new tools and capabilities to amplify the human
experience. To lead this transformative shift in mobility, we’ve built a world-class team advancing the state of the art in AI, robotics, driving, and material

This is a paid 12-week internship opportunity and is a hybrid, in-office role.
Here’s a glimpse into the Internship experience from some of our TRI interns!
The Vision
We envision a future with robots that help people in real environments through interactions that allow robots to understand, adapt, and grow with us. We believe
robots should work well alongside people, helping where wanted and enabling people to spend more time on the activities they enjoy most. Achieving this vision
requires robots that operate reliably in messy, unstructured environments and that people can understand, collaborate with, and rely on.
The Team
Our goal is to revolutionize robotics by enabling long-horizon dexterous behaviors to be efficiently taught, learned, and improved over time in diverse, real-
world environments with people.
Our team brings together expertise in hardware, simulation, perception, controls, machine learning, and human-robot interaction. PROps supports this research
through robot operations, data collection, fleet maintenance, prototyping, lab infrastructure, and the processes that keep this work safe and consistent.
The Opportunity
As a Robotics Operations Intern, you will work with the PROps team to learn the end-to-end robotic data collection pipeline and help improve the processes that
support it. During this internship, you will gain hands-on experience with robot operations while helping to maintain the documentation of our workflows and
developing practical improvements for the team. This internship is suited to someone who enjoys learning technical systems and making complex work clearer, more
consistent, and easier to execute.
Responsibilities
Learn and support the end-to-end robotic data collection pipeline, including system setup, teleoperation, data handling, validation, and issue reporting.
Participate in supervised robot operation and data collection to understand operator workflows and operational constraints firsthand.
Document current workflows and maintain standard operating procedures, checklists, training materials, and other operational resources.
Help organize operational information and track basic measures that support scheduling, planning, and continuous improvement.
Collaborate with robot operators, operations specialists, engineers, researchers, and project managers to understand requirements and incorporate feedback.
Assist with the setup and maintenance of robotic systems and workstations while following all safety protocols.
Summarize findings, completed improvements, and recommended next steps for the PROps team at the end of the internship.
