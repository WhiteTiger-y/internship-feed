The Econometrics team is responsible for developing analytical capabilities for SAS Econometrics and SAS/ETS, helping users address complex problems through
classical and Bayesian methodology, causal discovery and causal inference, cross-sectional and panel data analysis, risk modeling, and simulation. The team
combines econometrics and software engineering expertise to build high-performance modeling tools, including work in emerging areas such as Causal Analysis
Agents.
As an intern, you might:
- Research econometric methods and computational algorithms, including approaches to causal discovery and causal inference.
- Contribute to research and development of agents for performing econometric analysis.
- Contribute to software development in Python or C/C++ to support SAS Econometrics capabilities.
- Design and run tests and simulation studies to assess model accuracy, computational performance, and software reliability.
- Create technical documentation and examples that demonstrate how to apply econometric methods to real-world problems.
- Collaborate with developers, domain experts, and testers, and present your project findings and recommendations to the team.
