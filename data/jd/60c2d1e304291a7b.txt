Who Are We?
The Toro Company (TTC) has delivered market-leading, innovative products and outstanding customer care for more than 100 years. With over 11,000 employees
worldwide, TTC is headquartered in Bloomington, Minnesota, USA.
Our company designs and manufactures turf maintenance equipment; turf irrigation systems; landscape and lighting products; snow and ice management solutions;
agricultural irrigation systems; rental, specialty and underground construction equipment; and residential turf and snow equipment. We market and sell TTC
products worldwide through a network of distributors, dealers, mass retailers, hardware retailers, equipment rental center and home centers—as well as online
direct to end users.
What Will You Do?
In order to grow and build a successful career with The Toro Company, you will be responsible for:
- Design, Develop, test and deploy AI and machine learning models for predictive analytics, classification, recommendation, anomaly detection, forecasting and
other business use cases.
- Validate, optimize, and continuously improve models for roll-out and analyze the performance of existing models.
- Assess and implement the usefulness of new data sources, transformations, and modeling techniques.
- Mine large datasets in multiple databases to draw patterns and inferences to build models and support leaders with information that will help make business
decisions.
- Support end-users to design strategies based on newly developed machine learning models.
- Conduct advanced analysis and identify business opportunities.
- Test, evaluate, and deploy new models and features to achieve better outcomes for key metrics.
- Work collaboratively with other team members, data engineering team, and business stakeholders to craft solutions with a holistic view of the problem.
- Present work to all levels of the organization including senior leadership. Translate data and models into compelling stories.
What Do You Need?
To be considered for this role, an individual should meet the following minimal requirements:
- Pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Information Science, Machine Learning, Statistics, Computer Science, Data Mining, Applied Mathematics,
Engineering, or other technology/quantitative program.
- Must be proficient in one/or more of the following: Python, R, Spark, Scala, SQL, or other analytical/statistical programming environment.
- Understanding of AI Engineering, Gen AI, RAG, MCP, supervised and unsupervised model building techniques, such as: neural networks, extreme gradient boosting
(XGBoost), support vector machines (SVM), gradient boosted models (GBM), and random forest.
- Hands on experience with information retrieval from high dimensional data, dimensionality reduction and feature extraction.
- Demonstrated ability to work independently and collaboratively as a team.
Preferred Qualifications
