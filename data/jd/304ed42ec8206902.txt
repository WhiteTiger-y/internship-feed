and a leader in sustainable products in the world. CULTURE IS KING AT POET We’re all on the same team. We always communicate. And we park our egos at the door.

It’s the best way to optimize health, wellness, and productivity in a sustainable way. We work hard. We play hard. We have frequent team events, volunteer
efforts, and we know how to celebrate wins! JOB SUMMARY & RESPONSIBILITIES POET is a fast-paced, data-driven biotech company which operates a complex business
affected by hundreds of variables, and data is used every day to help drive decisions and improve outcomes. Our data comes from many areas of the business,
including sensors in our bioethanol plants, commodity markets, financial systems, logistics, and other business applications. The Data Engineering Intern will
work alongside the Data Engineering team to gain hands-on experience with the systems and processes used to collect, organize, transform, and deliver data
throughout the organization. This internship is intended to provide meaningful project experience while introducing the intern to enterprise data systems,
tools, and development practices. This is an on-site role located in Sioux Falls, SD. Responsibilities include: Assist with building and maintaining data
pipelines used to collect, clean, transform, and deliver data. Write and troubleshoot SQL queries to retrieve, validate, and analyze data. Help identify and
resolve data quality issues. Assist with automating manual or repetitive data processes. Participate in testing and validating new or updated data solutions.
Work with Data Engineers, analysts, and business stakeholders to better understand how data is used throughout POET. Create and maintain documentation for data
processes, pipelines, and systems. Gain exposure to databases, orchestration tools, APIs, industrial data systems, and other enterprise technologies. Contribute
to one or more projects that provide measurable value to the Data Engineering team or the business. Since we’re all about teamwork and getting the job done,
your skills may be put to a lot of other uses! The POET Never Satisfied Internship openings are for Summer 2027. We post Internships in early September with the
intention of having interns selected by November 1st. QUALIFICATIONS & SKILLS Currently pursuing an Associate’s or Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science,
Information Systems, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field. Basic understanding of relational databases and SQL. Some programming or scripting
experience with languages or tools such as Python, SQL, PowerShell, C#, Java, or similar technologies is preferred. Interest in learning about data engineering,
automation, databases, and enterprise technology. Strong problem-solving and critical-thinking skills. Ability to learn new technologies and concepts quickly.
Good written and verbal communication skills. Attention to detail and a willingness to ask questions. Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.
Working knowledge of Microsoft Office applications and the ability to learn new programs. A SUCCESSFUL CANDIDATE WILL HAVE A strong desire to learn and develop
new technical skills. Curiosity and an interest in understanding how data and systems work. A strong customer service mindset with a value placed on
relationships. Demonstrated problem-solving and critical-thinking skills. The ability to take directions, receive feedback, and apply what they have learned.
The willingness to ask questions and seek help when needed. An innovative mindset and willingness to explore new ideas. Strong interpersonal skills and the
ability to work effectively with others. A high level of personal integrity. The ability to adapt to changing priorities and unfamiliar challenges. The ability
