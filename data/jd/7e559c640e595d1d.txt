About the Role We are seeking a motivated and analytical Summer Intern to join our Insurance Data and Technology team. This internship offers a unique
opportunity to work on real-world projects involving Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, insurance operations, and risk management technology. The intern
will collaborate with business and technology professionals to develop innovative solutions that improve operational efficiency, data quality, and decision-
making across the insurance lifecycle. Responsibilities Assist in the development and testing of AI-powered tools and automation solutions. Analyze insurance,
claims, and risk-related datasets to identify trends and business insights. Support enhancement and optimization of existing insurance technology platforms and
applications. Participate in requirements gathering, user interviews, and solution design sessions. Create reports, dashboards, and visualizations using
business intelligence tools. Document business processes, workflows, and technical solutions. Research emerging technology trends, including Generative AI,
machine learning, and automation. Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Risk, Claims, Operations, Data Analytics, and Technology. Present project
findings and recommendations to business leaders at the conclusion of the internship. Potential Internship Projects Examples of projects may include: Building
an AI agent for document extraction and data validation. Enhancing insurance technology platforms and workflow automation. Developing predictive analytics
models for claims or premium analysis. Creating Business Intelligence dashboards and operational reporting solutions. Evaluating opportunities to leverage
Generative AI within insurance business processes. Supporting data integration and modernization initiatives. Qualifications Required Current junior
undergraduate student at an accredited four-year institution Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in: Computer Science Information Systems Data
Analytics Data Science Business Analytics Engineering Mathematics Related field Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Basic understanding of databases
and data analysis concepts. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Ability to work independently and within a team environment. Preferred Experience
with Python, SQL, or similar programming languages. Familiarity with Power BI, Tableau, or data visualization tools. Interest in Artificial Intelligence and
Machine Learning. Understanding of cloud technologies and modern software development practices. Exposure to insurance, financial services, or risk management
concepts. What You'll Gain Hands-on experience working on enterprise technology initiatives. Exposure to insurance operations, risk management, and analytics.
Mentorship from technology and business leaders. Opportunity to present recommendations directly to senior leadership. Experience developing AI and automation
solutions with real business impact. Success Measures By the end of the internship, successful candidates will: Deliver a meaningful project with measurable
