We’re seeking AI & People Transformation Interns to help reimagine how our People teams work. You’ll examine how process design, data, and emerging AI tools can
help simplify and improve everyday experiences for employees, managers, recruiters, and People team partners.
Based in our New York City office and working within the People PMO, you’ll partner with mentors, project leaders, People teams, and IT to map operational
workflows, identify friction points and opportunities for AI or automation, and help assess or test practical solutions. We’re looking for interns who bring a
fresh perspective, look for opportunities to rethink existing workflows, and connect ideas across projects. The work is transformation-focused: looking beyond
existing processes to redesign work thoughtfully and build improvements that can scale.
What we are looking for in you
- In the process of receiving a Bachelor’s or Master's degree in business, management, operations, information systems, or business analytics; we welcome other
backgrounds that demonstrate an interest in operational improvement.
- Have hands-on exposure to AI tools and curiosity about how to use them effectively in practical workflows.
- Communicate clearly, work collaboratively, and bring curiosity, sound judgment, and eagerness to learn.
- You can research a process or question, synthesize what you learn, document it clearly, and approach problems in a structured way.
- You’re organized, attentive to detail, curious about employee or candidate experience, and motivated to make processes simpler.
- Have a minimum of one quarter/semester remaining in university studies after the internship concludes. Graduation date in Fall 2027 or Spring 2028.
- Must be authorized to work in the United States.
