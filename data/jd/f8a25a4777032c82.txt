About Cavalla
Cavalla is reimagining warehouse automation. Our autonomous forklifts automate repetitive tasks and can be reconfigured with the click of a button to support
the most demanding logistics operations. Learn more at Cavalla.ai.
We're backed by venture funds including Emerson Capital, Founder Inc, and the Thiel Foundation, plus angels from leadership at Google, Microsoft, and more. Our
core team comes from CMU, MIT, ETH Zurich, EPFL, IIT Madras, NASA JPL, and Caterpillar, and includes a Thiel Fellow.
The Role
We're looking for a Perception Engineering Intern to help our forklifts see and understand the warehouse around them. This is a 3-month internship (Can be
extended to full time depending on performance). You'll own real problems, ship code that runs on real robots, and see your work move freight.
What You'll Do
- Build vision pipelines that fuse camera and depth data into a reliable 3D picture of the scene
- Develop methods to recover the full 3D position and orientation of objects the forklift needs to engage with, accurately enough to act on
- Get models and algorithms running in real time on onboard compute
- Build evaluation tools and metrics
Traits We Look For
- Missionary: You care deeply about the work and want to build something that matters.
- Generalist: You understand that changing x in perception changes y and z in planning, control, and hardware.
- Obsessive: Obsession beats experience every time. You get hyperfocused on making things work.
- High Agency: Give you "make this work" and it gets done.
What We're Looking For
- At least 2-3 years of research experience working with perception models
- Solid foundation in 3D geometry, camera models, and coordinate transforms
- Experience working with point clouds or depth data
- Hands-on experience training and deploying deep learning models (PyTorch or similar)
- Comfortable debugging on real hardware with noisy, real-world data
Nice to Have
- PhD or Master's degree in Robotics, Computer Science, or a related field
- Publications at top computer vision or robotics venues (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICRA, RSS, CoRL, or similar)
- Research, competition robotics, or personal robot projects
Why Join
You'll work directly with a small, sharp founding team on real robots in real warehouses. You'll learn more in 3 months here than in years elsewhere, and strong
interns will have a path to a full-time role.
