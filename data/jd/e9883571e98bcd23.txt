Launch Your AI Career: AI Automation Intern
Step into the future of enterprise automation and build impactful artificial intelligence solutions that transform financial operations at scale. In this six-
month internship, you will develop cutting-edge AI applications, work directly with modern data architectures, and automate critical revenue accounting
workflows. You will gain hands-on experience solving complex problems while collaborating closely with experienced mentors.
About Us
Trimble is a global technology company that connects the physical and digital worlds, transforming the ways work gets done. With relentless innovation in
precise positioning, modeling and data analytics, Trimble enables essential industries including construction, geospatial and transportation. Whether it's
helping customers build and maintain infrastructure, design and construct buildings, optimize global supply chains or map the world, Trimble is at the
forefront, driving productivity and progress. For more information about Trimble (Nasdaq:TRMB), visit: www.trimble.com.
What Makes This Role Great
In this role, you will be at the forefront of designing and deploying revenue-intensive AI applications, directly influencing how data flows from upstream
systems to downstream financial reports. You will take ownership of building standalone AI components—including Cortex Analyst pipelines and intelligent
chatbots—while working with modern enterprise technologies.
Key Exciting Responsibilities
- Architect and deploy AI/ML models, scripts, and automated workflows to optimize revenue accounting, reconciliations, and reporting operations.
- Build and evaluate interactive AI chat assistants and large language model workflows to enhance team enablement and drive workflow automation.
- Partner with technical leads to support and develop integrations across revenue management engines, Salesforce, and modern database platforms.
- Design data intelligence dashboards to monitor real-time revenue system health and operational metrics.
- Take ownership of standalone technical initiatives from concept through deployment while managing deliverables with minimal day-to-day supervision.
Essential Skills & Experience
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Decision Sciences, or a related discipline.
- Strong foundational programming proficiency in Python and robust database skills in SQL.
- Practical understanding of Git workflows, version control fundamentals, and command-line interfaces.
- Foundational familiarity with Large Language Models (LLMs) and general artificial intelligence concepts.
- Proactive self-starter with strong analytical problem-solving abilities, high attention to detail, and excellent communication skills across global time
zones.
Bonus Points For
- Experience or strong willingness to learn Snowflake, Cortex Analyst, and modern cloud data pipelines.
- Familiarity with AI and LLM engineering techniques such as prompt engineering, RAG architectures, and agentic workflows.
- Exposure to PowerShell, deployment scripting, or multi-agent pipelines.
- Interest or prior coursework in finance, accounting concepts, or enterprise ERP systems.
Logistics

- Travel Requirement: 0%
