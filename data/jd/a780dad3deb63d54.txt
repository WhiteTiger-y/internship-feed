What you will be doing:
The department of Pathobiology in PSA includes members with broad and diverse scientific expertise in anatomic and clinical pathology, histology, and image
analysis providing support to discovery and developmental efforts on Vertex portfolio. We are seeking a summer intern to support a study evaluating an AI-based
tool for detecting and quantifying microscopic abnormalities in rodent and non-rodent tissues.
The intern will work with scientists and pathologists within the Pathobiology department to help train and optimize the tool and assess its performance on an
independent dataset. The position will provide an opportunity to build experience in discovery pathology and advanced training in state-of-the-art histology and
image analysis approaches and/or data interpretations.
Key Responsibilities
- Help organize and review digitized tissue images and associated study data.
- Support supervised model training by documenting relevant tissue changes and reviewing AI-generated results with the study pathologists.
- Assist with validation analyses, including assessments of detection accuracy and consistency of morphometric measurements.
- Summarize findings, document limitations, and present recommendations for further refinement.
- Organize and compile results in support of data interpretation and sharing with team members in a timely manner.
Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a degree in biology, biomedical sciences, data science, computer science, or a related field.
- Interest in digital pathology, image analysis, or AI applications in biomedical research.
- Strong attention to detail, analytical skills, and ability to communicate results clearly.
- Experience with histology, microscopy, or data analysis is a plus.
- Legal authorization to work in the United States, now and in the future. Please note that Vertex does not provide sponsorship for internships/co-ops or entry
level roles within this part of the organization.
- You must be enrolled in an advanced degree program if graduating before May 2027
- You must be available to work full-time, 40 hours per week from May – August 2027
