Our paid internship program offers an opportunity to build practical skills, grow your leadership capabilities, and see how your ideas can make an impact.
You’ll work alongside experienced professionals and connect with mentors, industry experts and senior leaders, while gaining career insights, building
relationships and exploring how CHS businesses work together to empower agriculture.
CHS has an exciting opportunity within our Information Technology division. We are seeking a Data Engineer Intern to join our Data Engineering team for the
summer 2027 and gain hands-on experience building scalable, governed, and business-aligned data products across the enterprise.
The ideal candidate is curious, analytical, eager to learn, and interested in data engineering, analytics, or cloud technologies. This internship provides an
opportunity to work alongside data engineers, analysts, data scientists, product owners, and business stakeholders while gaining practical experience with
modern data engineering tools, development practices, and enterprise data platforms.
Responsibilities
- Work alongside data engineers and business partners to understand business requirements, source systems, and analytics needs.
- Assist with building, testing, and maintaining data pipelines, transformations, data models, and analytics-ready datasets.
- Develop SQL and Python solutions with guidance from experienced engineers.
- Gain hands-on experience with Snowflake, dbt, AWS, Microsoft technologies, and other modern data engineering tools.
- Support the development of governed data products used for reporting, analytics, data science, automation, and business decision-making.
- Participate in testing, documentation, version control, and CI/CD development practices.
- Assist with investigating data quality issues, pipeline failures, and opportunities to improve or automate existing processes.
- Collaborate with data engineers, analysts, data scientists, DevOps teams, architects, and other technology partners.
- Learn and apply enterprise data governance concepts including access controls, naming standards, data quality, and documentation.
- Contribute to team discussions, technical problem-solving, and continuous improvement initiatives.
- Develop an understanding of how data engineering solutions move from business requirements through development and into production.
- Embody a culture of ownership, curiosity, collaboration, continuous improvement, innovation, and learning.
Minimum Qualifications (required)
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Technology, Management Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics, or
a related field.
Additional Qualifications
- Foundational knowledge of SQL and/or Python through coursework, personal projects, internships, or other hands-on experience.
- Basic understanding of databases, data structures, software development, analytics, or data engineering concepts.
- Coursework or project experience involving SQL, Python, databases, data pipelines, data modeling, analytics, or software development.
- Exposure to cloud technologies such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, or similar platforms.
- Exposure to Snowflake, dbt, Git, Azure DevOps, APIs, Power BI, or related technologies.
- Familiarity with version control, software development practices, or CI/CD concepts.
- Experience through academic projects, student organizations, personal projects, or previous internships involving data or software development.
- Interest in modern data engineering, cloud platforms, artificial intelligence, analytics, automation, or enterprise technology.
- Interest in agriculture, energy, supply chain, logistics, trading, finance, gtm, or operations data domains.
