Job Overview
This internship provides the opportunity to work within an agile Research & Development environment focused on evaluating new applications and assessing the
current performance of microbial identification technologies. The intern will collaborate closely with R&D team members and gain hands‑on experience with
evolving initiatives in experimental design and execution, raw data analysis, model development, database structure development and interpreting results to
understand their potential impact on product performance and operational efficiency.
The pay range for this position is $24.00 - 30.00 per hour. Please note that salaries vary within the range based on factors including, but not limited to,
experience, skills, education, certifications, and location
Position is open to anyone, regardless of age, who meets the educational and experience requirements as posted.
Must be eligible to work in the US without requiring a sponsor visa.
Job Description
Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Assist in the planning, execution, and analysis of R&D and/or Operations experiments, including evaluating data to identify and monitor trends.
- Contribute to a wide range of early‑stage and late‑stage R&D initiatives, gaining meaningful exposure to multiple scientific disciplines, experimental
platforms, and research objectives as priorities evolve in a fast‑moving environment.
- Maintain accurate, complete, and well organized documentation to ensure data integrity and compliance with internal quality standards.
- Compile, organize, and present experimental data and results in a clear and concise manner.
- Assist in the designing of Databases structure
- Use Statistical Tools to analyze data cohorts
- Develop and deploy data pipelines
- Develop and run code
Job Qualifications
- Education: currently enrolled in an accredited college/university degree program pursuing a master’s degree or PhD in Data Sciences, Mathematics,
Bioinformatics or related field.
- Completion of bachelor’s degree with a GPA of 3.0 or above.
- Exposure to scientific or research environments is preferred (undergraduate research experience is a plus).
- Ability to work on multiple projects, strong attention to detail, and effective communication and listening skills in a teamwork environment.

About Charles River
Charles River is an early-stage contract research organization (CRO). We have built upon our foundation of laboratory animal medicine and science to develop a
diverse portfolio of discovery and safety assessment services, both Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) and non-GLP, to support clients from target identification
through preclinical development. Charles River also provides a suite of products and services to support our clients’ clinical laboratory testing needs and
manufacturing activities. Utilizing this broad portfolio of products and services enables our clients to create a more flexible drug development model, which
reduces their costs, enhances their productivity and effectiveness to increase speed to market.
With over 20,000 employees within 110 facilities in 20 countries around the globe, we are strategically positioned to coordinate worldwide resources and apply
multidisciplinary perspectives in resolving our client’s unique challenges. Our client base includes global pharmaceutical companies, biotechnology companies,
government agencies and hospitals and academic institutions around the world. And in 2019, revenue increased to $2.62 billion.
At Charles River, we are passionate about our role in improving the quality of people’s lives. Our mission, our excellent science and our strong sense of
purpose guide us in all that we do, and we approach each day with the knowledge that our work helps to improve the health and well-being of many across the
globe. We have proudly supported the development of 80% of the drugs approved by the FDA in the last five years.
