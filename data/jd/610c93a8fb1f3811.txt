Responsibilities
We are seeking a 2027 summer intern to support our Learning Management and Support Services (LMSS) contract with our Intelligence Community client. At Noblis,
our LMSS contract has numerous teams that support the educational and professional development for our client’s workforce. In this project, the intern will work
with our Special Projects and Innovations team to identify, develop, and implement data science, machine learning, and artificial (super) intelligence
strategies for a variety of different teams, applications, and types of data. You will be involved in innovative and meaningful work that supports the adult
learning life cycle. You will work alongside and gain exposure to industry leading expertise that is transforming the tradecraft and overall mission
effectiveness.
A successful candidate will lead their own project(s) that integrate process improvements across a large-scale learning enterprise. They will have strong
communication, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills. They will be a self-starter who is comfortable working both independently and with teams. They
will also be adaptable to shifting priorities and creative solutions to challenges. This internship requires you to work three days a week in Reston, VA and one
day a week in Springfield, VA.
This Internship Has The Ability To Be Customized To Your Skills. Some General Areas Of Technical Expertise That Lend Themself To This Position Could Include:
- Data Science/Engineering
- Computer Science
- Software Development/Engineering
- AI/ML Engineering
- Project/Business Analysis
- Other technical skills
Responsibilities:
- Learn the interdependence of cross-functional teams and implement data strategy using data science skills.
- Use data software tools to design, implement, and evaluate organizational improvements.
- Report insights and help clients to make decisions and find improvement opportunities.
- Integrating legacy tools with advanced AI capabilities.
- Translating client needs into technical requirements while navigating technical and network restrictions.
- Support innovation-related projects by identifying user needs, collecting data, performing analysis & interpretation and visualization of time series process
data.
