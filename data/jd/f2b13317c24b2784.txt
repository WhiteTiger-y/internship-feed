Who We Are:
Ai2’s Climate Modeling team (Ai2CM) has developed ACE, the world’s leading AI-based global atmosphere emulator for climate applications. ACE can be trained to
replicate weather variability and climate change at 100 km horizontal resolution using historical observations or outputs from any physics-based climate model.
It runs hundreds of times faster on a single GPU than such a model running on a supercomputer. For climate-planning applications, we have implemented diffusion-
based super-resolution for downscaling ACE simulations to kilometer-scale local detail. We also recently began a collaboration with Google Research to advance
NeuralGCM, a hybrid AI-physics model, for climate projection applications.
