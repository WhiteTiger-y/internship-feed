Introduction
ASML US, including its affiliates and subsidiaries, bring together the most creative minds in science and technology to develop lithography machines that are
key to producing faster, cheaper, more energy-efficient microchips. We design, develop, integrate, market and service these advanced machines, which enable our
customers - the world’s leading chipmakers - to reduce the size and increase the functionality of their microchips, which in turn leads to smaller, more
powerful consumer electronics. Our headquarters are in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and we have 18 office locations around the United States including main offices
in Chandler, Arizona, San Jose and San Diego, California, Wilton, Connecticut, and Hillsboro, Oregon.
Job Mission
The Controls team develops high-speed, real-time control solutions that operate and regulate the EUV light source in a highly automated environment.
The Data & AI Controls Intern will work with controls, software, firmware, and data/AI engineers to investigate how data-driven modeling, machine learning,
optimization, and AI-enabled engineering workflows can complement physics-based control approaches.
The internship will focus on solving real engineering problems using machine and simulation data, while considering the practical requirements needed to move
algorithms from exploration toward implementation.
This position may require access to controlled technology, as defined in the Export Administration Regulations (15 C.F.R.
- 730, et seq.). Qualified candidates must be legally authorized to access such controlled technology prior to beginning work. Business demands may require the
Company to proceed with candidates who are immediately eligible to access controlled technology.
Job Description
- Work in a multidisciplinary engineering team alongside experienced controls, software, firmware, and data/AI engineers.
- Analyze experimental, simulation, and high-rate time-series data from complex mechatronic and control systems.
- Develop and evaluate data-driven or AI/ML models for applications such as {{b}}state estimation, virtual sensing, system modeling, anomaly detection,
optimization, or control-system performance improvement{{/b}}.
- Develop reproducible data-processing and analysis workflows using Python and engineering data-science tools.
- Combine {{b}}physics-based/domain models with data-driven approaches{{/b}} where appropriate, rather than treating machine learning as a standalone solution.
- Evaluate models not only for predictive accuracy, but also for robustness and engineering constraints such as latency, sampling rate, bandwidth, compute
requirements, and deployment feasibility.
- Support prototyping and validation using simulation, test benches and, where appropriate, experimental machine data.
- Apply optimization techniques to engineering problems and compare data-driven approaches against established baselines.
- Document assumptions, methods, results and limitations, and communicate findings to technical stakeholders.
- Present internship results and recommendations to the Controls team at the end of the assignment.
