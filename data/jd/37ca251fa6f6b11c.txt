About Strava
Strava is the app for active people. With over 200 million athletes in more than 185 countries, it’s more than tracking workouts—it’s where people make progress
together, from new habits to new personal bests. No matter your sport or how you track it, Strava’s got you covered. Find your crew, crush your goals, and make
every effort count. Start your journey with Strava today.
Our mission is simple: to motivate people to live their best active lives. We believe in the power of movement to connect and drive people forward.
As an Engineering Intern at Strava, you will be embedded in a team working on real projects that ship to our athletes and within the company, treated like a
full-time member of the engineering team. You'll collaborate with cross functional teams that help us support and deliver to our athletes faster, while keeping
them inspired and motivated.
We’ll give you experienced mentors and all the tools you need to make a meaningful impact on our product. To see examples of what past interns have contributed,
read our Engineering Blog!
Available Internship:
- Strava is looking for a Machine Learning Engineering Intern to join the Data Products team for Summer 2027.
- The overall AI and Machine Learning team at Strava is responsible for sophisticated machine learning models and systems that power key Strava experiences,
providing value to our athletes through personalization, recommendation, search, and trust and safety. This team also maintains the ML platform and
infrastructure that lets us iterate on models quickly and deploy them reliably at scale.
- This internship will specifically support the Data Products team, which sits at the core of Strava’s AI strategy, turning Strava’s unique community and
activity data into reliable, reusable, enriched datasets that power experiences across the app. The team operates at the intersection of data engineering, ML
platform engineering, and server engineering, building the pipelines and platform layer that let our proprietary embeddings, algorithms, and models reach
athletes at scale.
We follow a flexible hybrid model that requires you in our SF office four days per week.
What You’ll Do:
- Contribute to a focused project: Work with your mentor to pick and deliver a scoped piece of the Data Products team’s work. Depending on the team’s needs and
your interests, this could be building a model that supports one of the team’s data products, designing an evaluation to measure how well our embeddings and
models perform, or investigating a promising new dataset.
- Experiment and analyze: Use Python, PyTorch, and SQL to explore Strava’s fitness and Geo data, run experiments, compare results against baselines, and
document what you learn so the team can build on it.
- Learn from the team: Work alongside machine learning engineers, data engineers, data scientists, and product managers, and pick up the practices that keep ML
and data work reliable, like code review, reproducible experiments, and evaluation.
- Share what you learned: Present your results to the team at the end of the summer.
We’re excited about you because:
- You have worked on machine learning problems through coursework, research, internships, or personal projects, and can break a problem down into incremental
steps.
- You write clear Python and have used tools like scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy, and PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- You are comfortable with SQL and exploratory data analysis.
- You understand core ML concepts such as supervised learning, train/validation splits, evaluation metrics, and overfitting.
- You communicate clearly, ask good questions, and enjoy collaborating with people of all experience levels.
- You are curious about deep learning, and about how fitness and Geo data can improve the athlete experience.
- You are currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, with an expected graduation date of May 2027, December 2027, or May
2028, OR have recently completed or are currently enrolled in a coding bootcamp, technical training program, or are transitioning into software engineering
through equivalent practical experience.
Bonus points:
- Experience with transformers, embeddings, or representation learning
- Experience with large-scale data tools like Spark, or cloud tools like AWS and SageMaker
- Research, open-source, or hackathon projects involving ML
The Summer Internship Program:
- The program will be in our San Francisco, CA office with a hybrid office attendance requirement of 4 days per week
- Housing Stipend for Summer is provided to live in SF for 3 months
- Length of program: 10-12 weeks (Summer 2027 roughly June through August).
- Working Hours: 40 hours per week
- This is a paid internship.
