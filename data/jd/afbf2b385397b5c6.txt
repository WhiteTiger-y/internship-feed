Spring 2027 Information Technology and Data Analytics Student Opportunities
Our client, a leader in the automotive manufacturing industry, is seeking motivated university students for their Spring 2027 Co-op and Internship program based
in Milford Center, Ohio. This session typically runs from January through April or May.
We are looking for candidates who are currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a closely related technical field. These roles
offer the chance to gain professional experience in a high-tech manufacturing environment.
Potential Work Areas and Responsibilities
Project and IT Service Management
Support the planning and execution of technical projects by tracking milestones, schedules, and budgets.
Liaise with business partners to define system and data requirements.
Assist in creating standardized documentation and operational procedures in compliance with ISO standards.
Manufacturing Analytics
Partner with production control teams to develop reports that drive plant-level decision-making.
Build and maintain reporting tools for production associates.
Use data analysis to support the rollout of new manufacturing initiatives and training.
Data Science and Systems Modeling
Organize and analyze complex datasets to identify trends and process improvement opportunities.
Develop machine learning algorithms, simulations, and computational models to predict system performance.
Provide data-driven insights to teams focused on new model development and design testing.
Evaluate existing business workflows to identify opportunities for automation or custom coding solutions.
Communication and Business Intelligence
Translate complex analytical findings into clear dashboards and actionable insights.
Present recommendations and technical results to company leadership and internal stakeholders.
Candidate Requirements
Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without current or future visa sponsorship.
Currently enrolled in a Bachelor or Master degree program for Information Technology, Computer Science, or similar.
Sophomore standing or higher by credit hour.
Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 or higher with a transcript available for review.
Able to work full-time (40 hours per week) for at least 10 weeks.
Valid US driver license and reliable personal transportation to the work site.
Successful completion of general business coursework.
Application Details
These positions are available for both single and multi-semester engagements. Our client provides a structured environment where students can apply classroom
knowledge to real-world automotive and manufacturing challenges. Please ensure you are applying for the specific term and major that matches your current
academic path.
