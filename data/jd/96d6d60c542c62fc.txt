be responsible for supporting product data management initiatives that improve the quality, consistency, and usability of product information across the
organization. The Product Data Analyst, Marketing Intern will gain hands-on experience with product data, data governance standards, and business systems while
supporting accurate product information for Marketing, Sales, Operations, customer experience, and channel partner success. This position reports to the
Manager, Product Commercialization. This role is onsite and is based in N Andover, MA. Primary Job Duties and Responsibilities Maintain product data integrity
by adhering to established data governance standards and business rules. Review, cleanse, and update product information to correct inconsistencies, standardize
terminology, and improve data quality. Research and enter dimensional, specification, and product attribute information from technical documentation and product
literature. Assist with identifying and resolving product data gaps across systems and databases. Support communication of product data updates and changes to
internal stakeholders and external channel partners. Learn and utilize various software systems and tools used to manage product information across the
business. Analyze data to identify trends, inconsistencies, and opportunities for process improvement. Collaborate with Marketing, Product Management, Sales,
Operations, and other business functions to support data-related initiatives. Assist with documentation of data standards, processes, and best practices.
Participate in internship development activities, networking opportunities, and cross-functional learning experiences. Assume responsibility for other projects
and duties as assigned by the Manager, Product Commercialization or Company management. Demonstrate integrity, accountability, continuous improvement, and
transparency by maintaining accurate product information, following data governance standards, identifying process improvements, and clearly communicating data
