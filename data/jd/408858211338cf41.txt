What It’s Like
Our T-Life Quality Engineering team is small enough that you’ll be seen and big enough that your work will matter. We deliver seamless, reliable, high-quality
digital experiences for millions of T-Mobile customers, driving end-to-end quality across complex journeys that span T-Life, Retail, Care, and integrated
backend systems. Our scope covers quality strategy, test automation, regression testing, cross-channel integration validation, and release readiness. We’re also
building AI-powered quality engineering platforms that enable intelligent testing, predictive insights, faster defect detection, and proactive quality
assurance. The pace is fast and the problems are real. You’ll be treated like a teammate from day one, with room to share ideas and see them put to use.
What You’ll Do
As an intern on the Quality Engineering team, you’ll work on real-world projects focused on AI-powered quality engineering platforms, intelligent test
automation, and next-generation digital testing solutions. Working alongside experienced engineers and cross-functional partners, you’ll design, develop, and
enhance AI-driven tools that improve test efficiency, automate complex customer journeys, predict quality risks, and accelerate software delivery. You’ll
explore Generative AI, Agentic AI, machine learning, and intelligent automation to build scalable solutions that change how T-Mobile delivers quality across
T-Life, Retail, and Care. Your work will improve developer productivity, release confidence, and the customer experience, and it will help shape the future of
AI-enabled quality engineering at T-Mobile.
Key Responsibilities
- AI-Powered Platform Development: Design, develop, and enhance quality engineering platforms that use Generative AI, Agentic AI, and machine learning to
improve testing efficiency, engineering productivity, and software quality.
- Intelligent Test Automation: Build AI-driven automation for test case generation, execution, self-healing scripts, and end-to-end validation of customer
journeys across T-Life, Retail, and Care applications.
- AI Innovation & Prototyping: Research emerging AI technologies, develop proofs of concept (POCs), and experiment with intelligent agents, LLMs, and automation
frameworks to solve real quality engineering challenges.
- Data Analytics & Predictive Quality Insights: Develop AI-enabled dashboards, quality metrics, and predictive models that identify testing gaps, detect defects
early, and give engineering teams actionable insights.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration & Continuous Improvement: Partner with Quality Engineering, Product, and Development teams to integrate AI solutions into
existing workflows, improve release confidence, and deliver scalable, customer-centric quality solutions.
What It Takes
- Programming & Software Development: Strong foundational knowledge of Python, Java, JavaScript, or TypeScript, with experience developing applications, APIs,
automation scripts, or technical projects.
- AI & Emerging Technologies: Familiarity with Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI, prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(RAG), and AI frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, or similar technologies.
- Mindset: Curiosity and a willingness to challenge assumptions, including your own, plus the urgency to move quickly and manage multiple priorities.
- At least 18 years of age
- Legally authorized to work in the United States now and in the future
- Must have one or more course(s) remaining in a Bachelors or Graduate degree program post internship
- Employees of T-Mobile or Metro by T-Mobile are ineligible for external Summer Internships, please see Magenta Accelerator if interested
Relocation assistance will be provided to program participants who reside more than 50 miles from the internship location.
Intern wages range from $27/hour to $50/hour. The successful candidate’s actual pay will be based on education/STEM vs non-STEM designation.
