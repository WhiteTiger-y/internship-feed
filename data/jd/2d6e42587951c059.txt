A Taste of What You'll Do
Join GALLO's award-winning Information Technology team as an IT Data Intern and help turn data into insights that support the world’s largest family-owned
winery. In this summer internship, you’ll gain hands-on experience across business systems analysis, data analysis, and developer/programmer opportunities while
working with leading technology and software.
You’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams across areas such as Marketing, Sales, Finance, Manufacturing, Winemaking, and Research & Development to help
answer business questions, improve reporting, and support data-driven decision-making. Through project rotations within Application Development, Business
Analysis, and/or Data Services, you’ll build practical skills while contributing meaningful work from day one.
This internship is designed to help you grow professionally, apply your education in a dynamic industry, and explore future career opportunities with GALLO.
You’ll be part of a program that values your skills, interests, and educational experiences while helping build a strong pipeline for future full-time roles.
What You'll Need
- Currently enrolled at a college or university and working towards a Bachelor’s degree OR Master's degree in one of the following areas: Computer Science,
Management Information Systems, Business Analytics, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science, Data Analytics, Informatics, Marketing Research, Cognitive
Science, or Applied/Quantitative Psychology to be obtained between December 2027 and June 2029.
- Experience defining problems, collecting data, establishing facts, and drawing valid conclusions.
- Capable of working with data in Python, R and SQL.
- Familiar with advanced statistical concepts such as random forests, ensemble methods, LNearest Neighbors, K-Means, etc.
- Strong written, verbal, and analytical skills.
- Required to be 18 years or older. This may be in addition to other age requirements, if applicable, as listed in this job description.
