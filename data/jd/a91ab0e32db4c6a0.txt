This Role: Data Science Internship
Do you love solving problems with data? Are you excited by the opportunity to use machine learning, statistics, and experimentation to shape smarter products
and better business decisions? If so, the Data Science Internship at SharkNinja is for you.
As part of our data science team, you’ll gain hands-on experience applying statistical modeling, machine learning, and data storytelling to real-world problems.
You’ll work closely with data engineers, analysts, and product stakeholders to turn data into actionable insights that drive product innovation and customer
impact.
Key Responsibilities
During your time at SharkNinja, you’ll contribute to:
- Model Development & Prototyping
Build and test machine learning models on real product and business datasets.
- Data Wrangling
Clean, explore, and transform data using Python, SQL, and common data science libraries.
- Visualization & Storytelling
Create dashboards and visualizations that make insights clear to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Collaboration
Partner with engineers, analysts, and business teams to define data science opportunities and translate results into impact.
- Learning Modern Tools
Gain exposure to AI ML tools and platforms
Attributes & Skills
- Education: Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s program in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Applied Math, or a related field.
- Must be able to work a full-time, 40-hour-per-week schedule with a minimum of # DAYS per week onsite in TOWN, STATE.
- Proficiency in Python and working knowledge of SQL.
- Exposure to data science libraries
- Strong foundation in statistics, probability, and data analysis.
- Intellectual curiosity with a problem-solving mindset and attention to detail.
- Ability to explain analytical results clearly and collaborate with diverse stakeholders.
- Experience with data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, or matplotlib/seaborn/Plotly) is a plus.
- Familiarity with cloud data platforms or big data tools is desirable.
