Job Description
Visa's Internship Program offers a 12-week immersive experience where you'll work on impactful projects that advance Visa's mission. As a Visa intern, you'll
build meaningful connections, strengthen your communication and business skills, and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic, global environment.
You'll have access to interactive workshops and learning sessions designed to build expertise, expand industry knowledge, and elevate your professional skills.
You'll not only learn, you'll contribute, collaborate, and innovate. Beyond professional development, enjoy social events that foster community and connection.
The program culminates in a final presentation, where you'll share your project achievements and recommendations with Visa leaders-a chance to demonstrate
impact and growth.
Our Team
Visa Research expands Visa's technology research capabilities globally, conducting both applied and fundamental research. We partner closely with technology,
product teams, academics, and governments to explore and develop technologies critical to the future of payments.
Artificial Intelligence
The GenAI Research Team Drives Purpose-driven Research To Advance The Next Generation Of AI For Secure, Intelligent, And Scalable Commerce. Our Work Spans
- Foundational AI: large language models, representation learning, embeddings, adaptive model training, and methods to advance fraud detection, risk modeling,
personalization, and commerce intelligence.
We unite long-term scientific inquiry with practical experimentation, partnering with product and engineering teams to translate research into real-world
impact.
