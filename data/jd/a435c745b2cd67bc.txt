Department Overview
The Data Science team uses advanced analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data engineering to solve complex business and manufacturing
challenges across Micron. By combining statistical analysis, predictive modeling, AI-enabled solutions, and large-scale data platforms, the team transforms data
into actionable insights that improve business performance, operational efficiency, and decision-making.
Position Overview
As a Data Science Intern, you will apply statistics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data engineering techniques to solve real-world business and
engineering challenges. You will work alongside Data Scientists and engineering teams to develop predictive models, build analytical workflows, evaluate AI-
enabled solutions, and generate insights from large and complex datasets while gaining hands-on experience with modern AI and data science technologies.
Responsibilities
- Develop and apply statistical, machine learning, and AI-based models to identify patterns, generate predictions, and support business decision-making.
- Extract, clean, integrate, and analyze structured and unstructured data from multiple sources using programming, analytics, and data engineering techniques.
- Design and implement analytical workflows, software tools, automation solutions, and reusable data science processes.
- Explore and apply Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), agentic AI, and AI-assisted analytics to improve data analysis, automation, and problem-solving
capabilities.
- Collaborate with Data Scientists and engineering teams to communicate findings, develop recommendations, and deliver actionable insights through reports,
visualizations, and user documentation.
Minimum Qualifications
- 0-2 years of relevant experience through internships, research projects, academic coursework, competitions, or data science projects involving analytics,
machine learning, artificial intelligence, or software development.
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Physics, or a related
quantitative field, with plans to pursue a Master's or PhD program.
- Experience with Python, including tools such as Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Jupyter Notebooks, and data analysis workflows.
- Knowledge of statistics, machine learning, analytics, SQL, data cleansing, and analytical modeling techniques.
- Experience using AI-enabled tools, Generative AI, and Large Language Models (LLMs) to support data analysis, software development, automation, or problem-
solving, with the ability to critically evaluate AI-generated outputs.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with machine learning, predictive analytics, statistical modeling, or advanced analytical methods through coursework, research, or projects.
- Familiarity with Generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Model Context Protocol (MCP), or agentic AI
workflows.
- Experience with cloud platforms and data technologies such as Snowflake, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), AWS, Azure, or big-data environments.
- Experience with Python-based AI/ML libraries, Git-based development workflows, automation, or software engineering practices.
- Experience building reusable AI workflows, intelligent agents, analytics solutions, dashboards, or data-driven applications and an interest in applying AI to
business and engineering challenges.
