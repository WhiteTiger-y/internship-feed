Job Description:
At Takeda, the Clinical Data Science (CDS) organization partners across Research and Development (R&D) to apply statistical thinking, quantitative methods, and
data science to support evidence generation and informed decision-making. The team works collaboratively with clinicians, scientists, programmers, and other
cross-functional partners to address important drug development questions and advance innovative analytical approaches.
We are seeking a highly motivated and talented PhD candidate to join our Clinical Data Science team for the 2027 Summer Internship Program.
This internship offers an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the pharmaceutical industry while contributing to innovative statistical and AI/ML research
relevant to clinical drug development.
The summer intern will work closely with experienced statisticians on an innovative research project focused on advancing quantitative methodologies for
clinical drug development, with an emphasis on oncology applications. The project will involve methodological development and implementation, statistical
analyses and simulation studies, and evaluation of the performance and practical applications of proposed approaches, to address complex scientific challenges
and support data-driven decision-making under uncertainties. The intern will also have opportunities to present research findings to scientific and cross-
functional audiences and potentially contribute to a manuscript for publication in a peer-reviewed statistical journal.
How You Will Contribute:
- Conduct focused literature reviews on advanced statistical methodologies and AI/ML applications in clinical drug development.
- Develop, implement, and evaluate innovative statistical and AI/ML approaches to address research questions relevant to clinical trials and drug development.
- Design and conduct simulation studies to assess methodological performance under various scenarios and assumptions.
- Develop reproducible analytical workflows and implement statistical models using R and/or Python.
- Collaborate with statisticians and interpret research findings to explore their potential applications.
- Present key findings and deliver a final presentation to the broader team at the conclusion of the internship
- Prepare a scientific manuscript summarizing the research methodology and findings for potential publication in a peer-reviewed statistical journal.
Internship Development Opportunities:
- Gain practical experience applying advanced statistical and AI/ML methodologies to real-world challenges in pharmaceutical research and development.
- Develop a deeper understanding of clinical trial design, oncology drug development, and the role of quantitative sciences in clinical decision-making.
- Receive mentorship and technical guidance from experienced statisticians and data scientists.
- Strengthen research, programming, problem-solving, and scientific communication skills.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams and gain exposure to the pharmaceutical drug development process.
- Explore opportunities to contribute to scientific publications or conference presentations.
Job Requirements:
Education:
- Currently enrolled in a PhD program in Statistics, Biostatistics, or a closely related quantitative discipline.
- Preferably completed at least two years of doctoral-level coursework or research.
Required Skills:
- Strong foundation in statistical theory, statistical modeling, and computational methods.
- Advanced knowledge and demonstrated hands-on experience in AI/ML methodologies, such as random forests, gradient boosting, Bayesian Additive Regression Trees
(BART), neural networks/deep learning, and other supervised learning approaches.
- Strong understanding of AI/ML model development, training, validation, performance evaluation, and interpretation.
- Solid knowledge of survival analysis, including Kaplan-Meier estimation, Cox proportional hazards models, and other time-to-event modeling approaches.
- Familiarity with oncology clinical trial endpoints, including overall survival (OS), progression-free survival (PFS), and objective response rate (ORR).
- Strong programming proficiency in R and/or Python, with experience implementing statistical and AI/ML models and conducting simulation studies.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Knowledge of Bayesian statistical methods, causal inference, or advanced predictive modeling.
- Experience with survival prediction, treatment effect estimation, or analysis of clinical trial data.
- Familiarity with oncology drug development, clinical trial design, or pharmaceutical research.
- Experience conducting independent methodological research or developing reproducible analytical workflows.
