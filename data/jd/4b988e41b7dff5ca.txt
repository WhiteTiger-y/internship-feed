Job Summary
Western Digital's Manufacturing Engineering team is looking for an enthusiastic intern to assist in developing an automated Off-hold Tracking solution. The
intern will support efforts to reduce manual data compilation in the Factory Quality Assurance Process (FQAP). This is a learning-focused role ideal for
students eager to apply their academic knowledge in a real-world manufacturing setting.
ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Assist in building a simple automated system to track Off-hold status and reduce manual compilation
- Learn and apply basic data extraction and cleaning techniques on FQAP-related data
- Help create simple reports or dashboards to display Off-hold tracking results
- Work with team members to understand existing manual workflows
- Support data validation by comparing automated outputs with current manual records
- Prepare basic documentation on the work completed
- Participate in team meetings and share progress updates
REQUIRED:
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Mathematics, Computer Science, or Information Technology (penultimate or final year)
- Basic knowledge of at least one programming language (e.g., Python or R)
- Willingness to learn new tools and technologies
- Able to commit to the internship duration (3–6 months)
- Good attitude, curiosity, and willingness to ask questions
PREFERRED:
- Exposure to data analysis concepts through coursework or personal projects
- Basic familiarity with Excel (formulas, pivot tables)
- Some awareness of databases or SQL (even from coursework)
- Interest in automation or process improvement
SKILLS:
- Basic programming (Python, R, or VBA)
- Fundamental data handling (sorting, filtering, basic analysis)
- Microsoft Excel (intermediate level)
- Willingness to learn visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, etc.)
- Good communication and teamwork
- Eager to learn and take initiative
