Job Description
The Future Talent Program within the Data, AI, and Genome Sciences (DAGS) Department is offering co-op opportunities that last a period of six months. There is
one position in the Patient Data Learning group. Candidates can apply for the opportunity to be involved in a computational project designed to enable students
to further develop their skills and to evaluate if we are the right company for their long-term goals. We are seeking highly motivated and talented students
with computational skills and experience.
Patient Data Learning in Precision Genetics (1 position, Cambridge MA):
- Design and build agent-assisted system or components to support feasibility assessment and early exploration of phenotypes and clinical profiles of patients
in EHR-linked biobanks.
- Re-use and customize AI language models to turn scientific questions expressed in natural language into machine-generated queries to be evaluated in agentic
loops.
- Create guardrails and evals to promote and measure the reliability of models and agents.
- Assist with the application of these tools and technologies to answer feasibility questions.
Required Education And Skills
- Currently enrolled in a minimum of a Master's degree program in Clinical Informatics, Epidemiology, Computational Medicine, Biomedical Informatics,
Biostatistics, Computer Science with strong interest in biomedical applications, or a related field of study. Students enrolled in a PhD program are highly
encouraged to apply.
- Computational/software engineering fundamentals sufficient to be able run and tune local LLMs, build local agentic systems, and comfortable with applying or
outlining software and agent development practices (version control, packaging, tests & evals).
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, ideally database-agnostic querying such as ibis
- Applied knowledge sufficient to query structured EMR/EHR data, for example to create patient features, build cohorts, or report summary statistics, or a
demonstrated interest in learning to do so and clinical applications.
- Available to work full-time for 6 months in 2027-2028 as part of a formal co-op program.
- Excellent academic achievement and analytical ability.
- Demonstrated superior communication and interpersonal skills and must be able to work well in a collaborative team environment.
Preferred Skills And Experience
- General interests/experience in ANY of the fields of clinical informatics, clinical machine learning, computational medicine, natural language processing and
LLM research, epidemiology
- Good data systems fundamentals, and have at least notions of data engineering and database-agnostic design.
- Agent reward systems
- Knowledge in statistical methods for epidemiology or econometrics.
- Familiarity for the OMOP CDM and medical coding system (ICD, SNOMED, CPT, LOINC, etc.)
- Experience with Databricks.
- Knowledge of R and tidyverse packages.
Please note that this position may be closed before the posted end date or may remain open longer, at the discretion of the company.
