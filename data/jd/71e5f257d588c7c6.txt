What We Do The SEI helps advance software engineering principles and practices and serves as a national resource in software engineering, computer security, and
process improvement. The SEI works closely with defense and government organizations, industry, and academia to continually improve software-intensive systems.
Our core purpose is to help organizations improve software engineering capabilities and develop or acquire the right software, defect free, within budget and on
time, every time. Position Summary As an AI Engineer Intern within the SEI AI Division’s Mission Innovation Lab, you will help take applied AI research ideas
from concept to prototype in support of defense and national security missions. You will work alongside senior researchers and machine learning engineers to
explore and apply large-scale foundation models (such as GPT and LLaMA), agentic AI workflows, and traditional machine learning techniques to real-world mission
use cases. During your internship, you will assist in designing experimental prototypes, building data and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, and
running evaluations to help translate emerging AI advancements into secure, reliable, and scalable mission capabilities. Requirements · Enrolled in a degree
granting program (Bachelor’s, Master’s, or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, Applied Mathematics, or a related
field). · Willingness to travel to various locations to support the SEI’s overall mission. This includes within the SEI and CMU community. · You will be subject
to a background check and must be eligible to work in the United States without Visa sponsorship. Duties · Assist in designing and prototyping autonomous agents
and multi-step pipelines using frameworks such as LangChain, ReAct, tool-calling, or the Model Context Protocol (MCP). · Build and maintain data pipelines, ETL
processes, and backend services using Python, C/C++, or Java. · Contribute to Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines that integrate external knowledge
bases with Large Language Models (LLMs) to improve factual accuracy. · Support rapid prototyping, benchmarking, and robustness or adversarial testing of AI
models in realistic environments. · Research and stay current with emerging LLM architectures, agentic paradigms, parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT/LoRA)
methods, and AI-safety techniques. Knowledge, Skills and Abilities · Proficiency in Python and familiarity with at least one compiled language (such as C/C++ or
Java), REST/GraphQL APIs, or containerization. · Foundational understanding of machine learning theory (supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning)
and standard evaluation metrics. · Academic or practical experience working with deep learning frameworks (such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX) and LLM
libraries (such as Hugging Face Transformers or LangChain). · Analytical thinking with the ability to decompose complex AI problems into tractable components
and iterate rapidly. · Strong written and verbal communication skills for documenting designs and presenting results to technical and non-technical
stakeholders. · Proven teamwork, high curiosity, and autonomy to collaborate in interdisciplinary groups, contribute to shared codebases, and explore emerging
technologies. Desired Experience · Experience fine-tuning LLMs or building multi-tool agents, planner-executor loops, or tool-calling pipelines through
coursework, research, or personal projects. · Exposure to deploying ML/LLM APIs (e.g., FastAPI, gRPC) or working with data-pipeline tools (e.g., Airflow,
Prefect, Ray). · Familiarity with building evaluation frameworks, benchmarks, or data quality pipelines for machine learning or agentic systems. · Contributions
to open-source AI/ML libraries, agentic frameworks, or prior research experience relevant to defense or government AI applications. Job Function Breakdown 50%
AI Prototyping & Software Engineering: Assisting in the design, coding, and deployment of agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, and backend services in Python,
C/C++, or Java. 30% Applied Research, Benchmarking & Testing: Conducting literature reviews on emerging LLM/agentic paradigms, running benchmarks, and assisting
with robustness and adversarial evaluations. 10% Documentation & Knowledge Sharing: Documenting experimental designs, codebases, and evaluation results, and
presenting findings to team members and stakeholders. 10% Team Collaboration & Mentorship: Collaborating with senior researchers and engineers to refine problem
