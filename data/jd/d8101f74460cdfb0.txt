What you’ll do
Looking for *that* internship? The game-changing one that’ll help you learn, grow, and chart your path forward? You’ll find it at SAS. Our interns aren’t coffee
runners – they do real, meaningful work. Our award-winning internship program is focused on development, culture, and community. We’ll help you grow
professionally, find (or further) your passion, and make memorable connections that last beyond the summer.
Applied AI Modelling (AAIM) team, within SAS R&D leads the design, development, and implementation of prebuilt models for SAS customers across many industries
such as CPG, manufacturing, retail, insurance, among others.
As an intern, you might:
- Build Generative AI models to solve the challenges of SAS customers using large language models (LLMs) and/or visual language models (VLMs).
- Use libraries like LangChain, LangSmith etc. to build applications leveraging LLMs and VLMs.
- Collaborate with data scientists and assist in prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workflow to solve the business problems of SAS
customers.
- Researching and developing high-quality demos to illustrate the application of Generative AI in solving practical problems.
- Optimize the deployment pipeline for Generative AI models.
- Part of a team of experts with different specialized skill sets including machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision etc.
