About The Program
The Moody’s Ratings and Research Support (RRS) internship program is a 10-week experience designed to help students develop skills that are valuable for a
future career in financial services. This is an internally facing role reporting to a Team Manager. Summer Interns will be assigned to one of the following
rating groups:
- Corporate Finance Group (CFG)
- Financial Institutions Group (FIG)
- Public Finance Group (PFG)
- Project and Infrastructure Finance (PIF)
- Structured Finance Group (SFG)
This full-time internship offers a comprehensive view of Moody’s business and culture, along with opportunities to build professional and technical skills.
Program elements include networking, mentorship, leadership speaker series, and volunteer events. Interns complete meaningful project work determined by their
team, contributing to real business priorities while building confidence and practical experience. Throughout the internship, participants receive mentorship
from Moody’s professionals, gain exposure to diverse career paths, and develop a broader understanding of the company. As a Moody’s intern, you will gain real-
world experience, turn inputs into action, and contribute as an integral part of the team.
Successful Summer Interns may be considered for an offer to join Moody’s as a Ratings Associate after graduation.
