Job Summary
Work with subject matter experts to understand warranty data. Leverage data analytics skillset to build custom dashboards that show various warranty metrics /
key performance indicators. Respond to ad-hoc requests for various metrics. Analyze warranty data warehouses and identify opportunities to automate workflows
and improve team processing efficiency. This position is located in Renton, WA.
Internships are scheduled to begin either May or June 2027
Here's what you will do in this role:
- Automate Business Reporting
- Build Custom Warranty KPI Dashboards
- Mine warranty data and generate business use cases to improve efficiency / automate workflows
Required Skills:
- Experience leveraging object-oriented programming and analytics platforms to automate data collection/cleansing and dashboard generation
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite
- Programming Languages: SQL (Jupyter Notebook, MS SQL Server, etc.) / Python
Preferred Skills:
- Analytics Platforms: Tableau / Snowflake
- Snowflake experience not required if SQL skillset is strong
- Knowledge of lean principles
Education
The candidate we seek will be graduating in 2027 or is currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Business Administration,
Economics, Statistics, Finance, Marketing or related program.
