Job Description
Change the world. Love your job.
At TI, Systems engineers focus deeply on understanding the technical needs, and future trends of an industry or end equipment, then create new products and
innovative forward-looking product roadmaps to solve them. Systems Engineers are an integral part each phase of new product development at TI. In the early
stages of product development, systems engineers interface with key stakeholders (customer decision-makers, application engineers, marketing, management, sales,
IC design engineers, technology development) to negotiate specifications, perform trade-offs, understand the competitive landscape, and ultimately develop
detailed technical definitions for new products. They then collaborate with the full IC development team (design, applications, test, product engineers) to
deliver products to the market which are compelling, competitive, cost-conscious, manufacturable, and importantly, successful in growing TI’s business.
We are seeking a highly motivated PhD student to join our Embedded AI team in Kilby Labs this summer to work on synthetic data generation and augmentation for
edge AI on resource-constrained microcontrollers. Limited availability of high-quality training data is one of the biggest barriers to adopting edge AI, and you
will help us find and evaluate better ways to address it. As a key member of our team, you will survey the state of the art, develop and test new techniques,
and rigorously evaluate how well they work on real-world data and on embedded hardware.
In this systems engineering intern role, you’ll have the chance to:
- Research and develop synthetic data generation and augmentation techniques that address data scarcity for edge AI on resource-constrained microcontrollers
- Survey the state of the art and identify the most promising approaches for your problem
- Work with time-series sensor data from a variety of applications, supporting both classification and regression tasks
- Design and run rigorous experiments to evaluate whether synthetic data improves model performance on real-world data
- Evaluate techniques under embedded hardware constraints, including low-precision quantization
- Analyze the quality, coverage and usefulness of generated data
- Collaborate with internal research, software and application teams
- Document findings and present results, trade-offs and recommendations to technical and business stakeholders
Qualifications
Minimum Requirements:
- Currently pursuing a PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, with research focused on machine learning,
signal processing, or embedded AI
- Cumulative 3.0/4.0 GPA or higher
Preferred Qualifications
- Solid background in machine learning for time-series data and signal processing
- Research experience in synthetic data generation, data augmentation, generative models (e.g., GANs, diffusion models, TimeGAN), or learning from limited and
imbalanced data, ideally demonstrated by publications
- Experience with sensor signal analysis (e.g., vibration, motor/line current, PIR, spectral and time-frequency analysis) and an interest in fault detection and
predictive maintenance
- Strong programming skills in Python, with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Experience with machine learning model optimization, quantization (including sub-8-bit and quantization-aware training), and deployment on resource-
constrained devices
- Rigorous experimental design and statistical evaluation skills, including handling class imbalance and reporting variance across runs
- Familiarity with embedded ML libraries (e.g., TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers, CMSIS-NN) and microcontroller platforms
- Ability to establish strong relationships with key stakeholders critical to success, both internally and externally
- Strong verbal and written communication skills to audiences of varied background
- Ability to simplify complex problems and navigate uncertainty
- Ability to quickly ramp on new systems and processes
- Demonstrated strong interpersonal, analytical and problem-solving skills
- Ability to work in teams and collaborate effectively with people in different functions
- Ability to take the initiative and drive for results
- Strong time management skills that enable on-time project delivery
