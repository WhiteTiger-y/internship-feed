Our Internship Program
Our Internship Program Our summer internship program lasts 10-12 weeks beginning in May/June and ending in August/September. We offer a fast-paced, flexible,
fun environment where you will have the opportunity to work on meaningful projects and face new challenges every day.
Our Company
At Teradata, we believe that people thrive when empowered with better information. Teradata Autonomous Knowledge Platform activates enterprise intelligence by
unifying data, knowledge and business context to achieve tangible outcomes. With Teradata, organizations can provide agents with full context for impact when it
matters. Our solution lets businesses connect and scale on premises, in the cloud, or through a hybrid approach. Teradata delivers real business value with AI.
What You'll Do
In this role, you will build and evaluate self-improving AI agents that make Teradata's enterprise agent system more capable, adaptive, and reliable over time.
- Prototype continual learning methods that let agents and models acquire new knowledge and skills from ongoing data and interaction without catastrophic
forgetting.
- Build self-learning and recursive self-improvement (RSI) loops in which an agent critiques, revises, and verifies its own prompts, tools, code, or policies.
- Design and run reinforcement learning experiments, such as RL with verifiable or learned rewards and policy optimization for LLM agents.
- Develop evaluation harnesses that measure real improvement, robustness, and safety, so each result is reproducible and comparable against baselines.
- Direct AI coding and research agents to accelerate your own experimentation, and document which workflows work and which do not.
- Integrate successful prototypes with Teradata's agent system and platform alongside ML engineers and architects, with attention to governance, compliance, and
scalability.
Who You'll Work With
You will join the AI engineering team that builds Teradata's agent system and AI capabilities and turns research into product features. You'll collaborate with
a world-class team of AI architects, ML engineers, and domain experts in Silicon Valley, working together to build the next generation of enterprise AI systems.
Required
What Makes You a Qualified Candidate
- Currently pursuing a Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Experience with LLM post-training (SFT, DPO, GRPO or similar), reward modeling, or LLM-as-judge evaluation.
- Working knowledge of modern agent system design, including agent harnesses, skills, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and tool integrations.
- Demonstrated ability to read academic papers and implement their ideas, with reproducible experiments and clear write-ups.
- Fluency with AI-assisted ("vibe") coding and the ability to direct, supervise, and verify the output of multiple coding agents.
Preferred
- Hands-on reinforcement learning experience (research, coursework with substantial projects, or publications), including familiarity with policy-gradient or
RLHF-style methods.
- Prior work or publications in continual or lifelong learning, meta-learning, self-play, or self-improving or agentic systems.
- Experience building agent frameworks (tool use, memory, planning, multi-agent orchestration) and agent evaluation benchmarks.
What You'll Bring
- Experimental rigor: you run ablations and baselines, apply statistical care, and report negative results honestly.
- Research-to-product mindset: you are curious about enterprise-scale data problems and want your research to become practical systems.
- Ownership: you turn an open-ended research question into hypotheses, experiments, and a clear result without waiting to be told the next step.
- Healthy skepticism: you verify AI-generated code and results before trusting them, and you know when an agent is wrong.
- Clear communication: you explain technical results to both researchers and engineers, and welcome feedback.
What You'll Gain
- Hands-on experience building and evaluating self-improving agents on a production enterprise agent system.
- Ownership of an end-to-end research project, from idea through prototype to a team readout.
- Practical skill in directing multiple AI coding agents on a real research workflow.
- Mentorship from AI architects and ML engineers.
- Exposure to how governance, compliance, and scalability shape enterprise AI systems.
