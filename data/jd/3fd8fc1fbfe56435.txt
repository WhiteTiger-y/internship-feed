Overview
Hussmann's Internship Program provides meaningful, real-life experiences while working as a solutions leader in the food retailing industry. Hussmann interns
receive competitive compensation, participate in community stewardship, network with company leadership, and learn about career opportunities available at
Hussmann.
As a Data Engineering intern in the 2027 Hussmann Summer Internship Program, you will gain experience with large datasets, developing data infrastructure, and
using tools and technologies that support data-driven decision-making.
Responsibilities
- Clean and validate data sets to ensure accuracy for downstream analytics teams.
- Collaborate with the team using Git to manage code and track project updates.
- Network with senior leaders, associates, and other program interns to learn about our organization.
- Tour Hussmann's 1.6 million square foot manufacturing facility, our Global Hub for hosting customers, and the R&D Lab where we test product quality.
- Participate in community stewardship outreach, career development events, and other team-building offsite events.
- Compete in the annual Ideation & Innovation Challenge.
Ideation & Innovation Challenge : Throughout the twelve-week program, all Hussmann interns will divide into cross-functional teams and compete with one another
for the Hussmann Ideation & Innovation Challenge. Working with Hussmann mentors and leaders, you and your team will identify a solution to a provided problem
statement. At the end of the summer, you’ll present your team’s business proposal to a panel of judges, executive leaders, and the organization – and possibly
create a roadmap for Hussmann’s next great innovation.
Qualifications
- Currently enrolled in school pursuing a minimum of a bachelor’s degree
- Minimum 3.0 GPA
- Basic understanding of cloud infrastructure, specifically core AWS services.
- Good foundational knowledge of SQL and basic programming skills in Python.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills.
- Ability to effectively interact with all levels of the organization
- Highly analytical, proactive problem solver, and eager to learn complex data architectures.
- Ability to relocate, if not local
The summer internship will run from the third week of May through the first week of August. These dates may be changed to meet the needs of the student.
About Hussmann
For over a century, Hussmann Corporation has been a global leader in commercial refrigeration equipment — delivering solutions that meet the demands of the
modern food retailing industry. Headquartered in St. Louis, MO, Hussmann offers a diverse range of products and connected solutions designed to enhance
operational efficiency, optimize visibility of merchandise, and keep food fresh. With a nationwide network of skilled technicians, Hussmann goes beyond
manufacturing — a trusted partner, relentlessly driving customer success.
For more information about Hussmann, please visit  www.hussmann.com .
Hussmann is a subsidiary of Panasonic USA.
Hussmann is proudly committed to creating an inclusive environment. All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to veteran
status, disability, age, race, color, religion, sex, national origin, genetics, medical condition, or any other characteristic protected by law.
