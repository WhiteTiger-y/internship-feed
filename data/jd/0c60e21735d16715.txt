The Internship Programme at Graphcore
Explore a career at Graphcore through a 12-week summer internship, gaining hands-on experience on real projects. You’ll bring fresh ideas, put your learning
into practice and help shape what we’re creating. Along the way, you’ll tackle challenging problems with a team who welcome your questions and support you as
you build your skills and confidence.
What you’ll work on
Use your programming skills and curiosity to help AI models run efficiently on next-generation Graphcore hardware.
In AI Compute Libraries & Performance, you’ll contribute to C++ libraries and compute kernels for machine learning workloads. Your work will include:
- Developing compute kernels: Implement and improve operations such as matrix multiplication (GEMM), convolutions, reductions, elementwise operations and fused
kernels that combine multiple operations.
- Understanding and improving performance: Use profiling and microbenchmarks to measure execution speed and investigate how threading, cache locality, memory
layout and kernel launch efficiency affect performance.
- Checking correctness and reliability: Build regression tests and numerical checks, investigate issues and improve the quality of the libraries our customers
use.
Through these projects, you’ll develop practical experience connecting numerical computing with how code runs on hardware, helping deliver software that is
fast, accurate and reliable.
Your team and support
You’ll join a team building high-performance compute libraries that help customers get more from Graphcore hardware. Our work brings together machine learning,
numerical computing and accelerator performance, with colleagues sharing their expertise to solve engineering problems.
You’ll contribute to clearly scoped projects and build your skills with guidance from engineers working in performance and low-level software. Together, we use
profiling, experiments and technical reviews to compare approaches, understand trade-offs and improve our solutions.
Your buddy will help you settle in, get familiar with our tools and ways of working, and find the right people to ask. Your manager will meet with you regularly
to agree goals, offer feedback and discuss your development, including time for learning.
We welcome different perspectives and make space for everyone to contribute. Questions, alternative approaches and requests for help are part of how we work. As
you take on more responsibility, your colleagues will help you work through challenges, and your ideas will be listened to and discussed respectfully.
How you’ll learn and grow
Over the 12 weeks, you’ll build performance engineering skills through hands-on work on compute kernels, benchmarks and tests.
Profiling and experiments will help you understand where time is spent and how changes to code and data layout affect performance. Numerical validation and
regression testing will show you how to check that an optimisation preserves correct results and continues to work as software changes.
Working with specialists and discussing technical trade-offs will help you develop your engineering judgement, explain your findings and connect mathematical
operations to efficient implementations on hardware.
What we're looking for
- Currently studying towards a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Computer Science, Maths, Machine Learning, Data Science or a related field
- Experience with C or C++11, plus familiarity with Python or scripting tools for automation and testing
- An understanding of linear algebra, numerical methods or scientific computing
- Problem-solving skills and collaborative approach to unfamiliar technical challenges
Previous industry experience is not required. Tell us what you built or investigated through coursework, personal or team projects or other relevant experience,
how you approached it and what you learned.
Coursework or experience with machine learning frameworks, parallel programming, code optimisation, maths libraries or performance analysis would be useful.
These are additional strengths, rather than essential requirements.
