Overview
Systems Planning and Analysis, Inc. (SPA) delivers high-impact, technical solutions to complex national security issues. With over 50 years of business
expertise and consistent growth, we are known for continuous innovation for our government customers, in both the US and abroad. Our exceptionally talented team
is highly collaborative in spirit and practice, producing Results that Matter . Come work with the best! We offer opportunity, unique challenges, and clear-
sighted commitment to the mission. SPA: Objective. Responsive. Trusted.
The Strategic Division (SD) supports high-reliability organizations including Navy Strategic Systems Programs (SSP), Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, and the
Department of Energy (DOE), among others. Our support across our client base is expanding rapidly and encompasses important services, such as data analytics and
visualization, threat analysis, risk management, modeling and simulation, cost estimating, earned value management, program management, system engineering,
independent evaluation, and High Consequence Event prevention.
The Strategic Development Group (SDG) supports critical U.S. government programs providing systems engineering support, systems safety support, financial
analysis support as well as security policy, compliance, and assessments. SDG has a need for a summer 2027 intern to support the Navy’s Strategic Systems
Programs, specifically working in support of the organization’s Reentry Branch.
Applications can be submitted and will be reviewed until 13 November 2026.
Responsibilities
The SPA intern will be part of a cohort of first-time and returning interns providing on-site support to SPA’s Navy customer, Strategic Systems Programs (SSP).
Interns will complete a 10-week, 40 hour per week program starting in May 2027 and concluding in July or August 2027. Interns may be extended the option to work
remotely during the school year at a reduced level of effort, approximately 10 hours per week based on school work load. Upon graduation, SPA considers
successful interns for full-time employment. Housing and transportation are the responsibility of the intern.
Interns work under the overall cognizance of an SPA manager while receiving specific, daily tasking from their government mentor in the assigned SSP branch.
Tasks are related to the management of the Navy’s Fleet Ballistic Missile (FBM) program and include administrative, organizational, and management functions. A
technical knowledge base will help in accomplishing daily tasks. Specifically, this intern will:
- Support the development and implementation of data analysis, data pipleline engineering, and software tools
- Fulfill technical, programmatic, and organizational tasking set out by a government mentor in support of the assigned branch’s business rhythms
- Organize and attend meetings related to the branch’s technical function
- Perform technical research
- Draft, review, and/or edit documents
- Complete a “summer project”
- Attend weekly SPA informational roundtables and working lunches with SPA leadership
Qualifications
Required Qualifications
- Must be a U.S. Citizen with the ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Secret security clearance.
- Active enrollment in an accredited undergraduate or post-graduate program in Computer Science, Data Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering,
Information Systems, Mathematics, or related technical or engineering field.
- Must have completed at least 2 years of undergraduate experience.
- Must be able to work on-site full-time, 5 days per week, at a government facility in the Washington Navy Yard and conduct multi-day domestic travel as
required (fully reimbursed).
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Ability to adapt quickly, prioritize tasks, and perform in a fast-paced, mission-focused work environment.
Desired Qualifications
- Desire to learn and work with DoD acquisition processes.
- Previous internship experience in computer science, data science or related fields.
- Experience with developing data analysis or software tools through coursework, projects, or internships.
- Experience with Python, Java, C++, MATLAB, or SQL through coursework, projects or internships.
- Professional certifications in software, data, or cloud technologies are a plus, but not required (for example, AWS Certified Data Engineer, Microsfot Azure
or Fabric data certifications, Google Professional Data Engineer, Databricks Certified Data Engineer, CompTIA Security+, or in a specific programming language
or application).
