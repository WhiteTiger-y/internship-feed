Position Summary
Our Oncology Discovery Biology / In Vivo Pharmacology team is developing innovative T-cell engager (TCE) therapies for cancer, with the goal of addressing unmet
therapeutic needs for patients.
The intern will contribute to oncology discovery research using in vitro assays, flow cytometry, mammalian tissue culture, and animal tissue processing and
analysis. Through hands-on laboratory experience and collaboration with scientists, the intern will build practical knowledge of oncology biology, immuno-
oncology, drug discovery and translational research.
Key Responsibilities
- Support in vitro studies characterizing biological responses to investigational oncology therapeutics, including T-cell engagers.
- Perform mammalian cell culture, including routine cell maintenance and preparation of cells for experiments.
- Assist with flow cytometry sample preparation, data acquisition and analysis.
- Support animal tissue collection, processing and downstream analysis from preclinical oncology studies.
- Document experimental procedures, observations and results in accordance with laboratory practices.
- Organize and analyze experimental data with appropriate scientific software.
- Prepare figures, data summaries and presentations for the project team.
Qualifications & Experience
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Biology, Cancer Biology, Immunology, Pharmacology, Biochemistry, Biomedical Sciences or a related
discipline.
- Strong academic performance and demonstrated interest in biomedical research and oncology drug discovery.
- Prior academic or research lab experience preferred; exposure to mammalian tissue culture preferred.
- Experience with flow cytometry, cell-based assays or biological sample processing desirable.
- Familiarity with common scientific and data-analysis software.
- Strong attention to detail, organization, communication and teamwork skills.
- All candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States at the time of hire and for the duration of their employment. Please note that visa
sponsorship, including for student or temporary work authorization programs, is not available for this role. Candidates must have work authorization that does
not require sponsorship now or in the future.
Core Skills
- Mammalian tissue culture and aseptic technique
- Basic understanding of cancer biology, immunology, cell biology or pharmacology
- Flow cytometry sample preparation and basic data analysis
- In vitro cell-based assays
- Animal tissue processing and preparation
- Accurate lab documentation and record keeping
- Data organization, interpretation and presentation; Microsoft Office
