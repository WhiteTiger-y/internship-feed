About The Role
We are seeking interns to join a small pod building an enterprise AI capability that helps leadership move faster from information to insight. The capability
will retrieve from document collections, query structured data and call live data sources, and it must be accurate, traceable and controlled. You'll work on the
parts that decide whether it can be trusted: agent design, retrieval, grounding and citation, evaluation, guardrails and the architecture reviews needed to take
it toward production.
What You Will Be Responsible For
- Business process to agent logic - document current business processes, decision rules and handoffs with stakeholders, then translate that logic into agent
steps, tools and approval points within an orchestrated, multi-agent workflow.
- Build agent workflows - design and prototype agent orchestration, tool use, retrieval and prompts on approved enterprise AI platforms (such as Microsoft
Foundry and Copilot Studio).
- Retrieval and grounding - experiment with chunking, embeddings, hybrid search, re-ranking and metadata filtering, and make sure every answer cites its source
and shows how current it is.
- Evaluation and testing - build question sets from real executive questions with known-good answers, then measure accuracy, groundedness, citation fidelity and
hallucination rates on every change.
- MVP - help define versions of the tool that works and can be added to.
- Monitoring, cost and performance - track answer quality, latency and consumption over time and recommend tuning.
- Architecture and governance readiness - prepare inputs for architecture review, Responsible AI and model risk validation, records retention and e-discovery,
operational resilience, and audit readiness.
- Leadership updates - provide regular, concise progress updates to senior leadership, covering what was delivered, key findings, risks or blockers, and next
steps, and adjust the level of detail for executive and technical audiences.
Every pod member wears more than one hat
You'll Work In a Small, Cross-functional Pod Alongside Interns In AI Engineering, Data, Product, Governance And Cybersecurity. Depending On Your Strengths, You
May Also
- Turn governance and risk requirements into concrete design patterns, such as human approval points and access boundaries.
- Write clear technical documentation and decision records.
- Partner with the data and cybersecurity interns on secure connectors and test plans.
Required
- Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, AI/Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering or a related field.
- Strong Python and hands-on experience with LLM APIs or frameworks (such as Azure OpenAI, Semantic Kernel, LangChain/LangGraph or LlamaIndex).
- Practical understanding of RAG, embeddings, vector search and LLM evaluation methods.
- Awareness of LLM reliability and security risks, including hallucination, prompt injection and data leakage.
- Familiarity with software engineering practices: Git, testing, APIs and basic CI/CD.
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD program. Underclassmen may apply.
- Available January to June 2027 for at least 30 hours per week, including working onsite 4 days per week.
- Clear written and verbal communication, including explaining technical work to non-technical and senior audiences.
- Curiosity, ownership and comfort working with ambiguity in a highly regulated environment.
- Experience with multi-agent or tool-calling architectures, or with Azure AI Search / Microsoft Foundry / Copilot Studio agents.
- Coursework or interest in AI governance, Responsible AI, model risk, regulation or technology law.
Preferred
- Experience with financial, market or news data, or with complex legal or financial documents.
- Experience presenting to senior leaders or executive audiences, with excellent verbal presentation skills and the ability to act as a bridge between business
and technology teams, explaining technical work in business terms and turning business needs into clear technical requirements.
