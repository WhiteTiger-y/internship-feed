AECOM is seeking a motivated and analytical Engineering/Data Analytics Intern to support transportation operations, quality assurance, and engineering
initiatives associated with the Georgia Department of Transportation (GDOT).
This position offers hands-on experience in data analytics, Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), traffic operations, quality management, and process
improvement. The intern will collaborate with engineers, analysts, and transportation professionals to evaluate operational performance, develop data-driven
solutions, and identify opportunities to enhance efficiency and service delivery.
The ideal candidate is a proactive problem-solver who demonstrates curiosity, initiative, attention to detail, and a willingness to learn and challenge existing
processes.
The responsibilities of this position include, but are not limited to:
1. Data Analytics & Visualization
- Collect, validate, and analyze operational datasets to identify trends, performance gaps, and improvement opportunities.
- Support the development and optimization of Power BI dashboards, data dictionaries, and performance reporting tools.
- Analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) and translate complex data into actionable insights for leadership and stakeholders.
2. Quality Assurance & Process Improvement
- Support quality audits of TMC operator calls, communications, and operational activities to evaluate compliance with established procedures.
- Assist in reviewing reports, dashboards, and other deliverables for accuracy, consistency, and completeness.
- Identify opportunities to automate repetitive workflows and improve efficiency using tools such as Python, Excel, and Power Query.
3. Transportation Operations & Lane Closure Analysis
- Analyze traffic data, including speeds, volumes, travel times, and congestion trends, to evaluate the impacts of lane closures and traffic incidents.
- Support incident performance evaluations, including verification, response, and clearance times.
- Assist in preparing After-Action Reports (AARs), operational assessments, and data-driven recommendations.
4. ITS Engineering & QPL-48 Support
- Assist in reviewing ITS product applications for GDOT's Qualified Products List (QPL-48), including technical documentation, specifications, and compliance
matrices.
- Support product evaluation tracking, field testing coordination, and engineering documentation.
- Gain exposure to ITS technologies, including CCTV cameras, dynamic message signs, radar, LiDAR, and communications equipment.
5. Project Collaboration, Stakeholder Engagement & Professional Development
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams on engineering assessments, performance reporting, and continuous improvement initiatives.
- Gain exposure to professional communication and coordination with government agency representatives, engineers, contractors, vendors, and other project
stakeholders.
- Develop effective written and verbal communication skills through technical discussions, project meetings, presentations, and client-facing interactions.
- Participate in operational shadowing at the TMC to better understand transportation management workflows.
- Take ownership of assigned tasks, communicate findings, and contribute innovative ideas to improve existing processes.
Qualifications

Analytics or another related field and must have completed at least 1 year of study. Candidates who have graduated with a Bachelor’s degree and plan to continue
with the Master’s degree are eligible to apply for these positions. Graduates must have been enrolled in their bachelor’s degree in the most previous school
term with an interest in pursuing an advanced degree in the same or similar discipline at the conclusion of the internship.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Strong analytical, critical-thinking, and problem-solving skills.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel; familiarity with Power BI, Python, SQL, or other data analytics tools is a plus.
- Interest in transportation engineering, ITS, operational analytics, or process improvement.
- Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and effective communication.
- Self-motivated, adaptable, and eager to learn in a collaborative environment.
