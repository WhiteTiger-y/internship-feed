About InterDigital
InterDigital is a global research and development company focused primarily on wireless, video, artificial intelligence (“AI”), and related technologies. We
design and develop foundational technologies that enable connected, immersive experiences in a broad range of communications and entertainment products and
services. We license our innovations worldwide to companies providing such products and services, including makers of wireless communications devices, consumer
electronics, IoT devices, cars and other motor vehicles, and providers of cloud-based services such as video streaming. As a leader in wireless technology, our
engineers have designed and developed a wide range of innovations that are used in wireless products and networks, from the earliest digital cellular systems to
5G and today’s most advanced Wi-Fi technologies. We are also a leader in video processing and video encoding/decoding technology, with a significant AI research
effort that intersects with both wireless and video technologies. Founded in 1972, InterDigital is listed on Nasdaq.

Summary
We are seeking a highly motivated Ph.D. student to join our Wireless Research and Innovation team. This role focuses on next-generation wireless systems
integrated with AI/ML, including 6G research, and AI-native communication systems.
The ideal candidate combines strong theoretical foundations with hands-on simulation experience in Python and MATLAB. The candidate will contribute to advanced
research, AI-native wireless systems and simulations, standards-oriented innovation, publications, and patentable technologies.
Key Responsibilities
- Conduct advanced research in wireless communication systems in Physical layer, and AI/ML design
- Work on internal technology development efforts and ensure alignment with broader project objectives
- Design and evaluate AI/ML-driven wireless algorithms (e.g., semantic communications, wireless foundation models, token communications)
- Develop and implement link-level and system-level simulations using tools such as Python, Sionna, MATLAB or equivalent frameworks
- Analyze and optimize system performance using theoretical and data-driven approaches
- Contribute to technical reports, publications, and patent filings
- Contribute to the generation of pre-standard inputs and industry publications in areas of expertise
- Collaborate with cross-functional research teams across wireless, AI, and standards
Required Qualifications
- Pursuing Ph.D. (3rd year or later) in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a closely related field.
- Strong foundation in the following areas:
- Wireless communications
- Signal processing
- Solid knowledge of AI/ML techniques and their application to wireless communication
- Experience with simulation and programming tools, such as:
- Sionna, Python, MATLAB, or similar languages
- Machine learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, GPU)
- Demonstrated ability to translate theory into working simulations
Preferred Qualifications
- Hands-on experience with System-level or link-level simulators (e.g., Sionna or equivalent)
- Research track record including publications, patents, or technical contributions
- Familiarity with cellular standards (4G/5G/6G evolution)
- Experience in AI-native wireless systems, semantic communications, wireless foundation models, token communications, etc.
What You Bring
- Curiosity and passion for pushing the boundaries of wireless and AI convergence
- Ability to balance theoretical rigor with practical implementation
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
- A collaborative mindset aligned with a global research environment
Locations: Conshohocken, PA or Melville, NY
