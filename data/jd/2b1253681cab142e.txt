General Summary
GTI Energy, a leading energy research, development, and training organization, is seeking an intern to join its Modeling and Energy Systems Analytics (MESA)
Team for Summer 2027. The intern will enhance our capabilities in data science, modeling, analytics, statistics, or other quantitative methods by supporting
enterprise-wide initiatives. In this role, the Intern will support ongoing projects, including scientific research and the development of new technologies,
novel insights, and low-carbon energy systems using data-driven modeling and analysis.

Primary Responsibilities
In this role, you may:
- Perform exploratory data analyses and apply statistical modeling and machine learning approaches to answer research questions and develop tools to support
data-driven decision making
- Scrape and wrangle data from open-source repositories
- Create data visualizations and dashboards to communicate insights and findings to stakeholders
- Develop energy systems models to characterize interactions among energy supply and demand, as impacted by new technologies, climate, and weather
- Apply and develop advanced analytical techniques such as AI, computer vision, and neural networks
- Develop presentations and written reports to communicate findings
- Develop unique approaches to investigating key research questions, including leading programs related to methane emissions mitigation, digitalization in
energy, and net zero energy systems
- Support ongoing research efforts using a disciplined approach and standard best practices
- Participate in 1-on-1 and team meetings and contribute to team projects
- Participate in GTI Energy Modeling and Energy System Analytics team building and networking events
- Perform other duties as assigned
