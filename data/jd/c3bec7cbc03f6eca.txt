About the Role
We're looking for curious, analytical, and innovative Data Science & Analytics Interns to join us for Summer 2027.
As an intern, you'll have the opportunity to work on meaningful, real-world challenges that directly impact eBay's business and the experiences of millions of
buyers and sellers. You'll take ownership of projects, turn complex data into actionable insights, and contribute ideas that help shape business and product
decisions.
You'll collaborate with experienced Data Scientists, Analysts, Engineers, Product Managers, and business leaders, gaining hands-on experience, mentorship, and
exposure to how data informs decisions at scale.
This is an opportunity to grow your technical and problem-solving skills, explore new approaches and technologies, and make a measurable impact while learning
from teams across eBay.
What You Will Accomplish
- Uncover meaningful trends in large-scale customer, transactional, and product data, translating analysis into actionable recommendations that improve
marketplace experiences and business decisions.
- Apply statistical methods, predictive modeling, machine learning, and customer segmentation to understand behavior, investigate performance drivers, and
identify opportunities for growth.
- Help improve customer engagement, retention, discovery, and personalization by evaluating customer journeys and recommending data-informed product and
marketplace improvements.
- Partner with Product, Engineering, Data Science, and business teams to define success metrics, support experiments and A/B tests, and assess the impact of
product changes and business initiatives.
- Explore Generative AI and large language models to make analytics workflows more effective, including natural-language-to-SQL and AI-assisted analysis, while
assessing output accuracy and reliability.
- Build clear dashboards, visualizations, and data stories; share findings with technical and nontechnical partners, and grow your skills through hands-on work
and collaboration with experienced teammates.
What You Will Bring
Basic Qualifications
- Currently enrolled full time in a 4-year accredited U.S. Master's program in Data Science, Analytics, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related
quantitative field, with an expected graduation date between December 2027 and Summer 2028.
- No more than 3 years of relevant professional experience, excluding internships, co-ops, fellowships, and similar student experiences.
- Proficiency in SQL and Python to analyze, manipulate, and draw insights from data.
- A strong foundation in statistics, analytical methods, and data science, with experience using datasets to identify patterns, test hypotheses, and solve
problems through coursework, research, projects, or work.
- Familiarity with at least one NoSQL or big data tool, such as Hadoop, Hive, Spark, Pig, or H2O.
- Familiarity with Generative AI, LLMs, or AI-assisted analytics tools, combined with analytical problem-solving and the ability to communicate findings and
collaborate with diverse partners.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with machine learning, predictive modeling, or customer segmentation.
- Exposure to AI concepts such as prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI agents, or natural-language-to-SQL.
- Experience using Tableau or similar tools to visualize data and communicate insights.
- Familiarity with distributed data processing technologies such as Spark, Hadoop, or Hive.
- Experience with experimentation, A/B testing, or statistical hypothesis testing.
- Interest in product analytics, customer behavior, e-commerce, personalization, or marketplace dynamics; exposure to R or Java is a plus.
What Makes You Successful
- You're naturally curious and enjoy understanding not just what the data shows, but why it matters.
- You combine strong analytical thinking with a willingness to experiment, learn new technologies, and challenge assumptions.
- You're comfortable working through ambiguity, collaborating with others, and connecting technical insights to meaningful business outcomes.
- Most importantly, you're excited to use data, analytics, and emerging AI technologies to solve real-world problems and make an impact at scale.
