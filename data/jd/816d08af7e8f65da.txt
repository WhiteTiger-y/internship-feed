Job Description
The Composites team is committed to pushing the boundaries of innovation in polymer and ceramic matrix composite material systems. Our expertise includes the
development and refinement of processing techniques, the characterization of material properties and behavior, and the integration of processing,
microstructure, and properties to optimize composite material systems for high-performance applications.
As a Composites Intern, You Will
- Work on programs focused on developing process models for polymer matrix composite (PMC) material systems.
- Design and execute test plans to analyze material behavior using methods such as rheology, thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), differential scanning calorimetry
(DSC), and X-ray diffraction (XRD).
- Analyze experimental data and develop numerical models of material behavior and processing.
- Fabricate test coupons for characterization, including resin mixing, resin castings, and composite layup.
- Document your work, including materials used, processing details, test and characterization details, and results.
- Present your work in weekly team meetings with stakeholders to communicate progress, solicit input and guidance, and share results.
At GE Aerospace Research, scientists, engineers, and technicians innovate for the future of flight. An internship with us will provide valuable hands-on
experience to help you launch your career and you will have the opportunity to work on really cool things with smart and collaborative people.

Desired Qualifications
