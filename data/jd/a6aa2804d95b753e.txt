The Cisco Product Internship Program offers a 12-week summer experience designed to welcome early-career talent into this mission. We embed interns within high-
impact teams, where they collaborate directly on the products, platforms, and architectures shaping Cisco's future and powering the AI era. Throughout the
program, interns have the opportunity to participate in targeted learning sessions, multi-functional networking, and direct engagement with leaders across the
organization. By immersing interns in real-world challenges, we help them build the technical skills, confidence, and collaborative approach needed to envision
a long-term, impactful career at Cisco.
Your Impact
You’ll dive into the development and implementation of cutting-edge generative AI applications using large language models like GPT-4, Claude, and Llama. You’ll
optimize neural networks for natural language processing and machine perception, focusing on performance, scalability, and reliability as you train and fine-
tune models for deployment. By building and fine-tuning cutting-edge generative AI and transformer-based models to deliver high-impact, intelligent experiences,
you keep Cisco at the forefront of AI innovation and help us shape the future of intelligent products.
