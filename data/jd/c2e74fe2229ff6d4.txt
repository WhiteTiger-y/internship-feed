Responsibilities
Peraton is seeking Summer 2027 Cyber Research Interns in our Basking Ridge, NJ, Silver Spring, MD, Bedford, NH, West Lafayette, IN or other Peraton Labs office
locations.
Peraton Labs delivers innovative solutions and revolutionary new capabilities to solve the most difficult and complex challenges for government agencies,
utilities, and commercial customers. With a distinguished heritage tracing back to Bell Labs, Bellcore, DHPC, and Telcordia, we are a self-sustaining research
center within Peraton that provides applied research and engineering to enable government agencies, utilities and commercial enterprises to fully exploit the
future of communications, analytics and cyber security. Peraton Labs’ cybersecurity research protects mission-critical systems and national cyber infrastructure
through a broad range of initiatives in computer network defense, cyber operations, secure-by-design techniques, and experimentation platforms.
Responsibilities:
Qualified applicants must be able to perform applied research and development in one or more key areas of interest:
- Design and implement state-of-the-art Deep Learning models to detect cyber security threats.
- Design and implement cyber deception technologies to deter adversaries
- Conduct literature survey on state-of-the-art cyber deception technologies.
- Conduct literature survey on adversarial attacks against Deep Learning architectures.
- Rapidly develop Proof-of-Concepts (PoCs) demonstrating research findings.
- Prepare documentations and presentations summarizing research findings.
- Collaborate with team members and integrate PoCs/prototypes into a unified technology.
- Create a broader impact by developing implementation plans for transferring the technology to Government customers.
- Full-spectrum cyber operations, offensive and defensive cyber operations.
- Software reverse engineering, vulnerability research, and exploit development.
- Machine learning methods as applied to cyber security problems.
- Program analysis (including vulnerability detection and/or remediation)

Qualifications
Required Qualifications :
- HS diploma and some advanced level training.
- US Citizenship required.
- Must be a current student actively pursuing a BS/BA, or preferably a MS or PhD, degree majoring in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Computer Engineering,
Cybersecurity, Information Science, or related field.
- Demonstrated hands-on experience building systems in Python (PyTorch and Tensorflow) or C/C++; additional languages (Java, Rust, Go, etc) is a plus.
- Knowledge of Linux and Windows platforms.
- Ability to integrate, build, and test high-quality technical solutions.
- Effective communication and writing skills.
Desired Qualifications :
- Familiarity with the principles and technologies used in modern TCP/IP networks is a plus.
- Experience working with cyber datasets is a plus.
- A passion to learn something new every day and to stay up-to-date with developments in your field.
- Adept at independent problem-solving and comfortable with a general approach of taking initiative to find and develop solutions.
- Ability and desire to quickly acquire knowledge of new subject matter areas.
- Working knowledge of modern computer networking technologies.
