Job Description
The Future Talent Program within our Research & Development Division Data, AI, and Genome Sciences (DAGS) Department is offering co-op opportunities that last a
period of six months. The position is in the Precision Genetics group with focus on phenotyping cardiometabolic diseases. Candidates can apply for the
opportunity to be involved in a project designed to enable students to further develop their skills.
Precision Genetics, Cardiometabolic Diseases (1 Position, South San Francisco)
- Develop quantitative and longitudinal phenotypes from EHR using AI/ML methods in large human cohorts
- Define clinically meaningful patient populations and translate disease concepts into reproducible biobank phenotypes
- Assist with performing genetic analyses for drug targets identification and prioritization (GWAS, Mendelian Randomization, colocalization)
- Build and maintain reproducible pipelines in cloud workspace
Required Education and Skills:
- Candidates must be currently enrolled in a minimum of a Master's degree program in Statistical Genetics, Genetic Epidemiology, Bioinformatics, Computational
Biology, Statistics, or a related field of study. Students enrolled in a PhD program are highly encouraged to apply
- Candidates must be available to work full-time for 6 months in 2027 as part of a formal co-op program
Preferred Skills And Experience
- Experience with analyzing large-scale genomic datasets, including GWAS and sequencing data
- Experience with multidimensional and longitudinal data analysis in biology or medicine and familiarity with medical coding system (ICD, SNOMED, CPT)
- Experience with cloud computing platforms (e.g., Databricks, AWS) and biobank research analysis platforms (e.g., UKB RAP, All of Us Workbench)
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, R and experience with machine learning and deep learning methods
- Track record of scientific communication and of independent research (publications, conference presentations) and strong communication skills
Please note that this position may be closed before the posted end date or may remain open longer, at the discretion of the company.
