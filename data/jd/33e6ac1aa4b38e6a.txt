Job Description Work Arrangement:  Hybrid: This internship is categorized as on-site. The selected intern is expected to report to the office 3 days a week. 
Location:  Sunnyvale, CA  About the Team Our Mapping organization is building next-generation mapping systems that go beyond static high-definition maps. We use
onboard sensor data, algorithms, and machine learning to help reconstruct and maintain maps at scale. These maps support localization, perception, simulation,
and autonomous driving. The Role We’re looking for a Software Engineering Intern to join our Mapping Engineering team. You’ll work with experienced engineers on
a scoped project related to map data, geospatial algorithms, or mapping pipeline quality. Depending on the project, you may help process sensor-derived data,
improve how map features are represented or validated, or evaluate methods for identifying map changes. You’ll receive guidance from a mentor and collaborate
with partner teams such as Perception, Localization, and Simulation. This internship is an opportunity to apply your software engineering skills to real-world
mapping challenges while learning how research and prototypes become dependable engineering systems. What You’ll Do Contribute to a well-defined mapping or
geospatial engineering project, with support from your mentor and team. Write, test, and document code in Python and/or C++ for tasks such as geometry
processing, map data analysis, or pipeline tooling. Explore road-network and map concepts, including lanes, intersections, boundaries, connectivity, and map
attributes. Help analyze sensor-derived or third-party map data and investigate data quality or map consistency issues. Prototype or evaluate algorithms and
machine-learning approaches for feature extraction, map change detection, or map quality checks, as relevant to your project. Build or improve tests, validation
tools, metrics, and visualizations to help assess the correctness of map data or software. Share project progress, findings, and a final demo or summary with
your mentor and teammates. Participate in code reviews, design discussions, and team learning activities. Minimum Qualifications Currently pursuing a bachelor’s
or master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, GIS, or a related technical field. Experience programming in Python or C++ through
coursework, research, internships, or personal projects. Familiarity with core software engineering practices such as version control, testing, and writing
readable code. Foundational understanding of data structures and algorithms. Interest in geospatial systems, robotics, autonomous vehicles, computer vision, or
machine learning. Ability to communicate technical ideas, ask questions, and work collaboratively. Preferred Qualifications Coursework, research, or project
experience in geospatial computing, computational geometry, robotics, computer vision, machine learning, or graph algorithms. Exposure to map or geographic data
formats and tools such as GeoJSON, OpenStreetMap, GeoPandas, GDAL/OGR, or PostGIS. Experience working with camera, lidar, radar, point-cloud, or other spatial
data. Experience with C++ and Python, or an interest in working across both languages. Familiarity with machine-learning workflows such as preparing datasets,
evaluating models, or building prototypes. Prior internship, research, or team-project experience developing software collaboratively. Compensation:  The
