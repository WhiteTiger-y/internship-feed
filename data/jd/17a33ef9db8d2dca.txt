Intern at Mary Kay!
Mary Kay is one of the world’s leading beauty and skincare companies, known for our iconic brand, entrepreneurial spirit, and people-first culture. Our
corporate teams support Independent Beauty Consultants around the globe through innovation, collaboration, and a shared commitment to enriching lives.
Mary Kay University is our 10-week summer internship program designed to give undergraduate and graduate students meaningful, real-world experience across a
variety of business functions. Your ideas matter here. As a Mary Kay intern, you’ll have the opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects, solve real
business challenges, and build the skills, relationships, and confidence to help shape your future.
Mary Kay University Offers
- Real world business experience
- Hands-on experience in your field
- A formal mentorship program
- Social and volunteer activities
- Collaboration with teams across the business
- Networking opportunities with leaders across Mary Kay
About Mary Kay Research And Development
Mary Kay's Research & Development teams are passionate about advancing beauty through science and innovation. From product formulation and safety to clinical
research, regulatory affairs, and process development, R&D professionals help bring high-quality, innovative products to consumers around the world.
Internship Opportunities Within R&D May Include
- Product Formulation
- Product Integrity
- Process Development & Commercialization
- Package Engineering
- Regulatory Affairs
- R&D Compliance
- Nutrition Science
- Product Safety
Minimum Qualifications
- Currently pursuing an undergraduate or master's degree and enrolled in an accredited university program at the time of application (full-time undergraduate or
part-time/full-time graduate students). Rising juniors and seniors preferred.
- Preferred Degrees: Chemical Engineering, Data Science, Science, Chemistry, Biology, Sensory Science, Life Science, Microbiology, or similar
The Value You Bring

- Interest in Artificial Intelligence
- Ability to quickly learn business software systems
- Strong attention to detail
- Passion for the beauty industry and cosmetic products

- Interest in sustainability (preferred, not required)

Program Details
- We offer a 10-week internship from late June to Mid-August
- All our internships are paid, starting at $20/hour.
- Our internships are Monday – Friday, 40 hours per week, and require an in-office presence 4-5 days per week.
- Our internships are located at our Global Headquarters in Addison, TX or our Manufacturing/ R&D Facility in Lewisville, TX.
- Mary Kay Inc does not offer sponsorship of job applicants for employment-based visas or any other work authorization for full-time employment at this time.
Intern Benefits
- Mary Kay Product discount
- Holiday Pay
- 24/7 Fitness Centers
- Onsite Health Clinics
- 401(k) eligibility
What To Expect From The Application Process
- We encourage you to apply as soon as possible, as internship opportunities are filled on a rolling basis.
- If selected to move forward, our campus recruiter will reach out to schedule a phone interview. Our interview process is virtual.
- Internship assignments will vary based on business needs, team priorities, and your interests and background. As opportunities are finalized, we'll match
candidates to the teams and projects that best align with their skills, experience, and career interests.
