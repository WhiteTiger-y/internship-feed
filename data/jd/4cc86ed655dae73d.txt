Fueled by innovation at the intersection of biology and technology, we’re developing the next generation of smarter, less invasive, more personalized

Are you passionate about improving and expanding the possibilities of surgery? Ready to join a team that’s reimagining how we heal? Our Surgery team will give

We are searching for the best talent for Biomedical Engineering Intern – Surgical Imaging, located in Raritan, NJ
Our team is at the forefront of developing advanced imaging technology aimed at improving laparoscopic and robotic surgeries. Project development currently
requires use of in vivo models, which present limitations due to resource requirements, slow turnaround times, and difficulty controlling the tissue state.
To overcome these challenges, we are initiating the development of benchtop ex vivo and in vitro models. These models are designed to simulate human blood and
tissue accurately, facilitating quicker and more controlled testing scenarios. Through our enhanced lab capabilities, we will be able to significantly expedite
and refine development of our laparoscopic camera's software and algorithms.
Key Responsibilities
You will be responsible for:
- Design and develop benchtop ex vivo and in vitro models simulating human tissue and blood
- Plan and implement experiments to validate model performance and imaging capabilities
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including imaging scientists and software engineers
- Automate testing workflows to improve efficiency and reproducibility
- Maintain detailed documentation of experimental procedures and results
- Present findings and insights to the broader R&D team
- Support validation of imaging algorithms using benchtop models
- Explore innovative materials and methods to improve model realism and functionality
