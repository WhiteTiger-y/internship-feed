The Role
This is a full-time summer internship based in Norwood, MA from January 18, 2027 – June 25, 2027 . Candidates must be available for the entire duration of the
program.
The CBL Compliance Intern will support laboratory compliance within Moderna’s GCLP internal laboratory at the Norwood CBL site. This role offers meaningful,
practical experience in a regulated laboratory environment, with the opportunity to contribute to ongoing laboratory and compliance initiatives.
Here’s What You’ll Do
- Support laboratory compliance activities within the CBL in alignment with GCLP guidelines, applicable procedures, and quality requirements.
- Assist with review and organization of laboratory governance documents, SOPs, forms, worksheets, and compliance records to support document accuracy,
traceability, and readiness for use.
- Support training compliance activities, including review and organization of training records and follow-up of documentation to help maintain complete and
current records.
- Gain exposure to Quality Event and issue management processes by assisting with documentation, tracking, data gathering, and follow-up activities under the
guidance of the Compliance team.
- Support audit and inspection readiness activities, including document preparation, request tracking, record organization, and other logistical support for
internal or external assessments.
- Assist with review of laboratory documentation for good documentation practices and ALCOA+ principles, including attributable, legible, contemporaneous,
original, accurate, complete, consistent, enduring, and available records.
- Collaborate with laboratory scientists, study teams, Compliance, and other cross-functional partners to support day-to-day compliance activities and project
deliverables.
- Contribute to special projects that strengthen laboratory quality systems and provide broad exposure to compliance within a regulated clinical laboratory
environment.
Here’s What You’ll Need (Minimum Qualifications)
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program in a STEM field (e.g., Biology, Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, Chemical Engineering, or
related discipline).
- Foundational laboratory, compliance quality skills applicable to analytical testing
- At Moderna, we are focused on delivering on our mission by enabling talent to thrive. For this role, we are currently unable to offer immigration sponsorship.
Candidates must already hold work authorization in US and be able to maintain that status without the need for future sponsorship.
Here’s What You’ll Bring to the Table (Preferred Qualifications)
- GPA of 3.5 (on a 4.0 scale) or equivalent academic achievement.
- Demonstrated ability to work independently while contributing effectively within a team environment.
- Strong written and verbal communication skill.
- High attention to detail and commitment to quality and compliance.
- Interest in working within a regulated laboratory environment.
- A desire to make an impact as part of a high-growth, transformational company that is Bold, Relentless, Curious, and Collaborative.
