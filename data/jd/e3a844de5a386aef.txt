Role Overview
ID.me is looking for an Analytics Engineer Intern to help us put AI to work across our data organization. You are a curious builder who loves turning data into
answers, and you’re ready to use AI to put those answers in the hands of the teams who rely on them every day.
As an intern, you'll build conversational analytics chatbots, automate data pipelines, and create detection agents that help us get more from our data and
improve the member experience.
Location: Mountain View, California- onsite in office five days a week
What You'll Work On
As an intern, you will own a focused set of AI enablement projects, working alongside analytics engineers, data analysts, and data engineers.
- Conversational analytics chatbots: Build chatbots that let teams ask questions of our data in plain language and get trusted answers.
- Data pipeline automation: Use AI-assisted tooling to automate and streamline parts of our data pipelines.
- Detection agents: Design agents that monitor data and flag anomalies, quality issues, and unusual patterns.
- Data architecture and domain-driven design: Explore how AI can improve our data models, documentation, and overall architecture.
Responsibilities
- Build and iterate on chatbot prototypes that support conversational analytics on top of Snowflake and dbt models
- Automate data pipeline tasks such as testing, documentation, and monitoring using AI tools
- Develop detection agents that surface data quality problems and anomalies
- Apply domain-driven design principles to help improve data models, metric definitions, and metadata
- Partner with analysts, engineers, and data scientists to understand needs and gather feedback
- Document your work and present results and learnings to the team at the end of the internship
Basic Qualifications
- College junior with an expected graduation year of 2028, currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Data
Analytics, Statistics, Math, or a related quantitative field. Students with a software engineering background who want to build a career in data are encouraged
to apply.
- Interest in AI and large language models, including prompting and building simple applications on top of them
- Basic understanding of data modeling and data warehousing concepts
What You'll Gain
- Hands-on experience building AI-powered analytics tools on a modern data stack
- Mentorship from experienced analytics engineers
- A real project with visible impact on how ID.me uses its data
- Exposure to data architecture, domain-driven design, and data governance in practice
- A portfolio-ready project to present to the team at the end of the internship
