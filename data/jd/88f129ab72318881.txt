The Nike Sport Research Lab (NSRL) is a multidisciplinary team of researchers, innovators, and scientists who lead with science to make athletes* measurably
better. We deliver proven insights and state-of-the-art capabilities to drive the future of Nike products and services. The NSRL Footwear Research team conducts
research that provides insights leading to innovative footwear and measurable benefits for athlete. We apply scientific and research results to drive new
footwear concepts and performance hypotheses across multiple innovation teams.
Who We Are Looking For
As a NIKE Sport Research Lab (NSRL) Graduate Researcher Intern, you will work on a project aimed at delivering work focused on validated performance insights
that will lead to technologically innovative footwear, apparel and equipment that are globally relevant.
Who You Will Work With
You will work closely with Lead and Principal Researchers to create research visions and plans. You will act as a creative scientist – questioning methods,
results, and applications – and think outside normal paradigms to find creative ways around barriers to enhancing performance or preventing injury.
What You Will Work On
You will participate in the development of new tools, methods and procedures that will streamline data acquisition. In addition, you will become a resident
expert for all biomechanical research tools used by the Nike Sport Research Lab staff. You will be a member of the Product Creation Process, interacting with
Lead Researchers, Principal Researchers, and Product Innovators in all phases of research to provide a holistic evaluation of innovation concepts.
What You Bring
- Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Engineering, Kinesiology, Human Factors, or Ergonomics with a graduation date between December 2027 and June 2028.
- Significant Python experience writing programs to analyze biomechanical data is strongly preferred
- Experience using in-lab and field-based biomechanical data collection tools with human test participants
- Demonstrated experience being a creative scientist – ability to apply scientific insights to performance product, and ability to problem solve are key
- Bias toward action and resilience in the face of challenges
- Exposure to industry research experience preferred
- Knowledge of footwear and equipment product creation processes is a plus
