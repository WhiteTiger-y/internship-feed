Meta Reality Labs Research is looking for an intern to help us develop the next-generation of policy learning algorithms for robotics applications. In
particular, we are seeking candidates who have experience with reinforcement learning, robotic policy learning, diffusion and flow models, vision-language-
action models (VLAs), and sim2real to devise effective algorithms to train control policies for dexterous robot manipulation.Our internships are twelve (12) to
twenty-four (24) weeks long and we have various start dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, Policy Learning for Robotics (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Develop and implement strategies for training dexterous robot manipulation policies that are efficient and robust under the complexity of contact.
- Implement and optimize the methods devised on a large-scale cluster infrastructure.
- Collaborate with research scientists and software engineers to build demos and experiences based on the developed tools.
- Plan, write and submit the research to high impact conferences, journals or workshops in the field of robot learning or reinforcement learning for broader
visibility.
Minimum Qualifications:
- Research skills involving defining problems, exploring solutions, and analyzing and presenting results
- Experience implementing numerical methods and/or deep learning models in a large-scale code
- Proficient in Python
- Must obtain work authorization in the country of employment at the time of hire and maintain ongoing work authorization during employment
Preferred Qualifications:
- Proven track record of achieving significant results as demonstrated by grants, fellowships, patents, as well as first-authored publications at leading
workshops or conferences such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, RLC, IROS, ICRA, RSS, CoRL or similar
- Experience with training deep learning models and familiarity with tools like PyTorch
- Experience with parallel distributed large-scale model training on CPU/GPU clusters
- Experience working and communicating cross functionally in a team environment
- Availability for minimum 16 consecutive week internship
