Who we are looking for We are looking for a motivated AI Engineering Intern to join the our team. The candidate will work with experienced engineers to design,
develop, and test end-to-end AI applications powered by Large Language Models (LLMs). This is a hands-on engineering role for students who are passionate about
AI and interested in turning emerging AI technologies into practical applications. What you will be responsible for The candidate will be involved in: Design
and develop LLM-powered applications and AI agents using Python. Work on key components of end-to-end AI solutions, including prompt engineering, RAG
(Retrieval-Augmented Generation), agent workflows, tool integration, and APIs. Explore and evaluate AI frameworks and technologies such as LangChain, LangGraph,
vector databases, and LLM APIs. Write clean, maintainable code and develop unit tests to ensure solution quality and reliability. Participate in debugging,
testing, and performance improvement of AI applications. Collaborate with senior developers, QA engineers, and other team members throughout the software
development lifecycle. Research and experiment with new AI/LLM technologies and contribute ideas to prototypes and production solutions. What we value Strong
interest in AI, Large Language Models, and Agentic AI. Solid problem-solving skills and willingness to learn new technologies quickly. Ability to work with
senior developers on AI application development, testing, and troubleshooting. Hands-on mindset and curiosity about how AI systems work beyond simply calling an
LLM API. Good communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively within an engineering team. Education & Preferred Qualifications Currently pursuing a
BS/MS in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field. Good programming skills in Python. Basic
understanding of software engineering concepts, including APIs, Git, unit testing, and debugging. Basic understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs) and
generative AI concepts. Experience with one or more of the following is a plus: LangChain / LangGraph or similar AI frameworks RAG and vector databases Prompt
engineering Cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure Machine Learning / NLP Previous AI/LLM projects, coursework, hackathons, or open-source contributions are a
plus, but not required. Most importantly, a strong passion for AI and eagerness to learn and build. Salary Range: $41,600 - $65,000 Annual The range quoted
