Job Purpose
Research, develop, and evaluate perception technologies, algorithms, and processes that enable machines to sense and interpret their environment in real time,
in support of an assigned goal or specific technical problem.
Key Responsibilities
- Architect robust perception systems capable of processing data from cameras, LiDAR, radar, and thermal sensors in real time.
- Develop and maintain computer vision, sensor fusion, and machine learning algorithms.
- Design for extreme environments, ensuring perception algorithms resist dust, mud, vibration, and shifting lighting.
- Oversee field-testing campaigns to validate system performance in real-world crop conditions and diverse growth stages.
Experience Required
- Masters' degree in Robotics or relevant field
