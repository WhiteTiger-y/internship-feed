A Taste of What You’ll Do
Join our team as a GIS (Geospatial Data Science) and be at the forefront of innovation in wine grape production. We are dedicated to fostering an inclusive
environment where diverse talents and perspectives drive our success in advancing digital agriculture practices. As an intern, you will contribute to research
and production support initiatives that aim to enhance yield, fruit composition, and wine quality. You will work alongside experts from various Winegrowing
Research disciplines and collaborate with internal partners and leading industry and academic collaborators.
You will process and analyze geospatial data from satellite and drone imagery, soil maps, climate information, and ground observations. Using machine learning,
you will contribute to projects involving crop health monitoring, disease detection, yield prediction, and vine water status. You will also use Python, R, and
cloud computing to automate spatial data processing and help develop accurate, high-quality spatial databases.
- Interns will develop and apply GIS and remote sensing solutions for advancing vineyard management and grape production practices.
- Assigned tasks include utilizing machine learning techniques to monitor crop health, disease detection, yield prediction, and vine water status.
- Interns will learn how GIS is applied to wine grape production across a wide-range of California production regions, grape varieties, and growing practices.
Embrace this opportunity to develop your skills and be part of a team that values innovation, collaboration, and diverse perspectives.
