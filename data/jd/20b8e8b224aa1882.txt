The Summer Intern will join the Inflammation Computational Biology team for a 12-week research internship focused on developing methods that enable scientists
to evaluate disease biology, systematically address unmet needs, and support early drug discovery programs. Understanding which biological programs drive a
disease, and how they relate to severity, patient stratification, treatment response, and related diseases, still depends largely on manual literature review
and is performed differently across indications. The intern will build agentic AI tools that bring together human omics data, published literature, and
computational models to answer these questions in a systematic, reproducible, and evidence-linked way. Results will inform target selection, patient selection,
mechanism of action, and indication expansion across inflammatory and fibrotic diseases such as inflammatory bowel disease (IBD).
How You Will Contribute:
- Process and integrate large public and internal human datasets (e.g., single-cell and bulk RNA-seq).
- Build a working prototype of an AI agent workflow that retrieves data, runs analyses, checks quality, and summarizes the evidence with references.
- Wrap existing analysis methods and models as callable tools (e.g., via function calling or MCP) that an agent can run, explain, and chain into multi-step
workflows.
- Combine these components into an agent prototype that scientists can query in plain language, with every answer linked back to its source data or publication.
- Deliver reproducible, version-controlled code (Git, containers, documented pipelines), short documentation, and a final presentation to the team and
leadership at the end of the summer.
Internship Development Opportunities:
- Own an end-to-end project that feeds directly into Takeda’s AI-enabled Target Identification & Validation product strategy.
- Gain hands-on experience applying agentic AI and large language models to target discovery in industry.
- Learn how computational evidence shapes target selection and portfolio decisions.
- See how scientific products are scoped, prioritized, and adopted, working alongside product, technical, and subject-matter expert leads.
