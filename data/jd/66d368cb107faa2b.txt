About The Team
The Onboard Autonomy team develops the real-time intelligence that enables autonomous vehicles to perceive their surroundings, plan safe and comfortable
behavior, and operate reliably on the vehicle. Our work spans sensor fusion, structured perception, trajectory generation, ML planning, local planning,
controls, guardrails, evaluation, and vehicle integration.
We combine machine learning, robotics, optimization, and software engineering to build production-oriented autonomy systems that operate in real time and handle
challenging driving scenarios.
About The Role
As an Onboard Autonomy Intern, you will contribute to a focused research or engineering project within the onboard driving stack. You will work with experienced
researchers and engineers to prototype, evaluate, and improve algorithms or software using vehicle data, simulation, or production-oriented environments.
Potential focus areas include:
- Sensor Encoders and Fusion: Convert camera, lidar, radar, and other sensor inputs into useful representations for autonomy.
- Structured Perception: Improve object tracking, online maps, scene understanding, and long-tail scenario handling.
- ML Planning and Trajectory Generation: Predict behavior, generate trajectories, and select safe driving actions.
- Local Planning and Controls: Balance collision avoidance, traffic rules, vehicle dynamics, kinematic feasibility, and passenger comfort.
- Guardrails and Integration: Validate and integrate autonomy components across vehicle platforms to support safe, reliable operation.
What You’ll Do
- Prototype and evaluate algorithms for perception, prediction, planning, trajectory generation, controls, or integration.
- Work with real-world driving data and simulation to measure performance and investigate failures.
- Build data-processing, visualization, testing, and evaluation tools.
- Contribute to production-quality software through design reviews, code reviews, automated testing, continuous integration, and documentation.
- Analyze results, improve system performance, collaborate across disciplines, and present technical findings.

- Strong Python skills and experience with C++ or another systems programming language.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.

Preferred Qualifications
