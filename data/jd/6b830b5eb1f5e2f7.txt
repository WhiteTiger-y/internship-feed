this role. The Summer 2027 Predictive Modeling & Data Science Graduate Internship provides graduate students with an opportunity to gain hands-on experience
developing advanced analytics and data science solutions that support meaningful business decisions, processes, and strategic initiatives. This internship is
designed for students who are passionate about using data, statistics, and machine learning to solve complex problems and translate analytical findings into
actionable business insights. As a member of the advanced analytics team, the intern will work alongside experienced data scientists, analysts, and business
partners to help design, develop, and evaluate analytical solutions that address real-world business challenges. Participants will gain practical experience
applying statistical and machine learning methodologies, developing predictive models, analyzing complex datasets, and visualizing results to support data-
driven decision-making. The program provides exposure to the end-to-end analytics lifecycle, from understanding a business question and exploring available data
through model development, evaluation, visualization, and delivery of findings. Interns will also have opportunities to explore new data sources, analytical
tools, and emerging techniques while contributing to next-generation analytics initiatives. Typical responsibilities include conducting exploratory and
statistical analysis, supporting predictive model development, preparing and transforming data, evaluating model results, creating data visualizations,
researching new analytical approaches, and communicating findings and recommendations to technical and business audiences. Through mentorship, cross-functional
collaboration, and project-based learning, participants will strengthen their technical, analytical, problem-solving, and storytelling skills while building a
