Job Description:
As a Systems Engineering Graduate Intern , you will help develop and validate next-generation Robotics and Physical AI platforms by integrating hardware,
software, and Intel compute technologies into complete system solutions. Working alongside multidisciplinary engineering teams, you will gain hands-on
experience with robotics hardware, AI workloads, system integration, performance optimization, and technical troubleshooting.
This role is ideal for candidates passionate about robotics, artificial intelligence, and system-level engineering who want to contribute to cutting-edge
technologies while developing practical industry experience.
What You Will Do
- Support the design, integration, and validation of robotics and Physical AI platforms.
- Work with robotics hardware including sensors, cameras, compute platforms, networking equipment, and AI accelerators.
- Develop, test, and troubleshoot hardware and software solutions in a laboratory environment.
- Assist in deploying and evaluating AI and machine learning workloads on edge and robotics platforms.
- Conduct system-level debugging and root cause analysis to identify and resolve performance or integration issues.
- Measure and analyze key system metrics including latency, throughput, power consumption, and overall performance.
- Collaborate with hardware, software, silicon, and systems engineering teams to evaluate new technologies and solutions.
- Document findings, communicate technical results, and contribute to recommendations for future product development.
Qualifications:
Minimum Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a PhD degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field
- Knowledge of computer architecture and computing systems, including CPUs, GPUs, and AI accelerators.
- Experience through coursework, research, academic projects, or internships in AI/ML and robotics-related applications.
- Familiarity with Linux operating systems and development environments.
Preferred Qualifications
- Strong academic performance with a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher preferred.
- Experience with ROS/ROS 2 or other robotics software frameworks.
- Hands-on experience debugging and troubleshooting hardware and software systems.
- Understanding of AI model deployment and system performance evaluation.
- Experience working with robotics hardware, sensors, cameras, and embedded systems.
- Familiarity with AI inference, edge computing, and real-time applications.
- Experience benchmarking and optimizing AI or robotics workloads.
- Understanding of networking concepts and system integration.
- Prior laboratory or research experience in Robotics, Autonomous Systems, or Physical AI.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Demonstrated ability to work effectively in cross-functional engineering teams.
- Self-motivated with a passion for learning emerging technologies.
