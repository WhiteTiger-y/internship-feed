About the Oracle Veteran Internship Program (OVIP):
Oracle is proud to sponsor an internship and integration program that exposes transitioning military veterans and active-duty Military Spouses new to the
corporate culture, provides hands-on job-skill training and experience, and offers enhanced professional and personal development. At Oracle, we are committed
to the development and professional growth of our veterans and military spouses. Our paid internship program is specifically designed to aid military veterans,
transitioning servicemembers and active-duty military spouses new to the corporate sector in their transition to a career in the private or public sector.
Veterans and Military Spouses accepted into our program will work closely with corporate leadership, military veteran coaches and HR professionals to help
prepare for a successful transition. Interns will engage in on-the-job training and professional development in fields such as information technology,
technical/systems consulting, technical support, facilities, finance, human resources, logistics, marketing, sales or developmental training in sales or for
customer support (as available).

Responsibilities
We are seeking a highly motivated AI Agent Intern to join Oracle’s Supply Chain Applications (SCM) Solution Engineering organization. This intern will work at
the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Logistics Execution, and Oracle Fusion SCM Applications, helping design, prototype, and demonstrate next-generation
AI Agents for Supply Chain.
You will support the development of AI-driven capabilities across Oracle Transportation Management (OTM), Global Trade Management (GTM), Warehouse Management
(WMS), Inventory, and SCM Analytics. You’ll collaborate with senior Solution Engineers, product experts, and Oracle’s AI innovation teams to build proof-of-
concepts, demo assets, and internal accelerators that showcase the “Autonomous, Agentic Supply Chain of the Future.”
This role is ideal for someone who is passionate about AI, optimization, and modern logistics—and wants hands-on experience building intelligent supply chain
solutions used by the world’s largest organizations.
Key Responsibilities
AI Agent Development & Prototyping
- Assist in designing and building AI Agents using Oracle AI Services, OCI Generative AI, and Fusion SCM data.
- Prototype agent workflows for logistics scenarios (e.g., shipment risk detection, exception resolution, trade compliance, warehouse task optimization).
- Support development of RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipelines using Oracle’s Agent Studio and Vector Store.
- Conduct data exploration and create sample datasets for agent testing.
Solution Engineering & Demo Support
- Build and maintain demo assets for OTM, GTM, WMS, Inventory, and SCM Analytics.
- Support interactive scenarios illustrating AI-driven logistics (e.g., temperature excursions, carrier delays, customs risks, inbound ETA disruptions).
- Help create reference architectures, diagrams, and demo scripts.
Research & Analysis
- Evaluate logistics AI use cases (forecasting, resilience, risk management, optimization).
- Conduct analysis across leading AI supply chain platforms.
- Document best practices for designing AI Agents in Oracle SCM.
Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Work with senior SCM SEs across Logistics, Order Management, Inventory, and Manufacturing.
- Engage with Oracle AI Champions, Applied Technologists, and Product Strategy.
- Participate in team enablement sessions, conferences/trade shows, and industry-focused prototype work.
