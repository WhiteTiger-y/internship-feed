About the team
We are seeking an exceptional Research Scientist Intern to join our team to pioneer the next generation of Enterprise Agentic Solutions and Self-Evolving AI
Agents. This role bridges cutting-edge research with production impact. You will work with proprietary internal datasets (including USDS and compliance-
restricted environments) to design, evaluate, and deploy autonomous agents that offload routine workflows in engineering development, operations (DevOps/SRE),
and product growth. Your goal is to deliver measurable speedups in project execution, significantly reduce operational expenditure, and publish high-impact
research papers on enterprise agentic architectures.
We are looking for talented individuals to join us for an internship. Our internship program offers students hands-on experience, industry exposure, and
opportunities to apply their knowledge to real-world challenges while building a strong foundation for personal and professional growth.
Interns will gain practical experience, explore potential career paths, and participate in social events, learning programs, and development workshops alongside
industry professionals.
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so we encourage you to apply early. Please clearly state your availability in your resume, including your start
and end dates.
Responsibilities:
- Conduct cutting-edge research in self-improving agents, evolutionary optimization, and multi-agent systems to enable autonomous code generation, system
maintenance, and decision-making.
- Formulate novel research ideas, design rigorous methodologies, conduct experiments, and lead papers for top-tier AI/ML conferences.
- Investigate methodology for agentic self-evolution using proprietary, compliance-restricted datasets (USDS), solving challenges around data safety, memory
optimization, and long-horizon runtime stability.
- Develop robust evaluation frameworks to measure the concrete operational impact (e.g., runtime efficiency, task completion speedup, labor cost offloading) of
agentic solutions in engineering and growth environments.
Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications:
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
- Expertise in one or more of the following research domains: AI Agents & LLM Tool Use / Harness Engineering, Reinforcement Learning & Evolutionary Algorithms /
Genetic Programming, Code Generation, Automated Software Engineering, or Self-Improving Systems
- Strong implementation skills in Python, paired with system-level language proficiency (Rust, C++, or Go).
Preferred Qualifications:
- Currently pursuing a Master's Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
- Deep research exposure to iterative self-evolution paradigms (e.g., SGPO, recursive code improvement, execution-guided synthesis).
- Experience conducting research in restricted/internal data environments or handling compliance-sensitive execution loops.
- Demonstrated history of bridging high-impact theoretical research with rapid, functional open-source or production demos.
