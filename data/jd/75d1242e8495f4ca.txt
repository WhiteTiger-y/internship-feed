Learning begins in the classroom but there’s no better place to gain real-world experience than with Stantec. Interns are an integral part of our teams, working
to solve some of the world's most complex challenges.
As an intern, you’ll work alongside our experienced practitioners and with our clients and communities. This hands-on experience will equip you with practical
skills, build your network, and provide opportunities that set you up for success.
This Data Science, Machine Learning, and AI Intern will work on a range of projects focused on the implementation and advancement of research and digital tools
and technologies focused on the water, wastewater, biosolids and energy sectors. The intern will work closely with Water BOU’s data strategy manager, machine
learning leader, director of innovation and technology and other team members. The intern will contribute to the initiatives such as Water BOU’s Digital
Transformation and development of digital solutions to support project delivery. Depending on project needs, work may include exploratory data analysis (EDA),
statistical testing, machine learning, generative AI, data visualization, and the development of reproducible data workflows. The intern will report to an
Innovation Portfolio Manager in OI&T.
What You Will Do
- Clean, explore, and analyze structured and unstructured data from water and infrastructure projects.
- Build and evaluate statistical, machine learning, or AI prototypes using appropriate validation methods and clearly documented assumptions.
- Create clear visualizations, summaries, and demonstrations that communicate findings to both technical and nontechnical audiences.
- Contribute to reproducible workflows using Python, notebooks, version control, and well-organized documentation.
- Collaborate with ORIT team members and subject-matter experts to define problems, test ideas, and iterate through short proof-of-concept cycles.
- Research emerging data science, machine learning, generative AI, and low-code/no-code capabilities and assess their relevance to real business needs.
- Follow responsible AI, data privacy, security, and quality practices when working with project information.
Knowledge, Skills, And Abilities
- Strong analytical thinking and a practical approach to problem solving.
- Working knowledge of Python for data analysis or machine learning. Experience with common libraries such as pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, or
TensorFlow is helpful.
- Foundational understanding of statistics, data preparation, model evaluation, and responsible use of AI.
- Ability to explain technical work clearly through writing, presentations, visualizations, and well-documented code.
- Ability to manage assigned work, ask thoughtful questions, and communicate progress or blockers.
- Curiosity, willingness to experiment, and comfort learning unfamiliar tools or subject areas.
- Collaborative mindset and willingness to share ideas, receive feedback, and work with multidisciplinary teams.
- Familiarity with Git/GitHub or Azure DevOps, SQL, cloud analytics platforms, Microsoft Fabric, Azure, Power BI, or low-code/no-code tools is a plus, not a
requirement.
Education and/or Experience
- Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in data science, computer science, statistics, applied mathematics, engineering, environmental science, or
a related quantitative field.
- Coursework, research, personal projects, hackathons, or prior work experience involving data analysis, machine learning, AI, or software development.
- Experience with Python is required.
