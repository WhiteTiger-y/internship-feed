businesses and consumers around the world. We serve agriculture customers and consumers across the United States and around the world. Most of our employees are

Summary
CHS is looking for a Field Research Intern to join the Product Development team for the Summer of 2027. The Field Research Intern will gain experience across
all aspects of CHS research and development to ensure we are bringing the highest quality products to market. The individual will have the opportunity to spend
time with various technical specialists across Minnesota, North and South Dakota, Nebraska and Iowa, learning how a product concept is taken from an idea to
reality. The individual will develop strong agronomic knowledge and understanding of how a technical specialist forms a partnership of replicated research with
account managers, technical specialists, dealers, and growers.
Responsibilities
- Assist technical product specialist with day-to-day activities including: Coordinating replicated third party research trials to assess the efficacy of CHS
products including adjuvants, starter fertilizers, micronutrients, biologicals, inoculants, seed treatments, and fertilizer additives. As well as assist with
coordinating field scale side by side and strip trials.
- Visiting plots to collect data and visuals.
- Analyzing data and creating tools to support product sales.
- Help conduct small scale replicated research including trial setup, pesticide applications, treatment evaluations, trial maintenance & more.
- Learn features and benefits of all CHS products.
- Learn about CHS crop protection product development pipeline.
- Assist with product applications as needed.
- Travel mainly throughout South Dakota, but could be required to travel into North Dakota, Minnesota, Iowa and Nebraska; if opportunity to learn from other
technical specialists allows.
- Responsible for an individual project throughout the summer, culminating in a presentation delivered in a company or group meeting.
- Other duties as assigned
Minimum Qualifications (required)
- Enrolled in relevant coursework in 2 or 4 year higher education institution.
- Interest in agricultural business.
