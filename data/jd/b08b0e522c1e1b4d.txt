Job Description:
The Role and Impact
As an AI Software Engineering Graduate Intern, you'll play a pivotal role in designing, developing, and optimizing AI software solutions across domains such as
computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning. You'll contribute to implementing and fine-tuning models for enhanced performance and accuracy, while also
exploring techniques like model compression to improve efficiency. This internship provides an excellent opportunity to build technical skills and knowledge
through hands-on projects, directly supporting Intel's AI goals in a collaborative environment.
Business group
This position is part of Intel's Sales and Marketing Group (SMG), which focuses on connecting customers with innovative solutions that drive Intel's success in
global markets. SMG is committed to addressing customer challenges, fostering collaboration, and delivering technologies that shape the future of AI and
computing. The team offers an engaging and growth-oriented environment aligned with Intel's broader mission to advance cutting-edge technologies.
Key Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and optimize AI software solutions, including algorithms, frameworks, and architectures.
- Assist in implementing and fine-tuning deep learning models for improved accuracy and performance.
- Explore model compression techniques such as quantization and pruning to enhance system efficiency.
- Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to develop scalable AI solutions for system-level deployment.
- Conduct applied research to refine AI methodologies and support hardware-software integration.
- Document technical findings and contribute to knowledge-sharing initiatives within Intel.
Qualifications:
Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, with 0-1 years of hands-on experience in the domain, including demonstrated
skills gained through internships, academic projects, coursework, or hands-on training, OR a Master's degree in a relevant field with no prior professional
experience.
- Proficiency in Python or C++ for AI software development and debugging.
- Solid understanding of deep learning fundamentals, including large language models (LLMs).
- Familiarity with model compression techniques such as quantization and pruning.
- Knowledge of AI frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch.
Preferred Qualifications
- Strong problem-solving skills and self-motivation to tackle technical challenges.
- Passion for technological exploration, innovation, and practical engineering applications.
- Experience in fine-tuning AI models, inference optimization, or agent development is a plus.
