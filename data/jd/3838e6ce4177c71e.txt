In the role of Wetland Scientist-in-Training Intern, we'll count on you to:
- Obtain real-world experience on exciting wetland, ecological restoration, water resources, infrastructure, sustainability, resiliency and environmental
permitting projects
- Connect with recent college graduates and our company leaders through mentoring and Young Professionals Employee Network Group (ENG) and other Inclusion,
Diversity and Equity ENGs
- Assist project managers, scientists and environmental professionals with field investigations, data collection, analysis, and reporting in freshwater and
marine aquatic environments
- Apply standard environmental science and ecological assessment techniques and procedures
- Support environmental permitting and regulatory compliance activities in New Jersey and New York, including New York City
- Assist sustainability experts with gathering resiliency documentation
- Perform assignments under the direct supervision of an experienced environmental scientist or Project Manager
- Perform other duties as needed
Example Projects:
- Wetland and waterbody delineation
- Ecological and Water Quality Monitoring
- Vegetation and plant community assessments
- Hydrology and soil evaluations
- GPS/GIS field data collection and mapping
- Habitat assessments and ecological field investigations
- Wetland permitting and regulatory support
- Habitat mitigation and restoration planning and site monitoring
- Stream and riparian corridor assessments
- Preparation of field documentation, technical reports, and permit application materials
- Environmental support for water, transportation, energy, and other infrastructure projects

- Interest in developing wetland delineation and field identification skills
- Familiarity with GIS/GPS tools and environmental data collection is a plus

- Strong written and verbal communication skills
