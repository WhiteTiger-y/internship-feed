Who We Are
Neros is a defense technology company rebuilding America’s drone industrial base. We design and manufacture high-performance unmanned systems that are tested in
combat, iterated at startup speed, and built at massive scale. Our team culture is fast, hands-on, and obsessed with closing the gap between design and
deployment.
As drones transform the character of warfare, Neros is delivering the systems the West needs to compete on the modern battlefield and deter the adversaries of
democracy. We’re hiring engineers, operators, and builders who want to move fast, take on extreme ownership, and get capability into the hands of warfighters in
months, not years.
What You Will Be Doing
As an Autonomy Software Engineering Intern at Neros, you will be a key hands-on member of our autonomy team, supporting the design, development, and testing of
the software that flies our drones. Within our team we tackle a very diverse set of problems from perception, state estimation through guidance and navigation,
simulation and the system that measures whether our algorithms work on the actual drone. This role is perfect for a current student or early-career engineer who
is eager to learn and grow. By the end of the summer, you will have your own autonomy code running on a real drone, and you will get to watch it fly.
Responsibilities
- Write autonomy software in C++ and Python that runs on the drone
- Implement, test, and tune algorithms against recorded flight data and in closed loop simulation before they go on the real drone
- Help build the tooling that lets us validate new functionality, like evaluation on recorded logs and in simulation
- Take a feature from a design discussion through implementation, code review, simulation, and flight test, and see it fly
- Debug real failures on real data, tracing a bad flight back to the line of code or the bad assumption that caused it
- Work directly with senior engineers across perception, estimation, guidance, and platform to integrate your work into a single autonomy stack that runs on
every drone we build
You should have the following
- Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical Engineering, Aerospace, or a related field
- Experience in robotics, such as perception, state estimation, localization, tracking, guidance, navigation, planning, or control
- Strong programming ability in C++ and/or Python, and the judgment to write code that other engineers can read, review, and keep running
- Comfort working inside a large existing codebase using Git, and comfort in a Linux development environment
- A strong desire to learn, a coachable attitude, and sharp problem solving instincts
Nice to have
- Hands on experience with robots, drones, autonomous vehicles, or embedded compute
- Coursework or projects in perception, computer vision, tracking, SLAM or VIO, state estimation, control, planning, or machine learning
- Experience with a robotics middleware such as ROS or ROS2
- Familiarity with sensor data: cameras, IMUs, thermal and infrared imagery, calibration, and time synchronization across sensors
- Experience with numerical and data tooling in Python (NumPy, pandas, matplotlib) for analysis and test automation
