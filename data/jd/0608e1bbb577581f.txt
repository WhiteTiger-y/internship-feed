MITRE’s Autonomous and Intelligent Systems Department is an innovative team of engineers focused on the development of cutting-edge autonomous systems, robotics
capabilities, and AI solutions. The department has a new opportunity for a strong technical candidate to work with an innovative team of engineers focused on
developing Artificial Intelligence and/or Autonomous System solutions for sponsor needs. The right candidate will enjoy a fast-paced team environment and
working with groundbreaking technology. We are seeking candidates that can assist in building robots, artificial intelligence projects, and evaluating state-of-
the-art systems. The candidate for this position will work on challenging activities in direct support of sponsor programs and/or internal R&D supporting
MITRE’s AI and autonomy
efforts.
Job Responsibilities:
As a Robotics and Autonomous Systems Intern, you will collaborate with a multidisciplinary team of engineers and researchers to apply your academic knowledge
and gain hands-on experience in one or more of the following areas:
- Autonomous ground, air, surface, or underwater vehicles
- Robot perception and sensor fusion (e.g., cameras, LiDAR, radar, IMU, GPS)
- Motion planning, navigation, and control for robotic platforms
- Real-time and embedded software for robotics
- Robotics-focused Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML)
- Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) and state estimation
- Human–Robot Interaction (HRI) and operator interfaces
- Multi-agent and swarm robotics
- Modeling, simulation, and digital twins for robotic systems
- Safety, reliability, and verification of autonomous systems
- Cybersecurity for robotic and autonomous platforms
- Field testing, experimentation, and data collection with robotic systems
- Robotics hardware and software integration
Basic Qualifications:
- Must be a full-time student pursuing an accredited degree program in Robotics, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics,
Aerospace Engineering, or a closely related discipline.
- Must continue to be enrolled the semester following the internship.
- Foundational knowledge of robotics and/or autonomous systems concepts such as robotic architectures, kinematics, dynamics, control, perception, or planning,
and familiarity with relevant software tools (e.g., C/C++, Python, ROS, MATLAB/Simulink, or similar).
- Must have sustained excellence in academic performance.
- Demonstrated interest in serving the public or advancing societal missions through previous work experience, volunteer activities, or other relevant
engagements—especially in areas involving robotics or autonomous systems
Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience applying robotics or autonomous systems coursework in an internship, research lab, project team (e.g., robotics club, competition team), or
independent project.
- Proficiency with robotics software frameworks and tools (e.g., ROS/ROS2, Gazebo or other simulators, embedded development environments, version control such
as Git).
- Experience with one or more of the following:
- Robot perception and computer vision
- Path planning, navigation, and control algorithms
- Real-time and embedded systems for robotics
- Machine learning for perception or decision-making in autonomous systems
- Modeling, simulation, and hardware-in-the-loop testing
