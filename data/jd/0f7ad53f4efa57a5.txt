Responsibilities
We are seeking a 2027 summer intern to support our Learning Management and Support Services (LMSS) contract with our Intelligence Community client. At Noblis,
our LMSS contract has numerous teams that support the educational and professional development for our client’s workforce. In this project, the intern will join
our Course Assessments and Data Analytics and Visualization teams to identify, develop, and implement data science strategies for a variety of different teams,
applications, and types of data. These tools will be leveraged together to achieve strategic goals and objectives and to align innovation efforts with the
organization’s mission.
You’ll leave this internship with:
- Skills in client consultation and translating customer data requests into actionable insights
- Hands-on experience with dashboard lifecycle from intake to delivery
- An impactful final project that is used in the workforce, not just assigned as busy-work.
- Mentorship from seasoned learning and development professionals.
- Leadership, communication, and public speaking experience.
- An extensive network of Noblis’ professionals and 2027 intern cohort.
Responsibilities:
- Learn the interdependence of cross-functional teams and implement data strategy using data science skills.
- Use data software tools to design, implement, and evaluate end-of-course and learning transfer assessments.
- Support workforce training and education data-related projects by identifying user needs, collecting data, performing data analysis & interpretation to
provide customer insights.
- Report insights and help clients to make decisions and find improvement opportunities.
Required Qualifications
- U.S. Citizenship
- TS/SCI clearance and an ability to obtain a CI polygraph
- GPA of 3.3 or higher
- Problem solving, critical thinking, and organizational skills
- Ability to be a self-starter with demonstrated customer support and collaboration expertise
- Ability to work in a team-based environment
- Experience with basic coding and data visualization skills such as: Python, R, JavaScript, Tableau
- Experience with databases and management tools such as: SQL and Access
Desired Qualifications
- Pursuing an undergraduate or graduate degree in a STEM field
- Experience with large datasets
- Familiarity with training and experience leveraging AI models including large language models, agent-based systems, and retrieval augmented generation (RAG)
- Familiarity with Agile development workflows
- People skills that lend themselves to emotional intelligence and the human centered design process to exemplify the customer experience
- Consulting experience
- Briefing/Public Speaking Skills
