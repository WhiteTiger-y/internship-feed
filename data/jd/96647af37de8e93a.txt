What You'll Do
In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise.
We are seeking a motivated graduate intern with a strong interest in Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science to join our technology team. In
this role, you will support the development of applied AI solutions that address real business needs while gaining hands-on experience with enterprise-scale
technology. The ideal candidate is curious, collaborative, and eager to apply technical skills to build scalable applications with measurable impact.
Key Responsibilities
Depending on your skills and interests, you may work on:
- Ship real AI features — Python code that goes into existing applications and platforms
- Build agentic systems — agent servers, prompt management, skill workflows, MCP tools, and RAG knowledge bases.
- Benchmark the latest models — evaluate open-source and commercial LLMs and shape what we adopt.
- Turn ideas into products — partner with engineering and business teams on proof-of-concepts that graduate into production.
- Automate manual workflows — replace them with AI and document the playbooks.
- Present your results — to technical leaders and business stakeholders.

- Built with LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, vector databases, orchestration frameworks, or agent workflows.
- Familiar with data pipelines and downstream integrations.
- Exposure to Python data tools, APIs, SQL/NoSQL, cloud services, or workflow automation.
- Familiar with CI/CD and deployment automation.
