MISO 2027 SUMMER INTERNSHIP PROGRAM
Ready to power up your future?
Are you pursuing a graduate level degree in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science/Technology, Computer Engineering, Data Science, or a related field and
searching for a career-igniting summer internship in 2027 ? Do you want to see your ideas make a real impact—helping MISO keep the lights on for 45 million
people by driving innovations that boost system reliability, resilience, and performance, and tackling some of the most complex technical challenges in the
energy industry?
If that sparks your interest, keep reading…
What You’ll Do As a MISO Intern:
- Work on real AI and software engineering projects that help MISO explore and build new technologies — from early prototypes to production‑ready tools using
Python, GitHub, cloud platforms, and modern AI frameworks.
- Support AI‑enabled workflows such as data pipelines, automation, LLM/RAG agents, observability dashboards, or pilot features that test new ideas quickly and
safely.
- Collaborate closely with engineers, product teams, and emerging‑tech specialists to refine requirements, test concepts, evaluate performance, and improve
system reliability.
- Attend community service events, intern socials, networking activities, and development programming to help you grow your confidence, build relationships, and
understand MISO’s broader mission.
- Wrap up the summer with a final presentation summarizing your project work, recommendations, and areas of impact.
What We Offer:
- Competitive pay plus housing assistance for qualified students.
- A collaborative, mission-driven culture where your contributions matter.
- An internship that blends technical challenge, professional development, and fun.
- An average of 40 hours per week on site in our Carmel office.
- Potential to extend internship to part-time remote work during the school year.
We Are Looking For Students Who…
- Are pursuing Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science/Technology, Computer Engineering, or Data Science at the undergraduate or graduate level.
- Have skills in Python, GitHub, GitHub Copilot, generative AI, LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, agent frameworks, and automation principles.
- Understand or are excited to learn cloud infrastructure concepts, networking basics, observability, and security fundamentals.
- Are curious, creative, collaborative learners who enjoy experimenting, solving problems, and exploring new technologies.
- Communicate well, ask thoughtful questions, and thrive in hands‑on technical environments.
To be eligible for the program, you must be available to start Wednesday, June 2 nd , 2027 and continue through Friday, August 13 th , 2027. No VISA sponsorship
