We are currently looking for an AI Intern to join our team in Dallas, TX or Franklin, TN. Our team manages technology systems and applications supporting
Nissan's Sales Finance companies across the United States and Canada. We are responsible for delivery and operations across core lending systems while also
supporting customer and dealer systems, data quality initiatives, and artificial intelligence solutions. This internship opportunity will be within the Data
Quality & AI team, where the selected candidate will develop predictive AI models to support NMAC portfolio risk analysis. The intern will work closely with the
NMAC Risk team and may have the opportunity to present their solutions and findings to senior leaders, including the VP of Operations and President.
A Day in the Life:
- Develop predictive AI and machine learning models to support portfolio risk analysis and business decision-making.
- Build Early Warning Vintage Monitoring models to improve loss forecasting and proactive risk detection.
- Develop forecasting and planning models to enhance lifetime loss projections and net credit loss forecasts.
- Perform data quality assessments, feature engineering, and analysis of large datasets to identify meaningful risk indicators.
- Partner with Risk, Operations, and Technology teams to translate portfolio behavior into actionable model features and business insights.
- Present project outcomes, recommendations, and analytical findings to business stakeholders and leadership.
