Your Role And Responsibilities
As a Quantum Hardware EDA & Circuit Design Intern at IBM Quantum, you will contribute to the design, simulation, and optimization of circuits and design
workflows that support scalable quantum computing systems. Projects may span microwave and RF circuit design, electromagnetic simulation, circuit modeling,
automation, and electronic design automation (EDA) methods, including the application of AI-enabled methods for circuit and system design, simulation,
optimization, verification, and automation. You will collaborate with circuit designers, EDA developers, and researchers to develop practical tools and design
approaches for qubit control, readout, packaging, and related quantum hardware.
