Overview:
If you're looking for the stability of a profitable, growing company with the entrepreneurial spirit of a startup, we’re hiring. SageSure, a leader in
catastrophe-exposed property insurance, is seeking an Intern to join the Rising Pioneer Program as part of our Data Science team.
Over the course of this 10-week program, interns will gain tangible, hands-on experience and insight into our business operations. After 3 days of orientation
and training, you’ll join our Data Science team, where you’ll be assigned projects with unlimited opportunity for challenges and achievement. We have, very
often, considered our interns for post-graduation roles within the company.
What you’d be doing:
- Learn and network with employees, leaders, and other interns during an on-the-job experience.
- Potential to earn college credit.
- Provide research and analysis support on ad-hoc projects.
- Fulfill tasks and project assignments set out by team leaders.
- Receive coaching, mentoring, and feedback from knowledgeable SageSure professionals.
- Support the development, testing, and evaluation of data science models and analytical solutions.
- Assist with collecting, cleaning, transforming, and analyzing structured and unstructured data.
- Explore datasets to identify trends, patterns, relationships, and opportunities that can inform business decisions.
- Apply statistical and analytical techniques to support business questions and data-driven problem solving.
- Partner with Data Scientists, Data Engineers, and business stakeholders to understand project objectives and translate business needs into analytical
approaches.
- Assist with model validation, performance evaluation, and documentation of analytical methods and results.
- Develop visualizations, summaries, and presentations that clearly communicate findings to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Support experimentation and testing to evaluate model performance, business impact, or potential improvements.
- Participate in team meetings, project discussions, and technical reviews.
- Learn and apply data science best practices related to reproducibility, documentation, testing, and responsible use of data.
We’re looking for someone who has:
- Is currently enrolled in a relevant degree program with a focus on Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, or a
related quantitative field, with an anticipated graduation date in 2028.
- Has foundational experience working with data through coursework, academic projects, research, or prior work experience.
- Has experience with Python, R, SQL, or another programming or analytical language.
- Demonstrates strong analytical, quantitative, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills.
- Has a basic understanding of statistics, data analysis, or machine learning concepts.
- Is comfortable working with data and interpreting analytical results.
- Has strong written and verbal communication skills and can explain technical concepts in a clear and understandable way.
- Is detail-oriented, curious, and willing to explore different approaches to solving problems.
- Is comfortable multitasking and eager to learn new tools, technologies, and analytical techniques.
- Has the ability to work independently as well as collaboratively as a team member.
- Is authorized to work in the United States.
Highly preferred candidates also have:
- Prior internship, research, technical project, or relevant professional experience.
- GPA of 3.0 or higher.
- Experience using Python for data analysis, modeling, or machine learning.
- Familiarity with SQL and relational databases.
- Exposure to machine learning techniques, statistical modeling, or predictive analytics.
- Experience with data visualization tools or libraries.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms, Databricks, or other modern data and analytics technologies.
- Experience presenting analytical findings or recommendations to non-technical audiences.

About SageSure:
Named among the Best Places to Work in Insurance by Business Insurance for four years in a row (2020-2023), SageSure is one of the largest managing general
underwriters (MGU) focused on catastrophe-exposed property in the US. Since its founding in 2009, SageSure has experienced exceptional growth while generating
underwriting profits for carrier partners through hurricanes, wildfires, and hail. Available in 16 states, SageSure offers more than 110 home, flood, and
commercial products on behalf of its highly rated carrier partners. Today, SageSure manages more than $3.2 billion of inforce premium and helps protect more
than 970,000 policyholders.
We have more than 1,000 employees in a distributed workforce environment across 12 offices—Fairfield, CA; Mountain View, CA; Cheshire, CT; Jacksonville, FL;
Tallahassee, FL; Tampa, FL; Chicago, IL; Jersey City, NJ; Marlton, NJ; Cincinnati, OH; Houston, TX; Sheboygan, WI—who are tackling the industry’s toughest
challenges.
