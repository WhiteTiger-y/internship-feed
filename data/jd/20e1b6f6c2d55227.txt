This position resides under The Resource Group, an end-to-end supply chain partner to healthcare organizations across the United States, wholly-owned by
Ascension. At The Resource Group, we build strong relationships with provider teams, working alongside them to improve supply chain operations and lower non-
payroll expenses, all in support of delivering quality patient care. Responsibilities for this role can be found below:
Support financial analysis and project activities by assisting with reporting, performance measurement, and data analysis to identify opportunities for facility
supply expense improvement and value realization.
Develop and maintain analytical tools, dashboards, and reports using Google Workspace, Microsoft Excel, Tableau, and other technologies to support
implementation activities, performance tracking, and decision making.
Analyze financial, operational, and performance data to identify trends, interpret results, and contribute data driven recommendations that support business and
contract decisions.
Collaborate with project teams and stakeholders to support contract implementation activities, project coordination, and research efforts while gaining an
understanding of contract impact and participant needs.
Assist with the development of internal controls, validation of supply chain rebates, and other analytical activities that promote data accuracy, compliance,
and operational effectiveness.
Prepare clear, accurate, and professional presentations, reports, and project documentation while contributing to process improvements, knowledge sharing, and
special projects.
