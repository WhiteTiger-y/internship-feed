The Position
2027 Summer Intern - Service Data and Analytics
Department Summary
The Service Data and Analytics team within the Roche Support Network (RSN) powers operational excellence and customer support across North America. We are a
specialized, agile product squad uniting Data Scientists, an AI Full-Stack Engineer, and Business Systems Analysts to evolve our organization from traditional
reporting to predictive, driver-based software tools. Our squad builds modern web applications, scalable data pipelines, and machine learning models that
analyze service records and operational workflows. By turning complex diagnostic service data into real-time tools, we help ensure laboratory instruments
nationwide remain running and reliable—directly supporting patient care when it matters most.
This internship position is located in Indianapolis, IN, On-Site.
The Opportunity
- Data Exploration & Feature Engineering: Analyze large-scale field service, instrument performance, and work order datasets to uncover operational trends and
root-cause drivers
- Model & Metric Prototyping: Partner with Data Scientists to prototype statistical analyses, predictive models, or driver-based algorithms using Python
- Pipeline & Application Support: Assist in building data transformation pipelines (ETL) and connecting analytics endpoints to our modern cloud-hosted web
applications in AWS
- Cross-Functional Scoping: Collaborate closely with our Business Systems Analyst to understand field service workflows and translate business rules into
technical requirements
- Executive Showcase: Own an end-to-end project deliverable and present your findings, software features, and measurable business impact to senior leadership
Program Highlights
- Intensive 12-weeks, full-time (40 hours per week) paid internship
- Program start dates are in May/June (Summer).
- A stipend, based on location, will be provided to help alleviate costs associated with the internship.
- Ownership of challenging and impactful business-critical projects
- Work with some of the most talented people in the biotechnology industry
Who You Are (Required)
Required Education: You meet one of the following criteria:
- Must be pursuing a Bachelor's Degree (enrolled student)
- Must be pursuing a Master's Degree (enrolled student)
Required Majors: Data Science, Computer Science, Business Analytics, Statistics, Informatics, Industrial Engineering, Applied Mathematics, or a related
quantitative STEM discipline.
Required Skills
- Academic or hands-on experience using Python and/or SQL for data extraction, manipulation, and analysis
- Foundational understanding of statistical concepts, exploratory data analysis, or machine learning principles
- Demonstrated problem-solving ability with a structured, analytical mindset
- Ability to work on-site at our Indianapolis, IN campus for the duration of the 12-week program
Preferred Knowledge, Skills, And Qualifications
- Excellent communication, collaboration, and interpersonal skills
- Complements our culture and the standards that guide our daily behavior & decisions: Integrity, Courage, and Passion
- Experience with version control systems
- Familiarity with modern data and cloud technologies
- Exposure to data visualization tools or lightweight application frameworks
- Eagerness to learn in an Agile team environment and a genuine interest in healthcare and biotechnology
