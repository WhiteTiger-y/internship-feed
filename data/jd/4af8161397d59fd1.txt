About Cubist
Cubist Systematic Strategies, an affiliate of Point72, is one of the world’s premier investment firms. The firm deploys systematic, computer-driven trading
strategies across multiple liquid asset classes, including equities, futures, and foreign exchange. The core of our effort is rigorous research into a wide
range of market anomalies, fueled by our unparalleled access to a wide range of publicly available data sources.
