About Amplify
Amplify helps teachers bring delight and rigor to students every day. We have become a leader in K–12 literacy, biliteracy, math, and science by building
inspiring teaching and learning experiences based on research. The Amplify Classroom platform combines curriculum, assessment, and supplemental learning into
one coherent high-quality instructional system. A pioneer in education since 2000, Amplify has developed deep relationships in states and districts by
partnering with educators to drive implementation quality and improved outcomes. Today, Amplify serves more than 18 million students and teachers across all 50
states and on six continents. For more information, visit Amplify.com .
Create an impact by shaping the world of education!
Amplify is seeking a detail-oriented and analytically driven Data Analytics Intern to join our team this summer and help us build data-informed experiences for
the educators, students, and families who use our innovative educational products. This paid, remote 10-week internship offers the chance to make a real impact
on products used by millions of students and teachers.
If you're comfortable with data analysis, visualization, and structured reporting, and you're genuinely curious about how data drives K–12 learning outcomes,
this could be a perfect fit for you. The ideal candidates will love digging into complex datasets, bring a collaborative, ask-questions-first energy to their
work, and feel excited to help build data infrastructure and reporting capabilities that move the needle for K–12 learners.
Though remote, interns will join a 2-day in-person experience at Amplify HQ in Brooklyn, NY. Throughout the program, interns will engage with Amplify's culture
via product demos, team-building activities, and engagement initiatives, plus grow through a virtual speaker series featuring Amplify executives, additional
product demos, and dedicated mentorship.
While our summer intern roles are remote positions, interns must be authorized to work in the US, reside in the US for the internship's duration, and be
available full-time for the entire program, June 7 – August 13, 2027.
