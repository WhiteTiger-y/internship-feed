Job Description
The Future Talent Program within our Research & Development Division Data, AI, and Genome Sciences (DAGS) Department is offering internships that last 10-12
weeks. We have 1 open position on the AI/ML Data Science, In Silico Perturbation Team. Candidates can apply for the opportunity to be involved in one or more of
the projects listed below. The projects are designed to enable students to further develop their skills and to evaluate if we are the right company for their
long-term goals. We are seeking highly motivated students with computational skillsets.
Location: Cambridge, MA
Term: Full-time, 10-12 weeks
AI/ML Data Science
- Develop statistical and computational methods/pipelines to utilize, analyze, interpret, and validate perturb-seq datasets.
- Develop approaches to assess, interpret, and validate in silico perturbation predictions from single-cell foundation models.
Required Education And Skills
- Must be currently enrolled in a PhD degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, or related technical field.
- Must be available to work full-time for 10-12 weeks beginning in May or June of 2027.
- Demonstrated experience with single-cell omics data (e.g., scRNA-seq, perturb-seq).
- Excellent software design and development skills and strong proficiency in Python.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work collaboratively in a multi-disciplinary team.
Preferred Skills And Experience
- Motivated to impact biological projects with independent project leadership experience.
- Experience with biological foundation models (e.g., single-cell foundation models).
- Experience with advanced statistics and/or Bayesian methods.
- Proficiency in modern ML frameworks (e.g., PyTorch) and experience implementing scalable solutions for complex tasks.
- Publications in journals or conferences.
Please note that this position may be closed before the posted end date or may remain open longer, at the discretion of the company.
