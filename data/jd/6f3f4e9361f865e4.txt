About Lucid
At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-
defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose
between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that
have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.
We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing
facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s
possible and shape the future of mobility.
The Vehicle Infotainment team develops applied AI and machine learning solutions for intelligent vehicle features and engineering applications. Our work spans
computer vision, machine learning, generative AI, large language models, AI-powered engineering tools, and AI model deployment on embedded automotive platforms.
As an AI/ML intern, you will work alongside experienced engineers and contribute to a real-world project using production-relevant data, tools, and development
workflows. You will gain hands-on experience across the AI development lifecycle, including data preparation, model development, training, evaluation,
optimization, integration, and validation.
Role And Responsibilities
- Based on your skills and interests, you will be matched to projects in computer vision, machine learning, generative AI and LLM applications, or edge AI.
- Prepare datasets and develop, train, fine-tune, and evaluate AI/ML models.
- Build Python-based AI tools, prototypes, and development workflows.
- Improve model performance and support integration and validation in automotive or embedded environments.
- Collaborate with engineers, document results, and present project outcomes.
Must Have Skills
- Proficiency in Python and familiarity with libraries such as NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, or OpenCV.
- Understanding machine learning fundamentals, data preparation, model training, and evaluation.
- Experience with at least one deep learning framework, such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Familiarity with Git and collaborative software development practices.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills.
Nice To Have Skills
- Experience with computer vision tasks such as object detection, classification, segmentation, or tracking.
- Experience with LLMs, prompt engineering, embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation, or AI agents.
- Familiarity with Hugging Face, LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar AI frameworks.
- Experience with C++, ONNX, Docker, model optimization, quantization, or edge AI deployment.
- Exposure to embedded systems, automotive software, or simulation tools such as CARLA.
Education
- Field(s) of study: Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Statistics, Applied Mathematics, or related fields
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree program at an accredited university
- Proof of enrollment in current or into next program (MS, PhD)
