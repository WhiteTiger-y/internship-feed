Position Purpose
The Artificial Intelligence (AI) Engineering Co-Op/Intern assists in the design, development, testing, and deployment of AI and machine learning solutions for
engineering, manufacturing, and business applications. This role collaborates with technical and business teams to analyze data, support AI initiatives,
evaluate emerging technologies, and contribute to the delivery of innovative, data-driven business solutions while gaining hands-on industry experience.
Essential Functions
- Assist in the development, testing, and deployment of AI and machine learning models and applications.
- Assist with prototyping, evaluating, and deploying intelligent applications that improve engineering, manufacturing, and business processes.
- Collect, clean, organize, and analyze data to support AI initiatives.
- Collaborate with engineers, software developers, manufacturing teams, and business teams to identify and implement AI-driven solutions.
- Support the integration of AI technologies into existing systems, applications, and business processes.
- Conduct research on emerging AI, machine learning, and automation technologies.
- Evaluate model performance and assist with troubleshooting, optimization, and continuous improvement efforts.
- Develop and maintain technical documentation, reports, and user guides.
- Participate in software development activities, including coding, debugging, testing, and code reviews.
- Assist with data visualization, reporting, and presentation of findings to stakeholders.
- Adhere to company policies, cybersecurity standards, data privacy requirements, and ethical AI practices.
- Perform other duties as assigned.
Education and Training
- Must be a current enrolled student or recent graduate in a computer science, data science, engineering, or a related technical program.
- Must have a minimum grade point average (GPA) of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
Minimum Qualifications
- Exposure to machine learning, AI, software development, and/or data analytics concepts.
- Experience with programming languages.
- Understanding of software development principles, data structures, and algorithms.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills.
- Effective written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment.
- Strong organizational skills with attention to detail.
- Eagerness to learn new technologies and adapt to evolving priorities.
