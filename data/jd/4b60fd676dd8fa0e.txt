Who We Are
Aptima is a technological leader in the national security industry. Our mission is to drive the future of national security by engineering scalable solutions
that fuse technological innovation with human potential to transform how individuals and teams train, develop, and perform in mission-critical environments.
Our culture is rooted in our core values, which have evolved over time and our employees have embraced: Integrity, Ingenuity, Excellence, Respect, Engagement,
Teamwork. At our core, Aptima researches, develops, and innovates within an area that engineering firms largely ignore, the human component. To impact the world
in meaningful ways, you must bring those innovations to light, and that is precisely what we do.
How You’ll Make An Impact
As a Summer AI Engineer Intern at Aptima, you will contribute to the development of machine learning solutions by supporting data preparation, model training,
and performance evaluation. You’ll gain hands-on experience applying cutting-edge AI tools and techniques to address real-world challenges while collaborating
with experienced engineers and researchers.
Key Responsibilities
- Researching the latest generation of AI models
- Obtaining and cleaning training data sets
- Developing feature encodings
- Coding and implementation of machine learning methods
- Analyzing and validating models with statistical techniques
- Developing interfaces for exploring and utilizing AI
Requirements
- The ability to obtain and maintain a Security Clearance
- B.A./B.S. degree in a relevant engineering discipline (e.g., Mathematics, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Data Science).
Current enrollment in an accredited graduate program working towards a Master's Degree or Ph.D. in a similar or related discipline is a plus, but not required
- 2 years of relevant experience (including undergraduate school) developing and applying AI/ML technologies
