Our Internship Program
Our Internship Program Our summer internship program lasts 10-12 weeks beginning in May/June and ending in August/September. We offer a fast-paced, flexible,
fun environment where you will have the opportunity to work on meaningful projects and face new challenges every day.
Our Company
At Teradata, we believe that people thrive when empowered with better information. Teradata Autonomous Knowledge Platform activates enterprise intelligence by
unifying data, knowledge and business context to achieve tangible outcomes. With Teradata, organizations can provide agents with full context for impact when it
matters. Our solution lets businesses connect and scale on premises, in the cloud, or through a hybrid approach. Teradata delivers real business value with AI.
What You'll Do
Are you a curious, motivated individual passionate about the next frontier of AI? Do you envision building intelligent systems that can reason, learn, and act
autonomously? Join us as an Agentic AI Intern and help shape the future of enterprise intelligence.
- Build Agentic Systems: Contribute to the design, implementation, and testing of components for robust AI agents and multi-agent orchestration.
- Leverage Vector Stores: Work with Teradata’s Enterprise Vector Store to develop intelligent retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines.
- Integrate LLMs: Explore how Large Language Models (LLMs) and generative AI models enable autonomous agent behavior.
- Work with Real-World Data: Gain experience processing large-scale, complex datasets within the Teradata ecosystem.
- Research & Prototype: Engage with the latest research in agentic AI, prompt engineering, and autonomous systems to prototype innovative ideas.
- Collaborate & Grow: Partner with a dynamic team of engineers and scientists, receive mentorship, and contribute meaningfully from day one.
- System Integration: Help integrate LLM-based agents with retrieval tools, structured/unstructured inputs, and downstream Teradata products.
Who You'll Work With
You’ll collaborate with experienced AI engineers and data scientists on cutting-edge projects that redefine how businesses harness data. This is a hands-on
opportunity to apply your academic knowledge and contribute to the development of intelligent agents that drive insights and automate complex workflows.
What Makes You a Qualified Candidate
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a Master’s or Ph. D. in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field—or have equivalent experience.
- Interested in intelligent agents, orchestration frameworks, or multi-step reasoning workflows.
- Curious about data, workflows, and multimodal reasoning.
- Familiar with LLM APIs (e. g. , OpenAI, Claude, Gemini) and agent toolkits (e. g. , AgentBuilder, AutoGen, LangGraph, CrewAI).
- Understanding of chain-of-thought reasoning, prompt tuning, or context window management.
- Knowledge of evaluation metrics for agent performance, latency, and reliability.
What You’ll Bring
- Passionate about innovation and advancing the state of AI.
