About Rivian
Rivian is on a mission to keep the world adventurous forever. This goes for the emissions-free Electric Adventure Vehicles we build, and the curious, courageous
souls we seek to attract.
As a company, we constantly challenge what’s possible, never simply accepting what has always been done. We reframe old problems, seek new solutions and operate
comfortably in areas that are unknown. Our backgrounds are diverse, but our team shares a love of the outdoors and a desire to protect it for future
generations.

Our Spring Co-Op program runs from Jan - Aug, 2027. In order to be considered for this role, you must be available to work full time (40 hours per week),
onsite, for the entire duration. If you are unable to work during the spring semester, please apply to our Summer Internship Program.
As a Machine Learning Research Intern on Rivian's Autonomy team, you will advance neural scene reconstruction and world modeling that turn real-world driving
data into high-fidelity, re-renderable 3D environments. This research directly strengthens how we develop, test, and validate the ML models behind the
autonomous driving system in Rivian's vehicles — raising both the speed and the safety confidence with which we ship autonomy to customers.
Responsibilities
- Research, prototype, and train 3D machine learning models for scene reconstruction, novel view synthesis, and neural rendering from multi-sensor driving data
(camera, lidar, radar, and vehicle pose).
- Investigate and extend modern approaches such as neural radiance fields, 3D Gaussian splatting, feed-forward reconstruction, depth and geometry estimation,
and generative/diffusion-based scene synthesis.
- Design quantitative and qualitative evaluation methodology for reconstruction and rendering quality, including photometric, geometric, and downstream task-
driven metrics.
- Scale training and inference pipelines to large real-world datasets using distributed, multi-GPU infrastructure.
- Collaborate with perception, simulation, and infrastructure teams to integrate research prototypes into our internal research platform.
- Present results regularly to the team, contribute to internal technical documentation, and deliver a final presentation summarizing your research
contribution.
Qualifications
- Must be currently pursuing a PhD degree at an accredited university.
- Actively pursuing a degree or one closely related in computer science, electrical engineering, statistics, or a closely related quantitative field.
- Research background in one or more of the following: 3D scene reconstruction, neural rendering / novel view synthesis, depth or geometry estimation, 3D scene
understanding, or generative modeling.
- Track record of first-author publications at top-tier machine learning or computer vision conferences and journals (e.g., CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML,
ICLR, SIGGRAPH, CoRL, RSS).
- Strong proficiency in Python and a modern deep learning framework (PyTorch preferred), with demonstrated ability to implement, train, and debug models
independently.
- Solid foundation in the mathematics underlying 3D vision: multi-view geometry, camera models, coordinate transforms, and 3D representations (point clouds,
meshes, voxels, implicit fields).
- Experience applying 3D reconstruction, neural rendering, or world modeling to autonomous driving, robotics, or other embodied domains.
- Experience with large-scale distributed training and multi-GPU/multi-node workflows.
- Familiarity with simulation, sensor simulation, or synthetic data generation for training and evaluating ML systems.
- Experience working with large multi-sensor datasets and the calibration, synchronization, and data-quality challenges they involve.
- Open-source contributions, released research code, or a strong public portfolio of 3D ML work.
