internal and regulatory standards. Build and maintain scripts to test model performance and automate monitoring using tools such as Python or R. Support data
quality monitoring and preparation within the Enterprise Data Lake (EDL). Collaborate with analytics, automation, and business stakeholders to support strategic

reporting tools. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a high attention to detail. Excellent verbal, written, and presentation skills. Ability to
