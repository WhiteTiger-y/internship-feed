Job Description NOTE: To ensure a fair and equitable recruitment process, our Foundation Staff and Board do not host preliminary conversations with applicants
outside of our standard interview process. Position: Evaluation & Learning Intern - Summer 2027 Reports to: Senior Evaluation Officer Location: Chicago, IL
(Hybrid) Duration: June 8 - August 11, 2027 Compensation: Paid Internship (Hourly rates will start at $22.00/hour) About the Foundation The John D. and
Catherine T. MacArthur Foundation is one of the nation’s largest independent foundations. The Foundation supports creative people, effective institutions, and
influential networks building a more just, verdant, and peaceful world. MacArthur invests in solving some of the world’s most pressing social challenges,
including advancing global climate solutions, promoting local justice reform in the U.S., revitalizing local news, expanding who creates, uses, and benefits
from artificial intelligence, and strengthening the well-being of Native communities. In addition to the MacArthur Fellows Program and the global 100&Change
competition, the Foundation continues its historic commitments to the role of journalism in a responsive democracy as well as the vitality of our headquarters
city, Chicago. The Foundation also maintains offices in Nigeria and India. About Evaluation and Learning The Evaluation and Learning department addresses
measurement, evaluation, and learning needs for the institution and its grantmaking programs. We do this in a variety of ways: conduct evaluations, often in
partnership with long-standing evaluation and learning partners; facilitate and support strategy design; analyze data to understand changes to outcomes,
landscapes, networks, and ecosystems; conduct sensemaking and meaning making of complex data; and perform data analysis and visualization of different data
types (quantitative, qualitative, and spatial). We support organizational change, emergent strategies, and effective grant craft. We believe that values-driven,
informed decision-making leads to useful insights, more effective stewardship of the Foundation’s resources, and meaningful results. We center learning and
create space for critical thinking and reflection. We prioritize relationships and seek to be responsive to what the programs and institutions need. We are
rigorous, flexible, and creative in evaluation design, data collection, analysis, and reporting. About the Internship The purpose of the intern in the
Evaluation and Learning team will be two-fold: (1) to meaningfully engage and contribute to various activities and projects to gain practical experience in
evaluation and learning; and two, to gain wide exposure to evaluation and learning activities, including different measurement and evaluation tools and
application of evaluation and learning tools in different sectors, including climate change, impact investing, news and journalism, and technology and AI. They
will also exercise flexibility in contributing to tasks that arise and smaller projects that will contribute to their professional development. This intern will
also have opportunities to shadow different staff members to understand various roles and responsibilities. Responsibilities may include: Conducting bespoke
research projects using AI or supporting the design and facilitation of emergent learning exercises. Practicing hands-on evaluative and learning tools Create
and conducting presentations, meeting with different internal and external partners, and practicing project management. Attend EL team meetings and relevant
Foundation events. Qualifications The ideal candidate is a student in an undergraduate or graduate program, possibly in a nonprofit management, social sciences,
public health, or public policy program. Must have interest in evaluation, learning, research, and data analysis and visualization. Completed coursework that
involved research, qualitative or quantitative data collection, or data analysis is strongly desired but not necessary. Must possess strong writing and
communication skills. This internship is based in Chicago, Illinois. Our work environment is hybrid with designated in-office days and virtual days. Interns
will be required to reside in the Chicagoland area for the duration of the program. Housing assistance for non-local students may be available and will be
discussed during the interview process. Application Process: The application deadline is MONDAY, NOVEMBER 30, 2026 AT 11:59 PM CST. Applicants should submit a
cover letter and resume. As a requirement, students “must be returning back to school” to be eligible. Students meeting the minimum requirements should apply
