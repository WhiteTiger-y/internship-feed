Position Overview
Novelis is one of the world leaders in aluminum recycling and rolling and a leading sustainable aluminum solutions provider. Driven by our purpose of shaping a

Responsibilities & Qualifications
Position Overview
The Global Automotive Technology team is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented co-op student to support automotive research, competitive benchmarking and data
analytics. This co-op will begin May 2027 and end December 2027. The student will organize vehicle and market information, develop analytical tools, identify
trends and communicate findings to technical and business stakeholders.
Responsibilities
- Research vehicle specifications, technologies, market trends and publicly available competitive information.
- Collect, organize and maintain structured automotive datasets from approved sources.
- Support vehicle benchmarking and lightweighting-related analyses across selected vehicle segments and systems.
- Develop Excel- or Power BI-based tools, dashboards and visualizations.
- Apply basic statistical and data-quality methods to identify trends, gaps and inconsistencies.
- Prepare concise updates, summaries and presentations for technical and business audiences.
- Collaborate with engineering, materials, sustainability and commercial team members.
Key Projects
- Automotive benchmarking database development and data-quality review.
- Assessment of vehicle features, specifications and market positioning.
- Lightweighting opportunity screening and decision-support tool development.
- Dashboard creation, data visualization and executive communication.
Minimum Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Data Science, Business Analytics
or a related field.
- Strong Excel and analytical skills; Power BI, Python, database or statistical-tool experience is a plus.
- Interest in automotive technology, vehicle design, sustainability, materials or product strategy.
- Ability to research technical information, work with large datasets and document sources carefully.
- Strong attention to detail, problem-solving ability and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaborate in a cross-functional environment.
