Company Description
Pilot Company is an industry-leading network of travel centers with more than 30,000 team members and over 750 retail and fueling locations in 44 states and six
Canadian provinces. Our energy and logistics division serves as a top supplier of fuel, employing one of the largest tanker fleets and providing critical
services to oil operations in our nation's busiest basins. Pilot Company supports a growing portfolio of brands with expertise in supply chain and retail
operations, logistics and transportation, technology and digital innovation, construction, maintenance, human resources, finance, sales and marketing.
Founded in 1958 by Jim A. Haslam II and currently led by CEO Adam Wright, our founding values, people-first culture and commitment to giving back remains true
to us today. Whether we are serving guests, a fellow team member, or a trucking company, we are dedicated to fueling people and keeping North America moving.

Job Description
Are you a curious and analytical student with a passion for using data to solve business problems? Pilot Company is seeking a Data Governance Intern to join our
team in Knoxville, Tennessee, for the summer of 2027. This is a unique opportunity to directly improve operational efficiency through analyzing data, driving
data quality visibility, and ensuring robust data lifecycle management.
In this role, you will be an integral part of our Data Governance team. Your work will involve ingesting and analyzing data to build dashboards and
presentations for business leaders. This is an ideal position for someone who is a collaborative team player and enjoys building with others to drive real
change.
What You’ll Do
Your responsibilities may include:
Partner with the business: Collaborate with business partners to gather requirements and understand their needs.
Drive Data Governance & Visualization: Map and develop end-to-end flows of data from core source systems into interactive visualizations and executive
presentations, translating complex process data into clear business insights.
Visualize & Monitor Data Quality: Build dashboards and visual metrics to monitor data accuracy, flag anomalies, and ensure business stakeholders can rely on
high-integrity data.
Data Lifecycle Management & Compliance: Assist in tracking and managing data across its entire lifecycle to ensure processes remain compliant with internal
governance frameworks and external regulatory standards.
Contribute to key initiatives: Help accelerate the team's delivery and assist with Pilot’s strategic initiatives by handling essential tasks.
Qualifications
What We’re Looking For
Education: A rising or graduating senior pursuing an undergraduate or master's degree in Analytics, Computer Science, or a related field.
Technical Skills: Proficiency in SQL and MS Office. Experience building out visualizations is also essential.
Soft Skills: A curious and collaborative team player who is open to hearing other perspectives and enjoys building solutions with others.
Qualities: We are looking for someone who is driven, reliable, authentic, and welcoming.
Additional Information
Why Pilot Company?
Our culture is built on four core virtues: Driven, Reliable, Authentic, and Welcoming. As an intern, you will see these values in action every day. You'll be
part of a team that is driven to deliver results, reliable in its commitment to accuracy, authentic in its communication, and welcoming to new ideas and
perspectives.
This is a full-time, in-person internship, June 7 – August 13, 2027. You’ll be working alongside our team in our Knoxville headquarters, where you’ll have the
opportunity to build meaningful relationships and experience the full scope of life at Pilot Company.
How to Apply
If you're ready to take on a challenging and rewarding internship with a leading company, please submit your application. The interview process will include a
screening with a recruiter, followed by 45-minute interviews with leaders of the Data Governance team. We look forward to hearing from you!
