AutoZone's Top 100 Internship Program is designed to provide students with challenging, real-world experience in a corporate retail environment.
What Our Top 100 Internship Program Offers
- 9-week paid, full-time summer internship (June 7 - August 6, 2027)
- Meaningful, hands-on work experience
- Cross-functional job shadowing opportunities
- Dedicated one-on-one mentorship
- Professional and personal development workshops
- Networking and community service events
You will work in a dynamic, team-oriented environment and assist with executing assigned projects. AutoZone is seeking a tech-savvy and motivated Data Science
Intern to join our team for the summer. In this role, you will work on analyzing large datasets, developing predictive models, and collaborating with cross-
functional teams to solve business challenges. Your contributions will play a key role in enhancing operations and customer experiences through data-driven
insights.
Throughout your internship, you will collaborate in teams and manage your own projects, working closely with business partners, analysts, and technology teams.
This hands-on experience will ensure your success and provide you with tangible results by the end of your internship.
- Applicants must be rising juniors, seniors, or graduate students enrolled in an accredited college or university during the Summer 2027 internship program
(June 7 - August 6, 2027). Students who graduate before June 7, 2027, are not eligible for the program.
- Must be able to reside in the Memphis area for the duration of the internship. Relocation assistance is available for eligible non-local students. Non-local
students are defined as those who do not have available family housing in the Memphis area and whose college or university is not located in Memphis.
- Applicants should apply to no more than two internship roles that best align with their interests, skills, and experience. If you are uncertain which
department is the best fit, please use this general application.
- Applications close on Friday, December 4, 2026.
Majors We Are Searching For
- Data Science
- Computer Science
- Statistics
- You should be educated in technical languages like Python, R, SQL, Java, Tableau and Alteryx
