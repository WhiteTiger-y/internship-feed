Chicago, IL (Hybrid) | 2027 Internship | Summer 2027 | 40hours/week
Reports to: Manager, BI & AI Solutions
About The Role
Camping World is using AI across the customer experience and business operations, and the BI & AI Solutions team builds much of it. As a 2027 AI Engineering
Intern, you will work with our AI engineers to build and ship AI agents, full stack web applications, and machine learning services on our Azure platform. Each
project will be scoped with clear goals, and a senior engineer will review your work. Our goal is for you to take at least one feature from development to
production before your internship ends. We use AI coding tools every day, and we will show you how to use them well and how to check their output.
What You'll Do
- Features and fixes for our internal web applications
- AI agents built on enterprise AI platforms, along with the tools and integrations they use
- APIs and backend services that connect AI features to our web frontends
- Data integrations with Snowflake and other databases that give agents and applications the data they need
- Small containerized services deployed to Kubernetes on Azure through our standard pipelines
- A final project that you will demo to the team and to leadership
Key Responsibilities
- Build scoped features and agent capabilities from a written spec, with a senior engineer reviewing your design and code
- Write Python and JavaScript/TypeScript for the frontend, backend, and data layers of our internal web applications
- Build LLM integrations on enterprise AI platforms, including prompts, tool use, and function calling
- Write SQL against Snowflake and other databases to build and test the data your features depend on
- Package your services with Docker and deploy them to Kubernetes on Azure through our CI/CD pipelines
- Write automated tests and follow the team's security and production standards, such as keeping secrets out of code, validating inputs, and using secure
authentication
- Monitor your services with our observability tools after they go live and help fix the issues you find
- Attend code reviews, standups, and sprint planning with the rest of the team
- Document your work so the team can support it after your internship ends
What You'll Learn
- How an AI feature moves from prototype to production at a large company, including code review, testing, deployment, and monitoring
- How to design AI agents and measure whether they are working
- How to use AI coding tools productively and review the code they produce
- How containers, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and API gateways work together on a production platform
- How to scope your own work, give status updates, and present results to technical and business audiences
Required
What You'll Need to Have for the Role:
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field
- Working knowledge of Python or JavaScript/TypeScript from coursework, projects, or prior internships
- Basic understanding of software engineering fundamentals such as Git, testing, and how APIs work
- Some experience with a major LLM platform, even from a class or personal project
- Familiarity with SQL and relational databases
- Comfort using AI coding tools and checking the code they produce
- Open to feedback and comfortable asking questions
Preferred
- Coursework or project experience building a web application with React or a similar framework
- Exposure to Docker, Kubernetes, or a cloud platform (Azure preferred)
- Familiarity with AI agent frameworks or MCP servers
- Exposure to RAG, embeddings, or vector search
- Exposure to Snowflake or another cloud data warehouse
- Awareness of common security issues such as exposed secrets, injection risks, and insecure dependencies
- A GitHub profile or portfolio with projects you built yourself
Education
Undergraduate or graduate student in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field, or a recent graduate. Class projects, hackathons, open source
work, and prior internships all count, and we give a strong portfolio a lot of weight. No professional experience required.
How You'll Work
You will be part of a small team within BI & AI Solutions and work closely with our AI engineers, data architects, and the business teams that use what we
build. Your projects will be scoped at the start with clear goals, and a senior engineer will meet with you regularly and review your code. You will own your
project and be expected to move it forward, and you can ask for help at any time. Hybrid out of Chicago, 3 days per week.
Why This Role
Camping World is a $6B+ retailer with 180+ locations, a large customer base, and years of operational data. Our interns work on projects that ship. You will
write code that people across the company use, work on the same stack as our full time engineers, and finish your internship with production work you can point
to.
