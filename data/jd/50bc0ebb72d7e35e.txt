About the Role: The Automation & AI Solutions Intern works directly with technology partners and business leaders to design, build and deploy automation and
artificial intelligence solutions used in live deal workflows. This is a hands-on engineering role focused on delivering production-ready tools that improve
efficiency, decision-making and execution across the investment banking deal lifecycle. The intern will be embedded within Baird’s Global Investment Banking
Technology Solutions team and will partner closely with deal teams and functional groups to identify manual, repetitive processes and translate them into
scalable automation and AI-driven solutions. This is a hybrid internship, working Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday in our downtown Milwaukee, WI office and
Monday and Friday remote. The intern must be able to work full time during the summer, 37.5-40 hours per week, and work part time during the school semesters,
15-20 hours per week. We are seeking a local student who can start in June 2027. The Impact You’ll Make: Identify and automate manual processes across the
Global Investment Banking deal lifecycle using Power Automate and Power Apps Design and build end-to-end workflow solutions that integrate data from systems
such as Salesforce, SQL, Snowflake, SharePoint and Excel Prototype, test, and deliver AI-enabled use cases using tools such as Microsoft Copilot, Copilot
Studio, ChatGPT, Claude and Rogo Build custom Copilot agents and AI-driven tools to support research, document synthesis, data extraction and decision support
Actively participate in discussions with business and technology stakeholders and present delivered solutions and outcomes Produce clear technical documentation
to ensure long-term maintainability and scalability of delivered solutions What You’ll Bring to Baird: Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data
Science, Information Systems or a related field Anticipated graduation date of May 2028 or later Proficiency in at least one programming language (Python
preferred) Working knowledge of SQL and relational data concepts Curiosity and enthusiasm for automation and artificial intelligence, with hands-on
experimentation using tools such as Power Automate, Copilot, or large language models Ability to clearly communicate technical concepts to non-technical
stakeholders Self-starter mindset with the ability to take ownership, prioritize effectively, and work independently in a fast-paced environment Strong problem-
