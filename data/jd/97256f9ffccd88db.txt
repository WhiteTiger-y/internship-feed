About Our Group
You will join the HAMR Media Mechanical R&D group at Seagate’s Recording Media Research Center in Fremont, CA, where we develop advanced media interfaces,
coating technologies, characterization methods, reliability testing capabilities, and AI/ML solutions for HAMR magnetic recording media. Our team collaborates
closely with sputter process, recording physics, equipment engineering, materials science lab, drive design and recording head design teams, and manufacturing
teams across Seagate’s global organization. Together, we develop next-generation, high-capacity storage technologies for AI, cloud computing, edge computing,
and other emerging applications.
About The Role - You Will
You will work closely with R&D engineers to apply data science, machine learning, and generative AI to magnetic recording media R&D. You will contribute to one
or more projects that improve understanding of media performance and failure mechanisms, make technical knowledge easier to access, and improve R&D efficiency.
Responsibilities Include
- Develop reusable data analysis workflows and interactive dashboards that integrate design, process, manufacturing, and reliability data to identify
performance drivers and support engineering decisions.
- Work with domain experts to develop and evaluate predictive models for media performance and reliability, with attention to data quality, appropriate
validation, and interpretation of results.
- Prototype and evaluate GenAI applications, such as technical knowledge bases and agentic workflows, to support knowledge retrieval, research synthesis, and
engineering analysis.
