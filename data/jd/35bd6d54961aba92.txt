Job Description
Our Research & Development Division Data, AI & Genome Sciences (D&GS) 2027 Future Talent Program
The Future Talent Program within our Research & Development Division Data, AI & Genome Sciences (DAGS) Department is offering two computational co-op positions
for a six-month term. These roles are designed to give students hands-on experience applying computational methods to real drug-discovery questions, develop
their skills, and help both the student and the team evaluate longer-term fit. We are seeking highly motivated, talented students with strong computational and
quantitative skills.
Location: Cambridge MA, Boston MA, or South San Francisco CA (remote may be considered)
Term: Full-time, six months
The Roles
- Computational Oncology
Analyze and integrate large transcriptomic datasets to understand tumor biology and support target and biomarker discovery. Projects may include:
- Analysis of bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data - quality control, cell-type annotation, and characterization of tumor and tumor-microenvironment cell states.
- Relating molecular profiles to drug and therapy response using large-scale screening and sequencing datasets, to help prioritize and evaluate candidate
targets.
- Characterizing the transcriptional response to therapeutic perturbation (e.g. targeted agents, antibody-drug conjugates, immunotherapy) to inform mechanism of
action.
- Building clear, reproducible analyses and visualizations that communicate findings to project teams.
- Computational Immunology
Apply Computational Methods To Immune-profiling Datasets To Better Understand Disease Biology And The Immune Response, And To Support Target Evaluation.
Projects May Include
- Analysis of immune-focused sequencing data (e.g. single-cell and bulk RNA-seq) to characterize immune cell states and their changes across disease and
treatment.
- Analysis, implementation, and visualization of methods for studying interactions among disease-relevant biological networks.
- Data mining of internal and external molecular databases to support therapeutic discovery, disease stratification, and biomarker identification.
Required Education And Skills
- Currently enrolled in at least a Bachelor's degree program in biological sciences, computational biology, bioinformatics, or a related quantitative field.
Students in graduate programs (MS or PhD) are highly encouraged to apply.
- Available to work full-time for six months.
Preferred Skills And Experience
- Excellent academic achievement and analytical ability.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills, and the ability to work well in a collaborative team.
- Demonstrated interest or experience in any of - molecular biology, immunology, oncology, cancer biology, neuroscience, or bioinformatics.
- Programming experience in R and/or Python.
- Experience analyzing bulk or single-cell sequencing datasets (e.g. RNA-seq, scRNA-seq) and familiarity with standard statistical and visualization methods.
- Comfort working with large datasets and cloud or high-performance computing environments is a plus.
Please note that this position may be closed before the posted end date or may remain open longer, at the discretion of the company.
