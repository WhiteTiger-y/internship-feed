About Zendar
Zendar builds physical agents: mobile robots that can go anywhere in the physical world, understand what they see and act on it. We are deploying them to
automate enterprise sites, doing in the physical world what AI agents already do in software.
What makes our robots special is the navigation foundation model for autonomous systems that Zendar has developed over the past 9 years. Our radar-centric
autonomy stack lets mobile robots traverse any environment, from offices and warehouses to roads, in any weather and at high speeds. Our physical agents get
wherever they need to go, quickly, reliably and safely.
We are a small team that owns the whole stack, from sensors and perception to planning, AI and the robots in the field. We are scaling deployments rapidly, so
you will see your work running on real robots at real sites and delivering value to our customers.
Although AI is central to what we build, our hiring process is intentionally human: every resume is reviewed by a real person.
The Perception and Machine Learning Team is looking for a final-year PhD student to join us as a Research Intern on learned planning and actuation, on-site in
Berkeley, CA. This internship offers an opportunity for both the intern and the team to assess mutual fit for a potential full-time research position.
The Role
You Will Own One Research Direction On The Production Planner, Working With The Researchers Who Ship It, On Real Fleet Data, And Present Your Results To The
People Who Decide What Ships. What a Successful Planner Means To Us, And What We Will Measure You Against
- Understands what is missing: finds the mechanism behind a driving planner's failures and shows which gap matters before changing anything.
- Handles the long tail, never gets stuck and knows how to get itself unstuck: agile, it commits, launches, re-plans and recovers on its own.
- Collision- and comfort-aware, and generalizes across structured environments (roads, lanes, intersections) and unstructured ones (lots, yards, sites, robotics
settings).
- Knows how to test and evaluate actuation and planning pipelines: open-loop, closed-loop and on-vehicle, with metrics that predict road behavior.
- Production quality, not a proof of concept: tested code, reproducible results, behavior that holds across checkpoints and scenes. This is our standard and our
measure of success.
Potential Projects
- Generative planning with flow matching: a flow-matching or diffusion policy over ego trajectories with few-step inference and explicit constraint guidance in
the sampler.
- The long tail: data balancing and hard-case mining, counterfactual and generative scenario synthesis, or reasoning-augmented planners.
- Closed-loop evaluation and training that matches our sensors: counterfactual observations faithful to radar and camera features, so closed-loop metrics
predict road behaviour.
- Post-training with reinforcement learning: rule-reward or closed-loop fine-tuning, parallel imitation + RL—and whether the gain survives a real sensor stack
and a safety layer.
- Your own idea: tokenized planning with learned scoring, a world model as the planner's critic, uncertainty-aware ranking, self-play traffic.
Every change is evaluated closed-loop and open-loop against the current planner, failures examined and not just counted, and results presented to the team's
leads. We support publication of non-proprietary findings.
What We Look For

internship in Berkeley.

learning for control, or closed-loop simulation and evaluation.
