Job Description
About the Role
The AI Solutions Intern will support the development and deployment of practical agentic AI and automation solutions across Bosch Central Sales. Working with
business, sales, engineering and enabling-function teams, the role will translate clearly defined business problems into usable solutions that deliver
measurable benefits.
The successful candidate will take ideas from initial discovery through rapid proof of concept, user testing, controlled deployment and adoption. They will work
within Bosch-approved technologies, information-security requirements and responsible AI principles, with appropriate technical and business oversight.
This is a delivery-focused internship offering practical experience in AI solution development, process improvement, stakeholder engagement and change adoption
within the Central Sales organisation.
Key Responsibilities
Solution Development & Deployment
- Rapidly develop working proofs of concept using Bosch-approved AI, automation and data technologies in response to prioritised Central Sales business
problems.
- Work with process owners and subject-matter experts to define requirements, success measures, data needs and expected business benefits.
- Extend successful proofs of concept into robust working solutions that can be adopted across Central Sales and, where appropriate, the wider Bosch UK
organisation.
- Test solutions with users, capture feedback and refine functionality, usability and reliability before deployment.
- Measure and communicate outcomes such as time saved, improved quality, reduced manual effort, faster decision-making or improved customer support.
Stakeholder Engagement & Adoption
- Engage Central Sales stakeholders across business and engineering functions to identify, assess and prioritise suitable AI use cases.
- Create clear communications that explain the business problem, the proposed solution, expected benefits and any changes required from users.
- Produce practical user guidance and training content to support solution rollout and adoption across the Central Sales team.
- Support demonstrations, training sessions, workshops and knowledge-sharing activities across Central Sales and the wider Bosch UK network.
Documentation, Governance & Support
- Document solution objectives, workflows, data inputs, operating instructions, ownership and support arrangements.
- Apply Bosch requirements for information security, data protection, responsible AI and solution governance throughout development and deployment.
- Engage the user community after launch, monitor utilisation and identify opportunities to improve or scale solutions.
AI Capability Development
- Develop knowledge of emerging AI technologies and assess their practical relevance to Central Sales processes and customer-facing activities.
- Share reusable approaches, lessons learned and good practice with colleagues and relevant Bosch UK communities.
- Contribute to a Central Sales pipeline of AI opportunities and support prioritisation based on value, feasibility, risk and scalability.
Qualifications
Essential
- Currently studying towards a degree in a quantitative subject for example Computer Science, Data Science, Software Engineering, Engineering, Mathematics or a
related discipline.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Demonstrable interest in artificial intelligence, automation and emerging technologies.
- Experience of programming, automation or data analysis gained through university, placements or personal projects.
- Good communication, presentation and stakeholder-engagement skills.
- Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively across multiple teams.
Desirable personal Attributes
The ideal candidate will be:
- Curious, proactive and eager to learn.
- Able to simplify code.
Additional Information
This is a 12-month internship beginning in July 2027. This position is open to undergraduate students who are eligible to partake in a work placement as part of
their university course.
Location: you will be based at the Coventry office.
As an Intern at Bosch, you will participate in an exclusive learning and development programme called the BUILD Programme designed to support you through your
internship journey. It includes a mixture of in-person and virtual sessions on topics including Effective Communication and Leading Myself provided by expert
external trainers.
