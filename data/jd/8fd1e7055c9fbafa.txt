A Career with Point72's Market Intelligence Team
On our proprietary research team—Market Intelligence—you’ll partner with our investment professionals and Compliance team to work with alternative data to
uncover insights about companies, industries, and the broader economy through deep fundamental research and applying data science and engineering techniques.
You’ll work alongside a talented team with diverse skills, backgrounds, and perspectives. Our sector data analysts, data scientists, data managers, and
engineers work together to build compliant research products that are incorporated into the research process of our investment professionals. We look for other
bright, motivated, and collaborative people to join our team and grow with us—a majority of the leaders in our group were promoted from within.
What you'll do
This 6-month intensive fellowship offers hands-on experience in data-driven research, business analysis, cross-functional collaboration, and the discretionary
investment process within a fast-paced, compliance-focused environment. Data Research Fellows will be considered for permanent placement on the team following
completion of the program.
During the 6-month program based in our New York office, you will:
- Analyze alternative data from multiple sources to identify trends, themes, and business insights across companies and industries
- Develop sector expertise by studying key performance indicators, competitive dynamics, and operating drivers within an assigned coverage area
- Produce research for internal investment professionals through written analysis, verbal updates, and clear data visualization
- Partner with data analysts, data scientists, and engineers to transform datasets into research products that support investment decision-making
- Interact with investment professionals to learn the discretionary investment process and contribute to informed research discussions
- Collaborate with Data Sourcing, Data Science, Data Engineering, and Compliance to ensure research is rigorous, useful, and compliant
- Translate complex analytical findings into practical insights for technical and nontechnical partners
- Apply alternative data within a broader fundamental research process while building skills for potential permanent placement
What's required
- Undergraduate degree conferred in 2025 or 2026
- Strong analytical skills and demonstrated academic success
- Basic proficiency with coding or programming, including exposure to SQL or Python
- Comfort analyzing data and explaining concepts clearly to technical and nontechnical audiences
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Strong organizational skills and ability to manage a demanding workload
- Understanding of how companies, industries, or sectors operate, with a strong interest in continued learning
- Intellectual curiosity, creative thinking, maturity, and a strong work ethic
- Ability to collaborate productively and build relationships across teams
- Commitment to the highest ethical standards
