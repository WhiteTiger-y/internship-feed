Title:
Branded Fuels - Business Analytics Intern
Date:
Oct 14, 2026
Location:
Dallas, TX, US, 75219
Basic Function
HF Sinclair is seeking a Business Analytics (Branded Fuels) Intern for Summer 2027 located in Dallas, TX. This intern will operate under close and frequent
supervision while aiding the Branded Marketing organization with business analysis (operational and financial), preparing market analyses, and assisting with
other reporting assignments for our Sales team, Operations, Brand, and Pricing departments.
Job Duties
- Assists with monthly Operational Analysis of business for Monthly Scorecard
- Key Performance Indicators - drivers for volume (consumer demand (national/regional vehicle miles traveled, consumer retail gas station site visits,weather
events, etc.), refinery performance), pricing environment, identify distributor discrepancies, highlight discounts or pricing outliers
- Assists with reviewing and/or auditing branded retail station project economics– assessing if the correct assumptions were used for branding retail gas
stations based off project economic assumptions submitted.
- Validating site volume performance, assess loyalty program usage (% DINOPAY transactions, DINOPAY SG&A cpg), % product grade (Unleaded Gasoline, Midgrade
Gasoline, Premium Gasoline, Diesel), ratio of deals done with Distributor/site packages and overall conversion of sites from prospect to branded, credit card
processing margin, use of capital, product margin, lead time for branding and contractor feedback, etc.
- ROI on Marketing Investments by region
- Assists with analyzing Premium Fuel Market - identify our biggest opportunities, how do our competitors perform in this space
- Assists with identifying top distributor qualities - come up with set of metrics to rank distributors
- Quantitative: Meeting contract requirements, growth in site count, discounts offered to them, capital requirement/structure, quality of sites/secret shopper
scores, AR turnover metrics, etc.
- Assists with creation of Regional KPI’s - benchmark opportunities against what we consider “good” for that region
- Average retail gas station site throughput, site size (# of pumps), attrition rate, NTB rate, NTI rate, avg. secret shopper score
- Assist with management reports that utilize data visualization (Power BI) to showcase key performance indicators for effective and timely decision making
Special assignments or tasks assigned to the employee by their supervisor, as determined from time to time in their sole and complete discretion.
Education Level
Some completed courses needed for a bachelors degree in business or a related field are required.
Required Skills
Strong business acumen and ability to effectively communicate with strong verbal communication skills, analytical, problem solving and organizational skills;
good interpersonal skills; proficient and demonstrated ability working with spreadsheets, word processing and database applications; ability to identify
discrepancies and determine alternative courses of action. Strong working knowledge of Microsoft products, PowerPoint in particular. Ability to perform
intermediate level mathematical calculations. Must demonstrate critical thinking.
Preferred Skills:
Excel modeling, Tableau/Power BI, SQL Proficiency, statistical analysis, and other Microsoft products
Work Conditions
Office based with up to 10% travel by land and air required. Subject to all weather and varying road conditions.
