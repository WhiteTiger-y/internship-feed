Role Summary
URBN is seeking a Data Science Intern who will support the Data Science team. You will design, build, evaluate, and support deployment of machine learning
models and systems. This includes working closely with other data scientists, ML engineers, and data engineers to perform different tasks.
About the Summer Internship Program: Each summer, URBN Inc. welcomes candidates from across the country to immerse themselves within various teams across our
portfolio of brands and gain hands-on learning experience in the area of business they choose to pursue. Our paid internship program also offers a summer full
of internfocused events, including exposure to senior leadership through small-group settings, insight into company initiatives, off-site field trips, and, for
those who qualify, the opportunity to interview for a full-time position. See for yourself what our past and present interns are up to @urbncareers!The URBN
2027 Summer Internship Program will run from 6/7 through 8/13 at our Home Office in Philadelphia, PA.
Summer Internship Program Application Process:
1) Apply to one (1) internship posting that best aligns with your interests and skills. If there is a project component included in the application (see role
qualifications below), it is required and must be submitted at the time of application to be considered for the position.
2) Complete a Video Interview: Candidates selected to move forward will receive an email with a link to complete a video interview. Once the email is received,
candidates will have two (2) days to complete this portion of the application.
3) Interviews with our internal teams will be scheduled on a rolling basis.
4) To ensure you receive the most up-to-date information regarding your application, please make sure URBN is added to your safe senders or contacts so our
emails do not go to spam. If you are using a school email address, please also include a personal email address in your profile as an alternate point of
contact.
Applications will close at 5:00 PM EST on October 30, 2026.
Role Responsibilities
- ML Modeling : Evaluate and build statistical and machine learning models with an emphasis on business impact and time-to-production. Approaches span from
basic regressions to advanced neural networks.
- Analysis : Support opportunities to add value to the business through application of statistical and ML techniques. Quantify the impact of those opportunities
and communicate the outcome to peers and leaders.
- ML Deployment : Support ML engineers in deploying data science algorithms in production. This includes containerizing algorithm training and inference
workflows, building data pipelines, and monitoring already-deployed ML systems.
Role Qualifications
Machine Learning Techniques :
- Understanding of machine learning algorithms, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling applied to real-world business use cases
- Proficiency in one or more analytics methodologies such as time series analysis, clustering, and classification.
Machine Learning Tools :
- Proficiency in SQL.
- Familiarity with relevant data science libraries and tools (e.g., Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, ggplot2).
- Hands-on experience with machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, etc.
Opportunities to Learn with the Team
- Cloud platforms (Google Cloud Platform)
- Cloud infrastructure (Virtual Machines, Cloud Storage, IAM, etc.)
- Performance evaluation of deployed applications, simulations, and ML models
