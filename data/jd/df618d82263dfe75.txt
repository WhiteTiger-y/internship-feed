Description
Data Science Summer Internship Program It’s a data-driven world. Be part of a growing community of data science professionals that harness the power of data and
cutting-edge technology to analyze performance trends, identify opportunities, and keep Liberty Mutual moving forward.
The details
Calling all aspiring data scientists! Here’s the perfect opportunity to grow your knowledge and skills in key areas such as GenAI, machine learning, and various
modeling techniques and software, just to name a few.
In this 11-week Data Science Summer Internship Program, you’ll work both independently and as part of a team to research, gather, and analyze data, build or
enhance robust models, and recommend solutions that impact business decisions – all while you augment your technical skillset and build business acumen.
This internship program may be your first step toward pursuing a rewarding career at Liberty Mutual, one of the leading property and casualty insurers in the
United States and a global Fortune 100 company. Our interns often transition into full-time data scientists at our Boston Headquarters.
Note: We encourage interested candidates to apply promptly, as the role may be unposted once we’ve received a sufficient number of candidates. We conduct
interviews and extend offers on a rolling basis.
Qualifications
What you’ve got
- Currently pursuing a master’s degree in Statistics, Data Science, Analytics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or a related discipline
- 0-2 years of professional experience
- Intermediate experience with programming languages such as Python or R
- Foundational knowledge of statistical analysis concepts, techniques, and tools
- Demonstrated experience with exploratory data analysis, regression analysis, and model development and evaluation through coursework and/or internships
- Strong communication, analytical, critical thinking, and interpersonal skills
- Permanent work authorization in the United States
