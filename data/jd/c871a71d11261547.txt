Job Details
Job Description:
Position Overview We are seeking a highly motivated graduate engineering intern to support SATAS modeling, simulation, digital twin development, and system
optimization activities. The intern will develop parameter-based engineering models that enable the SATAS team to evaluate manufacturing system capabilities,
configurations, and design alternatives. These models will support What-If (WIF) scenarios used to assess equipment architecture, system sizing, material flow,
capacity, throughput, equipment utilization, factory space, automated material handling requirements, energy productivity, and waste-reduction opportunities.
The role combines analytical modeling, discrete-event simulation, systems engineering, optimization, sustainability analysis, and data analysis. The intern will
work with engineering subject-matter experts to translate process flows, equipment specifications, operating assumptions, energy and utility inputs, material-
loss drivers, and facility constraints into reusable decision-support models.
Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications
- Graduate student pursuing an MS or PhD degree in Industrial Engineering, Systems Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Operations
Research, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical discipline.
- 3+ months of educational, research, laboratory, internship, or industry experience.
- Strong academic performance with a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher preferred
- Coursework or project experience in modeling and simulation, manufacturing systems, operations research, systems engineering, optimization, or industrial
engineering.
- Programming experience using Python, MATLAB, C++, R, or a similar technical language.
- Experience developing analytical models using spreadsheets, engineering software, simulation tools, or programming environments.
- Understanding of statistical analysis, data visualization, and structured engineering problem solving.
- Ability to organize complex technical assumptions into a logical and maintainable model structure.
- Ability to communicate modeling methods, assumptions, limitations, and results clearly.
- Demonstrated ability to work independently while collaborating with a multidisciplinary technical team.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with discrete-event simulation software such as Siemens Plant Simulation, AnyLogic, FlexSim, Simio, Arena, or equivalent.
- Experience developing digital twin, manufacturing simulation, logistics, or material-flow models.
- Experience with mathematical optimization, design-space exploration, sensitivity analysis, Monte Carlo simulation, or design of experiments.
- Knowledge of manufacturing capacity planning, factory physics, queueing theory, production scheduling, or operations research.
- Coursework or project experience in industrial energy analysis, sustainable manufacturing, life-cycle thinking, material efficiency, yield improvement, or
waste reduction.
- Experience developing parameter-driven tools, reusable model libraries, application interfaces, or engineering dashboards.
- Experience with Python data and optimization libraries, SQL, structured datasets, data pipelines, version control, or similar analytical toolsets.
- Familiarity with CAD layouts, equipment footprints, facility modeling, or spatial analysis.
- Knowledge of automated material handling systems, robotics, mobile robots, overhead transport, conveyors, or automated storage systems.
- Familiarity with semiconductor manufacturing, assembly and test operations, advanced packaging, or high-volume manufacturing.
- Experience verifying and validating models against physical systems, experimental data, or operational results.
- Strong technical writing, presentation, and stakeholder communication skills.
