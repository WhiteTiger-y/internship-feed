Overview: This program is designed to foster a beneficial relationship and provide growth opportunities for Vizient, our employees, and collegiate students.
Since 2000, our summer intern program has provided college undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students with practical experience that exposes them to
multiple aspects of a career field and the healthcare industry. This full-time, paid intern program offers an opportunity to accelerate academic learning, apply
classroom knowledge, and introduce students to a professional setting. Interns are assigned a supervisor to mentor/develop their business skills, as well as
monitor their work experiences. Interns are integral members of the company’s team, setting goals, working toward deadlines, and actively participating in
community service projects. The program requires the intern to commit to 10 weeks of participation in all formal training, mentoring, on-the-job learning, and
opportunities to interact with senior leaders. Program starts June 1, 2027, and ends August 6, 2027 Pay rate: $25 per hour. About the team and role The Revenue
Intelligence team Marketing, Sales, and Account Management make better decisions and execute more effectively by shaping strategy, improving processes, and
turning commercial data into useful reporting and insights. This internship will support the team by analyzing marketing and sales data, helping with campaign
and pipeline reporting, identifying account-planning insights, validating data, and contributing to projects related to the Unified Commercial Platform. The
intern will see how data and technology are used to help Vizient better understand its customers, manage its sales activity, and grow revenue. What You’ll Do
Analyze marketing, sales, pipeline, account, and customer data to identify trends, opportunities, and risks. Support campaign performance reporting, including
lead progression, engagement, pipeline contribution, and conversion analysis. Assist with sales and pipeline health reporting, forecast analysis, and
identification of data quality issues. Help develop and improve dashboards, recurring reports, and ad hoc analyses using tools such as Power BI, Excel, and
Microsoft Dynamics 365. Support account planning by analyzing customer relationships, business priorities, whitespace opportunities, and account activity.
Contribute to Unified Commercial Platform (UCP) initiatives through data validation, business process analysis, user testing, and reporting. Partner with
Marketing, Sales, Revenue Operations, Finance, and technology teams to understand business questions and translate them into actionable analysis. Document
analytical methods, business requirements, findings, and recommendations. Present insights and recommendations clearly to team members and business
stakeholders. What You’ll Learn How marketing and sales data work together to support revenue growth and commercial decision-making. How campaign performance,

tools such as Power BI, Excel, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and related data platforms to analyze and communicate information. How to evaluate data quality, identify
