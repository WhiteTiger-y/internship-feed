Internship - Machine Learning and image-based metrics for EUV system setup

Your Assignment
In ASML EUV Light Sources, high power lasers interact with Sn material to create the Plasma that generates EUV light. Achieving the correct alignment between
the lasers and the Sn material is essential for efficient and stable operation. High-speed camera images captured during EUV formation contain valuable spatial
information about this interaction. Your goal will be to develop image-based Key Performance Indicator (KPIs) that describe the alignment between lasers and Sn
material. .
Key Idea and Deliverables
You will apply machine learning, and image analysis techniques to high-speed images collected during controlled experiments. The objective is to identify image
characteristics that reliably indicate good laser-to-Sn alignment, and translate them into simple, measurable KPIs.
The project will involve:
- Collecting and organizing high-speed image data from controlled experiments
- Extract relevant image features using a mix of conventional image-processing techniques and/or explore ML/AI methods
- Develop one or more image-based KPIs that summarize the relevant information
- Validate the propose KPIs against performance measurements, and their robustness across different operating conditions
Success Criteria
Identification of image-based KPIs that show clear, repeatable trends with laser and Sn material alignment quality and demonstrate improved observability
compared to current methodology in use on EUV sources. .
