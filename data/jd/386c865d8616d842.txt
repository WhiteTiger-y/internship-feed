About Ragle
Since 1993, Ragle Inc. has built infrastructure across Texas and beyond, specializing in highway, bridge, excavation, grading, and other heavy civil
construction.
We're looking for a Data Analytics Intern to join our team for a 10-week summer internship. You'll gain hands-on experience using data and technology to help
improve real construction operations while learning from experienced professionals.
What You'll Do
Build and maintain dashboards and reports in Power BI
Analyze project and operational data to identify trends and insights
Use Python and SQL to support reporting and automation
Help automate manual and repetitive processes
Support KPI tracking across construction projects
Work with project controls, operations, and IT teams
Contribute to real analytics projects that impact the business
What We're Looking For
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Analytics, Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Construction Management,
or a related field
Basic experience with Power BI, Python, SQL through coursework, projects, or previous experience
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Detail-oriented, curious, and willing to learn
Strong communication and teamwork skills
Available to work 40 hours per week for the full 10-week program
