Responsibilities
Peraton is seeking a Summer 2027 Social Media & Marketing Analyst Intern to support the County of San Diego Contract from June 2027 to August 2027.
As a Social Media & Marketing Analyst Intern students help tell the story of how technology is improving County services. You'll develop content across
LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and X, benchmark performance against peers, and turn findings into presentations for leadership.
Peraton is the County of San Diego's IT and telecommunications partner, supporting the technology that County departments rely on to serve more than 3 million
residents, from public health and social services to public safety and libraries. As a intern/co-op on the CoSD program, you'll work alongside Peraton
professionals on real projects that modernize County services, including emerging work applying AI to how software is designed, built, and delivered.
Internship structure: Full-time (40 hours/week) for a 10-week summer internship. Hybrid, based in San Diego, CA.
