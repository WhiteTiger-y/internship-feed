Job Description
Primary Function of Position
The Reinforcement Learning Intern will report to future forward research lead and contribute to the development and evaluation of reinforcement learning (RL)
algorithms in support of supervised autonomy initiatives. The intern will identify, train, and test several RL algorithms using data collected from the
Intuitive surgical robot systems. While the bulk of this work will take place on offline-collected data (i.e., not in real-time on the robot), a stretch goal
would be to deploy an RL algorithm to control the robot in real-time.
Essential Job Duties
- Collect tele-operated simulation and/or real data from the surgical robot while performing a realistic exercise (e.g., Fundamentals of Laparoscopic Surgery
pick-and-place). This is referred to as trajectory data and is critical for RL training.
- Train off-the-shelf reinforcement learning algorithms (e.g., policy gradient methods, actor-critic methods, etc.) on the trajectory data and benchmark their
performance using established metrics.
- Adapt reinforcement learning algorithms to the surgical domain and exercise of interest and identify techniques for improving performance relative to off-the-
shelf RL models.
- Recommend a suggested algorithm / experimental setup moving forward (beyond the internship) based on experimental results, and present findings to the broader
team.
- Deploy chosen RL algorithm either in simulation or on the dV5 to validate its performance in real-time (vs. purely offline evaluation).
