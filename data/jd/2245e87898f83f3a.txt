What You Will Do
- Own an end-to-end analytics project in a business area, from problem definition through a final presentation to stakeholders.
- Apply statistical and machine learning techniques where they fit the problem (for example, forecasting, segmentation, or classification).
- Collect, clean, and combine data from multiple sources, using SQL and Python/R, to create analysis-ready datasets.
- Perform exploratory analysis to uncover trends, anomalies, and actionable insights in data that may not look meaningful at first.
- Build and maintain BI dashboards that turn data into clear, decision-ready views.
- Develop ETL processes and automated workflows that refresh data sources and deliver scheduled email reports.
- Partner with business stakeholders to understand their needs and translate findings into recommendations.
- Present results to technical and non-technical audiences, including senior leaders, and document successes and limitations.
- Perform other duties as assigned
- Work effectively in an environment shaped by artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, data analytics, and cloud‑based tools, using insights responsibly
and in alignment with our standards for data governance, security, and ethical use
