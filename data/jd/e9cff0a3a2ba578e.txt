HOW WE WORK AI has changed how fast a data scientist can move. It has not changed what makes one good. We look for people who use AI tools aggressively to move
faster, and who can still explain every line they ship, defend the assumptions behind a model, and recognize an answer that is fluent but wrong. Strong
fundamentals first, AI as a multiplier. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Learn the business: how our supply chain operations run, what the underlying data represents, and
where decisions get made. Perform exploratory data analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and actionable insights. Write and optimize SQL queries to
extract, transform, and analyze data from multiple sources. Develop statistical and machine learning models to support supply chain decision-making. Contribute
to GenAI and LLM initiatives, including AI-powered search, automation, and business applications, building reusable components where the work can scale. Design
and enhance dashboards and reports using modern tools (Tableau, Power BI, or Custom built). Partner with stakeholders to understand business challenges, gather
requirements, and identify opportunities for improvement. Translate business needs into technical requirements and develop scalable analytical solutions.
Communicate findings and recommendations to both technical and non-technical audiences. TECHNICAL SKILLS Required Foundations Coursework or hands-on experience
with databases and data modeling (RDBMS concepts, primary and foreign keys, table relationships, basic normalization). Working knowledge of SQL (joins,
aggregations, subqueries; CTEs and window functions a plus). Working knowledge of Python for data analysis (Pandas, NumPy). Introductory statistics and machine
learning fundamentals (supervised versus unsupervised methods, train/test split, basic evaluation metrics, distributions, correlation versus causation).
Familiarity with data visualization using Tableau, Power BI, or a comparable tool. Bonus: Tooling and AI Experience Exposure to data engineering concepts
