Company Description
INFICON is a leading provider of innovative instrumentation, critical sensor technologies, and Smart Manufacturing/Industry 4.0 software solutions that enhance
productivity and quality of tools, processes, and complete factories. These analysis, measurement and control products are essential for gas leak detection in
air conditioning/refrigeration and automotive manufacturing. They are vital to equipment manufacturers and end-users in the complex fabrication of
semiconductors and thin film coatings for optics, flat panel displays, solar cells and industrial vacuum coating applications. Other users of our vacuum-based
processes include the life sciences, research, aerospace, packaging, heat treatment, laser cutting and many other industrial processes. We also leverage our
expertise in vacuum technology to provide unique, toxic chemical analysis products for emergency response, security, and environmental health and safety.
Job Description
INFICON is a global leader in vacuum technology and gas analysis instrumentation. We are seeking a motivated STEM intern for our team, which develops next-
generation mass spectrometry components through materials science, advanced coatings, and precision fabrication. The intern will support hands-on work in
fabrication, and characterization, build prototypes and fixtures, run experiments on vacuum and analytical equipment, and document results. Ideal candidates
have lab safety experience, can read engineering drawings, and have foundational vacuum knowledge.
What You’ll Do
- Support experimental work in fabrication, coating, and characterization of mass spectrometry components
- Assist with prototype builds, including mechanical models and fixtures
- Help set up and run experiments on vacuum and analytical equipment
- Collect, analyze, and document data, and contribute to technical reports and presentations
- Work alongside scientists and engineers on cross-functional development projects
Qualifications
Required:
- Currently pursuing a degree in a STEM field (e.g., physics, chemistry, materials science, chemical, mechanical, or electrical engineering)
- Practical experience in a chemistry or research lab, with a solid understanding of lab safety practices
- Ability to read engineering drawings and contribute to building models or prototypes
- Foundational knowledge of or experience with vacuum systems
Preferred
- Academic background or experience in vacuum science, spectroscopy, or plasma physics
- Coursework or experience in mechanical or electrical engineering
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Curiosity, initiative, and a collaborative attitude
