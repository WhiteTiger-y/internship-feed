Join the power movement as an AI & Infrastructure Engineering Intern
We are looking for a talented AI & Infrastructure Engineering Intern to join our team! CFS interns are highly valued within the organization and placed into
meaningful and challenging roles.
Program Details:
- 26 week internship
- 40-50 hours per week with set hourly salary based on year in school
- Opportunity on site in Devens, MA. If an intern is going to be on site, you will be responsible for your own transportation and housing
- Specific project assignments and required technical skillset will vary based on the team’s existing need
- Opportunities available for currently enrolled college students in all levels of schooling from bachelor’s degree to PhD
- Interns must be authorized to work in the United States for any employer
What you'll do:
- Serve as first-line support for employees using AI tools:
- Help people build and version their own applications
- Follow repo and file-management best practices
- Get unstuck on the technical side of AI implementation
- Handle AI tooling onboarding:
- Process license requests through the Service Catalog
- Set up GitHub and AI tool access
- Guide teams through creating and publishing marketplace plugins
- Develop and maintain internal AI applications:
- Implement scoped features and write automated tests
- Turn recurring user questions into training content and company documentation
- Meet with teams across the business to document current AI usage, identify aspirational use cases and bespoke problems, and coach interested individuals
toward becoming self-sufficient AI developers
What we’re looking for:
- Python backend development
- Git/GitHub workflows: branching, pull requests, code review, etc
- Experience building with AI coding tools, including configuring project rules, skills, or custom agents
- Working with LLM APIs: tool use, structured outputs, prompt and context management
- Building or deploying an MCP server
- Agent frameworks and observability or evaluation tooling
- RAG fundamentals: embeddings, vector stores
- Docker and Linux basics
- Authentication concepts: SSO, role-based access
- Strong communication - the intern will spend time meeting with non-technical teams
- Security and data-handling awareness - the role touches SSO, access provisioning, and internal data
Must-have Requirements:
- Willingness to travel or work required nights/weekends/on-call occasionally
- Perform activities such as typing, standing, or sitting for extended periods of time
- Dedication to safety to mitigate industrial hazards that may include heat, cold, noise, fumes, strong magnets, lead (Pb), high voltage, and cryogenics
