Job Description
In this position, you will participate in BAE Systems' nationwide LEAP Internship & Co-op Program, where you will Learn, Engage, Apply, and Progress within the
company. The LEAP Program will provide you with the opportunity to work alongside experienced professionals on impactful projects, engage in career development
opportunities, and network with our business leaders and the other students in the program.
The Opportunity
Skills
BAE Systems is seeking an AI/ML Engineer Intern for Summer 2027 with a Computer Science, Data Engineering, Software Engineering, or Computer Engineering
background. Join our AI-Enabled Digital Engineering Team and become a member of a diverse, enthusiastic, and highly productive team, where each employee has the
opportunity to grow their skills and work on rewarding projects. The selected candidate will contribute to the design, development, and deployment of
intelligent systems that can learn from data, make predictions, classify objects, and generate insights. As an AI/ML Engineer, you will play a critical role in
developing high-quality analytic algorithms that drive business value and mission-critical use cases in support of the US Navy's Strategic Systems Programs—a
program that keeps our nation safe with a credible sea-based nuclear deterrent. The selected candidate will work closely with the AI-Enabled Digital Engineering
Team to support the development of AI/ML tools by performing the following functions:
- Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML models using popular libraries and frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, Pytorch, Scikit-learn, Keras).
- Conduct feature engineering and feature selection to optimize model performance.
- Collaborate with Data Engineers to prepare data for AI/ML model development, ensuring that the data is in a suitable format for model training.
- Implement AI/ML algorithms, including design, training, testing, verification, and validation activities.
- Develop and implement data pipelines to enable retrieval of data from federated data systems (e.g., cloud storage or on-premises databases).
- Develop and implement monitoring systems to ensure AI/ML model reliability and performance, using tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, or New Relic.
- Stay up to date on best practices and emerging technologies in AI/ML, including advancements in deep learning, natural language processing, and computer
vision.
