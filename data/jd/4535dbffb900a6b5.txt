Job Description
Reporting to a Senior Manager within the Post-Irradiation Examination Characterization Department, you will support the development of an integrated and
autonomous platform for testing mechanical properties, combining multiple testing modes and length scales into a single automated workflow. The platform
integrates mechanical testing through nanoindentation and tensile tests, alongside characterization using digital image correlation (DIC) and electron
backscattered diffraction (EBSD). Using real-time data generated during testing, you will help develop intelligent decision-making algorithms to dynamically
recommend additional testing under varying conditions to evaluate mechanical properties
These algorithms will leverage Bayesian optimization with Gaussian process (GP) surrogate models to adaptively select the next experimental condition, while
active learning and convolutional neural networks (CNNs) will enable real-time interpretation of nanoindentation and DIC datasets. This adaptive approach
ensures the testing protocol is continuously optimized to thoroughly probe material behavior. This work will be conducted in the Structural Properties
Laboratory (SPL) at the Materials and Fuels Complex (MFC), Idaho National Laboratory (INL).
This team works a 4x10 schedule onsite. Start date and duration of internship vary based on project and student availability.
