Job Title: 2027 Summer Intern US Advanced Analytics
- Grade: Intern

About The Job
Looking to launch your career at the cutting edge of healthcare? Join Sanofi for a chance to develop with mentoring and guidance from inspirational leaders
while helping to make an impact on the lives of countless people worldwide. We are seeking a highly motivated Summer Intern to join our US Advanced Analytics
team.
You will be responsible for leveraging data science expertise in machine learning, statistics, and optimization. This person will join the US Advanced Analytics
team within the overarching US Go to Market Capabilities (GTMC) Insights and Analytics team, which serves as strategic partner to the US commercial teams
(Executive Leadership, Marketing, Sales, and Patient Support Services). Key responsibilities of this internship will include developing a proof-of-concept model
for a prioritized business use case. Furthermore, this role will be responsible for creating innovative data visualizations and interpreting model outputs to
communicate findings to leadership to drive business decisions at the conclusion of the summer internship. This position will report to the Senior Director, US
Advanced Analytics and will be based in either Cambridge, MA or Morristown, NJ.
Ready to grow your skills while helping shape the future of healthcare? At Sanofi, you’ll be empowered to learn, ask questions, and bring your ideas to life –
all while supported by inspiring mentors and collaborative teams.
