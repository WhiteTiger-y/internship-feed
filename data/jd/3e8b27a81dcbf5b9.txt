About The Job
We are a group of social scientists who conduct experimental, survey, and archival research to inform people-related business decisions. Example content areas
include, but are not limited to: decision making, leadership, employee well-being, hiring, performance management, performance development, personality,
longitudinal data analyses, and social networks. We have strong research backgrounds, with PhDs in I/O Psychology, Organizational Behavior (micro & macro),
Sociology, Social Psychology, Anthropology, Behavioral Economics, and related fields.
