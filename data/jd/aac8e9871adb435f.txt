Purpose
As a Production Business Intelligence at Divergent Technologies Inc., you’ll be a vital part of a dynamic team that thrives on solving complex problems through

The Role
Data Analysis and Reporting:
- Extract, transform, and analyze datasets using cutting-edge BI tools and methodologies.
- Create robust and well documented code and data infrastructure that can provide long-term value.
Manage Company-Wide Business Intelligence Assets:
- Build and improve data tools to ensure clean and consistent data access for stakeholders across the company.
- Monitor and maintain existing data assets to ensure compliance with company policies and procedures, and repair assets as issues arise.
Data Integrity Analysis and Exception Reporting:
- Take ownership of existing data tools to create enduring solutions to proactively maintain healthy and clean data assets.
- Create scalable, automated exception reports to notify stakeholders of issues in source data systems
