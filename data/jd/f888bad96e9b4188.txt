The Opportunity
We are seeking a research intern to advance continual adaptation for robot policies. Our goal is to enable robots to improve from experience, personalize their
behavior, preserve learned capabilities, and build on prior learning to acquire new behaviors more efficiently.
You will work closely with our robotics and machine learning researchers on a project shaped around your interests, expertise, and the team’s priorities. The
work will combine algorithm development with experiments in simulation and on real robots, emphasizing reliable adaptation with limited robot experience and
human effort.
The Research
We are interested in how robots can improve through ongoing experience while preserving and building on what they have learned. Your project could explore one
of the following directions:
Continual learning: Preserve existing capabilities through successive adaptations and use prior experience to learn new behaviors more efficiently. Investigate
how useful adaptations can be retained, combined, and reused, including settings where the original training data are unavailable.
Efficient learning and adaptation: Enable robots to improve with limited experience by identifying where adaptation is needed and making effective use of prior
knowledge, new interactions, and guidance.
Personalization from human guidance: Use demonstrations, corrections, or preference feedback to change how a robot performs a task while maintaining task
success. Investigate when guidance is sufficient and when further learning is needed.
Possible approaches include reinforcement learning, imitation learning, agentic tool use, modular policies, and distillation, depending on the research
question.
Responsibilities
Work with your mentor to formulate a focused research question, develop an approach, and implement it within the team's research systems.
Design and run controlled experiments with strong baselines in simulation and on real robots, using measures appropriate to the project, such as task success,
retention and transfer of learned capabilities, adherence to user preferences, and the robot experience and human effort required.
Analyze failure modes and maintain reproducible code and experimental records.
Collaborate with researchers and engineers, present findings, and contribute to technical reports and potential publications in robotics and machine learning
venues.
Qualifications
Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in robotics, machine learning, computer science, or a related field.
Research experience in reinforcement learning, imitation learning, continual learning, or closely related machine learning methods.
Strong programming skills in Python and experience with a deep learning framework such as PyTorch or JAX.
Ability to design experiments, reason critically about results, work independently with mentorship, and communicate clearly.
Bonus Qualifications
Experience with generative robot policies, diffusion or flow models, vision-language-action, or world-action models.
Experience with online reinforcement learning, interactive learning, personalization, preference learning, or continual learning.
Experience with post-training, parameter-efficient adaptation, modular models, or agentic approaches using pretrained models.
Experience with robotics simulation, real-world robot learning, or robot data collection and evaluation systems.
Relevant research contributions demonstrated through publications, open-source projects, or substantial research projects.
