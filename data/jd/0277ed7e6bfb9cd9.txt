Key Responsibilities
- Develop an image analysis algorithm to quantify laser-induced damage on crystalline optic surfaces.
- Validate the algorithm's reliability and repeatability across image sets.
- Apply the algorithm to historical laser damage images to generate quantitative data.
- Correlate extracted damage features with laser damage lifetime and laser fluence.
- Summarize methodology and results in a report or poster presentation.
Requirements
- Currently pursuing a degree in Physics, Materials Science, Computer Science, or an Engineering discipline.
- Exposure to coding languages and familiarity with basic computation and data analysis.
- Must be available for 10 weeks during Summer 2027 (May-August 2027) with a graduation date of December 2027 or later
- Be interested in working for Corning, Inc after graduation, if presented with a full-time job opportunity.
- Be willing to work at the posted job location
Desired Skills
- Basic experience in any of the following: Java, Matlab, C, C+, C#, Python, or other high level programming languages.
- Data analysis using Excel, JMP, Origin, or similar tools.
- Introductory knowledge of optics and materials science of semiconductor materials.
- Laboratory or Research experience.
Success Measures / End-of-Term Outcomes
- A reliable algorithm that quantifies laser damage on optic surfaces.
- Quantitative data generated from the historical image set.
- Documented correlations between damage features and lifetime and fluence.
Skills / Competencies
- Translates raw optical images into quantitative, repeatable measurements.
- Documents methods and results so others can reproduce the analysis.
- Interprets data trends and explains findings clearly to technical teammates.
- Works with engineers and scientists to refine the approach based on feedback.
