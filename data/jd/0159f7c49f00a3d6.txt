About Our Group
The Advanced Data Storage Device Physics Group within the Seagate Research Group conducts research on advanced data storage and memory technologies through
experimentation and physics-based simulation. Our work informs the company’s long-term storage technology roadmap.
We are seeking a motivated PhD student to explore data-driven approaches to deepen our understanding of materials and their chemical properties. This internship
combines analytical chemistry, materials characterization, and AI/ML to extract meaningful insights from complex experimental data.
About The Role - You Will
- Develop and evaluate AI/ML and statistical approaches for materials characterization and chemical analysis.
- Build computational tools to process experimental data and extract chemically and physically meaningful insights.
- Review scientific literature and propose innovative research approaches.
- Collaborate with engineers from diverse disciplinary backgrounds.
About You
- Self-motivated and able to independently define research questions, develop approaches, and troubleshoot challenges.
- A collaborative team player with strong communication and interpersonal skills.
- Attentive to detail and committed to rigorous research.
Your Experience Includes
- A peer-reviewed publication record demonstrating independent research ability.
- Practical experience applying machine learning or statistical methods to scientific datasets.
- Experience with materials characterization; familiarity with chromatography is a plus.
- Currently pursuing a PhD degree in Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
