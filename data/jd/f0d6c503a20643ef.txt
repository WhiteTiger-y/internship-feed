Lab Summary
Vision Intelligence Team at Samsung AI Research Center (AIC) located in Mountain View, California, is currently recruiting outstanding research interns. Our
goal is to perform long-term research with potential for direct impact on future Samsung products reaching hundreds of millions of users worldwide. We are
focused on pushing the state-of-the-art in perception and multimodal understanding.
Position Summary
In this position, the student will collaborate with leading researchers on a project related to the lab’s interests in multimodal (image, video, 3D) retrieval,
question answering, reasoning & understanding.
Position Responsibilities
- Conduct research on multi-modal models in one the above-mentioned area.
- Write maintainable code to implement research ideas
- Work closely with the team to discuss findings and improve the method.
- Executing experiments, documenting their progress and presenting insights
- Prepare findings for submission to a major conference or journal
Required Skills
- Currently pursuing PhD degree
- Experience with programming in Python, Pytorch, Transformers etc.
- Previous publications in top-tier conference and journals in CV/ML/AI such as CVPR, ECCV, ICCV, ICML, NeurIPS, AAAI
- Experience in one or more of the following areas:
- Vision-language models, including architecture design, pretraining, or post-training techniques such as supervised fine-tuning (SFT), RLHF, and GRPO.
- Streaming or egocentric video understanding.
- Long-form video understanding, temporal reasoning, and event recognition.
- Non-generative world models, including joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPA).
- Visual or spatial memory for temporally extended understanding.
- Multimodal Embedding, representation learning and cross-modal alignment.
- Real-time or efficient inference for multimodal models.
- Strong oral and written communication skills
