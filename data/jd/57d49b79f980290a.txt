ICF is seeking a detail-oriented, self-motivated summer intern to support our Energy Programs, Analytics, and Security line of business. The intern will
contribute to projects that help government and commercial clients address complex challenges involving energy programs, markets, technologies, analytics,
infrastructure, and security. Working as part of a collaborative, multidisciplinary team, the intern will support a variety of research, data analysis, program
implementation, technical assistance, communications, and operational activities. The position will provide exposure to high-profile energy initiatives and the
opportunity to work with experienced professionals across a diverse portfolio of clients and projects. This is a full-time, 10-week internship that will begin
in early June 2027. This role will be hybrid (2x per week) from Arlington, VA, Reston, VA, or New York, NY. At this time, relocation or housing assistance is
not available. Pay is expected to be $23/hour for undergraduate interns.  Primary Duties and Responsibilities Conduct research on energy programs, markets,
