At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently seeking multiple 2027
Co-ops in Data Science & Digital Innovation to join our Preclinical Manufacturing & Research Information Technology (IT) team, which builds the tools that make
sense of Regeneron's manufacturing and research data.
These positions offer the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.
When & Where
Location: Tarrytown, NY
Timeframe: Our Co-op positions are full-time and onsite from January-August (Spring-Summer).
Pay: Intern and Co-op pay ranges are determined by level of education (year in school) and degree program. The hourly rate range for these locations is:
$17.90-$50.90 per hour.
What Youll Do
Build custom applications, dashboards, and automation workflows that support day-to-day lab and manufacturing operations
Develop artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) models that predict bioreactor performance (data science)
Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
Support data-platform migrations for research teams
Contribute to scientific data solutions used across the organization
Write and test code to support ongoing development projects
Collaborate with scientists and engineers to understand data and business needs
Analyze data to uncover insights supporting manufacturing operations
Present progress and findings to the broader team
Relevant majors: Computer Science, Data Science, Bioinformatics, Information Systems, Chemical/Biomedical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, Mechanical
Engineering
