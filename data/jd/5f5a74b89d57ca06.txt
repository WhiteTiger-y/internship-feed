About The Role:
The OverWatch Research and Development team, known as the Strategic Counter-Adversary Research (SCAR) team, conducts applied research, unbounded by specific
technologies, to enhance our ability to expose and counter adversaries' activities through the development of novel detection technologies and tooling to
optimize analyst workflows. The team consists of Engineers, Researchers, and Subject Area Experts who work together to conceive, design, prototype, and
implement novel tooling. The position for the internship is as a Research Intern, taking part in innovation research and prototype development.
The SCAR internship is a hybrid remote role based out of CrowdStrike’s Austin, TX or Arlington, VA office. The intern is expected to work from the CrowdStrike
office two times a week and should be within travel distance from the office.
What You’ll Do:
- Research and develop technology in support of cybersecurity threat hunting
- Support a key machine learning project focused on enhancing threat hunting capabilities
- Contribute to various ongoing R&D team projects as required
- Shadow different members of the team to gain exposure to threat hunting methodologies
