Your Opportunity as a Finance Technology Intern As an intern on our Finance Technology team, you will gain hands-on experience supporting the data, analytics,
automation, and technology solutions used across Finance. You will work alongside experienced developers and business partners to analyze data, develop and
maintain reporting solutions, improve business processes, and test changes to Finance technology tools. This role is a great opportunity for students interested
in applying analytics and modern technology, including AI, to solve real business problems. Location: Orrville, Corporate Offices Work Arrangements: Hybrid -
onsite a minimum of 9 days a month primarily during core weeks as determined by the Company; maybe more as business need requires In this role you will: Analyze
and model data to help Finance teams generate insights and take action. Assist with building and maintaining dashboards, reports, and interactive
visualizations. Prepare, clean, validate, and analyze data used by Finance teams. Learn existing Finance processes, technology workflows, and business use
cases. Support the creation, maintenance, and enhancement of existing data models and reporting solutions. Assist with small automations and process
improvements that help Finance teams work more efficiently. Develop and execute test plans for changes or upgrades to Finance technology tools. Support testing
and documentation for certified reports subject to key SOX controls. Help ensure data, reports, and technology solutions follow enterprise data governance,

learning from experienced developers and technical leaders. What we are looking for: Pursuing a degree in Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Data Science,

intelligence, data visualization, or analytical tools such as Spotfire, Tableau, Power BI, SQL, R, or Python through coursework, projects, or previous
