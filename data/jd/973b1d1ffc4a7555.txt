What You’ll Do
Panasonic Energy is looking for interns to join our innovative tea m s in the Panasonic Transformation Group (PTG) . Our team members are hard-working, goal-

Essential Duties:
- Work on projects, programs, and improvement ideas in digital transformation area
- Work on elements of the manufacturing digital solutions
- Update and maintain digital system and process documentation to support standardization
- Analyze and organize data to show trends and help prioritize focus areas that will make the biggest impact to the business
- Create enterprise data models to standardize naming conventions and display the upstream/downstream relationship of data
- Perform larger analysis to understand and show the business impact of Smart Factory initiatives
- Bring an outsider’s perspective and come up with improvement suggestions that challenge current processes
- Collaborate with other functional areas and interns to leverage processes and ideas to and from other parts of the business
- Other duties as assigned.
