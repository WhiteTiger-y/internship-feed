Research Scientist Intern, Robotics Responsibilities:
- Develop novel state-of-the-art algorithms for robot perception, manipulation, navigation, and control, leveraging modern deep learning and foundation-model
techniques.
- Train and evaluate robot learning policies — including imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and vision-language-action models — in simulation and on
physical hardware.
- Based on the project, help analyze and improve the efficiency, robustness, scalability, and real-time performance of algorithms deployed on robot platforms.
- Perform research to advance the science and technology of intelligent machines.
- Perform research that enables learning the semantics of multimodal and embodied data (images, video, depth, point clouds, tactile and force signals,
proprioception, language, audio).
- Contribute to data collection, teleoperation, and sim-to-real transfer pipelines that support large-scale robot learning.
- Collaborate with researchers and cross-functional partners across hardware, controls, software, and product, including communicating research plans, progress,
and results.
- Publish research results and contribute to research that can be applied to Meta product development.
