The Role
Hadrian is looking for a Data Science/ Data Engineer Intern to join the team building the data backbone of our autonomous factory. Every machine, part, and
process on our floor generates data, and how well we move, model, and interpret that data determines how fast we can scale. You'll work alongside experienced
engineers and analysts on real production data- from the pipelines that move machine telemetry and quality records into our warehouse to the models and analyses
that shape how we run the factory. This is a hands-on role where your work will ship, and your contributions will directly influence operational decisions at
Hadrian.
What You'll Do
Build and maintain data pipelines that move machine, production, quality, and program data from the factory floor into our warehouse
Model and structure datasets that engineering, operations, and quality teams rely on for daily decision-making
Analyze production data to surface insights on throughput, capacity, cycle time, yield, and machine utilization
Develop dashboards, metrics, and reporting that improve visibility into factory performance and program status
Prototype statistical or machine learning approaches for problems like anomaly detection, demand forecasting, or process optimization
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including operations, program management, and quality to translate open questions into measurable analyses
Write clean, well-tested, well-documented code and participate in code reviews
Surface data quality issues and contribute to discussions on how we instrument and govern our data
What We're Looking For
Current university student pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Applied Math, Engineering, or a related
field, with an expected graduation date between December 2027 and June 2028
Proficiency in Python and SQL
Familiarity with data pipelines, ETL/ELT workflows, or cloud data warehouses
Working knowledge of statistics and the ability to turn a messy dataset into a defensible conclusion
Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work through ambiguous technical challenges
Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to explain analysis to non-technical partners
Strong attention to detail and follow-through
Comfortable working in a fast-moving, highly ambiguous environment
Willingness to work on-site in Los Angeles, CA
Internships are available for our Fall 2026, Spring 2027, and Summer 2027 cohorts. Indicate your preferred term(s) when applying.
What Will Set You Apart
Prior internship or project experience building production data pipelines or shipping analysis that changed a decision

What You'll Gain

A front-row seat to how Hadrian is transforming aerospace and defense manufacturing through software, automation, and data
