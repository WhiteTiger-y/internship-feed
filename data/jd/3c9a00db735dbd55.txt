The Human Health & Performance Systems Group develops human-centered technologies to overcome operational challenges and to enhance human capability in domains
of interest to national security. Our research programs focus on innovative and objective solutions in the areas of integrated wearable systems, human-machine
teaming, enhanced communications, neurocognitive analytics, and medical technologies. Our group is highly interdisciplinary and includes scientific experts in
physiology, cognitive science, neuroscience, psychology, biomechanics, computer science, engineering, and physics. Our core technical competencies include
system-level modeling and gap analysis, advanced sensing and signal processing, machine learning and artificial intelligence, computational modeling, hardware
and software prototyping, model-based systems engineering, and human data collection in laboratory and field environments.
Position Description
Our team is looking for a Co-Op student with an interest in solving challenging AI/ML problems in multimodal imaging. Through this opportunity, you will work
with a multidisciplinary team of engineers, including roboticists, optical designers, and electrical engineers with expertise in signal and image processing.
You will prepare and process multimodal imaging datasets, develop image registration methods to align data across imaging modalities, and develop and evaluate
algorithms for multimodal analysis and image fusion. The work will also explore physics-informed AI models that incorporate imaging physics into machine
learning methods to improve image reconstruction and interpretation. We are looking for students who are self-motivated and interested in image processing,
machine learning, deep learning, and scientific computing.
Requirements And Skills
- The candidate is a student in an M.S., or Ph.D. program in Biomedical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or other relevant degree.
- Proficiency in Python and experience with standard scientific computing and machine learning libraries, and deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or
TensorFlow/Keras, through coursework or practical projects.
- Familiarity with GPU-accelerated computing using CUDA-enabled deep learning frameworks.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in biomedical or multimodal image processing, image registration, image fusion, or multimodal analysis.
- Interest in physics-informed AI models and incorporating physical models or constraints into machine learning methods.
- Ability to work collaboratively across disciplines and communicate technical results clearly.
