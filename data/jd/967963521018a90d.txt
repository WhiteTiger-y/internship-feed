From applied research to product development, SRI International’s Advanced Technology and Systems Division (ATSD) advances the state of the art in diverse areas
such as radar and electronic warfare, novel imaging technology, robotics, quantum sensing & metrology, marine technology and applied optics.
ATSD's Ann Arbor, MI office is hiring Radar Research Interns who will participate in multi-disciplinary, collaborative teams and contribute to the development
of state-of-the-art active/passive radar remote sensing, imaging systems and surveillance technologies. Projects span a wide variety of activities and include
development of advanced sensor concepts, waveform design, signal & image processing and development of new exploitation methods. Graduate students are
encouraged to apply their research topics and activities to projects. Interns will work onsite at the office.
Responsibilities
- Contribute to developing algorithms for processing and exploitation of radar sensor data.
- Formation and analysis of imagery products, development of signal processing tools for image understanding and imagery exploitation.
- Programming, algorithm development, design and execution of radar computational models & simulations, and analysis of results.
Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s/Master’s/Ph.D. degree in Physics, Engineering or a related field.
- Candidates must be a U.S. Citizen with the ability to obtain a security clearance
Desired Skills
- Coursework in Physics, Electrical Engineering and Mathematics.
- Some experience coding (Python, MATLAB, C, C++).
- Experience with UNIX/LINUX operating systems.
- Solid analytical ability.
