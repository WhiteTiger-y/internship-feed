The Security Science Laboratory, or SS Lab, at Fujitsu specializes in integrating research in AI and security to build trust in the increasingly diverse digital
society. We are seeking fall-through-spring exceptional interns to join our SS Lab in the US. This position provides an opportunity to solve problems on safety,
privacy, and AI alignment in LLM-based agentic systems and Robotics. We value individuals with vision to pick up new knowledge, see through complex scenarios
and arrive at simple, elegant yet workable solutions. Self-driven nature, creativity, communication skills, and attention to details are traits of a successful
researcher in this role. An intern has an option to work either full time or part time during the academic year.
Job Responsibilities
- Develop algorithms and systems, conduct experiments, and analyze the results. Potential research topics include, but are not limited to, detecting and
preventing multi-turn and multi-stage jailbreaking, adversarial coordination, data poisoning, contextual privacy leakage, improving misalignment with user
perceptions in agentic AI systems and robotics.
- Publish research papers in scientific conferences on AI, NLP, ML, and Security. Showcase the work via presentation in talks, conferences and workshops.
Requirements
- Currently enrolled in a PhD program in computer science or a related discipline, with demonstrated research experience applying AI, NLP, and/or ML techniques
to security and privacy challenges in agentic AI or robotics.
- Track record of coming up with new ideas or improving upon existing ideas in computing, demonstrated by accomplishments such as publications, projects, or
competitions.
- Experience in evaluating tools and technologies in processing data from diverse but specialized fields of science.
