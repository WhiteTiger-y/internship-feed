Job Summary
We are seeking an enthusiastic and forward-thinking student to drive hands-on projects that help implement AI best practices across our organization. This role
is ideal for someone passionate about artificial intelligence, operational excellence, and solving real business problems with practical, scalable solutions.
The AI Automation Intern will drive two key initiatives: Develop an AI‑driven magnetics design automation tool that accelerates the design of inductors and
transformers; and automate the firmware modification and calibration process by replacing manual updates to hard‑coded values with automated adjustments
generated from engineering specifications. Candidate must be able to commute to both Orange and Irvine offices.
Key Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end‑ projects focused on implementing an AI best practice.
- Develop an AI enabled magnetics design automation capability that leverages machine learning models trained on historical designs, simulation outputs, and
performance data to deliver rapid, reliable design recommendations and reduce engineering iteration cycles.
- Develop automation workflows to update firmware configuration values and calibration gains from defined specifications.
- Reduce manual editing of hard-coded parameters and gain values in C source files and related configuration artifacts.
- Work with engineers to translate product requirements and specs into reliable firmware change rules.
- Support validation of generated firmware changes through test cases, build checks, and result review.
- Document the automation process, tool usage, and limitations for team adoption.
- Develop documentation outlining datasheet standards, governance, and updated processes.
- Build simple prototypes, process maps, or proof‑of‑concept demonstrations as appropriate.
- Implement AI Agents or other productivity tools that drive simplification, efficiency, and speed.
- Present final project outcomes, impact, and recommendations to business and technical leaders.
Minimum Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master's degree in Engineering, Data Science, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
- Coursework, projects, or hands‑on internship experience with artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, or data analytics.
- Prior experience using AI productivity tools (Copilot, ChatGPT, or similar).
- Experience with C programming for embedded or firmware applications.
- Familiarity with TI IDE tools and TI chipsets.
- Experience with firmware configuration, calibration, or parameter tuning.
- Experience using automation, scripting, or AI tools to reduce manual engineering work.
- Familiarity with Microsoft 365 tools in a professional or academic setting.
Desired Qualifications
- Strong interest in artificial intelligence, automation, or data-driven process improvement.
- Ability to translate business needs into working AI solutions.
- Experience with research, data analysis, or process mapping methodologies.
- Basic understanding of AI concepts (e.g., machine learning, large language models, responsible AI).
- Prior experience in magnetics or power electronics design is preferred.
- Strong organizational and project management capabilities.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Effective written and verbal communication, including the ability to translate technical concepts for non‑technical audiences.
- Comfortable working independently while leveraging mentors and subject‑matter experts for guidance.
