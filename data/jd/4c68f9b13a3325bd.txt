You will support commercial project management activities for Bosch's GM portfolio while helping drive business process improvements through analytics,
automation, and AI-enabled solutions. In this role, you will work closely with commercial, engineering, finance, purchasing, and program management teams to
analyze data, improve reporting processes, and support customer-facing initiatives. You will have the opportunity to identify inefficiencies, develop automation
solutions, and leverage emerging AI technologies, including Agentic AI, to improve team productivity and decision-making. This position provides hands-on
exposure to automotive business operations, commercial program management, and digital transformation initiatives.
Job Responsibilities
- Commercial Project Support – You will support commercial project managers in tracking program deliverables, schedules, customer commitments, and business
objectives.
- Reporting & Analytics – You will create, maintain, and improve dashboards, reports, and data visualizations that support business and customer decision-
making.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration – You will work with engineering, finance, sales, purchasing, and program management teams to gather information and support
program execution.
- Process Improvement – You will identify opportunities to simplify and standardize business processes, improving efficiency and reducing manual effort.
- AI & Automation Development – You will leverage AI, Copilot, Agentic AI, and automation tools to create solutions that improve productivity and business
outcomes.
- Data Management – You will organize, validate, and analyze project, commercial, and operational data to support business decisions.
- Customer & Executive Presentations – You will assist in preparing reports, presentations, status updates, and executive-level communication materials.
- Digital Transformation Initiatives – You will support implementation of innovative technologies and automation workflows across the organization.
- Change Management Support – You will assist with commercial change management activities, including tracking program changes and related business impacts.
- Continuous Improvement – You will research emerging technologies and recommend new tools, processes, and best practices that create measurable business value.
Travel Requirements
- Up to 10% domestic travel as business needs require.
Required Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Finance, or a related
field.
- Must be enrolled in an accredited university throughout the duration of the internship/co-op.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0.
- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Teams.
- Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Experience working with data analysis, reporting, or business process improvement projects.
- Ability to work independently while managing multiple priorities and deadlines.
Preferred Qualifications
- Strong communication and presentation skills.
- Experience with Power BI, Power Automate, Power Apps, SQL, Python, or other analytics tools.
- Interest in automotive technologies and program management.
- Familiarity with Artificial Intelligence platforms such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar tools.
- Experience developing automation workflows, copilots, agents, or AI-enabled business solutions.
- Knowledge of Agentic AI concepts, prompt engineering, business process automation, or intelligent workflow orchestration.
- Curiosity, initiative, and a passion for identifying opportunities to improve efficiency through technology and innovation.
