Job Description
Programme Eligibility
To join one of our 12-month Internships you must meet one of the following criteria:
- You’re currently an undergraduate studying at University and returning to full time education in Autumn 2028
- You’re looking to gain work experience after completing your A-Levels, an equivalent course or an Apprenticeship
Additionally, you must also:
- Be eligible to work in the UK without restriction for the duration of the internship from Monday 5th July 2027 – Friday 21st July 2028
- Be able to work in NBCUniversal's Central London office on-site four days per week
We recommend applying to just one internship role, this helps ensure your application is considered for the opportunity that best matches your interests and
skills. Choosing one allows us to focus on what excites you most and where you’ll shine brightest.
What will I be doing?
You’ll join a team who are passionate about media and have curious minds when it comes to data. The team all bring different strengths and experiences to the
table, and strive to build an open, inclusive culture where everyone can bring their full selves to work, while achieving their career goals.
As an Audience Insights & Analytics Intern, you will assist the content sales business of NBCU. Interns are a crucial part of the team and work on a wide
variety of important projects, such as performance analysis of TV/Film content, generation of insights that feed directly into strategic business decisions, and
support for the sales of major series and movies to broadcasters and digital platforms. This internship provides a great opportunity to learn about the media
business and analytical techniques, as well as make a real difference to the success of the company.
What will I learn from this opportunity?
Analyse large and complex sets of data
Learn to process various datasets from TV ratings to streamers viewership.
Transform data into valuable business insights
Ability to distil large datasets into actionable insights that will help stakeholders make better business decisions.
Media industry knowledge
Get in-depth industry knowledge through analysis of content and industry trends.
Qualifications
What do I need to bring to the role?
- Passion and knowledge for media, particularly TV and/or Film.
- Comfortable working with numbers and analysing data.
- Great attention to detail.
- Able to communicate findings and discuss data.
- Eager to work in a company environment and keen to understand the role of research
Internship Programme Benefits & Perks
In addition to valuable hands-on experience, UK interns receive a competitive benefits package that supports their physical, financial, and emotional wellbeing.
Additional perks include exclusive employee discounts, access to ‘myPerks’ NBCU’s employee programme with opportunities to win tickets to attend movie
premieres, swag bundles and more. Plus, you’ll get free streaming access to your favourite platforms like Hayu and NOW TV.
The responsibilities associated with this position are not limited to the above description and may be modified at any time by the Company.
