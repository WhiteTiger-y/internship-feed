About Our Group
The Corporate Development & Strategy team partners across Seagate to shape enterprise strategy, evaluate growth opportunities, and support informed business
decisions. We are a collaborative team that combines strategic insight, market and competitive intelligence, and technology-enabled ways of working to help
translate priorities into action.
As a Strategy AI Intern, you will design and build AI-enabled applications that strengthen how the Corporate Development & Strategy team conducts research,
analyzes information, communicates strategy, and makes decisions. You will work at the intersection of software engineering, applied AI, data, and business
strategy, partnering closely with strategy and corporate development professionals to translate business needs into practical, scalable solutions.
About The Role - You Will
- Design, develop, test, and deploy Python-based applications that automate research, synthesize competitive and market intelligence, and support strategic
analysis and communication.
- Build AI agents that connect with approved internal and external data sources, automate analytical workflows, and improve the speed and quality of decision
support.
- Apply technologies such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Model Context Protocol (MCP), large language models, and agentic frameworks to relevant
business use cases.
- Translate technical concepts, limitations, and recommendations into clear language for technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Document solution architecture, data flows, operating procedures, and maintenance requirements to support sustainable adoption and knowledge transfer.
About You
- You are a hands-on developer who enjoys solving complex business problems with well-designed technology solutions.
- You combine strong engineering fundamentals with curiosity about corporate strategy, competitive dynamics, and business decision-making.
- You can work independently in an evolving environment while building alignment with stakeholders across functions and levels.
- You apply sound judgment when evaluating AI outputs, data quality, security, privacy, access, and potential failure modes.
- You communicate clearly and can translate between business requirements and technical design decisions.
- You are collaborative, accountable, and committed to continuous learning as AI technologies and enterprise standards evolve.
Your Experience Includes
- Proficiency in Python and experience developing, testing, and maintaining software applications.
- Experience with data engineering, APIs, databases, data integration, and structured and unstructured data.
- Hands-on experience with large language models and applied AI solutions, including prompt design, evaluation, and production-oriented implementation.
- Experience with one or more AI development approaches or frameworks, such as RAG architectures, LangChain or similar orchestration frameworks, MCP, or agent-
based applications.
- Experience applying software development practices such as version control, code review, testing, documentation, monitoring, and issue resolution.
- Ability to assess AI-generated outputs for accuracy, relevance, reliability, and business risk.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, written, and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to partner effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders in a global, cross-functional environment.
- Demonstrated Python capability through professional experience, project work, or a portfolio of relevant applications.
- Experience building or deploying AI agents, AI-enabled applications, or automation solutions in a professional or project environment.
- Currently pursuing a PhD, Master's, or Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Information Systems, or a related technical
field and must be enrolled in Fall 2027 classes.
