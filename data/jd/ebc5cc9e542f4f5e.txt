Bosch Research and Technology in Pittsburgh is seeking a motivated student intern to advance foundation models for multimodal sensing. Our research explores how
models can learn from diverse sensor streams to understand physical environments, recognize events and activities, and transfer knowledge across tasks and
sensing systems. The position offers opportunities to explore applications across interior and exterior environments, mobility, connected devices, industrial
systems, and other emerging domains. You will work alongside experienced researchers to develop broadly applicable methods at the intersection of deep learning,
signal processing, and cyber-physical systems.
Responsibilities
- Contribute to the design, training, and evaluation of foundation models for heterogeneous sensor data, such as radar, acoustic, vibration, image, lidar, Wi-
Fi, inertial, and other time-series signals.
- Investigate self-supervised learning, multimodal representation learning, and cross-modal alignment to learn from large collections of labeled and unlabeled
data.
- Develop methods for sensor fusion and adaptation across tasks, environments, devices, and sensor configurations.
- Evaluate models on representative tasks such as scene understanding, activity recognition, event detection, tracking, and anomaly detection.
- Explore robustness to missing modalities, noisy measurements, and changes in operating conditions, along with efficient adaptation and inference.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to prototype sensing solutions and communicate findings through reports, presentations, and potential publications or
patents.
Benefits
- Hands-on experience with multimodal sensing and foundation model research across diverse application domains.
- Mentorship from researchers in AI, signal processing, and cyber-physical systems.
- Opportunities to contribute to research publications, intellectual property, and technologies with potential impact on products and processes.
Required
- Currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Robotics, Data Science, or a related technical field.
- Strong knowledge of machine learning and deep learning principles.
- Proficiency in Python and experience with frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience with multimodal learning, time-series modeling, computer vision, or sensor data analysis.
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills, with an ability to collaborate in a research environment.
- Interest in developing transferable learning methods for real-world sensing applications.
Preferred
- Research experience in foundation models, self-supervised learning, contrastive learning, masked modeling, or generative modeling.
- Experience working with raw sensor data, such as radar, audio, lidar, images, vibration, or inertial measurements.
- Knowledge of signal processing, sensor fusion, or multimodal representation learning.
- Experience with domain adaptation, few-shot learning, cross-modal transfer, or learning with incomplete sensor inputs.
- Familiarity with efficient model architectures, edge AI, embedded systems, or deployment constraints.
- Publications in machine learning, computer vision, robotics, or signal processing venues.
