Data Quality & Systems Intern Location: Weybridge JOB PURPOSE: As businesses become increasingly connected and automated, the quality and reliability of data
have never been more important. As our Data Quality & Systems Intern, you'll join the B2B Application Support team at Sony Semiconductor Solutions, helping to
ensure the systems and data that support our supply chain, sales and marketing activities run smoothly and efficiently. You'll gain hands-on experience
analysing data, identifying inconsistencies and solving real business challenges, while exploring opportunities to improve processes through automation and
smarter ways of working. Working alongside experienced colleagues, you'll develop valuable analytical, problem-solving and technical skills, gaining an insight
into how technology and data support business decisions and day-to-day operations across an international organisation. This is an opportunity to go beyond
working with data, you'll help make it more accurate, reliable and valuable to the business. Start date: 5 July 2027 Disclaimer: Due to high volumes, this job
posting may be closed earlier than planned, so don’t hesitate to apply if you are interested. The Job What will you be doing? Purchase Order and Reservation
Order Reconciliation: Perform daily reconciliation of purchase orders and reservation orders between SAP and our demand planning tool. Data Quality Checks:
Review daily reports from the demand planning tool to identify missing six-digit material codes and take the necessary corrective action. SharePoint User
Management: Update SharePoint user groups monthly to ensure that access permissions reflect current user requirements. SharePoint Improvement Project
(Optional): Support a project to remove outdated documents, refresh existing content, and improve the overall look and structure of SharePoint. Customer-
Material Registration: Register new customer and material combinations in our demand planning tool. Customer EDI Interface Monitoring: Review customer EDI
interface reports, identify missing or incomplete combinations and information, and liaise with the relevant business teams to ensure that records are updated.
Forecast Reporting: Generate ad hoc reports covering sales forecasts, customer forecasts, and marketing forecasts. What do we need you to bring? Analytical
Thinking: An analytical mindset with an eye for detail and a willingness to investigate data discrepancies. Communication Skills: The ability to communicate
clearly and effectively with colleagues across different teams. Enthusiasm for Microsoft Excel: A strong interest in using Excel to analyse data, identify
discrepancies, and improve processes. Collaboration: A willingness to work closely with different teams and stakeholders. Business Curiosity: An interest in
understanding business processes, customer requirements, and the reasons behind data discrepancies. A Willingness to Learn: The confidence to try new
approaches, learn from mistakes, and ask for support when needed. Teamwork: Be willing to support teammates and learn from the people around you Our Recruitment
