The group you’ll be a part of
Join the SABRE Process Development team to help shape the next generation of semiconductor electroplating equipment. This is a 6-month internship preferably
starting in January 2027.
What You’ll Do
This role focuses on applying engineering principles, data analytics, machine learning, and automation to improve tool performance, process understanding, and
intelligent equipment capabilities. You will work on projects involving tool design concepts, experimental data analysis, predictive modeling, digital twins,
machine vision, sensor integration, and AI-driven process optimization.
Who We’re Looking For
The ideal candidate enjoys solving complex engineering problems, working hands-on with hardware and data, and exploring how AI can transform advanced
manufacturing systems.
Preferred Qualifications
- Pursuing MS or PhD in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, Robotics, or related field
- Strong engineering fundamentals and problem-solving skills
- Experience with Python, data analytics, machine learning, or computer vision
- Interest in semiconductor equipment, automation, control systems, or digital twins
- Ability to work across hardware, software, and process disciplines
