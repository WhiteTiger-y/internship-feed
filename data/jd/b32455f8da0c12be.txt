The Company
PayPal has been revolutionizing commerce globally for more than 25 years. Creating innovative experiences that make moving money, selling, and shopping simple,
personalized, and secure, PayPal empowers consumers and businesses in approximately 200 markets to join and thrive in the global economy.
We operate a global, two-sided network at scale that connects hundreds of millions of merchants and consumers. We help merchants and consumers connect,
transact, and complete payments, whether they are online or in person. PayPal is more than a connection to third-party payment networks. We provide proprietary
payment solutions accepted by merchants that enable the completion of payments on our platform on behalf of our customers.
We offer our customers the flexibility to use their accounts to purchase and receive payments for goods and services, as well as the ability to transfer and
withdraw funds. We enable consumers to exchange funds more safely with merchants using a variety of funding sources, which may include a bank account, a PayPal
or Venmo account balance, PayPal and Venmo branded credit products, a credit card, a debit card, certain cryptocurrencies, or other stored value products such
as gift cards, and eligible credit card rewards. Our PayPal, Venmo, and Xoom products also make it safer and simpler for friends and family to transfer funds to
each other. We offer merchants an end-to-end payments solution that provides authorization and settlement capabilities, as well as instant access to funds and
payouts. We also help merchants connect with their customers, process exchanges and returns, and manage risk. We enable consumers to engage in cross-border
shopping and merchants to extend their global reach while reducing the complexity and friction involved in enabling cross-border trade.
Our beliefs are the foundation for how we conduct business every day. We live each day guided by our core values of Inclusion, Innovation, Collaboration, and
Wellness. Together, our values ensure that we work together as one global team with our customers at the center of everything we do – and they push us to ensure
we take care of ourselves, each other, and our communities.
Job Summary:
As a Data Science intern, you will have an impact on PayPal’s products all around the world and you will have the opportunity to gain hands-on experience and
exposure to the world of data science. This internship is designed to provide a talented and aspiring data scientist with real-world challenges and projects to
sharpen their skills while contributing to our data-driven initiatives.
Our interns work to better understand our end-users, identify opportunities, and improve our products by creating personal and smart experiences. By making use
of PayPal’s endless amount of data, you will leverage your data and machine learning skills for the research and delivery of end-to-end ML solutions. If you are
interested to be a Data Scientist/Machine learning and have sharp analytical skills and a passion for deriving new insights, solving unique challenges,
leveraging machine learning algorithms, and working in a highly collaborative, values-driven environment.
Job Description:
As a Data Scientist Intern, you will work alongside experienced data scientists, engineers, and product teams to analyze data and uncover insights that inform
business and customer decisions. You’ll contribute to defining metrics, conducting analyses and experiments, and communicating findings and recommendations,
with guidance from your team. You should be collaborative, curious, and eager to learn as we use data to improve PayPal’s products and services.
Essential Responsibilities:
* Analyze large, complex datasets to identify trends, explain business performance, and develop actionable recommendations.
* Partner with product, engineering, and data teams to define success measures, improve data quality, and support reliable analytics.
* Design and evaluate analyses and experiments to inform product decisions and business priorities.
* Communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders, including senior leaders.
* Manage multiple projects and deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment.
Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications:
Minimum Qualifications:
* Experience analyzing complex datasets and translating findings into actionable recommendations.
* Proficiency in SQL and experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau or Looker; Python or R experience is a plus.
* Strong knowledge of statistics, including hypothesis testing and regression, and familiarity with experimentation.
* Strong communication, problem-solving, and organizational skills; able to work through ambiguity and collaborate across teams.
Internship Program Requirements:
* This is a Summer 2027 Internship program. Spring and Fall 2027 internships are not available.
* Must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship.
* Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, software engineering, or a related technical field at an accredited college or
university.
* Must be returning to school in Fall 2027.
* Applications will be accepted through Sunday December 6th, 2026.
