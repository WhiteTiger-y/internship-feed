Job Description
The Advance Computing Solutions group in the NLR Computational Science Center has an opening for a graduate intern in computational synthesis, with a particular
emphasis on development and utilization of an agentic AI framework that automates the exploration of relevant reaction energetics using machine-learned
interatomic potentials (MLIPs). The researcher will work with an interdisciplinary team to develop agentic AI that automates the training of MLIPs of desirable
accuracy and uses the trained MLIPs to discern the effect of varying different reaction conditions on the final synthesized Fe-N-C fuel cel catalyst.
We are looking for a dynamic, motivated researcher with a strong technical background and an interest in the mission of NLR. The successful candidate will
collaborate with NLR staff and researchers to develop agentic AI for the optimization of Fe-N-C catalyst synthesis.
Responsibilities Include
- Development of agentic AI framework that automates the training of MLIPs and the exploration of the combinatorial space of reactions relevant to Fe-N-C
catalyst synthesis and their energetics
- Collaboration with NLR researchers to use the developed AI to study the chemistry of graphitization and Fe incorporation into the N-C matrix for different
synthetic precursors, e.g. iron salts and zeolitic imidazolate framework (ZIF) linkers.
- Development of AI that processes, analyzes, and visualizes results from simulations provide chemical insight and intuition into important chemical reactions.
- Authoring, presenting and assisting in the preparation of technical papers, reports and conference proceedings on topics related to AI-automated MLIP studies
of the synthesis of Fe-N-C fuel cell catalysts.
This role will be a hybrid work arrangement primarily at our South Table Mountain campus with some work from home time.
