Job Title: Data Science Intern
FLSA Status: Non-Exempt
Department: IT
Reports To: Senior Data Scientist
Ready to put your data and AI skills to work on real-world challenges? Join our team as a Data Science Intern and gain hands-on experience developing AI-powered
solutions, machine learning applications, and automation tools in a Microsoft Azure environment. You'll collaborate with experienced data scientists, work with
technologies like Python, LLMs, NLP, and computer vision, and help build innovative solutions that make a meaningful impact on our business and customers. If
you're curious, analytical, and eager to learn, we'd love to hear from you.
Position Summary
This individual will participate in a full-time internship program, working 40 hours per week for approximately four months. The intern will contribute to data
science and AI application development projects addressing real-world dental insurance challenges. Projects may include developing AI agents to support business
operations and benefits inquiries, enhancing internal tools, and applying computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), and large language models (LLMs).
The intern will gain hands-on experience in a cloud-based Microsoft Azure environment, with opportunities to build backend services, integrate APIs and data
sources, automate workflows, and develop AI testing and evaluation capabilities. Additional responsibilities may include data preparation, model development,
deployment, data visualization, and other assigned tasks based on business needs and the intern’s skills and interests.
Essential Functions And Job Responsibilities
- The intern may be assigned a wide variety of tasks depending on department workload and priorities, as well as the individual’s strengths, skillset, and
background. Tasks may vary from routine work to complex, multi-step projects. Specific responsibilities include:
- Supporting the development of AI agents that assist with dental operations and member and provider benefits inquiries
- Assisting in building Azure Function Apps and backend services to orchestrate AI workflows and integrate with enterprise APIs and data sources
- Developing automated evaluation tools and test scenarios to assess AI response accuracy, reliability, and adherence to business rules
- Enhancing AI agent capabilities through improvements to prompts, data retrieval, tool integrations, and workflow logic
- Utilizing computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), and large language models (LLMs) to support AI-powered applications and agentic workflows as
required by the projects
- Developing and supporting automated workflows to reduce manual processes
- Collaborating with business users to gather requirements, test functionality, and incorporate feedback into AI applications
- Documenting application workflows, technical components, and evaluation results to support ongoing maintenance and improvement

PyTorch, or OpenCV a plus

- Familiarity with APIs, SQL, data processing, scripting, or backend development preferred
- Familiarity with large language models (LLMs), prompt development, or AI agents preferred; experience with computer vision or natural language processing a
plus
- Interest in developing automated tests and evaluation methods to assess AI accuracy, reliability, and performance
- Familiarity with AI-assisted coding tools such as Claude Code, Codex, or GitHub Copilot preferred, including an understanding of the importance of reviewing
and validating generated code
- Familiarity with Git and collaborative software development practices a plus
- Experience with cloud platforms, particularly Microsoft Azure, and services such as Azure Functions, cloud databases, or workflow automation a plus
- Ability to break down complex problems, research potential solutions, and seek guidance when needed
- Attention to detail and a commitment to accuracy, data privacy, and responsible use of AI
- Strong verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to explain technical concepts to nontechnical audiences
- Motivated with a strong interest in learning new technologies, skills, and tasks
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment
