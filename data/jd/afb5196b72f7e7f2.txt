A Career with Point72’s Market Intelligence Team
On our proprietary research team—Market Intelligence—you’ll support Data Research team members as they work with our investment professionals and Compliance
team to uncover insights about companies, industries, and the broader economy through deep fundamental research and applying data science and engineering
techniques to alternative data sets. You’ll work alongside a talented team with diverse skills, backgrounds, and perspectives.
Our industry researchers, product managers, and data scientists and engineers work together to build compliant research products that answer the questions our
investment professionals care about most. We look for other bright, motivated, and collaborative people to join our team and grow with us.
About the Sector Analyst Internship
As a sector analyst, you will provide industry context and business fundamentals to our team of data scientists and engineers and support the team that delivers
tailored and synthesized insights to our investment professionals.
You should be curious about global industry trends, business models, and supply chains, and enjoy developing deep sector expertise. You should excel at
distilling clear takeaways from complex qualitative and quantitative information—including alternative data—and be energized by an experiment-driven research
approach, shifting priorities, and close collaboration across investment, research, and compliance teams.
During the 10-week program based in our New York office, you will gain exposure to:
- Developing subject-matter expertise on an assigned sector related to key themes and KPIs that drive the industries and companies within your sector
- How data researchers support investment professionals and help inform their investment decisions
- Composing research reports and synthesizing findings
- Collaborating with our other proprietary research teams to source and extract the most value out of new and existing data assets
Summer Internship Program at Point72
In addition to the learning and development you’ll receive in your day-to-day role with your team, as a part of our summer internship program you will:
- Work alongside your fellow interns and be mentored by experienced professionals
- Meet your peers through designated intern programming, volunteer opportunities, and social events
- Attend sessions with senior leaders discussing their expertise and career paths
- Get hands on access to the wealth of tools and resources that Point72 employees use every day
- Gain exposure to groups and functions with unique responsibilities and perspectives on our industry
- Develop technical, writing, and presentation skills
- Participate in networking opportunities across the firm, with groups such as Trading, Finance, Trade Support, Compliance, and Technology
