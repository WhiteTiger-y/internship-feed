About The Team
The State Estimation, Alignment, and Mapping (SEAM) team develops the sensing and spatial understanding capabilities that help autonomous vehicles know where
they are, interpret their surroundings, and maintain reliable operation over time. The team works on sensor fusion, state estimation, real-time localization,
static and dynamic sensor calibration, next-generation mapping, and capabilities that support primary and fallback driving systems.
SEAM combines robotics, computer vision, estimation theory, machine learning, geometry, and production software engineering. Our work supports both onboard,
real-time autonomy and offboard, high-fidelity map and data-generation workflows.
About The Role
As a SEAM Intern, you will work on a well-scoped research or engineering project related to sensing, estimation, localization, mapping, or autonomy reliability.
You will collaborate with experienced engineers and researchers to design algorithms, implement software, evaluate system performance, and understand how
sensing capabilities operate in real-world autonomous driving systems.
Potential focus areas include:
- State Estimation and Sensor Fusion: Combine information from multiple sensors and vehicle signals to estimate position, orientation, motion, and other states
robustly.
- Sensor Alignment and Calibration: Develop methods for static and dynamic calibration, sensor registration, and maintaining alignment as vehicle and
environmental conditions change.
- Localization: Improve real-time localization and map-based positioning across diverse driving environments.
- Next-Generation Mapping: Build or evaluate high-fidelity, large-scale maps that provide useful spatial context beyond the immediate sensor range.
- Fallback and Secondary Capabilities: Develop reliable estimation, localization, or mapping functions that support safe operation when nominal system
capabilities are unavailable or degraded.
What You’ll Do
- Develop, prototype, and evaluate algorithms for sensor fusion, state estimation, localization, calibration, mapping, or related autonomy capabilities.
- Work with camera, lidar, radar, inertial, vehicle, map, and other multimodal data sources.

- Analyze accuracy, robustness, latency, and failure modes across diverse operating conditions.
- Apply software engineering best practices, including design reviews, code reviews, automated testing, continuous integration, and documentation.
- Collaborate with perception, planning, controls, robotics, systems engineering, and vehicle-program partners.
- Communicate technical findings and contribute to design discussions and project documentation.
