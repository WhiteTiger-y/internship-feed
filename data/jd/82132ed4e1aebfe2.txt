Department Details
The AI Design Intern supports the AI Design Hub in developing, testing, and deploying AI-enabled workflows that improve speed, capability, knowledge access, and
decision-making across the Design development process. This is a project-based assignment, not an administrative one. Interns are embedded with designers and
studio engineering, work on real pain points, and are expected to leave having built things that Design can actually use.
What They Will Work On
- AI workflow development: prototype AI agents, automations, and tools built around real Design pain points.
- 3D and geometry automation: support experimentation with AI-assisted 3D creation, surfacing, morphing, Blender workflows, and geometry translation.
- Packaging intelligence: help develop AI-assisted packaging tools drawing on engineering standards, vehicle architecture data, competitive benchmarks, and
Design requirements.
- Design knowledge: organize and structure Design data so AI can retrieve historical programs, standards, lessons learned, and best practices.
- Rapid prototyping: turn ideas from designers and studio engineering into working proof-of-concepts quickly.
- Testing and validation: work directly with designers to test prototypes, document performance, and identify where AI genuinely improves the workflow.
- Value measurement: help quantify time saved, process compression, rework reduction, capacity created, and other FastLane 2030 benefits.
Expected Internship Output
Each intern is expected to finish the internship with two to three working AI prototypes tied to real Design workflows, each with measurable before-and-after
impact and a recommendation for what should scale next.
Each intern is paired with a mentor and works alongside the Hub lead, the AI Portfolio and Execution Lead, regional AI and data leads, and matrixed ICT
partners, with exposure to the AI Design Ambassador network across the Chrysler, Jeep, Dodge, and Ram studios. Project assignment will consider company needs
and student preference, and interns will present their prototypes and results to Design leadership at the end of the program.
Tools And Environment
Interns build inside the Stellantis-approved AI environment, using company platforms rather than personal accounts for company data. The Hub provides:
- Microsoft Visual Studio Code as the primary development environment, with GitHub Copilot for code assistance
- Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio for daily productivity and for building agents
- The Stellantis GenAI Playground, the internal no-code chat environment for testing prompts and ingesting data across ChatGPT (GPT), Claude, and other approved
large language models
- The GenAI Gateway, which gives secure, cost-controlled API access to the GPT, Claude, Mistral, Cohere, DeepSeek, Llama, Nova, and Titan model families
- Stell.AI, the Databricks platform for Python development, machine learning with scikit-learn, PyTorch, and XGBoost, and generative AI through the Gateway or
open-source libraries such as Hugging Face Transformers
- Snowflake for data warehousing, together with Blender and the studio 3D toolchain for geometry and visualization work
