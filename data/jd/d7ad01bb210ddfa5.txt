Job Description
What’s the AI Platform, Enterprise AI Services, and Data Engineering teams?
Our AI Platform, Enterprise AI Services, and Data Engineering teams work together to create the trusted data, governed platforms, reusable intelligence,
engineering patterns, and responsible AI capabilities that support modern technology solutions across Wellmark.
The AI Platform team builds and operates the governed foundation for safe, secure, reliable, and scalable AI. The Enterprise AI Services team builds reusable
domain assistants, agents, shared AI services, and agent skills that can be consumed across products, portfolios, and business areas. The Data Engineering team
builds and supports the pipelines, data products, APIs, services, and lakehouse capabilities that make trusted data available for applications, analytics, and
AI. Interns may contribute across all three areas and learn how trusted data, platform capabilities, and reusable intelligence come together in production-ready
AI solutions.
What projects might I support?
Although projects are subject to change, interns may work together as a small delivery team on a shared, end-to-end project. Depending on project needs and
interests, you may:
- Collaborate with fellow interns, engineers, product partners, and subject matter experts to define a problem, divide work, integrate components, and
demonstrate a working solution.
- Build or enhance data ingestion and transformation pipelines that prepare trusted data for analytics, applications, or AI use cases.
- Work with structured and semi-structured data using lakehouse and medallion concepts, including bronze, silver, and gold data layers.
- Develop APIs, microservices, serverless components, or event-driven workflows that connect data and AI capabilities.
- Support AI platform capabilities that enable the safe, secure, responsible, observable, and scalable use of generative AI, large language models, agents, or
related services.
- Build or enhance reusable enterprise AI services, domain assistants, agents, agent skills, prompts, tools, knowledge integrations, or workflows using approved
platform capabilities.
- Apply data quality, governance, lineage, security, privacy, and access-control considerations throughout the solution lifecycle.
- Create automated tests, monitor solution health, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve reliability and performance.
- Use infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD practices to make cloud deployments repeatable, reviewable, and maintainable.
- Create technical documentation, code samples, architecture diagrams, and best-practice guidance that help other teams adopt the solution.
- Present project outcomes, lessons learned, and recommendations to technical and business stakeholders.
