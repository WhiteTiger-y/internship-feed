Responsibilities for this Position
REPRESENTATIVE DUTIES AND TASKS:
Apply your classroom-learned skills to a program supporting the defense of our country. GDMS has an immediate opening for an Engineering Intern to join our
team. The position provides an opportunity to work on projects that support some of our nation’s fundamental defense services. GDMS employees work closely with
esteemed customers to develop solutions that allow them to carry out high-stakes national security missions.
The selected candidate will work with collaborative teams on various technical projects associated with our core mission. In addition to receiving a competitive
wage, the Engineering Intern will enhance their skill set by working among a talented and technically accomplished group of colleagues. Interns will also enjoy
a flexible work environment where contributions are recognized and rewarded.
Key Responsibilities
Profile source-system data and document its structure, quality, ownership, and authoritative source.
Work with functional subject matter experts to define business objects, properties, relationships, business rules, and approved actions.
Develop ontology mappings for an active AIRE use case and connect semantic definitions to metadata, lineage, provenance, and data-quality requirements.
Help represent the resulting model in a machine-readable form suitable for knowledge graphs, application integration, and agent use.
Produce reusable templates and validation criteria for future business domains.
Minimum Qualifications
Pursuing a degree in Systems Engineering, Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Science, or a related field.
Coursework or project experience in data modeling, database design, systems architecture (MBSE/SysML/UML), or structured information management.
Familiarity with reading and manipulating structured data formats (such as SQL tables, CSVs, JSON, or YAML).
Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to interview stakeholders and synthesize findings into clear technical documentation.
Preferred Qualifications (Nice to have)
Experience with data profiling, data quality, metadata, lineage, ontologies, knowledge graphs, semantic models, Python, SQL, APIs, or AI applications and
agents. Familiarity with business analysis, data architecture, governance, provenance, or validation criteria is desirable.
