Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis
Be a part of our 2027 summer intern cohort! We are currently accepting applications from undergraduate college students who are interested in learning more
about the nation's central banking system while contributing in real ways to our work. As an intern, you'll be matched with a department that can leverage your
skills and experience, and you’ll perform work that helps teams meet their goals—ultimately supporting the organization’s mission. You’ll also have
opportunities to participate in workshops designed for our intern cohort, build your professional network, and gain exposure to a wide variety of careers.
This internship runs for 12 weeks from May 24, 2027, to August 13, 2027. Interns work 40 hours/week and are paid $23.00/hour. This position requires a regular
on-site presence.
Applications open November 1, 2026, and close January 3, 2027 at midnight.
Sponsorship: Individuals who need immigration sponsorship now or in the future are not eligible for this position.
Skills Development:
Interns develop foundational research, data, and communication skills used in economic and community development research. Skill-building may include:
- Investigative research techniques
- Statistical programming in R, Stata, and GitLab environments
- Literature review and data cleaning
- Factchecking and research communication
- Economic and community development research methods
Examples of work performed include:
- Researching options to guide financial education efforts
- Processing and cleaning datasets
- Supporting survey and engagement research, including work involving Native Nations
- Conducting SIPP wealth and debt data analysis
- Creating communications materials and newsletters
- Assisting with housing affordability research and quantitative data projects
Qualifications:
- Currently pursuing a post-secondary degree. You must be enrolled when you apply, but not necessarily during the internship. Open to students graduating
December 2026 or later.
- Excellent oral and written communication skills
- Familiarity with standard office software (e.g., Microsoft Office) and willingness to learn new tools
- Effective interpersonal communication skills
- Demonstrate behaviors that align with our core values such as: Service, Integrity, Inclusion, Initiative, Accountability, and Objectivity
- Some positions require U.S. citizenship
Please note: We do NOT provide relocation assistance, housing, or travel reimbursement.
