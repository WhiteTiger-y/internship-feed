Contract: 12-month paid internship

The Challenge Ahead
Just 13 miles south of Manchester and a 15-minute train journey, is the attractive market town of Wilmslow. Here amongst the boutique shops and independent
cafes and bars is the stunning Playdemic studio. Acquired by EA in 2021, Playdemic is home to the top 10 grossing, BAFTA-winning mobile smash – Golf Clash.
Having opened at the beginning of 2020, our award-winning, bespoke studio space offers conventional desk space as well as indulgent breakout areas, meeting
rooms and alternative workspaces. The office has been ridgeline-designed to create an environment that encourages and allows our team to do their very best work
Responsibilities:
You will report to the Feature Development Manager, working on client feature development, client requests and managing user data
You will gain experience of data architecture for a live operations driven game
You will investigate how to incorporate AI driven improvements for process and code improvements
You will write high quality, well implemented code
Eligibility:
You are currently studying in your penultimate year of an undergraduate Bachelor’s degree with a planned placement year (not a Masters or Graduate student)

Skills:
Knowledge of C
Knowledge of Visual Studio Code
Knowledge of current AI systems (Claude, Co-pilot etc.)
Understanding of relational databases
