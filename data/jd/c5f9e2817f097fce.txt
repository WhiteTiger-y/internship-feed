The ICE Internship Program offers a dynamic opportunity to combine hands-on, impactful work on projects that shape our business, alongside practical career and
professional development. You'll build meaningful connections, strengthen your interpersonal skills, and contribute in ways that truly make a difference.
Beyond your day-to-day responsibilities, you'll participate in weekly sessions led by industry leaders, connect with fellow interns, and deepen your
understanding of the financial services, technology, and mortgage industries.
The Financial Engineering Intern will support data quality analysis on next-generation fixed income analytics and data pipeline projects, while contributing to
broader analytics and product initiatives.
Responsibilities
- Produce historical analysis highlighting current analytic trends across asset types
- Perform data quality analysis on assigned projects, including a next-generation Fixed Income Analytic calculator and data pipeline projects
- Audit and validate financial data in production processes to ensure critical data deliverables are available to clients the following morning
- Establish baseline metrics to give the product team insight into performance results of version upgrades and enhancements
- Participate in new product feature design discussions
Knowledge and Experience
- Must be currently pursuing a Master's degree in Math, Financial Engineering, Mathematical Finance, Computer Science, or Finance
- Excellent quantitative analytic skills
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, or C++ for model development and data analysis, and SQL
- Excellent communication skills
- Experience with data pipelines a plus
- CFA Level 1 or higher preferred
Internship Program Schedule
To accommodate varying academic calendars, the ICE Internship Program offers two schedule options:
- Option 1: May 24, 2027 – August 6, 2027
- Option 2: June 7, 2027 – August 20, 2027
Candidates will be asked to select their preferred session during the application process.
Please Note: Interns must be available to work full-time, five days a week, at our Atlanta office for the entire duration of one of the designated program
schedules. Partial participation or alternate dates cannot be accommodated.
