Job Description At Revelyst Adventure Sports, our purpose is to amplify Impact by bringing together iconic brands on one powerful platform. Through industry-
leading brands including Fox Racing, Bell, Giro, CamelBak, and Blackburn, we create products, experiences, and innovations that inspire people to ride, race,
explore, and push their limits outdoors. We are seeking an IT (Business Intelligence) AI Process Innovation Intern to join our team for a 90-day paid summer
internship beginning June 1, 2027. This intern will support innovation program focused on governed data analysis, reporting, dashboard development, and AI-
assisted business intelligence workflows. The intern will partner with IT, Business Intelligence, business stakeholders, and the Program Administrator to
translate reporting needs into documented requirements, trusted datasets, prototypes, dashboards, and reusable analytical solutions. This position reports to
the Vice President of Transformation and is based out of our Irvine, CA headquarters. As the IT (Business Intelligence) AI Process Innovation Intern, you will
have an opportunity to: Interview business users, analysts, and data owners to understand reporting needs, data sources, metrics, definitions, recurring
analyses, and decision points. Document current-state and future-state reporting workflows, data lineage, business rules, transformations, dependencies, and
access requirements. Assist with data profiling, cleansing, validation, modeling, reconciliation, and preparation using approved systems and non-production or
authorized data. Develop proof-of-concept dashboards, reports, semantic models, queries, AI-assisted analytical workflows, documentation, or lightweight
applications. Identify opportunities where AI can summarize trends, classify requests, generate narratives, detect exceptions, document metadata, or improve
report development while maintaining human review. Partner with IT to follow approved architecture, source-control, data classification, security, privacy,
retention, monitoring, and role-based access requirements. Validate metrics, calculations, filters, data quality, performance, and usability with business and
technical subject matter experts. Create technical documentation, data dictionaries, test results, user guidance, handoff materials, and measurable benefit
statements. You have: Currently pursuing a degree or recent academic experience in information systems, business intelligence, data analytics, computer science,
statistics, engineering, or a related field. Interest in business intelligence, data visualization, analytics, artificial intelligence, automation, and
enterprise problem solving. Working knowledge of spreadsheets, relational data, data visualization, structured requirements, and analytical methods. Ability to
learn new systems quickly and translate business questions into defined metrics, data requirements, and prototype concepts. Strong written and verbal
communication skills with the ability to explain data and technical concepts to non-technical audiences. Strong attention to detail, logical reasoning,
