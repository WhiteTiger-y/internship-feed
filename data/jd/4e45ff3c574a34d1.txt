A Taste of What You’ll Do
Join our dynamic team as a Viticulture or Grape Breeding Research Intern and immerse yourself in the innovative world of wine grape production. We are committed
to fostering an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives drive our success. As an intern, you will play an integral role in advancing precision growing
practices and cutting-edge technologies aimed at enhancing yield, fruit composition, and wine quality. Collaborate with experts from various Winegrowing
Research disciplines, and engage with our internal partners and esteemed collaborators from industry.
We invite individuals passionate about viticulture and grape breeding, who are eager to learn and contribute to the future of wine production, to apply. Embrace
the chance to grow professionally and make a meaningful impact in a field that values innovation and collaboration.
Placement Opportunities
Plant Breeding
- Interns support the characterization of a wide variety of grapevine phenotypic traits including bud fruitfulness, cluster architecture, cluster and berry
size, berry color and canopy structure.
- Tasks include field data collection, collection of tissues for genetic analysis, fruit sampling for maturity and compositional analyses, and the determination
of vine yield components at harvest.
- Interns become familiar with a large number of diverse grape species and their specific attributes utilized for the genetic improvement of wine grape
cultivars.
Viticulture Interns
- Interns assist with a wide variety of vineyard experiments involving irrigation, canopy management, mechanization, spatial variability, and the application of
data analytics for vineyard management.
- Tasks include field data collection, operation of sensors and field instrumentation for measuring vine water status and leaf gas exchange, fruit sampling and
the determination of vine yield components at harvest.
- Interns are exposed to a wide variety of commercial wine grape growing practices, grape varieties, and wine grape production regions in California.
