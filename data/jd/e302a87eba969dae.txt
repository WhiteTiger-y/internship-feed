Data and Analytics Intern (Summer 2027)
About the Role
We're looking for a Data & Analytics Intern to join our Data team and help turn exacare ai's rich operational and clinical data into useful insights, reporting,
and tools for customers and internal teams. You'll work closely with the Data Lead, Product, Sales, and BizOps to build customer-facing dashboards, answer
important business questions, and help identify opportunities to make data and analytics a more valuable part of our product and customer experience.
This role is a great fit for someone who wants hands-on experience at a fast-moving healthcare startup, enjoys working with data and solving ambiguous problems,
and is excited to turn analysis into clear, practical recommendations.
What You’ll Do
- Support the development of customer-facing dashboards, reports, and data-driven materials
- Analyze operational, product, and customer data to identify trends, benchmarks, and opportunities that create value for our customers
- Assist with developing internal analytics use cases that help Sales, BizOps, Product, and leadership make better decisions
- Work closely with customers and cross-functional partners to gather data requirements, validate outputs, and translate business questions into analytical
insights and roadmap opportunities
- Help organize and respond to reporting requests from customers, while identifying opportunities to standardize and improve processes
- Support the Product team in defining reporting needs for new features and evaluating how customers use data and analytics
- Help document data definitions, reporting processes, and best practices so our analytics work can scale
- Take on independent projects in Data & Analytics as you ramp up
What You’ll Bring
- Currently pursuing (or recently completed) a degree in Business, Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, or a related field
- Stellar track record as a top performer in academic and previous experience.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with comfort working in Excel or Google Sheets
- Curiosity about data analytics, product analytics, B2B software, or health tech; prior experience is a bonus, not a requirement
- Experience with, or interest in learning, SQL, BI tools, dashboards, or data visualization
- Ability to communicate data and insights clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences
- Comfort with ambiguity and a bias toward action; you'd rather try something and iterate than wait for the perfect brief
- Detail-oriented, organized, and able to juggle multiple projects at once
- Genuine interest in healthcare, startups, or building things from scratch
Benefits and Perks
- Competitive salary, with the potential to build toward a full-time opportunity at exacare ai
- Hands-on mentorship and the opportunity to work closely with experienced leaders across the business
- Meaningful ownership from day one on projects that directly support our growth
- Hybrid work from our Toronto office at King & Spadina
- A high-achieving, collaborative team including ex-FAANG engineers and alumni of Bain , BCG , Goldman Sachs , and more
- Great startup culture, including team events and company off-sites
