Overview
Research Internships at Microsoft provide a dynamic environment for research careers with a network of world-class research labs led by globally-recognized
scientists and engineers, who pursue innovation in a range of scientific and technical disciplines to help solve complex challenges in diverse fields, including
computing, healthcare, economics, and the environment.
The Microsoft Applied Sciences Group (ASG) incubates disruptive technologies for Microsoft’s next-generation Windows and Devices products. The team works
closely with research and product teams to develop new experiences powered by signal processing, computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning.
We are seeking a Research Intern to work on cutting-edge research in computer vision and deep learning, with a particular interest in image and video
processing, low-level vision, and visual quality enhancement. The Research Intern will have the opportunity to work with researchers and engineers on novel
algorithms, models, and prototypes and contribute to research that can advance the state of the art.
Responsibilities
Research Interns put inquiry and theory into practice. Alongside fellow doctoral candidates and some of the world’s best researchers, Research Interns learn,
collaborate, and network for life. Research Interns not only advance their own careers, but they also contribute to exciting research and development strides.
During the 12-week internship, Research Interns are paired with mentors and expected to collaborate with other Research Interns and researchers, present
findings, and contribute to the vibrant life of the community. Research internships are available in all areas of research, and are offered year-round, though
they typically begin in the summer.
Additional Responsibilities
- Conduct research in computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning, particularly for image and video applications.
- Develop and evaluate novel algorithms and deep-learning models.
- Explore research problems in areas such as image/video enhancement, super-resolution, low-level vision, and visual quality.
- Design experiments, analyze results, and develop research prototypes.
- Collaborate with researchers and product engineers across Microsoft.
- Contribute to publications, patents, prototypes, and technology transfer when appropriate.

Preferred Qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a Doctorate degree program in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related STEM field.
- At least one additional quarter/semester of school remaining following completion of the internship.
- Experience with programming in Python and/or C/C++.
- Knowledge of computer vision, machine learning, or deep learning.
- Research experience in computer vision, deep learning, image processing, or video processing.
- Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or similar deep-learning frameworks.
- Research experience in low-level vision, especially image/video restoration, enhancement, or super-resolution.
- Experience conducting research experiments and analyzing quantitative and qualitative results.
- Publications in major computer vision / machine learning venues are a plus.
