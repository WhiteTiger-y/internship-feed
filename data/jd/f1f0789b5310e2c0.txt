About Lucid
At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-
defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose
between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that

possible and shape the future of mobility.
As an AI & Data intern, you will work alongside data scientists, data engineers, analysts and AI FDEs to build AI and analytics solutions on real data across
Lucid. You must possess a strong understanding of machine learning fundamentals and data analysis. Our ideal candidate exhibits a can-do attitude and is curious
about emerging AI technologies.
Please note: this role sits within Lucid’s AI & Data team. Based on your skills and interests, you will be matched to projects in at least one of the following
focus areas:
- Generative AI and LLM Applications (retrieval-augmented generation, AI agents, and assistants)
- Machine Learning and Data Science (predictive modeling, forecasting, and anomaly detection)
- AI Data Engineering and MLOps (data pipelines, model deployment, and monitoring)
Role And Responsibilities
- Own one or more AI/ML projects from problem definition through a working prototype or production handoff
- Build and evaluate LLM-powered applications, such as retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistants and AI agents, that help teams across Lucid find and use
data and documents faster
- Develop, train, and evaluate machine learning models on real-world vehicle, manufacturing, and business data
- Explore, clean, and transform large datasets using SQL and Python, and engineer features for modeling
- Analyze data and build dashboards and reports that turn model results and business data into actionable insights for stakeholders
- Help deploy models and AI services as APIs or internal tools, and monitor their performance over time
- Partner with data scientists, data engineers, analysts, and business stakeholders to translate their needs into AI solutions
- Document your work and present your results to the team and leadership at the end of the internship
Must Have Skills
- Proficiency in Python and core data science libraries such as pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn
- Working knowledge of SQL and experience exploring and cleaning real-world, messy datasets
- Experience with data analysis and business intelligence (BI), including building dashboards and reports in tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or Looker, and
turning data into clear, actionable insights
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals, including supervised and unsupervised learning, feature engineering, overfitting, and model evaluation
metrics
- Familiarity with Git and collaborative software development practices
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to apply coursework to real-world scenarios
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical results to non-technical audiences
Nice To Have Skills
- Experience building with large language models (LLMs), including prompt engineering, RAG, embeddings, and vector databases
- Familiarity with LLM APIs and frameworks such as OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face, LangChain, or LlamaIndex
- Experience with at least one deep learning framework, such as PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Experience fine-tuning or adapting pre-trained models (for example, Hugging Face Transformers or LoRA)
- Experience evaluating ML or LLM systems, such as building test sets and measuring accuracy, drift, or hallucination rates
- Exposure to big data and orchestration tools such as Spark, Airflow, or Databricks, and to a cloud platform (AWS, Azure, GCP, or OCI)
- Basic model deployment and MLOps skills, such as building REST APIs (FastAPI or Flask), Docker, or experiment tracking with MLflow
- Exposure to knowledge representation techniques such as knowledge graphs
