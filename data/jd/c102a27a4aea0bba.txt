Job Title
2027 CO-OP-Hardcoat Research and Development
Summary
The Hardcoat Research and Development CO-OP will contribute to the growth of Hardcoat Technology through support of NPI programs and Platforms. The Intern will
develop critical structure-property knowledge that can be applied to multiple programs/customers. The CO-OP will investigate the effect of formulation and
processing changes on hardcoat performance, particularly with respect to coating performance failures for thermal cure hardcoat systems on polycarbonate or
metal substrates, glass substrates and other substrates. Additionally, the work could include documentation and standardization of Hardcoat developmental data.
Responsibilities Include
Contributions to Hardcoat Thermal Cure Next Generation Platforms which includes the following focus areas:
- Effect of chemistry and process parameters on chemical resistance, adhesion and cracking.
- Effects of additives to improve adhesion to wider range of substrate types.
- Effects of pigments and matte agents on appearance and performance.
- Development of in-house test methods of importance to Consumer/Industrial space.
- Improvements to performance of metal protection hardcoats
- Improvements to anti-fingerprint performance of hardcoats.
Qualifications
Applicant must be comfortable working in a wet chemistry lab and also with performing physical property testing and characterization:
- Experience with coating processes and test methods
- Experience with polymer chemistry and mechanical property testing on polymeric systems
- Experience with statistical data analysis
- Experience with Microscoft tools (Excel, Powerpoint) and data storage platforms (Sharepoint).
Program Minimum Qualifications
- The candidate must be actively enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate program at the time of application and for at least one semester following the
conclusion of the internship.
- The candidate is expected to be available 40 hours a week for the duration of six months from January 2027 through June 2027 ideally
Education Requirement
- Must be pursuing an MS or PhD in a polymer or materials science program or polymer chemistry program which is at a higher payrate then posting range.
