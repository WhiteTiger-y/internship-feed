per week or as determined by the team. This internship may be considered remote for an individual in the Seattle area. Locations: Sunnyvale, California About

scalable distributed systems. Experience writing Go or other languages commonly used for production services. Understanding of Unix/Linux, SSH, and networking
fundamentals. Able to work full-time, 40 hours per week. Preferred Qualifications: Attention to detail, and a desire to improve processes and systems around
