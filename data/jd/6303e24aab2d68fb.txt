Customer Experience Intern
As a member of the Strategic Operations & Customer Experience team, you will collaborate closely with Reporting Analysts and cross-functional teams to enhance
business performance and support strategic initiatives. This role provides valuable hands-on experience with essential business operations, data analysis, and
system management tools. Your contributions will directly have an impact on the efficiency and effectiveness of customer experience processes, helping to drive
continuous improvement across the organization.
This position will start in May 2027 and conclude at the end of August 2027. This position will be located in Thornhill, ON and is a full-time role working 40
hours a week and, on a hybrid (in office/remote) basis.
Reporting and analytics:
- Execute routine updates and refreshes for various reports across business areas, ensuring data accuracy and timely delivery.
- Implement adjustments and enhancements to existing reports based on stakeholder requests or business needs.
- Utilize Snowflake/SQL for data querying, management, and report generation and visualization in Power BI, supporting the team’s efforts in leveraging data for
business insights.
Customer Experience:
- Assist Reporting Analysts in gathering, cleaning, and organizing customer experience data from various sources.
- Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the Order-to-Cash (OTC) business model.
- Identify trends, anomalies, and insights in customer and operational data for improvement and implementing adjustments to optimize performance
- Provide summarized findings to inform team discussions or presentations.
Communications:
- Communicate with reporting stakeholders to gather feedback, identify areas for improvement in reporting, and implement enhancements to drive greater business
decisions.
- Create and refine PowerPoint decks for presentations, including data visualizations and report summaries to support meetings.
- Participate in cross functional meetings to gain and provide reporting insights
Requirements:
- Currently enrolled in a college or university program working towards a degree in business, data analytics, or a related field.
- Strong communication skills, both written and verbal.
- Detail-oriented with the ability to ensure accuracy in tasks.
- Strong understanding of data analysis and demonstrated experience using tools such as SQL and Power BI through coursework or practical application.
- Ability to translate data and insights into clear, compelling stories tailored to diverse audiences.
- Ability to oversee a continuous improvement initiative by coordinating stakeholders, tracking progress, and supporting implementation.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint).
- Team player with a willingness to learn and contribute.
