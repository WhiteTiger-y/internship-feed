Every day is different as a Business Analyst Intern. You'll work alongside experienced analysts and a dedicated mentor on real data analysis projects that
directly impact business efficiency. You might spend your morning diving into datasets to perform a root cause analysis on an operational challenge, then spend
your afternoon building and refining a performance metrics dashboard to track key business outcomes. You will partner with teams across different geographies,
present data-driven recommendations, and contribute fresh perspectives to solve real business problems.
Amazon is a data-driven business, so no matter which project you own, part of your role will involve turning raw data into actionable insights. You'll identify
process improvement opportunities, support the development of business cases for new programs, and present your findings to senior stakeholders — building your
analytical, technical, and strategic thinking skills in a fast-paced, global environment.
Note: You must have the right to work in the country of employment by the start date.
About the team
Intern Community:
As an Amazon Intern, you'll be part of a dynamic, supportive community that empowers your growth and development. You'll collaborate with interns from around
the world, networking and participating in social events to broaden your perspectives. You can also join specialized groups focused on areas like sustainability
to explore your diverse interests.
Support:
The internship program provides a robust support system, including weekly intern office hours, dedicated IT and HR support, and a program team committed to your
success. This ensures you have the guidance and assistance needed to thrive.
Learning Sessions:
Exclusive learning sessions will further enhance your skills, granting you access to Amazon's industry-leading learning platforms and the expertise of industry
leaders. These structured development opportunities will prepare you for the next step in your career.
Opportunities:
Upon successful completion of the internship, you may be considered for a graduate role or a second internship, allowing you to continue your journey with
Amazon. Additionally, you'll enjoy a competitive monthly salary, relocation support, and access to a range of employee benefits.
Basic Qualifications
- Speak, write, and read fluently in English at C1 level or above
- Knowledge of Microsoft Excel at an advanced level, including: pivot tables, macros, index/match, vlookup, VBA, data links, etc.
- Knowledge of SQL
- Currently in your penultimate or final year and working towards a university bachelor’s or master’s in Business Analytics, Finance, Accounting, Economics,
Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Supply Chain, Logistics or a similar discipline
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience using databases with a large-scale data set
- Experience in prior internships
- Experience defining requirements and using data and metrics to draw business insights
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills, able to collaborate effectively with others.
- Demonstrated curiosity, proactivity, and willingness to take on challenges in an ambiguous, fast-paced environment.
