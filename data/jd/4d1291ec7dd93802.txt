As an AI Acceleration Intern, you will help turn emerging AI capabilities into practical, responsible solutions that support Boston Scientific teams and, the
patients they serve. You will work with partners across engineering, manufacturing, quality, and business functions to identify high-value opportunities;
rapidly prototype applied AI and Generative AI experiences; and translate the strongest ideas into measurable, well-documented workflows. This is a hands-on
builder role with exposure to the people, processes, and technologies that bring medical devices to market.
What You Will Do
- Partner with technical and business stakeholders to frame AI opportunities, clarify user needs, and define success measures such as time saved, quality,
adoption, or decision support.
- Build and demonstrate rapid prototypes using data, APIs, Python, low-code tools, or other appropriate technologies to validate practical value early.
- Contribute to Generative AI assistants, retrieval-augmented experiences, and AI agent or workflow concepts where they are appropriate, with clear task
boundaries and human oversight.
- Analyze operational, engineering, or business data and create clear visualizations, dashboards, or decision-support artifacts that make findings actionable.
- Apply responsible AI practices, including attention to data handling, access controls, privacy, transparency, failure modes, and appropriate human review.
- Collaborate with engineering, manufacturing, quality, digital, and business partners to learn the operating context and communicate technical work in clear,
audience-appropriate language.
- Document prototypes, evaluation results, design decisions, and reusable playbooks so successful approaches can be understood and scaled.
- Share progress through demos and a summer capstone, with regular mentorship, technical feedback, and learning opportunities from cross-functional experts.
