Trex Company, the world’s number one decking and railing brand, and leader in high-performance, low-maintenance outdoor living products, is seeking a Research &
Development Engineering Intern to take part in our 2026 Summer Internship program.
Research & Development Interns Can Expect To
- Assist engineers with routine tasks, including data collection, analysis, and reporting; test equipment setup; and product testing and reporting. Projects may
include the following:
- Analyze data from dimensional measurement equipment for profile extrusion.
- Set up color measurement equipment for use in quality control.
- Assist with product testing for building code requirements, including internal testing and testing at third-party labs.
- Assist with first-article inspection of supplied components.
Requirements
Applicants must be currently enrolled in an accredited engineering program and must have successfully completed at least two semesters of coursework with a GPA
of 3.0 or above.
