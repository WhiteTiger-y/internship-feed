Job Description
Your future in media starts here. The NBCUniversal Summer Internship Program is your chance to help shape what’s next in media and entertainment. Whether you’re
supporting major news coverage, creating content for iconic brands, or contributing to innovative projects, you’ll bring your ideas, creativity, and perspective
to make an impact throughout your internship.
Beyond your day-to-day role, you’ll take part in a thoughtfully designed program focused on career development, building your professional presence, and growing
your workplace confidence. Through real-world experience and a community of fellow interns, you’ll explore what’s possible in this ever changing and exciting
industry.
In addition to all of our internships being paid, we will also offer the following for our Summer 2027 interns:
- Paid company holidays for full-time interns.
- Robust networking, learning and development opportunities.
- Complimentary Peacock subscription during your internship.
- Free admission to Universal Parks to use during your internship.
- Access to mental health resources including counseling sessions.
- Financial assistance to offset expenses for transportation and housing may be available on a needs-based basis for eligible in-person interns. More details
about eligibility for this assistance will be provided at the time of offer.

Operations & Technology, Media Tech : Here at NBCU, we strive to be at the forefront of leading-edge technology and are looking for the next generation of
technologists to join our Media Tech Internship Program.
The Media Tech Internship Program provides students with practical experience across NBCUniversal’s Operations & Technology organization, giving interns the
opportunity to apply their technical skills to real-world projects while gaining exposure to the teams and technologies that power our business.
The internship also serves as a key entry point into our early-career technology pipeline and the primary pathway for consideration into the Media Tech
Associate Program, our two-year rotational program designed to build technical breadth, business knowledge, and professional skills through meaningful work
experiences, mentorship, networking, and development opportunities.
Throughout the internship, students will gain insight into how one of the world’s leading media and entertainment companies uses technology to solve business
challenges, build innovative solutions, and shape the future of media.
Many of our past interns have gone on to join the Media Tech Associate Program. More information can be found at nbcunicareers.com/mediatech/associate-program.
Areas of placement may include, but are not limited to:
- Ad Sales Tech, Ad Smart 2.1: Our team within Data Science works on products and research projects relating to decision optimization, natural language
processing, video processing, ai agents and agentic evaluations. Our suite of products is mostly related to the ad sales space and the workflow from sales
pipeline through to the planning and execution of ads both linear and digital. However, we also work on projects relating to products outside of Ad Sales - like
the Mural NBCU content understanding suite of agents. The intern will receive experience working on one of our applications that utilizes ai agents to either
explore data, explain algorithm decisions, or interact with end users. They will learn the pros and cons of utilizing AI agents vs. when to use more traditional
machine learning and NLP techniques.
- Ad Sales Tech, Ad Smart 2: Our team provides viewership forecasts that support ads planning across NBCUniversal. We partner closely with the Product team,
which serves as our internal customer and is led by SVPs. An intern on our team will gain exposure to forecasting workflows, applied data analysis, engineering
collaboration, and the process of turning analytical work into practical business tools. The internship will also provide an opportunity to contribute to
ongoing efforts to improve our forecasting capabilities, including work related to next-generation AI forecasting approaches.
- Enterprise Product, Finance – Data & Analytics: Our Data & Analytics team supports enterprise technology initiatives focused on modernizing reporting,
analytics, planning, and data capabilities. The team works across SAP and enterprise platforms, with exposure to solutions such as SAP Business Data Cloud
(BDC), SAP Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC), S/4HANA, and enterprise reporting platforms. An intern would gain exposure to data and analytics, reporting
and planning solutions, master data, solution design, and emerging AI/data capabilities.
