Job Description
Internship: Computational Modeling of Polymer Solution Behavior
Location: Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL), Foundational Technologies Directorate
Project Overview
Join a multidisciplinary team at the Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) for a high-impact internship focused on polymer physics. As a PhD-level intern, you
will contribute to the computational polymer physics research team by modeling the rheological behavior of liquid crystal polymers (LCPs) in flow and under
external electric fields. You will work with experimentalists to predict the molecular-level rheological response of LCPs and compare molecular dynamics
simulations to experimental data.
Key Responsibilities
- Leverage molecular dynamics simulations to model the rheological behavior of LCPs.
- Refine simulated models to align with and explain experimental data.
- Actively engage with AFRL scientists, academic collaborators, and industry partners to refine research objectives.
- Document findings and potentially contribute to a manuscript for peer-reviewed publication.
Required Qualifications
- This position involves working within a government facility and requires US citizenship
- Currently enrolled in a Ph.D. program in Polymer Science, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, or a closely related field.
- Experience with coarse-grained polymer modeling
- Proficiency in Python and large-scale simulation software (e.g., LAAMPS).
- Excellent oral and written communication skills.
Preferred Skills
- Experience with modeling rheology of complex fluids and soft matter
- Familiarity with high-performance computing (HPC) environments.
- A record of research contributions in peer-reviewed journals
