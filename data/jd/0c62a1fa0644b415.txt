Job Description:
The Precision & Translational Medicine (PTM) team, part of Oncology Therapeutic Area (OTAU), is on a mission to deliver transformational cures to patients
through innovative translational research and development. By translating scientific innovation into the clinic, we aim to de-risk and expedite pre-clinical and
clinical development, ensuring the right medicines reach the right patients at the right time. The PTM Strategic Innovations (PSI) team drives advancements in
clinical-decision making by integrating cutting-edge technologies into clinical workflows. PSI’s initiatives are grounded in rigorous scientific evaluation and
pilot validation, ensuring that innovations are tested in real-world settings and aligned with organizational goals.
Digital pathology is transforming oncology drug development by enabling high-resolution, AI-powered analysis of histology images. At Takeda, our Digital
Pathology Accelerator (DPA) team is pioneering efforts to extract spatial and cellular insights from H&E and IHC slides using advanced machine learning models.
These models help quantify immune cell populations, automate stain scoring, and correlate tissue features with patient response data. This internship will
explore the development and validation of AI/ML models for digital pathology applications and their expanded use in clinical pipelines.
How You Will Contribute:
As a AI/ML Digital Pathology Imaging Intern, you will have the opportunity to…
- Support development of a reusable end-to-end digital pathology workflow spanning image and metadata preparation, quality control, model deployment,
standardized outputs, and analytics.
- Test and evaluate the end-to-end workflow on independent internal and external datasets and different foundation models to assess performance,
reproducibility, and generalizability across cohorts.
- Design and implement task-specific downstream prediction heads that adapt pre-trained foundation models for translational pathology questions, such as
predicting molecular biomarker status from H&E images.
- Assist in the design and execution of experiments to evaluate and validate AI pathology models and new capabilities, ensuring their efficacy and reliability
for use in clinical trials
- Collaborate with scientists, pathologist subject matter experts, and external partners to interpret machine learning model outputs related to disease patterns
in clinical pathology images.
- Document methods, findings, limitations, and recommendations in technical documentation and communicate results through a final scientific presentation
Internship Development Opportunities:
The intern will learn
- Interpreting AI/ML outputs from clinical pathology images in collaboration with pathology subject-matter experts and understanding their potential
implications for patient diagnosis, treatment, and clinical research.
- Understand the scientific, technical, and operational challenges involved with developing and applying quantitative imaging biomarkers in real-world settings.
- Working in multidisciplinary teams across translational medicine, clinical development, data science, technology, and external innovation functions within a
large pharmaceutical company.
- Help evaluate the translational and clinical utility of AI-powered tissue analytics in precision medicine and oncology drug development.
- Applying structured, data-driven approaches to real-world problems in drug development
