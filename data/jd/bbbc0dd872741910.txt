Your Future Org
Atlassian’s Intern program combines hands-on technical training, professional growth opportunities, dedicated mentorship, and strong social connections. This
holistic approach empowers students to hit the ground running and sets them up for a successful and impactful career at Atlassian!
- This is a hybrid role located in Seattle, WA or San Francisco, CA. You must be willing and able to work out of Seattle or San Francisco to qualify.
This position is not eligible for F1 and J1 students. It is not available for any work sponsorship.
At Atlassian, we build AI/ML products, platforms, and infrastructure that solve business, product, security, and engineering challenges across the company. Our
work spans AI-powered governance and compliance, applied ML systems for recommendation and optimization, and foundational ML platforms, partnering with teams
across Engineering, Product, Growth, Sales, Marketing, and Security. Depending on team placement, you may contribute to governance and compliance intelligence,
recommendation and optimization systems, or platforms for ML development, serving, and observability. You will work with experienced mentors on a focused
project, developing your skills while delivering measurable improvements in quality, reliability, or user outcome.
Responsibilities
Responsibilities
- Build and evaluate applied AI/ML solutions for real-world product and business problems, with mentorship from experienced engineers.
- Contribute to machine learning and Generative AI projects, including models, agents, retrieval, and evaluation workflows.
- Translate business and product needs into well-scoped technical projects, from experimentation and prototyping to working implementations.
- Evaluate and improve AI/ML solutions through experimentation, error analysis, user feedback, and measurable results.
- Communicate findings clearly and document results, trade-offs, limitations, and next steps.
