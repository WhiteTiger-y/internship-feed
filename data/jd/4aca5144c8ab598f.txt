- Job Title: Automotive Creative Design & Research Intern (Summer 2027)
- Employment Type: Internship / Co-op (Full-Time)
- Location: Torrance, CA, United States
About the Role:
American Honda Motor Co., Inc. is seeking an innovative Creative Design and Research Intern for the Summer 2027 session in Torrance, CA. You will collaborate
with vehicle styling, digital modeling, and UI/UX teams to create futuristic vehicle concepts and digital user experiences.
Key Responsibilities:
• Create vehicle styling sketches, digital concept models, and design renderings for upcoming programs.
• Research color, materials, and finish (CMF) palettes and develop physical and digital concept boards.
• Design user experience (UX) and user interface (UI) graphics, motion assets, and digital dashboard interactions.
• Conduct consumer research on North American automotive trends, lifestyle shifts, and mobility desires.
• Collaborate with cross-functional design teams to build physical prototypes and present to design executives.
Required Skills & Qualifications:
• Enrolled in an accredited Bachelor's or Master's degree in Transportation Design, Product Design, or UX/UI.
• Sophomore standing or above with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5.
• Available for full-time work (40 hours/week) for a minimum 10-week summer term.
• Strong creative design portfolio showcasing automotive sketching, 3D digital modeling, or UI/UX design.
• Valid U.S. Driver's License and reliable daily transportation.
