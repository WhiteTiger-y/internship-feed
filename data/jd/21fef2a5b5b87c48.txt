What We're Looking For
The purpose of this internship is to prepare students for entry into the business world by providing a thorough understanding of the various functions of the
Zebra Technologies organization. The program is designed to provide students with experiences that provide insights into a career with Zebra. Students will be
positioned for success with training and hands-on experiences, invited to social and development activities, and provided a professional mentor relationship.
Our team owners the of Software development for all Zebra Handheld Mobile Computers. We handle the Android OS, working with different organizations to ensure
our software meets all requirements. We also create applications to demonstrate our value-added features to help enable sales.

Essential Duties And Responsibilities
- On-Device Telemetry Integration: Interface with Android system APIs and platform frameworks to capture real-time CPU, battery, and thermal sensor metrics.
- Cold-Start Data Collection: Build high-efficiency on-device data logging pipelines to compile structured datasets for machine learning training.
- Multi-Modal Android Demo Apps: Support demo application development by integrating RFID, Vision AI, Barcode Scanner, and Camera into Android applications.
- Autonomous Agentic Systems: Architect LLM-powered agents to automate workflow parsing, extract system insights from unstructured data, and auto-validate
developer API integrations.
- Develop planning, organizational and leadership skills.
- Increase technology knowledge and skills, especially as it relates to Zebra's product portfolio.
- Manage team-based work assignments.
- Practice interpersonal skills to connect with day-to-day business contacts through follow-up activities
Minimum Qualifications
- Current enrollment in an undergraduate (BS/BA) or graduate (MS/MA) degree program in Computer Science or Computer Engineering
- Must have an anticipated graduation date between May 2027-May 2028
- Must be authorized to work in the US without requiring sponsorship now or in the future.
Preferred Qualifications
- Strong Android Systems & Low-Level C++/Java Foundations
- Writing clean Python, C++ and Java/Kotlin code.
- Basic familiarity with Machine Learning concepts and experience using standard libraries (such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn) to train or run simple
models.
- Experience with LLM Orchestration & Agentic Frameworks
- Strong Python skills to quickly deploy supporting agentic infrastructure (e.g., using Streamlit, GCP services, and secure secret handling).
- Communication Skills – Clear and effective verbal and written communication
- Adaptability and Learning Agility – Willingness to learn quickly, embrace new tools or methods, and thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment
- Technical Skills – Basic proficiency in Microsoft Office tools (e.g., Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and basic familiarity with technology solutions)
- Integrity – Commitment to ethical behavior and confidentiality
