Who We Are:
Payments, Orchestrated.
Chattanooga-based Transcard offers comprehensive global payment orchestration solutions for banks, fintechs, and businesses. Our embedded payment solutions
enable seamless transactions, collaboration, and data sharing with customers and suppliers worldwide. Features include disbursements, receivables, account-to-
account payments, cross-border payments, AI insights, and supply chain financing.
Transcard’s solutions combine multi-rail capabilities, embedded workflows, system of record integration, effortless reconciliation, and bank-grade security to
support B2B and B2C payments for businesses of all sizes. Streamline financial operations with digital payments, monitor real-time activity, reduce fraud risk,
and improve customer and vendor experience with an all-in-one payment portal.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
The Artificial Intelligence internship will start with a one-on-one meeting with the Chief Technology Officer or your designated personnel to discuss the
professional goal of your academic coursework to ensure you meet all academic requirements during the internship. Additionally, the internship will be
supervised by the Senior Vice President of Technology to provide routine feedback and status updates. There will be an exit interview with the Chief Technology
Officer and the Director of Human Resources for continual improvement of the program. The Artificial Intelligence internship will provide exposure to a variety
of opportunities, such as: building and testing features for AI-powered applications, participation in sprint/scrum and design reviews for AI projects, and
collaboration with engineering, sales, and marketing teams to translate business needs into AI-driven solutions.
· Learn and apply foundational concepts in artificial intelligence and machine learning through hands-on project work.
· Assist in developing and improving features for the company's AI-powered applications and tools.
· Help troubleshoot and resolve issues within AI-powered applications under the guidance of senior engineers.
· Support testing and quality checks on AI systems and related components to help ensure reliability and safety.
· Assist with reviewing and supporting security and access-related configurations for AI systems.
· Participate in code reviews, sprint planning, and design discussions to gain exposure to the AI development lifecycle.
· Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand how AI features support broader business and customer needs.
Requirements
· Familiarity with Python or another programming language commonly used in AI/ML development.
· Exposure to machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), or large language model (LLM) concepts through coursework or personal projects.
· Basic understanding of version control systems (e.g., Git) and collaborative development workflows.
· Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to troubleshoot and debug technical issues.
· Interest in responsible AI practices, including data privacy, security, and AI safety considerations.
· Ability to learn new tools, frameworks, and systems quickly in a fast-paced environment.
· Preferred: Coursework or project experience involving APIs, cloud platforms, or chatbot/conversational AI development.
· Ability to work remotely and independently from direct supervisor as needed.
· Must have great time management, communication, and organizational skills.
· Proficient in MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
Education and/or Certifications
· Completed or currently working towards a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine
Learning, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
· Preferred coursework: Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Data Structures & Algorithms, Statistics/Probability, Software
Engineering, or Cybersecurity/Information Security.
· Preferred: Computer Engineering Technologies & Technicians.
