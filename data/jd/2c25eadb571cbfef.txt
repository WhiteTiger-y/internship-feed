About GlobalFoundries GlobalFoundries (GF) is a leading manufacturer of essential semiconductors, enabling AI at scale from the cloud to the physical world.
Through deep partnerships with customers, GF delivers differentiated, power‑efficient and high‑performance solutions for high‑growth markets. GF is a trusted
and holistic technology partner for customers around the world. GF’s talented, global team remains focused every day on security, longevity and sustainability.
For more information, visit www.gf.com. Internship Program Overview: Our Interns & Co-ops are our entry-level talent pipeline for GF across the globe. Our goal
is to provide students with a meaningful work experience that will equip them with the skills to embark on a career in the fast-paced and growing semiconductor
industry after graduation. As an intern at GF, you’ll experience one-on-one mentorship, work assignments that prioritize your growth and potential, professional
development opportunities, and the chance to network with executives. Summary of Role: GlobalFoundries is seeking an intern to support the development of AI-
enabled methods for semiconductor device modeling. The intern will combine device physics, measured silicon data, simulation data, and modern AI/ML techniques
to help improve model extraction, predictive accuracy, and scalable modeling workflows for CMOS and emerging devices. Essential Responsibilities include:
Support the development and evaluation of machine-learning methods for semiconductor device modeling, including parameter extraction, surrogate modeling, model
calibration, and prediction of device characteristics. Build reproducible Python-based data pipelines that combine measured device data, compact-model
simulations, TCAD results, and relevant process or test metadata. Train, validate, and compare AI/ML models using appropriate baselines, holdout datasets, error
metrics, uncertainty analysis, and physics-based constraints. Apply methods such as neural networks, Gaussian processes, optimization, sensitivity analysis, and
physics-informed machine learning to device-modeling problems. Assist with automation of model fitting, feature extraction, hyperparameter optimization,
regression testing, visualization, and reporting. Collaborate with device, characterization, compact-modeling, PDK, EDA, and circuit-design teams to translate
technical requirements into usable modeling solutions. Document datasets, assumptions, model architecture, training methods, validation results, limitations,
and recommended usage. Contribute to proof-of-concept demonstrations and present project findings to the technical team at the conclusion of the internship.
Other Responsibilities: Perform all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and
programs. Required Qualifications: Education – Actively pursuing a Master’s or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Data
Science, Applied Physics, Physics, or related field through an accredited degree program during the time of internship. Must have at least an overall 3.0 GPA
and be in good academic standing. Language Fluency - English (Written & Verbal) Travel - Up to 10% Ability to work at least 40 hours per week during the
internship. Preferred Qualifications: Prior related internship or co-op experience Demonstrated prior leadership experience in the workplace, school projects,
competitions, etc. Strong interest in leveraging AI tools and emerging technologies to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and support effective
decision-making. Project management skills, i.e., the ability to innovate and execute on solutions that matter; the ability to navigate ambiguity. Strong
written and verbal communication skills Strong planning & organizational skills Academic or project experience in machine learning, deep learning, statistical
modeling, optimization, or scientific data analysis. Strong Python programming skills and experience with scientific or machine-learning libraries such as
NumPy, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX. Foundational understanding of semiconductor device physics and transistor electrical behavior.
Experience working with structured scientific or engineering datasets, including data preparation, visualization, feature engineering, and reproducible
analysis. Exposure to compact transistor models, SPICE simulation, parameter extraction, TCAD, cryogenic device behavior, photonic devices, superconducting
devices, or quantum hardware. Experience with Linux, Git, experiment tracking, high-performance computing, GPU acceleration, containers, or automated workflows.
