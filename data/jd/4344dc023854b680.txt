Summer 2027 Intern – Enterprise Data - Enablement, Service & Support An internship with American Family Insurance is a great opportunity to build your skillset
and explore your career interests. Our summer student internship is a 10 to 14 week program that starts in mid to late May and ends in August. During your
internship, you'll work on impactful projects that make a difference at our company, with our customers, and in our communities. You'll also gain exposure to
different areas of our business and have numerous opportunities for networking and coaching. Interns may be eligible for a one-time hiring bonus to assist with
housing costs depending upon proximity to an AmFam office location. Compensation Minimum: $25.00 Compensation Maximum: $39.00 The Enterprise Data - Enablement,
Service & Support intern will perform exploratory data analysis, develop scalable and extensible data pipelines, work on cross-functional teams to develop and
deploy data-driven applications and products, assist with prototyping emerging technologies, build and maintain tools to increase the productivity, develop and
automate data quality checks, and develop big data applications and data visualization tools. You will report to the Senior Manager, Engineering.
Responsibilities You will develop an understanding of our business, corporate culture and structure to support your work. You will contribute creative ideas and
strategic approaches in response to changing situations. You will perform exploratory data analysis to determine which questions can be answered effectively
with a given dataset. You will develop highly scalable and extensible data pipelines from internal and external sources. You will work on cross-functional teams
to develop and deploy data-driven applications and products, particularly within the space of data science. You will assist with prototyping emerging
technologies involving data ingestion and transformation, distributed file systems, databases and frameworks. You will build and maintain tools to increase the
productivity of application development and client facing teams. You will develop and automate data quality checks. You will develop big data applications and
data visualization tools. Requirements Must be in student status at an accredited 4-year college or university during the internship, graduating August 2027 or
later. Must be pursuing a Bachelors, Masters, or PhD. Major/program must align with internship role. Knowledge of SQL and experience using a variety of data
stores (e.g. RDBMS, analytic database, scalable document stores). Hands-on programming experience in Python or java, with an emphasis towards building ETL
workflows and data-driven solutions. Working familiarity with Linux scripting to automate workflows. Understanding of database internals, such as indexes,
binary logging, and transactions. Knowledge of cloud computing platforms (e.g. AWS, GCP, Azure). Understanding of Infrastructure as Code (e.g. Docker,
