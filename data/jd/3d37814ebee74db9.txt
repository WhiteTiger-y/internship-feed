The Role
As an AI Engineering Intern, you help build and ship AI applications, agents and platforms for our clients, alongside our AI Applied Engineers, AI Architects
and AI Product Managers.
This is a paid internship in New York for students in a Bachelor's or Master's programme. You work on real client missions alongside our Engineering & Deploy
teams and learn by building.
What You'll Do
- Build features of AI applications and agents with guidance: front end, back end or data.
- Write tested Python code and contribute to evals for model and agent outputs.
- Help build data pipelines, retrieval or platform components.
- Turn prototypes into maintainable code, and document what you build.
- Use AI coding tools daily to ship faster without lowering quality.
What We're Looking For
- You are studying computer science, engineering, data science or a related field.
- You write Python and know the basics of software engineering (Git, tests).
- You have built something with LLMs, agents or RAG, even a side project.
- You explain your technical choices clearly, in fluent English.
You don't need to tick every box to apply. If you match most of these and want to learn the rest, we want to hear from you.
Recruitment process
- HR interview (45 min)
- Technical interview (60 min) with an engineer from your archetype
- System design (60 min) with two engineers from different archetypes
- Hands-on build (60 min) with an engineer from your archetype
- Fit interview (30 min) with a Director
