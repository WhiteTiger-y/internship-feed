The Position
2027 Summer Intern - AI for Drug Discovery (AIDD)
Department Summary
Join Genentech’s AI for Drug Discovery (AIDD) Frontier Research team for a 3–6 month research internship developing new machine learning methods for molecular
design. You will investigate open research questions in generative modeling, with applications to protein and antibody design and the opportunity to tackle
problems relevant to ongoing internal projects.
The project will explore problems related to the generation of molecular sequences and three-dimensional structures. The emphasis will be on developing methods
with broader relevance to machine learning, supported by rigorous computational evaluation.
You will work closely with other ML researchers and collaborate with computational scientists across AIDD. Your goal will be to develop original research
suitable for publication at leading machine learning venues, with the potential for internal application.
This internship position is located in New York, on-site.
The Opportunity
- Formulate and investigate research questions at the intersection of generative modeling, post-training and molecular design.
- Design, implement, train, and evaluate new models and algorithms.
- Develop reproducible experiments, baselines, and ablation studies.
- Collaborate with ML researchers and domain experts to connect methodological advances with meaningful scientific applications.
- Contribute to a research manuscript targeting a machine learning / bio venue.
Program Highlights
- Intensive 6 months, full-time (40 hours per week) paid internship.
- Program start dates are in May/June (Summer).
- A stipend, based on location, will be provided to help alleviate costs associated with the internship.
- Ownership of challenging and impactful business-critical projects.
- Work with some of the most talented people in the biotechnology industry.
Required Education
Who You Are
You meet one of the following criteria:
- Must have attained a Bachelor's degree (recent graduates not currently enrolled in a grad program),
- Must have attained a Master's Degree.
- Must be pursuing a PhD (enrolled student).
Required Majors: Machine Learning, Computational Biology (optional).
Required Skills
- Strong foundations in machine learning, deep learning.
- Experience implementing, training, and evaluating models using Python and a modern deep learning framework such as PyTorch.
- Ability to formulate research hypotheses, design experiments, and interpret results.
- Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work independently on an open-ended research problem.
Preferred Knowledge, Skills, And Qualifications
- Research experience in one or more of the following: generative modeling, post-training, multimodal learning, deep learning.
- Familiarity with generative models (e.g. autoregressive models, diffusion models, flow matching...).
- Evidence of research contributions through publications, preprints, or research projects.
- Interest in applying machine learning to scientific discovery. Prior experience in biology or drug discovery is welcome but not required.
- Complements our culture and the standards that guide our daily behavior & decisions: Integrity, Courage, and Passion.
