Role overview
CarGurus is looking for a highly motivated Data Platform Engineer Intern for the Data Engineering team to support evolving our modern data warehousing platform
to world-class levels! As CarGurus enters the next phase of dramatic growth with new data sources, new business lines, and business acquisitions, we are looking
for a candidate who is interested in learning and being part of revamping how we do data at the company.
As a Data Platform Engineer Intern, you will join an 11-week Summer Internship Program designed to give you hands-on experience, expand your professional
network, and build core industry skills. This full-time opportunity (40 hours per week) runs from June through August 2027. CarGurus operates on a hybrid
working model, and candidates should expect to work out of our Boston, MA office 60% of the time (approximately three days per week).
What you'll do
- Gain a deep understanding of our evolving data and analytics stack
- Understand and follow the decisions around the Data Engineering domain while providing input into strategic decisions around the overall data pipeline process
- Help build robust and scalable data integration (ETL/ELT) pipelines using Fivetran, Python, SQL and Airflow
- Write clean data pipeline code that is easily testable, understood by other developers, and handles errors
- Partner with stakeholders / leads to develop testing
- Collaborate with upstream data producers and downstream data consumers, and maintain a healthy work environment
- Deploy and manage our data infrastructure using Terraform
What you'll bring
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Management, or a related field, with eligibility to complete
a full-time internship.
- Exposure to data engineering and interest in designing and building scalable and reliable data pipelines and infrastructure
- Proficiency with Python programming skills and knowledge of SQL
- Experience working in a cross-functional team environment
- Good understanding of overall data pipeline system architecture and data modeling
- Good problem-solving skills and attention to detail
- Working knowledge of workflow management tools like Airflow, Luigi etc
- Understanding of requirement gathering, analysis, code review, unit and integration testing
- Great teammate, interested in sharing knowledge with other team members, and is curious about learning new technologies
