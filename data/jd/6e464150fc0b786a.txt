Why Altman Solon?
As a student, you're looking for a successful career – and Altman Solon offers you precisely that. You'll work on challenging and impactful projects alongside a
diverse, high-performing team, tackling real-world problems for some of the most innovative companies and investors in the TMT industry.
Benefit from direct mentorship and personalized support to accelerate your development. Our collaborative culture ensures that you'll thrive in a dynamic
environment that fosters professional fulfillment and work-life balance.
For Graduates
The Altman Solon start for graduates
Altman Solon has open consulting roles across our offices that are open to students who have completed their Bachelor’s or Master’s degree with high academic
performance. Ideal candidates have also spent some time abroad, with fluency in English and preferably one other language. This opportunity allows you to
collaborate across a global team, lead client engagements, receive mentorship, and pursue an MBA or Doctoral program with financial support from Altman Solon
after you’ve worked with the firm for at least two years.
Internship Opportunities
Internships at Altman Solon
Altman Solon offers internship opportunities for students pursuing their Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees. Students join the Altman Solon team for two to three
months to support exciting client projects, engage in important business development initiatives, and explore what a career in consulting can look like. Our
internships provide you with mentorship by an experienced consultant, exposure into the business of strategy consulting and the TMT industry, and experience
contributing to client-facing work on o ne or two specific projects alongside a global team.

Americas
The Analyst role is an entry-level position open to undergraduate students who are currently Seniors on track to graduate in December 2026 or spring of 2027 and
Master’s degree students on track to graduate in December 2026 or spring of 2027.
The Summer and Winter Analyst roles are internship positions open to undergraduate students on track to graduate in December 2027 or spring of 2028, and to
Master’s degree students on track to graduate in December 2027 or spring of 2028.
Both entry-level Analyst and Summer/Winter Analyst intern roles will open on Monday, August 10, 2026. Please see below for specific deadlines for applications:
University of Southern California: Tuesday, September 8, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
Yale University: Thursday, September 10, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
University of California, Los Angeles: Thursday, September 10, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
Brown University: Thursday, September 17, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
University of California, Berkeley: Monday, September 14, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
Harvard University: Monday, September 21, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
Dartmouth College: Monday, September 28, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
All other schools: Monday, September 21, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET
Application instructions:
- Include your resume, cover letter, and transcript when submitting your application. Your application will not be considered if it is missing any of these
items.
- Shortly after submitting your application, you will be sent a link to an online assessment. Completion of this assessment is required for your application to
be considered complete. The assessment takes roughly 30 minutes to complete, and part of the assessment is timed. You will need to complete this assessment
within 48 hours of submitting your application, so please plan accordingly.
- Our team will review applications throughout the month of September and will be in touch with those selected to interview.
Summer/Winter Analyst interviews consist of one round: two 1-hour interviews, each featuring an interviewer-led case and a brief behavioral portion.
Analyst interviews include two rounds:
First round: two 1-hour interviews, each with an interviewer-led case and a brief behavioral portion.
Final round: two 1-hour interviews (interviewer-led case and brief behavioral portion) plus one 45-minute, longer-form behavioral interview.
We will extend offers on a rolling basis until the classes are filled.
