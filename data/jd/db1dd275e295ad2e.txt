About The Job
The Chief Operating Office organization supports the execution, technology, data, operating platforms and transformation capabilities that enable State Street
Investment Management to serve its clients and deliver against its strategic priorities.
The State Street Investment Management (SSIM) Data, Analytics & AI Services (DAAIS) is seeking to hire a Data Science Intern at our Boston office. You’ll work
on exciting projects that bring AI solutions to life for our Investment Management business. This is a great opportunity to learn how AI supports technology
platforms and services in a global financial organization.
As part of our team, you’ll gain hands-on experience with modern AI tools and frameworks, contribute to solution design, and learn best practices for AI
modelling and data governance. You’ll be supported by experienced professionals and have the chance to develop technical and problem-solving skills in a
collaborative environment.
What You’ll Do
- Contribute to the design and development of AI and data science solutions addressing real business challenges within investment management.
- Explore and evaluate Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) for potential business applications, including RAG, agentic AI, and other emerging
approaches.
- Assist with building, testing, and benchmarking models and AI prototypes, evaluating factors such as accuracy, quality, performance, and reliability.
- Work with structured and unstructured data to prepare datasets, identify patterns, develop models, and generate actionable insights.
- Collaborate with data scientists, technologists, and business partners to translate business requirements into technical approaches and solutions.
- Document methodologies, assumptions, testing results, and limitations while incorporating Responsible AI, data governance, privacy, and risk considerations
into the development process.
- Own a summer capstone project that applies data science or Generative AI to an investment management business challenge, culminating in a working prototype or
analytical solution, documented evaluation results, and a final presentation of findings and recommendations to business and technology stakeholders.
What You’ll Learn
- How AI, machine learning, and data science are applied to real-world business problems within investment management and financial services.
- How modern Generative AI solutions are designed and evaluated, including LLMs, RAG architectures, agentic workflows, and emerging AI technologies.
- How data scientists move from business problem definition through experimentation, model development, validation, and implementation.
- How to evaluate AI solutions using structured testing and benchmarking, including considerations such as model quality, accuracy, reliability, scalability,
and business value.
- How Responsible AI, data governance, privacy, security, and model risk influence AI development within a highly regulated financial-services environment.
- How to effectively communicate technical findings and recommendations to business and technology stakeholders, culminating in a final project or presentation.
Skills And Qualifications
Required Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field.
- Working proficiency in Python and experience with common data analysis or machine-learning libraries.
- Foundational understanding of machine learning, including model development, testing, validation, and performance evaluation.
- Familiarity with Generative AI and LLMs, including common applications, capabilities, and limitations.
- Experience completing an academic, research, personal, or internship project involving data science, machine learning, AI, or software development.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to learn new technologies quickly and explain technical findings clearly.
Preferred Qualifications
- Hands-on experience with Generative AI or LLM applications, including prompt engineering, model evaluation, or application development.
- Familiarity with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, vector search, or semantic retrieval techniques.
- Exposure to agentic AI and orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI, LlamaIndex, or similar technologies.
- Experience with structured and unstructured database tools such as Snowflake or Databricks.
- Familiarity with ML/deep-learning frameworks.
- Exposure to advanced LLM concepts such as Model Context Protocol (MCP), fine-tuning, tool/function calling, structured outputs, or multi-agent workflows.
- Experience using AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot
- Understanding of Responsible AI, including model bias, explainability, privacy, data governance, security, hallucination risk, and appropriate human
oversight.
