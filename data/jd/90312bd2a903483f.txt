Job Requisition ID # 26WD101097 Autodesk Research – Intelligent Construction Summer 2027 | 12 weeks Position Overview Modern AI systems increasingly depend on
their ability to access and use the right context at the right time. An important research challenge is how large, evolving bodies of information should be
represented to support reliable inference. Autodesk Research provides a distinctive setting for this work. Construction projects are rich, complex information
environments whose state changes continuously over time, making them a compelling domain for studying context and knowledge systems for AI. As a Research
Intern, you will own a focused research problem in this area. You will investigate new approaches to building and retrieving structured context, develop
experimental prototypes, and evaluate how effectively they support downstream AI reasoning. You will work closely with Autodesk researchers and engineers and
have substantial freedom to bring ideas from your own research. Strong contributions may form the basis of an academic publication and future Autodesk research.
Responsibilities • Conduct original research in context and knowledge systems for AI. Investigate methods for representing and retrieving information to support
reliable contextual inference • Develop new approaches to context construction. Explore how relevant knowledge can be identified and assembled dynamically from
large and evolving information environments • Build research prototypes. Implement experimental systems using graph-based, semantic or hybrid approaches and
apply them to realistic project information • Design and conduct rigorous experiments. Develop suitable research questions, baselines and evaluation methods to
understand the strengths and limitations of proposed approaches • Collaborate with Autodesk researchers and engineers. Work across AI research, software
engineering and domain expertise to refine ideas and translate them into experimental systems • Communicate research findings. Present results within Autodesk
Research and contribute, where appropriate, to research papers, technical reports and demonstrations Minimum Qualifications • Currently enrolled in a PhD
program in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Information Science or a closely related field, with at least one academic term remaining
after the internship • Research experience in an area related to knowledge representation, information retrieval, data management or contextual information
systems for AI • Strong programming skills and experience developing experimental research software • Understanding of contemporary AI systems and how retrieval
and contextual information support modern reasoning or agentic workflows • Experience formulating research questions and designing empirical evaluations. •
Strong written and verbal communication skills Preferred Qualifications Research experience in one or more of the following areas would be valuable: • Knowledge
graphs or graph-based information systems • Advanced information retrieval and hybrid retrieval methods • Retrieval-augmented generation, including GraphRAG or
related approaches • Context engineering or memory architectures for AI systems • Semantic and structured knowledge representation • Temporal, provenance-aware
or evolving data systems • Heterogeneous data integration at scale • Graph machine learning applied to representation or retrieval A strong research publication
record in a relevant area is desirable Prior experience with architecture, engineering or construction is not required. We are particularly interested in
candidates with deep technical expertise who are motivated by the opportunity to test their research against a complex real-world information environment. About
the Team Autodesk Research explores technologies that may shape how the world is designed and made. We work across fundamental and applied research, developing
new ideas and testing them against problems grounded in professional practice. The Intelligent Construction team investigates how emerging forms of AI can
support a more connected and adaptive construction industry. An important part of that challenge is understanding how fragmented project information can become
a coherent and evolving source of knowledge for intelligent systems. You will join a multidisciplinary research environment with expertise spanning AI, data
systems, software engineering and construction. You will work closely with experienced researchers while having substantial ownership of your own research
problem. Expected Research Outcome This is a focused 12-week research internship. The research question will be developed with the successful candidate to make
use of their existing expertise while addressing a problem relevant to the team's research direction. By the end of the internship, we expect the intern to have
developed and evaluated an original approach to a clearly defined research problem. This should include a working research prototype, rigorous experimental
