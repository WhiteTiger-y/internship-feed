What's the role?
As an Applied AI Research Intern, you will work with outstanding engineering team of AI Engineers, Data Scientists and Research Engineers to gain hands-on
experience in deriving insight from various data pipeline for the Enterprise organization. You will assist in the design and implementation of algorithms,
analyze, and leverage sensor data for various applications. As part of our dynamic team, you'll have the opportunity to contribute to the design and development
of machine learning and AI models and tools for Geospatial AI innovation and improve product quality across products related to Places POI, Logistics, last-mile
and last-meter delivery, routing and ETA in multi-modal transportation system , Robotics and Physical AI.
Key Responsibilities
- Assist in the research and development of AI models for new product innovation in the area of Physical AI to support last meter navigation for package
delivery.
- Support the analysis of large data sets and translation of machine learning models into code, build POCs and support their integration into production
systems.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis on various automotive sensor data and derive insights for feature engineering and model choices.
- Review on-going applied research activities and publications and convert them to product use-case and design and implement custom methods for products.
Who are you?
You have a strong engineering background, or any quantitative field and you are self-motivated and enthusiastic to research, learning and apply new technologies
to solving problems.
- MS or PhD student in a discipline such as Statistics, Computer Science or Engineering.
- Basic knowledge of Machine Learning, data mining and analytic methods such as regression, classifiers, Clustering, Probabilistic Graphical Models, Deep
Learning and Graph Neural Networks, etc.
- Programming experience with Python, C++, Javascript, SQL, Linux, AWS, NVIDIA, etc.
- Knowledge of foundation models in AI and their application to various downstream tasks. Including LLM, VLM, VLA, Scene Understanding (VQA), BEV and World
Models.
- Experience working on GNSS probe sensor data and computer vision data is a plus
