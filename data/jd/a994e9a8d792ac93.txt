Smart and efficient – Bosch Home Comfort offers its customers throughout the world solutions for their room climate, domestic hot water and decentralised energy
management requirements. For this Bosch Home Comfort applies highly efficient technologies which in many cases use renewable energies and make a significant
contribution to implementing the energy transition.
Bosch Home Comfort, based in Wetzlar and Wernau/Stuttgart, manufactures in more than 120 engineering locations in in Europe, America and Asia and achieved sales
of 5.5 billion euros in 2018. The product portfolio ranges from floor-standing and wall-hung gas and oil boilers and water heaters to solar thermal systems,
heat pumps for heating and cooling and large-scale commercial and industrial systems such as industrial boilers, CHP and waste heat recovery systems for
industrial processes.
Bosch Home Comfort Belgium, situated in Mechelen, is a pure sales country unit that operates under the brands Buderus and Bosch.
