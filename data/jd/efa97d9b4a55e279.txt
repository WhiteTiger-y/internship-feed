The Role
As an AI Research Intern, your mandate is to research and prototype foundation models, both transformer-based and graph-based, applied to OnePay's customer,
transaction, and merchant data. You'll work on real modeling problems and build a working prototype that demonstrates the approach. This role will impact
OnePay's vision by providing a remarkable customer experience, driving positive business outcomes, and improving operational efficiency. You will work closely
with the product, engineering and applied science team(s) working across all business units. This role reports to the Head of AI/ML.
This role is responsible for:
Research, design, and train foundation models on OnePay's data, working with transformer and/or graph-based architectures.
Run experiments, evaluate results rigorously, and turn promising findings into a working prototype.
Opportunity to work in an extremely fast paced growing company
Partner with Product, Engineering and Analytics teams to ensure we are providing a remarkable customer experience.
Driving significant impact on the company’s bottom line.
You Bring

Critical Thinking & Problem-Solving Skills: You enjoy tackling complex challenges, analyzing problems, and developing solutions. Clear communication of ideas is

Organized & Detail-Oriented: You’re a keen listener with strong organizational skills, capable of managing multiple tasks efficiently.
