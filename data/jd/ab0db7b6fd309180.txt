About Our Internship Program
Zoox's internship program offers hands-on experience with cutting-edge technology, mentorship from some of the industry's brightest minds, and the opportunity
to make meaningful contributions to real projects. We seek interns who demonstrate strong academic performance, engagement beyond the classroom, intellectual
curiosity, and a genuine interest in Zoox's mission.
Job Description
The Safety Learnings team within Operational Safety helps keep Zoox's autonomous vehicles safe on the road by turning safety-related events into fast, accurate
decisions. We apply AI to the pipelines that detect, triage, and resolve these events, working at the intersection of Autonomy and Fleet Operations.
As an AI intern, you will own a scoped project from the first prototype to something the team uses. You will be paired with a mentor, work alongside experienced
engineers, and present your results to the broader organization at the end of the summer.
In this Role, You Will…
- Develop AI workflows across Operational Safety that automate manual analysis and speed up how safety events are reviewed.
- Contribute to auto-triage methodologies for safety-related pipelines, helping classify, prioritize, and route events accurately.
- Build a pipeline that detects out-of-distribution scenarios and unhuman-like driving behaviors in vehicle data, so they can be assessed for safety impact.
- Define and test detection criteria, then validate flagged events with engineers and safety analysts to separate real concerns from noise.
- Evaluate your workflows against real safety data, measure accuracy, and refine them based on what you find.
- Present your work in team reviews and a final intern showcase, and hand off documented code the team can build on.
