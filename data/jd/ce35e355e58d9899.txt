Founded in Silicon Valley in 2009 by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, Andreessen Horowitz (aka a16z) is a venture capital firm that backs bold entrepreneurs
building the future through technology. We are stage-agnostic. We invest in seed- to venture- to growth-stage technology companies, across AI, bio + healthcare,
consumer, frontier, enterprise, fintech, games, and companies building toward American dynamism. a16z has $100B+ under management across multiple funds.
We’ve established a team that is defined by respect for the entrepreneur and the company-building process; we know what it’s like to be in the founder’s shoes.
Our team is at the forefront of new technology, helping founders and their companies impact and change the world.
The Role
a16z Frontier research is a multidisciplinary lab that bridges the worlds of academic theory and industry practice. The lab has recently expanded its focus from
crypto-focused research to include other emerging areas of technology, ranging from AI-enabled applications to new scientific developments in materials, energy,
and hardware. In addition to fundamental research, we collaborate with portfolio companies to solve hard technical and conceptual problems. Research interns
will have the opportunity to learn from and collaborate with the firm’s investment and engineering teams.
We are seeking students with a strong technical background and an interest in emerging technologies to join the group for the summer. Example research areas of
interest include AI and machine learning, agentic commerce, cryptography, security, distributed computing, hardware design, economics (micro, macro, and
empirical), energy, material science, biology, incentives, quantitative finance, market and mechanism design, foundations of computing, and pure mathematics.
This list is not exhaustive and we encourage applicants with different backgrounds, who may have unique perspectives on the emerging technologies, to apply.
We anticipate selecting a wide range of applicants, including ambitious undergraduate students with a unique grasp of emerging technologies, graduate students
looking to transition from academic work to building and entrepreneurship, and PhD students pursuing a more traditional academic career.
This role will be based in New York City, NY and requires in-office presence in our New York City, NY office.
To join our team, you should be excited to:
- Pursue fundamental research on topics relevant to the firm.
- Collaborate with a16z Frontier’s full-time researchers and other student interns.
- Collaborate with portfolio companies on technical research problems.
- Quickly develop expertise in emerging areas of science and technology.
- Take full advantage of the latest generation of AI tools.
- Contribute to blog posts, white papers, and other public expository content.
- Meet with visitors from academia and industry and attend research seminars.
Preferred Qualifications
- Pursuing an undergraduate or graduate degree in a relevant field such as computer science, economics, mathematics, operations research, electrical
engineering, mechanical engineering, materials science, physics, chemistry, biology, etc.
- Passionate, knowledgeable, and optimistic about science and technology.
- Experience with absorbing and producing fundamental research.
