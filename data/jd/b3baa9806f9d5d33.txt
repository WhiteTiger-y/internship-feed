Job Overview
We are seeking an enthusiastic and motivated Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning Engineer 2 – Intern to support the State of Texas
This opportunity is part of State of Texas AI Apprenticeship Program, designed to develop emerging AI talent and support the organization's AI Strategic Plan.
The selected candidate will work under the guidance of experienced professionals, contributing to AI/ML model development, data pipelines, prototype
applications, and enterprise application support. The role also involves supporting the OnBase team in developing, configuring, and maintaining enterprise
content management solutions.
Key Responsibilities
AI / Machine Learning Responsibilities
- Assist in evaluating emerging AI trends, tools, and vendor solutions against business requirements.
- Contribute to developing AI/ML models and prototype applications for prioritized business use cases.
- Help design and document data and AI pipelines that integrate with existing enterprise systems.
- Prepare reports, analyses, and presentations to communicate technical findings and project outcomes.
- Collaborate with data, engineering, software development, and governance teams.
- Explore low-risk AI use cases to improve business processes and operational efficiency.
OnBase Application Development & Support
- Assist in developing, configuring, and deploying OnBase solutions, including workflows, forms, Unity scripts, and document management functionalities.
- Collaborate with senior developers and business analysts to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
- Participate in supervised testing, debugging, release validation, and application documentation.
- Provide end-user support and training as required.
- Assist with system performance monitoring, ticket intake, troubleshooting, and resolution of routine OnBase issues.
- Maintain technical documentation, knowledge articles, support procedures, runbooks, and maintenance checklists.
Required Skills & Qualifications
Experience & Education
- Typically 1–3 years of academic, internship, or entry-level experience in AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Coursework toward or completion of a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related discipline.
- Demonstrated interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and applied analytics.
Technical Skills
- Proficiency in Python programming.
- Familiarity with Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) concepts.
- Experience with version control systems.
- Exposure to data analysis, data migration, and basic model development.
- Understanding of fundamental software development and testing concepts.
Preferred Skills
Candidates with exposure or academic experience in the following areas will be preferred:
AI / ML & Cloud Technologies
- Data pipeline tools such as Apache Airflow, Prefect, or cloud-native equivalents.
- Model deployment concepts, including REST APIs and serverless architecture.
- Cloud platforms and AI services: AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI).
- Containerization concepts, particularly Docker.

OnBase & Enterprise Application Support
- Familiarity with OnBase by Hyland, including relevant certifications or completed Hyland training modules.
- Exposure to Document Management Systems (DMS) or Enterprise Content Management (ECM) platforms.
- Experience with Microsoft SQL Server or other relational databases.
- Understanding of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Agile methodologies.
Key Attributes for Success
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
