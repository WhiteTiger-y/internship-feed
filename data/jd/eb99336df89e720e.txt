About Us David Yurman is a celebrated American jewelry company founded in New York by David Yurman, a sculptor, and his wife, Sybil, a painter and ceramicist.
When the artists began collaborating, their goal was simply to make beautiful objects to wear. Led today by their son Evan, David Yurman creates timeless, yet
contemporary collections for women and men defined by inspiration, innovation, consummate craftsmanship and cable – the brand’s artistic signature. David Yurman
collections are available at retail stores throughout the United States, Canada, Hong Kong and France and at locations worldwide, through their exclusive
authorized fine jewelry and timepiece network of retailers. Our Values At David Yurman, creativity, innovation and impeccable craftsmanship are at the core of
everything we do. Fueled by the energy of our hometown, New York City, artistic passion informs each and every jewelry design. The Yurman family’s guiding
vision continually defines and redefines what it means to be the preeminent American luxury jewelry brand. We are committed to responsible business and social
practices and to the protection and advancement of human rights. We believe in partnering with others who share these values and understand their importance.
