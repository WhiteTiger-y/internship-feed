Job Description
The Team:
At GM, we look to move humanity forward. We translate breakthrough technologies into vehicles and experience that people love. It all starts with research and
development.
In the Connected Experience and Agentic System Research lab, we transform the mobility experience by creating integrated solutions for safe and connected
vehicles that our customers love. We are looking for R&D Interns to research, design, and develop groundbreaking technologies in autonomous, connectivity,
vehicle architectures, safety, cybersecurity, user experience, displays and simulation. In this internship, you will plan and execute collaborative research to
generate new knowledge and sophisticated technology for our vehicles. Our culture of "Innovate Now" gives you the opportunity to be creative and implement
designs that could possibly be driving around in the near future!
What You’ll Do:
- Conduct research and/or experiments to collect required data and validate research ideas in the Connected Experience and Agentics System Research Lab (CEASR)
- Document and present results. Depending on the assignment, results could lead to external publications and/or intellectual property
- Execute technical and special assignments related to displays and optics
- Develop and execute specific research plans, including concept generation, development, implementation, and validation
- Collaborate effectively with peers, management, operations groups, and outside organizations
Required Qualifications:
- Pursuit of a Bachelor’s degree in one of the following areas: engineering or science
- Must be a current student
- Able to work fulltime, 40 hours per week during the summer months
- Must be a current PhD student or second year masters student in engineering or science
- Research experience in simulation of ray tracing
- Strong theoretical, experimental and software programming skills
- Ability to work both independently and in teams
- Consistently demonstrate critical thinking and strong teamwork
- Effective verbal and written communication skills
- Excellent persuasion, active listening, delegation, and leadership skills to work effectively with GM internal and external customers
Preferred Qualifications:
- Programming skills in Python or MATLAB
- Modeling Experience in Zemax, Lumerical
- Research experience in one or more of the following topics: free form optics, waveguide, displays, spatial light modulation, holography
- Knowledge of Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Outstanding interpersonal and relationship management skills to effectively collaborate with varying levels of the organization as needed
- Demonstrates leadership in field of study, extra-curricular activities, or service organizations
- Strong critical thinking and analytical skills, especially the ability to synthesize large amounts of data involving multiple variables into comprehensive,
impactful insights
- Ability to work on multiple projects simultaneously, leading deliverables and goals
- Willingness to ask questions, take initiative and be inventive
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to distill sophisticated subjects to a wider audience
- Emerging partnership and teamwork skills and ability to learn from and share knowledge with co-workers in a rapidly moving environment
- High level of integrity, ability to deal with ambiguity, takes initiative/self-directed
- Evidence of strong character with integrity, honesty, accountability and trust
