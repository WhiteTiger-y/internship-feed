Overview:
Revvity’s In Vivo Imaging group develops preclinical instrumentation, software, and imaging solutions used by researchers studying cancer, cardiopulmonary
conditions, metabolic disorders, infectious disease, and other areas of biomedical research. Our interdisciplinary team combines software, engineering, biology,
physics, and imaging expertise to transform new ideas into reliable research tools and workflows.
As a Software Engineering Intern, you will work with the R&D team on a defined project supporting in vivo imaging research or product development. Depending on
project needs, the work may include image processing, 3D image analysis and visualization, scientific data analysis, workflow automation, software prototyping,
or machine-learning experimentation. You will receive guidance from experienced engineers while gaining hands-on experience with professional software-
development practices.
Responsibilities:
- Develop and test software components or prototypes for in vivo imaging applications.
- Assist with scientific image processing, quantitative image analysis, data visualization, or workflow automation.
- Write readable, maintainable code and participate in code reviews.
- Develop unit tests, evaluation scripts, or other tools to assess software performance and reliability.
- Work with imaging datasets to evaluate algorithms, identify issues, and summarize results.
- Investigate technical approaches and present findings and recommendations to the project team.
- Troubleshoot software defects and document technical findings.
- Use version control and the team’s development and project-tracking tools.
- Collaborate with software engineers, scientists, and other members of the interdisciplinary team.
- Prepare technical documentation and provide periodic demonstrations or progress updates.
Basic qualifications:
- Currently enrolled in an undergraduate degree program in computer science, biomedical engineering, electrical engineering, computer engineering, data science,
or a related technical field.
- Completed introductory coursework or equivalent project experience in software development.
- Programming experience in at least one general-purpose language, such as Python, C++, C#, Java, or JavaScript.
Preferred qualifications:
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently on defined assignments and collaboratively within a technical team.
- Coursework or project experience in scientific computing, medical imaging, computer vision, machine learning, data analysis, or visualization.
- Experience with Python and scientific-computing libraries such as NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch, or similar tools.
- Experience with C++ or object-oriented software development.
- Familiarity with image-processing or medical-imaging tools such as 3D Slicer, VTK, ITK, MONAI, or OpenCV.
- Familiarity with 2D or 3D image data, segmentation, quantitative image analysis, or visualization.
- Experience using Git or another version-control system.
- Experience with software testing, debugging, and development environments.
- Demonstrated initiative through coursework, research, open-source contributions, internships, or independent projects.
- Interest in learning the scientific principles and user needs that drive life-science imaging products.
