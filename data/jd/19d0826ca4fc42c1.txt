What we are looking for
We're searching for Summer 2027 Behavior & Planning Interns. You'll work on how the Aurora Driver decides what to do — forecasting how other agents move,
planning and selecting trajectories, and the imitation-learning/reinforcement-learning models behind motion planning.
In this role you will
Develop end-to-end planning or prediction models for the Aurora Driver using imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and/or classical motion planning
Formulate ambiguous driving-behavior problems as concrete ML or optimization problems
Reason about competing objectives (e.g. progress vs. safety) and uncertainty in your model design and evaluation
Work with autonomy/robotics-scale datasets and benchmarks, not just toy datasets
Present a project plan and regular progress updates to the team, working closely with a mentor
Required qualifications
Currently pursuing a Bachelors, Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Robotics, or a related field, with a research or ML focus
Strong ML foundations with hands-on experience in PyTorch, including custom dataloaders, architectures, and loss functions
Some exposure to imitation learning, reinforcement learning, or motion planning
Experience training models on large-scale, non-toy datasets or benchmarks
Ability to reason mathematically about optimization objectives
Awareness of the compounding-error / distribution-shift problem in imitation learning
Desirable qualifications
Publication record at a peer-reviewed venue
Experience with constrained optimization or preference tuning (e.g. RLHF, DPO)
Experience with online reinforcement learning or interactive learning (e.g. DAgger)
