Agios is a full stack research organization within Reality Labs Research, spanning materials and manufacturing, sensors and actuators, simulation, and learned
control. Our mission is to build systems that enhance human capabilities through intelligent machine collaboration. Today that work centers on what we call the
last bottleneck: AI has compressed the digital side of research and development, while the physical side still moves at human speed. Dexterous Manipulation is
the pillar attacking that problem directly, pairing a custom dexterous robotic hand with in-house wearable sensing, high accuracy simulation, and glove-driven
teleoperation.As a Research Scientist Intern, you will work on fast, stable, learned feedback control for multi-fingered robotic hands. Your focus will be
reinforcement learning in high-fidelity simulation, tactile and proprioceptive feedback, and transfer to real robot hands, with mentors who span hardware,
simulation, controls, and policy learning.Our internships are sixteen (12) to twenty-four (24) weeks long and we have various start dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, Dexterous Manipulation and Contact-Rich Control (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Drive an independent research thread on learned reactive control for dexterous hands, from problem formulation through simulation studies and real-robot
evaluation.
- Design controlled experiments and benchmarks that isolate issues with manipulation dexterity.
- Integrate proprioceptive signals into control policies, and close the sim-to-real gap for contact through system identification, domain randomization, and
realistic models of upstream policy error.
- Collaborate across an interdisciplinary team of hardware, simulation, controls, and policy researchers, and communicate research plans, progress, and results.
Minimum Qualifications:
- Research experience in reinforcement learning or learning-based control for robotic manipulation or locomotion, demonstrated through publications, open-source
code, or substantial projects
- Experience with Python, and with a physics simulator such as MuJoCo, Isaac, or Drake
- Must obtain work authorization in the country of employment at the time of hire and maintain ongoing work authorization during employment
Preferred Qualifications:
- Proven track record of achieving significant results as demonstrated by grants, fellowships, patents, as well as first-authored publications at leading
workshops or conferences such as RSS, CoRL, ICRA, IROS, T-RO, IJRR, NeurIPS, ICML, or ICLR
- Experience with sim-to-real transfer of contact-rich, locomotion, or dexterous manipulation policies, including domain randomization, system identification,
and teacher–student distillation
- Experience using tactile sensing, force/torque sensing, or contact estimation in policy learning or control
- Experience in retargeting for locomotion or manipulation
- Grounding in manipulation mechanics and control, such as contact modeling, impedance and admittance control, and trajectory optimization
- Hands-on experience with multi-fingered robotic hands or grippers on real hardware
- Experience working and communicating cross functionally in a team environment
- Availability for minimum 16 consecutive week internship
