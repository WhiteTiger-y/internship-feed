The Program
Our future success depends on the innovation and fresh ideas students bring to HP, Inc. We are hiring students with a diverse set of skills and experiences to
join us, across the different organizations that make us HP, inc. Our intern program is intended to enhance your overall learning experience, give you an
opportunity to make an impact, have some fun, and meet great people along the way.
Join us for a 12-week paid summer internship designed to let you apply your classroom learnings to real world challenges. Our internships have a thoughtful
balance of networking, executive speakers, community involvement, big meaty projects (no paper filing here!) and fun!The AI Applied Engineering Intern will
support the optimization of HP’s Agentic AI ecosystem for edge devices. The intern will focus on improving the inference layer to enhance AI model performance,
efficiency, responsiveness, and deployment on resource-constrained hardware.
The role combines software engineering, machine learning, and system design to evaluate AI workflows, identify performance bottlenecks, and develop practical
optimization solutions. The intern will collaborate with engineering and strategy teams while using AI development tools such as Codex, Copilot, and Claude.
Key Responsibilities
- Optimize AI inference workflows for edge-device environments.
- Analyze latency, memory usage, computational efficiency, and model performance.
- Identify bottlenecks within agentic AI architectures.
- Develop and test software prototypes and optimization approaches.
- Support the integration of AI models and agents with edge hardware.
- Document findings, technical designs, and performance recommendations.
- Collaborate with software, hardware, AI, and strategy stakeholders.
- Use AI-assisted development tools to accelerate coding, testing, and debugging.
Education
- Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Cybersecurity, or a related
technical discipline.
- Has no more than 24 months of prior work experience professionally related to the intern requisition they are applying to (not including internships, teaching
assistance, research work, or military experience)
- Able to obtain work authorization in the United States in 2027, and not require sponsorship in the future.
Skills
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Coding and software development
- AI inference and performance optimization
- Software and systems engineering
- Technical design and prototyping
- Testing, debugging, and performance analysis
- Agentic AI and edge-computing concepts
- AI development tools such as Codex, Copilot, and Claude
- Strong analytical, communication, and collaboration skills
