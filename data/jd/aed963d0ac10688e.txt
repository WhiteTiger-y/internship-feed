About Bobyard
Construction is a multi-trillion-dollar industry facing a shortage of roughly 349,000 workers — and it still estimates by hand. Contractors spend weeks
measuring and counting materials off drawings, and that caps how many jobs they can bid. Bobyard’s AI takes a contractor from a set of plans to a bid in minutes
instead of weeks. Our customers run real construction jobs on Bobyard, not demos. We raised a $35M Series A led by 8VC, with Primary and Pear.
Who We Are

Owners. There’s no layer between you and the problem, and no permission slip required. When you see the solution, you own it, end to end.
Independent minds. Strong convictions, open minds. We hire for how you think, not where you learned it — show us the work, not the résumé.
Fast movers. We decide quickly and optimize for impact, not consensus. You’ll be judged by the people in the problem with you, on what you deliver.
