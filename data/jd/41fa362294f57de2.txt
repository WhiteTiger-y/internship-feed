About the Role:
Our data team is organized across three groups: Data Engineering, Data Science, and Strategic Analytics. Data Engineering owns the warehouse, the orchestration
layer, and the pipelines that move data from our operational systems to everyone who depends on it. This year, with the credit portfolio scaling and our
modeling needs getting heavier, focus areas include real-time event ingestion, the risk decisioning service redesign, AI-agent data access on Redshift
Serverless, ERP/EDI standardization with Finance and Accounting, and AI-assisted workflows across the engineering lifecycle.
As a Data Engineering intern, your main responsibility is to learn, but you'll learn it by doing real work. During the summer of 2027, you'll build data
pipelines, help design tools and infrastructure for analyzing large volumes of data, and work through real data warehousing problems alongside the team that
owns them. You'll partner directly with stakeholders across Risk, Commerce, Marketing, Ops, and Finance, and get hands-on with our full stack, which includes
AWS (Redshift, Glue, ECS, DMS), Fivetran, Airflow, DataHub, Terraform, Python, and SQL.
Our greatest strength is our people, and we’d love for you to be one of them!
What to Expect from the Internship:
Within your first week, you'll:
- Introduce yourself on Slack and meet your team.
- Get oriented in the warehouse, walk through our most-used pipelines with the rest of the team, and read enough of the codebase to start asking useful
questions.
Within your first month, you'll:
- Get fluent enough in our Airflow setup and Redshift conventions to navigate the codebase independently.
- Scope your first real project with a mentor, including the design decisions and trade-offs involved.
- Ship your first commit. It could be a bug fix, a documentation update, or a small improvement.
By the end of your internship, you'll:
- Have shipped a pipeline, tool, or infrastructure improvement that the team relies on after you're gone.
- Have presented your work to the broader team, and walked away with a real sense of what production data engineering looks like day to day.
- Have met with various business owners across the company to build your network and get a feel for how a data team fits into a fast-paced startup.
