Job Description
Role Overview
Join the AI Enablement team within HPE Labs to turn promising AI research and prototypes into dependable, reusable capabilities for enterprise adoption. Work at
the intersection of AI innovation, engineering, and business impact.
We're looking for Master's and PhD students who are curious researchers and hands-on builders, eager to investigate hard problems, code, experiment, learn from
experienced teams, and create tangible impact.
What You'll Do
- Solve real business problems. Explore emerging AI technologies against business challenges, user needs, and opportunities for impact.
- Research, experiment, and build. Turn promising approaches into working solutions using LLMs, generative AI, agentic systems, machine learning, and other
emerging technologies.
- Go beyond prototypes. Help prepare solutions for real-world use, addressing integration, evaluation, guardrails, security, reliability, and operational
requirements.
- Explore the AI lifecycle. Gain hands-on experience in problem framing, experimentation, solution design, evaluation, implementation, and operationalization.
- Build for reuse. Create components, tools, reference implementations, and patterns that accelerate future AI initiatives.
- Collaborate across disciplines. Work with researchers, AI and software engineers, product teams, and business stakeholders to connect technical decisions to
business outcomes.
- Share what you learn. Explain approaches, findings, tradeoffs, and recommendations to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Contribute to innovation. Depending on the project and results, contribute to intellectual property, publications, open-source projects, or other knowledge
sharing.
Who We're Looking For
Graduate students who learn deeply, build quickly, and bring curiosity, strong technical fundamentals, and a drive to turn ideas into working solutions. You
don't need to know everything before you start.
Education & Experience
- Pursuing a Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or a
related technical or quantitative field.
- Applied AI/ML or software engineering experience through coursework, research, projects, open-source contributions, internships, or professional work.
- Experience building AI/ML applications, systems, or prototypes, ideally shown through meaningful projects or GitHub/open-source work.
- We value candidates who combine research with hands-on building, and who can evaluate and explain what they built. Publications are welcome, not required.
Knowledge & Skills
- Strong fundamentals in AI/ML, data science, or software engineering.
- Familiarity with the current AI landscape, including LLMs, generative AI, agentic AI, and related tools and frameworks. Deep expertise is a plus, not a
requirement.
- Python proficiency and experience with relevant AI/ML frameworks and development tools.
- Ability to design experiments, analyze results, and iterate toward working solutions.
- Interest in moving AI experiments toward production-quality capabilities.
- Familiarity with, or eagerness to learn, AI evaluation, responsible AI, guardrails, security, and data privacy.
- Strong problem-solving skills and curiosity about how technology works and why it matters.
- Clear communication and effective collaboration across technical and business backgrounds.
- A builder mindset: make things work, learn from setbacks, and iterate.
What You'll Get From The Experience
Learn how promising AI ideas move from research to production. Work on real initiatives with researchers and engineers, from problem framing and experimentation
through building, evaluation, and preparation for broader adoption.
Deliver a well-researched, reusable, and evaluated solution that the team can continue building on. Connect technical work to business value, learn how
enterprise AI works in practice, and leave behind work others can use and extend.
