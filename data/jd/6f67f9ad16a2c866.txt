Department Overview
The Digital Solutions and Enterprise Applications team develops innovative software platforms that enable business operations, automation, and digital
transformation across Micron. By using modern software engineering practices, AI technologies, and cloud-native solutions, the team delivers scalable
applications that improve productivity, decision-making, and operational efficiency throughout the organization.
Position Overview
As a Software Engineering Intern, you will help design, develop, and support software solutions that address critical business needs across Micron. You will
gain hands-on experience building full-stack applications, AI agents, automation solutions, and enterprise platforms while working with modern development
tools, cloud technologies, and AI-enabled engineering practices.
Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and enhance software solutions that improve business processes, productivity, and operational efficiency.
- Build and integrate AI agents, intelligent automation solutions, and Model Context Protocol (MCP)-enabled applications that support business workflows and
decision-making.
- Develop full-stack applications using modern programming languages, databases, APIs, and web technologies.
- Create monitoring, reporting, and analytics capabilities that provide operational visibility, usage insights, and system health metrics.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement scalable, secure, and reliable software solutions while supporting continuous improvement initiatives and
AI-enabled development workflows.
Minimum Qualifications
- 0-2 years of relevant experience through internships, academic projects, research, software development activities, hackathons, or technical work experience.
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or a
related technical field.
- Experience with one or more programming languages such as Python, C#, Java, C/C++, JavaScript, or similar technologies.
- Understanding of software development fundamentals, object-oriented programming, software architecture, and application design principles.
- Familiarity with web technologies, databases, APIs, version control systems, and modern development environments.
- Demonstrated experience using AI-enabled tools and large language models (LLMs) such as GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or similar technologies to
improve software development productivity and problem-solving.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, written, and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience developing AI agents, AI-assisted workflows, prompt engineering solutions, or generative AI applications.
- Familiarity with Model Context Protocol (MCP), agentic workflows, tool integration frameworks, or AI orchestration platforms.
- Experience with frontend technologies such as JavaScript, Angular, AJAX, CSS, or similar web development frameworks.
- Knowledge of databases including Microsoft SQL Server, MySQL, MongoDB, or comparable platforms.
- Familiarity with Linux environments, containerization technologies such as Docker or Kubernetes, and cloud-native development practices.
- Experience with web servers and application platforms such as Apache, Tomcat, IIS, Node.js, or similar technologies.
- Experience using GitHub, CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, or software delivery workflows.
- Demonstrated interest in applying AI, automation, and emerging technologies to solve business challenges and improve enterprise productivity.
- Strong leadership, communication, and collaboration skills with the ability to work across technical and business teams.
