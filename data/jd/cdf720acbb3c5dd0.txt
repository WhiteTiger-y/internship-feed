The Summer 2027 Enterprise Data Warehouse Systems Undergraduate Internship provides students with an opportunity to gain practical, hands-on experience
supporting enterprise data warehouse and analytics solutions within a large healthcare organization. This internship is designed to develop the next generation
of technology and business professionals through direct exposure to data systems, business analysis, software development, quality assurance, cloud
technologies, and agile project delivery. As a member of the Enterprise Data Warehouse Systems team, the intern will collaborate with Systems Analysts,
Developers, Quality Assurance Testers, Product Owners, and business stakeholders to help deliver high-quality data and technology solutions that support
enterprise reporting, analytics, and business operations. Participants will gain valuable insight into how large-scale data platforms and enterprise systems are
designed, tested, implemented, and maintained. The program provides hands-on training and exposure to multiple career paths within data management, information
technology, business analysis, software development, cloud technologies, and enterprise analytics. Interns will have the opportunity to participate in the full
software development lifecycle while supporting requirements gathering, testing, documentation, problem-solving, and solution delivery activities. Typical
responsibilities include supporting agile project teams, assisting with business and technical analysis, participating in testing activities, performing data
analysis, documenting requirements and processes, supporting quality assurance efforts, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and helping ensure solutions
meet business needs and enterprise standards. Through mentorship, project-based learning, and exposure to modern technologies, participants will strengthen
their analytical, technical, communication, and professional skills while building a strong foundation for a future career in technology and data systems.
