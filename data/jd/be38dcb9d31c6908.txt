The Marvin Group is a Strategic Partner for Global Alternate Mission Equipment and Sustainment
The Marvin Group, a leading defense contractor, plays a crucial role in the development and manufacturing of various systems for military aerospace and
vehicles. Based in Southern California near the Los Angeles International Airport, The Marvin Group consists of Marvin Engineering (MEC), Marvin Test Solutions
(MTS) and Marvin Land Systems (MLS). Marvin Engineering designs and produces military Alternate Mission Equipment. Marvin Test Solutions designs and
manufactures armament Test Equipment. Marvin Land Systems specializes in the design and build of power and thermal management systems. For over 60 years we have
maintained “A Tradition of Excellence”, serving as a trusted source for our unwavering commitment to supporting all branches of the Department of Defense and
our global allies. Our collaboration extends across a diverse array of programs and platforms, including major military prime contractors such as Lockheed
Martin, Northrop Grumman, and Raytheon.
Summary
The selected Intern, will support AI Development, prototyping, building, and shipping useful AI-enabled internal applications. The intern works directly with
users, turns practical workflow needs into maintainable software, and improves solutions through feedback and measured results.
Essential Responsibilities
- Build and iterate on AI-enabled internal web applications from working prototype through reliable release.
- Integrate LLM APIs, structured outputs, retrieval, and tool or function calling where they solve a clear user need.
- Develop application logic in Python and/or TypeScript, with practical front-end, back-end, API, and web-interface work.
- Connect applications to databases, business data, and external services while handling authentication, errors, and data quality.
- Create evaluations and automated tests; review outputs, dependencies, permissions, and data handling with a security-minded approach.
- Work with users to observe results, troubleshoot issues, document systems and decisions, and improve usefulness, reliability, and maintainability.
Required Qualifications
- Must be enrolled or have completed an undergraduate or postgraduate, or accredited technical program and be a Sophomore, Junior, Senior or Postgraduate
student by the start of summer, pursuing Information Technology, Computer Science, or related field.
Preferred Qualifications
- Demonstrated ability to build and ship functioning software applications, including examples that use LLMs or other AI services.
- Working proficiency in Python and/or TypeScript plus core web concepts such as APIs, HTML/CSS, and client-server behavior.
- Experience with SQL or other application databases, data integration, version control, debugging, testing, and deployment workflows.
- Ability to evaluate nondeterministic AI behavior, define acceptance checks, recognize failure modes, and communicate tradeoffs clearly.
- Hands-on familiarity with modern AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code and OpenAI Codex, paired with sound engineering judgment and review of
generated changes.
