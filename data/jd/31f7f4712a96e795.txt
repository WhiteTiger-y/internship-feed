About Our Group
The Photonics & Physical Intelligence (PPI) group at Seagate Research explores next-generation technologies at the intersection of photonics, optics, sensing,
artificial intelligence, and advanced manufacturing. Our team develops innovative optical and photonic systems that can enable future data storage, sensing,
automation, and intelligent physical systems. We combine simulation, laboratory experimentation, and system-level engineering to transform new scientific
concepts into practical technologies with long-term business impact.
We are seeking a highly motivated Photonics Engineering Intern to support the design, modeling, characterization, and experimental validation of advanced
photonic devices and optical systems. This role offers a unique opportunity to work alongside experienced researchers and engineers while gaining hands-on
experience with state-of-the-art photonics tools, equipment, and laboratory techniques.
