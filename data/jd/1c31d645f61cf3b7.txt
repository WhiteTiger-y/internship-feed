About the Roles: Capital Markets Compliance Data Analytics Baird’s Compliance interns work alongside experienced professionals to learn the laws, regulations
and ethical standards that govern the financial services industry while gaining hands-on experience through meaningful projects. The Capital Markets Compliance
team supports Equity Capital Markets, Equity Research, Global Investment Banking, Fixed Income, Public Finance, and firmwide regulatory and policy functions. In
this role, interns will help design, develop and test regulatory technology (RegTech) data solutions while gaining valuable insight into Baird’s Capital Markets
businesses through a compliance lens. Global Investment Banking Data Analytics This Data Analytics Intern will work closely with experienced data and analytics
professionals on Baird’s Global Investment Banking (GIB) business to transform complex data into actionable insights that support senior leadership and
stakeholders. Through hands-on analytics, reporting and data governance work, interns gain practical exposure to enterprise tools and real-world decision-making
in a high-performing financial services environment. This intern will gain firsthand insight into how analytics support Investment Banking strategy and
execution. Private Wealth Management Data Analytics The PWM Analytics & Insights team delivers data-driven insights, reporting solutions, and business
intelligence capabilities that support advisor growth, practice management, and strategic decision-making across Private Wealth Management. In this role,
interns will gain hands-on experience working with data, dashboards, process improvement initiatives, and emerging AI technologies while collaborating with
business partners and stakeholders across the organization. Through meaningful project work and mentorship, interns will develop technical and analytical skills
while gaining valuable insight into how analytics supports business strategy and client-focused growth within the financial services industry. These are hybrid
internships, working 3-4 days per week in our downtown Milwaukee, WI office and 1-2 days remote. The intern must be able to work full time during the summer,
37.5-40 hours per week, and work part time during the school semesters, 15-20 hours per week. The Capital Markets Compliance team is seeking a local student who
can start in January 2027, or earlier if available. The Global Investment Banking and Private Wealth Management teams are seeking a student who can start in
June 2027. The Impact You’ll Make: Work with internal business partners to understand how the data we provide impacts the greater decisions at Baird Partner
