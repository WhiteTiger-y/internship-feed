Baird’s Global Investment Banking (GIB) business to transform complex data into actionable insights that support senior leadership and stakeholders. Through
hands-on analytics, reporting and data governance work, interns gain practical exposure to enterprise tools and real-world decision-making in a high-performing
financial services environment. This intern will gain firsthand insight into how analytics support Investment Banking strategy and execution. This is a hybrid

June 2027. The Impact You’ll Make: Support senior leadership and Investment Banking stakeholders by delivering accurate, well-structured analytics and reporting

business needs and translate them into scalable BI solutions Contribute to data governance, documentation, and quality assurance efforts to ensure trusted
reporting Assist in enabling business users through training, guidance, and self-service reporting support Leverage AI-assisted tools responsibly to improve
analytics workflows and productivity What You’ll Bring to Baird: Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Computer Science or a
related field Anticipated graduation date of May 2028 or later Experience building reports in Power BI Desktop, including foundational DAX Experience writing
