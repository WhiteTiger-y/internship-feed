Job Description
Lazard is one of the world’s preeminent financial advisory and asset management firms. Our people and culture make the difference. While global in presence and
reach, ours is a close, collaborativecommunity of just over 3,000 professionals. Lazard is a place of continuous knowledge sharing, skill development and
relationship building, where professionals grow and succeed together. Our entrepreneurial culture, flat structure and embrace of individual differences, allow
creative ideas, original concepts, and unique perspectives to drive our business forward — and for careers to take flight.
The Quantitative Research Team at Lazard Asset Management applied advanced statistical and computational techniques to address real-world investment challenges.
We combine cutting-edge quantitative methods with deep market insights to drive better investment outcomes for clients.
Our hands-on, 10-week internship is designed for students who are interested in pursuing a career in quantitative research. You will work directly with the
investment professionals on real investment problems where your contributions have measurable impact from day one. You'll be paired with a dedicated mentor
invested in your growth, while gaining direct access to industry veterans and thought leaders who bring decades of market experience to share. As part of a
close-knit team embedded within a global organization, you'll enjoy the best of both worlds: the collegiality and hands-on exposure of a small group, back by
the resources and reach of a world-class firm. It's an environment built to sharpen the technical, analytical, and interpersonal skills that form the foundation
of lasting career in finance or quantitative research.
