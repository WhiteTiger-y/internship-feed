The Position
2027 Summer Intern - Software Engineer (Robotics) - Lab Automation
Department Summary
At Genentech (gRED) and Roche (pRED), we’re applying software, robotics, and advanced AI to solve one of humanity’s hardest challenges: building fully
autonomous, self-healing laboratories for lights-out drug discovery. Advances in AI, data, and computational sciences are transforming drug discovery and
development. The Computational Sciences Center of Excellence (CoE) and the Data and Digital Catalysts (DDC) team are building the foundational platform for
autonomous labs—turning frontier AI models, rich physical telemetry, and multi-vendor robotics into resilient, intelligent systems that run complex science
unattended around the clock to deliver life-saving medicines to patients faster than ever before.
This internship position is located in South San Francisco, on-site.
The Opportunity
The global Lab Automation software team is looking for a highly motivated summer intern to join us in applying physical AI and robotics to bridge the gaps
between automated workflows and truly autonomous laboratories. You will explore how intelligent software and robotic systems can perceive their environment,
reason about changing conditions, and take action in the physical world. Working alongside software engineers and scientists, you will help develop and evaluate
capabilities that make laboratory workflows more adaptable, resilient, and capable of operating with less human intervention.
- Develop software prototypes that connect AI-based reasoning, robotic capabilities, and laboratory instrumentation.
- Integrate robotic hardware, sensors, and software to enable more flexible and coordinated laboratory workflows.
- Explore approaches for recognizing and responding to unexpected conditions that limit laboratory autonomy.
- Test and evaluate integrated systems, with an emphasis on reliable behavior, safe operation, and clear performance measures.
- Collaborate with scientists and engineers to translate laboratory challenges into working demonstrations, reusable code, and technical documentation.
Program Highlights
- Intensive 6 months, full-time (40 hours per week) paid internship.
- Program start dates are in May/June (Summer).
- A stipend, based on location, will be provided to help alleviate costs associated with the internship.
- Ownership of challenging and impactful business-critical projects.
- Work with some of the most talented people in the biotechnology industry.
