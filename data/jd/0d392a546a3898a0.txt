Hawaii. The program offers interns challenging and meaningful work that allows them to demonstrate their abilities, apply knowledge acquired through college
courses, and be evaluated for possible future employment opportunities within Servco. Our internships create opportunities for interns to establish important
relationships with experienced professionals. This is a full-time (40 hours per week) internship program which spans over a period of 8 weeks: June 9 - August
