The ICE Internship Program offers a dynamic opportunity to combine hands-on, impactful work on projects that shape our business, alongside practical career and
professional development. You'll build meaningful connections, strengthen your interpersonal skills, and contribute in ways that truly make a difference.
Beyond your day-to-day responsibilities, you'll participate in weekly sessions led by industry leaders, connect with fellow interns, and deepen your
understanding of the financial services, technology, and mortgage industries.
The Data Science Intern will support the team’s use of AI tools to research and develop quantitative models and explore new data sources that provide insight
into financial markets.
Responsibilities
- Engage with the team to learn about all the tools and data sources
- Meet with business partners and contribute to projects that drive significant value
- Use AI tools to research, prototype, and validate quantitative models for financial data products
- Analyze structured and alternative datasets to uncover relationships with financial markets
- Apply statistical and machine learning techniques to model market behavior and pricing
- Perform additional duties and contribute to projects as assigned
Knowledge And Experience
- Must be currently enrolled in a degree program at an accredited university and be at least advancing to their junior year of study
- Interest in financial markets, with a drive to learn more about them
- Experience with AI tools such as Claude, Codex, or Copilot, with an interest in learning more
- Deep knowledge of Python with SQL
Internship Program Schedule
To accommodate varying academic calendars, the ICE Internship Program offers two schedule options:
- Option 1: May 24, 2027 – August 6, 2027
- Option 2: June 7, 2027 – August 20, 2027
Candidates will be asked to select their preferred session during the application process.
Please Note: Interns must be available to work full-time, five days a week, at our New York (11 Wall Street) office for the entire duration of one of the
designated program schedules. Partial participation or alternate dates cannot be accommodated.
Hourly Pay Rate for New York Candidates
The expected hourly rate for this role is $35 per hour.
