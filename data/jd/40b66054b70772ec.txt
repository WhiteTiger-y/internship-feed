Position Overview:
As an AI & Internal Tools Software Engineering Intern at Inversion, you will help build the software products that enable our engineering and business teams to
move faster, work smarter, and solve complex problems. You will develop internal applications, automation platforms, and AI-enabled tools that directly improve
how we design, manufacture, and operate next-generation spacecraft.
You will join our Internal Software Tools team and take ownership of a major software project, either building a new product from the ground up or developing a
substantial feature in an existing application. You will work directly with engineers and other stakeholders to identify problems, develop solutions, and
deliver software that people across the company use every day.
This is an opportunity to work across the full software stack, from frontend applications and backend services to APIs, data infrastructure, and modern AI/LLM
integrations. We're looking for builders who enjoy turning ideas into real products and are excited about exploring how software and AI can transform the
aerospace industry.
Key Responsibilities
- Own a major software engineering project from initial concept and requirements gathering through design, implementation, testing, and production deployment.
- Design and build full-stack applications, including intuitive user interfaces, backend services, APIs, databases, and integrations.
- Develop AI-enabled tools and intelligent automation systems that streamline engineering and business workflows.
- Integrate large language models (LLMs), AI agents, and other emerging technologies into practical software applications where appropriate.
- Collaborate directly with engineers, operations teams, and other stakeholders to understand their workflows and identify opportunities for improvement.
- Gather feedback from real users, iterate on solutions, and deliver polished, production-quality tools.

- Experience building software applications using one or more languages such as TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, Go, or Rust.
