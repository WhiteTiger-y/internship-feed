Are you ready to turn your classroom knowledge into real-world experience? Do you enjoy collaborating with others and working as part of a team? If so, the
Federal Home Loan Bank of Indianapolis (FHLBank) invites you to apply for our 2027 Summer Internship Program! We’re looking for well-rounded, motivated students
to join us for a 10-week paid internship where you’ll gain hands-on technical experience in your field of study, opportunities to develop key professional
skills, and valuable networking connections to support future career growth. Beyond traditional internship responsibilities, participants join a cohort of 11
interns across multiple departments, engaging in collaborative group activities while also working closely with their assigned teams to build essential
technical and professional competencies. Interns must be located in Indianapolis during the duration of the program and be willing to commit to a hybrid
schedule of 3 days in office/2 days remote each week. What You’ll Experience Our Member Analytics and Operations team is responsible for the administration,
optimization, and strategic support of core business development platforms. They serve as key liaisons between Sales, Marketing, Operations, Analytics,
Information Technology, and other Bank business units to ensure systems effectively support member engagement, relationship management, and data-driven
decision-making. As a Business Intelligence Intern at FHLBank, you’ll: Analyze large datasets to identify trends and generate actionable insights for strategic
decision-making and operational improvements. Develop and maintain reports and dashboards using tools like Power BI to communicate findings to stakeholders.
Perform regular reconciliation tasks between systems and develop methods to streamline and automate those processes. Utilize SQL and data visualization
techniques to support data-driven initiatives. Apply strong problem-solving and communication skills to collaborate across teams and present analytical results
effectively. Contribute to meaningful projects that align with your field of study. Gain exposure to finance, banking, credit, and insurance industries. Utilize
publicly available market and institution data to drive findings about specific market trends regularly, and new trends as they emerge. Participate in team-
building activities, a group project, a community service event, and professional development sessions (including dining etiquette!). Who We’re Looking For
We’re seeking students who: Are actively enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree program. Have completed at least their sophomore year of undergraduate
studies. Graduate students encouraged to apply! Are majoring in Finance, Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Data Science, or Information Systems. Communicate
effectively, both in writing and verbally. Work well independently and in team settings. Are detail-oriented, organized, and eager to solve problems. Are
proficient in Microsoft Excel, preferably with some experience in SQL, Power BI, or Python. Program Highlights 10-week paid internship from June to August.
Flexible hybrid workforce model: Onsite three days a week and two days remote. Competitive hourly rate. “Dress for your day” dress code: You choose the
appropriate work attire based on what your day looks like. Professional development and networking opportunities. Inclusive and collaborative work environment.
