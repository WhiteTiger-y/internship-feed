What You'll Be Doing
As a Deep Learning Scientist Intern, you will work on research, design, and development of large language models, multimodal LLMs, and agentic AI systems. You
will use machine learning and deep learning techniques across language, vision, speech, audio, and video to develop and improve next-generation foundation
models.
- Research and develop large language models, multimodal foundation models, and agentic AI systems across text, vision, speech, audio, and video.
- Develop, train, fine-tune, and evaluate foundation models using supervised fine-tuning, instruction tuning, preference optimization, reinforcement learning,
and related post-training techniques.
- Develop and evaluate capabilities including reasoning, multimodal understanding, planning, tool use, conversational interaction, and multi-step task
completion.
- Build and curate high-quality datasets and evaluation benchmarks for model training, post-training, and performance analysis.
- Prototype new model architectures, algorithms, training strategies, and inference techniques, and collaborate with researchers and engineers to scale
successful ideas.
What We Need To See
- Pursuing an MS or PhD degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Applied Mathematics, or a related field.
- Strong knowledge of machine learning and deep learning techniques, algorithms, and modern foundation model architectures, including Transformers.
- Experience or exposure to large language models, vision-language models, or multimodal models, with an emphasis on reinforcement learning for agentic
reasoning, planning, tool use, and coding.
- Excellent programming skills in Python and experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch, NeMo, Megatron-LM, TensorFlow, or related frameworks.
- Experience developing, training, fine-tuning, or evaluating deep learning models, including familiarity with data processing, dataset development, experiment
tracking, and model evaluation workflows.
- Strong problem-solving skills and a demonstrated ability to rapidly prototype, experiment, and evaluate new ideas.
Ways To Stand Out From The Crowd
- Experience with large language models, multimodal foundation models, vision-language models, or agentic AI research and development.
- Background with post-training techniques such as instruction tuning, preference optimization, RLHF, RLVR, GRPO, or related reinforcement learning methods.
- Experience with multimodal reasoning across text, image, video, speech, or audio, including conversational or audio-language models.
- Experience with reasoning, planning, tool use, function calling, or multi-step agentic systems.
- Background with large-scale model training, distributed frameworks such as NeMo or Megatron-LM, GPU computing, or inference technologies such as vLLM or
