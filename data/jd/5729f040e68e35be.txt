About the team
Explore Robotics Teams at Amazon
At Amazon, robotics is where innovative research meets real-world deployment. There are many different specialized robotics teams, including the few highlighted
below. Learn more about Robotics at Amazon here: https://www.amazon.science/research-areas/robotics
Fulfillment Technologies and Robotics
We build dynamic partnerships between people and intelligent machines. This intricate collaboration helps Amazon fulfill orders with unmatched accuracy. Working
in symphony with our robotic technology, you’ll have the opportunity to extend your technical capabilities by working alongside some of the industry’s most
advanced technologies. This includes our fleet of autonomous mobile robots, sophisticated control software, and technologies like language perception, machine
learning, object recognition, and semantic understanding of commands. These technologies help us deliver an ever-improving customer and employee experience, as
well as improve the safety of our facilities.
Amazon Robotics
Amazon Robotics is at the epicenter of robotics innovation. We design, test, and build mobile robots and mechatronic systems. Our offices, labs, test floors,
and factories are close together, which means we can design the hardware, write the code, test, build, and watch it all come to life. Our areas of focus include
advancements in autonomous mobility, AI, robotic management software, and much more.
Fauna
Fauna is focused on building capable, safe, and genuinely delightful robots for everyday life. Fauna’s goal is simple: make robots people actually want to live
and interact with everyday. Fauna develops tightly integrated hardware and software systems that make it faster, safer, and more intuitive to create real-world
robotic products. Fauna’s work spans the full stack: mechanical design, control systems, dynamic modeling, and intelligent software. The focus is not just
functionality, but overall experience.
Amazon Fulfillment Technologies
Amazon Fulfillment Technologies develops tech solutions that create network optimizations for all Amazon sites worldwide. We own network infrastructure and
devices in all buildings and labor planning and scheduling technology for fulfillment centers in North America and Europe.
