Gallagher is a unique organization defined and made better every single day by our dedicated employees. You will have an opportunity to work in our Risk
Management Services division, focusing on leveraging data to support the division’s goals and business objectives.
How you'll make an impact
- Creating Data Visualization Reports
- Conducting primary and/or secondary research to identify required data to solve a given business challenge and/or opportunity
- Packaging and presenting insights from analysis to clients and/or executive team
- Answering complex questions by generating hypotheses and quantitatively testing them; For example:
- Determining why injured workers at some companies return to work faster than at others, through analysis of data on hundreds of thousands of injured workers
- Identifying opportunities for specific companies to get workers back to work faster
- Writing white papers on trends that impact the insurance or workers' compensation industries
- Building financial models to show impact of macro trends on Gallagher and its clients
- Adopting Gallagher’s unique culture, the “Gallagher Way,” on how to ethically serve our external and internal clientele; Participating in various local
community service events
- Assisting in various regional and/or branch projects to support Gallagher’s sales and service initiatives
About You
We're looking for people who have a passion for using data to solve complex business challenges and opportunities.
You'll be able to organize yourself and your work, have the ability to multi-task, and have a passion to build a supportive network.
If you have all that, we know you'll thrive within our growing entrepreneurial Fortune 500 organization!
Requirements
- Sophomores and juniors with an expected graduation date between December 2027 and June 2029
- Availability to participate within program dates: June 7, 2027 - August 6, 2027
- Proficient with Excel and PowerPoint; Access or enterprise database programs
- Experience with Microsoft SQL Server, SSRS, PowerBI, Tableau, or other Data Visualization software a plus
- Unrestricted work authorization to work in the United States. For U.S. employment opportunities, Gallagher hires U.S. citizens, permanent residents, refugees,
and temporary residents. Temporary residence does not include those with non-immigrant work authorization (F, J, H or L visas), such as students in practical
training status. Exceptions to these requirements will be determined based on shortage of qualified candidates with a particular skill. We will require proof of
work authorization.
