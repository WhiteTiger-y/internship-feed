Acuity Inc. (NYSE: AYI) is a market-leading industrial technology company. We use technology to solve problems in spaces, light and more things to come. Through
our two business segments, Acuity Brands Lighting (ABL) and Acuity Intelligent Spaces (AIS), we design, manufacture, and bring to market products and services
that make a valuable difference in people’s lives.
We achieve growth through the development of innovative new products and services, including lighting, lighting controls, building management solutions, and an
audio, video and control platform. We focus on customer outcomes and drive growth and productivity to increase market share and deliver superior returns. We
look to aggressively deploy capital to grow the business and to enter attractive new verticals.
Acuity Inc. is based in Atlanta, Georgia, with operations across North America, Europe and Asia. The Company is powered by approximately 13,000 dedicated and
talented associates. Visit us at www.acuityinc.com .
Job Summary
Are you a driven and talented individual looking for the place to do your best work while developing into an impactful leader? Look no further than the Acuity
Brands Internship Program! The Acuity Brands Internship Program is a 10–12-week experience designed to develop high-potential candidates into marketing leaders
through hands-on experiences that connect interns’ strengths and interests with the needs of the business.
In addition to making an impact through your participation in key projects and transformative processes, you will have an opportunity to mentor and network with
leaders throughout Acuity Brands. Come join Acuity as a Data Science & Business Analyst Intern and gain hands-on experience in data analytics, automation, and
business strategy. This internship provides exposure to modern data tools, cross-functional collaboration, and mentorship from experienced professionals.

Key Responsibilities
Candidates will put analytical skills to the test and roll up the sleeves to complete actions that will contribute to the Digital Commerce Experience team. This
will include:
- Data Science & Insights: Assist in predictive modeling, machine learning, and dashboard creation using Power BI.
- Data Analysis & Market Research: Analyze customer channels, market trends, segmentation, and competitive benchmarking.
- Database & Infrastructure: Work with structured/unstructured data, support pipelines, and ensure data integrity.
- Process Improvement & Automation: Apply Lean/AI tools, automate simple business processes, and use Better Smarter Faster (BSF) Tools.
- Technology & Innovation: Explore tools like Figma, Confluence, Miro, Power BI and Power Automate; evaluate new technologies for business needs.
- Team Collaboration: Partner with sales, marketing, finance, and IT teams; contribute to cross-functional projects and reporting.
This is a fantastic opportunity for someone passionate about supply chain innovation to gain hands-on experience in a dynamic and fast-paced environment.
