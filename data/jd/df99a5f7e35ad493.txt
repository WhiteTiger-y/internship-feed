Job Description
As an Analytics Intern, you’ll join our Analytics team in the summer for 10 weeks in the summer (June 21 2027 to August 27 2027) . You’ll be onboarded with the
global internship cohort in one of our offices and learn the basics of being a Wiser, as well as understand how analytics work here.
During this time, you will work on 1-2 Analytical projects and use them to propose your own ideas to take your team further, doing what’s best for our
customers.
In this role, you will:
Collaborate cross-functionally to shape and build our product
Carry out data analysis using various statistical methods and tools to help us prioritise the most significant changes. All with the help of other senior
analysts that will support you during your journey
Collaborate with other stakeholders in the team and have the opportunity to share your ideas, working on projects that will be the most impactful for our
customers
Growth and Development:
At Wise, we believe in continuous learning and development. You will have access to:
Mentorship / buddy programme: We’ve a Buddy Programme for you to give you as much possible connection with people who know what you’re going through.
Intern Insights Sessions: Our bi-weekly Intern Insights sessions are a core part of your intern experience, designed to give you a comprehensive understanding
of the diverse functions and career paths within Wise.
Qualifications
What you’ll bring:
You are a penultimate year student from a Bachelors or Masters degree potentially in Data Science/Analytics, Computer Science, STEM or
Economics/Business/Finance. However, a degree in a technical field is not a must as our selection process prioritises your skills.
You are able to start a 10 weeks internship from 22nd June 2027
You enjoy using numbers and data visualisation to tell a story
You used coding to solve problems before (SQL, Python, R). That could be during your studies or as part of a personal project you’re passionate about
You’re curious, keen to learn and proactive by nature
You’re open to and value feedback in order to improve
Don’t worry, we don’t expect you to know everything!
