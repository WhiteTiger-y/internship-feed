Job Description
The intern will develop an AI-enabled competitive intelligence capability that collects, organizes, summarizes, and analyzes publicly available information
about cruise competitors and relevant travel-market trends. The intern will research competitor pricing, promotional activity, deployments, itineraries, product
changes, and market developments, then combine those findings with suitable analytical models to identify potential opportunities and risks for revenue
management.
Day to day, the intern will conduct AI-assisted research, evaluate source quality, prepare structured datasets, prototype repeatable research and summarization
workflows, build analytical models, create dashboards, and produce concise executive briefings. The intern will collaborate with Michael Mahoney, Fredrick
Gardner, Revenue Science, Data Engineering & MLOps, Revenue Management, Pricing, Deployment, and other commercial stakeholders to refine requirements and
validate outputs.
By the end of the internship, the student will deliver a working proof of concept, documented competitor-intelligence dataset and workflow, analytical findings,
executive-ready summaries, and recommendations for future production development.
Essential Functions:
- Use AI-enabled research and supporting tools to monitor publicly available competitor pricing, promotions, deployments, itineraries, product changes, and
industry trends.
- Assess the relevance, reliability, and completeness of external sources, documenting citations, assumptions, and known limitations.
- Collect, clean, categorize, and structure competitor and market data for analysis.
- Develop repeatable workflows that automate research, classification, summarization, and reporting where appropriate.
- Build statistical, machine learning, or comparative analytical models to evaluate competitive actions and potential revenue management implications.
- Create dashboards, visualizations, and executive summaries that translate complex findings into clear commercial insights.
- Collaborate with Revenue Science, Data Engineering & MLOps, Revenue Management, Pricing, Deployment, and commercial stakeholders to define and refine
requirements.
- Test model and summary outputs for accuracy, consistency, usefulness, and reproducibility.
- Present project updates, findings, risks, and recommendations to technical and business leadership.
- Document methods and identify opportunities to scale successful prototypes into sustainable production capabilities.
Qualifications:
- Current undergraduate or graduate student pursuing a quantitative, computing, analytical, economics, or business-related degree.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience using generative AI, large language models, copilots, research agents, or information-retrieval tools.
- Coursework or project experience in machine learning, natural language processing, statistics, forecasting, optimization, or econometrics.
- Experience collecting or integrating data from public websites, APIs, research databases, or other external sources.
- Experience with Power BI, Tableau, Streamlit, or another visualization and reporting platform.
- Familiarity with Git, cloud analytics platforms, workflow orchestration, or MLOps concepts.
- Interest in revenue management, pricing, travel, hospitality, cruise operations, or competitive strategy.
Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities:
- Working knowledge of Python, SQL, R, or another analytical programming language.
- Strong research, analytical, and quantitative problem-solving skills.
- Ability to evaluate sources critically and distinguish evidence from assumptions or generated content.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to summarize technical findings for non-technical audiences.
- Ability to work independently, manage priorities, document work, and collaborate across teams.
- Strong attention to detail and commitment to accurate, reproducible analysis.
