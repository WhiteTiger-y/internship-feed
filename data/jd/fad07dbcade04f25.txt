Job Summary
The Data Analyst intern is a member of the pricing analytics team, developing pricing models and tools to enable the business to make data-driven pricing
decisions. The responsibilities include pricing data management, visualization/analytical tool and methodology development, tool maintenance, impact
measurement, and developing/enhancing machine learning models for established and new product pricing recommendations. This position is in Erlanger, KY
(Cincinnati, OH metro area)
The ideal candidate will have:
- knowledge of data science techniques
- ability to drive effective support of data analysis and model management
- ability to communicate technical concepts to a non-technical audience
Job Responsibilities
- Design, develop and maintain data science products, PowerBI visualizations, and dynamic ad-hoc frameworks that help commercial teams with day to day pricing
and sales activity
- Self-starter with continuous improvement mindset
- Translate complex business problems into concrete analysis that help drive actions
- Write optimized code improving efficiency, scalability, and stability
Requirements for this position include:
- Rising Junior/Senior in a bachelor’s program or completed first year of master’s program in Business Analytics, Statistics, Computer Science, Physics, or
related field
- Coursework or internships with exposure to statistical programming and analysis methodologies
- Experience with data visualization and data processing tools (e.g., PowerBI, Python/R, Databricks)
- Demonstrate strong analytical and problem solving skills
- Good communication skills, able to explain something technical with the appropriate amount of detail
Complete additional duties based on the needs of the assigned location.
