About Marvell Marvell’s semiconductor solutions are the essential building blocks of the data infrastructure that connects our world. Across enterprise, cloud
and AI, and carrier architectures, our innovative technology is enabling new possibilities. At Marvell, you can affect the arc of individual lives, lift the
trajectory of entire industries, and fuel the transformative potential of tomorrow. For those looking to make their mark on purposeful and enduring innovation,
above and beyond fleeting trends, Marvell is a place to thrive, learn, and lead. Your Team, Your Impact The rapid growth of cloud computing, AI/ML, generative
AI, 5G wireless, and high-performance data centers is driving unprecedented demand for higher bandwidth, lower power consumption, and efficient connectivity
solutions. Marvell delivers industry-leading semiconductor technologies, including high-speed optical interconnects and transceivers, that power next-generation
cloud, enterprise, and networking infrastructure. Our team leverages large language models (LLMs), agentic AI, machine learning, and cloud-based data platforms
to improve hardware testing, debugging, and performance optimization through intelligent automation and data-driven engineering. What You Can Expect Design and
develop scalable AI infrastructure, cloud-based data pipelines, and backend services to support engineering applications. Build and integrate LLM-powered
applications, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, and multi-agent AI frameworks for intelligent hardware testing, debugging, and automation. Develop
AI-driven test harnesses to automate test execution, evaluate AI agent performance, and validate system reliability. Integrate AI agents with engineering tools,
databases, APIs, and hardware test environments to enable autonomous workflows. Collaborate with AI, software, and hardware engineers to improve system
performance, scalability, and reliability. Gain hands-on experience building production-oriented AI solutions that address real-world semiconductor engineering
challenges. Work with emerging technologies in generative AI, cloud computing, and intelligent automation while contributing to impactful engineering projects.
What We're Looking For Minimum Qualifications: Currently pursuing a bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence,
Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field, with an anticipated graduation date between Fall 2027 and Spring 2028. Proficiency
in Python and familiarity with software development fundamentals. Foundational understanding of AI/ML, LLMs, or generative AI technologies. Familiarity with
databases, APIs, and data processing workflows. Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently and
collaboratively. Preferred Qualifications: Experience with LLM APIs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, or agentic AI frameworks such as
LangChain, LangGraph, or CrewAI. Familiarity with multi-agent architectures, AI agent evaluation, automated test harnesses, or AI workflow orchestration.
Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure and data platforms such as Snowflake or Databricks. Familiarity with backend development, REST APIs,
Docker, or CI/CD pipelines. Experience with machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn. Knowledge of DSP, semiconductor hardware,
