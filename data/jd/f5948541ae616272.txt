Enterprise Business Intelligence Internship
Redlands, CA
Apply
Overview
Turn data into insight that helps teams make better decisions.
As an Enterprise Business Intelligence Intern at Esri, you will work alongside experienced professionals to build analytics and reporting solutions that support
real business decisions. You will gain hands-on experience with tools such as Microsoft Fabric and Power BI while learning how teams move from stakeholder needs
and project scope to validated, production-ready dashboards.
Responsibilities
What You’ll Experience
- Understand stakeholder needs, project goals, and scope.
- Gather, analyze, and document business and data requirements.
- Design potential solutions through mock-ups and prototypes.
- Collaborate with data teams to identify gaps and document data needs.
- Validate results using quality assurance methods.
- Deliver dashboards and reporting solutions that are ready for production use.
Real Projects You May Work On
- Create a dashboard or reporting solution that turns business data into clear, actionable insight.
- Develop mock-ups or prototypes to help stakeholders evaluate a proposed information solution.
- Perform a data gap analysis and document requirements for a reporting or analytics project.
- Test dashboard results, investigate discrepancies, and help improve data quality and usability.
What You’ll Gain
- Hands-on experience with business intelligence, analytics, reporting, and data visualization.
- Practical experience using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and enterprise business systems.
- An understanding of the project lifecycle, from requirements gathering and solution design through quality assurance and delivery.
- Mentorship, feedback, and experience collaborating with business stakeholders and technical teams.
- Connections with fellow interns and professionals across Esri.
Requirements
We’re Excited to Meet Students Who
- Are currently enrolled in a four-year undergraduate, graduate, or PhD program at a university in the United States studying Business Management and
Operations, Finance, or Mathematics graduating in 2027 or 2028.
- For undergraduate students, are in junior or senior standing.
- Have knowledge of at least one application or system such as Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Access, SAP, Oracle, or another ERP system.
- Are curious, collaborative, detail-oriented, and interested in learning through real work.
- Can complete a consecutive, onsite 12-week internship beginning in May or June 2027 in Redlands, California.
- Are authorized to work for any employer in the United States. Visa sponsorship is not available for this posting
Program Timeline
- Applications Accepted:  September 2–December 31, 2026 at 5:00 pm PST
- Interviews:  Students selected for phone interviews will be notified by email on a rolling basis October 2026–January 31, 2027
- Internship:  12 weeks beginning in late May or June 2027
