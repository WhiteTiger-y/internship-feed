TOOLS PROGRAMMER INTERN (GRAPHICS)
What You'll Do
We're looking for a Tools Programmer Intern to evolve artist tools within the Unreal Editor. An important aspect of this role is supporting both user-driven and
agentic workflows, including exploring and implementing new interaction paradigms to accelerate content creation. You'll directly impact artist productivity by
developing user-facing tools for one of the leading game engines used by developers worldwide.
In this role, you will
- Resolve bugs and workflow issues for our internal teams and licensees
- Explore new artist workflows on the intersection of agentic and interactive tools
- Work closely with team members to modify existing and add new features
- Have independence in making and owning implementation decisions
- Be part of an experienced distributed team
What we're looking for
- Strong C++ skills
- Experience in collaborating with other developers
- Demonstrated ability in working on graphics tools, e.g. geometry, texture processing, terrain authoring, procedural generation
- Experience with using agentic coding tools
- Someone who is pragmatic, professional, and has a positive attitude
- Prior experience with Unreal Engine is a plus, but not required
- Fluent written and verbal communication skills in English
This internship has a flexible start date in 2027. Recruitment will be ongoing until teams find an ideal match. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in
the posting location for the duration of the internship. For more information about Epic’s Early Career Program, visit epicgames.com/earlycareers . This is
going to be Epic!
