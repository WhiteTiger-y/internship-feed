Meta Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) is looking for Research Scientist Interns to advance the science of AI alignment and ML systems. Our teams work on making AI
systems more reliable, efficient, more measurable, and better aligned with human intent- spanning reasoning and agents, evaluation and robustness, efficiency,
safety and security, privacy, world modeling, and the societal dimensions of AI governance.In this role you will embed with a world-class, multi-disciplinary
team of research scientists and engineers to pursue an open research question end to end- from problem framing through experimentation to publication. Interns
are expected to drive their own project with mentorship, not execute a predefined task list.Applicants from a broad range of technical backgrounds are
encouraged to apply, including machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, reinforcement learning, statistics, and the social sciences as
applied to AI systems. Our internships are twelve (12) to twenty four (24) weeks long, and we have various start dates throughout the year.
Research Scientist Intern, AI Alignment Responsibilities:
- Develop novel state-of-the-art algorithms and corresponding systems, leveraging various deep learning techniques
- Analyze and improve various aspects of the corresponding algorithms and systems, including efficiency, scalability, stability, fairness, security, and privacy
- Perform state of the art research to advance the science and technology of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence
- Collaborate with researchers and cross-functional partners including communicating research plans, progress, and results
- Publish research results and contribute to research that can be applied to Meta product development
