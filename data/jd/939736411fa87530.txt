Job Description
Research Scientist Intern
Are you a curious and innovative thinker with experience doing machine learning research? Thomson Reuters Labs is seeking a machine learning scientist intern
with experience in natural language processing, information retrieval, and AI agent design and evaluation.
What does Thomson Reuters Labs do?
We experiment, build, and deliver. We support the organization and our customers through applied research and development of new products and technologies. The
Labs team innovates across our core segments in Legal, Tax & Accounting, Government, and Reuters News. We undertake a diverse portfolio of projects, while
investing in long term research for the future.
As a Research Scientist Intern, you will contribute to foundational research focused on building the next generation of machine learning systems that power our
products.
Key Responsibilities
As a Research Scientist Intern, you will:
- Research and Design: Research and design a new method, framework, or evaluation for search, deep research, or document drafting. Your work will build on
state-of-the-art methods in the machine learning literature and focus on making a novel contribution that pushes the boundaries of what's achievable in our
domain.
- Experiment: Run experiments to test your idea using both internal and public data, validating the generality and practicality of your approach.
- Influence and Share: Have the opportunity to publish your research and influence the direction of applied science work within Thomson Reuters Labs. At the end
of your internship, you'll share your experimental findings and recommendations for using your work within internal systems.
Required Qualifications
You are a fit for the Research Scientist Intern role if your background includes most of the following:
- Are currently enrolled in a PhD program in a relevant discipline and have research experience.
- Have published research in top conferences such as ACL, NAACL, EMNIGIR, ECIR, AAAI or other related venues.
- Have expertise in Information Retrieval and Natural Language Processing, including training and evaluating language models and building AI Agents.
- Are versed in the scientific method, have strong statistical thinkesigning and evaluating machine learning experiments.
- Are experienced in one or more programming languages (preferably Python) and have experience working in the Cloud (such as AWS or Azure).
- Have experience with ML/NLP libraries such as PyTorch, TensorFlow,and HuggingFace.
- Are comfortable using AI coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, oring.
- Are experienced working with unstructured datasets and are knowledhniques for data cleaning and processing.
- Have good communication and writing skills and the ability to work collaboratively as part of a team.
- Are experienced using AI to assist you without outsourcing your critical thinking.
