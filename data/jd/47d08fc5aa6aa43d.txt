Stratolaunch accelerates the development of next-generation aerospace and defense technologies through reusable hypersonic flight testing. Our autonomous, air-
launched Talon-A vehicles provide access to operationally realistic flight environments at speeds exceeding Mach 5, delivering critical data that helps
customers validate system performance and advance technologies toward deployment. Our team brings together expertise in engineering, manufacturing, and flight
operations to solve complex challenges and advance capabilities essential to national security.
We are seeking a motivated Business Intelligence & Systems Analyst Intern to support the development of analytics capabilities across Stratolaunch’s enterprise
systems. This role will work with business and technical stakeholders to identify reporting needs, analyze data, and build meaningful reports, dashboards, and
visualizations across ERP, MES, MRO, and related applications.
The intern will gain hands-on experience with business intelligence, data analysis, requirements gathering, data validation, and process improvement in an
aerospace and engineering environment. The ideal candidate is analytical, curious, detail-oriented, and able to translate business questions into clear,
actionable insights.

analytics requirements.
- Analyze data from ERP, MES, MRO, and other business applications to identify trends, gaps, inconsistencies, and opportunities for improvement.
- Assist with data extraction, transformation, validation, reconciliation, and documentation.

- Support the creation of data models, queries, dashboards, and recurring operational reports.
- Document data sources, definitions, assumptions, report logic, and dashboard functionality.
- Participate in requirements gathering, user acceptance testing, report validation, and stakeholder reviews.
- Investigate data-quality issues and coordinate with system owners to identify root causes and corrective actions.
- Assist with establishing consistent reporting practices, data governance standards, and self-service analytics capabilities.
- Present findings and recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Support other business systems, data analysis, and process-improvement initiatives as assigned.
