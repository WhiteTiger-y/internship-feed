Job Description:
Intel Foundry Automation - Data Analytics and Process Group is seeking a motivated and technically curious intern to join our Manufacturing Automation team for
a minimum of 6-months engagement. This role offers hands-on experience in delivering AI/ML solutions driving factory operations improvement and industrial
innovation.

As an AI/ML Manufacturing Automation Intern, you will work directly with our automation development team to support the development, deployment, and
documentation of automation and artificial intelligence solutions in manufacturing environments. This is an excellent opportunity to gain real-world experience
in industrial AI applications while contributing to meaningful projects that impact production efficiency and quality.

Assist in the development and testing of AI/ML models for manufacturing processes;
Support data collection, preprocessing, and analysis
Participate in solution integration and deployment activities;
Technical Support:

Monitor system performance and assist with optimization efforts;
Support user training and knowledge transfer activities
Assist with system maintenance and updates;
Documentation and Communication:
Create and maintain technical documentation for AI/ML solutions;
Develop user guides, process documentation, and training materials;
Assist in preparing project reports and presentations

Understanding of software development lifecycle
