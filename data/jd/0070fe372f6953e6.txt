Introduction
We are seeking a PhD research intern to explore advanced artificial intelligence and machine-learning methods for e-beam defect inspection and SEM image
analysis. The intern will work with a multidisciplinary team to investigate emerging AI technologies and evaluate their potential for semiconductor inspection
and scientific imaging applications.
This internship offers an opportunity to develop innovative AI solutions, conduct applied research, and contribute to future technologies for semiconductor
defect inspection.
Role and Responsibilities
As an AI Research Intern, you will support research initiatives focused on developing and evaluating advanced AI solutions for defect inspection and scientific
imaging applications.
- Conduct research on advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques for semiconductor defect inspection and SEM image analysis.
- Develop, implement, and evaluate proof-of-concept machine learning and deep learning models.
- Design experiments and apply quantitative and qualitative methods to assess model performance.
- Analyze results and evaluate model accuracy, robustness, generalization, and limitations.
- Collaborate with researchers, engineers, and subject matter experts to understand technical challenges and identify innovative solutions.
- Document research methodologies, experimental results, and technical recommendations.
- Contribute to technical reports, publications, presentations, patent disclosures, or other research deliverables as appropriate.
Education and Experience
- Currently pursuing a PhD in Physics, Chemistry, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related technical field.
- Research experience in one or more of the following areas: Machine Learning and Deep Learning Vision-Language Models (VLMs) Computer Vision Image Processing
and Image Analysis
- Experience conducting research projects involving experimental design, model development, and performance evaluation.
- Strong programming skills using Python and common scientific computing or machine learning libraries.
- Experience with data analysis, model validation, and statistical evaluation techniques.
- Publications, research presentations, or open-source contributions are beneficial but not required.
Skills
Working at the cutting edge of tech, you’ll always have new challenges and new problems to solve – and working together is the only way to do that. You won’t
work in a silo. Instead, you’ll be part of a creative, dynamic work environment where you’ll collaborate with supportive colleagues. There is always space for
creative and unique points of view. You’ll have the flexibility and trust to choose how best to tackle tasks and solve problems.
To thrive in this job, you'll need the following skills:
- Apply machine learning and deep learning techniques to complex scientific and engineering problems.
- Develop and evaluate computer vision models for image analysis and defect detection applications.

- Evaluate model accuracy, robustness, explainability, and generalization performance.

- Collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams of researchers and engineers.
- Demonstrate scientific curiosity, critical thinking, and a strong problem-solving mindset.
