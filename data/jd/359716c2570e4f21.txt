About our Emerging Talent Program:
Intuit invests in Emerging Talent because fresh perspectives strengthen our products and teams. The program is backed by a culture that values curiosity and
collaboration, and you'll be supported by dedicated managers and mentors who are invested in your growth. As an intern or co-op, you'll work on meaningful
projects with business impact, many centered on AI and emerging technologies, and contribute to driving decisions. You'll build skills through function-specific
learning opportunities, connect with a global community of interns and co-ops through dedicated programming and social events, and get exposure to senior

Team Overview
Join our Strategy & Planning team as a Business Data Analyst Summer Intern. You'll gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced environment, synthesizing data
across strategic, operational, and financial domains to deliver actionable insights. This is an excellent opportunity for aspiring analysts to learn, grow, and
apply their skills while collaborating with cross-functional teams to accelerate business outcomes.
Responsibilities
- Accelerate Business Outcomes: Translate raw strategic, operational, and financial data into immediate, actionable insights that allow leadership to pivot
quickly and capture growth opportunities.
- Deliver Cross-Domain Insights: Eliminate data silos and accelerate decision-making by synthesizing metrics across our three critical pillars: strategic input
goals, operational performance, and financial allocations.
- Optimize Resources & Velocity: Maximize the impact of our investments and accelerate outcomes by utilizing AI tools to optimize headcount planning, ensuring
resources are instantly directed to our highest-priority initiatives.
- Align Performance to Goals: Bridge the gap between planning and execution by creating cohesive dashboards that give leadership a real-time view of our
velocity and performance against our own team objectives and Intuit's broader business goals.
- Collaborate & Present: Work closely with cross-functional partners to understand data requirements, validate findings, and present clear, data-driven
recommendations to key stakeholders.
Qualifications
- Analytical & Critical Thinking: Strong ability to analyze data, identify trends, and translate complex datasets from financials, operations, and strategy into
actionable insights.
- Structured Problem Solving: Ability to look at a broad problem—from headcount planning to budget tracking—and break it down into structured, measurable
components.
- Communication & Collaboration: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to confidently present findings and collaborate with
stakeholders across different business domains.
- Tech Savvy & AI Familiarity: Quick learner with an interest in or exposure to AI programs, tools, and concepts.
- Academic Enrollment: Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business Analytics, Data Science, Finance, Economics, or a related
technical/analytical field.
- Graduation Date: A graduation date after the internship end date (at least four months post-internship).
- Authorization: Must be legally authorized to work in the US on a full-time basis during the duration of the internship.
- Onsite Presence: Ability to work onsite for a minimum of 3 days per week.
Nice to Haves
- Data Visualization: Experience with data visualization platforms (like Tableau, Power BI, or advanced Excel).
- Predictive Modeling: Familiarity or academic coursework in predictive modeling, forecasting, or statistical analysis.
- Querying Skills: Basic understanding of SQL or other database querying languages.
