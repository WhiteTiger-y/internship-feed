This job posting is for an existing vacancy.
As a member of the Field Operations team, you will work closely with the Senior Reporting and Process Analysts on various tasks that support the efficiency and
effectiveness of our business. This role will offer the opportunity to gain hands-on experience with critical business functions, system and data management
tools.
This position will start in May 2027 and conclude at the end of August 2027. This position will be located in Thornhill, ON and is a full-time role working 40
hours a week and, on a hybrid (in office/remote) basis.
Responsibilities:
Reporting and analytics:
- Communicate with report stakeholders to gather feedback, identify areas for improvement in reporting, and implement enhancements to drive greater business
decisions.
- Execute routine updates and refreshes for various reports across business areas, ensuring data accuracy and timely delivery.
- Implement adjustments and enhancements to existing reports based on stakeholder requests or business needs.
- Utilize Snowflake/SQL for data querying, management, and report generation, supporting the team’s efforts in leveraging data for business insights.
Staffing model:
- Maintain and improve a staffing model that forecasts staffing needs of the Field Operations team based on business growth, attrition, seasonality, workload,
etc.
- Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the staffing model and make adjustments as necessary.
Communications:
- Maintain and update the SharePoint site, ensuring that resources such as standard work, guidelines, and reports are current and easily accessible to team
members.
- Create and refine PowerPoint decks for presentations, including data visualizations and report summaries to support meetings.
Requirements:
- Currently enrolled in a College or University program and working towards a degree in Business, Data Analytics, or a related field.
- Strong communication skills, both written and verbal.
- Detail-oriented with the ability to ensure accuracy in tasks.
- Basic understanding of data analysis or experience using tools like SQL through coursework.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint).
- Team player with a willingness to learn and contribute.
