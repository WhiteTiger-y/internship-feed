Overview
Being good neighbors – helping people, investing in our communities, and making the world a better place – is who we are at State Farm. It is at the core of how
we operate and the reason for our success. We're moving with urgency to become a Next Gen Good Neighbor, with technology that strengthens human connections and
enhances the customer experience. As an employee, you'll spend less time navigating systems and more time applying judgment, empathy, and expertise in the
moments that matter most.
Grow Your Skills, Grow Your Potential
Responsibilities
A Day In The Life Of A Data Scientist Intern
We work with huge volumes of data to Help Life Go Right® for tens of millions of insurance and financial customers.
The P&C Actuarial and Underwriting Modeling and Analytics Department is a place where interns complete innovative, meaningful research in a collaborative
environment focused on developing technical and business skills - a perfect complement to the classroom.
As a Fortune 50 company, we hire the best employees to serve our customers, making us a leader in the insurance and financial services industry. We are seeking
innovative individuals who are self-starters and like being part of a team to develop and implement creative solutions through:
- Data mining and predictive modeling such as regression, forecasting, and machine learning
- Research on emerging industry trends and hot topics
We offer development through extensive training programs, mentoring programs, and leadership.
Qualifications
We are Looking for candidates with:
- MUST be current GRADUATE student who will have completed first year graduate studies by start of the summer internship. Candidates pursuing a Master's degree
in Statistics, Data Science or a related field are preferred.
- Related fields may include: Mathematics, Economics, Quantitative Management, Industrial Engineering, Operations Research or a similar quantitative field.
- GPA of 3.5 or higher is preferred.
- Experience with languages such as Python required.
- Experience with Cloud-based AWS is preferred, but is not required
- Must demonstrate fundamental knowledge of multiple modeling techniques including but not limited to regression, neural networks, decision trees, and
clustering
- Strong communication, interpersonal, organization, leadership and critical thinking skills
- Ability to work in a team environment
- Competencies: Initiative; Teamwork; Relationship Building; Communication; Technical Functional; Strategic Business Focus
Internship Information
- Office locations: Bloomington, IL; Dunwoody, GA; Richardson, TX, Tempe, AZ
- Our internship will occur: June 2, 2027 to August 11, 2027
- Hourly Rate: $30.00/hour based on a 38.75-hour work week (Monday-Friday)
- In addition, interns selected for our summer internship program will be eligible for a $2,000 stipend (before taxes)
- Hybrid work environment - Combination of in-office time (at one of the locations noted above), and working from home
- Competitive candidates will be invited to participate in online interviews during the fall of 2026
- Internship Program Eligibility : To be eligible for the internship program, you must be a full-time student currently enrolled in a GRADUATE program
throughout the duration of the internship, with at least one semester remaining following the internship. Graduation date of Dec. 2027 or later.
