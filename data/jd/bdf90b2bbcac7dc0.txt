Job Summary
Holland’s Rail Measurement Systems and Services (RMSS) provide railroad track measurement and testing services and technologies since 1995. RMSS Product
Development Team design, build and deploy track inspection and measurement systems domestically and internationally. The product line includes hi-rail, rail-
bound and locomotive attended and autonomous measurement systems.
We are seeking a Computer Vision Engineering Intern for a full-time summer internship (40 hours per week). The intern will develop image processing algorithms
for our track geometry and rail profile measurement systems, and develop and optimize image recognition algorithms for video recordings. Final-year
undergraduate and graduate (Master’s or PhD) students are preferred.
Key Responsibilities
- Develop image processing algorithms for camera- and laser-based rail profile and track geometry measurement.
- Develop, train, and optimize image recognition models to detect track features and assets in video recordings.
- Prepare and label image and video datasets from field recordings.
- Test algorithm accuracy and speed against reference data, and document results.
- Work with the software team to integrate prototypes and present final results to the team.
Basic Qualifications
- Final-year Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD student in Computer Science, Electrical/Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Coursework or project experience in computer vision, image processing, or machine learning.
- Proficiency in Python and OpenCV; experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Available to work 40 hours per week on-site for the full summer.
Preferred Qualifications
- C++ and/or GPU (CUDA) experience.
- Experience with object detection on video, camera calibration, or laser/3D measurement.
- Interest in railway or infrastructure inspection.
Physical And Environmental Requirements
- Primarily office and lab work; occasional outdoor field work near railroad tracks with required PPE.
- Ability to lift up to 25 lbs. with or without reasonable accommodation.
