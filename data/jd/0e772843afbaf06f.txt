Job Description Launch your career in data with Vanguard’s College to Corporate (C2C) Internship Program. As a Data Analyst intern, you’ll help turn data into
insights that support better business decisions and better outcomes for our clients while building leadership skills through collaboration and real-world
impact. This immersive internship combines real-world project work, mentorship, and structured development to help you grow your technical, analytical, and
business acumen in a collaborative, purpose-driven environment. In this internship you will: Analyze data to answer business questions and uncover insights that
support decision-making across Vanguard. Support the creation of dashboards, reports, and visualizations that help teams monitor performance and communicate
results clearly. Partner with business and analytics teams to understand needs, translate requirements into an analytical approach, and deliver solutions that
drive value. Work with structured and unstructured data, helping to validate data quality, identify trends, and support repeatable reporting processes Build
foundational leadership skills by taking initiative, collaborating across teams, and contributing ideas that improve outcomes. Build relationships with program
managers, alumni, senior leaders, and peers across the program. Receive coaching and mentorship from your department manager and program leaders to support your
growth. What it takes: In pursuit of a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Data & Analytics, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Business Analytics, or a
related field Strong interest in using data to solve business problems and influence decisions. Demonstrated leadership skills and/or leadership aspirations.
Strong analytical, communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills. Ability to work independently and as part of a collaborative, team-oriented
environment. Cumulative GPA of 3.2 or higher preferred. Additional Information: This program is a full-time, paid 10-week summer internship Vanguard is not
