The 3D Technical Intern plays a pivotal role in supporting the execution of immersive retail and experiential environments by ensuring that all 3D assets are
accurate, optimized, and ready for production. This role bridges the gap between design vision and technical delivery maintaining material libraries, following
asset workflows, and troubleshooting model issues to support seamless handoffs between creative, engineering, and production teams. With a strong understanding
of rendering requirements, model integrity, and fabrication compatibility, the Technical Coordinator enables teams to visualize, iterate, and deliver high-
impact spatial solutions efficiently. Working under guidance and mentorship, precision, adaptability, and strong collaboration skills are essential in driving
excellence across branded environments and physical design experiences.
Shift available: Monday through Friday 8:00 am – 5:00 pm
Compensation: $21.00 Hourly
A variety of these traits will help land you this job if you:
- are working towards a Bachelor’s or Associate's degree in Industrial Design, Computer Graphics, Digital Design, Video Games, or a related field
- have at least 1 year of hands-on experience working with 3DS Max, including modeling, rendering, and file optimization
- have proven experience (1+ years) in 3D asset management, file organization, optimization, and troubleshooting within design or production environments
- possess a strong understanding of industry-standard file formats (e.g., OBJ, FBX, STL, STEP) and workflow compatibility across design, visualization, and
fabrication pipelines
- demonstrate an interest in, visual literacy for, and willingness to learn the fundamentals, intricacies, materials, and trends of retail design
- showcase a portfolio or work samples demonstrating technical accuracy, clean geometry, 3D material application, and spatial visualization skills
On top of that you must:
- possess 3D Software Proficiency & Platform Versatility: demonstrate intermediate skills in 3DS Max with Corona and/or V-Ray for high-quality modeling and
rendering, alongside versatility in SketchUp, Cinema 4D (C4D), and other 3D platforms as needed
- bring File Optimization & Model Troubleshooting Skills: show expertise in converting and streamlining 3D files for performance and compatibility—diagnosing
and repairing technical issues in 3D models such as non-manifold geometry, inverted normals, or poor UV mapping
- have 3D Material & Surface Understanding: exhibit a foundational understanding of 3D material creation and application to accurately simulate a variety of
surfaces for visual clarity and realistic presentations.
- demonstrate Organizational Ability & Resource Management: maintain strict attention to detail in asset preparation, file naming conventions, version control,
digital asset management (DAM) platforms, and maintaining shared 3D material/texture databases
- bring strong Communication & Collaboration habits: possess solid listening skills, the ability to present technical ideas concisely and effectively (both
verbally and in writing), and a desire to partner seamlessly across Design, Product Development, Engineering, IT, and Sales teams
- exhibit Adaptability & Curiosity: stay current with emerging 3D tools, rendering techniques, and file optimization strategies while remaining adaptable to
shifting project requirements, software updates, or workflow changes
In this position, you will:
- collaborate with designers to prepare 3D models and visual components for use in concept development and design presentations
- ensure 3D assets are optimized for visual clarity, efficient rendering (reducing polygon counts, refining geometry), and downstream production workflows
- maintain organized material libraries, texture databases, and shared 3D resources to support active and future projects
- uphold standards for file naming, version control, archiving, and use asset management tools to track file usage and ensure clean project hand-offs
- convert 3D assets into production-ready formats (e.g., OBJ, STL, STEP, FBX) tailored for internal stakeholders or vendor requirements
- diagnose and repair model geometry errors to guarantee model integrity and fabrication compatibility
- partner with IT and technical teams to explore new software tools, plug-ins, and systems that support 3D design operations
- complete miscellaneous technical and asset support tasks as assigned by management
