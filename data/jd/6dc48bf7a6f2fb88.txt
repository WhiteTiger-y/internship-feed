You will join the Physics-Informed AI research group within Siemens’ Data and Artificial Intelligence organization — our central research hub for artificial
intelligence — for a Spring 2027 internship.
We are looking for a Research Intern in Machine Learning for Power Systems. This position will be remote.
You’ll make an impact by:
- Developing machine learning algorithms for power systems.
- Using artificial intelligence to speed up simulations, improve forecasting, and strengthen the cybersecurity posture of the power grid.
- Exploring foundation models for engineering data modalities such as tabular and time-series data.
- Embedding physics into generative artificial intelligence models for power system applications.
- Working on fast-paced co-creation projects with quickly changing requirements at the intersection of machine learning and power systems.
- Building demonstrators, supporting technology transition to Siemens businesses, and documenting your work through technical reports and academic publications.
You’ll win us over by having the following qualifications:
Basic Qualifications:
- Currently enrolled as a PhD student in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- Research experience applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to power systems.
- Experience designing and applying graph neural networks for power system applications.
- Proficiency in Python, PyTorch, and PyTorch Geometric (PyG), and in the effective use of agentic coding tools.
- Minimal travel required (less than 10%).
- Legally authorized to work in the United States on a continual and permanent basis without company sponsorship.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Research experience with foundation models for engineering data modalities beyond language and image, such as tabular or time-series data.
- Experience embedding physics into generative artificial intelligence models.
- Publications at leading artificial intelligence venues (for example, NeurIPS, ICML, or ICLR) or power systems venues (for example, IEEE PES).
