In this full-time (~40 hours/week) role, you’ll work with SMEs (Subject Matter Experts) across operations, strategy, and consumer protection teams to clean and
analyze real program data and use cases. You'll r esearch and compare consumer protection frameworks and audit digital and print marketing materials from
vendors for compliance with program requirements. Using this data, you will develop (and present!) your own recommendations to support our goals for equitable
and sustainable solar program deployment throughout Illinois. Your recommendations will directly improve our customers' experiences and program effectiveness.
You'll also help customers directly by building a public-facing savings calculator. This calculator will help them understand the impact of escalation rates and
total costs of PPAs (Power Purchase Agreements) and leases over standard 15- and 20-year terms. In addition, you will create accessible, easy-to-understand
graphics for our website about community solar subscriptions, leases, or PPAs, which will draw and empower potential customers. These combined resources will
help customers understand why and how our solar program can benefit them, their families, and their communities.
