Job Description
Primary Function of Position
The Global Access, Value and Economics (GAVE) organization at Intuitive is seeking a highly motivated, hard-working, and professional Data Science Intern to
join our team for Summer 2027. The Data Science Intern will support GAVE’s mission of generating clinical evidence to drive the adoption of robotic-assisted
surgery. They will work within the Global Access and Evidence Management Center of Excellence to develop a system to optimize evidence management and synthesis
processes. They will design a tool to identify, deduplicate, validate, and ingest new publications related to Intuitive products. They will also work with the
team to integrate existing large language model (LLM) pipelines into this solution. The intern will get hands-on experience with software development, data
management, machine learning, and systems design thinking. Beyond technical skills, this internship will require effective communication with numerous cross-
functional stakeholders.
Essential Job Duties
- Design an end-to-end data pipeline to optimize critical evidence management, synthesis, and generation processes.
- Implement and automate publication search and deduplication strategies.
- Assist in integrating additional large language model (LLM) workflows into this system.
- Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to develop the solution.
- Present and interpret findings to a broad range of teams across the organization.
