About The Role
As a Research Science Engineer Intern at Analog Devices (ADI), you will gain hands-on experience contributing to the development of next-generation technologies
through applied research, experimentation, and technical analysis. Working alongside experienced engineers and researchers in multidisciplinary teams, you will
support the investigation of scientific principles, evaluate emerging concepts, conduct experiments, analyze results, and contribute to innovative solutions
with potential applications in future products and technologies.
Key Responsibilities
- Support applied research, modeling, simulation, experimentation, and data analysis to explore new technologies and solutions.
- Assist in investigating the feasibility of emerging concepts, materials, devices, systems, or architectures for future applications.
- Support the design and execution of experiments, characterize results, document observations, and communicate technical findings.
- Collaborate with engineers, researchers, and cross-functional teams on research and technology development activities.
- Contribute to technical reports, research documentation, presentations, and knowledge-sharing activities as appropriate.
- Develop awareness of industry trends, emerging technologies, and state-of-
- the-art research developments relevant to assigned projects.
Must Have Skills
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Academic or project-based experience with laboratory experimentation, simulation, modeling, programming, or data analysis.
- Ability to communicate technical concepts and findings clearly and work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
Preferred Education And Experience
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Physics, Materials Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related
technical discipline.
- Relevant coursework, academic research, laboratory experience, capstone projects, or other technical project experience.
- Exposure to semiconductor technologies, device physics, materials research, signal processing, embedded systems, AI/ML, communications, sensing technologies,
or other advanced engineering domains.
- Familiarity with simulation, characterization, test, programming, or research tools relevant to the technical field.
- Exposure to technical publications, academic research projects, competitions, or innovation-focused activities is a plus
