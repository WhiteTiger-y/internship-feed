Title: Operations/Royalty Processing & Business Intelligence Summer Intern
Department: Operations
Job Location: Washington, DC (Hybrid: in office Tuesdays & Wednesdays)
Position Summary
SoundExchange 2027 Summer Internship Program
We’re SoundExchange, and we’re on a mission to efficiently collect and distribute royalties, advocate on behalf of creators, and develop trusted business
solutions for the music industry. Why? Because we love music and we value the people behind it.
If you’re as passionate about music and championing creators as we are, we want you to join the band! SoundExchange offers a robust 10‐week paid internship
program each summer for talented, motivated students to work in our Washington, DC office. The program is designed to expand exposure to the music tech industry
and provide relevant and practical work experience in an area of interest.
As a SoundExchange intern, we’ll welcome you as an integral member of our dedicated and diverse team. You will have the unique opportunity to take on roles
similar to those of a full‐time hire and dive into projects that contribute to the organization’s mission while growing your skills, forging new professional
relationships, and enhancing your resume.
Program Highlights & Details
- $20.00 per hour
- One‐time taxable stipend of $2,000.00 to assist with housing and transportation
- 35‐40 hours per week
- Flexibility to work hybrid (two days, Tues and Weds on‐site required)
- Participate in a comprehensive educational series and engage with various SoundExchange leaders across the organization
- Build your network with your fellow interns and SoundExchange staff
Internship Key Dates
- Program dates: Approximately June 1, 2027 – August 6, 2027
- Deadline: All applications must be received no later than November 7th, 2026
- Internship interviews: September 2026 ‐ November 2026
- Selection & Notification: December 2026
What To Expect As an Operations/Royalty Processing & Business Intelligence Intern
- BI Analytics Potential Work: Move queries that previously went off staging tables to use the new Datamart tables developed by DW. The intern will learn about
staging vs datamart tables and how ETLs transform data.
- BI Engineer Potential Work: Develop a DV Report that helps track BI Report Usage and flags any reports that have not been used recently. The intern will learn
BI Development and governance skills.
- Data Mining Potential Work: We need help getting results of data mining exercises into confluence so we have documentation and can use in the future. The
intern will learn about what goes into data mining and skills on technical documentation.
- All interns are required to give a presentation at the conclusion of their internship
Eligibility & Requirements
- Personify SoundExchange’s Core Values (Driving Excellence, Integrity In All That We Do, Championing Creators, Respect Why We’re Here, Teamwork That Empowers)
- Demonstrate inclusivity, advocacy, and appreciation for diversity in personal, academic, or professional experiences
- Thrive in a fast‐paced environment
- Resourceful, have great attention to detail, highly organized
- Ability to collaborate well with others and tackle challenges enthusiastically
- Sharp written and verbal communication skills
- Be 18 years of age or older on or before the first day of the internship program
- Undergraduates preferred (heading into your junior or senior year is also preferred); graduate students may still apply
- Be enrolled in a degree‐seeking program in an accredited college or university (with a minimum 3.0 GPA), or be a student at a relevant professional boot camp
or advanced educational program
To be considered for this exciting opportunity, attach your cover letter, most recent transcript, and resumé .
