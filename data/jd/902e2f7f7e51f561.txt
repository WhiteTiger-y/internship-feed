Responsibilities
The Multimedia Lab builds highly efficient, scalable video codec hardware solutions (FPGA and ASIC) from the ground up, serving billions of users. We bring
together algorithm, architecture, software, firmware, and hardware design and verification expertise, designing high-performance video silicon IP across ASIC
and SoC digital design, verification, and next-generation video codec hardware. Alongside the silicon work, we advance research in video understanding,
perceptual quality assessment, image and video enhancement, and intelligent coding, including using multimodal models to improve coding decisions.
Quality, efficiency, and speed rarely improve together. Sharper images and better perceived quality usually cost bitrate, compute, or latency, and a technique
that works in research does not always survive production at global scale.
You will take ideas from research through to silicon and production systems, where research decisions show up in the cost and quality of video across
ByteDance's products.
About The Team
Our team is building industry leading, highly efficient and scalable video codec hardware solutions (FPGA and ASIC) from the ground up to better serve our
billions of users. We are looking for strong software and firmware engineers to design algorithms, C-model, and firmware for advanced video encoding and
processing implemented in dedicated hardware accelerators. The successful candidate will be part of a fast growing team that includes algorithm, architecture,
software, firmware, and hardware design and verification experts with a dedication to technical excellence and a passion to build large-scale and high-
performing video platforms and services.
We are looking for talented individuals to join us for an internship. PhD internships at Our Company provide students with the opportunity to actively
contribute to our products and research, as well as to the organization's future plans and emerging technologies.
Our dynamic internship experience blends hands-on learning, enriching community-building and professional development events, and collaboration with industry
experts.

Responsibilities:
- Design video analysis (ROI, content understanding, feature extraction etc.) and quality assessment algorithms, and participate in database creation, algorithm

- Participate in designing strategy and solutions for E2E video quality optimization with a combination of video analysis, processing and encoding algorithms,

Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications:
- Individuals who are completing or have recently completed a PhD degree in computer science, electrical engineering, mathematics, statistics or a related
discipline.
- Excellent programming, debugging, and optimization skills in one or more general-purpose programming languages, including but not limited to: C/C++, Python.
- Knowledge of image/video analysis algorithms or subjective/objective image/video quality algorithms, and state-of-the-art technologies, such as VMAF, PSNR and
SSIM and etc.
- Hands-on experience with machine learning or deep learning and familiarity with frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Research and hands-on experience in designing, training, or applying deep learning models, including CNNs, Transformer architectures, and multimodal large
language models (MLLMs).
- Knowledge of common video processing algorithms, such as supper resolution, defusion model, etc.
- Knowledge of video codecs (AVC, HEVC, AV1, VP9, etc.) and experience in video codec design.
- Experience with embedded processors like ARM Cortex M0/M3/M4 or RISC-V architecture and real-time development on RTOS environment.
- Great communication, eager to learn, and always passionate about turning cutting-edge technologies into real life use cases.
