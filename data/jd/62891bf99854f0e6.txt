Introduction
The TAC AI team is applying Generative AI to computational lithography products to help users create, debug, optimize, and automate complex engineering
workflows. Our mission is to enable users to go directly from intent to solution using AI agents, knowledge systems, and intelligent automation. Key challenges
include building reliable agent workflows, managing long-term knowledge and memory, and integrating AI into real engineering applications.
Your Assignment
As a Generative AI Development Intern, you will help develop the next generation of AI-powered engineering assistants.
Your responsibilities include:
- Designing and implementing agent workflows for different use cases.
- Developing memory and knowledge systems for AI agents.
- Building solutions that connect user intent to automated problem solving.
- Organizing and maintaining knowledge repositories used by AI agents.
- Experimenting with modern agent frameworks and Generative AI technologies.
Why this internship is interesting
- Work on real-world Generative AI applications in computational lithography.
- Gain hands-on experience with LLMs, AI agents, memory systems, and knowledge management.
- Help shape the future of AI-assisted engineering products.
Your Profile
- Pursuing a Master's, or PhD degree in Computer Science, AI, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Familiarity with agent frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, DeepAgents, or similar technologies.
- Basic knowledge of databases, RAG, and agent memory systems.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Self-motivated, curious, and passionate about Generative AI.
- Good communication and teamwork skills.
