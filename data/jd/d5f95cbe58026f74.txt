This is a paid 12-week internship opportunity and is a hybrid, in-office role.
Here’s a glimpse into the Internship experience from some of our TRI interns!
The Team
This internship opportunity falls within the Human-Centered AI Division (HCAI). We are an integrated team of ML researchers, behavioral scientists, and human-
computer interaction experts. At the core of our work, we aim to create purposeful human futures enabled through technology.
The Internship
We are seeking a highly motivated PhD research intern to join our Future Product Innovation team and advance the frontiers of Generative AI for product design.
The ideal candidate will have a strong background in Computational Cognitive Science with a demonstrated interest at the intersection of human preferences,
creativity, and machine learning. In this project, the intern will build models of human preference and flexible decision making, with a particular focus on
understanding how people negotiate multiple goals across contexts and over time.
Over the course of the project, in addition to Future Product Innovation group meetings, the intern will also participate in all Human-Centered AI division
meetings and meetings with stakeholders from relevant Toyota business groups. The intern will be exposed to how interdisciplinary industrial research works and
learn from our team as well as the other interns in our division. Furthermore, the intern will engage in strategy discussions about how their research connects
to business impact at Toyota.
Responsibilities
In collaboration with your mentor and the other researchers in the department, you will:
- Scope the project to align with core research efforts;
- Be the primary driver of the technical plan (e.g., model development, analysis) with regular feedback from mentors;
- Execute the project using TRI resources; and
- Present the project’s approach and findings in research meetings.
Qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a PhD program in Computational Cognitive Science, Computational Neuroscience, or a related field.
- Have demonstrated expertise in reinforcement learning, including applying reinforcement learning models to human behavior.
- Experience evaluating theories of human behavior using computational approaches.
- Strong data science skills with proficiency in Python.
- Proven track record of working autonomously while soliciting feedback.
- Proven track record of publications at top venues (e.g., NeurIPS, RLDM, CCN).
- Strong interpersonal skills and a track record of being a great teammate.
- Strong interest in interdisciplinary work.
Qualifications
- Experience with inverse reinforcement learning.
- Experience working with multimodal human behavior datasets.
- Experience with interdisciplinary research.
- Demonstrated interest in connecting behavioral science insights to business use cases.
