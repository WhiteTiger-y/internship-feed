About Our Internship Program Nasdaq’s Internship Program is a 10-week summer experience designed to give students real exposure to how global markets and
technology come together. Our interns work on meaningful projects alongside Nasdaq teams, gaining hands-on skills that make an impact on the business. We
believe the best way to learn is by doing — that’s why you’ll be paired with a mentor, connected with senior leaders, and included in professional development
sessions. You’ll also join a diverse global intern community, sharing experiences and building networks that last beyond the program. Our goal is to provide a
supportive, engaging, and fun environment where your work feels valued and your growth comes first. Check out our Nasdaq Internship Program page to learn more
and hear directly from past interns about their journeys. Data Engineering Internship At Nasdaq, you’ll have the chance to start your career in an environment
where learning, teamwork, and innovation come first. In this role, you’ll support our Data Access and Analytics team and gain hands-on experience with Eqlipse
Intelligence Platform, a SaaS solution for enabling Financial Market Infrastructure organizations to unlock the full potential of their data. You don’t need to
know everything on day one — we’ll help you build the skills, confidence, and network to grow your career. What You’ll Do Support our Data Engineering team with
day-to-day tasks and contribute to ongoing initiatives. Learn how to build out advanced analytics in a variety of financial assets and markets; design and
develop data models to ensure data quality, consistency, and integrity throughout the data lifecycle. Collaborate with teammates across different functions and
regions. Bring your ideas and curiosity to problem-solving and project work. Develop your technical and professional skills through training and mentorship.
What You’ll Bring Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in computer science or related area. Eagerness to learn, ask questions, and take on new
challenges. Ability to work well with others and contribute to a team environment. Good communication and organizational skills. Curiosity about technology,
finance, and global markets. Basic skills in SQL, programing language such as Java, Python, or Scala. Nice-to-Have Internship or project experience related to
data analytics, data modeling or big data technologies. Involvement in student organizations, volunteering, or leadership roles. Familiarity with data analytics
