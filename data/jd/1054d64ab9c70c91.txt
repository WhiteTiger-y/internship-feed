The Client & Customer Experience (C&CE) team is responsible for the servicing of Cigna customers across multiple lines of business, including US Medical,
Pharmacy Benefits, and more. C&CE owns the delivery of exceptional client and customer experience while operating Cigna’s call centers effectively and
efficiently for the business. The Business Analytics Intern will be responsible for supporting critical analytical work that impacts C&CE strategic priorities.
This role is part of a Business Analyst team that provides direct support to the C&CE organization, including driving strategic improvements in customer
experience, redesigning the destination servicing experience, and supporting improvements in the associate experience through better capabilities and tools.
During the internship, the associate will work closely with operational partners to deeply understand 1-2 assigned business case projects, develop analytical
insights and build/share compelling presentations that positively influence future strategy. What You Will Do: Develop 1-2 business cases that evaluate options
and assess costs/benefits to guide prioritization decisions Leverage data to solve business problems by identifying data needs, pulling data from relevant
sources, and performing analysis Identify business opportunities using trend analysis and mining operational data for insights Distill work into crisp
presentations that educate, inform, and inspire action Role Components: Strategy – Influence action through data-driven analysis and business acumen. Identify
and assess potential opportunities for change over the next 3-5 years. Business Judgment – Develop and use frameworks to scope and address ambiguous problems.
Understand where the leverage is and focus problem-solving efforts to uncover insights that drive action. Analytics – Evaluate business opportunities and make
strategic recommendations. Identify an effective method, perform data analysis, and develop recommendations that account for benefits, costs, risks, and
alternatives. Data – Ground analysis in data as much as possible. Define needs, identify data sources, ensure data quality, and partner with data teams to
complete analysis. Coding experience in a relevant language (e.g. SQL or Python) is a preference. Communications – Influence leaders across C&CE and our
stakeholder teams. Distill complex analytical work into key insights, recommendations, and next steps. Teamwork – Partner across Operations, Tech, Finance, and
business teams. Learn from supportive Analytics leaders and join an inclusive, empowering team where you can grow professionally and have fun! Basic
Qualifications Rising senior working towards a Bachelor’s degree (Business Analytics, Business, Economics, Math, Statistics or related field preferred) Strong
communication (oral and written), presentation, and facilitation skills Ability to structure project plans and independently manage to deadlines Ability to
partner across organizations and roles to influence and deliver work Preferred Qualifications Advanced knowledge of data analysis and visualization tools (e.g.
Excel, SQL, Tableau) Proficiency in an analytics-focused coding language, e.g. SQL, Python, or R Experience with building, managing, or using business models,
e.g. optimization, predictive analysis, or financial / credit modeling Additional Information Location: This role is located at our Bloomfield, CT headquarters
office. Housing: Summer housing is available for interns who live more than 50 miles from the Bloomfield, CT office. Work Authorization: One goal of this
