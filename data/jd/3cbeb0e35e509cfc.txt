Overview
Research Internships at Microsoft provide a dynamic environment for research careers with a network of world-class research labs led by globally-recognized
scientists and engineers, who pursue innovation in a range of scientific and technical disciplines to help solve complex challenges in diverse fields, including
computing, healthcare, economics, and the environment.
Join M365 Research and contribute to build the next generation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) agents that take on long-running work, remember what matters, and
get better with experience. Our team drives applied research that reimagines how agentic systems are built, evaluated, and deployed at enterprise scale, and we
transfer these advances into Microsoft products. We closely collaborate with multiple research teams and product groups across the globe, and we communicate our
research through peer-reviewed publications, open-source releases, blog posts, patents, and industry conferences.
Our topics of interest include:
- Agent memory and skills: building and maintaining memory, context, and reusable skills so long-running agents stay current and get better with experience.
- Reliable, self-improving agents: diagnosing failures in long agent trajectories, improving agents without regressions, and evaluating them in realistic
simulated environments.
- Proactive, long-horizon agents: planning, multi-agent coordination, and self-monitoring for agents that work over extended periods.
Responsibilities
Research Interns put inquiry and theory into practice. Alongside fellow doctoral candidates and some of the world’s best researchers, Research Interns learn,
collaborate, and network for life. Research Interns not only advance their own careers, but they also contribute to exciting research and development strides.
During the 12-week internship, Research Interns are paired with mentors and expected to collaborate with other Research Interns and researchers, present
findings, and contribute to the vibrant life of the community. Research internships are available in all areas of research, and are offered year-round, though
they typically begin in the summer.

Preferred Qualifications
- At least 1 year of experience in conducting research and authoring peer-reviewed publications.
- Experience in machine learning, NLP, or related AI areas such as LLMs and AI agents.
- Hands-on experience with agent memory, skill or tool learning, RL or post-training, agent evaluation, self-improvement, planning, or multi-agent systems.
- Demonstrated coding skills, including building agent systems and rapid prototyping with AI coding agents.
- Proficient written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to collaborate across research and product teams and apply research to real-world problems.
- Publications in top venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLM, AAAI, IJCAI, KDD, or SIGIR.
