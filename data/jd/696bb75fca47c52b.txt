Job Responsibilities
- Contribute to the development of AI agents using agentic frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, or custom orchestration layers) that automate
complex, multi-step business workflows across the enterprise
- Help implement tool-calling architectures, function-calling interfaces, and MCP (Model Context Protocol) integrations that allow agents to interact with
enterprise APIs, databases, and external services in a controlled and auditable manner
- Support the build-out of retrieval augmented generation (RAG) pipelines with vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, pgvector, OpenSearch) to ground agent
responses in authoritative enterprise data and reduce hallucination in high-stakes contexts
- Assist with AI red teaming exercises including adversarial prompt injection testing, jailbreak resistance evaluation, tool-use abuse scenarios, and multi-turn
manipulation testing to help identify vulnerabilities before agents reach production
- Help build and test runtime guardrails, input/output validators, content filters, and human-in-the-loop approval gates that enforce safe agent behavior within
a multi-layer defense-in-depth architecture
- Support benchmarking and evaluation of frontier foundation models (GPT-4, Claude, Llama, Gemini, Mistral, and emerging open-source models) for agentic use
cases, measuring task completion accuracy, latency, cost, safety, and failure modes
- Contribute to observability and tracing infrastructure for agent execution including structured logging, token-level attribution, decision audit trails, and
cost tracking across multi-agent workflows
- Prototype agentic solutions on cloud AI platforms (AWS Bedrock, SageMaker, AWS AI DLC, Azure AI) using containerized deployments (Docker, Kubernetes),
serverless architectures, and CI/CD pipelines
- Support evaluation of AI startups and emerging agentic technology partners, helping with technical due diligence on architectures, safety postures, and
enterprise integration feasibility
- Contribute to AI governance practices including model onboarding documentation, agent risk assessments, responsible AI compliance, and alignment with
regulatory requirements in healthcare and life sciences
