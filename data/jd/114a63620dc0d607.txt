The Role
Joining Moderna means advancing mRNA science to transform medicine. Work with exceptional global teams on a broad pipeline and build a career that makes a real
difference for patients.
Moderna is strengthening its presence in Cambridge, Massachusetts, home to our global headquarters and a center of mRNA innovation. Working closely with leading
universities and research institutions, our Cambridge team advances groundbreaking science that shapes the future of medicine. We welcome talent driven by
innovation and committed to improving global health.
This is an individual-contributor intern opportunity based at Moderna’s Binney Street location in Cambridge, Massachusetts, from January 19, 2027 – June 25,
2027 . Applicants must be available for the full duration of the intern term.
You will gain meaningful, hands-on experience supporting Data Management Leads across the clinical study lifecycle in a regulated biopharmaceutical environment.
Working closely with experienced DMLs, you will contribute directly to the quality, integrity, and operational execution of clinical data through data review,
documentation, testing, reconciliation, quality control, and study coordination activities.
Your work will help create additional capacity for Data Management Leads to focus on study strategy, risk management, vendor oversight, cross-functional
decision-making, and delivery of key study milestones. Alongside your core Data Management responsibilities, you will have opportunities to learn how emerging
digital and Generative AI tools can support efficient ways of working in a highly regulated environment, while maintaining appropriate quality, data integrity,
and compliance standards.
Here’s What You’ll Do
- Partner with experienced Data Management Leads to learn and apply GxP regulations to clinical data collection.
- Provide operational support to Data Management Leads throughout the clinical study lifecycle.
- Perform hands-on clinical data review to identify inconsistencies and errors and conduct trend analysis to support data quality.
- Support documentation, testing, reconciliation, quality control, and study coordination activities.
- Adhere to Good Documentation Practices (GDP) and Data Integrity requirements to maintain accurate, complete, and inspection-ready records.
- Help enable Data Management Leads to dedicate greater capacity to study strategy, risk management, vendor oversight, cross-functional decision-making, and
achievement of key study milestones.
- Complete all required training assignments in a timely manner.
- Contribute to special projects and continuous improvement initiatives as assigned.
- Build practical experience working within a regulated biopharmaceutical environment and contribute to ongoing clinical and operational initiatives.
- Gain exposure to opportunities for responsible use of digital and Generative AI tools to enhance productivity, learning, and operational workflows within
appropriate regulated processes.
