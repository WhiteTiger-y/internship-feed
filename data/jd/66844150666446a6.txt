Development (R&D) work within EA’s Sports Quality Verification Engineering organization. Primarily, this work will focus on the Machine Learning and Artificial
Intelligent domain and will be the foundation for our next generation of tools and solutions for driving effectiveness and efficiencies in our QV process. The
successful candidates will play a pivotal role in this transformation.
We encourage candidates from all backgrounds who meet the core requirements to apply, even if you do not meet every qualification.
Primary Responsibilities
Gathering use-case requirements from our partner teams and organizations.
Providing research into possible solutions, running experiments, providing proofs of concept, demonstrating your results, and providing recommendations.
Providing improvements to existing solution implementations.
Updating existing Machine Learning models and enhancing data sets.
Helping to productize AI/ML solutions and roll them out to our partners.
Providing documentation and gathering feedback from our partners.
Requirements:
You must be legally authorized to work in Canada for the entire internship - visa sponsorship is not available.
You must be a Masters student enrolled in an accredited degree program and return to school for at least one semester following your internship with EA.
You must be graduating in May 2028 or later , including the time needed to complete your internship.
Relocation support may be available if you meet specific eligibility criteria.
Qualifications
Working towards a Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
Practical experience or strong academic background in machine learning fundamentals, including supervised and unsupervised learning techniques.
Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, C++, or C# for software and model development.
Familiarity with updating existing machine learning models, fine-tuning, and enhancing datasets for performance.
Demonstrated proficiency in agentic workflows and the optimization of Large Language Model (LLM) prompts.
Understanding of software development life cycles (SDLC), engineering best practices, and collaborative version control tools like Git.
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills with the ability to research, experiment, and prototype solutions for R&D challenges.
Basic knowledge or experience in gathering use-case requirements and translating them into technical specifications.
Aptitude for technical writing, documenting experimental results, and communicating findings to partner teams and stakeholders.
Proactive mindset with a demonstrated ability to independently complete tasks, run experiments, and escalate technical issues appropriately.
Creative and logical thinking skills focused on building effective and efficient tools for quality verification (QV) processes.
Familiarity with the game of soccer and EA Sports gaming portfolio is an asset.
