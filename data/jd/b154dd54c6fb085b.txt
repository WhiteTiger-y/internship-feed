What you’ll do
Looking for *that* internship? The game-changing one that’ll help you learn, grow, and chart your path forward? You’ll find it at SAS. Our interns aren’t coffee
runners – they do real, meaningful work. Our award-winning internship program is focused on development, culture, and community. We’ll help you grow
professionally, find (or further) your passion, and make memorable connections that last beyond the summer.
The Time Series Research and Development team advances SAS forecasting and artificial intelligence capabilities for temporal data. The team researches modern
machine-learning methods, develops scalable production software, and integrates new forecasting and generative modeling technologies with the SAS Viya platform.
As an intern, you might:
- Research and compare leading open-source and commercial time series foundation models, focusing on model architecture, pretraining data, training objectives,
adaptation strategies, and evaluation methodology.
- Curate, clean, standardize, and document diverse time series datasets for large-scale pretraining and evaluation.
- Design and implement components of a transformer-based time series foundation model tailored to SAS forecasting use cases.
- Develop and run distributed pretraining experiments, analyze model behavior, and improve zero-shot and few-shot forecasting performance.
- Build reproducible benchmarks against open-source foundation models and SAS forecasting intellectual property using accuracy, robustness, efficiency, and
generalization criteria.
- Explore transformer-based synthetic time series generation as a source of pretraining data and as a standalone capability.
- Prototype integration with SAS Viya and communicate research findings through technical reports, code, and presentations.
