The candidate will be working full time in a research study where fully autonomous robots are deployed in the homes of older adults with dementia to offer them
assistances with activities of daily living.
Duties/Responsibilities
- Writing new codes to expand the existing software framework
- Maintaining the current software
- Making deployment visits to participants home
- Troubleshoot the system whenever needed
- Collect and analyze data for dissemination
Qualifications
- The candidate should have a bachelor’s or a master’s degree in robotics or a related discipline
Preferred Qualifications
- Some field deployment experiences
Knowledge, Skills & Abilities
- Prior experience with robotics
- Knowledge of ROS2
- Large scale robot software development
