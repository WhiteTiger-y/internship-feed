College Intern- Corporate-Information Technology Job Description Internship Program Overview Marriott Vacations Worldwide offers a comprehensive internship
program at one of our corporate office locations, available in 3- or 6-month durations, and built around the following core pillars: Gain in-person, on-site
work experience within a designated department at one of our corporate office locations. Receive personalized guidance and support from experienced department
leaders to help shape your career path. Participate in exclusive sessions with senior company executives to gain strategic insights and leadership perspectives.
Spend a day shadowing a different department to broaden your understanding of MVW business. Conduct two interviews with senior leaders to learn about their
career journeys and leadership philosophies. Collaborate on a meaningful project that encourages creativity and contributes to business innovation. Join
interactive online sessions designed to provide exposure to various areas of the MVW business. Connect with fellow interns and share your journey through our
dedicated internship social media platform. Corporate HR Internship Information: Internship Timeline: Summer 2027 Start: May 2027 Pay: $18.50 Location: MVW HQ
in Orlando, FL (Hybrid) Candidates must be actively pursuing a college degree at any level. Candidate must be pursuing a degree in the following fields:
Information Systems, Computer Science, Technology Required skills: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, passion around technology Preferred
skills: Proficiency in Python development and scripting. Experience with cloud platforms; Microsoft Fabric is a plus. Exposure to Apache Spark or distributed
data processing tools. Knowledge of relational and modern database technologies-Oracle, SQL Server, and PostgreSQL. An understanding of data integration,
transformation, and pipeline development. *Housing accommodations and relocation are the students’ responsibility. Internship Description Assist with department
specific day-to-day operations. Support project planning and execution under the guidance of team members. Attend team meetings and contribute ideas and
feedback. Gather and analyze data to support projects, reports, or presentation. Contribute to the development of MVW’s enterprise data platform, supporting the
build of a modern, scalable data ecosystem. Partner with Data Engineers to design and implement data pipelines and integration solutions. Utilize Microsoft
Fabric and modern data technologies to support platform capabilities and data accessibility. Perform data validation, transformation, and conformance activities
to ensure data quality and consistency. Explore and apply emerging data technologies and industry trends to enhance platform performance and innovation.
