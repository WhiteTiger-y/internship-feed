One Application, Multiple Opportunities
Apply once to be considered for multiple internships focused on building, automating, testing, and improving technology solutions. As you move through the
process, you’ll learn about specific teams and assignments so we can match your skills and interests with the right opportunity.
Your Impact
AI, automation, and software are changing how work gets done. In the roles you may be considered for, you’ll help create practical technology solutions that
make experiences faster, smarter, and more connected.
Assignments may span software or AI engineering, automation, cloud and platform work, application development, DevOps, testing, IT operations, or technical
program management—giving you a chance to build, integrate, troubleshoot, and improve solutions that solve real problems.
What You May Work On
- Design, build, test, or enhance software applications, AI-enabled tools, and technology solutions
- Develop automated workflows that improve efficiency, reduce manual effort, or create better digital experiences
- Work with cloud platforms, APIs, data integrations, development environments, or monitoring tools
- Evaluate technical requirements, troubleshoot issues, and improve the performance, reliability, or usability of solutions
- Collaborate with technical and business teams to translate real problems into scalable technology solutions
What You’ll Bring
We're seeking students who are energized by solving problems, asking questions, and continuously learning.
Successful Candidates Often Demonstrate
- Strong problem-solving and analytical-thinking skills
- Curiosity and a desire to understand how technology works
- Effective communication and collaboration skills
- Comfort navigating ambiguity, troubleshooting, and complex technical challenges
- Interest in software development, AI, automation, cloud technologies, or digital innovation
- Initiative, adaptability, and a growth mindset
- A desire to build, create, test, and continuously improve solutions
Prior experience with programming languages, software development, AI technologies, cloud platforms, automation tools, testing, or technical projects may be
required for some, but not all of the roles within this pathway.
Education & Eligibility
You must be enrolled at an accredited college or university at the time of the internship. We primarily seek rising seniors graduating between August 2027 and
June 2028; students graduating in 2029 may also be considered.
Students from a variety of academic backgrounds may be a strong fit, including:
- Computer Science, Software Engineering, or Computer Engineering
- Information Systems, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, or Network Engineering
- Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, or other Engineering disciplines
Students from other disciplines with demonstrated technical aptitude and an interest in building technology solutions are also encouraged to apply.
Compensation
Intern pay ranges: $26–$46 per hour.
Compensation is based on your academic level, role classification (technical vs. non-technical), experience, and geographic location.

Background Screening
If you are selected for a position, there will be a background screen, which may include checks for criminal records and/or motor vehicle reports and/or drug
screening, depending on the position requirements. For more information on these checks, please refer to the Post Offer section of our FAQ page. Job-related
concerns identified during the background screening may disqualify you from the new position or your current role. Background results will be evaluated on a
case-by-case basis.
Pursuant to the San Francisco Fair Chance Ordinance, we will consider for employment qualified applicants with arrest and conviction records.
