Research Scientist Intern, Generative Recommendation Systems (PhD) Responsibilities:
- Initiate and lead efforts toward long-term, ambitious research goals while identifying intermediate milestones, including rethinking the architecture and
underlying logic of recommendation systems around generative recommendation models, and advancing research on the generative recommendation paradigm across
diverse product use cases.
- Conduct original research that can eventually be applied to Meta product development, and engage with the wider research community, including publishing
papers and releasing open source software where appropriate.
- Design, train, and support AI/ML libraries and models, and continuously improve the performance of generative recommendation models across key directions such
as model scaling, reinforcement learning (RL)–based preference alignment, and LLM/MLLM knowledge alignment.
- Collaborate with researchers and cross-functional partners, including communicating research plans, progress, and results.
