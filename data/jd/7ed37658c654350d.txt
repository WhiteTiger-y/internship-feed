Are you a Software Engineering or Computer Science student interested in building with AI and applying it to real engineering problems?
Tektronix is seeking a 12-week Software Engineering Intern to help explore and develop AI-enabled tools for engineering applications. You’ll work directly with
experienced engineers, learn emerging AI technologies, and build prototypes that explore new ways to solve engineering problems, find and use technical
knowledge, and improve engineering workflows.
What You’ll Do
- * Develop and experiment with AI-enabled software prototypes for real engineering applications.
- * Work with technologies such as Python, AI models, APIs, and modern software development tools.
- * Explore areas such as AI agents, information retrieval, engineering knowledge systems, and workflow automation.
- * Test ideas, evaluate results, and learn where AI provides value and where it doesn’t.
- * Collaborate with engineers to understand problems and turn promising ideas into working prototypes.
- * Document your work and demonstrate your results to the engineering team.
Requirements
- * Pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- * Junior-level standing preferred.
- * Programming experience through coursework, projects, internships, or personal development.
- * Strong curiosity, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
- * Interest in artificial intelligence and enthusiasm for learning new technologies.
- * Ability to communicate clearly and work collaboratively with others.
- * Prior experience with AI technologies is helpful, but not required.

performance instruments, sensors, and subsystems fuel mission-critical advancements across industries, enabling real-world impact where it matters most. At
Ralliant we're building the future, together with those driven to push boundaries, solve complex problems, and leave a lasting mark on the world.

Is this role subject to ITAR?
The essential duties of this position require adherence to U.S. Government export control regulations. Accordingly, candidates must either be U.S. Persons
(i.e., U.S. citizens, U.S. lawful permanent residents, or protected individuals as defined by 8 U.S.C. 1324b(a)(3)) or be prepared to collaborate with the
company in securing the necessary U.S. government export authorizations. While the company encourages all interested applicants to apply, please be aware that
ongoing employment is dependent upon obtaining the appropriate government export authorizations.
