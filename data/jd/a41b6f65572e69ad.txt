About Our Group
The Data Analytics (DA) team in the Characterization and Analytics Group develops big-data analytics, machine learning (ML), artificial intelligence (AI), and
production software technologies that support R&D design and development activities within the Recording Head Technology organization.
Our work connects engineering data, Linux compute clusters, production data pipelines, monitoring, and AI-enabled analysis to help R&D teams move from data to
decisions more efficiently.
Help modernize the data and compute systems behind next-generation recording-head R&D. You will build practical monitoring and automation solutions, improve
production data flow, and explore frontier AI approaches such as natural-language-to-SQL and causal root-cause analysis.
