Meta was built to help people connect and share, and over the last decade our tools have played a critical part in changing how people around the world
communicate with one another. In the pursuit of personal superintelligence, Meta's AI systems are becoming more capable, and evaluating their safety is
critical. Meta is seeking Research Interns to join our Safety Evals and Red Teaming team within the Products and Applied Research org. We are seeking
individuals passionate in areas such as Agent Safety, Benchmarks and Evaluations, Synthetic Data Generation, Automated Red teaming, LLM-as-a-Judge, benchmark
design, eval harnesses, prompt injection, agent environments.Our internships are 16 weeks long and we have various start dates throughout the year.What You
Could Work On:Depending on your interests and the team’s priorities, a project could address questions such as:
- How reliably do LLM-based judges identify safety-policy violations across different tasks, prompts, and adversarial conditions? How should we measure their
errors and consistency?
- How should we evaluate prompt-injection risks when an agent interacts with tools and untrusted information? Which environments and attack scenarios expose
failures that simpler tests miss?
- Which automated red teaming methods uncover meaningful safety failures, and how well do those failures generalize beyond the prompts or environments used to
find them?
- How can we use synthetic data and benchmark design to improve coverage while checking that scores reflect the behavior we intend to measure?
