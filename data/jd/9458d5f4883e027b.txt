Are you interested in applying data science to real business and product challenges? As a Data Science Intern at Resideo, you will work alongside data
scientists, engineers, and business stakeholders to explore data, develop analytical solutions, and communicate actionable insights. You will gain hands-on
experience with Python, SQL, and Databricks while supporting data preparation, visualization, and predictive modeling. With guidance from the team, you will
complete a scoped project and present your findings and recommendations. Help create innovative technologies that make the world cleaner and more sustainable,
more secure, connected, energy efficient, and productive.
Job Duties
- Explore and analyze data to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for business or product improvement.
- Clean and prepare datasets, addressing missing values, inconsistencies, and data quality issues.
- Write SQL queries and Python scripts in Databricks to extract, transform, and analyze data from multiple sources.
- Develop Databricks notebooks, dashboards and visualizations to communicate key findings and track performance metrics.
- Support predictive modeling, including feature development, model training, and performance evaluation.
- Analyze product usage and performance to understand customer behavior and identify opportunities for improvement.
- Automate recurring analyses and reports to reduce manual effort and improve consistency.
- Collaborate with data scientists, engineers, and business stakeholders to translate business questions into analytical tasks.
- Document datasets, methods, code, and findings using team standards and Git workflows.
- Complete and present a scoped capstone project, explaining the approach, results, limitations, and recommended next steps.
You Must Have
- Active student status pursuing a degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field.
- Foundational experience with Python and SQL through coursework, academic projects, or internships.
- Basic understanding of statistics, data analysis, and machine learning concepts.
We Value
- Ability to communicate findings clearly, collaborate with others, and explain analytical work to technical and nontechnical audiences.
- Experience cleaning, exploring, and visualizing data through coursework, research, or personal projects.
- Familiarity with Databricks, Apache Spark, or cloud data platforms, and an interest in learning these tools.
- Exposure to Python libraries such as pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, or Matplotlib, and to Git for version control.
- Curiosity, attention to data quality, and the ability to break practical problems into manageable analytical tasks.
- Willingness to learn from feedback, document work, and collaborate with data scientists, engineers, and business stakeholders.
What's In It For You
- Hands-on experience: Apply Python, SQL, and Databricks to real business and product data.
- Mentorship and collaboration: Learn from experienced data scientists and engineers while contributing to meaningful projects.
- Professional development: Build data science skills and present a capstone project with clear results, limitations, and recommendations.
