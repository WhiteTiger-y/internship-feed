About Propopia
Propopia is building the intelligence layer for real estate — connecting fragmented data, client context and AI to help professionals make better decisions.
We leverage artificial intelligence, data and automation to transform how real estate professionals access information, understand opportunities and make
informed decisions.
About The Role
As an AI Engineer Intern, you will contribute to building and improving AI-powered capabilities across the Propopia platform. You will work on practical
applications of artificial intelligence, large language models (LLMs), machine learning, intelligent agents and automation to transform complex real estate
information into actionable intelligence.
You will have the opportunity to experiment with modern AI technologies, develop intelligent workflows, integrate AI models and build real-world applications.
This is an opportunity to gain hands-on experience developing AI-powered products at an early-stage technology company while working on real-world engineering
challenges at the intersection of AI, data and real estate.
What You'll Work On
- LLM Application Development: Build and experiment with applications powered by large language models (LLMs), including OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini
and open-source models.
- AI Agents & Automation: Develop intelligent AI agents and multi-step workflows to automate research, information processing and real estate intelligence
tasks.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Build systems that connect LLMs with structured and unstructured data to enable context-aware responses, semantic search
and intelligent information retrieval.
- Prompt Engineering: Design, test and optimize prompts and model interactions to improve the accuracy, consistency and usefulness of AI-generated outputs.
- AI-Powered Data Processing: Develop solutions for extracting, classifying, summarizing and structuring information from various data sources.
- AI Integration: Integrate AI models, APIs and services into Propopia's applications and backend systems.
- Machine Learning: Explore machine learning techniques for classification, prediction, recommendation and other real estate intelligence applications.
- Research & Experimentation: Evaluate emerging AI technologies, frameworks and tools to identify opportunities to improve Propopia's products and technical
capabilities.
Responsibilities
- Assist in designing, developing and testing AI-powered applications and intelligent workflows.
- Build integrations with LLM APIs, AI services and third-party data sources.
- Develop and experiment with RAG pipelines, vector search, embeddings and knowledge retrieval systems.
- Support the development of AI agents and automated workflows using modern AI frameworks.
- Process, clean and structure data for AI and machine learning applications.
- Evaluate model performance, test outputs and improve AI application reliability.
- Collaborate on backend development and API integrations supporting AI-powered product features.
- Research and prototype new AI capabilities that can be incorporated into Propopia's platform.
- Document technical implementations, experiments and development processes.
Qualifications
Interest in artificial intelligence, software engineering, machine learning or applied AI.
- Programming Languages: Familiarity with Python; experience with JavaScript or TypeScript is a plus.
- LLMs & AI APIs: Exposure to OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Meta Llama, Mistral or Hugging Face models and APIs.
- AI Frameworks: Familiarity with LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, AutoGen or similar AI agent and orchestration frameworks.
- RAG & Vector Databases: Understanding of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, semantic search and vector databases such as Pinecone, Weaviate,
Chroma, FAISS or pgvector.
- Machine Learning: Exposure to PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn or other machine learning libraries.
- Backend & API Development: Familiarity with FastAPI, Flask, Django, REST APIs or Node.js.
- Databases & Data Processing: Basic understanding of PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Supabase, Redis or data processing libraries such as Pandas and NumPy.
- Cloud & Deployment: Exposure to AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Docker, Vercel or serverless deployment environments is a plus.
- AI Evaluation & Observability: Interest in prompt engineering, model evaluation, LLM observability and tools such as LangSmith, Ragas or Langfuse.
- Development Tools: Familiarity with Git, GitHub, Jupyter Notebook and modern software development environments.
What You'll Gain
Hands-on experience developing AI-powered products at an early-stage technology company, working on real-world engineering challenges at the intersection of AI,
data and real estate.
