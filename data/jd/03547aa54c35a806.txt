Requisition Summary
Kenworth Truck Company is seeking a Data Analytics Intern to support business and manufacturing teams through data analysis, reporting, and process automation.
This role will help identify trends, develop data visualizations, automate data processes, and provide insights that drive business decisions while gaining
hands-on experience with analytics and business intelligence tools.
Internships are scheduled to begin May or June 2027
Responsibilities
- Identify valuable data sources and automate collection processes
- Undertake pre-processing of structured and unstructured data
- Automate manual data pulls
- Analyze large amounts of information to discover trends and patterns
- Present information using data visualization techniques
- Propose solutions and strategies to business challenges
- Collaborate with manufacturing and business teams
Requirements
- Experience in Data Visualization and Analysis
- Knowledge of R, Snowflake, SQL and Python; familiarity with Java or C++ is an asset
- Proficient in using SQL code in development of complex queries
- Experience using PowerBI business intelligence tool and Tableau
- Background in using Low-Code Software for Application development
- Analytical mind and business acumen
- Problem-solving aptitude
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- BSc/BA in Computer Science, Engineering, or relevant field
- Perform other duties as assigned
Education
Bachelor's Degree in progress focusing on Business, Supply Chain Management, or other related fields.
- Junior or senior standing preferred
