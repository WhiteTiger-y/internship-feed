Summary
Reporting to the Director of Analytics, you will use data to optimize the gameplay systems that power world-class EA SPORTS titles like Madden NFL , directly
influencing the experiences of millions of players worldwide.
Responsibilities
Analyze gameplay and player behavior data across Madden and College Football EA SPORTS franchises.
Use SQL, Python, R, or similar tools to test hypotheses and identify behaviors associated with retention and engagement.
Build a meaningful analytical project from problem definition through final presentation with support from the analytics team.
Use game knowledge to propose and build novel analyses
Translate complex analyses into clear findings for fellow Analysts, Product Managers, and Game Designers.
Qualifications
Pursuing an undergraduate or graduate degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, Data Science, or a related field.
Experience with SQL and familiarity with Python, R, or similar analytical tools.
Awareness of modern modeling techniques and statistical applications
Passion for gaming; familiarity with Madden, College Football, American football, or sports games.
You must be available for a full-time paid internship in the summer of 2027.
At this time, we are considering Undergrad students who will be enrolled in an accredited degree program in the summer of 2027, slated to graduate no earlier
than December 2027 . Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis during the 12-week internship. Visa sponsorship is
not available for this position.
Please ensure that your graduation date is visible on your resume.
