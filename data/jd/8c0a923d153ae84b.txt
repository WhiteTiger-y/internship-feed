Title: Research Assistant Intern (unpaid) Location: Gilbert, Arizona Department: Early Clinical Research Job Status: Part-time, Unpaid
Overview
We are seeking motivated and enthusiastic interns to join our research team. This is an excellent opportunity for students and recent graduates to gain hands-on
experience in clinical research activities.
Position Summary
The Research Assistant Intern position is designed to provide you with valuable insights into clinical research methodologies and practices, while offering the
opportunity to contribute to impactful studies that advance medical knowledge. The ideal candidate will be passionate about research and eager to learn in a
collaborative environment.
Key Responsibilities
- Assist in the design and implementation of clinical research studies.
- Conduct literature reviews and summarize findings.
- Support data collection efforts, including participant recruitment and interviews.
- Analyze research data and prepare preliminary reports.
- Maintain accurate and organized records of research activities.
- Collaborate with team members to ensure timely project completion.
- Participate in team meetings and contribute ideas for research initiatives.
Qualifications
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in a related field (e.g., Life Sciences, Public Health, Nursing, Psychology).
- Strong interest in clinical research and data analysis.
- Excellent organizational and time-management skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint).
- Familiarity with statistical software (e.g., SPSS, R) is a plus but not required.
Benefits
- Gain practical experience in clinical research.
- Mentor support from experienced researchers.
- Opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects that impact patient care.
- Networking opportunities within the research community.
- Flexible working hours to accommodate academic commitments.
