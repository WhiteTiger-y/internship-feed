What Makes This Role Great
In this role, you will work directly under the hood of enterprise-scale artificial intelligence ecosystems, collaborating with senior engineering leads and
experimenting in safe sandboxes to build intelligent software that drives measurable business efficiencies across the organization.
Key Exciting Responsibilities
- Design, code, and evaluate intelligent prototypes, agentic workflows, and automated pipelines that tackle enterprise business challenges.
- Partner directly with cross-functional business application teams to turn complex workflow requirements into functional AI-driven automations.
- Conduct rapid prototyping and sandbox testing to evaluate model performance, test technical hypotheses, and refine smart tools.
- Collaborate with senior engineering mentors to adopt industry best practices for model integration, APIs, and modern AI engineering standards.
Essential Skills & Experience
- Active pursuit of a degree in Computer Science, Management Information Systems, Data Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent demonstrable coding
proficiency.
- Practical programming foundation in languages such as Python, TypeScript, or C++ with sound software design fundamentals.
- Hands-on technical experience building or engineering AI solutions, integrating LLM APIs, or scripting custom tools beyond standard end-user prompt
applications.
- Collaborative mindset, strong problem-solving initiative, and clear verbal and written communication abilities.
Bonus Points For
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform, AWS, or Azure.
- Exposure to modern AI development frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or vector databases.
- Hands-on experience developing, integrating, or consuming RESTful APIs.
