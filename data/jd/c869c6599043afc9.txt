About the role We are seeking interns to build the data foundation for an enterprise AI capability serving senior executive leadership. You'll source, ingest,
validate and document reporting data the capability depends on, and help define what gets built and why by working directly with business stakeholders. You'll
work across Databricks and cloud environments in a highly regulated setting where data quality, lineage and controls matter as much as speed. What you will be
responsible for Agent-ready data and process content - convert business processes, policies, reports and data into structured, well-labeled formats (such as
decision tables, data dictionaries, tagged documents and clean tables) that agents can reliably search, interpret and act on. Data pipelines - build and test
ingestion and transformation logic in Python, SQL and Databricks from reporting data. Entity resolution and KPI definitions - match clients consistently across
sources, including parent/subsidiary structures, and standardize metric definitions so numbers are calculated the same way every time. Reconciliation and data
quality - reconcile figures to official sources and track completeness, accuracy, lineage and freshness. Integrate, don't duplicate - work with existing client
data platforms and data stores rather than creating new copies. Requirements and backlog - capture requirements from executive office stakeholders, maintain the
backlog, and map current-state processes and data flows. Evaluation sets and success metrics - turn real executive questions into test cases, and define
adoption and delivery measures. Product management - help shape the product vision and roadmap by prioritizing features on value, effort and risk, running user
feedback sessions and demos, and turning what users say into clear next steps for each iteration. Leadership updates - provide regular, concise progress updates
to senior leadership, covering what was delivered, key findings, risks or blockers, and next steps, and adjust the level of detail for executive and technical
audiences. Every pod member wears more than one hat You'll work in a small, cross-functional pod alongside interns in AI engineering, data, product, governance
and cybersecurity. Depending on your strengths, you may also: Translate business questions into data requirements and acceptance criteria. Build clickable
prototypes and wireframes to test ideas with stakeholders. Prepare structured and unstructured data for AI retrieval, such as table design, data dictionaries,
document parsing and metadata tagging. What we value Pursuing a degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Information Systems, Financial Engineering
or a related field. Strong Python (pandas, PySpark or similar) and working SQL. Experience cleaning, joining and validating messy real-world data (JSON, CSV,
PDF, HTML, Parquet). Exposure to cloud data platforms such as Databricks, Azure or AWS. Understanding of data quality, lineage and responsible handling of
sensitive data. Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD program. Underclassmen may apply. Authorized to work in the United States without current or
future visa sponsorship. Available January to June 2027 for at least 30 hours per week, including working onsite 4 days per week. Clear written and verbal
communication, including explaining technical work to non-technical and senior audiences. Curiosity, ownership and comfort working with ambiguity in a highly
regulated environment. Preferred Product management, business analysis or requirements documentation experience. Experience with financial, client (CRM) or
market data. Experience with REST APIs, Git, or document parsing and OCR. Experience presenting to senior leaders or large audiences, with excellent verbal
presentation skills and the ability to act as a bridge between business and technology teams, explaining technical work in business terms and turning business
needs into clear technical requirements. When applying, please have hands-on academic, research or project experience working with real-world datasets in Python
