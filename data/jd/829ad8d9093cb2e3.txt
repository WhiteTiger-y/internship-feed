At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently seeking multiple 2027
Interns in AI/ML & Data Engineering teams. These teams build the AI tools, data pipelines, and machine learning systems that help Regeneron develop medicines
faster and reduce manual work.
These positions offer the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.
When & Where
Location: Tarrytown, NY / Warren, NJ / Armonk, NY / Cambridge, MA / Sleepy Hollow, NY
Timeframe: Our internship positions are full-time from May/June-August; most are onsite, and some roles are hybrid
Pay: Intern and Co-op pay ranges are determined by level of education (year in school) and degree program. The hourly rate range for these locations is:
$17.90-$50.90 per hour.
What You'll Do
- Build and test AI solutions that are used on real systems — not shadow projects.
- Projects may include building AI agents and LLM-powered applications, mining text with natural language processing, building and modernizing data pipelines
and semantic data models, evaluating and validating AI tools and models, and automating routine workflows with AI-assisted coding tools.
- Expect hands-on software and data engineering, working with scientists and business teams to understand the problem, and responsible, well-governed use of AI.
- Python, SQL, and software engineering fundamentals are typically expected, along with familiarity with LLM prompting or machine learning packages. Several
projects are better suited to master's students.
Relevant majors: Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Bioinformatics, Information Technology, Mathematics, Biomedical Engineering
