Role Summary
As an intern on the Advanced Modeling & AI Solutions (AMAIS) team, you will help support development and prototyping of advanced physics-based and machine
learning models, and contribute to agentic AI systems that integrate with modeling tools and engineering workflows. You will also help design and implement
user-facing UI experiences (dashboards, apps, or lightweight front ends) that make insights and agent outputs actionable for engineers.
This is a hands-on role where you will collaborate with multi-functional engineering partners, contribute production-quality prototypes, and communicate results
through clear visualizations and storytelling.
Responsibilities
- Architect and prototype Agentic AI and LLM workflows that use tools, planning, actions, observations, and feedback loops to automate multi-step engineering
tasks.
- Develop RAG-based assistants, machine learning models, physics-based models, and other AI-Enabled solutions that improve engineering analysis, prediction,
diagnostics, and decision-making.
- Build intuitive web applications, dashboards, visualizations, and APIs that translate model and Agent outputs into clear insights, confidence indicators, and
recommended actions.
- Develop repeatable workflows for preparing structured and unstructured data and evaluating model and Agentic AI performance using appropriate metrics,
validation methods, and structured evaluations.
- Partner with engineers and data owners to scope problems, validate assumptions, document solutions, and communicate model performance, limitations, and
business or engineering impact.
Minimum Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a BS, MS, or PhD or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Applied
Mathematics, Physics, or a related technical field.
- Demonstrated programming experience with Python for data preparation, modeling, experimentation, or application development.
- Coursework, academic research, or project experience applying machine learning fundamentals and frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Coursework, research, or project experience with LLMs, Agentic AI, Agent workflows, or RAG, including an understanding of planning, execution, tools, and
observation concepts.
- Demonstrated experience developing user interfaces with modern web technologies such as React and working with APIs or services to display tables, charts,
visualizations, or interactive results.
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with Agentic AI orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, AutoGen, or similar technologies.
- Experience Using AI for code generation or development workflows, including tools such as Claude Code, or experience developing low-code or no-code solutions.
- Experience implementing structured evaluation approaches for Agent or LLM quality, including evaluation harnesses, prompt regression testing, or reliability
assessments.
- Experience with CI/CD, MLOps, machine learning pipelines built for real-world applications, or deployment practices.
- Experience with semiconductor or manufacturing datasets, high-dimensional sensor/process data, numerical methods, physics-informed modeling, multiphysics
modeling, multiscale modeling, or machine learning based on simulations.
