personalized, and secure, PayPal empowers consumers and businesses in approximately 200 markets to join and thrive in the global economy.

withdraw funds. We enable consumers to exchange funds more safely with merchants using a variety of funding sources, which may include a bank account, a PayPal

as gift cards, and eligible credit card rewards. Our PayPal, Venmo, and Xoom products also make it safer and simpler for friends and family to transfer funds to

Job Summary:
MLE PhD Intern plays a critical role in ensuring PayPal’s adherence to a complex and evolving landscape of local and global regulations. By leveraging advanced
machine learning, cutting-edge research, and data-driven insights, we design innovative solutions that streamline compliance processes, strengthen risk
mitigation, and empower PayPal to deliver a secure, trusted, and seamless financial experience worldwide.
Job Description:
As a Machine Learning Engineer PhD Intern, you will work with experienced engineers, data scientists, and product teams to research, develop, and evaluate
machine learning solutions. You’ll take on complex technical problems, work with large datasets, and contribute to model development and deployment. You should
be comfortable applying advanced machine learning knowledge, communicating your findings, and collaborating to bring solutions into PayPal products and
services.
Essential Responsibilities:
- Conduct applied research to identify and develop machine learning solutions for complex business challenges.
- Design, build, and evaluate machine learning models, using appropriate methods to assess their effectiveness, reliability, and suitability for use.
- Analyze data and conduct experiments to generate insights and guide model development.
- Collaborate with engineers, data scientists, and cross-functional partners to translate business needs into scalable machine learning solutions.
- Contribute to the development and deployment of machine learning pipelines and solutions in production environments.
- Monitor model performance and identify opportunities to improve outcomes, reliability, and efficiency.
- Document research, experiments, and technical decisions, and communicate findings to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Stay informed about developments in machine learning and assess their potential relevance to PayPal’s products and services.
Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications:
Minimum Qualifications:
- Currently pursuing a PhD in computer science, machine learning, statistics, or a related field.
- Strong foundation in machine learning and statistics, with research experience developing and evaluating models.
- Proficiency in Python and experience with machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn.
- Strong analytical, communication, and collaboration skills. Relevant research publications are preferred.
Internship Program Requirements:
- This is a Summer 2027 Internship program. Spring and Fall 2027 internships are not available.
- Must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship.
- Currently pursuing a PhD degree in computer science, machine learning, data science, computer engineering, or a related technical field at an accredited
college or university.
- Must be returning to school in Fall 2027.
- Applications will be accepted through Sunday December 6th, 2026.
