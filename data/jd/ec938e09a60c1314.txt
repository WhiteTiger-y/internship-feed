Job Description
Job Description:
We are seeking highly skilled and motivated candidates to join our Computer Vision team at Johnson & Johnson. As a summer intern, you will have the unique
opportunity to contribute to the development and implementation of state-of-the-art AI/ML methods for medical image and video understanding. Specifically, your
role will focus on utilizing advanced AI techniques to extract valuable insights from a wide range of medical imaging modalities such as endoscopy and
ultrasound videos, X-rays, dermatology/skin images, retinal OCT scans, and histopathology slides as well as building foundation and multimodal models that
generalize across these data types. By leveraging these cutting-edge technologies, you will enhance our understanding of medical conditions, procedures, and
interventions, and ultimately help improve clinical trial efficacy and efficiency.
Key Responsibilities
- Conceive, develop, and implement interviews with key internal clinical personnel to understand needs and use cases around AI tools for medical imaging and
video;
- Participate in cross-functional interviews, drive discussion and follow-up questions to interviewees, and compile answers into briefing reports;
- Extract insights from a collection of briefing reports focusing on the business value of AI pipelines across therapeutic areas;
- Design, develop, and evaluate computer vision and multimodal models on medical imaging and video data;
- Clearly articulate highly technical methods and results to diverse audiences and partners to drive decision-making.
Qualifications
Required Qualifications:
- Ongoing Master's/PhD degree in a quantitative discipline (e.g., physics, mathematics, computer science, electrical engineering, or similar);
- Demonstrated experience driving research in and applying computer vision techniques (e.g., Transformers, CNNs, RNNs, GANs);
- Demonstrated experience with state-of-the-art techniques for image and/or video understanding (e.g., foundation models, transformers);
- Proficiency with one or more programming languages such as Python or C++;
- Extensive experience with traditional computer vision applications, such as OpenCV, object detection, edge detection, and image segmentation;
- Experience with machine learning algorithms, including random forest, SVM, boosting, neural networks, etc.;
- Strong publication record and ability to effectively communicate technical work to a wide audience;
Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with medical imaging or video modalities, such as endoscopy, ultrasound, X-ray, dermatology/skin imaging, retinal OCT, or histopathology;
- Experience with foundation models and/or multimodal AI;
- Familiarity with and exposure to drug discovery and clinical development processes;
Experience working closely with healthcare professionals
