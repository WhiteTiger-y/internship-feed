MITRE’s Data Science and Engineering department is looking for applicants to bring their data science, data engineering, machine learning, artificial
intelligence, and applied research skills to bear on solving problems of critical national importance. MITRE’s diverse work program provides opportunities to
apply your expertise and creative thinking in challenging domains such as national security, intelligence, transportation, and healthcare. Employees may also
participate in MITRE’s internal research and development program, which provides funding for innovative applied research that addresses our sponsors’ hardest
problems.
The successful candidate for this position is a hybrid Data Scientist and Data Engineer who combines foundations in computer science, mathematics, and software
engineering with experience in data systems and applying machine learning and AI techniques to solve problems. The candidate should be able to operate across
the full data lifecycle from data modeling and data processing to statistical analysis, machine learning, and experimentation. Our organization values
innovation and believes that keeping up with the latest research and technologies is essential.
The candidate should have some experience in several of the areas specified below, however it is not required that a candidate has experience in all of these
areas.
Advanced Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning and Model Evaluation (e.g., supervised, unsupervised, deep learning, reinforcement learning)
- Statistical Analysis and Exploratory Data Analysis (e.g., descriptive statistics, regression, visualization)
- Large Language Models and AI-Assisted Workflows
- Natural Language Processing (e.g., summarization, classification, entity recognition, topic modeling)
- Anomaly Detection and Predictive Modeling
- Graph Analytics (e.g. centrality, similarity, link prediction)
Data Engineering
- Distributed Data Processing and Big Data Architectures (e.g., Spark, Hadoop, Ray, Dask, Kafka)
- Data Pipeline/ETL Development and Orchestration (e.g., Airflow, Dagster, NiFi)
- Data Modeling, Schema Design, and Data Quality Assessment
- Cloud-based Data Platforms and Storage Architectures
Software Development
- Computer Programming and Databases – Proficiency in Python and SQL required
- Linux-based Development Environments, Git, Containers, and Cloud Platforms

- Hands-on proficiency with advanced Data Science techniques, including Machine Learning (e.g., supervised, unsupervised, deep learning, reinforcement

- Computer programming skills (e.g., Python, R, C/C++, Java, Scala, Julia, Rust, MATLAB).
