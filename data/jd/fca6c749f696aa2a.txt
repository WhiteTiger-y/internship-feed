Your Role And Responsibilities
We are seeking a highly motivated Research AI Engineer Intern to join our team and help build, test, and deploy real-world applications of AI. In this role,
you’ll work closely with engineers, researchers, and product managers to translate cutting-edge AI techniques into practical solutions for enterprise and user-
facing applications, as well as contribute to the development of AI tools and platforms that improve how AI systems are built and deployed.
This internship is ideal for MS/PhD students who are passionate about LLMs, generative AI, and applied machine learning—and who want hands-on experience
creating AI-driven tools, workflows, and prototypes.
Responsibilities
