The Company
PayPal has been revolutionizing commerce globally for more than 25 years. Creating innovative experiences that make moving money, selling, and shopping simple,
personalized, and secure, PayPal empowers consumers and businesses in approximately 200 markets to join and thrive in the global economy.
We operate a global, two-sided network at scale that connects hundreds of millions of merchants and consumers. We help merchants and consumers connect,
transact, and complete payments, whether they are online or in person. PayPal is more than a connection to third-party payment networks. We provide proprietary
payment solutions accepted by merchants that enable the completion of payments on our platform on behalf of our customers.
We offer our customers the flexibility to use their accounts to purchase and receive payments for goods and services, as well as the ability to transfer and
withdraw funds. We enable consumers to exchange funds more safely with merchants using a variety of funding sources, which may include a bank account, a PayPal
or Venmo account balance, PayPal and Venmo branded credit products, a credit card, a debit card, certain cryptocurrencies, or other stored value products such
as gift cards, and eligible credit card rewards. Our PayPal, Venmo, and Xoom products also make it safer and simpler for friends and family to transfer funds to
each other. We offer merchants an end-to-end payments solution that provides authorization and settlement capabilities, as well as instant access to funds and
payouts. We also help merchants connect with their customers, process exchanges and returns, and manage risk. We enable consumers to engage in cross-border
shopping and merchants to extend their global reach while reducing the complexity and friction involved in enabling cross-border trade.
Our beliefs are the foundation for how we conduct business every day. We live each day guided by our core values of Inclusion, Innovation, Collaboration, and
Wellness. Together, our values ensure that we work together as one global team with our customers at the center of everything we do – and they push us to ensure
we take care of ourselves, each other, and our communities.
Job Summary:
Machine Learning Engineer Interns at PayPal help develop innovative AI and machine learning solutions that support products and services used by millions of
people every day. Working across PayPal, Braintree, Venmo, and Paydiant, our teams apply machine learning to complex challenges in connected payments. We’re
looking for candidates with strong programming and analytical skills, curiosity about machine learning, and a desire to build reliable solutions that improve
functionality, performance, and customer experiences.
Job Description:
As a Machine Learning Engineer Intern, you will work alongside experienced engineers, data scientists, and product teams to explore and build machine learning
solutions. You’ll contribute to data preparation, model development, experimentation, and deployment, with guidance from your team. You should be collaborative,
curious, and eager to learn as we develop scalable systems and new experiences across PayPal’s products and services.
Essential Responsibilities:
- Assist with developing, testing, and optimizing machine learning models and algorithms for business and customer needs.
- Prepare, clean, and analyze datasets, helping assess data quality and identify patterns that support model development.
- Run experiments and evaluate model performance using appropriate methods and metrics.
- Collaborate with engineers and data scientists to support scalable ML pipelines and the deployment and monitoring of models in production.
- Write clean, maintainable code and document experiments, results, and technical decisions.
- Participate in code and design reviews, follow team standards and development practices, and incorporate feedback.
- Stay curious about developments in machine learning and explore how relevant techniques can be applied to PayPal products and services.
Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications:
Minimum Qualifications:
- Experience with at least one programming language, such as Python, Java, C++, or similar.
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts through coursework, research, internships, or personal projects.
- Exposure machine learning frameworks or tools such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or similar.
- Familiarity with data structures, algorithms, data analysis, or statistical concepts.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.

- Must be returning to school in Fall 2027.
