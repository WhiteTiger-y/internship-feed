Job Description
As an Intern at the GE Aerospace Research Polymers and Performance Coatings Laboratory, you will work closely and directly with the individuals who have a
direct role in influencing and advancing the future of flight. Your project work will directly support researchers in the Coatings Lab at GE Aerospace Research
in their development of advanced surface solutions for the next generation of extreme environment-facing turbine components. You will participate in process
development research and establishing process-property-performance relationships for coatings that have potential applicability in future jet aircraft. This
program includes seminars on cross-center technology and soft skills development as well as opportunities for networking and showcasing project accomplishments.
We are seeking a motivated PhD-level intern to support the development and characterization of advanced coating systems for high-temperature applications. The
selected candidate will work closely with research engineers to develop new coating chemistries, optimize deposition processes, and analyze coating performance
through microstructural and mechanical testing.
Required Qualifications
- Current enrollment in a full-time Ph.D. degree program in Mechanical Engineering or related disciplines at an ABET accredited university
- Permanent legal authorization to work in the US is required. This role is restricted to U.S. persons. GE will require proof of status prior to employment.
- Willing to work at the GE Aerospace Research site in Niskayuna, NY
- Minimum GPA 3.0 / 4.0 scale
Desired Characteristics
- Formulate and prepare coating slurries and precursor suspensions.
- Assemble and operate spray rigs and deposition systems.
- Prepare and mount coated samples for metallographic analysis.
- Perform microscopy and imaging (SEM/EDS, optical microscopy, profilometry) to evaluate coating microstructure and thickness.
- Record and analyze experimental data; prepare concise test reports and presentations.
- Assist in troubleshooting experimental setups and improving process reproducibility.
- Excellent academic performance with technical research orientation
- Ability to adapt to change and willingness to be flexible in a global team environment
- Strong interpersonal skills, initiative and organization; demonstrated ability working with a team
- Strong problem solving skills, thorough attention to detail
