Reality Labs (RL) teams bring together a world-class R&D team of researchers, and engineers to build innovative photo, video, and AR/VR experiences. We work on
technologies that ship across all Meta surfaces ranging from Facebook to Instagram to the AR/VR Oculus products. We work at the intersection of Computer Vision,
