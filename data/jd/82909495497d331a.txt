The Enterprise Intelligence (EI) team is involved in every aspect of data at EA, using it to deliver valuable insights and solutions for game teams and players.
We drive strategy, build data and AI tools, and support personalization, experimentation, and analytics. Join D&I to work with experts dedicated to improving
understanding, workflows, and player experiences.
The Role
As an Analytics Intern on The Sims, you will work with massive volumes of player behavioural data to deliver insights to inform game development. You’ll join a
group of experienced analysts as a member of the embedded-and-dedicated analytics team and work closely with a large but tight-knit studio of enthusiastic
industry craftspeople from all walks of life.
This is a hybrid role based in Vancouver. You will report into an Advanced Analyst.
Key Responsibilities
Build dashboards and insight reports to help partners make better, faster decisions
Propose and analyze experiments and causal studies to drive business performance
Generate gameplay and player behaviour insights, embedding data in daily decision-making
Present findings to both technical and non-technical audiences
Explore and apply generative AI tools to increase the efficiency of your own work and support scalable insights
Qualifications
Pursuing an undergraduate degree in Statistics, Economics, Physics or a related field
Experience with SQL (able to write aggregations, joins, window functions)
Experience with at least one programming language for data analysis (e.g., Python, R)
Experience working with large datasets (e.g., coursework, research, or internships)
Experience with visualization tools (e.g., Looker)
Familiarity with basic statistical concepts (e.g., probability, correlation, regression)
Familiarity with experimentation
Familiarity with causal inference is a bonus
Familiarity with generative AI is a bonus
Passion for gaming; familiarity with The Sims™ or life simulation titles
You must be available for a full-time paid internship in the summer of 2027.
At this time, we are considering Undergrad students who will be enrolled in an accredited degree program in the summer of 2027, slated to graduate no earlier
than December 2027 . Applicants must be legally authorized to work in Canada on a full-time basis during the 16-week internship. Visa sponsorship is not
available for this position.
Please ensure that your graduation date is visible on your resume.
