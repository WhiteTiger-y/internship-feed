About This Opportunity
Inmar Intelligence is seeking a part-time Data Science Intern to support the MarTech Data Science team. This position provides an opportunity to gain practical
experience while contributing to ongoing data science and analytics initiatives.
The MarTech Data Science team designs, develops, and deploys data science and engineering solutions supporting Inmar's media and incentives businesses. The
intern will assist with data pipelines, machine learning models, and analytics solutions that support business needs and product development.
What You'll Do
- Assist in developing and maintaining reusable components for data transformation, feature extraction, and model deployment using cloud technologies, primarily
Google Cloud Platform.
- Support the development and deployment of machine learning models and analytics pipelines.
- Collaborate with internal teams to develop data-driven solutions for business and product needs.
- Assist with ad-hoc analyses and identify opportunities to improve or standardize recurring analytical processes.
- Utilize AI tools to support coding, analysis, and problem-solving while following appropriate validation and review practices.
- Contribute to ongoing team projects and technical documentation as needed.
Your Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Data Engineering, Computer Science, or a related quantitative field.
- Experience with SQL and cloud-based data platforms, preferably Google Cloud Platform. Experience with AWS, Azure, or Spark is also relevant.
- Experience with statistical programming languages and machine learning frameworks, including Python, R, SAS, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
- Familiarity with AI-assisted coding and problem-solving tools, including appropriate validation and review practices.
- Strong analytical, organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively with cross-functional teams.
- Ability to work part-time onsite in Winston-Salem, NC, while enrolled in an academic program.
Why Intern at Inmar Intelligence
This part-time internship provides an opportunity to apply academic knowledge in a professional setting while gaining experience with data science, machine
learning, and cloud-based technologies.
Interns work alongside experienced professionals, contribute to ongoing projects, and develop technical and professional skills through practical experience and
collaboration.
The position is designed to accommodate continued academic enrollment while providing meaningful work experience during the school year.
