Job Description
About the Team:
GM’s AV/AI Platform organization builds the software foundation and engineering tools that power safe, reliable, and scalable autonomous driving and advanced
driver-assistance systems. We are building a state-of-the-art AI training platform in close collaboration with model teams to deliver Autonomous car milestones,
maximizing model flop utilization and iteration speed, and enhancing inference systems. We are augmenting training, data processing, evaluation and the
deployment loop with a data flywheel to validate performance and continuously improve Autonomous driving and Active Safety. We accelerate compute performance
through architectural changes and low-level optimizations, informed by advanced HIL platforms, AI tooling and workflows and instrumentation built in-house. We
streamline compiler/build and modelbench performance; enhance data processing, preprocessing, and batch inference; and improve monitoring and availability to
increase velocity and quality.
About the Role:
As an intern, you will help develop and optimize our AI training platform by improving data processing pipelines, accelerating model training and inference
workflows, and/ or enhancing testing infrastructure. You will support tool development, instrumentation, and system monitoring to boost reliability, reduce
latency, and increase iteration speed for advancing autonomous driving performance.
What You’ll Do:
- Develop scalable infrastructure and tools to support model training, model deployment to the edge devices, egression, and rules-based models, operations, and
inference.
- Suggest, collect and synthesize requirements and work with your managers or tech leads to create feature roadmap
- Code deliverables in tandem with the engineering team.
- Adapt standard machine learning methods to best exploit modern parallel environments (e.g. distributed clusters, multicore SMP, and GPU).
- Perform specific responsibilities which vary by team.
