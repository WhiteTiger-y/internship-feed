We are hiring for this position in Trinity’s Waltham, MA office or NYC offices. Trinity Life Sciences is looking for a talented, committed, and energetic
individual to join our team as a Data Science Intern to support our growing Enterprise Analytics Delivery (EAD) function. This position will work side-by-side
with other consultants and analysts. Opportunity to gain exposure to a wide variety of strategic business issues, as well as the opportunity to gain in-depth
knowledge of the life sciences industry, ranging from small biotech’s to large pharma. Further, the intern will gain exposure to many of the leading patient-
level data sources in the industry as well as innovative technology and AI tools. Interns will develop their skills through formal and informal training, as
well as receiving mentorship that encourages them to build their strategic, analytical, teamwork, and communication skills. This is a 9-week, in-person Summer
internship and the program will run from June 15 to August 13, 2027. Interns will be paid $35/hour and are expected to work 40 hours/week. Applicants for this
position must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship. Trinity is unable to consider candidates requiring
