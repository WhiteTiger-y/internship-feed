About the Role
As an AI Product Intern , you will work closely with engineers, researchers, and product leaders to help design, build, and deploy AI-powered software solving
real-world problems. This is a highly hands-on role where you’ll gain exposure to the full product lifecycle, from understanding customer needs to shipping
production-grade AI applications.
You’ll contribute directly to live projects used by governments, healthcare systems, and industrial partners, while learning how cutting-edge AI is turned into
real, scalable products.
In This Role, You Will:
Build & Ship Real Products: Support the design and development of AI-powered features across front-end, back-end, and data workflows. Help prototype and ship
internal tools and customer-facing applications using modern web technologies
Learn Product + Engineering in Practice: Work with product managers and engineers to translate customer problems into clear product requirements. Gain exposure
to how AI models are integrated into real-world software systems
Collaborate Closely: Partner with engineers, researchers, and operators to test, iterate, and improve AI-driven workflows. Participate in design discussions,
sprint planning, and code reviews
Optimize & Improve: Assist with improving performance, reliability, and usability of deployed applications. Help analyze data, evaluate AI outputs, and iterate
based on real customer feedback
Make a Real Impact: Contribute to projects modernizing government workflows, improving healthcare outcomes, or optimizing critical infrastructure. See your work
deployed and used by real users — not just internal demos
