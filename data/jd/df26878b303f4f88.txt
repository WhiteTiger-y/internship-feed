Job Responsibilities
Position Overview
Magna’s ADAS technologies are shaping an autonomous future and improving lives. The ADAS Intern/Co-op supports the ADAS Environmental Modelling teams by
integrating, developing, testing, and debugging low-speed ADAS algorithms such as parking, trailering, surround view, and camera calibration, as well as high-
speed sensor fusion algorithms.
Essential Duties And Responsibilities
- Develop building blocks for ADAS features and algorithms
- Design and implement conventional image processing algorithms
- Design, develop/tune, and optimize deep learning models for ADAS computer vision features
- Demonstrate knowledge of late (object-level) and early (raw-data) sensor fusion
- Create or modify test cases based on requirements to cover new or modified functionality
- Debug and root-cause issues
- Report and document issues; propose corrections
- Perform system testing on bench and in vehicles to ensure the product functions and performs per customer requirements
Qualifications
- Holds bachelor’s degree in engineering
- Valid Driver’s license
Education / Experience
- Enrolled in a master’s or PhD degree program in Mechanical Engineering / Electrical Engineering / Automotive Engineering/ Computer Science or equivalent
Mandatory
Special Knowledge / Skills:
- Experience in Digital Image Processing or Machine Learning or vehicle dynamics OR control systems OR digital signal processing through coursework or industry

- Experience with C/C++, Python
- MS Office Tools knowledge

- Working knowledge in structured engineering approach
