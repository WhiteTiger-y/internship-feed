What You Can Expect In This Role
As a Spectrum intern, you’ll spend the summer working alongside two teams: your respective department and your intern cohort.
Department and team-focused projects account for about 80% of your schedule. You’ll spend the other 20% on internship experiences that include a kickoff
conference, professional development sessions and networking activities, webinars, community service, cross-functional projects and final presentations.
This experience is designed to help you explore your interests, expand your network and leave with a deeper understanding of both your field and the impact of
our business.
Internship responsibilities may include:
- Our team drives edge compute strategy for critical network functions and leverages deep DOCSIS expertise to research reliability improvements that enhance
customer experience. We investigate next-generation telemetry, proactive maintenance, and specification enhancements to build a more resilient network for end
users. A data science intern would accelerate this mission by building AI/ML models to analyze network telemetry, detect impairments, and predict customer-
impacting issues before they occur. They would also develop a reusable evaluation platform to benchmark ML approaches across multiple reliability and edge
compute use cases.
- AI/ML Platform Development — Design and build a reusable experimentation platform to evaluate ML models across multiple network reliability use cases.
- Telemetry Analysis — Analyze DOCSIS network telemetry data to identify patterns that predict customer-impacting issues before service degradation occurs.
- Model Development & Benchmarking — Build and compare ML models for anomaly detection, impairment identification, and customer experience impact
classification.
- Data Visualization — Develop dashboards that translate network telemetry into actionable reliability KPIs for the team's DOCSIS experts.
- Edge AI Prototyping — Research and prototype AI-driven automation concepts for real-time, autonomous responses to network impairments at the edge.
- Documentation & Delivery — Document findings, present recommendations, and deliver a roadmap for production deployment of the most promising ML approaches.
