internal stakeholders and leadership. - Perform exploratory data analysis and statistical modeling to identify patterns in customer usage, predict peak demand,
or support asset management and predictive maintenance initiatives. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams (operations, customer service, engineering) to

automation of routine analytics processes using tools like Excel. - Document analytical findings, create presentations, and participate in team meetings to
communicate insights effectively. This position is for a May 31, 2027 start date. This position may have the possibility to extend beyond the internship’s
