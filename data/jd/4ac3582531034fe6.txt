About the Role
We are recruiting motivated interns to design, build, and integrate AI-Agents and automation tools that enhance engineering efficiency across AUTOSAR, embedded
firmware, and cybersecurity workflows. You will work closely with our project teams to prototype agents, automate developer tasks, and integrate with existing
toolchains (e.g., Jira, Git/GitHub, CI/CD, Confluence).
This role is ideal for students or fresh graduates who enjoy solving practical engineering problems with AI and automation, and who want hands-on experience in
automotive software development.
Responsibilities
• Design and implement AI-Agents to streamline tasks such as code review, requirements mapping, test generation and log analysis.
• Build automation scripts and services that integrate with engineering tools (Jira, Git/GitHub, CI/CD, Confluence) and project workflows.
• Develop utilities for embedded firmware, AUTOSAR and cybersecurity
• Collaborate with engineers to define user requirements, success criteria, and evaluation metrics (effort/time savings, adoption readiness, scalability).
• Document solutions (quick-start guides, examples, contribution templates) to enable cross-team adoption.
Required Qualifications
• Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s Electrical/Computer Engineering, or related field.
• Programming skills in Python or C/C++.
• Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and ability to write clear documentation.
• Good communication and teamwork skills; ability to gather requirements and iterate on feedback.
What You Will Learn
• How to design AI-Agents that scale across teams.
• Best practices for integrating agents with enterprise engineering toolchains.
• Practical AUTOSAR and embedded development workflows.
• Cybersecurity automation in an automotive context.
