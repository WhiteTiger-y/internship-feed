Description
Location: Durham, NC (will start as remote)
Department: North American Research Laboratory
Employment Type: Internship (Part-Time, Temporary)
About The Opportunity
We are seeking a motivated and technically curious Research Engineer Intern to join our North American Research Laboratory team. This internship provides an
opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge research focused on auditory prostheses, cochlear implants, and implantable hearing solutions.
The ideal candidate will have experience with computer programming and an interest in signal processing, biomedical engineering, auditory neuroscience, or
hearing sciences. Working alongside engineers, scientists, and audiologists, you will support ongoing research initiatives through software development, data
analysis, experimental evaluation, and technical documentation.
Key Responsibilities
Research & Technical Support
- Support research activities related to auditory prostheses and hearing technologies.
- Assist in the development and application of software tools used in hearing research and implantable hearing solution development.
- Participate in the design, execution, and analysis of bench and behavioral studies.
- Contribute to research projects involving sound coding, signal processing, and other hearing-related algorithms.
Software Development & Data Analysis
- Develop and maintain research software using MATLAB and/or Python.
- Implement modern signal processing techniques for the analysis of cochlear implant and hearing research data.
- Optimize existing signal processing and statistical analysis tools.
- Assist with the processing, visualization, and interpretation of research data.
Documentation & Reporting
- Support the preparation and documentation of research protocols.
- Contribute to technical reports, presentations, and summaries of research findings.
- Document software development activities, technical approaches, and statistical analyses.
Requirements
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD program in: Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Auditory Neuroscience, Speech and Hearing
Science, Computer Engineering, Or a related technical discipline
- Programming experience with MATLAB and/or Python.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Demonstrated organizational and interpersonal skills.
- Proficiency with standard computer applications and technology tools.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a multidisciplinary research environment.
