Job Description
Bosch Research and Technology in Pittsburgh is seeking a motivated student intern to advance multimodal sensor foundation models for healthcare. Our research
explores how models trained on diverse sensor data can learn transferable representations that support health monitoring across people, devices, and
environments. You will work alongside experienced researchers to develop and evaluate methods that combine ambient and wearable sensing with advances in self-
supervised learning, multimodal learning, and foundation models. Potential applications include physiological monitoring, activity understanding, aging in
place, and caregiver support.
Responsibilities
- Contribute to the development of foundation models for multimodal sensor data, including physiological signals, motion, radar, acoustics, and other health-
related measurements.
- Investigate self-supervised learning, cross-modal alignment, and sensor fusion methods to learn representations from heterogeneous and partially labeled
datasets.
- Adapt and evaluate pretrained models for healthcare tasks, with an emphasis on generalization across individuals, sensor configurations, and environments.
- Explore robustness to missing modalities, noisy signals, limited annotations, and changes in data distributions.
- Collaborate with researchers to prototype health-sensing solutions and assess model performance, reliability, and practical limitations.
- Document and communicate findings through internal reports, presentations, and potential publications or patents.
Benefits
- Hands-on experience with multimodal sensing and foundation model research for healthcare.
- Mentorship from researchers in AI, sensing, and cyber-physical systems.
- Opportunities to contribute to research publications, intellectual property, and technologies with potential real-world impact.
Qualifications
Required
- Currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical
field.
- Strong knowledge of machine learning and deep learning principles.
- Proficiency in Python and experience with frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience with time-series modeling, multimodal learning, or sensor data analysis.
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills, with an ability to collaborate in a research environment.
- Interest in applying machine learning to real-world healthcare challenges.
Desired
- Research experience in foundation models, self-supervised learning, contrastive learning, masked modeling, or transfer learning.
- Experience working with physiological or behavioral sensor data, such as ECG, PPG, IMU, radar, or acoustic signals.
- Knowledge of signal processing and multimodal representation learning.
- Experience evaluating model generalization, uncertainty, personalization, or learning with limited labeled data.
- Familiarity with privacy-aware methods and responsible use of health-related data.
- Publications in machine learning, signal processing, biomedical engineering, or digital health venues.
