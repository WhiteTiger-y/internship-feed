At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently seeking multiple 2027
Interns in Genomics, Computational Biology & Translational Data Science teams, including the Regeneron Genetics Center and Research data science groups. These
teams use large-scale genetic, clinical, and molecular data — and machine learning — to find new drug targets, understand disease, and guide how medicines are
developed.
These positions offer the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.
When & Where
Location: Tarrytown, NY & Sleepy Hollow, NY
Timeframe: Our internship positions are full-time and onsite from May/June-August
Pay: Intern and Co-op pay ranges are determined by level of education (year in school) and degree program. The hourly rate range for these locations is:
$17.90-$50.90 per hour.
What You'll Do
- Analyze real, large-scale biological data to uncover how genes, proteins, and health outcomes are connected.
- Projects may include genome-wide association studies using biobank data (such as UK Biobank), developing clinical and health-related phenotypes, analyzing
biomarker and gene-expression data, building analysis pipelines and dashboards, applying deep learning to pathology images and DNA sequences, using AI tools for
protein design, and evaluating data from digital health sensors.
- These are computational roles: expect programming, statistics, and presenting your findings to scientists.
- Programming in Python or R and some statistics are typically expected; genetics or biology coursework helps. Many projects are better suited to master's or
PhD students.
Relevant majors: Statistics, Biostatistics, Data Science, Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Computer Science, Genetics, Biology
