Responsibilities:
- Engine performance, continuously improved by Agents: Build agent workflows that profile the risk engine, identify performance bottlenecks (latency, CPU,
memory, cost), propose and verify optimizations, and detect performance regressions. Explore RSI (Recursive Self-Improvement) loops — propose, benchmark,
evaluate, iterate — so optimization becomes a sustained, closed-loop process rather than one-off tuning.
- Proactive rule governance by Agents: Design agents that actively audit the rule base across its lifecycle — detecting redundant, conflicting, expired, or low-
value rules, analyzing online impact, and generating safe change or decommission proposals with human-in-the-loop approval, rollback, and audit trails.

benchmarks and guardrails, observability, and safety controls — and take prototypes to production landing with measurable impact.
