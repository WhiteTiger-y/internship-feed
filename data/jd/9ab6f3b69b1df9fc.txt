This is a 6-month, in-person opportunity in San Francisco.
About Engramme
Engramme is a Frontier AI lab spun out of Harvard focusing on AI x Human Memory. Persistent memory is a fundamental open problem at the heart of AI. Our vision
is to build models of human memory, and to endow humans with perfect and infinite memory.
Before starting Engramme, the founding team has produced and commercialized extensive research in machine learning and neuro-science inspired memory models:
- Gabriel Kreiman (CEO & Co-founder): Professor of Neuroscience and AI, Harvard Medical School. Led key research on brain memory consolidation at Harvard
University.
- Spandan Madan (CTO & Co-founder): PhD in Computer Science from Harvard, designed AI models which were commercially deployed at Adobe, Fujitsu, and Meta.
