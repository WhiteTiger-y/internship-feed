About the team
Our team designs and optimizes the next-generation end-to-end video system (for video production, processing, delivery, and consumption) to improve the quality
of experience (QoE) for our billions of users. We are looking for strong video algorithm engineers from all areas of video understanding, video processing,
video coding, video streaming, and video quality assessment, etc., who have a dedication to technical excellence and a passion to build large-scale and high-
performing video platforms and services.
We are looking for talented individuals to join us for an internship. PhD internships at Our Company provide students with the opportunity to actively
contribute to our products and research, as well as to the organization's future plans and emerging technologies.
Our dynamic internship experience blends hands-on learning, enriching community-building and professional development events, and collaboration with industry
experts.
Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis, so we encourage you to apply early. Please clearly state your availability in your resume (Start date, End
date).
Responsibilities:
- Research and develop algorithms for end-to-end video quality optimization, combining video analysis, processing, and encoding techniques.
- Explore and prototype solutions for video-on-demand (VOD) and live streaming scenarios, including monitoring, data analysis, and objective quality evaluation.
- Support the design and validation of video algorithms through experiments, benchmarking, and data-driven analysis.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate research 成果 into production workflows and evaluate impact through online or offline testing.
- Contribute to advancing state-of-the-art technologies in video quality optimization and large-scale video systems.
Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications:
- Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related quantitative field.
- Solid knowledge in one or more of the following areas: video enhancement, video compression, subjective/objective video quality assessment, video streaming,
or related video algorithms.
- Strong programming skills in at least one of the following: C, C++, or Python.
- Ability to conduct independent research and translate ideas into practical algorithmic solutions.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Familiarity with machine learning and image/video processing tools such as scikit-learn, OpenCV, FFmpeg, etc.
- Familiarity with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Exposure to multimodal large models (MLLMs) and AI agent technologies, with hands-on implementation or research experience preferred.
- Familiarity with Linux development environments, shell scripting, distributed systems, or large-scale data processing tools such as HDFS.
- Strong communication skills, eagerness to learn, and enthusiasm for applying cutting-edge technologies to real-world use cases.
