The Clinical Informatics group are looking for qualified interns in the penultimate year of their study to support our team in our London office starting Summer
2027. This is a 12-month University Placement opportunity.
The Clinical Informatics team works collaboratively with Clinical Operations and other stakeholders to identify data needs and develop innovative solutions that
support clinical trial design, monitoring, and operational efficiency. The team applies informatics principles and techniques to query, manage, analyse, and
visualize clinical trial data into actionable insights and developing data products tailored to study-specific and wider business needs. This internship will
provide hands-on experience in query-based programming and data analytics. Interns will work alongside experienced analysts to explore data requirements,
evaluate study protocols, and contribute to the development and validation of data products that address operational challenges. The team is highly
collaborative with members in London and Cincinnati and provides interns the opportunity to work with international colleagues.
The internship includes a structured onboarding programme followed by a mentored path towards greater independence. Interns will participate in collaborative
problem-solving sessions, group discussions with the global team, independent data exploration, and programming activities. Medpace offers an open learning
environment where interns are encouraged to develop their technical skills, contribute ideas, and participate in innovative projects. Throughout the placement,
interns will develop their proficiency in R and SQL and gain practical experience with tools and technologies including Snowflake, Power BI, and Microsoft
Excel.
Responsibilities
- Assist in developing and refining programming scripts, analytical methods, and algorithms to describe, classify, and explore operational trends and potential
clinical trial outcomes
- Query, clean, transform, and analyse relational data at study and enterprise levels to support data exploration, reporting, and decision-making
- Contribute to the design, development, testing, and validation of data visualisations, dashboards, and other custom data products
- Work collaboratively with colleagues across the global Clinical Informatics team and software engineering teams to support the delivery and improvement of
data solutions
Qualifications
- Must be pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science/engineering, biomedical engineering, health information, statistics, bioinformatics, or
related field
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Excellent analytical skills
- Experience programming with R, or other computer language
- Introductory Microsoft Excel skills

Medpace Overview
Medpace is a full-service clinical contract research organization (CRO) providing phase l-lV clinical development services to the biotechnology, pharmaceutical,
and medical device industries. Our mission is to accelerate the global development of safe and effective medical therapeutics through the advancement of
clinical research. We leverage in-country regulatory and therapeutic expertise across all major therapeutic areas including oncology, cardiology, metabolic,
endocrinology, central nervous system, and anti-viral and anti-infective. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, we employ more than 6,500 people across 40+
countries.
