Job Description
As an Intern for Energy and Utilities Software Product Design and AI Development supporting Honeywell’s Forge for Utilities software, you will help design,
prototype, and evaluate AI-enabled capabilities that improve how utilities plan, operate, and optimize the distribution grid. You will work closely with AI
engineers, power-systems specialists, data scientists, product teams, and utilities to turn utility use cases into practical software solutions.
In this role, you will contribute to roadmap initiatives such as agentic AI for load-flow analysis, electricity rate design, virtual power plant and distributed
energy resource management system (DERMS) operations, peak-load reduction, and load and distributed-energy-resource forecasting. Your work will emphasize
technically sound methods, measurable results, and solutions that can be integrated into trusted utility workflows.
Responsibilities
Key Responsibilities
- Prototype and evaluate AI and machine-learning capabilities for electricity distribution planning and operations
- Develop agentic AI workflows that can assist with load-flow studies, scenario analysis, and engineering decision support while maintaining traceability and
appropriate human oversight
- Explore analytics and optimization approaches for rate design, DERMS and virtual power plant operations, demand flexibility, and peak-load reduction
- Build and validate forecasting models for load, distributed energy resources, and other grid-relevant time-series data
- Prepare, analyze, and quality-check utility datasets; define metrics and experiments to assess model accuracy, robustness, and operational value
- Collaborate with software, product, and domain teams to integrate prototypes into existing platforms, document findings, and communicate recommendations
Qualifications
YOU MUST HAVE
- Pursuing a Master’s or PhD degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, Operations Research, or a related field.
- Must continue enrollment in degree program upon completion of the internship.
- Validated academic excellence (3.0 GPA and higher).
- Coursework or project experience in electrical engineering, electric power systems, distribution networks, load flow, energy markets, demand response, DERMS
or visual power plants.
- Experience or coursework in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, optimization, or a related technical field
- Proficiency in Python and experience using data-analysis and machine-learning libraries
- Ability to design experiments, evaluate models using appropriate metrics, and communicate technical results clearly
- Familiarity with modern AI frameworks and software-development practices, including version control, testing, and reproducible workflows
WE VALUE
- Experience with time-series forecasting, optimization, simulation, reinforcement learning, large language models, or AI agents and tool use
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, curiosity about utility operations, and attention to data quality and model reliability
- Ability to work collaboratively across engineering, data science, product, and customer-facing teams
