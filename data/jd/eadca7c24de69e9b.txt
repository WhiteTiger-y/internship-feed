You will join the Physics x AI research lab within Siemens’ Data and Artificial Intelligence organization — our central research hub for artificial intelligence
— for a Spring 2027 internship.
We are looking for a Research Intern in Scientific Machine Learning for Physics and Simulation. This position will be remote.
You’ll make an impact by:
- Developing and evaluating neural surrogate models that predict physical fields and engineering quantities on complex geometries.
- Incorporating physical laws, boundary conditions, and engineering knowledge into artificial intelligence models.
- Studying model accuracy and generalization across geometries, meshes, and operating conditions.
- Designing experiments with appropriate baselines, ablation studies, and reproducible evaluation.
- Implementing research prototypes, data pipelines, and GPU training workflows, and validating predictions together with simulation experts.
- Communicating findings through technical reports, presentations, and scientific papers.
You’ll win us over by having the following qualifications:
Basic Qualifications:
- Currently enrolled as a PhD student in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Applied Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, or a related field.
- Research experience in machine learning and deep learning, including developing and evaluating new methods.
- Hands-on experience developing and training neural networks in Python with PyTorch or a comparable deep-learning framework, including designing experiments
and comparing models against baselines.
- A sound foundation in linear algebra, optimization, probability, and numerical computation.
- A background in physics, engineering, or physical modeling, with the ability to learn new scientific concepts with guidance from domain experts, and clear
written and verbal communication skills.
- Minimal travel required (less than 10%).
- Proficient in English both written and verbal
- Legally authorized to work in the United States on a continual and permanent basis without company sponsorship.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Publications at leading artificial intelligence conferences (for example, NeurIPS, ICML, or ICLR), or in relevant scientific journals.
- Surrogate modeling, neural operators, geometry-aware machine learning, or graph neural networks for meshes, point clouds, or CAD geometry.
- Physics-informed machine learning, differentiable simulation, uncertainty quantification, or learning from limited simulation data.
- Scientific foundation models, multimodal learning, or generative artificial intelligence (pretraining, fine-tuning, diffusion, or flow-based models) for
physical prediction, geometry generation, or inverse design.
- Agentic artificial intelligence for tool use and simulation workflows, or reinforcement learning for engineering design, optimization, or control.
- Computational fluid dynamics, heat transfer, numerical PDE methods, or multiphysics modeling (for example, Simcenter STAR-CCM+, OpenFOAM, or NVIDIA
PhysicsNeMo), and GPU or distributed computing for large simulation datasets.
