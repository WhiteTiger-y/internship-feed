Job Purpose
The CNH Soil and Crop Modeling (SCM) Team develops cutting-edge simulation technologies that help design and optimize agricultural equipment used around the
world. Our team combines expertise in engineering, physics, and computational modeling to simulate how soil and crop materials interact with machine components,
enabling faster innovation and more efficient product development.
SCM engineers utilize advanced modeling techniques, including the Discrete Element Method (DEM), Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), and Multi-Body Dynamics
(MBD), to evaluate machine performance in virtual environments. These tools allow the team to support concept development, solve complex engineering challenges,
and improve equipment designs long before physical testing begins.
As an SCM intern, you will work alongside experienced engineers on real-world projects, contributing directly to the development of virtual crop and soil
models, simulation workflows, and validation activities. Interns are valued members of the team and play an active role in advancing CNH's simulation
capabilities and engineering innovation.
Key Responsibilities
Your responsibilities may include:
- Prepare and run CFD model of a combine cleaning and/or residue treatment system or a portion of it using a CFD simulation software
- Post process CFD simulation data to derive useful insights and prepare the data for model training using machine learning
- Develop surrogate models from the CFD date and optimize model parameters
- Collect, organize, and process CFD simulation datasets to extract machine performance metrics
- Collaborate with crop flow modeling, design, and test engineering teams to support the design, validation, and continuous improvement of CNH harvesting
equipment.
